Chapter 92: The Dead End

发布时间：2026/8/25 15:47:44

第九十二章：走投无路的僵局

The official report from the Donglai Island authorities arrived three days later, carrying the weight of absolute, frustrating impotence.

东莱岛当局三天后的官方调查通报，带着一种令人沮丧而又无能为力的冰冷现实。

Despite formal police reports and frantic cyber-tracking attempts, the investigation hit a stone wall. The local police force, unequipped for sophisticated international cybercrimes, concluded that the operation was executed by a professional syndicate that had scrubbed its digital footprint instantly. By the time the IP addresses were flagged, the culprits had already hopped through multiple offshore proxies and fled the country. Given Donglai Island’s minor geopolitical standing and lack of mutual legal assistance or extradition treaties with those foreign jurisdictions, tracking them down or bringing them to justice was legally and logistically impossible.

尽管他们第一时间报了警，并且网络警察也进行了紧急追踪，但调查最终还是撞上了一堵厚厚的南墙。东莱岛本就缺乏应对高水平跨国网络犯罪的专业警力，调查结果显示，这是一场由专业团伙精心策划的作案，对方在得手瞬间便彻底清除了所有数字足迹。等警方锁定IP地址时，这群黑手早已通过数个海外代理服务器远遁海外。以东莱岛微弱的地缘政治地位，根本无力与那些国家签署引渡条约或发起司法协助，想要将这群跨国罪犯绳之以法，在现实层面上无异于天方夜谭。

Back in the living room of "Fresh Mart," the heavy silence was finally broken by Miyetsuko as she calmly closed her laptop.

在“新鲜玛特”的起居室里，压抑的沉默最终被合上笔记本电脑的美悦子打破。

"Jurisdictional vacuum combined with professional anonymity," Miyetsuko stated, her voice chillingly devoid of emotion, though her fingers gripped the edge of the desk hard enough to turn her knuckles white. "In legal terms, the perpetrators operate in complete impunity. The local authorities have officially closed the active search."

“管辖权真空叠加专业级的匿名隐匿，”美悦子平静地开口，声音里听不出一丝情绪，但她紧紧扣住桌面的指关节却已经泛白，“从法律层面来说，这群罪犯目前处于绝对的逍遥法外状态。本地当局已经正式宣布结案了。”

"Useless," Rin spat out, slamming her fist against the counter with a muffled crash. "A bunch of useless bureaucrats! Our privacy gets broadcast to the entire internet, and all they can do is shrug and say 'oops, they left the country'?"

“真是一群废物，”铃咬牙切齿地一拳砸在柜台上，发出沉闷的撞击声，“一帮吃干饭的官僚！我们的隐私都被赤裸裸地挂在全网了，他们倒好，两手一摊直接一句‘人已经出国了无能为力’就打发了？”

Sayuri sat quietly in the corner of the sofa, wrapped tightly in a thick blanket despite the lingering summer warmth. Her eyes were downcast, staring blankly at the floor. The humiliating images of the drone footage still haunted her mind, making her feel as though millions of unseen eyes were still staring at her.

小百合默默地缩在沙发的角落里，尽管夏末的余温犹存，她却紧紧裹着一条厚厚的毯子。她垂着眼睑，目光空洞地盯着地板。无人机视频中那些极尽羞辱的画面依然在她的脑海中挥之不去，让她感觉仿佛有无数双看不见的眼睛此刻正黏在自己身上。

Seeing her distress, Fujiwara didn't say a word of empty comfort. Instead, he walked over, pushed aside the blanket, and pulled her unresisting body straight into his arms, burying his face in her fragrant hair.

看到她这般失魂落魄的模样，藤原没有说那些苍白无力的安慰空话。他只是大步走过去，掀开厚重的毯子，将她纤弱无力的身躯紧紧拥入怀中，将脸埋在她馨香的发丝间。

"Hey... look at me," Fujiwara murmured softly, his voice a deep, grounding anchor in the storm.

“喂……看着我，”藤原低声呢喃，声音低沉而充满力量，宛如暴风雨中让人安心的定海神针。

Sayuri slowly raised her tear-stained eyes to meet his fierce, unwavering gaze.

小百合缓缓抬起挂着泪痕的眸子，迎上他坚定而炽热的目光。

"Let the whole world look if they want to," Fujiwara said, his lips curving into a dangerous, defiant smirk as his arms tightened protectively around her. "They can screenshot, they can gossip, and those cowards can hide behind foreign borders all they like. But they're never touching you again. As long as I'm breathing, this island is our fortress, and nobody—absolutely nobody—gets to lay a hand on my queens."

“管他全网怎么看呢，”藤原嘴角勾起一抹危险而霸气的弧度，双臂将她护得密不透风，“让他们去截图，让他们去八卦，让那些鼠辈躲在国境线后面去吧。但他们休想再碰你一根汗毛。只要我还在喘气，这座小岛就是我们的堡垒，任何人——绝对没有任何人，敢再动我的女王们一根手指头。”

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, a subtle, reassuring hint of approval returning to her eyes. Rin crossed her arms, letting out a sharp breath before a confident, fierce smile broke across her face.

美悦子推了推眼镜，镜片后重新浮现出一抹让人安心的笃定。铃双手抱胸，长舒了一口气，随后脸上也绽放出一个自信而锐利的微笑。

"Well," Rin muttered, walking over to squeeze Sayuri's shoulder gently. "Since the cops are useless, I guess that just means we'll have to keep our doors locked a little tighter from now on. And hey... at least we know who our real family is."

“行吧，”铃走过去，轻轻捏了捏小百合的肩膀，“既然警察指望不上，那就说明以后咱们得把大门锁得更严实点。再说了……至少这事儿让我们看清了，谁才是真正能豁出去保护咱们的人。”

Surrounded by the unwavering loyalty and fierce protection of the people who mattered most, the dark cloud over Sayuri's heart finally began to lift, replaced by a profound, unbreakable sense of belonging.

在身边最重要之人的坚定守护与绝对偏爱下，小百合心头阴霾的乌云终于渐渐散去，取而代之的是一种深沉而牢不可破的归属感。
