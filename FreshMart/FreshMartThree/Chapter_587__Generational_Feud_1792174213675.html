Chapter 587: Generational Feud

发布时间：2026/10/17 02:10:12

第五百八十七章：世代情仇

As the laughter slowly died down in the grand salon and Fujiwara began his vigorous, hands-on "inspection" of Ward 183828 right on the velvet sofa, Chika leaned against the armrest, munching on a fresh snack. Her curiosity, usually reserved for gossip and dairy metrics, finally caught up with her.

随着大起居殿内的笑声渐渐平息，藤原大帝已经开始在天鹅绒沙发上对美悦子的“183828号偏远领地”展开了全方位的深度实地考察。千夏靠在沙发扶手上，一边嚼着零食，一边把积攒了许久的八卦欲给勾了出来。

"Wait a second," Chika frowned, tossing an empty wrapper aside as she looked over at the little succubus queen. "How on earth do you know so much about the Heavenly Emperor's ex-girlfriends, Rin? Is there a public registry for discarded mistresses in the Succubus Realm? Did the succubi set up an ex-support group database?"

“等一下，”千夏皱起眉头，随手扔掉一个空包装袋，目光落在了那位正在晃脚丫的小魅魔女王身上，“小铃，你为什么对天帝那个老杂毛的感情史和前女友花名册知道得这么清楚啊？难道魅魔界里还专门成立了个‘天帝前女友互助会’不成？”

Blind Rin stopped swinging her legs. The little succubus tilted her head, her milky eyes gazing vaguely into the middle distance as she accessed the deepest, oldest vaults of her demonic memories.

盲人小铃停下了晃荡的双腿。小魅魔歪了歪脑袋，空灵的盲眼微微出神，仿佛思绪正飘向魅魔界那些尘封了无数年的古老禁区。

"Um... hello? It's not a secret support group!" Rin chirped, her sweet voice carrying a rare, historical gravity. "It’s a generational blood feud! The reason Rin knows all about the Heavenly Emperor’s trashy breakup habits is because he used to be my great-great-grandmother’s absolute worst boyfriend!"

“唔……才不是什么互助会呢！”小铃脆生生地答道，甜美的嗓音里难得带上了一丝历史的厚重感，“这是一场跨越世代的血海深仇！小铃之所以对天帝那些渣男分手套路了如指掌，是因为那个老杂毛……八百年前其实是我曾外祖母的死对头兼前男友！”

The entire room went still. Even Fujiwara paused his rhythmic pelvic thrusts against Miyetsuko for a split second, his eyebrows shooting up in profound shock.

整个大起居殿瞬间鸦雀无声。就连正在美悦子身上进行有节奏运动的藤原都动作一滞，挑起眉头露出了极其意外的表情。

"A generational blood feud between heaven and the Succubus Realm?" Sayuri set down her teacup, her velvet eyes narrowing with sharp analytical interest. "Elaborate, Your Majesty. What happened between the gods and the succubi?"

“天界和魅魔界之间还有过这种世代情仇？”小百合放下茶杯，丝绒般的眼眸中闪烁着敏锐的八卦之光，“快展开讲讲，陛下。神仙和魅魔老祖宗当年到底发生过什么惊天动地的狗血虐恋？”

"Well, about eight hundred years ago, back when the universe was much younger and the cosmic Wi-Fi was terrible, the Heavenly Emperor and the First Succubus Queen—my great-great-grandmother—were actually a hot, power-couple pair of star-crossed lovers!" Rin explained happily, swinging her legs again. "They used to hold romantic dates on the edge of the galaxy, eating cosmic cotton candy and holding hands."

“唔……故事要从大约八百年前说起啦！那时候宇宙还挺年轻的，宇宙网速也差得要命。当时的初代魅魔女王——也就是小铃的曾外祖母，和年轻时的天帝其实是一对轰轰烈烈、跨越仙魔两界的绝美情侣档呢！”小铃开心地晃着脚丫解释道，“他们以前经常手拉着手去银河边缘约会，一边吃着宇宙棉花糖一边看星星呢。”

"Wait. God and a Succubus?" Chika gasped, her jaw dropping. "That is the most unholy, cross-factional rom-com setup I have ever heard! What went wrong?"

“等等。神仙和魅魔谈恋爱？！”千夏震惊得下巴都要掉了，“这绝对是我听过最违背天条、跨界跨得离谱的魔幻爱情喜剧啊！后来怎么翻车的？”

"Greed and infidelity, obviously!" Rin chirped cheerfully. "The Heavenly Emperor was a total player! Even though he had the First Queen, he kept sliding into the DMs of every fairy, goddess, and mortal woman across the cosmos. My great-great-grandmother caught him cheating with three hundred cloud-nymphs simultaneously, threw a massive tantrum, and dumped him on the spot!"

“还能是因为啥，当然是因为渣男劈腿呗！”小铃甜甜地笑道，“天帝那老杂毛是个出了名的海王！明明家里有初代女王了，结果天天背着到处勾搭宇宙里的各路仙女、女神和凡间村姑。我曾外祖母当场抓包了他同时跟三百个云中仙女群聊暧昧的聊天记录，气得当场掀了桌子，一脚把他给甩了！”

Miyetsuko, gasping softly beneath the Emperor's rhythmic impacts, chimed in with deep sympathy. "Ah... so that explains why he was so emotionally distant during our weekend shifts. He was projecting his generational trauma onto me."

正被藤原大帝有节奏地撞击得娇喘连连的美悦子，此时也忍不住带着同病相怜的语气插话道：“啊……怪不得他在周末值班的时候总是显得心不在焉、冷冰冰的……原来他是在把我当成宣泄当年被甩阴影的工具人呐。”

"Exactly!" Rin nodded enthusiastically. "After getting dumped, the Heavenly Emperor's ego was so thoroughly shattered that he spent the next three hundred years writing angry poetry and deleting my ancestor's phone number. But things escalated around five hundred years ago. He got bitter, she got furious, and a massive, all-out dimension-wide war broke out between the Heavenly Palace and the Succubus Realm!"

“对呀对呀！”小铃用力点头，“被甩了之后，天帝那脆弱的自尊心碎得稀巴烂，花了整整三百年写酸诗骂街，还把老祖宗的联系方式全拉黑了。结果矛盾在五百年前彻底激化，他越想越气，我老祖宗也火冒三丈，两边直接撕破脸，在天庭和魅魔界之间爆发了一场打得天昏地暗的跨维度世界大战！”

Fujiwara finally stopped his physical inspection for a moment, resting his hands on Miyetsuko's plush hips as a slow, wicked grin spread across his face. "A full-scale war between heaven and the Succubus Realm? Let me guess—it ended in a disastrous stalemate where both sides realized nobody was winning, so they mutually agreed to sweep it under the rug and pretend it never happened."

藤原终于停下了手上的实地考察，把双手搭在美悦子的丰腴臀肉上，脸上渐渐浮现出一抹恶劣至极的坏笑。“天界和魅魔界打了一场世界大战？让我猜猜——最后的结果肯定是两败俱伤、谁也奈何不了谁，于是两边心照不宣地把这事儿当成黑历史合伙掩盖了，装作什么都没发生过？”

"Spot on, Master!" Rin giggled. "Both sides took massive casualties, the cosmic GDP dropped by half, and they were both too embarrassed to admit defeat. So they signed a secret non-aggression pact, deleted the history books, and the Heavenly Emperor started his cheesy ex-girlfriend serial-number support group as a passive-aggressive coping mechanism!"

“完全正确，主人！”小铃拍手娇笑道，“双方当时都打得元气大伤，宇宙GDP直接腰斩，两边又拉不下脸来认输，最后只能签了个绝密的互不侵犯条约，把历史书全烧了。至于那个什么前女友俱乐部和流水线编号大统领嘛……纯粹是天帝那老杂毛用来进行精神胜利法的恶趣味报复手段啦！”

The grand salon erupted into laughter once more. The grand, terrifying "Heavenly Palace" that had threatened to burn the empire to ashes just days ago suddenly felt a whole lot less intimidating now that they knew the Heavenly Emperor was just a deeply traumatized, serial-cheating ex-boyfriend with a historical commitment issue and a box full of recycled ID badges.

整个大起居殿再次爆发出一阵欢乐的哄笑。几天前还用灭世天火把帝国逼到绝境、高高在上的“天宫”，此刻在众人眼里顿时失去了神秘感与恐惧感——原来高高在上的天帝，不过是个当年被魅魔老祖宗甩了、至今心理阴影还没治好、只能靠发劣质前女友纪念勋章来维持尊严的过气老渣男。

"Well," Fujiwara grinned, pulling Miyetsuko back into action with renewed, mocking vigor, "since the Heavenly Emperor has a multi-century history of being dumped by succubi, I suppose it is the Fujiwara Empire's sacred duty to carry on the family tradition. Miyetsuko, as your reward for securing Ward 183828, tell the Heavenly Palace that the Grand Commander of the Underworld is officially filing for cosmic alimony!"

“行吧，”藤原咧嘴一笑，带着更加恶劣的戏谑重新开始了对美悦子的“深度考察”，“既然天帝这老小子有着几百年被魅魔无情抛弃的光荣传统，那咱们藤原帝国自然有责任将这门优良的家族传统发扬光大。美悦子，作为你成功拿下183828号领地的奖励，你现在立刻回天庭给那个老杂毛发个传票，就说咱们新上任的冥界大统领，今天要正式找他索要高维巨额抚养费了！”
