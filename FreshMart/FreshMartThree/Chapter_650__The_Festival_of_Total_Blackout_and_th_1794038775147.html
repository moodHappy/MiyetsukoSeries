Chapter 650: The Festival of Total Blackout and the Culling of the Weak

发布时间：2026/11/7 16:06:11

第六百五十章：熄灯大典与人口消灭计划

The obsidian lanterns of the imperial capital were being extinguished one by one, plunging the gargantuan galactic metropolis into a pitch-black, suffocating stillness.

京城的黑曜石街灯正一盏接一盏地被依次掐灭，将这座庞大无朋的星际大都会彻底拽入伸手不见五指的窒息死寂之中。

It was the eve of the Empire’s most sacred, terrifying festival: the Festival of Total Blackout (熄灯节).

这便是全帝国最神圣也最令人毛骨悚然的年度大典——熄灯节的降临之夜。

According to the unyielding imperial statutes that had governed the realm for three centuries, the rules of the Blackout were absolute and unforgiving: from sunset to sunrise, every single household across the star systems was strictly forbidden from emitting a single spark of artificial light. No matter how wealthy, noble, or powerful a grandee might be, turning on a lamp during the festival constituted an immediate act of high treason, punishable by public execution and the confiscation of all family assets.

依据这条延续了整整三百年的帝国铁律，熄灯节的规矩残酷而绝对：自日落至拂晓，全星系大大小小所有的府邸宅院，严禁亮起哪怕一丝一星的人工火光。无论你是权倾朝野的门阀显贵，还是日进斗金的超级富豪，只要在节日期间被举报开灯，当场便以叛国罪论处，施以极刑并抄没家产。

However, darkness bred lawlessness, and the true horror of the Blackout lay in its sanctioned nocturnal blood sport.

然而，黑暗滋生无法无天的野性，熄灯节真正的恐怖之处，远不止于禁灯。

During the festival, any stranger possessed the legal right to wander into another citizen’s residence and lay claim to the master’s wife. The sole precondition was primitive and brutal: the intruder had to physically defeat the husband in single combat. If the husband proved too weak to defend his hearth, the victorious stranger was legally permitted to occupy the household and ravish the man's wife throughout the entire night.

在此节日期间，任何陌生男子皆可大摇大摆地闯入任意一户人家，对别人的娇妻美眷起意调戏甚至强占。而唯一的先决条件粗野而原始：只要他能在拳脚肉搏中击败这家户主，便能理直气壮地在熄灯之夜霸占人家的妻子。

As a result, the weeks leading up to the festival transformed the empire into a massive, frantic gymnasium. Countless men spent their days pumping iron and practicing martial arts, eagerly scouting out target residences, mapping the floor plans, or casting lingering, predatory glances at the local beauties—with more than a few desperate bachelors plotting to hunt down the unreachable campus belles or former high-school goddesses they had hopelessly pined for years ago.

因此，每当节日临近的数周里，整个帝国的街头巷尾便化作了疯狂的健身房。无数血气方刚的男子日夜苦练格斗、挥汗如雨，提前踩好目标宅邸的点，死死盯着哪家藏着娇美的新婚少妇；更有不少处心积虑的家伙，暗中瞄准了当年自己苦苦追求却爱答不理、高不可攀的昔日校花与白月光，摩拳擦掌准备在今夜一雪前耻。

Yet human malice always found a way to corrupt even the most depraved traditions.

然而，人类的卑劣总能将最堕落的传统再度践踏出更无耻的花样。

Among the thousands of opportunistic intruders, a special breed of cowardly scum emerged: men who lacked the physical prowess to defeat a husband themselves, opting instead to hire brutal, professional street fighters to do the heavy lifting. Once the hired muscle violently crushed the helpless, incompetent husband and cleared the path, these sneeking cowards would slip through the back door, replace the battered fighter in the dark, and steal the night's reward with zero effort.

在这场全民掠夺的狂欢中，还滋生了一种极其恶劣的群体：有些体质孱弱却心思歹毒的家伙，舍得重金雇佣凶狠暴戾的地下打手替自己上门踢馆。待到打手把那无能的丈夫打得肋骨断裂、跪地求饶后，这群躲在暗处的懦夫便悄悄溜进内室，把打手替换下来，鸠占鹊巢地同那花容失色的美妇人共度春宵。这种行径严重破坏了传统节日的公平竞技精神，历来遭到官府的严厉打击。

Inside the luxurious grand salon of the imperial palace, the holographic tactical map projected the rising tide of domestic brawls across a billion worlds.

皇宫奢华的大起居殿内，全息战术沙盘正投射着数以亿计星球上不断升温的民间斗殴热度。

Emperor Fujiwara leaned lazily against the obsidian throne, swirling a glass of dark red wine while Chika and Sayuri sat on either side of him.

藤原大帝懒洋洋地斜倚在黑曜石王座上，手里摇晃着一杯深红色的葡萄酒，千夏和小百合左右相伴。

"A magnificent holiday," Fujiwara chuckled, watching a split-screen feed of a terrified merchant cowering while a muscular intruder kicked down his front door. "Look at the sheer vigor of the populace! Not a single dull moment from core worlds to the outer rims."

“妙不可言的传统佳节，”藤原轻笑着，看着分屏监控里一个满脸惊恐的富商正缩在角落，眼睁睁看着肌肉虬结的闯入者一脚踹开大门，“瞧瞧民间这生龙活虎的干劲！从核心星区到边陲外藩，压根儿就没有一刻冷场。”

Sayuri gently tapped her velvet fan, her lips curling into a cold, macroeconomic smirk.

小百合轻摇丝绒折扇，唇角勾起一抹冰冷且充满宏观经济学意味的讥诮。

"Indeed, a masterpiece of social engineering, my Lord," Sayuri purred smoothly. "While the peasants and low-ranking bourgeois believe it is merely a festival of romance and martial test, the historical core of the Blackout has always been population control. By legally unleashing hyper-aggressive, testosterone-fueled males upon one another every year, we systematically prune the surplus population, eliminate weak genetic lines, and relieve municipal welfare pressures without spending a single copper coin on retirement homes."

“确属一记堪称神来之笔的社会工程学杰作，夫君，”小百合软糯地剖析道，“底层平民和中产小市民虽以为这不过是一场释放荷尔蒙的浪漫比武，可这熄灯节延续三百年的本质，从来都是一场高效的人口清洗计划。通过每年合法地放纵这群暴戾好斗的雄性自相残杀，朝廷不仅神不知鬼不觉地消减了过剩的底层人口、淘汰了劣质基因，更替帝国的养老财政省下了一大笔天文数字的抚恤开支。”

Chika snorted, popping a grape into her mouth as her heavy breasts heaved with amusement.

千夏嗤笑一声，随手丢了颗葡萄进嘴里，胸前饱满的巨乳因幸灾乐祸而剧烈起伏。

"And think of all those pathetic husbands who spent the whole year working overtime to buy jewelry for their wives, only to have some sweaty street brawler break their nose and steal her in the dark!" Chika laughed out loud. "Talk about an ultimate consumer trap!"

“再想想那些窝囊废丈夫，整整一年累死累活加班加点，好不容易攒钱给老婆买首饰，结果倒好，半路杀出个满身臭汗的壮汉一拳砸碎鼻梁，顺理成章把老婆按在床上过夜！”千夏放声大笑，“还有比这更彻底的终极接盘侠碎钞机吗？”

Miyetsuko adjusted her 183828号 Death God glasses, checking the administrative casualty quotas on her terminal.

美悦子推了推鼻梁上的183828号死神眼镜，漠然核对着终端上的伤亡指标。

"The emergency hospitals are fully staffed, and the municipal crematoriums have pre-heated their plasma furnaces," Miyetsuko reported with clinical indifference. "Current projections estimate a three percent reduction in the surplus labor demographic by sunrise. A highly successful operational cycle."

“各大急救中心已全员上岗，市属火葬场的等离子熔炉也已提前预热完毕，”美悦子语气冰冷地汇报，“据当前模型测算，待到日出之时，多余的底层劳动力人口将实现约百分之三的稳步负增长。又是一个运作极其高效的丰收周期。”

Rin giggled, floating upside down above the obsidian armrest with her tail flicking mischievously.

小铃飘在黑曜石扶手上空倒挂着娇笑，尖尖的尾巴顽皮地左右晃动。

"Ooh, look at sector 409! A guy hired three thugs to beat up a shopkeeper, and then tried to sneak in himself, but the thugs ended up beating him up too and sharing his wife instead!" Rin clapped her hands in glee. "Serves the cheating coward right!"

“哇，快看409星区！有个蠢货花钱雇了三个打手去砸门，结果自己溜进去时，连那三个打手一块儿把他给揍了一顿，顺手连他自个儿带那妇人全给一块儿办了！”小铃拍手欢呼，“这叫恶人自有恶人磨，活该这缩头乌龟偷鸡不成蚀把米！”

Fujiwara burst into booming laughter, pulling Sayuri and Chika into his lap as he raised his wine glass toward the sprawling, darkening star map.

藤原大帝听得仰天狂笑，一把将小百合与千夏同时拉进怀里狠狠搂住，举起酒杯遥敬那片逐渐陷入全域黑暗的浩瀚星图。

"Let the lights go out across the universe!" Fujiwara declared grandly, his eyes gleaming with the absolute joy of a tyrant. "Let the strong take their prize, let the weak be culled, and let every single beautiful woman in the empire tremble in the dark as her door swings open! To the survival of the fittest, and the eternal glory of the Fujiwara bloodline!"

“让全宇宙的灯火彻底熄灭吧！”藤原意气风发地朗声宣告，眼眸中闪烁着暴君独有的狂热与快意，“让强者各取所需，让弱者在厮杀中消亡，让帝国境内所有的绝色佳人在黑暗中颤抖着等待房门被撞开！为了适者生存的优胜劣汰，为了藤原家族万世永存的无上荣光——干杯！”
