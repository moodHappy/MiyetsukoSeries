Chapter 144: The Real Purpose Behind the Eggs

发布时间：2026/8/26 23:28:25

第一百四十四章：鸡蛋背后的真实目的

True to her word, the stunning beauty returned to "Fresh Mart" the very next afternoon, storming through the front doors with an even more dramatic flair than before, demanding to speak directly to the manager.

果不其然，那位美艳动人的年轻女子在次日下午再次气势汹汹地登门造访。她一把推开“新鲜玛特”的大门，踩着高跟鞋踏进店里，摆出一副比昨天还要兴师问罪的架势，大声嚷嚷着要当面找老板讨个说法。

Fujiwara stepped out from behind the counter, letting out a weary sigh as he prepared himself for another round of absurd arguments about miraculous livestock. But before he could even utter a word, the beauty's fierce expression suddenly melted. Her cheeks flushed a deep, unmistakable crimson, and she bit her lower lip with sudden shyness.

藤原无奈地叹了口气，从柜台后走了出来，本以为又要应付一轮关于神奇母鸡的荒唐争辩。谁知还没等他开口，这位美女脸上的怒容瞬间烟消云散，双颊飞起两团娇艳的红晕，羞涩地咬住下唇。

"Alright, fine! I give up!" she hissed in a low, embarrassed whisper, leaning across the counter. "The grandma story was total BS, okay? I didn't come here because of any eggs!"

“好啦好啦！我装不下去了行了吧！”她压低了声音，羞赫地凑到柜台前坦白道，“什么奶奶怀孕根本就是我胡编乱造的借口！我才不是为了什么破鸡蛋来的！”

Fujiwara blinked, caught completely off guard. "Wait... what? Then why on earth did you cause such a massive scene yesterday?"

藤原当场愣住了，一脸错愕：“等等……什么？那你昨天费尽心思跑来店里大闹一场到底是图什么？”

The beauty crossed her arms, casting an admiring, greedy glance at Fujiwara as she dropped her final confession. "Because I've seen the kind of stunning, gorgeous women you keep around you here. A man who can charm and capture queens like that must be extraordinary! I just wanted a chance to get your attention... and honestly, I'm dying to spend a wild, passionate night in your arms."

美女双手抱胸，目光火辣而毫不掩饰地上下打量着藤原，吐出了压在心底的真实目的：“因为我注意到你身边总是环绕着那么多绝色迷人的极品女人。能把那样的女王们迷得死心塌地的男人，绝对有着非凡的魅力！我就是想引起你的注意……说实话，我做梦都想跟你共度一晚春宵！”

Hearing this unexpected, windfall-like confession, Fujiwara's eyes lit up with sheer, unadulterated triumph. A giant, roguish grin spread across his face as he realized a literal pie had fallen straight from the sky.

听到这突如其来的直白表白，宛如天上掉下个巨型馅饼砸中脑门，藤原的眼睛瞬间亮了起来，脸上立刻绽放出无比得意的坏笑。

Without wasting a single second, Fujiwara turned around, grabbed Rin who was lounging nearby, and cheerfully shoved the cash register keys into her hand. "Rin, watch the shop for a bit! I've got an urgent... diplomatic negotiation to handle in the back."

没有半点迟疑，藤原当机立断地转身一把抓住路过的铃，顺手把收银台的钥匙塞进她手里：“铃，店里交给你照看一会儿！我后室有个极其重要的‘外交紧急项目’要处理。”

Rin rolled her eyes, offering a knowing, amused snort as she tossed the keys in the air. "Go ahead, you lucky dog. Just make sure you don't break the sofa this time."

铃没好气地翻了个白眼，好笑地将钥匙抛了抛：“去吧去吧，你个走桃花运的色狗。动静小点，别把后室的沙发又给弄散架了。”

Grabbing the eager beauty by the waist, Fujiwara dragged her straight into the back room, slamming the door shut behind them. Within seconds, the quiet sanctuary of the stockroom echoed with the heavy, breathless sounds of absolute passion as Fujiwara gave her the thorough, relentless pounding she had been craving.

藤原一把揽住美女纤细的腰肢，迫不及待地将她推进了后室，反手“砰”地关上了房门。没过多久，逼仄而安静的后室里便传出了急促的喘息声与肉体撞击的响动，藤原毫不客气地将这个送上门的美人压在货架边，狠狠地进行了一场酣畅淋漓的深层征伐。
