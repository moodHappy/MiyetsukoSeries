Chapter 536: Afternoon Tea and Idle Chatter

发布时间：2026/10/10 21:59:37

第五百三十六章：下午茶与闲话家常

The winter afternoon sunlight filtered through the stained-glass windows of the grand salon, casting warm, golden patches across the plush Persian carpets. Outside, the heavy snow continued to drift lazily down, muffling the sprawling eight-hundred-acre estate in a peaceful, isolated silence. Inside, however, the imperial living room was as lively and chaotic as ever.

冬日的午后阳光透过大起居殿的彩绘玻璃，在厚实的波斯地毯上洒下一片温暖的金斑。窗外，厚重的积雪依旧懒洋洋地飘落着，将八百亩的庞大庄园包裹在一片宁静与世隔绝的寂静中。然而殿内，皇家客厅却一如既往地热闹而喧嚣。

Fujiwara reclined comfortably in the center of the massive leather sofa, a steaming cup of dark roast coffee on the low table before him. He wore a loose silk robe, looking every bit the relaxed, absolute tyrant taking a rare afternoon off from his grueling schedule of world domination and corporate liquidation.

藤原舒舒服服地斜倚在真皮沙发中央，面前的低矮茶几上放着一杯冒着热气的深焙咖啡。他穿着一件宽松的真丝长袍，看上去活像一个难得在繁重的征服世界与企业清算日程中偷闲的、放松惬意的绝对暴君。

"You know, looking at the weather forecast, this snowstorm is supposed to last another three days," Fujiwara casually remarked, blowing on the surface of his coffee. "At this rate, the private helipads will be completely frozen over. Miyetsuko, have the maintenance drones cleared the runway yet?"

“说起来，看这天气预报，这场暴风雪还得刮上整整三天呢，”藤原漫不经心地吹了吹咖啡表面的热气，随口道，“照这速度，私人直升机坪都要彻底冻上了。美悦子，清扫跑道的无人机派出去没有？”

Miyetsuko, seated neatly on a velvet footstool with a stack of estate ledgers in her lap, looked up and offered a serene, perfect smile. She was dressed in a high-collared knit sweater that contrasted sharply with her regal poise. "The maintenance crews completed the thermal de-icing of the north helipad two hours ago, Master. Though given the wind speeds, I took the liberty of grounding all non-essential flights. We wouldn't want any visiting foreign diplomats getting blown into the frozen koi pond."

正端端正正坐在天鹅绒矮凳上、膝头放着一叠庄园账册的美悦子抬起头，展颜露出一抹平静完美的微笑。她穿着一件高领针织衫，与她那贵气逼人的仪态相得益彰。“北面停机坪的热力除冰工作两小时前就由维护团队完成了，主人。不过考虑到风速，奴婢擅自主张将所有非紧急航班暂时停飞了。咱们可不想哪位来访的外国外交官被大风吹进冰冻的锦鲤池里。”

"A wise precaution," Fujiwara chuckled.

“考虑得很周到，”藤原低笑了一声。

Across from them, Chika was curled up in a sprawling, undignified heap on the chaise lounge, aggressively working her way through a bag of imported spicy potato chips. Her heavy, milk-rich chest bounced with every crunch she took. "Who cares about diplomats?! Boss, the real crisis is that the estate's pastry chef ran out of imported Belgian cocoa powder because of the snow blockade! If I don't get my midnight chocolate soufflé, I'm going on a dairy strike!"

在他们对面，千夏毫无仪态地盘腿瘫在贵妃榻上，正凶猛地消灭着一袋进口麻辣薯片，她那对沉甸甸、产奶丰沛的胸脯随着每一次咀嚼剧烈晃荡着。“谁管什么外交官呀？！老板，真正的危机是庄园的糕点大厨因为大雪封山，进口的比利时可可粉断货啦！今晚要是吃不到我的深夜巧克力舒芙蕾，我就要宣布奶源罢工！”

Sayuri, looking remarkably radiant and visibly softer around her waist and hips following months of the Emperor's strict "Tang Dynasty fattening decree," gracefully poured a cup of herbal tea. She gave Chika a mild, maternal side-eye. "Honestly, little cow, your priorities are perpetually fixed on your stomach. Can't you go one afternoon without demanding sugar? Besides, with the way Master has been feeding us, you should be worried about your own waistline catching up to mine."

几个月来在藤原严格的“盛唐增肥圣旨”调教下，如今看上去光彩照人、腰肢与臀围明显丰腴了一圈的小百合优雅地斟了一杯花草茶，给了千夏一个温柔中透着无奈的眼神。“说真的，小奶牛，你的心思就永远离不开吃。难道你一个下午不吃糖就会浑身难受吗？再说了，照主人这个喂法，你该担心的是你自己的腰围什么时候赶上我才对。”

"My waistline is purely functional!" Chika retorted indignantly, pointing a chip at Sayuri. "And besides, Queen Sayuri has zero room to talk—you've gained at least twenty-five pounds! Your designer dresses are bursting at the seams!"

“我这腰围可是极具功能性的！”千夏气哼哼地反驳道，手里捏着片薯片指了指小百合，“再说了，小百合皇后娘娘最没资格说这话——您老人家少说也胖了二十五斤吧！衣橱里的高定礼服快全撑裂了吧！”

"It's called 'imperial aesthetic opulence,' thank you very much," Sayuri retorted smoothly, smoothing down her plush silk skirt with a smug, velvety smirk. "Master explicitly ordered this glorious silhouette. You're just jealous that your dairy factory lacks such magnificent curves."

“这叫‘盛唐皇家美学丰满度’，谢谢，”小百合不慌不忙地轻抿了一口茶，抚平了真丝长裙上厚实的褶皱，挂着一抹丝绒般的得意坏笑，“这曼妙的身段可是陛下亲自下旨钦点的。你纯粹是嫉妒你的奶牛工厂练不出这么优美的曲线。”

Sitting quietly in the corner on a plush round cushion, blind Rin swung her legs back and forth, holding a small wooden puzzle toy. She tilted her head toward the center of the room, her milky, unseeing eyes wide with innocent curiosity.

安安静静地坐在角落圆垫上的盲人小铃晃荡着双腿，手里拿着一个木质的小拼图玩具。她歪着小脑袋望向客厅中央，空灵纯净的盲眼里满是好奇：

"Um... hello, everyone? Rin thinks you're all making too much noise!" she chimed in cheerfully. "If Chika-nee is mad about chocolate and Sayuri-nee is proud of her new fat, why doesn't Master just punish both of them in the master bedroom right now? That way, everyone is happy, and the snowstorm outside will melt from the heat!"

“唔……大家听我说好吗？小铃觉得你们吵得耳朵都快聋啦！”她开心地插嘴道，“要是千夏姐姐因为没有巧克力生气，小百合姐姐又在为自己的胖屁股骄傲，那主人为什么不现在就把她们两个一块儿抓进主卧里狠狠惩罚一顿呢？这样大家就都开心啦，说不定连外面的暴风雪都能被热气给融化掉呢！”

The entire salon burst into laughter at Rin's characteristically depraved logic. Fujiwara shook his head, a fond, wicked grin spreading across his face as he set his coffee down and stood up from the sofa.

听到小铃这番极具特色的下流逻辑，整间客厅瞬间爆发出愉快的哄笑声。藤原摇了摇头，脸上绽开一个宠溺又恶劣的坏笑，将咖啡杯往茶几上一搁，从沙发上站起身来。

"The little succubus actually makes a very compelling argument," Fujiwara declared, stretching his arms lazily. "Cabin fever is setting in, and I think it's high time the imperial household engaged in some indoor athletic activities. Ladies, consider yourselves summoned."

“小魅魔的这番提议倒确实很有说服力，”藤原懒洋洋地伸了个懒腰，“看来雪天宅在家里的确容易让人浮想联翩，是时候让后宫佳丽们开展一些室内体育锻炼了。爱妃们，准备接驾吧。”
