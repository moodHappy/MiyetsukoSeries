Chapter 627: The Cyber-Shrine Meltdown

发布时间：2026/10/31 23:51:21

第六百二十七章：赛博神坛崩坏

The grand salon of the imperial palace was bathed in the soft pink neon glow of the stellar network's trending feed. Emperor Fujiwara lounged on the obsidian throne, with Chika and Miyetsuko comfortably sprawled across his lap, while Sayuri presided over the holographic screen like a supreme tech pontiff, reviewing the latest wave of galactic internet absurdities.

皇宫的大起居殿内，星际网络热搜榜散发着柔和的粉色霓虹光芒。藤原大帝慵懒地斜倚在黑曜石王座上，千夏和美悦子舒舒服服地横坐在他腿上，小百合则像一位至高无上的科技教皇，主持着全息屏幕前的议事，审阅着银河系最新一轮的网络奇闻。

"Well, well, well. Look what the fiber-optic garbage disposal has just coughed up onto the cosmic mainframe," Fujiwara chuckled, tossing a grape into his mouth. "A viral post that has officially destroyed the internet's remaining faith in human dignity. The title reads: 'The Boy I Was Too Shy to Even Hold Hands With Got Sat on for One Second and Came Instantly.'"

“哟哟哟，瞧瞧光纤垃圾处理站今天又往宇宙主干网上吐出了什么玩意儿。”藤原轻笑着，将一颗葡萄扔进嘴里，“这是一篇彻底击穿全网人类尊严底线的爆款热帖。标题叫作：‘我连手都不敢牵的白月光男孩，被人坐了一秒就当场缴械了。’”

On the holographic projection, the full, unedited text of the viral post appeared in glowing neon pink. It was an absurdly dramatic account of romantic disappointment, sexual humiliation, and an increasingly spectacular emotional breakdown.

全息投影上，整篇未经删改的爆款网帖以粉色霓虹字体呈现出来。这是一段关于暗恋破灭、意外的性羞辱，以及情绪逐渐失控的荒诞自述。

I'm seriously losing my mind. That day, I ran into the innocent, wholesome guy I had a secret crush on at a bubble tea shop. I barely even dared to touch his fingertips. My heart was pounding so hard I thought I was going to suffocate. All I could think about was a pure, Platonic romance. I'd even started planning which planet our future children would attend kindergarten on.

我真的要疯了。那天我和我的纯情暗恋对象在奶茶店偶遇，我连他的手指都不敢多碰一下，心跳快得仿佛随时都会窒息，满脑子都是纯洁的柏拉图式恋爱，甚至连以后我们的孩子该上哪个星球的幼儿园都规划好了。

And then what happened?! Just half an hour later, some trashy girl in an absurdly short skirt, practically drenched in cheap perfume, casually walked past and plopped herself right onto his lap!

结果呢？！半小时后，一个穿着超短裙、浑身散发着廉价香水味的太妹路过，竟然大大咧咧地一屁股坐在了他的大腿上！

The guy's eyes practically bulged out of his head. His entire body jerked twice like he'd been electrocuted. Before he could even get a complete sentence out, he embarrassingly lost control of himself right there in his pants in what must have been the fastest biological reaction in human history!

那男的当场瞪大了眼睛，整个人像触电一样剧烈抽搐了两下，连一句完整的话都没来得及说，便狼狈地在裤子里完成了人生中最迅速的一次生理反应！

I stared at him, watching him lose all composure in an instant. Then I thought about my own supposedly pure youth, when I was too terrified even to hold his hand. I completely snapped! My soul practically left my body, and right there in the bubble tea shop, I transformed into an eight-headed cybernetic giant spider!

我看着他那副瞬间失去战斗力的模样，再想想自己连牵手都要战战兢兢的纯洁青春，整个人直接黑化了！我气得灵魂出窍，当场在奶茶店里蜕变成了一只长着八个脑袋的赛博大蜘蛛！

WHY?! Why does my pure love mean absolutely fucking nothing, while some random girl can simply sit on his lap and trigger such an astonishing physical reaction?! Unforgivable! This entire world has officially gone insane!

凭什么？！我的纯爱连个屁都不是，而人家随便往他腿上一坐，就能触发如此惊人的生理反应？！无法原谅！这个世界彻底癫了！

The entire hall fell silent for two seconds before Chika suddenly burst into uncontrollable laughter, nearly choking on the potato chips in her mouth. She doubled over with laughter, tears almost streaming down her face.

整个大殿安静了两秒钟，随后千夏突然爆发出一阵狂笑，差点被嘴里的薯片呛住。她笑得前仰后合，连眼泪都快流出来了。

"A cyber spider with eight heads, born from nothing but romantic heartbreak?!" Chika shrieked, wiping tears from the corners of her eyes. "And that description of the poor boy's spectacularly short-lived self-control! By the gods, this is the most unhinged account of romantic jealousy I've ever read!"

“纯爱破防居然能直接进化成八头赛博大蜘蛛？！”千夏尖叫着，擦去眼角笑出来的泪水，“还有那个可怜男孩瞬间崩溃的描写！我的天呐，这绝对是我这辈子看过最精神错乱、最丧心病狂的嫉妒文学！”

Sayuri stood gracefully before the holographic projection, tapping her velvet fan against her chin. As the pontiff of the AV Goddess Religion, she immediately began examining the post from a sociological, psychological, and religious perspective.

小百合优雅地站在全息投影前，用丝绒折扇轻轻敲着下巴。作为AV女神教的教宗，她立刻从社会学、心理学和宗教文化的角度，对这篇网帖展开了分析。

"A fascinating case study in modern romantic delusion," Sayuri remarked, her velvet eyes gleaming with amusement. "The author appears to be suffering from an acute case of Pure-Love Derangement Syndrome. She expects emotional intimacy to triumph over biological impulses, while completely overlooking the unpredictable nature of human physiology. We should seriously consider canonizing this post as an official teaching text of the AV Goddess Religion."

“这是一份非常有趣的现代爱情妄想症研究样本。”小百合意味深长地笑道，丝绒般的眼眸中闪烁着恶趣味的光芒，“发帖人显然患有严重的‘纯爱精神错乱综合征’。她居然指望精神层面的亲密关系能够战胜生理本能，却完全忽视了人体反应本身的不可预测性。本宫提议，应当将这篇网帖正式收录为AV女神教的官方教义教材。”

Miyetsuko adjusted her Death God glasses, flashing an aristocratic, razor-sharp smirk. "As the Supreme Goddess of Death, I must say that young man's nervous system deserves an award. To go from romantic innocence to complete physical embarrassment in a matter of seconds is quite a biological achievement. I'll make sure to reserve a VIP lane on the River Styx for his thoroughly exhausted dignity."

美悦子扶了扶死神眼镜，嘴角露出一抹贵族式的锐利坏笑：“身为至高死神，本宫必须说，这小男孩的神经系统简直值得颁个奖。能在短短几秒钟内，从纯情少年直接变成社死现场的主角，也算是一项了不起的生理成就了。我回头得在冥河上给他那已经彻底透支的尊严预留一条VIP快速通道。”

Rin bounced excitedly on the armrest of the throne, her succubus tail wagging furiously. "Ooh! Can we track down that eight-headed cyber spider girl and invite her to join the Succubus Legion? Anyone capable of turning romantic frustration into that level of supernatural rage would make an excellent demon general!"

小铃兴奋地在王座扶手上蹦来蹦去，魅魔尾巴疯狂摇晃：“好耶！咱们能不能顺藤摸瓜，把那个八头赛博大蜘蛛女孩给挖出来，邀请她加入魅魔军团呀？能把失恋怨气转化成这种超自然愤怒的女人，绝对是当恶魔大将的天选之才！”

Fujiwara burst into laughter, pulling Chika and Miyetsuko closer as he read the comments scrolling beneath the viral post.

藤原顿时爆发出一阵狂笑，一把将千夏和美悦子搂得更紧，同时津津有味地翻看着帖子下方不断滚动的网友神回复。

"Brilliant! Absolutely legendary!" Fujiwara roared. "Look at the top comment: 'Sister, don't be sad. Your crush didn't betray you emotionally. He simply received a direct firmware update to his reproductive hardware through unauthorized physical access.' Ha! Now that's philosophy at its finest!"

“太绝了！简直是神级文案！”藤原狂笑着说道，“你们看热评第一：‘姐妹别难过，你的白月光并没有在精神上背叛你。他只不过是通过未经授权的物理接入，给自己的生殖硬件强行升级了一套固件驱动而已。’哈哈！这觉悟简直高出天际了！”

Sayuri raised her fan, concealing her amused smile. "I particularly appreciate the phrase 'unauthorized physical access.' It transforms an ordinary romantic disaster into a matter of galactic cybersecurity. Perhaps we should establish a new department to investigate such incidents."

小百合举起折扇，遮住了嘴角的笑意：“本宫尤其欣赏‘未经授权的物理接入’这个说法。它成功地把一场普通的爱情事故上升成了银河级网络安全事件。或许我们应该专门成立一个部门，负责调查此类异常接入行为。”

"Excellent idea!" Miyetsuko replied. "I'll put the Death Bureau in charge. After all, we're already familiar with unauthorized access to the human body."

“好主意！”美悦子立刻接话，“那就交给冥界管理局负责吧。毕竟，关于人体的非法入侵业务，我们向来经验丰富。”

"Wait!" Rin raised her little hand. "If the girl has eight heads, does that mean she can operate eight livestream accounts simultaneously? That's an extraordinary commercial opportunity!"

“等等！”小铃突然举起小手，“如果那个女孩真的有八个脑袋，那是不是意味着她可以同时运营八个直播账号？这可是非常难得的商业机会呀！”

The entire hall erupted into another round of uncontrollable laughter.

整个大殿顿时再次爆发出一阵震耳欲聋的狂笑。

As the grand hall dissolved into a chorus of irreverent amusement, Fujiwara leaned back against the obsidian throne, drawing his queens closer. In a universe where romantic disappointment could supposedly transform a girl into a cybernetic monster, and a stranger's unexpected lap-sit could become a matter of galactic cybersecurity, the Fujiwara Empire's daily supply of surreal internet madness remained inexhaustible.

在大殿众人肆无忌惮的笑声中，藤原重新靠回黑曜石王座，将后宫众美搂在怀里。在这个连失恋都能让少女进化成赛博怪物、陌生人的一次意外落座都能上升为银河级网络安全事件的宇宙里，藤原帝国每天的荒诞网络狂欢，显然永远不会有枯竭的一天。
