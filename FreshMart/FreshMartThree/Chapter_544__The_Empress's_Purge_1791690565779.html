The political purge did not end with the captain's "exile" to the tropical villa. Later that evening, as Fujiwara was reviewing the guard roster, Sayuri gracefully approached him, gently massaging his shoulders while the other concubines gathered around with expressions of profound, patriotic concern.

政治清洗并没有随着女卫队长被“流放”到热带别墅而宣告结束。当天晚些时候，正当藤原在翻阅禁卫军花名册时，小百合优雅地走上前，温柔地替他捏着肩膀，而其他的佳丽们则围拢过来，脸上无一例外地写满了深切的、忧国忧民的担忧。

"My Lord," Sayuri murmured softly, her velvet voice laced with just the right amount of anxious hesitation. "It is wonderful that you dealt with the captain... but those Imperial Valkyries were entirely trained by her. They are her absolute confidantes. If even one of them harbors resentment over her punishment, what if they decide to take revenge? Your Majesty's safety is simply too precious to risk."

“夫君，”小百合轻声呢喃，软糯的嗓音里带着恰到好处的焦虑与迟疑，“您果断处置了队长，这自然是极好的……可剩下的那些皇家女武神，全都是那位绝美队长一手带出来的心腹。假如她们中有人对这处理结果心生不满，万一伺机报复……陛下的安危可是万金之躯，万万容不得半点闪失呀。”

Miyetsuko and Chika nodded vigorously in unison, painting a terrifying picture of highly trained, latex-clad assassins launching a coordinated nighttime coup. Convinced by his brilliantly paranoid harem, Fujiwara immediately disbanded the entire remaining batch of the stunning Imperial Guards, "exiling" the whole lot of them to various highly luxurious, strictly guarded "detention resorts" across the empire.

美悦子和千夏在一旁立刻默契地疯狂点头，共同描绘了一幅由身穿紧身乳胶衣的顶级女刺客在深夜发动武装政变的恐怖画面。在被他这群“深谋远虑”且被害妄想症发作的后宫一通疯狂洗脑后，藤原当机立断，将剩下这批美艳绝伦的禁卫军整个解散，并将她们统统“发配”到了帝国各地那些安保极其森严的超豪华“拘留度假村”里。

The next afternoon, with the Emperor temporarily away attending to syndicate business, the heavy doors of the grand salon were securely locked.

次日下午，趁着皇帝暂时离宫去处理辛迪加事务，大起居殿厚重的双开门被死死地反锁上了。

POP!

砰！

The cork of a highly expensive, imported vintage champagne flew across the room. The harem beauties were lounging luxuriously on the Persian rugs, clinking their crystal glasses together in an atmosphere of absolute, triumphant celebration.

一瓶极其昂贵的进口年份香槟的软木塞在房间里欢快地飞弹开来。后宫佳丽们极其慵懒奢靡地瘫坐在波斯地毯上，水晶高脚杯清脆地碰在一起，整个大殿洋溢着一种绝对胜利的狂欢气氛。

"Cheers to the Wise Empress!" Miyetsuko giggled softly, her cheeks flushed with alcohol as she took a delicate sip. The former princess smiled with a dark, deeply political slyness. "Thanks to Sister Sayuri planting that spy right by Master's side, and feeding him that completely fake intel about the captain and the minister having an affair... we pulled off a flawless coup without shedding a drop of our own blood!"

“敬我们伟大的贤后娘娘！”美悦子娇声笑着，白皙的脸颊因酒精泛起红晕，优雅地抿了一口酒。这位前朝公主露出了极其阴暗、深谙宫斗精髓的狡黠微笑。“多亏了小百合姐姐高瞻远瞩，在藤原身边安插了眼线，又巧妙地给陛下传了那队长与重臣有染的假消息……咱们兵不血刃就完成了一场完美的政变呢！”

Chika laughed so hard she spilled a few drops of champagne onto her heavy, milk-swollen cleavage. She wiped it away casually, her eyes sparkling with wicked triumph. "Right?! If we hadn't acted fast, those combat-trained Victoria's Secret models would have eventually completely replaced us! Can you imagine? We'd be out of a job, and the Boss would be spending all his time in the barracks!"

千夏笑得前仰后合，几滴香槟都洒在了她那对因胀奶而高高耸立的深邃事业线上。她毫不在意地随手抹掉，眼里闪烁着恶毒的胜利光芒。“可不是嘛！要不然，咱们这后宫早晚有一天要被那群胸大腿长、还练过格斗的维密超模给彻底取代啊！你们敢想象吗？到时候咱们统统失业，老板天天泡在军营里不出来！”

Sayuri leaned back against the plush cushions, her newly plump, Tang-dynasty curves looking absolutely magnificent as she elegantly swirled the golden liquid in her glass. She let out a soft, velvet sigh of mock pity.

小百合舒服地靠在毛绒软垫上，那刚被喂胖的盛唐丰腴曲线在此刻显得气场十足、美艳不可方物。她优雅地摇晃着杯中金色的酒液，发出了一声充满丝绒感、假惺惺的怜悯轻叹。

"It was a necessary defensive maneuver for the survival of this household," Sayuri purred, a wicked, matriarchal smile playing on her lips. "Though... I do feel a tiny bit of sympathy for that poor, devoted minister. He was dragged out and beheaded, and the poor fool probably went to his grave having absolutely no idea why."

“为了咱们这个家的生存，这只是一次必要的防御性战术机动罢了，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，唇边挂着一抹极其恶劣的主母微笑，“不过……对于那位可怜又忠诚的重臣，我倒确实有那么一丁点儿同情。就那么被人像拖死狗一样拉出去砍了脑袋，那可怜的糊涂蛋估计到死都不知道自己究竟是为什么掉的脑袋。”

From her corner, blind Rin happily shoved a piece of cake into her mouth, swinging her legs. "Rin thinks the minister should be happy! In demon culture, dying for a fake sex scandal is a very prestigious way to go!"

坐在角落里的盲人小铃开心地往嘴里塞了一大块蛋糕，晃荡着双腿：“小铃觉得那个大臣应该感到高兴才对呀！在恶魔的文化里，因为一桩虚假的性丑闻被砍头，可是一种非常光荣、极具声望的死法呢！”

The room erupted into another bout of ruthless, melodic laughter. The women drank and gossiped, reveling in their absolute control over the inner workings of the empire. After wiping a tear of mirth from her eye, Miyetsuko looked over at Sayuri with a slight hint of cautious curiosity.

大殿内再次爆发出这群蛇蝎美人清脆、无情的娇笑声。女人们开怀畅饮，随口八卦，尽情享受着她们对帝国核心内政的绝对掌控力。抹去笑出来的眼泪后，美悦子带着一丝谨慎的好奇看向小百合。

"Speaking of which, Sister Sayuri," Miyetsuko asked, setting down her glass. "The new batch of Imperial Guards we arranged to replace them... we are absolutely sure they are loyal to our cause, right?"

“话说回来，小百合姐姐，”美悦子放下酒杯，轻声问道，“咱们这次亲手安排顶上去的那批新禁卫军部队……应该都是对咱们绝对忠心的吧？”

Sayuri offered a brilliant, flawless smile, taking another leisurely sip of her champagne.

小百合展颜露出了一个完美无瑕的自信微笑，慢条斯理地又抿了一口香槟。

"Rest assured, my sisters. They have all undergone the strictest background investigations," Sayuri declared, her tone dripping with dark, domestic cunning. "I personally selected them. They are highly lethal, absolutely loyal to the harem's payroll, and most importantly... every single one of them is incredibly skilled in combat, but built like a brick wall with the facial aesthetics of a smashed potato. Master won't even look twice at them."

“妹妹们大可放心。她们全都是经过极其严格的背景调查的，”小百合信誓旦旦地宣告，语气中充满了阴暗的内宅心机，“这批人是我亲自挑的。她们不仅武艺高强、对咱们后宫发放的津贴绝对忠诚，而且最重要的是……她们个个身手敏捷，但身材全长得像一堵砖墙，脸蛋更是堪比被门夹过的土豆。保证陛下连多看她们第二眼的性欲都没有。”
