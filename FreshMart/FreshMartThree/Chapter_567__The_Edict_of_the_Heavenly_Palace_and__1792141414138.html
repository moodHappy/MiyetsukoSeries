Chapter 567: The Edict of the Heavenly Palace and the Imperial Compromise

发布时间：2026/10/16 17:03:32

第五百五十七章：天宫圣旨与帝国妥协

The grand salon of the imperial palace was usually filled with the pleasant sounds of clinking wine glasses, heavy breathing, and the occasional complaint about broken showerheads. Today, however, the atmosphere was frozen in stunned, absolute silence. A colossal, blindingly golden decree had abruptly materialized out of thin air, burning itself into the high-ceilinged dome of the throne room with terrifying divine authority.

皇室大起居殿里平时总是充满了碰杯的脆响、粗重的喘息声，以及对被砸坏的淋浴喷头的偶尔抱怨。然而今天，这里的气氛却冻结在了一片震惊而死寂的氛围中。一道巨大、耀眼夺目的金色圣旨毫无征兆地凭空降临，带着令人毛骨悚然的绝对神威，硬生生地烙印在了大殿挑高的穹顶之上。

The golden letters crackled with raw, celestial energy, spelling out a message that made even the depraved tyrants of the Fujiwara Empire break out in a cold sweat:

金色的文字闪烁着狂暴纯粹的仙界能量，上面的内容让即便堕落如藤原帝国的暴君们也倒吸了一口凉气：

"By decree of the Heavenly Palace: The Fujiwara Empire is utterly debauched, licentious, and utterly incapable of proper governance. Within three days, Heavenly Fire shall descend to completely incinerate the imperial capital and turn this nest of sin to ash!"

“天宫下旨：藤原帝国荒淫无度，倒行逆施，治理无方。不日将降下灭世天火，将皇城焚为平地，化为焦土！”

"However... an exception may be granted. If Emperor Fujiwara dispatches Miyetsuko to the Heavenly Palace to share a bed with the Jade Emperor for a single night, the Empire shall be forgiven."

“又补充道：除非藤原打发美悦子来与天帝陪睡一晚，方可网开一面，原谅尔等。”

"And do not attempt any petty tricks. In the eyes of the Gods, you pathetic mortals are nothing more than a bunch of transparent, naked little ants wandering around with your bare asses exposed."

“最后又说：不要耍花招，在神的眼里，你们这群光着屁股的蝼蚁不过是一群一丝不挂的小透明，任何伎俩都无所遁形。”

The decree faded, leaving the room in a heavy, suffocating gloom. The entire harem stared at the ceiling in blank disbelief. Even in a Type III civilization equipped with Dyson spheres, antimatter torpedoes, and artificial black holes, the sheer concept of an ancient, mystical "Heavenly Palace" dropping divine fire was a paradigm-shifting crisis.

金色文字缓缓淡去，大殿内陷入了一片沉重压抑的阴霾中。整个后宫佳丽都呆呆地盯着穹顶，满脸难以置信。即便这是一个装备了戴森球、反物质鱼雷和人造黑洞的三级文明，这种古老而神秘的“天宫”直接降下神罚的概念，依然是一场颠覆世界观的降维打击。

"Heavenly Palace? Heavenly Fire?" Chika shrieked, dropping her half-eaten bag of chips onto the Persian rug. Her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaved frantically. "Is this a galactic hacker prank?! Or did some religious cult hack into our planetary defense matrix?! How can a bunch of floating deities threaten a Type III civilization with fire?!"

“天宫？天火？！”千夏尖叫着，手里吃了一半的薯片掉在了波斯地摊上，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔剧烈起伏着，“这是哪个星际黑客的恶作剧恶搞吗？！还是说哪个宗教邪教组织黑入了我们的行星防御矩阵？！一帮飘在天上的神仙凭什么能用火威胁一个三级文明啊？！”

Sayuri, looking gloriously plump and impossibly regal in her Tang-dynasty silks, stepped forward with a tightly knit brow. The Wise Empress pulled up her holographic tactical analysis board, her face turning pale.

穿着盛唐真丝长袍、丰腴绝伦的小百合眉头紧锁地走了上前。这位贤后调出了全息战术分析面板，脸色微微发白。

"It's no prank, Chika," Sayuri murmured, her velvet voice trembling slightly. "I just ran a quantum scan on the residual energy left by the decree. The physics signatures don't belong to our dimension. They are operating on a higher-order, transcendental plane of reality. Their energy output ignores spatial distance and bypasses all our deflector shields. If they drop Heavenly Fire, our capital will genuinely evaporate into nothingness."

“这不是恶作剧，千夏，”小百合软糯地呢喃着，丝绒般的嗓音微微发颤，“我刚刚对圣旨残留的能量进行了量子扫描。其物理特征完全不属于我们这个维度。他们运行在更高维度的现实平面上。他们的能量输出无视空间距离，直接绕过了我们所有的偏导防护罩。如果他们真降下天火，咱们的皇城绝对会在物理层面上蒸发得干干净净。”

Miyetsuko, the subject of the divine extortion demand, dropped to her knees on the rug. Her exquisite face was drained of all color, her aristocratic posture completely shattered by sheer, unadulterated terror. She pressed her forehead against the floor, her plush rear hiked high in the air as she trembled like a leaf.

而作为神明勒索对象的当事人美悦子，“扑通”一声跪倒在地毯上。她那精致的小脸血色尽失，往日高傲的贵族仪态被纯粹的恐惧彻底击碎。她将额头死死贴在地砖上，丰腴的臀部高高撅起，浑身像筛糠一样剧烈颤抖着。

"T-The Jade Emperor... wants me...?" Miyetsuko whimpered, her voice cracking with despair. "A celestial deity demanding a mortal noblewoman for a night of bed-sharing... If Master refuses, we all die. But if Master hands me over... I shall obediently pack my bags and prepare to serve the divine majesty..."

“天……天帝……想要奴婢……？”美悦子带着绝望哭腔哀求道，“高高在上的天神竟然点名要一个凡间贵族女子陪睡……如果主人拒绝，大家就得一起死。可如果主人把我交出去……奴婢这就乖乖收拾行李，随时准备去侍奉神明陛下的神威……”

From her fluffy cushion, blind Rin swung her legs, her milky eyes wide with sweet, terrifying demonic insight.

坐在毛绒软垫上的盲人小铃晃荡着双腿，空灵的盲眼里闪烁着甜美而极其恐怖的恶魔洞察力。

"Um... hello, everyone? Rin thinks the Heavenly Palace is just a bunch of perverted old ghosts!" the little succubus chirped happily. "In our demon realm, when big bad guys demand sacrifices, it just means they are horny and want a new plaything! But wait... if Miyetsuko-nee goes to sleep with the Jade Emperor, does that mean Master is getting cuckolded by God?!"

“唔……大家听我说好吗？小铃觉得天宫那帮家伙根本就是一群好色老鬼呀！”小魅魔甜丝丝地娇笑道，“在我们魔界，当大坏蛋们索要活人祭品的时候，通常就意味着他们发情了、想要个新玩具！不过等等……如果美悦子姐姐去陪天帝睡觉，那是不是意味着主人被老天爷给戴了绿帽子啦？！”

The entire grand salon fell into a dead silence. Everyone slowly turned their gazes toward the Emperor.

整个大起居殿陷入了一片死一般的寂静。所有人的目光缓缓转移到了皇帝身上。

Fujiwara stood motionless in front of the massive obsidian desk, his dark eyes fixed on the spot where the golden decree had vanished. For a long, agonizing minute, the absolute ruler of the global empire—the man who had conquered kingdoms, built Dyson spheres, and enslaved dimensions—stared into the abyss of his own powerlessness. A Type III civilization could harvest stars, but it could apparently do nothing against higher-dimensional celestial blackmail.

藤原一动不动地站在巨大的黑曜石书桌前，漆黑的双眼死死盯着刚才金色圣旨消失的地方。漫长而痛苦的一分钟里，这位全球帝国的绝对统治者——这个征服过无数王国、建造过戴森球、甚至奴役过异次元维度的男人，第一次直面了自身权力的极限。三级文明虽然能收割恒星，但在更高维度的神仙勒索面前，显然也只能束手无策。

A slow, cynical, utterly unrepentant grin gradually spread across Fujiwara’s face. He let out a dark, booming laugh that rattled the crystal windows.

渐渐地，一抹玩世不恭、毫无悔意的坏笑爬上了藤原的脸庞。他爆发出一阵低沉而洪亮的狂笑，震得大殿的玻璃窗嗡嗡直响。

"A celestial audit? Divine blackmail? Threatening my capital with fire?" Fujiwara scoffed, crossing his arms. "Ha! It seems even gods are nothing more than hypocritical perverts who use floating projection screens to steal my concubines! They claim we are naked little ants in their eyes... well, if the gods want to play voyeur from the heavens, I suppose the Empire has no choice but to engage in high-level interstellar diplomacy!"

“天庭审计？神仙勒索？拿天火威胁朕的首都？”藤原冷笑着双手抱胸，“哈！看来神仙也不过是一群喜欢用全息投影屏幕来抢朕妃子的虚伪老色批罢了！他们自称我们在眼里是一群光屁股的小蚂蚁……既然神仙们喜欢在天上玩偷窥，那朕觉得，咱们帝国为了顾全大局，也只能勉为其难地开展一次跨维度的‘高层友好建交’了！”

Miyetsuko gasped, looking up in tearful shock. "Master... are you... are you really trading me to the heavens?!"

美悦子震惊地抬起头，眼泪汪汪地惊呼道：“主人……您……您真的要把奴婢送去天庭吗？！”

"Trading you? Nonsense! You are an imperial asset!" Fujiwara laughed wickedly, walking over and pulling Miyetsuko up from the floor, roughly planting a hard kiss on her tear-stained cheek. "Listen to me, my submissive little princess. You are going to the Heavenly Palace, yes... but you are going as the Empire's ultimate secret weapon! I am dispatching you as our special diplomatic envoy of seduction!"

“送人？胡说八道！你可是帝国的核心资产！”藤原恶劣地坏笑着走过去，一把将美悦子从地上拉起来，在她泪痕斑驳的脸颊上狠狠亲了一口，“听好了，朕的小顺从。你是要去天宫没错……但你是作为帝国的终极秘密武器去的！朕现在正式任命你为咱们帝国的‘跨维迪特殊诱惑外交特使’！”

Sayuri’s eyes flashed with brilliant, opportunistic intelligence as she immediately grasped the Emperor's depraved tactical genius.

小百合的眼眸中瞬间闪过一道精明而功利的光芒，她立刻心领神会地明白了皇帝那变态的战术天才。

"Brilliant, my Lord!" Sayuri purred, her velvet voice dripping with dark amusement. "If Miyetsuko sleeps with the Jade Emperor, she can secretly install a quantum surveillance router in the heavenly bedchamber, upload a virus into their cloud-based immortality server, and record every single divine secret! We won't just save the capital; we'll end up blackmailing the entire celestial pantheon!"

“太绝了，夫君！”小百合软糯地娇笑着，丝绒般的嗓音里透着一股阴暗的戏谑，“只要美悦子去陪天帝睡一觉，她就可以神不知鬼不觉地在天庭的龙床上安装一个量子监控路由器，顺便把他们的云端长生服务器给黑了，顺便录下诸神所有的隐私黑料！咱们不仅能保住皇城，还能顺手把整个天庭神界给集体敲诈勒索破产呢！”

"Exactly!" Fujiwara grinned, pulling Miyetsuko tightly against his chest while giving her heavy, plush rear a resounding slap. "Pack your sexiest silk robes, Miyetsuko. You aren't going there as a sacrifice; you are going there to conquer the Heavenly Palace from the inside out. Let's see how they like it when the 'naked little ant' ruins their divine purity!"

“正是如此！”藤原咧嘴大笑，一把将美悦子紧紧搂在怀里，顺手在她那沉甸甸的蜜桃肥臀上重重拍了一巴掌，“去把最性感的真丝睡衣打包带上，美悦子。你可不是去当牺牲品的，你是去从内部彻底征服天宫的！到时候倒要看看，当这只‘光屁股的小蚂蚁’把他们那一尘不染的神仙节操给彻底搅个稀巴烂时，这群神仙老爷还怎么装清高！”
