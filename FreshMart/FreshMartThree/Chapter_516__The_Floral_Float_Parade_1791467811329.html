Chapter 516: The Floral Float Parade

发布时间：2026/10/8 21:56:49

第五百一十六章：花车巡游

Despite the blazing wreckage of the pirate speedboats slowly sinking into the azure depths, the decadent rhythm on the yacht's bow didn't skip a single beat. Fujiwara shamelessly continued his "rugby ball stretching experiment," ruthlessly kneading and thrusting against the tearful, milk-swollen Chika until she was completely exhausted and whimpering for mercy against the teak railing.

尽管海盗快艇燃烧的残骸正在缓缓沉入湛蓝的深海，邮轮船头那糜烂的节奏却未曾停滞半分。藤原厚颜无耻地继续着他的“橄榄球拉伸实验”，对着泪眼汪汪、胀满奶水的千夏一通毫不留情地粗暴揉捏与冲刺，直到她彻底筋疲力尽，瘫软在柚木栏杆上泣音求饶。

However, upon returning to the grand inner palace of the East River district, the Emperor suddenly decided to put on a theatrical display of imperial wrath. Slamming his fist onto the mahogany desk, he issued a draconian decree to the syndicate's maritime fleet: instantly purge and eradicate every pirate haven and rogue island within a thousand-mile radius, leaving absolutely nothing alive.

然而，一回到东川特区宏伟的深闺内宫，这位帝王却突然决定上演一出雷霆震怒的戏码。他一拳砸在红木书桌上，向辛迪加的武装舰队下达了一道极其严酷的特令：立刻出兵，将方圆千里内所有的海盗据点与法外海岛彻底肃清，寸草不留。

The ladies of the harem could only roll their eyes at this tyrannical overreaction. In reality, the so-called "terror at sea" had inflicted less damage on their heavily armored floating fortress than a stray mosquito bite. It was blatantly obvious that Fujiwara was merely using the "assassination attempt" as a convenient excuse to indulge in further extravagant, wasteful depravity.

后宫的佳丽们对这种暴君般的过度反应只能狂翻白眼。事实上，这场所谓的“海上惊魂”对她们那艘重装甲海上堡垒造成的伤害，甚至还不如一只迷路蚊子叮的包来得大。明眼人都看得出来，藤原分明只是借着这场“遇刺”的由头，趁机大搞劳民伤财、荒淫无道的奢靡做派罢了。

To "celebrate their miraculous survival from misfortune," Fujiwara ordered the construction of a colossal, open-air floral float, draped in rare silk and overflowing with millions of dollars worth of imported fresh roses. The massive float, pulled by a convoy of armored SUVs, began a slow, majestic parade through the heavily guarded private avenues of the estate, showering the empty streets with fragrant petals.

为了“庆祝从不幸中奇迹生还”，藤原下令打造了一辆极其庞大的露天花车。车上披挂着罕见的珍贵真丝，堆满了价值数百万美元、全数空运而来的进口新鲜玫瑰。这辆由一队装甲越野车牵引的巨型花车，开始在庄园内重兵把守的私家林荫大道上缓缓进行着庄严的巡游，在空无一人的街道上沿途洒下芬芳的花瓣。

Lounging on the lavish velvet cushions at the center of the float, Sayuri swirled her champagne, her velvet voice dripping with elegant mockery. "Master's definition of 'trauma recovery' truly defies all economic logic. Spending the annual GDP of a small nation on roses just because a few stray bullets scratched the ship's paint. Are we supposed to wave at the empty trees like traumatized royalty?"

慵懒地倚在花车中央奢华的天鹅绒软垫上，小百合摇晃着香槟，软糯的嗓音里浸透了优雅的嘲弄。“主人对‘创伤后恢复’的定义，可真是完全颠覆了经济学逻辑呢。就因为几颗流弹擦破了点船漆，硬是把一个小国家一年的GDP全砸在了玫瑰花上。难道咱们现在要像受了惊吓的皇室成员一样，对着两边空荡荡的树干挥手致意吗？”

Miyetsuko, kneeling dutifully beside Fujiwara in a sheer floral kimono, gently fed him a peeled lychee with a softened smile. "If Master genuinely wanted to demonstrate military deterrence, hanging the pirates' remains from the bow would have been far more cost-effective. But organizing a botanical parade to showcase the harem... I suppose this is the highest form of tyrannical vanity."

美悦子穿着一袭半透的碎花和服，温顺地跪在藤原身侧，带着被彻底软化的娇笑，轻轻将一颗剥好的荔枝喂进他嘴里。“如果主人是真的想展示军事威慑力，把海盗的残骸挂在船头上显然性价比更高。但搞这么一场植物学大巡游来炫耀后宫……我想，这大概就是暴君虚荣心的最高境界了吧。”

"Vanity?! This is just public exhibitionism!" Chika pouted furiously, her over-pampered chest bouncing heavily as she tugged at the translucent silk ribbons binding her wrists to the floral archway. "He just wanted an excuse to tie me up in public surrounded by flowers! The only people watching are his own heavily armed bodyguards, and my boobs are freezing in this breeze!"

“虚荣心？！这根本就是公然的暴露癖！”千夏气急败坏地撅起小嘴，随着她挣扎的动作，那被过度娇惯的丰满胸脯一阵剧烈乱颤，拼命拉扯着将自己手腕绑在鲜花拱门上的半透明真丝缎带。“他分明就是想找个借口，在大庭广众之下把我绑在花堆里！沿途看热闹的全是他自己那些全副武装的保镖，我的奶子在风里都要冻感冒了！”

Blind Rin clapped her hands happily from a bed of white lilies, giggling with horrifying innocence. "Um... hello, everyone? Rin thinks this is a perfect succubus festival! In the demon realm, wasting unimaginable wealth just to be a little bit naughty in front of the servants is the truest expression of absolute power!"

盲人小铃在一簇白百合花丛中开心地拍着小手，发出令人毛骨悚然的纯洁娇笑。“唔……大家听我说好吗？小铃觉得这简直是个完美的魅魔狂欢节！在恶魔界，为了在下人面前干一点点坏事就挥霍掉无法想象的财富，这才是绝对权力最真实的体现呢！”

Fujiwara roared with shameless laughter, his large hands reaching out to unapologetically squeeze the floral-bound Chika's shivering assets. "Exactly! The Emperor’s wealth exists to be squandered on absolute nonsense! Let the commoners worry about efficiency; my empire celebrates surviving a mosquito bite with rivers of champagne and a parade of silk! Now smile, my beauties, the floral exhibition of extreme depravity has only just begun!"

藤原爆发出厚颜无耻的狂笑，一双大手肆无忌惮地伸过去，狠狠揉捏住被鲜花束缚着的千夏那微微发颤的本钱。“一点都没错！帝王的财富，生来就是为了在这种毫无意义的荒唐事上挥霍的！让底层的凡人去操心什么性价比吧；老子的帝国就算是为了庆祝被蚊子咬了一口，也要用香槟河与真丝巡游来大肆铺张！现在都给老子笑起来，美人们，这场极度荒淫的花车大秀，才刚刚开始呢！”
