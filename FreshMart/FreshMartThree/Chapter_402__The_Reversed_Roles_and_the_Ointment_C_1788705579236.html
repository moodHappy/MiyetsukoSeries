第四百零二章：性格大挪移与上药危机

The back room of Fresh Mart was submerged in a deeply unsettling, alternate dynamic. Fujiwara was once again sprawled face-down on a storage crate, clutching a tube of medicated hemorrhoid ointment while whimpering in absolute dread.

“新鲜玛特”的后仓里沉浸在一种极其诡异的错位氛围中。藤原再次面朝下趴在储物箱上，手里死死攥着一管治痔疮的药膏，发出绝望的呜咽。

"Please... anyone," Fujiwara pleaded into the dusty pillow. "Just tell me who's on rotation for the rectal tube insertion today. My sanity can't take much more of this."

“求求你们……随便谁都行，”藤原对着满是灰尘的枕头哀求道，“快告诉我今天轮到谁来执行直肠管插入了。我的理智真的快撑不住了。”

Miyetsuko stepped forward, leaning against the doorway with a heavy iron baseball bat resting casually on her shoulder. Her expression was fiercely aggressive, her eyes narrowed into a cold, menacing glare as she cracked her knuckles with a loud pop.

美悦子走了上前，懒洋洋地靠在门框上，肩膀上随意扛着一根沉重的铁棒球棍。她的神情变得极其凶狠暴躁，双眼危险地眯起，透着一股冰冷充满威胁的杀气，同时把指节捏得咔咔作响。

"Quit whining, you pathetic excuse for a manager," Miyetsuko barked, slamming her bat aggressively against the concrete floor. "If you don't shut up and take your daily medication right now, I'm going to break your kneecaps for corporate insubordination! Move an inch out of line, and you'll face immediate physical elimination!"

“少废话，你这个窝囊废经理！”美悦子厉声喝道，同时用球棍极其嚣张地猛砸着水泥地面，“你要是再敢废话一句、不马上把每天的药给老娘塞进去，我就以违反公司纪律为由打断你的膝盖骨！敢多动一下，信不信老娘当场物理消灭你！”

Fujiwara flinched violently at the sheer hostility. He quickly turned his gaze toward the corner, where Rin was quietly sitting on a stool.

藤原被这股扑面而来的暴戾之气吓得猛地一哆嗦。他赶忙把视线投向角落里正静静坐在凳子上的铃。

Rin had her eyes wide open, staring blankly ahead with a completely vacant, unseeing gaze. She was a gentle, blind masseuse, utterly defenseless and equipped with no weapons whatsoever. With a soft, hesitant smile, she gently waved her hands in the air.

此时的铃双眼睁得滚圆，空洞无神地直视着前方，彻底成了一个睁眼盲。她变成了一位温柔软糯、毫无防备且没有任何装备的盲人按摩女。只见她带着温柔而迟疑的微笑，轻轻在空中挥了挥手。

"Um... hello, Fujiwara-san?" Rin spoke in a soft, timid whisper, her voice trembling with sweetness. "Are you ready for your treatment? I brought a clean towel, and Ipromise I'll be very, very gentle... Please don't be scared of me."

“唔……你好呀，藤原桑？”铃用轻柔胆怯的耳语声说道，声音里透着让人心化的温柔，“你准备好接受治疗了吗？我带了干净的毛巾，我保证会非常非常温柔的……请不要怕我哦。”

Sayuri stood right beside her, equally docile and sweet-natured, gently patting Rin's shoulder to reassure her.

小百合乖巧温顺地站在她身旁，同样一副温柔体贴的模版，轻轻拍着铃的肩膀安抚着她。

"Don't worry, sweet Rin, you're doing wonderful," Sayuri murmured affectionately, turning her gentle, submissive gaze down toward Fujiwara. "Darling, please cooperate with our sweet massage therapist. I've already brewed a nice, warm cup of calming tea for you to sip while Rin inserts the applicator tube. We only want what's best for your poor bottom."

“别担心，小铃，你做得棒极了，”小百合温柔地呢喃着，随后将她那顺从关切的目光投向趴着的藤原，“亲爱的，请好好配合我们温柔的按摩师哦。我已经为你泡好了一杯暖洋洋的舒缓茶，等会儿让铃帮你插管子的时候你可以慢慢喝。我们可都是为了你那可怜的屁股着想呢。”

Chika stood off to the side, maintaining her usual chaotic energy as she watched the bizarre role reversal unfold.

千夏则站在一旁，一边看着这场诡异的性格大反转，一边保持着她那如常的电波少女状态。

"Well, the math checks out!" Chika chimed in cheerfully, holding up a clipboard. "Aggressive manager Miyetsuko is on security detail, gentle blind Rin is doing the medical insertion, and submissive Sayuri is handling emotional support. It's the ultimate balanced team!"

“好啦，数据完全对得上！”千夏欢快地举起一块剪贴板插嘴道，“暴躁经理美悦子负责安保，温柔盲人铃负责医疗插管，顺从小百合负责情绪抚慰。这简直是究极平衡的完美团队阵容嘛！”

Fujiwara buried his face back into the pillow, weeping silently. He was entirely surrounded, trapped between a terrifyingly violent female boss and a painfully polite, blind girl holding a medicinal tube.

藤原把脸重新埋回枕头里，无声地啜泣起来。他被彻底包围了，夹在一个暴力倾向爆表的雌老虎老板和一个拿着药管礼貌过头的盲人女孩之间，进退维谷。
