Chapter 656: The Emperor’s Impossible Siege

发布时间：2026/11/25 22:44:42

第六百五十六章：大帝的丰腴攻坚战

The grand audience chamber was unusually quiet, save for the soft rustle of velvet fans and the faint clinking of porcelain teacups.

大起居殿内罕见地一片安静，唯余丝绒折扇轻摇的微风与青瓷茶盏偶尔相碰的清脆声响。

Emperor Fujiwara—the fearless conqueror of ninety-six star systems, the unapologetic tyrant who laughed in the face of galactic rebellions and orbital blockades—was currently staring at a holographic tactical projection with a bead of cold sweat rolling down his temple.

向来天不怕地不怕、横扫九十六个星区、面对星际叛乱与轨道封锁都能仰天狂笑的藤原大帝，此刻正死死盯着面前的全息投影，额角竟罕见地渗出了一滴冷汗。

On the screen was the anatomical dossier of a newly discovered frontier titaness—a noble lady from a high-gravity world whose sheer, monumental proportions defied standard imperial metrics.

光幕上显示的，是一位新近发掘的边陲高重力星球女贵族的体态档案——那惊世骇俗、宏伟壮阔的丰腴体格，简直彻底超出了帝国现有的常规度量衡标准。

Fujiwara swallowed hard, gripping the obsidian armrests of his throne as his bravado momentarily evaporated.

藤原艰难地咽了口唾沫，双手死死攥紧王座的黑曜石扶手，平日里那副不可一世的狂妄气焰瞬间消散得无影无踪。

"Her backside..." Fujiwara muttered, his voice dropping an octave into genuine, existential trepidation, "is far too thick and overwhelmingly lush. Honestly, I am seriously worried I won't even be able to get past the perimeter."

“她那屁股……”藤原喃喃自语，嗓音低沉了一个八度，字里行间透着真切的、关乎尊严的深切畏惧，“未免也太厚实、太肥美了些。说句实话，朕甚至有些担心，自个儿压根儿突破不了外围防线、根本进不去。”

Dead silence fell over the inner council, followed immediately by an eruption of merciless, unbridled mockery.

内廷众人先是一阵死寂，紧接着便爆发出一阵毫不留情、肆无忌惮的群嘲。

Chika nearly choked on her tea, slapping her knee as she roared with laughter.

千夏差点被嘴里的茶水呛到，一巴掌拍在膝盖上放声大笑。

"Hold everything! Stop the presses! Did the invincible, omnipotent Emperor Fujiwara just admit to physical inadequacy?!" Chika howled, wiping tears of mirth from her eyes. "You conquer entire star clusters without blinking, yet one extra-plump rear end has you trembling in your royal boots like a rookie squire!"

“快停下！全宇宙的史官快拿小本本记下来！战无不胜、全知全能的藤原大帝，刚才居然亲口承认自己硬件长度和突破力可能不够用？！”千夏笑得眼泪都飙出来了，“你吞并整片星团连眼睛都不眨一下，结果碰上一个分量十足的大屁股，居然吓得在龙椅上直打哆嗦，活像个刚上战场的新兵蛋子！”

Sayuri gracefully covered her mouth with her velvet fan, though her shoulders shook with quiet, aristocratic amusement.

小百合优雅地用丝绒折扇掩住朱唇，双肩却因憋笑而微微颤动，眼底尽是促狭的戏谑。

"A fascinating reversal of imperial doctrine, my Lord," Sayuri remarked smoothly, her voice dripping with dry irony. "For years, you have preached that no fortress in the universe can withstand sovereign artillery. Yet today, faced with sheer geological mass and superior cushioning, the Supreme Commander suddenly fears getting bogged down in the outer trenches."

“这可真是帝国兵法史上的一大奇观呢，夫君，”小百合软糯地打趣道，语调中满是冷幽默，“这些年来，您老人家整日鼓吹全宇宙没有任何一座堡垒能挡得住天子炮火的轰击。可到了今日，面对如此厚实的天然防御纵深，最高统帅反倒怕自己陷在外围战壕里进退两难了。”

Miyetsuko adjusted her 183828号 Death God glasses, pulling up a geometric cross-section diagram on her terminal with clinical indifference.

美悦子推了推鼻梁上的183828号死神眼镜，神色冷漠地在终端上调出一份几何横截面测算图。

"From a purely structural engineering standpoint, His Majesty's fear is entirely rational," Miyetsuko stated dryly. "The depth of the adipose tissue layers exceeds standard imperial reach by roughly forty percent. Attempting a direct frontal assault without specialized siege equipment would indeed result in zero tactical penetration and severe ego damage."

“纯从结构工程学的角度来看，陛下的恐惧完全合情合理，”美悦子冷冰冰地分析道，“目标的脂肪层防御纵深超出了帝国常规兵器射程约四成。若不配备特种攻城器械便贸然发起正面强攻，确实会导致零战术突破，并引发严重的自尊心受挫。”

Rin floated upside down beside the throne, her succubus tail poking Fujiwara’s cheek as she giggled gleefully.

小铃倒挂在王座旁，尖尖的魅魔尾巴顽皮地戳了戳藤原的脸颊，咯咯直笑。

"Aww, look at the big bad tyrant getting intimidated by a cushion!" Rin teased. "Maybe we should issue you a royal snorkel and a grappling hook before you go in, boss! Wouldn't want the Emperor getting lost in the hills!"

“哎呀呀，快瞧瞧咱们不可一世的大暴君，居然被一只超大号肉垫子给吓破胆啦！”小铃起哄打趣，“要不咱们提前给老板配一根皇家呼吸管外加一把登山抓钩再进场？免得皇帝陛下迷失在崇山峻岭里出不来咯！”

Fujiwara’s face darkened with wounded pride, though he still eyed the massive holographic silhouette with lingering caution.

藤原一张老脸被众人挤兑得一阵青一阵白，可目光瞥向光幕上那惊人的丰腴剪影时，眼底依旧残留着几分忌惮与迟疑。

"Shut your mouths, the lot of you!" Fujiwara barked, crossing his arms defensively. "It is not fear—it is prudent reconnaissance! A wise general never marches his troops into a canyon without measuring the depth first! Dismissed—and tell the Ministry of Works to send over some structural blueprints before tonight!"

“都给朕闭嘴！”藤原恼羞成怒地抱起双臂强行挽尊，“这不叫害怕——这叫审慎的战前侦察！凡用兵之道，未曾测算峡谷纵深便盲目进军者，乃匹夫之勇！退下吧——顺道传旨工部，今夜之前把地形测绘图纸给朕呈上来！”
