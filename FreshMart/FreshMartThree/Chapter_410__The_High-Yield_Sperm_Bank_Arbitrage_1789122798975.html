“新鲜玛特”的后仓里回荡着令人捧腹大笑、前仰后合的狂笑声。作为坐拥世界首富宝座的无冕之王，藤原正瘫坐在纯金高管椅上，笑得直不起腰来，一边大口喘着气，一边盯着平板上的一则爆笑网络笑话。

"Oh, stop, my ribs!" Fujiwara howled, wiping a tear of mirth from his eye as he recounted the punchline to the room. "Listen to this classic: A guy and a girl meet in a hospital elevator. The girl says, 'I'm here to donate blood, they pay 100 bucks.' The guy says, 'I'm here to donate sperm, they pay a thousand.' The girl gasps, 'What?! A thousand?!' Then a month later, they meet in the same elevator again. The guy asks, 'Here to donate blood again today?' And the girl's mouth is all puffed up, and she shakes her head, going 'Mmm-mmm!'"

“哎哟，笑死我了，我的肋骨都要断了！”藤原怪叫着擦掉眼角笑出的泪水，把这个笑话的包袱讲给全屋人听，“听听这个经典段子：一男一女在医院电梯里聊天。女生说：‘我是来捐血的，捐一次有100元。’男生说：‘我是来捐精的，一次有1000元。’女生惊呼：‘蛤？！1000？’一个月后，两人又在同一台电梯遇到。男生问：‘今天又来捐血？’女生嘴巴鼓鼓的，摇摇头：‘唔唔。’哈哈哈哈！”

The entire room erupted into chaos, but the punchline hit dangerously close to home when Chika suddenly pointed a data-tracked finger right at Sayuri.

全场瞬间爆发出混乱的欢笑声，然而当千夏忽然把一根带着数据追踪的手指直接指向小百合时，这个笑话的寓意瞬间变得极其微妙而贴切。

"Wait a second!" Chika giggled hysterically, spinning around in her ergonomic chair. "That joke isn't just a meme—that is literally Sayuri’s exact business model from last month before we struck oil with the haptic internet!"

“等一下！”千夏在人体工学椅上转了个圈，歇斯底里地咯咯娇笑，“这个段子可不只是个梗——这简直就是上个月小百合在咱们靠触觉互联网发财前的真实业务写照啊！”

Sayuri didn't even try to deny it. She gracefully flipped her hair over her shoulder, letting out a musical, unbothered giggle as she leaned against the wall.

小百合甚至懒得否认。她优雅地把长发往肩上一撩，靠在墙上发出一声轻松惬意、满不在乎的娇笑。

"Oh, please, back when we were broke, a thousand bucks was a fortune," Sayuri chuckled, smoothing down her designer dress. "Though looking back at my experimental 'high-yield procurement' methods, I honestly can't believe I wasted my time smuggling Fujiwara's genetic samples to the clinic just for a measly grand."

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, a cold, analytical smirk crossing her face as she reviewed their current trillion-dollar financial portfolio.

美悦子推了推眼镜，看着他们目前那价值数万亿美元的庞大金融投资组合，脸上浮现出一抹冷酷而精算的讥笑。

"Indeed, the profit margins on that endeavor were statistically abysmal," Miyetsuko deadpanned, crossing her arms. "Considering our current global enterprise valuation and the astronomical cash flow generated by our tactile gigolo network, spending our morning discussing a measly one-thousand-dollar sperm bank payout is a gross misallocation of corporate brainpower."

“确实，那项业务的利润率从统计学角度来看简直低得发指，”美悦子面无表情地交叉起双臂评价道，“考虑到我们目前庞大的全球企业估值以及触觉牛郎网络带来的天文级现金流，把大好早晨浪费在讨论区区一千块的精子库报酬上，简直是对公司脑力资源的严重错配。”

Fujiwara leaned back in his gold throne, waving a hand dismissively with a smug, wealthy grin.

藤原靠在他的纯金宝座上，带着暴发户式的得意微笑满不在乎地挥了挥手。

"Exactly! We are fabulously, obscenely wealthy now, reigning supreme over the entire planetary economy," Fujiwara declared grandly. "Why on earth would anyone in our inner circle stoop to earning a pathetic thousand bucks the hard way? If I want to dispense my bodily fluids, I'll charge the entire global population for a live haptic broadcast—not hand it over to some cheap local clinic!"

“就是嘛！咱们现在可是富可敌国、穷极奢华，稳坐整个星球经济的头把交椅呢，”藤原大言不惭地宣布，“咱们核心圈子里的人，怎么可能还会自降身价去辛苦赚那可怜的一千块？朕要是想挥洒体液，直接向全人类收取现场触觉直播费不好吗——才不给什么破地方诊所打折呢！”

Blind Rin sat quietly on her stool in the corner, her wide, unseeing eyes blinking innocently as she tilted her head with a soft, timid smile.

盲人铃安安静静地坐在角落的凳子上，她那双睁得滚圆却空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，微微歪着头露出了一个温柔而胆怯的微笑。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered softly, her voice like a gentle breeze. "Are we... are we talking about bank donations again? If anyone needs a soft, cozy place to rest after a busy day of making money, I can knit them a nice blanket... Please don't boast too much, everyone. It's nice to be rich, but stay humble."

Chika giggled wildly from behind her tablet, rapidly typing out a brand-new satirical stock index.
