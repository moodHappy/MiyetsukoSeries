Chapter 420: The Outer Residence Tragedy

发布时间：2026/9/19 00:19:20

第四百二十章：外宅的血腥悲剧

Amidst the sprawling, chaotic collection of over two hundred outer residences, absolute uniformity was impossible. While most of Fujiwara's kept women enthusiastically indulged in the gigolo lifestyle to pass the long, lonely hours, a strange few managed to remain surprisingly chaste, stubbornly clinging to a sense of old-fashioned loyalty.

在这遍布都市的二百多处豪华外宅中，要想做到绝对的整齐划一显然是不可能的。虽然绝大多数被藤原包养的金丝雀都乐此不疲地通过包养小白脸来打发漫长孤寂的时光，但也有那么几个奇葩竟然能做到守身如玉，固执地坚守着某种老派的忠诚。

Conversely, another peculiar subset of mistresses chose a far more dramatic route: they secretly reconnected with the very same young boyfriends whom Fujiwara had forcefully torn them away from years ago, sneaking their old flames right back into the luxury villas to resume their previous romances.

与此相对，另一部分情妇则选择了更为戏剧化的路线：她们偷偷重新联系上了当年被藤原强行拆散的前男友，把当年的穷小子又悄悄接回豪华别墅里，继续重温旧梦。

However, out of all the bizarre subplots thriving across the metropolis, the most horrifying and grotesque tragedy finally struck one fateful midnight. One of the outer-residence mistresses, deeply infatuated with her lazy, ungrateful gigolo, pushed the domestic arrangement into an unhinged nightmare. Driven by a sudden, violent dispute over money and infidelity, the young gigolo snapped, murdered her in cold blood, and committed an unspeakable act of depravity: he severed her head in the dead of night and used the freshly detached skull as a masturbatory fleshlight.

然而，在这座都市无数光怪陆离的支线中，最骇人听闻、最毛骨悚然的血腥悲剧终于在一个命运的深夜爆发了。其中一位对外宅小白脸溺爱至极的情妇，将这种畸形的同居关系推向了彻底失控的噩梦。在一场因金钱与背叛引发的突发激烈争吵中，那个年轻的小白脸彻底理智崩塌，将其残忍杀害，并犯下了一桩令人发指的变态罪行：他在深夜砍下了她的头颅，然后竟然把那颗新鲜斩下的头颅当成了飞机杯疯狂自慰。

When the news finally reached the Fresh Mart headquarters, the chaotic dinner table fell into a brief, stunned silence before Miyetsuko slammed her iron baseball bat against the floor with a terrifying, thunderous crash.

当这桩骇人听闻的消息传回“新鲜玛特”总部时，原本喧闹混乱的饭桌瞬间陷入了一片短暂而震惊的死寂，随后美悦子用铁棒球棍重重砸向地面，伴随着一声惊天动地的巨响，暴怒开腔。

"Well, what an absolute circus!" Miyetsuko snarled, her eyes blazing with furious mob-boss authority. "I always said keeping two hundred idle, unsupervised mistresses across the city was a logistical ticking time bomb! If these ungrateful little parasites are going to turn our outer residences into a bloody slasher film, we need to tighten security immediately!"

“呵，这下可真是演成了一出荒诞的大马戏！”美悦子怒极反笑，双眼里燃烧着黑帮大佬般暴戾的怒火，“老娘早就说过，在全城养着两百个游手好闲、无人监管的情妇纯粹就是在给自己埋一颗后勤定时炸弹！这群不知感恩的小寄生虫要是敢把我们的外宅变成血腥恐怖片，咱们必须立刻全面加强安保！”

Sayuri sighed softly, delicately resting her chin in her hand as she offered a sympathetic, philosophical smile.

小百合轻轻叹了口气，优雅地单手托着下腮，露出一抹同情中带着哲理的微笑。

"Oh, the poor girl," Sayuri murmured. "To think she died just because she couldn't let go of her worthless ex. Truly, a cautionary tale for all of us about the dangers of poor taste in men. Though using her head as a novelty toy... honestly, even our most depraved corporate R&D experiments never stooped to that level of disgusting recycling."

“哎呀，这可怜的姑娘，”小百合轻声呢喃道，“没想到她居然会因为对一个废柴前男友念念不忘而丢了性命。这简直给我们所有人上了一课，深刻揭示了审美眼光太差的致命危险。不过居然把人家的脑袋拿去当玩具……说实话，哪怕是我们公司最堕落的R&D研发实验，也从来没沦落到这种恶心的回收利用地步呢。”

Fujiwara leaned back in his gold executive chair, casually sipping his vintage wine with an expression of supreme, unbothered indifference.

藤原靠在纯金高管椅上，脸上带着一副高高在上、毫不在乎的冷漠神情，慢条斯理地品着杯中的珍藏红酒。

"Relax, ladies, it's just a localized operational hazard," Fujiwara chuckled dismissively. "Out of two hundred villas, losing one to a psychotic gigolo is an acceptable statistical loss. Besides, if the guy likes using severed heads as sex toys, maybe we should hire him as the chief marketing officer for our new 'Neural Nightmare' product line!"

“行啦女士们，这不过是一起局部的运营风险罢了，”藤原不以为意地轻笑道，“两百栋别墅里折损一栋给精神病小白脸，这在统计学上完全属于可接受的损耗。再说了，既然那家伙这么喜欢拿人头当飞机杯，说不定咱们真该把他挖来当新产品线‘神经噩梦’的首席营销官呢！”

Blind Rin sat quietly at the edge of the table, her wide, unseeing eyes blinking innocently as she folded a soft, clean handkerchief with trembling fingers.

盲人铃安安静静地坐在餐桌边缘，她那双睁得滚圆却空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，用微微发抖的指头叠着一条柔软干净的手帕。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered softly, her voice like a gentle breeze. "Are we... are we talking about a scary bedtime story? If people are getting hurt, maybe we should lock the doors a little tighter... Please don't laugh about such gruesome things, everyone. It makes me feel terribly cold inside."

“唔……你好呀？”铃轻声呢喃着，声音如同柔和的微风，“我们是在……在讲什么可怕的睡前鬼故事吗？如果大家都在受伤的话，咱们是不是应该把大门锁得更严实一点呀……请大家不要拿这么血腥的事情开玩笑道。听得人家心里直打寒颤呢。”

Chika giggled wildly from behind her tablet, tapping away at a brand-new, highly morbid market statistic.

千夏则躲在平板后面疯狂地咯咯娇笑，手脚麻利地敲打着一份全新的、极度病态的市场统计数据。

"Brilliant!" Chika cheered. "I'm updating our security risk index right now: 'Outer-Residence Homicide & Skull Recycling Margins'! Let's see how much insurance payout we can collect from her tragic demise before launching our next wave of urban acquisitions!"

“太妙了！”千夏欢呼道，“我这就把安全风险指数更新一下：‘外宅谋杀与头骨回收利润率’！快算算，在她这起悲惨死讯中咱们究竟能薅到多少保险理赔金，然后顺便开启下一轮的城市房产收购潮吧！”
