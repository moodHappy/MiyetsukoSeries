The private screening room was completely drowning in hysterical giggles and breathless teasing. When the feed finally flickered into standby mode, the women turned their collective gaze toward Fujiwara, their expressions caught in an uproarious blend of absolute disbelief and uncontrollable amusement.

私人放映厅里彻底被一阵阵花枝乱颤的娇笑声与放肆的打趣给淹没了。当大屏幕终于闪烁着切回待机状态时，佳丽们齐刷刷地转过头，把目光投向正洋洋得意的藤原，脸上的表情精彩至极，满是哭笑不得的震撼与乐不可支的调侃。

"Boss, you are an absolute demon down to your marrow!" Chika shrieked, clutching her newly firmed, milk-heavy chest as she bounced against the couch cushions with laughter. "Here I was thinking the Emperor had suddenly grown a shred of human conscience! But no—you literally dispatched two courtesans on a 'poverty alleviation mission' just so you could plant covert 4K wiretaps and turn a poor bachelor's once-in-a-lifetime miracle into a family reality show! You couldn't even do a good deed without committing an international privacy violation! That is peak, world-class wickedness!"

“老板，你骨子里简直坏得流脓啊！”千夏捂着刚刚做完紧致提拉、饱满发胀的软嫩雪峰，整个人在沙发靠垫上笑得直打滚，“亏老娘刚才还真以为咱们帝王良心发现、长出了一星半点的凡人善心呢！结果可好——你派两个极品尤物去‘精准扶贫’，纯粹是为了在人家衣领上别微孔针孔摄像头，把那可怜光棍汉几辈子修来的祖坟冒青烟，当成全家围观的现场真人秀直播来看！你这家伙连搞个慈善慰问，都忘不了顺手干一票国际级的侵犯隐私啊！这恶趣味简直登峰造极、天下无敌了！”

Sayuri wiped a joyful tear from the corner of her eye, resting her chin on Fujiwara's shoulder as her long, elegant fingers playfully tugged at his earlobe.

小百合抬手轻轻抹去眼角笑出来的晶莹泪花，美艳绝伦的下巴亲昵地搁在藤原肩头，纤细白皙的指尖坏心眼地揪了揪他的耳垂。

"Truly, our Emperor’s generosity always comes with a dark tax," Sayuri purred, her velvet voice brimming with affectionate mockery. "Poor uncle thought the heavens were answering his prayers with warm abalone soup and giant bosoms, completely unaware that his tear-streaked, hyperventilating face was being broadcast in eight-thousand-pixel definition to the entire royal court! If that man ever finds out his wildest night of bliss was basically our bedtime comedy special, his heart really will burst on the spot. You gave him paradise, darling, but you certainly extracted your entertainment fee first!"

“真真是一点都不假，咱们主人的慈悲向来都是要收高昂利息的呢，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，嗓音里浸透了宠溺与戏谑，“那可怜的大叔还以为是老天开眼、菩萨显灵，给他送来了热腾腾的鲍汁羹与泼天的绝色艳福，浑然不知自己那副老泪纵横、捂着胸口快要背过气的丑态，正以8K超高清的画质全程投屏给整个后宫当睡前小品看呢！那大叔要是哪天知道了自个儿这辈子最销魂的奇迹之夜，其实只是咱们家茶余饭后的恶趣味消遣，非得当场羞愤得心梗暴毙不可。亲爱的，您这善事做的呀，门票可是一分都没少收呢！”

Down on the plush Persian carpet, Miyetsuko leaned against Fujiwara’s knee, hiding her blushing face in the soft fabric of her oversized sweater, her shoulders shaking with silent, helpless laughter.

在厚实华贵的波斯地毯上，美悦子顺势把软绵绵的身子倚在藤原膝头，将那张羞得通红的俏脸埋进宽大毛衣的领口里，双肩在无声的娇笑中剧烈颤抖。

"A covert audio-video surveillance package disguised as a humanitarian rescue mission..." Miyetsuko murmured in a sweet, muffled whisper, completely devoid of her former tactical seriousness. "I used to plant military-grade bugs in embassy compounds, but watching Master casually deploy high-end courtesans as Trojan horses just to satisfy a perverted, voyeuristic curiosity... It’s completely absurd. The poor guy didn't stand a chance. He was tactically conquered, pampered to tears, and publicly spectated, all in ninety minutes."

“披着人道主义救援外衣的特种微孔视听监控作战……”美悦子细声细语地轻喃着，嗓音里早就没了半点往日的铁血杀伐，只剩下被彻底软化后的娇憨，“老娘以前潜入使馆区安插军用窃听器都没这么缺德。看着主人如此顺手地拿高阶尤物当特洛伊木马，只为了满足自己那点偷窥狂魔般的恶趣味……简直让人笑得肚子疼。那光棍汉太惨了，九十分钟里被战术包抄、被伺候得痛哭流涕，最后还被咱们在百寸大幕前全方位无死角围观，输得连底裤都不剩。”

From her cushion, blind Rin clapped her delicate hands with terrifying, pure-hearted succubus innocence, tilting her face up toward the ceiling with a radiant grin.

角落软垫上的盲人小铃开心地拍着一双白嫩的小手，那张纯白无瑕的精致脸蛋上扬起宛如天使般无辜、却又透着丝丝暗黑魅魔本色的灿烂微笑。

"Um... hello, everyone? Rin thinks Master is the smartest philanthropist in the universe!" Rin giggled sweetly, tilting her head. "In the demonic realm, when you grant a mortal a miraculous wish, you always have to collect a tiny viewing ticket from their soul! Master gave him two real-life angels, and all Master took back was a funny bedtime video for us to laugh at together! It’s the fairest trade in the whole world! Can we send the Palace of Ample Charms girls to another sad uncle tomorrow? Rin really loved hearing him sniffle and gasp through the speakers!"

“唔……大家听我说好吗？小铃觉得主人明明是全宇宙最聪明的大慈善家呢！”小铃甜甜地娇笑起来，偏着小脑袋，“在恶魔的世界里，只要帮凡人实现了一个奇迹般的愿望，本来就必须从他们的灵魂里收回一张小小的观影门票嘛！主人白送了他两位活色生香的大仙女，自己只不过是顺手拷走了一段搞笑的睡前录像给全家人乐呵乐呵而已！这分明是全世界最公平的买卖啦！明天咱们能不能再派‘大胸宝殿’的姐姐去慰问别的可怜大叔呀？小铃刚才听着音响里他抽鼻子和大喘气的声音，真的快要笑死啦！”

"Rin! Don't you dare encourage his dark hobbies!" Chika laughed out loud, leaning over to gently pinch the blind girl’s cheek, before looking back up at Fujiwara with spoiled, glowing eyes.

“小铃！你可千万别由着性子撺掇他那些阴间爱好了！”千夏笑得前仰后合，俯身宠溺地捏了捏盲女滑嫩的小脸蛋，转头又用那双水汪汪、被千娇百宠惯坏了的眸子嗔怪地瞪向藤原。

Fujiwara let out a booming, unrepentant roar of laughter, throwing his head back against the leather headrest. He pulled Chika’s pliant body hard against his ribs and buried his hand into Sayuri’s silky hair, basking in the sheer, shameless joy of his household.

藤原仰起脖子爆发出一阵肆无忌惮、毫无悔意的洪亮大笑，彻底陷在真皮靠背里。他一手霸道地将千夏软糯无骨的娇躯死死搂在肋下，另一只大手深深插进小百合柔顺如瀑的秀发中，肆意享受着自家后宫这片毫无底线的荒诞欢愉。

"Guilty as charged!" Fujiwara declared grandly, his wolfish smirk widening across his face. "The Emperor does not engage in bland, boring charity! If I spend millions bringing a man back from the edge of despair, I damn well reserve the right to watch the show with my wives! That commoner had the greatest night of his miserable life, and we got our evening entertainment. A flawless domestic victory!"

“老子认罪！老子全盘认领！”藤原不可一世地大声宣告，嘴角那抹狼性的坏笑狂放到了极点，“堂堂帝王从不搞那种寡淡无味的白开水慈善！老子砸下大笔银子把一个绝望边缘的糙汉硬生生从地狱拉进极乐天堂，难道还没资格拉着自家老婆们看场真人电影乐呵乐呵了？那光棍汉享受了他这辈子最巅峰的高光时刻，咱们全家也收割了满屋子的欢声笑语。这笔买卖，双赢，赢麻了！”
