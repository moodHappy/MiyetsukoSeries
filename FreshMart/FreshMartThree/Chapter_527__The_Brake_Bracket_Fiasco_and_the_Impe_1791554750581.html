Chapter 527: The Brake Bracket Fiasco and the Imperial Upgrade

发布时间：2026/10/9 22:05:49

第五百二十七章：刹车断裂风波与皇家免费升级

Inside the grand imperial study, a sudden, violent crash shattered the lazy afternoon peace. Fujiwara hurled his custom titanium tablet across the room, smashing it into the mahogany wall. His face was twisted in absolute, tyrannical fury after reading the latest domestic news report circulating on the syndicate's internal network.

宏伟的帝王书房内，一声突如其来的猛烈巨响打碎了午后的慵懒宁静。藤原将手里那台定制的钛合金平板电脑猛地砸向红木墙壁，摔得粉碎。在看完辛迪加内网刚刚推送的国内最新新闻后，他的脸庞因绝对的暴君之怒而彻底扭曲。

The headline belonged to a pretentious, heavily-hyped luxury EV brand within his empire—the "Aotian Group," and their flagship model, the "Lingyun X9." During an independent emergency braking test, the cheap, glass-fiber-reinforced plastic brake pedal bracket had snapped clean off in three out of three test vehicles.

新闻头条属于他帝国境内一个极其做作、疯狂炒作的豪华电动车品牌——“傲天集团”，以及他们的旗舰车型“凌云X9”。在一次第三方的紧急制动测试中，那廉价的玻纤增强塑料刹车踏板支架，竟然在三辆测试车中接连出现了三起完全断裂的严重事故。

Instead of issuing a massive, immediate recall, the arrogant corporation had secretly scrubbed the testing videos from the internet, suppressed all public outcry, and released a shamelessly crafted PR statement: they claimed their standards were perfectly fine, and generously offered a "free upgrade" to existing owners.

然而，这家傲慢的企业非但没有立即发布大规模召回，反而暗中将全网的测试视频删得一干二净，疯狂控评压制公众的怒火，甚至还发布了一份极其厚颜无耻的公关声明：声称他们的标准绝对没问题，并“慷慨”地表示将为已购车的用户提供“免费升级”。

"Motherfucker!" Fujiwara roared, his voice shaking the crystal chandelier. "In my own empire, there actually exists an enterprise this brazenly arrogant?! A one-hundred-percent brake failure rate, and they don't issue a recall?! They just call it a 'free upgrade'?! I have lived my entire life and have never seen an operation this utterly absurd and shameless!"

“马勒戈壁的！”藤原咆哮着，声音震得水晶吊灯都在发颤。“在老子自己的帝国里，居然存在如此嚣张狂妄的企业？！百分之百的踩断率，居然还不召回？！只是轻描淡写地叫什么‘免费升级’？！老子活了这辈子，就他妈没见过这么离谱、这么不要脸的操作！”

Sayuri, reclining elegantly on the velvet sofa, blew gently on her hot herbal tea. Her lips curved into a velvet smirk laced with icy disdain. "Indeed, Master. A one-hundred-percent snapping rate, yet they have the sheer audacity to shamelessly frame it as a philanthropic 'free upgrade.' They practically expect their doomed customers to fall to their knees and weep tears of gratitude for the company's generosity."

优雅地斜倚在天鹅绒沙发上的小百合，轻轻吹了吹杯里的热花草茶。她的红唇勾起一抹浸透着冰冷蔑视的风情坏笑。“可不是嘛，主人。百分之百的踩断率，还厚颜无耻地包装成什么大发慈悲的‘免费升级’。他们那副嘴脸，似乎是觉得那些倒霉的顾客都应该跪在地上，对公司的慷慨感恩戴德呢。”

Chika gasped, her face turning pale as she frantically clutched her massive, milk-swollen chest, practically trembling at the thought. "If we had bought that stupid car, and encountered an emergency situation on the road, our entire family would be completely wiped out! This is way too dangerous! Your Majesty should immediately issue a decree and declare that wretched enterprise bankrupt!"

千夏倒抽了一口凉气，吓得花容失色，双手死死捂住自己那对巨大、胀满奶水的胸脯，光是想想都觉得后怕得发抖。“要是我们买了那种破车，遇到紧急情况，咱们全家都会死光光的！这太危险了！陛下应该立即下旨，宣布该企业破产清算！”

From her plush cushion on the Persian rug, blind Rin tilted her head, her milky eyes blinking with purely innocent, terrifying succubus logic.

坐在波斯地毯软垫上的盲人小铃歪了歪小脑袋，空灵的纯白盲眼眨巴着，脑子里转起了极其纯洁却又令人毛骨悚然的魅魔逻辑。

"Um... hello, everyone? Rin thinks this car company has a very succubus-like mindset!" she chimed in sweetly, clapping her hands. "In the demon world, if a succubus's 'brakes' break, it just means Master gets to ride her faster and harder until they both crash in a puddle of ecstasy! But since the human CEO loves 'free upgrades' so much, why doesn't Master bring his wife and daughters to the palace?"

“唔……大家听我说好吗？小铃觉得这家汽车公司非常有魅魔的思维方式呢！”她甜丝丝地插嘴道，开心地拍着小手。“在恶魔的世界里，如果一只魅魔的‘刹车’断了，那就意味着主人可以骑得更快、更猛，直到两人一起在极乐的水洼里彻底坠毁呀！不过，既然那个人类总裁这么喜欢搞‘免费升级’，主人为什么不把他的老婆和女儿全抓到宫里来呢？”

Rin let out a wicked, angelic giggle, swaying her shoulders. "Master can violently break their 'brackets' in bed tonight until they can't walk, and then we'll send the CEO a letter saying we generously offered his family a 'free upgrade' to the imperial harem! We won't even charge him for the service!"

小铃发出一阵罪恶又天使般的娇笑，摇晃着小肩膀。“主人今晚可以在床上把她们的‘支架’狠狠踩断，干到她们连路都走不了，然后咱们就给那个总裁发封信，说我们极其慷慨地为他的家属提供了进入皇家后宫的‘免费升级’！我们甚至都不会收他的服务费哦！”

Even Miyetsuko, the fallen princess kneeling dutifully at Fujiwara's leg, couldn't help but cover her lips, a sweet, pliant smile softening her elegant features. "Rin's tactical proposition is flawless, Master. Confiscate his assets, claim his women, and rewrite the definition of his arrogance. It is the perfect imperial response."

就连乖顺地跪在藤原腿边的没落公主美悦子，也忍不住掩住了红唇，一抹甜美柔顺的微笑软化了她高贵的面容。“小铃的战术提议简直完美，主人。抄没他的家产，霸占他的女眷，并用他的傲慢重新定义惩罚。这是最完美的皇家反击。”

Fujiwara burst into a booming, tyrannical laugh, his fury instantly melting into dark, sadistic amusement as he pulled Miyetsuko's royal head into his lap. "An imperial free upgrade! Brilliant! Sayuri, contact the shadow division immediately! I want that company's stock flatlined and delisted by morning, and I want the CEO's entire female bloodline delivered to the Palace of Ultimate Bliss for a 'mandatory safety inspection' tonight!"

藤原爆发出一阵洪亮、暴君般的狂笑，他的怒火瞬间融化成了阴暗、充满施虐欲的愉悦，一把将美悦子那颗高贵的头颅按进自己腿间。“皇家的免费升级！绝妙！小百合，立刻联系暗影部门！我要那家公司的股票在明早之前彻底崩盘退市，还要那个总裁家谱上所有的女眷，今晚统统给我送到‘极乐殿’去接受‘强制安全检测’！”
