Chapter 217: The Interrogation of the Superfan

发布时间：2026/8/30 01:40:31

第二百一十七章：巨星的审视

As the appetizers were served and the initial wave of starstruck tension began to settle, Sayuri gracefully took the lead in steering the conversation. With the smooth poise of a seasoned host, she turned to Kirara and formally introduced Fujiwara, framing him not just as her partner, but as a brilliant local store manager who adored her music.

📌 Key Expressions

  • appetizers were served = 前菜/开胃菜被端上来了
  • appetizer = 开胃菜；前菜
  • initial wave of... = 最初的一阵……
  • starstruck tension = 因见到偶像而产生的紧张感
  • starstruck = 见到明星/偶像而激动得不知所措的
  • settle = 平息；缓和下来
  • take the lead in... = 主动带头做……；主导……
  • steer the conversation = 引导谈话；掌控谈话方向
  • gracefully = 优雅地；得体地
  • smooth poise = 从容优雅的仪态
  • seasoned host = 经验丰富的主人/宴会主持者
  • turn to sb. = 转向某人；面对某人
  • formally introduce sb. = 正式介绍某人
  • frame sb. as... = 将某人塑造成/定位为……
  • not just A, but B = 不只是 A，而是 B
  • partner = 伴侣
  • brilliant = 杰出的；非常优秀的
  • local store manager = 当地商店经理
  • adore = 非常喜爱；热爱
  • adore her music = 非常喜爱她的音乐

随着前菜被逐一端上桌，最初那种粉丝面见偶像的狂热与紧张也渐渐缓和了下来。小百合游刃有余地掌控着桌上的节奏，以一种极为得体大方的方式向绮罗罗正式介绍了藤原，不仅将他定义为自己的伴侣，还大肆夸赞他是位经营杂货铺、平日里对她的作品赞赏有加的优秀青年。

"Ah, so you're Sayuri-sama's... partner?" Kirara blinked, her eyes shifting from the legendary retired icon to Fujiwara. A warm, polite smile bloomed on her face. "It's an absolute pleasure to meet you. To be honest, I'm quite envious—being able to share a home with someone as graceful and wise as Sayuri-sama must feel like living in a dream."

“啊，原来您就是小百合前辈的……伴侣？”绮罗罗眨了眨眼，目光从这位传奇隐退巨星身上移开，落在了藤原身上，脸上绽放出温和礼貌的笑容，“很高兴认识您。说实话，我真的挺羡慕您的——能和像小百合前辈这样优雅聪慧的人朝夕相处，感觉就像活在梦里一样吧。”

"H-Haha, yeah... something like that," Fujiwara stammered, his usual quick-witted banter completely failing him under the crushing weight of her celebrity aura. He managed a stiff, awkward laugh, awkwardly taking a sip of his water.

📌 Key Expressions

  • stammer = 结结巴巴地说；口吃般地说
  • something like that = 差不多就是那样；大概是那么回事
  • quick-witted = 机智敏捷的；反应快的
  • banter = 轻松的玩笑式交谈；斗嘴
  • quick-witted banter = 机智的插科打诨
  • fail sb. = 使某人失去能力；在关键时刻不管用
  • under the crushing weight of... = 在……的巨大压力/压迫感之下
  • celebrity aura = 明星气场；名人的强大气场
  • manage a... laugh = 勉强挤出一阵……的笑
  • stiff laugh = 生硬的笑
  • awkward laugh = 尴尬的笑
  • take a sip of... = 小口喝一口……
  • awkwardly = 尴尬地；不自然地

“哈、哈哈，是啊……差不多吧，”藤原结结巴巴地应道，在对方自带的光环压迫下，他平日里那些油嘴滑舌的本事全都不翼而飞，只能僵硬地干笑两声，端起水杯掩饰尴尬。

For the next twenty minutes, the dinner flowed into casual, lighthearted industry chitchat. Sayuri effortlessly bridged the gap, sharing funny anecdotes from her active years while asking about Kirara's current schedule, television appearances, and upcoming projects. Kirara eagerly chimed in, completely captivated by Sayuri's gentle charm and insider wisdom, treating her like the ultimate mentor she had always idolized.

📌 Key Expressions

  • for the next twenty minutes = 在接下来的二十分钟里
  • the dinner flowed into... = 晚餐中的谈话自然转入……
  • casual, lighthearted = 随意轻松的；轻松愉快的
  • industry chitchat = 行业内的闲聊
  • chitchat = 闲聊；聊天
  • bridge the gap = 弥合隔阂；充当双方之间的桥梁
  • effortlessly = 毫不费力地；轻松自如地
  • share funny anecdotes = 分享有趣的轶事
  • anecdote = 轶事；趣闻
  • active years = 活跃时期；事业活跃的那些年
  • current schedule = 目前的日程安排
  • television appearances = 电视节目露面；电视出演
  • upcoming projects = 即将开展的项目；未来作品
  • chime in = 插话；加入谈话并发表意见
  • eagerly chime in = 积极地加入谈话
  • be captivated by... = 被……深深吸引
  • gentle charm = 温柔的魅力
  • insider wisdom = 行业内的经验与智慧
  • treat sb. like... = 像对待……一样对待某人
  • ultimate mentor = 最理想的导师；心目中最顶级的导师
  • idolize = 崇拜；视为偶像
  • the mentor she had always idolized = 她一直以来所崇拜、视为偶像的导师

接下来的二十分钟里，晚宴在轻松闲聊中推进。小百合完美地发挥着润滑剂的作用，随口分享着自己当年从业时的趣事，同时关切地询问起绮罗罗近期的通告安排、电视节目以及未来的规划。绮罗罗听得津津有味，完全被小百合温柔的谈吐与行业见解所折服，俨然把她当成了毕生敬仰的人生导师。

And then, right on cue, the trap was sprung.

就在这时，预定好的“神助攻”时刻终于到了。

"Oh, my goodness... excuse me for a moment," Sayuri gently dabbed her mouth with a napkin, standing up with a soft, apologetic smile. "I seem to have lost track of time. I need to visit the powder room. Please, you two keep chatting; don't let my absence stop the conversation."

“哎呀……不好意思，失陪一下，”小百合优雅地用餐巾按了按嘴角，带着一抹歉意而温柔的微笑站起身来，“好像有些坐久了，我去一趟洗手间。你们俩先聊着，别因为我不在就冷场哦。”

Before either of them could respond, Sayuri gracefully turned and walked away, sliding the private door shut behind her.

还没等两人反应过来，小百合已经迈着轻盈的步伐走出了包厢，顺手将私密移门轻轻带上。

The private dining room instantly plunged into a suffocating, deafening silence.

包厢里瞬间陷入了一种令人窒息的安静。

Fujiwara froze, his heart slamming against his ribs like a sledgehammer. This is it. The moment of truth. The legendary, pre-planned assist from his girlfriend to hook up with his favorite idol.

藤原整个人僵在原地，心跳快得像是在用大锤砸胸口。来了！决战时刻到了。正牌女友亲手创造的单人偷情神助攻，就在眼前。

He sat rigidly across the table, his mind racing wildly, desperately trying to think of a cool, suave opening line that wouldn't make him sound like a raving lunatic.

他硬着头皮坐在桌对面，脑子里飞速狂飙，绞尽脑汁想要想出一句既成熟帅气又不会显得像个狂热变态的开场白。

Meanwhile, Kirara—who had been smiling obediently like a starstruck junior a second ago—suddenly shifted her posture. She leaned back against her leather chair, crossing her legs, and fixed her large, expressive eyes directly on Fujiwara.

与此同时，刚才还乖巧得像个迷妹的绮罗罗却忽然变了眼神。她靠向皮质靠椅，优雅地交叠起双腿，一双明亮动人的美眸饶有兴致地直勾勾盯着藤原。

The submissive, fan-girl aura evaporated instantly, replaced by a sharp, highly intuitive curiosity.

那种低眉顺眼的粉丝滤镜瞬间烟消云散，取而代之的是一种敏锐而好奇的审视。

"You know..." Kirara murmured, a playful, knowing smirk touching the corners of her lips as she leaned forward slightly. "For a man who is dating a legendary icon like Sayuri-sama... you look remarkably terrified right now."

“你知道吗……”绮罗罗微微倾身，嘴角勾起一抹玩味而了然的坏笑，轻声开口道，“作为一个能和像小百合前辈这样传奇的巨星谈恋爱的人……你现在的表现，好像慌得有点过头了吧？”

Fujiwara blinked, caught completely off guard by her sudden pivot. "T-Terrified? Who, me? Psh, nonsense. I'm totally calm. Ice cold. Just... admiring the interior design."

藤原猛地一眨眼，完全没料到对方会突然反客为主：“害、害怕？谁？我？怎么可能！我可冷静了。冷静得像块冰。我刚刚只是……在欣赏包厢里的室内装潢。”

Kirara chuckled softly—a melodic, teasing sound that completely defied her sweet public persona. She rested her chin on her hand, tilting her head.

绮罗罗轻笑出声——那是一种完全颠覆她平时荧幕甜美形象、带着几分御姐范儿的娇笑。她托着下巴，饶有兴致地打量着他：

"Is that so? Because to me, it looks like you're sweating bullets," Kirara purred, her eyes narrowing with amused intrigue. "Tell me, Fujiwara-san... what is a man like you actually doing with someone as extraordinary as Sayuri-sama? Because in all honesty, you're a complete mystery."

“是吗？可在我看来，你脑门上的汗都快把头发打湿了，”绮罗罗笑意盈盈地眯起眼睛，“跟我说说呗，藤原先生……像你这样的普通男人，到底是怎么把小百合前辈那种高高在上的传奇女神给泡到手的？说实话，你这个人……还真是让人挺好奇的。”
