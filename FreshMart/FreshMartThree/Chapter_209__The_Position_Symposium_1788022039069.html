Chapter 209: The Position Symposium

发布时间：2026/8/30 00:47:17

第二百零九章：体位座谈会

A few evenings later, after dinner, the family gathered around the living room to digest. Fujiwara leaned back against the sofa, casually picking his teeth, before dropping a sudden question that instantly silenced the room.

几天后的一个晚上，吃过晚饭，大家围坐在起居室里消食。藤原靠在沙发上，一边剔着牙，一边冷不丁地抛出了一个让全场瞬间安静的问题。

"Let me ask you guys something," Fujiwara said, scanning the women around him. "When we're doing it, which position actually feels the best for you? Tell me."

“问个事儿啊，”藤原环视着身边的女人们，“你们平时跟我做的时候，到底哪个体位最爽？说来听听。”

The room fell silent for two seconds.

空气安静了两秒钟。

Rin was the first to roll her eyes, unceremoniously grabbing a grape from the table and throwing it at him. "What kind of perverted nonsense are you spewing this late at night? How can you even ask something like that?"

铃第一个翻了个白眼，毫不客气地抓起桌上的一颗葡萄扔了过去：“大晚上的发什么骚？这种问题亏你问得出口。”

"How is this perverted? This is called caring about female user experience," Fujiwara laughed, catching the grape and popping it into his mouth. "Besides, weren't you all clueless during your first times with your exes? Since we've got nothing better to do tonight, let's just pool our experiences together."

“这怎么能叫发骚呢？这叫关怀女性体验，”藤原接住葡萄塞进嘴里，嘿嘿笑道，“再说了，你们跟前任第一次的时候，不也都懵懵懂懂的吗？正好今晚闲着也是闲着，大家总结一下经验嘛。”

The moment exes were brought up, the tone of the conversation instantly slid from erotica to a gossip tea party.

一提到前任，话题的画风瞬间从小黄文滑向了八卦茶话会。

Rin let out a cold smirk, crossing her legs on the sofa. "My first time? Don't even ask, it's total dark history. That ex-boyfriend from high school was pretty tall, but he was so nervous he was completely at a loss. Standard missionary position, stiff as a log, and it lasted less than two minutes while almost straining his lower back. My entire brain was just screaming 'why isn't this over yet,' let alone feeling any pleasure."

铃冷笑了一声，盘腿坐在沙发上：“我的第一次？别提了，那简直是黑历史。高中时谈的那个前男友，个子倒挺高，结果紧张得手足无措。标准的男上位，僵硬得像块木头，全程只维持了不到两分钟，腰还差点被他给闪了。当时我满脑子只想着怎么还没结束，根本谈什么爽不爽。”

Miyetsuko pushed up her glasses, adding in a calm tone, "My college ex was much the same. He was an incredibly rigid senior from the literature club who insisted on pursuing traditional romance, but because of the wrong posture, we fumbled around for ages without even finding the right entrance, leaving both of us dying of awkwardness. Compared to later experiences, the first time had literally nothing worth reminiscing about."

美悦子推了推眼镜，语气平静地补充道：“我的大学前任也差不多。那是个极其死板的文学社学长，非要追求什么所谓的传统浪漫，结果因为姿势不对，折腾半天连门都没找对，最后两个人都尴尬得要死。比起后来的体验，第一次实在没什么值得留恋的。”

Fujiwara burst out laughing. "So all your firsts were straight-up disaster movies? What about later then? After changing positions, which one do you like best?"

藤原听得哈哈大笑：“合着你们的第一次全都是灾难片啊。那后来呢？换了姿势之后，你们最喜欢哪种？”

"I prefer the side-lying position," Rin said casually, slapping her thigh. "It saves energy, lets us face each other, and most importantly, I don't get crushed to death."

“我比较喜欢侧卧位，”铃大大咧咧地拍了拍大腿，“省力气，还能面对面看着对方，最主要是不用被压得喘不过气。”

Miyetsuko paused to think for a moment, adjusting her glasses. "From a biomechanical perspective, I personally lean towards the cowgirl position. Wide field of vision, the rhythm is entirely in your own hands, and the efficiency is highest."

美悦子沉思了片刻，推了推眼镜：“从受力角度来说，我个人倾向于骑乘位。视野开阔，节奏完全掌握在自己手里，效率最高。”

Just as Fujiwara was listening with rapt attention, Sayuri, who had been sitting quietly to the side, let out a soft cough.

就在藤原听得津津有味时，一直安静坐在一旁的小百合轻轻咳了一声。

All eyes instantly converged on her. Everyone suddenly remembered that in terms of practical experience and professional literacy, the gentle and virtuous Sayuri was the true "industry titan" with absolute authority on the matter.

全场的目光瞬间聚焦到了她身上。大家这才想起来，论实战经验和专业素养，屋里这位温柔贤惠的小百合可是真正拥有绝对发言权的“行业泰斗”。

"Sayuri, what about you?" Fujiwara leaned over curiously. "With your exes, or rather... back when you were filming, having tried all those crazy positions, which one was actually the most intense?"

“小百合，你呢？”藤原好奇地凑过去，“你跟那些前任，或者说……你以前拍片的时候，试过那么多乱七八糟的姿势，到底哪个最带劲？”

Sayuri smiled shyly, her cheeks flushing slightly. She picked up her cup of tea to moisten her throat, speaking as gently as if she were discussing tonight's dinner menu:

小百合有些羞涩地抿嘴一笑，脸颊微微泛红。她端起茶杯润了润嗓子，语气温和得像是在讨论今天晚上的菜谱：

"Actually... back on set, directors would design all sorts of weird, high-difficulty positions just for visual effect. Like hanging your legs on custom brackets or dangling in mid-air. Those postures look fancy, but they're exhausting as hell and definitely not comfortable."

“其实吧……以前在片场的时候，导演为了追求视觉效果，确实设计过很多奇奇怪怪的高难度体位。比如把腿挂在特制的支架上，或者悬空吊着拍。那些姿势看着花哨，实际上累得要命，根本谈不上舒服。”

Sayuri paused for a second and continued, "As for my own preference... honestly, the most comfortable ones are actually the basic doggystyle, or an improved version with the waist propped up slightly. The angle is deep, and you don't have to split your focus to maintain balance. As for my first time..."

小百合顿了顿，继续说道：“至于我自己的感受……其实最舒服的，反而是最基础的后入式，或者是稍微垫高腰部的改良版。那种角度深，而且不用分心去维持平衡。至于第一次嘛……”

She couldn't help covering her mouth with a giggle. "I was even more nervous than you guys during my first shoot. My male co-star was very experienced, and seeing that I was about to cry, he comforted me while helping me regulate my breathing. But because we got too into it, neither of us heard the director yell cut, and we ended up shooting for an extra ten minutes straight."

她忍不住掩嘴笑了起来：“我第一场戏拍的时候比你们还紧张，搭档的男优经验丰富，看我快哭了，还一边安慰我一边调整呼吸。结果因为太投入，导演喊卡了我们俩都没听见，硬生生多拍了十分钟。”

Hearing this, Fujiwara was completely dumbfounded. "Good grief, as expected of a national-level AV idol—even reminiscing about work sounds this refreshingly mundane."

藤原听完，整个人目瞪口呆：“好家伙，不愧是国民级女优，连回忆工作都能说得这么清新脱俗。”

Rin and Miyetsuko were left utterly speechless. Rin rolled her eyes and quipped, "Compared to Sayuri, our naive early experiences back then were basically child's play."

铃和美悦子也是一阵无语。铃翻了个白眼吐槽道：“跟小百合一比，我们当年的那些青涩初体验简直就是过家家。”

"Alright, since everyone has confessed their preferences," Fujiwara grinned mischievously as he stood up from the sofa and swept Sayuri horizontally into his arms. "Since Sayuri is the expert, tonight the expert will take the lead, and we'll practice every single high-scoring position everyone just mentioned right on the spot."

“好啦，既然大家都坦白了各自的喜好，”藤原坏笑着从沙发上站起来，一把将身旁的小百合横抱抱起，“既然小百合是专家，今晚就由专家带头，把大家刚才提到的所有高分体位全部实地演练一遍。”

"Hey! Don't mess around this late at night!" Sayuri gasped, but she was hoisted straight over Fujiwara's shoulder as he marched toward the bedroom.

“喂！大晚上的别折腾了！”小百合惊呼一声，却被藤原直接扛着走向了卧室。

Accompanied by Rin and Miyetsuko's merciless jeers and teasing laughter, the living room returned to that lively, bustling warmth that belonged uniquely to "Fresh Mart."

伴随着铃和美悦子毫不留情的起哄声与笑骂声，起居室里重新恢复了那份独属于“新鲜玛特”的、热热闹闹的人间烟火气。
