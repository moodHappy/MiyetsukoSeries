Chapter 590: The Secret of the Mother of Earth

发布时间：2026/10/17 15:24:06

第五百九十章：大地之母的秘密

While Emperor Fujiwara was still busy testing whether Miyetsuko’s post-resurrection structural integrity had successfully survived her Styx-ward ordeal on the velvet sofa, the imperial science and mysticism console in the corner of the grand salon suddenly lit up with a blinding, celestial flash of golden, primordial light.

正当藤原大帝还在天鹅绒沙发上忙着对美悦子死而复生后的“硬件结构”进行严格的实地复查时，角落里的皇室科学与神秘学综合控制台突然闪烁起耀眼刺目的金色远古神光。

Sayuri strolled over, her Tang-dynasty silks rustling as she tapped the holographic quantum archeology screen. The data stream was scrolling at light-speed, revealing an astonishing, universe-shattering theological plot twist regarding the household’s resident snack-eating, milk-producing slacker.

小百合迈着优雅的步伐走了过去，盛唐真丝长袍沙沙作响，她纤细的手指在全息量子考古屏幕上轻轻一点。数据流正以光速滚动，揭示了一个颠覆整个宇宙观的、关于家里那位天天吃零食、产奶水的咸鱼千夏的惊天神话内幕。

"Well, well, well," Sayuri purred, her velvet eyes widening in genuine macroeconomic astonishment. "It turns out Chika’s legendary dragon-taming technique was never just a fluke. According to this ancient cosmic DNA registry, our dear Chika is actually the final, wandering soul fragment of the primordial Mother of Earth left behind at the dawn of the universe!"

“哎呀呀，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，丝绒般的眼眸中流露出货真价实的宏观经济学震撼，“原来千夏那神乎其技的御龙之术并非空穴来风啊。根据这份远古宇宙基因档案显示，咱们亲爱的千夏，竟然是远古时期‘大地之母’留在世间的最后一块流落灵魂碎片！”

The entire grand salon went dead silent. Fujiwara paused his hand on Miyetsuko’s hip, and even blind Rin dropped her cookie in pure shock.

整个大起居殿瞬间鸦雀无声。藤原搭在美悦子腰上的手猛地一僵，就连盲人小铃都震惊得连手里的饼干都差点掉在地上。

"The Mother of Earth?! You've got to be kidding me!" Chika choked, spitting out a mouthful of soda. She stared in horror at the holographic projection of a titanic, mythical goddess surrounded by roaring beasts and folding galaxies. "Me?! The creator of all planetary life and beast-tier entities?! Are you telling me I went through ten thousand reincarnations of absolute poverty, worked dead-end minimum wage shifts in greasy diners, and ended up in a galactic slum just to become the Empire's primary dairy supplier?!"

“大地之母？！开什么国际玩笑？！”千夏呛得一口苏打水喷了出来，目瞪口呆地看着全息投影上那个被无数洪荒巨兽和旋转星系环绕的远古创世女神影像，“我？！那个创造了所有星球生命和巨兽神龙的大地之母？！你们的意思是说，老娘历经了万世转生、受尽了人世穷苦、在油腻的小餐馆打过无数份最低工资的零工，兜兜转转折腾了一大圈，结果就是为了跑来给藤原帝国当专职奶牛供应商的？！”

"It all makes absolute mathematical sense!" Sayuri nodded analytically, tapping the holographic screen. "That explains why you could command an eighty-five-thousand-li plasma dragon simply by yelling at it like an irritated mother scolding a stray dog. To a primordial beast, you aren't just a trainer; you are literally their ancestral mother. Your scolding carries the supreme genetic authority of planetary creation!"

“这完全符合严密的数学逻辑！”小百合冷静地分析道，手指轻叩全息屏幕，“这就难怪你当初仅仅凭着像发火家长训斥野狗一样的语气训斥几句，就能让一头展翅八万五千里的等离子巨龙当场跪下瑟瑟发抖。对那些洪荒巨兽而言，你可不单单是什么御兽大师，你就是它们的祖宗本尊啊！你的每一句唠叨和怒吼，都自带创世级别的绝对基因压制！”

Miyetsuko, still adjusting her 183828号 underworld bureaucrat glasses while pinned beneath the Emperor, offered a deeply respectful, breathless bow toward Chika. "To think that the slacker who eats chips on the sofa all day is actually the supreme maternal deity of the cosmos... The imperial lineage has truly achieved peak theological diversity."

依然被压在皇帝身下手忙脚乱整理着183828号冥界大统领眼镜的美悦子，由衷地向千夏投去崇敬的目光：“谁能想到平时整天躺在沙发上吃薯片摸鱼的千夏姐姐，居然是整个宇宙最高贵的地母神明……咱们后宫的血统多样性当真是越来越离谱了呢。”

Fujiwara burst out into a loud, booming tyrannical laugh. The Emperor pulled Miyetsuko aside, stood up, and walked over to Chika, a wicked, entrepreneurial grin spreading across his face.

藤原爆发出一项宏大而恶劣的暴君狂笑。他顺手把美悦子拉到一旁，大步走到千夏面前，脸上露出了一个极其资本家、充满商业算计的邪恶坏笑。

"Hahaha! A divine mother of beasts as my concubine!" Fujiwara roared, clapping his hands together. "Forget the ten star systems! With the Mother of Earth running our domestic dairy and livestock logistics, we can establish a galaxy-wide monopoly on cosmic dragon-riding tours and premium organic milk! Chika, my eternal goddess of the soil, come give the Emperor a hug—and let's discuss how to market your primordial breast milk on the interplanetary stock exchange!"

“哈哈哈！堂堂大地之母居然成了朕的后宫妃子！”藤原大笑着拍手称快，“什么十个星系都弱爆了！只要让大地之母来亲自负责咱们帝国的畜牧业和奶水供应链，咱们立刻就能垄断整个银河系的星际龙骑观光旅游业和高端有机母乳市场！千夏，我的创世地母陛下，赶紧来让朕抱一个——咱们顺便好好规划一下怎么把你的神圣母乳打包上市去纳斯达克敲钟！”

Chika rolled her eyes, heavily crossing her arms as her massive, milk-swollen chest heaved with supreme annoyance. She popped another potato chip into her mouth and sighed.

千夏没好气地翻了个白眼，双手环抱胸前，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔随着她满脸的不爽剧烈起伏着。她又往嘴里塞了一片薯片，无奈地叹了口气。

"Fine, whatever. Universe creator, Mother of Earth, galactic milk maid—it all pays the same rent," Chika muttered, tossing a stray chip directly at the Emperor's head. "Just remember: if those giant dragons in the Andromeda colony don't start behaving themselves, I'm going down there to revoke their planetary breathing privileges!"

“行吧行吧，创世神也好，大地之母也罢，宇宙大奶牛也行——反正月底交房贷的水电费一分钱也不会少，”千夏嘟囔着，顺手把一片碎薯片精准地砸在了皇帝的脑门上，“不过丑话说在前头：仙女座殖民地的那些大蜥蜴要是再敢不听话，老娘哪天亲自去把它们全员的呼吸权给依法没收了！”
