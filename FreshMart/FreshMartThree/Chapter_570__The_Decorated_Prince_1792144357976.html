Chapter 570: The Decorated Prince

发布时间：2026/10/16 17:52:36

第五百七十章：战功赫赫的皇子

While the imperial household had spent the last few days recovering from higher-dimensional surveillance, negotiating cross-dimensional trade agreements with the Heavenly Palace, and marveling at Miyetsuko’s radiantly ravished post-diplomatic glow, the grand salon was suddenly interrupted by a thunderous, planetary-scale military salute.

正当皇室成员们忙着从高维监控的阴影中恢复过来、与天宫签订跨维度贸易协定、并惊叹于美悦子那红光满面的外交成果时，大起居殿外突然传来了震耳欲聋、响彻整颗行星的宏大军事礼炮声。

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

轰！轰！轰！

The heavy obsidian doors swung open, and stepping into the hall was none other than the Emperor’s 18-year-old biological son—the Prince. Unlike his awkward, bewildered self during his first visit eighteen years after birth, the young man now strode with the terrifying, absolute confidence of a seasoned military commander. He was clad in a dazzling suit of quantum-forged terminator power armor, his chest covered in a ridiculous array of galactic medals, and a fresh string of holographic annexation certificates floating behind him.

厚重的黑曜石大门轰然开启，大步走进大殿的正是皇帝十八岁的亲生儿子——那位皇子。与十八年前初次进宫时那副手足无措的窘迫模样截然不同，如今的年轻人迈着久经沙场的军事指挥官那绝对自信而冷酷的步伐。他身穿一套由量子锻造而成的终结者动力装甲，胸前挂满了密密麻麻、闪耀着夸张光芒的银河系功勋勋章，身后还悬挂着一长串刚刚签署的星系吞并全息证书。

"Father! Mothers! I have returned from the western galactic frontier!" the Prince bellowed, slamming his armored fist against his chest in a flawless military salute. "In the name of the Fujiwara Empire, I have successfully crushed the rebellion of the Crab Nebula, dismantled the Dyson-ring syndicates of Sector 7, and personally slaughtered the rebel warlords. I present to you the unconditional surrender and title deeds of ten brand-new, resource-rich star systems!"

“父皇！各位母妃！儿臣奉命征讨西方银河边境，如今凯旋归来！”皇子声如洪钟地高声汇报道，同时用装甲重重捶击胸口，行了一个无可挑剔的帝国军礼，“为了藤原帝国的无上荣光，儿臣已成功平定蟹状星云的叛乱，彻底瓦解了第七星区的戴森环黑帮同盟，并亲手斩杀了反叛军阀。儿臣今日特来献上十个资源极其富饶的全新星系之无条件投降书与地契！”

The grand salon went dead silent. Chika, who had been lazily munching on a bag of spicy chips while watching her daytime soap opera, nearly swallowed her own tongue. She dropped the snack bag and stared at her son in absolute, jaw-dropping shock.

大起居殿内瞬间陷入一片死寂。正懒洋洋地一边看肥皂剧一边啃麻辣薯片、试图找回存在感的千夏差点把自己的舌头吞下去。她手里的零食袋“啪嗒”掉在地上，瞪大眼睛，满脸都是活见鬼般的震惊。

"W-Wait a minute...!" Chika shrieked, pointing a trembling finger at the heavily decorated young man. "You went out there for a few months, and you brought back ten star systems?! Since when did my little milk-drinking baby turn into an interstellar conqueror?!"

“等……等一下……！”千夏尖叫着用颤抖的手指指着这个挂满勋章的年轻小伙，“你不过是被派出去几个月，怎么一回手就给老娘搬回来了整整十个星系？！我当年那个只会吃奶的小崽子什么时候变成星际征服者了？！”

Sayuri, looking gloriously plump and impossibly regal in her Tang-dynasty silks, elegantly set down her teacup. The Wise Empress’s eyes sparkled with sudden, ruthless macroeconomic brilliance as she calculated the real estate value of ten unexploited star systems.

穿着盛唐真丝长袍、丰腴绝伦的小百合优雅地放下了茶杯。这位贤后精明锐利的眼眸中瞬间闪过一道冷酷而璀璨的宏观经济学光芒，脑子里已经在飞速计算这十个全新星系的房地产开发估值了。

"Ten star systems... My god, Chika, your womb has successfully produced an interstellar real estate portfolio," Sayuri purred smoothly, a velvet smirk playing on her lips. "Forget the dairy factory; your son has just single-handedly elevated our family's net worth by forty percent. The 'Bush Lady' and Miyetsuko combined can't compete with this kind of quarterly revenue."

“十个星系……我的天哪，千夏，你的子宫居然一不小心产出了一支星际不动产投资组合，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，完美的红唇边挂着一抹丝绒般的冷笑，“什么奶牛工厂啊，你儿子这一出手，直接把咱们全家的家族净资产拉高了百分之四十。什么‘草丛夫人’和美悦子加起来，在这个季度的创收战绩面前也得靠边站。”

Miyetsuko, kneeling gracefully by the Persian rug, offered a deep, incredibly respectful bow to the heavily armored prince. The fallen princess’s exquisite face glowed with aristocratic admiration. "A magnificent campaign, Your Highness. To conquer ten star systems at the tender age of eighteen... You have officially secured your mother's place at the absolute top of the imperial pedigree."

优雅地跪在波斯地毯旁的美悦子，向这位身披重甲的皇子行了一个极其郑重、充满敬意的宫廷大礼。没落公主精致的脸上闪烁着贵族式的钦佩。“殿下当真是一场旷世大捷。在十八岁的芳龄就能一举拿下十个星系……您不仅为帝国开疆拓土，更让您的母妃在皇室血统阶层中的地位稳稳锁死了绝对王座。”

From her fluffy cushion, blind Rin clapped her hands with terrifying demonic joy, swinging her legs. "Um... hello, Big Bro General! Does this mean we own ten new planets to build chocolate slides and lava pools?! Rin wants to be the Governor of Planet Marshmallow!"

坐在毛绒软垫上的盲人小铃开心地拍着小手，晃荡着双腿：“唔……大将军哥哥好呀！这意思是不是说咱们现在拥有了十颗新星球，可以去上面建巧克力滑梯和岩浆温泉啦？小铃申请当‘棉花糖星球’的总督大人！”

Fujiwara, reclining on the central velvet sofa like an utterly unbothered, supreme patriarch, let out a booming, proud laugh. The Emperor didn't look threatened by his son's meteoric rise; instead, his dark eyes burned with the smug satisfaction of a CEO whose corporate nepotism had miraculously paid off.

像个泰然自若的最高大家长一样斜倚在中央天鹅绒沙发上的藤原，爆发出一阵洪亮而骄傲的狂笑。这位皇帝不仅没有因为儿子的彗星般崛起而感到任何皇位威胁，反而露出一副“老子搞裙带关系果然慧眼识珠”的得意神情。

"Hahaha! That's my boy!" Fujiwara roared, standing up to clap the young conqueror heavily on his armored shoulder. "Ten star systems! That's more territory than the Senate has managed to mismanage in a century! Who cares if you spent the first eighteen years of your life outside the palace walls? You have officially proven that the imperial genes—and Chika's relentless dairy output—can produce true galactic dominance!"

“哈哈哈！不愧是朕的种！”藤原大笑着站起身，重重地在年轻征服者的装甲肩膀上拍了一巴掌，“十个星系的战功！这比元老院那帮饭桶过去一百年里折腾出来的地盘还要大！谁在乎你前十八年是不是被关在外廷养大的？你用实力证明了，皇室的基因——再加上千夏那源源不断的强力奶水供应——绝对能孵化出真正的银河系霸权！”

The Prince flushed slightly beneath his helmet, dropping to one knee before his father. "I only followed Master's—I mean, Father's—brilliant teachings of absolute expansion and merciless exploitation. I place these ten star systems entirely at your feet."

皇子在头盔下微微红了脸，在父亲面前单膝跪地：“儿臣只是遵循了主人——不，是父皇——关于绝对扩张与残酷剥削的英明教导。儿臣愿将这十个星系的所有主权，全部供奉在父皇足下。”

Chika wiped a dramatic tear of pride from her eye, her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaving. "Alright, I forgive you for looking older than me! Since you brought home ten star systems, I will personally reward you with a lifetime supply of fresh, premium dairy straight from the source!"

千夏抹去眼角一滴极具戏剧性的骄傲泪水，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔剧烈起伏着。“行吧，看在你给家里搬回十个星系的份上，老娘原谅你长得比我还显老了！既然你这么争气，妈妈今天特批奖励你一辈子的无限量直供新鲜母乳！”

The Prince choked, turning violently red as the terrifying reality of his mother's "reward" hit him. Fujiwara, however, burst into fresh, uproarious laughter, wrapping one arm around Chika and the other around the heavily armored Prince.

皇子当场被噎住，一想到他妈所谓的“奖励”究竟是个什么画面，整张脸瞬间涨成了紫红。而藤原则爆发出更加响亮的狂笑，一只手搂住千夏，另一只手亲热地揽住这位战功赫赫的重甲皇子。

"Enough military formalities! Ten new star systems mean ten new sets of planetary luxury resorts to plunder!" Fujiwara declared, his eyes flashing with lust and ambition. "Chika, Miyetsuko, Sayuri—pack your bags! The Imperial Family is going on a grand, tax-exempt galactic vacation to our new colonies! Let's go show those alien planets what true imperial discipline looks like!"

“行了，别搞那些酸溜溜的军事礼节了！十个新星系，就意味着十套全新的、供咱们去狠狠搜刮的行星级奢华度假村！”藤原大眼放光地大手一挥，“小百合、千夏、美悦子——收拾行李！皇室全家这就组团去咱们的新殖民地进行一次免税的银河系大游玩！让那帮外星土著见识一下什么叫真正的帝国铁拳与核心纪律！”
