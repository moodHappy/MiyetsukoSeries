第百六十二章：老当益壮的花魁大礼

As the tense atmosphere in the dining room began to thaw, Rin suddenly slapped her forehead, her eyes lighting up with a mischievous, brilliant spark as a wild thought crossed her mind.

正当饭桌上的紧张气氛渐渐缓和时，铃突然一拍脑门，双眼亮起一道狡黠而兴奋的光芒，一个绝妙的鬼主意瞬间涌上心头。

"Wait a minute! I've got it!" Rin blurted out, pointing a finger in triumph. "Why are we even arguing over Chika? If your grand-uncle is so full of fire and needs someone to vent his urges, why don't we just send him that legendary top-tier courtesan we were just gossiping about?"

“等等！我有一个绝妙的主意！”铃打了个响指兴奋地喊道，“咱们干嘛非要为千夏争执不下？既然二大爷老当益壮、浑身燥热急着找人发泄，咱们直接把前几天聊的那位传说中的头牌风俗女送不就行了？”

Fujiwara blinked, momentarily stunned. "You mean... the one who was locked up by the poor guy and turned into a free-use meat toilet?"

藤原愣了一下，有些错愕地眨了眨眼：“你是说……那个被穷鬼绑架当成肉便器的主儿？”

"The very same!" Rin grinned mischievously. "Think about it! It completely solves your grand-uncle's problem, fulfills your filial duty as a devoted nephew, and on top of that, it throws some extra high-end business and cash her way to help the poor girl recover from her... occupational hazards. It's a win-win-win!"

“对呀！就是她！”铃坏笑着一拍桌子，“你想想看！这不仅完美解决了二大爷的刚需、尽了你这个大侄子的孝心，顺便还能给那个饱受摧残的可怜女人送去一笔丰厚的高档业务和外快，帮她好好补补身子。这简直是一箭三雕的慈善事业啊！”

The moment the proposal dropped, Miyetsuko pushed up her glasses, her analytical mind immediately calculating the sheer perfection of the logistics.

这个提议一出，美悦子立刻推了推眼镜，大脑飞速运转，瞬间对这套方案的完美逻辑给出了高度评价。

"An exceptionally optimal allocation of local resources," Miyetsuko declared with a clinical, approving smirk. "It recycles an existing market asset, stimulates the local pleasure economy, satisfies familial obligations, and maintains internal household stability. Flawless execution."

“极其高效的本地资源再分配方案，”美悦子嘴角勾起一抹冷静而赞许的微笑，“既盘活了现有的市场资产、刺激了风俗业经济，又尽到了家族孝道，同时还维稳了咱们家的内部资产安全。堪称完美。”

Sayuri clapped her hands together, her gentle eyes sparkling with warm, charitable amusement. "Oh, that is actually a wonderful idea! We're practically acting as neighborhood philanthropists, taking care of a lonely old man while offering a helping hand to a working girl in need."

小百合开心地拍了拍手，温柔的眼眸里闪烁着善良而好笑的光芒：“天呐，这主意听起来简直太有爱心了！咱们这简直是在行善积德，既照顾了孤寡老人，又帮衬了苦命的职业女性呢。”

Fujiwara stared at his brilliant women, his jaw dropping slightly before he burst into a roaring, appreciative laugh.

藤原看着这三个思维清奇的女人们，下巴差点掉下来，随后忍不住放声狂笑起来。

"Genius!" Fujiwara howled, slamming the table. "Absolute, top-tier genius! My grand-uncle gets a legendary beauty who has been thoroughly trained by both elite politicians and a desperate fugitive, the courtesan gets paid double, and Chika gets to stay safely right here where she belongs. I'm calling the parlor right now!"

“绝了！这主意简直绝了！”藤原大笑着拍桌子赞叹道，“不愧是我的女人！二大爷这回能享受到连政界大佬都眼馋的顶级花魁，头牌还能白拿一笔大红包，咱们家的小丫鬟也能安安稳稳地留下来。我这就给那家店打电话安排！”

In the warm, chaotic living room of "Fresh Mart," the family dissolved into another wave of helpless, triumphant laughter, proving once again that whenever Fujiwara's household put their heads together, even a simple family crisis could be turned into an absolute masterpiece of comedy.

在一片欢声笑语中，“新鲜玛特”的起居室再次被快活的空气彻底淹没。事实证明，只要这群无法无天的家伙凑在一起，哪怕是面对长辈的刁钻要求，也能轻而易举地将其变成一出让人捧腹大笑的荒诞喜剧。
