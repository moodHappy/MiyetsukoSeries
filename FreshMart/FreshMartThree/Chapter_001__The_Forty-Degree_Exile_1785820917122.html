Chapter 1: The Forty-Degree Exile

发布时间：2026/8/4 13:21:43

第一章：四十五度的流亡

The thermometer on the cracked concrete wall had long since stopped moving, stuck stubbornly at forty-five degrees Celsius. On Donglai Island, the heat wasn't just a weather condition; it was an active, crushing physical presence. The air shimmering above the asphalt felt thick enough to chew, smelling of baked dust, salt, and the low, constant hum of a struggling generator.

墙上那面布满裂纹的混凝土温度计早就不动了，死死卡在摄氏四十五度。在东莱岛上，酷热早已不是单纯的天气现象，而是一种具有实质压迫感的实体。柏油路上方扭曲的空气浓稠得令人窒息，弥漫着烤焦的尘土、咸湿的海风，以及一台苦苦支撑的发电机那低沉而持续的轰鸣。

Inside the dim, shuttered living room, the battery-powered radio hissed with static before a flat, automated voice cut through the room. “A record-breaking heatwave continues to sweep the region. Citizens are strongly advised that remaining indoors without air conditioning poses a severe, life-threatening risk. Power grids are operating at maximum capacity...”

在昏暗、百叶窗紧闭的客厅里，那台靠电池供电的收音机发出刺耳的电流声，随后一个毫无感情的合成音穿透了房间。“破纪录的热浪继续席卷该地区。特此强烈建议市民注意，在没有空调的情况下呆在室内将面临严峻的生命危险。电网正处于满负荷运转状态……”

Rin tossed a damp, lukewarm towel onto the table and let out a dry laugh. "Air conditioning? The grid here died three days ago, and these bureaucrats are still reading safety guidelines from an air-conditioned studio across the sea. They really think we care about a heat advisory while we're dodging their extradition notices."

铃把一条温热的湿毛巾扔在桌上，发出了一声干笑。“空调？这里的电网三天前就瘫痪了，那帮官僚居然还在海那头吹着空调演播室宣读安全指南。他们还真以为我们在躲避引渡令的时候，会有闲心听他们的防暑警报。”

Miyetsuko stood near the window, her fingers sliding a wooden slat apart just a fraction of an inch to peer outside. The glare off the ocean was blinding. But what caught her attention wasn't the sun—it was the silhouette of a gray maritime patrol vessel cutting a slow, deliberate line along the horizon, just outside territorial waters.

美悦子站在窗边，修长的手指将百叶窗拨开一条细缝向外望去。海面的反光刺眼夺目。但吸引她注意的不是太阳——而是一艘灰色的海上巡逻艇正沿着领海外缘缓缓驶过，划出一条刻意而傲慢的警戒线。

"They aren't just watching us with weather satellites anymore," Miyetsuko said, her voice completely devoid of panic, though her eyes were dangerously sharp. "The homeland is tightening the noose. The heat is just their silent partner. They’re waiting to see if we cook ourselves out of existence before they even have to send a wet-work team."

“他们现在不只是用气象卫星监视我们了，”美悦子平静地说道，声音里听不出一丝惊慌，但眼神却危险地锐利起来，“祖国正在收紧绞索。这高温不过是他们的无声帮凶。他们甚至不需要派暗杀小队，只要等着看我们自己会不会在这个蒸笼里活活烤死就行了。”

Sayuri, her skin flushed red from the oppressive humidity, was boiling a small pot of brackish water on a portable gas stove, the flame barely flickering in the stagnant air. She wiped sweat from her forehead with the back of her hand, looking over at Fujiwara with quiet concern.

小百合被这令人窒息的湿热闷得满脸通红，正用一个便携式燃气炉煮着一小锅发苦的淡化水，微弱的火苗在凝滞的空气中摇曳不定。她用手背擦了擦额头的汗，带着几分无声的担忧看向藤原。

"The water supply is down to the last two crates, Fujiwara," Sayuri said softly, her voice strained. "And the radio said the supply ships from the mainland won't dock until next week. If the government has blockaded the routes..."

“淡水只剩下最后两箱了，藤原，”小百合轻声说道，声音有些紧绷，“广播里说大陆的补给船下周才能靠岸。如果政府真的封锁了航线……”

Fujiwara was sitting on an overturned crate, cleaning the slide of a battered sidearm with a piece of oily rag. He didn't look up immediately. The confident, arrogant swagger of the "Fresh Mart" days had been stripped away, replaced by the grim, hardened focus of a fugitive who knew the walls were closing in.

藤原正坐在一只倒扣的木箱上，用一块沾油的抹布擦拭着一把磨损严重的随身手枪。他没有立刻抬头。经营“新鲜玛特”时的那种自信与狂妄已被剥离，取而代之的是亡命之徒那冷酷、坚毅的专注。

"Let them blockade," Fujiwara finally spoke, his voice deep and steady, cutting through the hum of the dying generator. He locked the slide of the pistol into place with a sharp clack. "They want us to sweat, they want us to starve, and they want us to panic. But we didn't run all the way out here to die of a heatstroke in a tin shack."

“让他们封锁好了，”藤原终于开口，声音低沉而平稳，盖过了发电机临死前的轰鸣。他伴随着一声清脆的咔哒声，将手枪套筒锁到位。“他们想让我们流汗，想让我们挨饿，想让我们恐慌。可我们大老远跑来这儿，可不是为了在一间铁皮屋里中暑等死的。”

He stood up, walking over to place a reassuring hand on Sayuri’s damp shoulder, before looking back at Miyetsuko and Rin.

他站起身，走到小百合身旁，将手轻轻搭在她汗湿的肩膀上，随后抬眼看向美悦子和铃。

"The 45-degree heat might be trying to kill us," Fujiwara grinned, a cold, dangerous glint in his eye, "but it works both ways. If the state thinks a little weather is going to break us, they've got another thing coming. Grab your gear. We're moving to the northern ridge tonight. Time to find out who's really watching who."

“四十五度的高温或许在想方设法弄死我们，”藤原咧嘴一笑，眼底闪过一抹冰冷危险的光芒，“但对两边都一样。要是那帮老爷觉得这点天气就能把我们击垮，那他们可就打错算盘了。收拾装备。今晚我们转移到北岭。是时候看看究竟是谁在监视谁了。”
