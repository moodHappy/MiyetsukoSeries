Chapter 407: The Tactile Tycoon and Global Domination

发布时间：2026/9/11 13:31:51

第四百零七章：触觉大亨与全球霸权

Against all statistical probability and common sense, Fujiwara’s unhinged cybernetic gamble did not collapse into a fiery bankruptcy. Armed with a revolutionary pitch deck and a terrifying amount of unverified digital signals, he actually secured massive venture capital funding. Backed by an elite team of top-tier scientists forced to work out of Fresh Mart's damp basement under Rin's relentless supervision, the product was polished to absolute perfection.

违背了所有的统计学概率与常识，藤原那场疯狂的赛博赌博竟然没有以火光冲天的破产收场。凭借着一份颠覆性的路演PPT和大量未经验证的数字信号，他竟然真的拉到了巨额的风投资金。在铃毫不留情的严密监视下，一帮被逼在“新鲜玛特”潮湿地下室里加班的顶级科学家团队对产品进行了精雕细琢，最终将这款产品打磨到了极致完美的境界。

By year's end, the official launch shook the entire globe. The haptic-suit revolution swept across nations like a digital wildfire, and the platform's crown jewel—Donglai Island's legendary remote hospitality network—shattered every global sales record in human history.

到了年底，官方产品的正式发布震撼了整个地球。触觉服革命像数字野火一样席卷全球，而该平台的镇店之宝——东莱岛传奇的远程头牌服务网络——直接打破了人类历史上所有的全球销售纪录。

Sitting atop his towering corporate empire, Fujiwara was officially crowned as the visionary founder of the tactile internet and the new undisputed richest person in the world.

坐在耸入云霄的企业帝国巅峰，藤原正式加冕为触觉互联网的远见创始人和当之无愧的世界首富。

"Look at them, ladies!" Fujiwara laughed triumphantly, leaning back in a solid-gold executive chair as holographic sales tickers flashed across his private penthouse. "Every rural village, every lonely wanderer, and every high-rolling oligarch is plugged into our network right now! We aren't just selling haptic feedback; we're monetizing human nervous systems on a planetary scale!"

“你们看这群人，女士们！”藤原得意洋洋地大笑着，靠在纯金打造的高管老板椅上，看着全息销售走势图在他的私人顶层公寓里不断闪烁，“每一个偏远乡村、每一个孤独的流浪汉、每一个腰缠万贯的富豪，现在全都接入了我们的网络！我们卖的不只是触觉反馈；我们在以整个星球的规模将人类的神经系统直接变现！”

Miyetsuko stood by the panoramic window, her iron baseball bat resting casually against the wall. She adjusted her glasses, her cold, calculating eyes reviewing the astronomical profit margins.

美悦子站在全景落地窗旁，手中的铁棒球棍随意地靠在墙边。她推了推眼镜，用冰冷而精算的目光审视着那天文数字般的利润率。

"Your absolute lack of a moral compass is statistically impressive, Fujiwara," Miyetsuko deadpanned, though a rare, approving smirk touched her lips. "By automating the extortion of global sensory data, our market capitalization has surpassed several sovereign nations. Try not to choke on your champagne before I finish auditing the next quarter's payroll."

“你对道德底线的彻底缺失从统计学上来看还真是令人印象深刻啊，藤原，”美悦子面无表情地评价道，尽管她的嘴角泛起了一抹罕见而赞许的冷笑，“通过将全球感官数据的敲诈勒索实现自动化，我们的市值已经超越了好几个主权国家。在我审核完下季度的工资账单之前，最好别让香槟把你给呛死。”

Blind Rin sat gracefully in a velvet armchair nearby, her wide, unseeing eyes fixed blankly ahead with a gentle, serene smile. She quietly folded a soft, premium haptic blanket for VIP users.

盲人铃优雅地坐在附近的一张天鹅绒单人沙发上，她那双睁得滚圆却空洞无神的眼睛茫然地直视前方，脸上挂着温柔而宁静的微笑。她正安静地为VIP用户叠着一条柔软高级的触觉毛毯。

"I'm so glad everyone is doing well," Rin whispered softly, her voice like a sweet breeze. "Even if I can't see the screens, I can feel the entire world vibrating through the floorboards. It’s like a million people holding hands across the ocean... Please keep working hard, everyone."

“看到大家都过得这么好，我真高兴，”铃轻声呢喃着，声音如同甜美的微风，“哪怕我看不到屏幕，我也能顺着地板感受到整个世界都在震动。就像成千上万的人隔着海洋手拉手一样……请大家继续加油哦。”

Sayuri stood right beside Rin, gently patting the blind girl's shoulder with an expression of supreme, submissive warmth. She turned her gorgeous, loving gaze toward Fujiwara, pouring a glass of the finest vintage wine.

小百合温柔地站在铃身旁，脸上带着极致顺从与温暖的表情，轻轻拍着盲人女孩的肩膀。接着，她将美艳深情的目光投向藤原，为他倒上了一杯顶级的珍藏美酒。

"To our magnificent trillionaire CEO," Sayuri murmured affectionately, handing him the glass. "Your genius has truly revolutionized the world. Now that you're the richest man alive, I assume your harem's daily allowance will finally be adjusted to match our newly inflated global GDP?"

“敬我们伟大的万亿富翁CEO，”小百合柔情似水地呢喃着，将酒杯递了过去，“你的天才之举真的彻底革新了整个世界。既然你已经是活着的全球首富了，我想我们的后宫日常津贴，终于也可以随着我们暴涨的全球GDP同步上调了吧？”

Chika spun around in her ergonomic chair, tossing a handful of golden stock certificates into the air with a wild giggle.

千夏则坐在人体工学椅上转了个圈，伴随着疯狂的咯咯娇笑，将一把金光闪闪的股票凭证抛向空中。

"We're richer than god!" Chika cheered. "I'm already programming the next expansion pack: 'Neural Nightmare 2.0'! Let's see how much more money we can squeeze out of humanity once we plug straight into their cerebral cortexes!"

“我们比上帝还要有钱！”千夏欢呼道，“我已经开始编程下一个扩展包了：‘神经噩梦2.0’！等我们直接接入人类的大脑皮层之后，快算算还能从全人类身上榨出多少钱来！”
