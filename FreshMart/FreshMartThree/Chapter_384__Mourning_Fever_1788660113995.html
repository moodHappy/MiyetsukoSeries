Chapter 384: Mourning Fever

发布时间：2026/9/6 10:01:52

第三百八十四章：哀悼热

By noon, the mourning queue outside Fresh Mart had formed a massive, chaotic zigzag that stretched entirely down the block.

到了中午，“新鲜玛特”门外的默哀队伍已经排成了一个巨大而混乱的之字形，一直延伸到了街区尽头。

Rin aggressively swung her metal bat against the pavement, glaring at a muscular sportsman who was trying to cut the line. She coldly warned him that if his foot moved even a single centimetre out of place, he would face immediate physical elimination.

铃极其嚣张地用金属棒球棍砸着人行道，恶狠狠地瞪着一个企图插队的肌肉发达的体育健将。她冷酷地警告他，如果他的脚敢越界哪怕一厘米，他就会立刻面临物理层面上的消灭。

Inside the store, Fujiwara was hunched over a crackling, outdated transistor radio, eagerly listening to the local news broadcast.

在杂货铺内，藤原正弓着背凑在一台滋滋作响、款式老旧的晶体管收音机前，急不可耐地收听着当地的新闻广播。

The solemn announcer reported the sudden prevalence of a mysterious "mourning fever" sweeping across the island, supposedly caused by extreme grief and lack of sleep among the loyal subjects.

神情凝重的播音员报道说，一种神秘的“哀悼热”正在全岛突然盛行，据说是由于忠诚的子民们极度悲痛和缺乏睡眠所引起的。

Fujiwara’s eyes lit up with pure capitalist inspiration. He immediately ordered Chika to mix tap water with expired vitamins to sell as a premium fake vaccination, aggressively pricing the murky liquid by the gramme.

藤原的双眼瞬间闪烁出纯粹的资本主义灵光。他立刻命令千夏把自来水和过期的维生素混合在一起，当成高端的假疫苗来兜售，并且丧心病狂地按克来为这种浑浊的液体定价。

A weeping local headmaster approached the counter, wiping his eyes with a dirty handkerchief. Sayuri leaned in seductively, offering him her new speciality: tiny vials of "Royal Tears," with the water level meticulously calculated down to the absolute last millimetre.

一位泣不成声的当地校长拿着脏兮兮的手帕擦着眼睛走近柜台。小百合妖娆地凑上前去，向他推销起自己最新的招牌特产：一小瓶一小瓶的“皇家之泪”，其水位被极其精细地算计到了绝对的最后一毫米。

Miyetsuko recorded the transactions using rapid shorthand on her tablet, noting that the store's measurable profit margins were highly noteworthy for a random Tuesday afternoon.

美悦子在平板电脑上用飞快的速记记录着交易流水，她指出，对于一个平平无奇的周二下午来说，店内这种实打实的利润率简直是极其令人瞩目的。

"We are looking at a ballpark figure of one million dollars by the end of the week," Miyetsuko stated flatly. "Additionally, turning a national tragedy into a ruthless exercise in corporate privatization proves that the manager's moral conscience remains completely underdeveloped."

“到本周末，我们大概能进账一百万美元的大致数目，”美悦子面无表情地陈述道，“此外，能把一场国家悲剧变成一场冷酷无情的企业私有化演练，这充分证明了我们店长的道德良知依然是完全发育不全的。”

Hearing the scathing insult, Fujiwara merely smiled mildly, gently patting his heavy, cash-filled pockets. To him, being diagnosed as a sociopath was just another badge of honor in the retail industry.

听到这番尖酸刻薄的侮辱，藤原只是淡淡地笑了笑，轻轻拍了拍他那沉甸甸、塞满钞票的口袋。对他而言，被确诊为反社会人格，只不过是零售业里的另一枚荣誉勋章罢了。
