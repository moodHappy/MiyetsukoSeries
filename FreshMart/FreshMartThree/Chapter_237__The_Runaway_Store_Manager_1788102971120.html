Chapter 237: The Runaway Store Manager

发布时间：2026/8/30 23:16:09

第二百三十七章：店长的离家出走

Beneath the roaring laughter and the absurd jokes about the "written consensus," a heavy, suffocating silence settled over Fujiwara’s heart once the lights went out.

在一片喧闹的哄笑声和关于“书面共识”的荒诞玩笑背后，当夜深人静、灯光熄灭时，一股沉重而窒息的孤独感悄然涌上藤原的心头。

Outwardly, he was the shameless, dominant store manager of "Fresh Mart," surrounded by gorgeous, unique women. But deep down, he was wearing a thick, agonizing "pain mask." Miyetsuko was his official wife, and Rin was his closest, most trusted playmate in all their gaming and daily mischief. Yet, the brutal reality stung like salt on an open wound: both of them had been intimately connected to Kenta—Miyetsuko's high school past and Rin's midnight esports hotel tryst.

表面上，他是被众星捧月、威风凛凛的“新鲜玛特”店长。可实际上，他的脸上正挂着一副沉重的痛苦面具。美悦子是他的正妻，铃则是他最合拍、最信任的灵魂玩伴。然而，残酷的现实却像一把尖刀刺进心窝：无论是美悦子那段不可告人的高中过往，还是铃在电竞酒店里的深夜失守，这两个对他最重要的女人，竟然都不约而同地和那个叫健太的男人产生了深刻的交集。

To Fujiwara, it felt like an unbearable betrayal. He was the one who provided shelter, food, and daily affection, yet his own household had secretly turned into a reunion spot for their shared past.

对于藤原来说，这种感觉无异于一种精神上的背叛。明明是他提供住所、提供日常照顾的家，怎么转眼间快变成别人回忆青春的集散地了？

The next morning, the living room of "Fresh Mart" was unusually quiet.

次日清晨，“新鲜玛特”的起居室里异常安静。

Sayuri walked into the kitchen, noticing that Fujiwara’s side of the bed was completely cold. Puzzled, she stepped out to check the common area, only to find a handwritten envelope resting squarely on the center of the coffee table.

小百合走进厨房，却发现藤原那侧的床铺早就冰凉一片。她疑惑地走到起居室，只见茶几的正中央静静地躺着一封手写信件。

To my ungrateful wives and housemates, the letter began in dramatic, sweeping strokes.

信封上龙飞凤舞地写着几个大字：致我那群没良心的老婆和室友们。

When you read this, I will already be gone. I have packed my broken dignity, my shattered ego, and my bleeding heart. A household where my legal wife and my best gaming partner both share a history with the same 'big, dark, and thick' transfer student is no longer a home—it is a tragedy of shared economics! Don't look for me. I have gone off to find myself... or at least a place where men aren't constantly cuckolded by childhood friends and high school ghosts.

— Yours truly, the heartbroken former store manager.

当你们看到这封信的时候，我已经离家出走了。我带走了我破碎的尊严、稀碎的自尊心以及饱受创伤的心灵。一个正妻和最好的玩伴都跟同一个‘大黑粗’有着剪不断理还乱关系的家，已经不再是避风港了，而是情感共享经济的修罗场！不用找我，我决定去寻找真正的自我……或者去找一个没有青梅竹马和高中旧情人出没的世界。

——绝望的、曾经的店长留。

Sayuri’s jaw dropped. She quickly called out to Miyetsuko, Rin, and Chika, reading the dramatic manifesto aloud.

小百合看完信，精致的眉头瞬间蹙紧，连忙把美悦子、铃和千夏全叫了过来，大声朗读了这封中二气息满满的离家出走信。

For a split second, the room was silent. Then, absolute panic broke out.

短暂的死寂过后，起居室里瞬间炸开了锅。

"Wait... he actually left?!" Rin gasped, her eyes wide with shock as she stared at the empty letter. "I was just joking around! Does he really think we care about some random high school history or a stupid midnight mistake?!"

“等一下……他来真的，居然真离家出走啦？！”铃瞪大了眼睛，手里捏着信纸惊呼出声，“我昨晚不就开个玩笑嘛！他该不会真以为我们对那些陈芝麻烂谷子的事有什么留恋吧？！”

Miyetsuko’s face turned pale. Pushing up her glasses with trembling fingers, her usual cold academic composure completely evaporated. "This is a critical systemic failure in our emotional management. If he wanders off in this state of psychological trauma, who knows what kind of reckless trouble he might get into!"

美悦子的脸唰地白了，手指颤抖着推了推眼镜，平日里的学霸冷静瞬间荡然无存：“这是我们家庭情感管理上的严重失误！如果他在这种心理创伤和应激状态下独自出门，天知道会做出什么冲动的事情来！”

Chika grabbed her coat in a panic. "What are we waiting for?! Let's split up and search the entire island before he does something stupid!"

千夏急急忙忙抓起外套：“那还等什么？！赶紧分头去找啊！东岛就这么大，掘地三尺也得把店长大人给抓回来！”

Without another word, the panicked household scattered in all directions, rushing out into the streets of Donglai Island to hunt down their runaway, dramatic store manager.

一时间，原本充满欢声笑语的“新鲜玛特”全员出动，踏上了全城搜捕离家出走店长的漫长征途。
