Chapter 327: The Inert State-of-the-Art

发布时间：2026/8/31 16:24:27

第三百二十七章：迟钝的最先进科技

Refusing to give up, Fujiwara shifted his single-minded obsession toward technology, purchasing a compilation of state-of-the-art security gadgets to protect his stockpile of imported candies.

拒绝认输的藤原将他那一门心思的痴迷转向了科技，购买了一大合集的最先进安保小工具来保护他囤积的进口糖果。

"Behold the ultimate digital defense grid! No one shall ever lay a finger on my imported gummies again!" Fujiwara declared, proudly holding up a box of plastic domes.

“见证终极数字防御网吧！再也没有人能碰我的进口软糖一根手指头了！”藤原骄傲地举起一盒塑料半球罩宣布道。

He attempted to accredit himself as a cyber-security expert, but it was self-evident that the inert plastic cameras were completely useless.

他试图将自己认证为网络安全专家，但不言而喻的是，那些迟钝死板的塑料摄像头完全没用。

"Are you seriously telling us you're a certified security expert because of some plastic toys?" a regular customer asked, raising an eyebrow at the fake lens mounted near the chips.

“你不会真觉得自己买几个塑料玩具就能当注册安全专家了吧？”一位常客挑起眉毛，看着挂在薯片区附近的假镜头质问。

The only alteration to the store was a prevalent annoyance among the non-aligned customers who felt he was trying to malign their honesty.

对店铺唯一的改变，就是在那些觉得他试图诽谤他们诚实度的中立顾客中引发了普遍的反感。

Rin, acting as a playful insurgent, decided to abduct the fake cameras and use them as toys.

铃扮演起一个调皮的叛乱分子，决定绑架那些假摄像头拿来当玩具。

"Yoink. These actually make pretty fun hockey pucks," Rin remarked casually, balancing a plastic dome on her sneaker.

“顺走咯。这东西拿来当曲棍球饼其实挺好玩的嘛，”铃漫不经心地评价道，用运动鞋颠着一个塑料半球。

When Fujiwara saw his gadgets missing, his erstwhile confidence vanished, replaced by a psychotic panic that made him hurtle through the aisles.

当藤原看到他的小玩意儿不见了时，他昔日的自信瞬间烟消云散，取而代之的是一种精神病般的恐慌，让他在过道里猛冲。

"They're gone! My technological deterrents have vanished into thin air!" Fujiwara shrieked, running in circles.

“不见了！我的科技震慑武器竟然凭空消失了！”藤原发出尖叫，在过道里团团转。

He accused the neighborhood kids of employing genetic engineering to outsmart his defenses, a claim that bordered on outright psychosis.

他指控附近的孩子们利用基因工程来智取他的防御，这种说法简直快赶上彻头彻尾的精神错乱了。

"They've evolved! The neighborhood children are using advanced genetic engineering and quantum stealth tech to bypass my perimeter!" Fujiwara panicked, grabbing his hair.

“他们进化了！这群小鬼头居然用了高级基因工程和量子隐形科技来突破我的防线！”藤原惊慌失措地抓着头发喊道。

Miyetsuko immediately intervened to toughen the store's actual policies, pointing out the obvious downside of his short-sighted investments.

美悦子立刻介入以加强店铺实际的规章制度，指出了他那些目光短浅的投资极其明显的弊端。

"Stop screeching and clean up your delusional mess," Miyetsuko ordered coldly, pinching the bridge of her nose.

“别鬼叫了，把你的妄想烂摊子收拾干净，”美悦子冷冷地命令道，捏了捏鼻梁。

She managed to acquit the disaffected children of any wrongdoing, while the eponymous Fresh Mart continued to suffer from its manager's self-conscious blunders.

她成功地宣告那些心生不满的孩子们无罪，而与招牌同名的“新鲜玛特”则继续忍受着自家店长那些扭捏作态的愚蠢失误。

"The kids didn't use quantum tech, they just picked up your trash," Miyetsuko sighed, stepping behind the counter as the eponymous Fresh Mart continued to suffer from its manager's self-conscious blunders.

“孩子们可没用什么量子科技，他们只是顺手捡了你的垃圾，”美悦子叹了口气，走回柜台后，而与招牌同名的“新鲜玛特”依然在自家店长那些扭捏作态的愚蠢失误下饱受折磨。
