第五百零二章：泡泡浴店危机

The relaxed morning in the grand salon was abruptly shattered when an impeccably suited estate runner hurried into the room, bowing so low his forehead nearly brushed the parquet floor. He was trembling as he held up an encrypted tablet displaying a flashing breaking news banner from the capital’s metropolitan press.

大起居殿内原本安逸宁静的清晨时光，被一位疾步闯入的黑西装小厮猛地打破。那小厮一路小跑进门，躬身行礼深得额头几乎要贴上擦得锃亮的人字拼木地板，双手颤巍巍地捧上一台加密平板电脑，屏幕上正赫然闪烁着都会新闻快讯的加粗猩红标题。

"Lord Fujiwara! An urgent incident in the Northern Pleasure Quarter!" the runner gasped, catching his breath. "The Metropolitan Police Department just conducted a massive dawn raid on our affiliated luxury soapland bathhouses—the Palais d'Or and The Grand Baron in the East River entertainment zone! They’ve arrested both shop managers, along with seven shift supervisors! They’re seizing the accounting books and alleging that over 4.7 billion yen in five-year revenue constitutes illicit proceeds under the Anti-Prostitution Act!"

“阁下！北区风月町出大事了！”小厮上气不接下气地慌忙禀报，“警视厅清晨突然发动突击搜查，把咱们辛迪加挂在东川娱乐特区名下的两家顶奢泡泡浴会所——‘金翼之馆’与‘大男爵’给一锅端了！三木店长和小栗店长，连同七名值班领班全被拘留了！警方正查扣账册，声称五年来近五十亿日元的营业额属于涉嫌违反《防卖春法》的非法所得！”

Fujiwara, who was reclining against a velvet chaise while leisurely sipping espresso, practically choked on his drink. His eyebrows shot up, and his face instantly darkened into an expression of thunderous, tyrannical disbelief.

正优哉游哉靠在天鹅绒贵妃榻上细品浓缩咖啡的藤原，差点被这口热咖啡当场呛住。他两道浓眉猛地倒竖，那张狂妄霸道的脸庞瞬间阴沉了下来，写满了不可置信的暴怒。

"What utter, incompetent nonsense is this?!" Fujiwara roared, slamming his porcelain cup onto the marble tabletop with a sharp clatter. "Illicit prostitution?! Those girls were recruited through our high-end, bespoke talent scouts with complete, enthusiastic consent! They signed platinum employment contracts with full medical coverage and six-figure signing bonuses! And whatever happens on those foam mats in the private suites is the pure, spontaneous chemistry between two consenting adults in a warm bath! A customer feeling deep, genuine romantic affection for the lovely maiden scrubbing his back is a sacred human emotion! How dare those badge-wearing peasants accuse my establishments of running a brothel?!"

“这他妈简直是滑天下之大稽的屁话！”藤原勃然大怒，将手里的骨瓷咖啡杯狠狠砸在昂贵的大理石桌面上，发出一声刺耳的脆响，“涉嫌违法卖春？！店里那些姑娘可都是咱们顶尖的星探团队精挑细选、你情我愿主动入职的！签的都是带全额医保和八位数安家费的白金合约！至于在包厢温水气垫床上发生的那点事，那分明是两个成年人在洗澡坦诚相见时碰撞出的纯洁爱情火花！客人对替自己擦背的温婉少女心生爱慕、情难自禁，那是神圣不可侵犯的人性真情！那帮吃官粮的泥腿子凭什么往老子的正规洗浴中心头上扣卖淫的屎盆子？！”

Without a second thought, Fujiwara snatched his encrypted satellite phone and punched in a direct line. The moment the call connected, he didn't even wait for a greeting, unleashing a barrage of unfiltered, venomous fury directly into the receiver.

藤原连半秒钟都没耽搁，一把抓过身旁的加密卫星电话，雷厉风行地拨通了一条最高直通热线。电话刚一接通，他甚至不等那头开口，狂风骤雨般的破口大骂便裹挟着雷霆之势狂轰滥炸了过去：

"Superintendent General! Are your district commanders blind, deaf, or just suffering from late-stage brain rot?!" Fujiwara bellowed into the phone. "Who authorized your dogs to kick down the doors of the East River district?! My bathhouses are licensed thermal establishments offering traditional Japanese bathing hospitality! You dare drag my managers away in cuffs over consenting adults bathing together?! You listen to me very carefully: whoever drafted that warrant had better hand in their official resignation to your desk by noon today, or I will slash your department's annual special technology budget to absolute zero and reassign your entire leadership council to traffic patrol on an offshore island! Release my staff immediately!"

“警视总监！你手下那帮辖区署长是全瞎了、聋了，还是脑浆子彻底烂光了？！”藤原对着听筒咆哮如雷，“谁借了你这帮看门狗的狗胆，敢去踹东川特区的大门？！老子的泡泡浴场是手续完备、弘扬传统大和沐浴温汤文化的正规康养场所！客人和技师情投意合洗个鸳鸯浴，你们也敢扣上手铐抓人？！你给老子听清楚了：今儿中午十二点之前，哪个没长眼的署长签的搜查令，就让他自个儿卷铺盖递辞呈滚蛋！否则老子下季度直接把你警视厅的特种技术赞助金砍到一分不剩，再把你整个警务高层全发配去外岛疏导自行车交通！立刻给老子放人！”

He slammed the phone onto the cushion, breathing heavily with offended imperial arrogance.

他把电话狠狠掼回天鹅绒软垫上，鼻孔里喷出粗重的怒气，浑身上下散发着被愚蠢凡人冒犯后的滔天狂傲。

Sayuri, draped in a sheer lavender kimono, walked over with a soft, velvet giggle, placing a gentle hand on his broad shoulder to soothe his temper.

身披一袭半透淡紫色和服的小百合软步轻移上前，唇边溢出一声甜糯娇媚的轻笑，温软柔嫩的玉手轻轻抚上他宽厚的肩膀，替他顺着心口的火气。

"Master, please don't pop a vein over a couple of local bathhouses," Sayuri purred playfully, teasing him with experienced indulgence. "Everyone in the underworld knows that the legal definition of 'spontaneous romance in a soapy tub' has always been a convenient fiction. The police probably just needed an end-of-year arrest quota to show the city council they're earning their salaries. Forty-seven hundred million yen is quite an eye-popping figure on a quarterly report, after all."

“好啦主人，快消消气，犯不上为了两家洗浴铺子急火攻心呢，”小百合软语温存地打趣着，美眸中满是老手特有的促狭与了然，“这风月行当里的规矩大家心知肚明，所谓‘水汽氤氲中擦出爱情火花’向来不过是遮人耳目的默契罢了。八成是底下那帮警官到了年底需要冲抓捕业绩，好向都议会交差证明自己没白领薪水呢。毕竟五年四十七亿日元的流水，在通报账本上确实打眼得很呢。”

Chika, who was still lounging against the sofa with her newly firmed, perky twin mounds bouncing slightly under her unbuttoned silk robe, let out a disdainful snort.

千夏这会儿正懒洋洋地斜靠在沙发里，睡袍半敞，那对刚刚做完顶级提拉、高耸挺拔的雪峰随着她的动作轻轻晃动。她不屑地轻哼了一声，小嘴撅得老高。

"Honestly, those police inspectors are completely financially illiterate," Chika scoffed, crossing her arms beneath her swollen chest. "Five billion yen over five years? That’s barely a billion a year for two mega-flagships! Between the imported Italian marble bathtubs, our custom-scented hypoallergenic lather, top-shelf champagne, and the girls' daily aesthetic upkeep, our operational overhead is astronomical! Our net profit margin is actually very modest! To call that 'exploitative organized crime profits' is an insult to proper corporate accounting!"

“依我看，那帮查案的经侦警察纯粹就是一群金融文盲，”千夏双手习惯性往胸下一托，翻了个大大的娇俏白眼，“五年才不到五十亿？两家顶级旗舰店摊下来一年也就十个亿不到！扣掉从意大利空运过来的定制大理石浴缸、特调防过敏香氛泡泡液、进口名贵香槟，还有姑娘们每天昂贵的美肤保养耗材，咱们的日常运营成本高得吓人好不好！实际净利润率其实低得很！居然把这扣上‘剥削性涉黑暴利’的帽子，简直是对咱们专业财务审计的奇耻大辱！”

Down on the plush rug, Miyetsuko rested her chin on Fujiwara’s knee, looking up with that thoroughly tamed, dewy-eyed expression that made her lethal past seem like a distant dream.

地毯上的美悦子把尖尖的下巴乖巧地枕在藤原膝头，仰起那张毫无煞气、眼波含春的精致俏脸，温顺得宛如一只彻底被驯服的小猫。

"From a territorial security standpoint, it doesn't make any sense either," Miyetsuko murmured softly, tracing a finger along the seam of his slacks. "In other prefectures, independent soaplands have to deal with territorial extortion, rival street scouts, or local yakuza syndicates shaking down the staff. But the East River district is strictly imperial property. Every street corner, every camera, and every scout agency answers directly to Master's private command. No traditional gang would dare step foot within three miles of our gates. The only people stupid enough to disrupt business are state officials who forgot whose empire they're living in."

“而且从地缘安保的角度来看，这事也荒谬得很呢，”美悦子细声细语地轻声道，纤细的指尖在他西裤缝线上轻轻划弄，“要是换作别处的杂牌野鸡浴场，少不了要被街头恶霸骚扰、争抢星探资源，或者被地盘上的黑道家族勒索保护费。可东川特区从头到脚全是主人的私人领地呀。每一条街口、每一个监控探头，连所有的星探公馆全归咱们统管，道上的极道社团哪有胆子踏进三里之内？这世上唯一蠢到敢跑来砸场子的，也就只有这帮忘了这片江山到底姓什么的糊涂差人了。”

From her cushion, blind Rin tilted her head, clutching a warm porcelain cup with angelic, succubus curiosity.

角落软垫上的盲人小铃歪了歪小脑袋，两手捧着温热的白瓷茶杯，语气里满是纯真而清奇的恶趣味。

"Um... hello, Master?" Rin piped up sweetly, blinking her unseeing eyes. "If the police officers are feeling so lonely and cranky that they have to raid the bathhouses, why don't we invite the Superintendent General to the Palais d'Or this afternoon? We can assign him our four most enthusiastic courtesans to give him an all-over bubble scrub! Once his bad mood is washed down the drain with warm suds, he will definitely write a very polite apology letter to Master!"

“唔……主人听得见吗？”小铃甜丝丝地娇笑着，眨巴着无辜的盲眼，“要是那些警察叔叔是因为自个儿太寂寞、火气太大才跑去砸场子的，那咱们今天下午干脆把警视总监请去‘金翼之馆’洗个澡好啦！安排四个最热情的姐姐，浑身打满厚厚的泡泡给他从头到脚好好搓一搓！等他浑身的邪火都被温水冲进下水道之后，他肯定会规规矩矩给主人写一封长长的小检讨呢！”

The entire salon dissolved into comfortable, wicked laughter. Fujiwara's scowl finally melted into a slow, imperial smirk. He reached down with one hand to stroke Miyetsuko's soft hair, pulling Chika onto his lap with the other, resting his chin comfortably atop her freshly pampered, fragrant assets.

满屋子顿时再度被一片惬意放肆的娇笑声所填满。藤原眉宇间的戾气终于烟消云散，嘴角重新扬起那抹不可一世的帝王坏笑。他一手向下抚摸着美悦子柔顺如丝的长发，另一只大手则霸道地将千夏揽进腿上，下巴心安理得地搁在她刚刚精炼保养、香风扑鼻的雪峰顶端。

"A sound business proposal, Rin," Fujiwara chuckled darkly, giving Chika’s waist an affectionate squeeze. "A scrub for the bureaucrats, a resignation for the precinct chief, and by this evening, the water in the baths will be running hotter than ever. Nobody disrupts the Emperor’s leisure economy."

“真是个充满建设性的商业企划啊，小铃，”藤原低沉地坏笑着，大掌在千夏软软的腰窝上用力捏了一把，“给官僚们好好搓个澡，让带头的署长麻利滚蛋，等到了今天傍晚，咱们浴场池子里的热水只会比以往烧得更旺。这片江山里，谁也休想坏了本帝王的极乐生意。”
