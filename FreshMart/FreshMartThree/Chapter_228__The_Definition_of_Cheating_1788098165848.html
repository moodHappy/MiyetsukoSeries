第二百二十八章：出轨的定义

As the clock ticked past three in the morning, heavy eyelids finally won the battle.

时间不知不觉滑向凌晨三点，困意如潮水般涌来。

Rin rubbed her tired eyes, yawned, and muttered that she needed to catch a few winks on the small sofa in the corner of the hotel room, immediately wrapping herself in a blanket and drifting off to sleep.

铃揉了揉发酸的眼睛，打了个哈欠，嘟囔着说太困了要在包间的单人沙发上眯一会儿，随后裹紧毯子迷迷糊糊地睡了过去。

In the dead of night, she felt a pair of warm arms gently wrap around her waist.

到了后半夜，迷糊中她感觉一双温热的手臂轻轻环住了她的腰。

"Who is it..." Rin mumbled sleepily, half-opening her eyes.

“干嘛呀……”铃迷迷糊糊地睁开眼，嘟囔着想要挣脱。

Kenta leaned in close, whispering with a soft, persuasive chuckle, "Relax, it's just me. We've known each other since we were kids—we're childhood friends. A little hugging and kissing between childhood friends is totally normal, right? No big deal."

健太凑到她耳边，压低了声音理直气壮地哄道：“别动，是我。咱俩可是青梅竹马从小光屁股长大的交情，这抱一抱亲一亲怎么了？青梅竹马之间搂搂抱抱，绝对合情合理，不算过分吧？”

Hearing that absurdly convenient childhood-friend logic, Rin felt a wave of strange warmth wash over her defenses. Half-asleep and unable to muster her usual sharp comebacks, she felt her cheeks burn, lowered her head, and shyly nodded, looking uncharacteristically like an obedient, quiet little sheep.

听到这套极其便利的青梅竹马歪理，铃原本清醒的大脑在深夜的困意下竟然有些宕机。她红着脸，脑子里闪过白天的斗嘴和深夜的电竞，竟然鬼使神差地红着脸点了点头，罕见地像只温顺的小绵羊般任由对方抱着。

Encouraged by her silence, Kenta pushed his luck further, his hands sliding lower as he leaned in for the ultimate step.

眼见铃没有激烈反抗，健太胆子顿时大了起来，手脚开始不老实地顺势往里探去，试图进行最后一步。

Suddenly, Rin snapped back to full awareness. She grabbed his wrist, glaring at him with wide, serious eyes.

就在这关键时刻，铃猛地一个激灵清醒了过来。她一把按住对方的手腕，睁大眼睛义正言辞地低声喝止：

"Wait! Hold on!" Rin whispered urgently. "Only this part? Absolutely not! If we cross that line, it officially counts as cheating on Fujiwara and the 'Fresh Mart' household constitution! That's crossing the line!"

“等下！唯独这个绝对不行！”铃一把推开他的胸口，满脸严肃地纠正道，“真要走到这一步，那在法理和道义上可就算实打实的出轨了！这是原则问题，绝对不能碰！”

Kenta froze, dumbfounded. "Uh... then what can we do?"

健太当场石化，一脸懵逼：“那……那你说怎么办？”

Rin blinked, her face flushed with a mixture of mischief and absurd innocence. She lowered her gaze, speaking in a soft, completely unconvincing tone of a "pure, innocent girl."

铃眨了眨眼，脸上泛起一抹复杂的羞红。她轻轻咬着下唇，用一种极其无辜、仿佛在宣读某种纯洁真理的语气说道：

"I know! I can help you with a blowjob instead," Rin whispered earnestly, looking up with big, innocent eyes. "Oral sex doesn't count as real cheating, right? It's just... helping a friend out! Totally pure!"

“要不……我帮你口交吧！”铃一本正经地拍了拍胸口，眼神清澈得像个刚进大学校门的高中生，“口交又不插进去，怎么能算真正意义上的出轨呢？这顶多算是帮老朋友解决生理困难……很单纯的！”

Before Kenta could recover from the sheer, staggering absurdity of her "pure girl logic," Rin had already slid down from the sofa, pulling him close as she wrapped her warm, soft lips around him, beginning a deeply passionate session of oral sex.

还没等健太从这套清奇无比的“纯情少女逻辑”里回过神来，铃已经主动滑下沙发，红着脸凑了过去，用温热柔软的双唇将他彻底吞没，开始了一场缠绵热烈的口交。

In the quiet, neon-lit shadows of the esports hotel room, the midnight gaming session quietly transformed into a hilariously distorted masterclass in moral gymnastics.

在这家深夜的电竞酒店包间里，原本充满电竞火药味的空气，最终彻底融化在了一场荒诞又刺激的深夜温存里。
