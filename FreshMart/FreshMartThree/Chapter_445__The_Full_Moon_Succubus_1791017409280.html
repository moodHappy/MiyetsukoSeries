Chapter 445: The Full Moon Succubus

发布时间：2026/10/3 16:50:07

第四百四十五章：月圆之夜的魅魔

The next morning, golden sunlight streamed brightly into the spacious living room of the Fresh Mart penthouse.

第二天清晨，金色的阳光洒满了“新鲜玛特”顶层公寓宽敞的客厅。

Rin sat quietly at the dining table, rubbing her sleepy eyes. Her hair was delightfully messy, and her cheeks still retained a soft, flushed pink from the night before. She held a small bowl of rice porridge, sipping it in tiny, slow sips, completely unaware that the rest of the family—Sayuri, Chika, and Miyetsuko—were all gathered around, staring at her with knowing, mischievous grins.

铃安静地坐在餐桌旁，揉着惺忪的睡眼。头发乱糟糟的，脸颊上还带着昨晚留下的淡淡红晕。她端着一小碗粥，小口小口地喝着，完全没注意到周围的几个人——小百合、千夏和美悦子正围坐在一起，带着心照不宣的坏笑偷偷打量着她。

"Well, well, look who's up," Chika giggled, propping her chin on her hand. "Rin-chan, I heard you had a little plumbing accident again last night."

“哎呀呀，看看是谁醒啦，”千夏单手托着下巴咯咯娇笑，“小铃啊，听说你昨晚又水漫金山了哦。”

Rin paused, blinking her unseeing eyes with innocent confusion. She tilted her head, her voice soft and drowsy. "Um... did I? Maybe... I think the blanket felt a little wet when I woke up..."

铃动作一顿，空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，歪着头软绵绵地嘟囔：“唔……有吗？好像……好像醒来的时候被子确实有点湿……”

Miyetsuko let out a sharp, sultry chuckle, crossing her arms with a teasing smirk. "A little wet? Miyetsuko heard Fujiwara getting up at dawn to strip the bedsheets and move you to the next room. That private midnight moving ritual of yours is getting quite legendary, you know."

美悦子发出一声性感而戏谑的轻笑，双手抱胸调侃道：“只是有点湿？我可是听见藤原大清早起来换床单、把你抱到隔壁房间的动静了。你们俩这半夜三更乾坤大挪移的专属仪式，现在可是越来越有传奇色彩了啊。”

Rin lowered her head, hiding her blushing face behind her porridge bowl, her ears turning a bright, adorable crimson.

铃把头往下埋了埋，试图用粥碗挡住自己通红的小脸，两只小耳朵红得快要滴血。

"Don't tease her too much, you two," Sayuri intervened with a sweet, musical giggle, though her eyes were twinkling with the exact same playful amusement. "After all, today is the morning after the full moon. We all know why our mighty manager is currently a complete wreck."

“别把她逗得太害羞啦，你们俩，”小百合带着甜美悦耳的娇笑打圆场，不过她眼底那抹揶揄的笑意却一点也不比别人少，“毕竟，今天可是月圆之夜后的早晨嘛。大家心里都清楚，咱们那位不可一世的经理今天为什么到现在还瘫在床上起不来。”

Chika's eyes sparkled with mischievous excitement. "Oh, absolutely! The legendary 'Full Moon Succubus' mode! It only happens a dozen times a year, but when it does, our trillionaire boss cancels every single global crisis, ignores all international stock markets, and locks himself in the bedroom with Rin for twenty-four hours straight."

千夏的眼睛兴奋得直发光：“对呀！传说中的‘完全体月圆魅魔’模式嘛！一年也就那么十来次，可一到这天，咱们的万亿大老板就把什么全球危机、国际股市全抛到脑后，跟铃关在房间里没日没夜地大战二十四个小时。”

"And looking at how exhausted Fujiwara is right now," Miyetsuko added, smirk widening, "he probably got sucked dry so thoroughly he won't be able to walk for the next three days."

“看藤原现在这副虚脱的样，”美悦子坏笑着补充道，“昨晚怕是连骨髓都被吸干了，接下来三天能不能下床走路都成问题咯。”

Rin buried her face completely behind the bowl, her voice coming out as a tiny, muffled whimper. "Mmm... I... I wasn't that bad... It's just... during the full moon, I can't really control myself..."

铃干脆把整张脸都埋在了碗后面，闷声闷气的辩解声软得像在撒娇：“唔……才……才没有那么夸张……只是……月圆的时候，人家实在控制不住嘛……”

Before the teasing could continue, a faint, exhausted groan echoed from the master bedroom down the hall.

还没等她们继续打趣，走廊尽头的主卧室里突然传出一声虚弱无力的呻吟。

"...Rin... water..."

“……铃……水……”

Fujiwara’s voice was remarkably hoarse, dripping with the absolute fatigue of a man who had just survived an all-night succubus marathon.

藤原的声音沙哑得厉害，透着一种刚经历过整晚魅魔马拉松后劫后余生的虚脱感。

The family burst into a chorus of smothered, delighted laughter.

客厅里的几个女人顿时忍俊不禁，笑作一团。

"Go on, little succubus," Sayuri teased gently, patting Rin's back. "Your master is calling. Better go feed him some water before he completely withers away."

“去吧，小魅魔，”小百合温柔地拍了拍铃的后背调笑道，“你家主人在叫你了。快去喂他喝点水吧，不然咱们的万亿首富今天可真要在床上枯萎啦。”

Rin set down her porridge bowl with a soft clink, scrambling out of her chair. As she hurried down the hallway toward the bedroom, she muttered shyly to herself:

铃把粥碗轻轻放下，慌慌张张地从椅子上站起身。她小跑着朝卧室走去，一边走还一边红着脸小声嘟囔：

"I'm... I'm not a succubus... I'm just a good manager..."

“才……才不是魅魔……人家只是个称职的管理员……”

Behind her, the living room erupted into peals of joyful, unadulterated domestic laughter.

在她身后，客厅里再次爆发出欢乐而温馨的笑声。
