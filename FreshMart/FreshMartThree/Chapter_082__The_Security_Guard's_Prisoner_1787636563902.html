Chapter 82: The Security Guard's Prisoner

发布时间：2026/8/25 13:42:42

第八十二章：保安室的俘虏

As the clock ticked past midnight, the ambient chatter in the back room finally died down. Miyetsuko covered her mouth with the back of her hand, suppressing a polite, clinical yawn.

随着时钟指向深夜，后室里的闲聊声渐渐平息。美悦子用手背掩住嘴，优雅地打了个克制而标准的哈欠。

"My cognitive functions are experiencing a noticeable decline due to sleep deprivation," Miyetsuko announced, standing up and adjusting her glasses. "I am retiring for the evening. Do not disturb my sleep cycle, Fujiwara."

“由于睡眠剥夺，我的认知功能正在显著下降，”美悦子站起身，推了推眼镜宣布道，“我要回房睡觉了。藤原，别试图打扰我的睡眠周期。”

Rin stretched her arms lazily overhead, letting out a heavy, contented sigh as she stumbled toward the doorway. "Yeah, I'm completely wiped out too. All that psychological warfare on screen made me sleepy. Have fun playing director, boss."

铃懒洋洋地伸了个大大的懒腰，打了个满足的哈欠，摇摇晃晃地朝门外走去。“行啦，我也彻底困了。看了一晚上的心理战弄得我都犯困了。老板，祝你今晚‘导演’愉快。”

With soft clicks of closing doors, the hallway fell completely silent, leaving just the quiet hum of the refrigerator and the two of them alone in the warm dimness of the back room.

随着房门轻轻合上的轻响，走廊里彻底安静了下来，只剩下冰箱低沉的嗡嗡声，以及后室温暖暗光中独处两人的呼吸声。

Fujiwara turned his head slowly, his dark, burning eyes locking onto Sayuri. The residual heat of ATID-309 still hung heavy in the air, charging the atmosphere with a dangerous, electric tension.

藤原缓缓转过头，幽暗炽热的目光死死锁定了小百合。刚才那部影片残存的余温依然在空气中弥漫，让整个房间充斥着危险而紧绷的张力。

"You heard the critics, my queen," Fujiwara murmured, his voice dropping to a husky, commanding purr as he reached out and gently traced the line of her jaw. "The industry might be in a steady decline, but I happen to know we have the greatest legendary actress of all time right here in this room."

“你听见几位评委怎么说了吧，我的女王大人，”藤原低声呢喃，声音沙哑而充满掌控力，伸手轻轻勾勒着她的下巴线条，“虽然整个行业在走下坡路，但我刚好知道，这屋里正坐着有史以来最伟大的传奇女优。”

Sayuri's breath hitched. She looked up at him through her lashes, her heart hammering against her ribs as she instantly recognized the dangerous gleam in his eyes.

小百合的呼吸微微一窒。她透过长长的睫毛抬头看着他，心脏在胸腔里剧烈跳动，瞬间读懂了他眼底那危险的炽热。

"You... you want to reenact it?" Sayuri whispered, her voice trembling slightly with a delicious mix of nervous anticipation and deep, submissive thrill.

“你……你想复刻那场戏？”小百合轻声呢喃，声音微微颤抖，夹杂着一丝紧张的期待与极致顺从的兴奋。

"Not just reenact it," Fujiwara growled softly, standing up and towering over her as he backed her slowly toward the wall. "I want a live, uncut performance. You play the cornered, helpless captive caught in the security office, and I'll play the ruthless, heavy-handed guard who refuses to let you go."

“不只是复刻，”藤原低吼着站起身，高大的身躯带着压迫感步步逼近，将她缓缓逼退到墙角，“我要一场现场直播、无删减的完整演出。你来演那个被困在保安室里、走投无路的绝望俘虏，而我来演那个不打算放过你的粗暴恶劣保安。”

A shiver of pure excitement ran down Sayuri's spine. Instead of cowering, a brilliant, professional spark lit up her soft eyes. If anyone in the world could deliver an Oscar-worthy performance of forced compliance, absolute humiliation, and melting surrender, it was her.

一股战栗般的兴奋顺着小百合的脊椎蔓延开来。她不仅没有畏缩，反而双眸中亮起了一抹属于顶级职业选手的耀眼光芒。如果说世界上有谁能把那种强制顺从、极致屈辱与彻底融化的沦陷演绎到奥斯卡级别，那绝对非她莫属。

"Understood, director," Sayuri whispered back, a wicked, innocent smile touching her lips as she leaned into his heavy embrace. "Let's see if your direction... can actually break me."

“遵命，导演大人，”小百合回以一个既清纯又带点坏笑的眼神，主动贴进他宽厚滚烫的怀抱里，“那就让我看看，你的导演功力……到底能不能把我彻底折服吧。”
