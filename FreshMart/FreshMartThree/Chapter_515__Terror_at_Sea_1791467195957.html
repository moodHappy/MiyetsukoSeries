Chapter 515: Terror at Sea

发布时间：2026/10/8 21:46:34

第五百一十五章：海上惊魂

The blazing midday sun cast shimmering reflections across the open waters of the high seas. On the expansive bow deck of the 15,000-ton luxury superyacht, the field of view was immensely vast, with sea breezes howling past and the air thick with an intertwined scent of salt, moisture, and utter decadence.

正午的烈日将公海的海面晒得波光粼粼。在一万五千吨级超级豪华邮轮开阔的船头甲板上，视野极度开阔，海风呼啸而过，空气中弥漫着咸湿与奢靡交织的气息。

Yet right beside the teak railing at the very front of this floating palace, an absurd romantic farce unfolded, utterly discordant with the magnificent seascape.

然而，这艘海上宫殿最前端的柚木栏杆旁，却上演着一幕与宏伟海景格格不入的荒诞春宫。

Chika had her hands tightly gripping the cold ship railing, her entire body bent forward at a ninety-degree angle as her legs trembled slightly. Behind her, Fujiwara was aggressively launching a fierce rear-entry assault. Those two full, oversized, soft and warm milk-swollen peaks—heavy from round-the-clock high yields—were being playfully and mischievously tugged from behind by Fujiwara. Under the intense stretching, they were forcibly elongated into hilarious, exaggerated long rugby-ball shapes, swinging wildly in mid-air with every brutal thrust.

千夏正双手死死扣住冰凉的船舷栏杆，整个人呈九十度向前躬身，双腿微微发颤。在她身后，藤原正肆意地从后方发动着凶猛的突袭。那两只饱满硕大、因终日高产而软糯温热的雪峰，此刻被藤原从后面恶趣味地狠拽着，在强烈的拉扯下，竟然硬生生被拉伸成了两颗滑稽而夸张的长形橄榄球形状，随着每一次粗暴的撞击而在半空中疯狂晃荡。

"Ah... slow, slower, Boss!" Chika flushed crimson, enduring the overwhelming ecstasy deep within while dying of shy mortification, her voice thick with a heavy nasal whine as she complained. "Don't—don't pull my breasts so hard anymore! They're going to get deformed... If the imperial physicians see this later, they'll definitely say I failed to maintain them properly! What are we going to do if their elasticity gets ruined and they can't bounce back to their original shape?!"

“呀啊……慢、慢一点啊老板！”千夏红着脸颊，一边承受着深处灭顶的快感，一边羞耻得快要哭出来，声音带着浓浓的鼻音娇嗔道，“别、别再使劲拽我的奶子了！都要被你拉变形了啦……要是被那四位太监御医看到，回头非得说我保养不当不可！要是弹性被你扯坏了、恢复不成原样了可怎么办呀！”

Fujiwara glanced down at the two grotesquely stretched mounds of supreme softness in his hands. Instead of letting go, he maliciously molded them into various shapes, throwing his head back with arrogant, domineering laughter from his position of absolute supremacy. "Deformed? What is there to fear? If my private breast treasury can't even handle this bit of physical stretching, those four eunuch masters drawing multi-million-dollar salaries would be useless! Besides, a rugby ball shape is so sporty—it matches today's nautical theme perfectly!"

藤原低头看着手里那两团被扯得变了形的极品软肉，不仅没有松手，反而恶劣地捏成各种形状，居高临下地狂妄大笑道：“变形？怕什么！朕的私人美胸库要是连这点物理拉伸都应付不了，那四位拿亿万年薪的净身大师岂不是白拿了！再说了，橄榄球形状多有运动感，正好配得上今天的航海主题！”

Just as Chika, flustered and embarrassed, was about to turn her head and snap back, an ear-piercing roar of motors suddenly drifted across the distant azure waves.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

就在千夏又羞又急、正准备扭头回骂之际，远处的碧波之上，忽然传来了一阵刺耳的马达轰鸣声。

砰！砰！砰！

Several muffled and abrupt gunshots tore through the sea breeze, and multiple bullets with sharp whistling sounds skimmed past the yacht's hull, shattering the glass railing along the edge of the deck.

几声沉闷而突兀的枪响撕裂了海风，数颗子弹带着尖锐的呼啸声擦着邮轮的船舷飞掠而过，击碎了甲板边缘的玻璃护栏。

Several high-speed motorboats flying black flags, carrying over a dozen maritime armed pirates wielding assault rifles, had somehow flanked them viciously from the other side of the horizon. The pirate leader even used a megaphone, howling arrogantly in the gale, "Cut the engines! This is a robbery! Hand over all valuables and women on board, you rich bastards!"

几艘挂着黑旗、载着十几个端着突击步枪的海上武装盗贼的高速快艇，不知何时从海平面的另一端恶狠狠地包抄了过来。领头的海盗甚至拿着扩音喇叭，在大风中狂妄地嘶喊：“停船！打劫！船上的有钱佬把值钱的东西和女人全都交出来！”

The air froze for a full second.

空气凝固了一秒。

Chika turned pale with fright, letting out a shriek as she nearly collapsed limply onto the deck. "Ah! Pirates! Gunshots! Boss, they're shooting at us!"

千夏吓得花容失色，尖叫一声差点软倒在甲板上：“呀！有海盗！有枪声！老板，他们朝咱们开枪了！”

Yet, facing the sudden maritime assault, Fujiwara didn't even pause his movements at his waist. He merely frowned with annoyance, expelling a dismissive, utterly contemptuous cold snort from his nose.

然而，面对突如其来的海上袭击，藤原甚至连腰上的动作都没停下来，只是不爽地皱了皱眉，从鼻腔里喷出一声极其轻蔑的冷哼。

"Where on earth did these blind, bottom-feeders crawl out from, daring to hijack the Emperor's ship?" Fujiwara sneered, glancing sideways at those clueless speedboats in the distance. "Didn't Miyetsuko say this ship is equipped with a full automated close-in defense system?"

“哪来的不长眼的底层杂碎，连本帝王的船也敢劫？”藤原冷笑着瞥了一眼远处那几艘不知死活的快艇，“美悦子不是说过吗？这艘船上装了全套的自动化近防系统。”

Almost the exact second his words fell, a piercing mechanical shriek of turning gears exploded high above the calm, luxurious superyacht.

Clack—clack!

几乎就在话音落下的瞬间，原本平静奢华的超级邮轮上空，机械齿轮转动的刺耳轰鸣声轰然炸响。

咔嚓——咔嚓！

The seemingly upscale teak and white decorative panels on both sides of the yacht's upper deck instantly slid outward. Several concealed, fully automatic titanium close-in weapon turrets and multi-barreled Gatling machine guns slowly rose, gleaming with a freezing, lethal cold light as their radar-locking system instantly locked onto those speeding motorboats.

Da-da-da-da-da-da—!

只见邮轮上层甲板两侧那看似高级的柚木和白色装饰板瞬间向两侧滑开，几座隐藏式的全自动钛合金近防炮塔与多管加特林机枪带着冰冷的死亡寒光缓缓升起，雷达锁定系统瞬间锁死了那几艘狂奔的快艇。

哒哒哒哒哒哒——！

Spitting tongues of fire, accompanied by deafening metal roars, those aggressive maritime speedboats didn't even have time to scream before they were ripped into countless splashing fragments amidst the dense curtain of bullets, turning into a mist of blood and seawater together. The entire process took less than three seconds.

火舌喷吐。伴随着震耳欲聋的金属轰鸣，那几艘气势汹汹的海上快艇甚至连惨叫都没来得及发出，直接在密集的弹雨中被撕成了无数飞溅的碎片，连带着海水一起被轰成了漫天血雾。整个过程不到三秒钟。

The gunfire on the deck abruptly ceased. Only burning wreckage and slowly sinking wood chips remained across the sea surface.

甲板上的枪声戛然而止。海面上只剩下一片燃烧的残骸和徐徐下沉的木屑。

Under the parasol, Sayuri elegantly sipped her cocktail without batting an eyelid. Sitting cross-legged on the rug, Miyetsuko calmly retracted her tactical tablet. Meanwhile, blind Rin by the pool clapped her small hands and shrieked with cheerful glee.

遮阳伞下的小百合优雅地抿了一口鸡尾酒，连眼皮都没抬一下；盘腿坐在地毯上的美悦子淡定地收回了手里的战术平板电脑；而泳池边的盲人小铃则欢快地拍着小手尖叫。

Continuing his ferocious thrusts from beneath, Fujiwara looked down at the trembling, panic-stricken Chika in his arms and grinned wickedly. "See that, little milk cow? Even pirates are rushing over to serve as fireworks for our nautical party. Now, we can resume our 'rugby ball stretching experiment'..."

藤原一边继续着腰底下的凶猛冲刺，一边低头看着怀里惊魂未定的千夏，坏笑道：“看吧，小奶牛，连海盗都急着赶来给咱们的航海派对当烟花看呢。现在，咱们可以继续刚才的‘橄榄球拉伸实验’了……”
