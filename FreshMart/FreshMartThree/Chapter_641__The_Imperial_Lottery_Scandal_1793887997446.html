Chapter 641: The Imperial Lottery Scandal

发布时间：2026/11/5 22:13:15

第六百四十一章：御批抽奖风波

A sensational marketing campaign launched by nineteen-year-old galactic mega-influencer Pan Duomei sent shockwaves across the entire imperial network.

十九岁的星际顶流女网红潘多美在全星网发起的一场狂热抽奖，迅速在整个帝国引发了轩然大波。

She announced a grand lottery to select one lucky follower for an all-expenses-paid, intimate luxury getaway to the legendary Valley of Ecstasy, the empire's most romantic resort planet.

她在全网高调宣布，将随机抽取一名幸运粉丝，由她全额承担所有奢华开销，一同前往帝国最浪漫、最负盛名的度假圣地“逍遥谷”共度良宵。

Within minutes, millions of lonely netizens and hopeless otaku across thousands of star systems flooded the lottery terminals, frantically reposting the event in desperate hopes of winning the maiden's company.

短短数分钟内，来自数千个星系的亿万光棍网民与狂热死宅蜂拥挤爆了网络终端，疯狂转发跟帖，无不渴望能有幸一亲芳泽。

Just as the frenzy reached a fever pitch, Emperor Fujiwara issued an emergency imperial decree, abruptly shutting down the entire sweepstakes.

正当这场网络狂欢推向顶峰之际，藤原大帝却突然降下一道紧急圣旨，以迅雷不及掩耳之势直接叫停了整场抽奖活动。

The official imperial edict sternly condemned the event, labeling it a "blatant act of moral degeneracy that corrodes the public order and pollutes the spiritual fabric of imperial society."

官府发布的明昭上措辞严厉，痛斥该活动“有伤风化、道德败坏，严重扰乱社会公共秩序，毒害帝国社会风气”。

While the public applauded the throne's apparent dedication to civic virtue, the reality behind closed doors told a completely different story.

正当民间还在为朝廷雷厉风行整肃社会风气拍手称快时，紧闭的深宫深处却在上演着截然相反的戏码。

Under cover of darkness, the imperial secret police intercepted Pan Duomei's private transport and escorted the terrified beauty directly into the Emperor's secluded pavilion.

夜幕掩护之下，大内密探早已在半道上扣下了潘多美的私人飞艇，将这位惊慌失措的娇柔美人一路秘密押送进了皇帝的深宫寝殿。

Clad in a delicate gown that failed to conceal her youth and supple curves, the young influencer knelt trembling before the obsidian dais, pleading for clemency.

少女身着一袭单薄的轻纱华服，根本遮掩不住青春娇嫩的曼妙身姿，战战兢兢地跪倒在黑曜石台阶前，垂泪乞求圣恩宽恕。

Fujiwara, however, stepped down from his seat with a broad, self-satisfied grin, loosening his dragon robes as his imperial lust surged unrestrained.

藤原大帝却带着一脸得意忘形的坏笑大步走下王座，随手扯开龙袍衣带，满腔的占有欲毫不掩饰地勃发而出。

"Fomenting social unrest with your charms is a capital offense against public decency," Fujiwara proclaimed solemnly, lifting her tear-stained chin with a calloused finger. "However, the Emperor is famously benevolent. If you serve the Crown dutifully in bed tonight, your transgressions shall be expunged completely."

“仗着生得倾国倾城便在民间惹是生非，按律可是破坏风化的大罪，”藤原粗糙的手指挑起她挂满泪珠的下巴，义正辞严地信口胡诌，“念在尔年少无知，朕向来宽大为怀。只要今夜在榻上好生伺候龙体、将功补过，朕便赦你无罪。”

What followed behind the silken bed curtains was an uninterrupted night of sovereign indulgence, with the weeping influencer forced to cater to the Emperor's every whim.

随后锦帐垂落的整整一夜，尽是君王的霸道索取，娇滴滴的小网红被迫百依百顺地曲意逢迎，承受着龙榻之上的肆意蹂躏。

By morning, the young beauty was thoroughly depleted, having spent hours kneeling to perform the most humiliating and intimate acts of appeasement to satisfy the sovereign's legendary appetite.

直至天明，少女早已浑身酥软、瘫软如泥，整夜被拘在枕榻之间极尽承欢伺候，方才勉强喂饱了暴君那无底洞般的狂暴欲望。

In the adjacent salon, the imperial consorts sipping their morning tea looked upon the spectacle with their customary cynical amusement.

偏殿内，正端着早茶的后宫众美隔帘瞧着这出闹剧，唇角尽是见怪不怪的讥诮之色。

"Shutting down a public lottery for 'public decency' only to confiscate the prize for your own bedchamber," Chika remarked with a loud snort, shaking her bountiful chest. "You really have zero shame, don't you, Boss?"

“前脚义正辞严地骂人家‘败坏风化’给封了，后脚就把头奖直接顺进自个儿被窝里独吞，”千夏翻了个白眼，托着沉甸甸的胸脯嗤笑道，“老板，您老人家这面皮可真是比城墙拐弯还厚啊！”

Sayuri folded her velvet fan gracefully, offering a cold, calculating smirk. "A classic imperial monopoly. Why let the unruly masses fight over limited luxury resources when the state can nationalize the entire asset for executive use?"

小百合优雅地合拢丝绒折扇，唇角勾起一抹冷艳深邃的弧度：“经典的帝国垄断做派。既然是不可多得的优质美色资源，与其任由市井贱民争抢引发骚动，不如由朝廷直接将其充公收归御用，岂非更合统筹之道？”

Miyetsuko adjusted her 183828号 Death God glasses, noting the incident down with administrative precision. "Case logged: Public gathering dispersed, regulatory fines waived, private compensation collected in full. An exceptionally efficient closure."

美悦子推了推鼻梁上的183828号死神眼镜，一本正经地在玉牒上提笔落款：“卷宗归档：非法集会已取缔，从轻免予罚款，涉案实体资产已足额肉偿入库。结案手续极为利落。”

Rin chuckled, swishing her succubus tail as she peeked past the drapery. "The poor girl wanted to travel to the Valley of Ecstasy, but she ended up stranded on the Emperor's mattress instead!"

小铃摇晃着尾巴，掀开珠帘探头探脑地嘻嘻直笑：“这可怜的小妮子原本还打算带人去逍遥谷快活呢，结果一头撞进了陛下的龙榻陷阱，这下可真成了‘彻底逍遥’啦！”

Fujiwara lounged back on the obsidian throne, sipping his warm wine without a single trace of remorse. "Governance is about preempting civil unrest! The Emperor merely took one for the team."

藤原慵懒地仰靠在黑曜石王座上，美滋滋地抿了一口温热的琼浆，毫不知耻地笑道：“治国之道在于防微杜渐！朕这是替天下苍生排忧解难，何错之有啊！”
