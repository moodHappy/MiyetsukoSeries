Chapter 239: The Runaway's Paradise

发布时间：2026/8/30 23:31:28

第二百三十九章：离家出走的“绝望”

Powered by a frantic mix of panic, guilt, and sheer determination, the four women rushed through the neon-lit streets of Donglai Island like an elite tactical squad on a rescue mission.

在一股混合着慌乱、愧疚与熊熊怒火的复杂情绪驱使下，四位女侠犹如一支精锐的战术突击小队，风风火火地穿梭在东莱岛霓虹闪烁的街道上。

Led by Miyetsuko’s academic GPS and Sayuri’s underground contacts, they finally burst through the sliding doors of "Moonlight Haven," the island’s most exclusive high-end entertainment club. Following the frantic directions of a terrified, sweating manager, they sprinted up to the VIP corridor on the top floor and locked onto Room 808.

在美悦子的精准定位和小百合的人脉指引下，她们终于冲进了东莱岛最高档的风俗会所“月光港湾”。在满头大汗的领班战战兢兢的指引下，四人一路狂奔至顶楼的VIP走廊，最终锁定了一扇镶金的豪华包间——808室。

Rin’s chest heaved as she stared at the heavy wooden door. Remembering how she had panicked all afternoon, worrying that her runaway store manager might do something drastic or self-destructive, a surge of fiery anger boiled up inside her.

铃站在厚重的实木大门前，气喘吁吁地挺起胸口。一想到自己这一整天提心吊胆、生怕这位离家出走的店长大人想不开去跳海，一股无名火瞬间直冲脑门。

"That suicidal idiot..." Rin snarled, her knuckles whitening. "I'm going to beat him black and blue!"

“这个该死的混蛋，居然敢害老娘白白担心一下午……”铃咬牙切齿地捏紧拳头，“看我今天不把他揍得生活不能自理！”

Bang!

砰！

Without a second warning, Rin raised her foot and delivered a vicious, textbook roundhouse kick right into the center of the door, bursting it wide open in a cloud of imaginary dust.

伴随着一声巨响，铃根本没有敲门的意思，抬腿就是一记势大力沉的暴力踹门，直接把VIP包间的厚重大门给轰然踹了开来！

"Fujiwara! You absolute moron, get your ass back home right now—!" Rin roared, storming into the room with fists raised.

“藤原！你个脑残智障，立刻给老娘滚回……”铃一边挥舞着拳头一边怒吼着冲了进去。

The rest of the squad surged in right behind her, ready to tackle, scold, or comfort their runaway man. But the exact microsecond they crossed the threshold, all four of them froze dead in their tracks, their jaws dropping so low they practically scraped the carpet.

其余三人也紧随其后蜂拥而入，准备好了一肚子或责骂或安慰的话语。然而，就在她们踏入房间的那一纳秒，四人脸上的表情瞬间凝固，下巴齐刷刷地掉到了地上，眼睛瞪得比铜铃还大。

The spacious VIP room wasn't a dark, gloomy cell of depression. Instead, it looked like the throne room of a decadent emperor.

想象中凄凉、绝望、借酒消愁的画面根本不存在。相反，眼前的豪华包间奢华得宛如荒淫无度的古代宫殿。

Fujiwara was reclining comfortably in the center of a plush leather sofa, a glass of expensive whiskey in one hand, while three of the club’s absolute top-tier, stunningly gorgeous hostesses swarmed around him in various states of scandalous undress, feeding him grapes, whispering sweet nothings, and engaged in a wild, dizzying whirlwind of passionate intimacy.

只见藤原正舒舒服服地斜靠在中央的真皮大沙发上，手里端着一杯昂贵的威士忌。而在他身旁，会所里身段妖娆、风情万种的三位顶级头牌正衣衫凌乱地将他环绕其中——一个喂着水果，一个揉着肩膀，现场正进行着一场活色生香、颠鸾倒凤的极乐狂欢。

"Oh? Hey girls, you're just in time," Fujiwara blinked lazily, not even bothering to adjust his shirt as he looked at the four pale, paralyzed women standing by the ruined doorway. He took a slow sip of his drink and flashed a cheeky, unbothered grin. "Relax, don't look so shocked. I was just... you know, conducting some vital stress-relief research of my own. No need to worry about me. I'll head back home tomorrow morning, honest."

“哟？这不是几位夫人嘛，来得正好，”藤原懒洋洋地眨了眨眼，甚至连衬衫领口都懒得系上，看着门口四个宛如石化般的女人，慢条斯理地抿了一口酒，露出一副无比欠揍的潇洒笑容，“别这么大惊小怪的嘛。我这不就是在进行一些必要的‘心理压力疏导实地调研’嘛。不用担心我，明天一早我就乖乖回家了哈。”

Before any of them could recover their speech or process the sheer audacity of what they were witnessing, one of the gorgeous hostesses giggled musically. She gracefully stood up, walked over to the door, and with a sweet, polite smile, gently shut it right in their faces.

还没等她们找回自己的声音、彻底消化眼前这震撼的一幕，其中一位风情万种的头牌美女便轻笑一声，优雅地站起身来走到门前，冲着目瞪口呆的四人甜甜一笑，然后“咔哒”一声，非常善解人意地把大门重新关上了。

Click.

门关上了。

Standing out in the dim, quiet hallway, Sayuri, Miyetsuko, Rin, and Chika stared at the closed wood panel in stunned disbelief.

站在昏暗安静的走廊里，小百合、美悦子、铃和千夏四人面面相觑，脑子里一片空白。

All that panic. All that desperate, island-wide mobilization of thousands of people, academic elites, underground contacts, and gamer armies. All those tears and frantic heartbeats because they thought their beloved store manager had run away with a broken heart.

折腾了一下午。全城动员了数千人，发动了学术界、风俗圈、电竞圈和大半个大学的人脉。甚至连眼泪都差点急出来了，以为自家店长大人遭受了毁灭性打击、脆弱的心灵已经彻底碎成了渣。

As it turned out, the bastard was living his absolute best, most carefree life surrounded by a harem of club queens.

结果倒好，这死变态居然躲在最高档的风俗店里左拥右抱，潇洒得简直快要上天了！
