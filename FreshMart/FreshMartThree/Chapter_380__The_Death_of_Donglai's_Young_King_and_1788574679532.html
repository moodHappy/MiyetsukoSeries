Chapter 380: The Death of Donglai's Young King and the Succession Crisis

发布时间：2026/9/5 10:17:57

第三百八十章：东莱国王驾崩与秘密继承人

After several days of relentless, back-breaking labor—ranging from scrubbing ketchup crime scenes to demolishing floor tiles and wearing industrial-grade nappies—the five housemates finally enjoyed a rare, peaceful evening. Lounging across the living room in comfortable clothes, they sipped hot tea and watched the national evening news broadcast on the glowing television screen.

经过连续几天惨烈无比的重体力劳动——从刷洗番茄酱凶案现场到暴力拆迁地砖，再到被迫穿上工业级纸尿裤——五个人终于迎来了难得的一个平静夜晚。大家换上舒适的便服懒洋洋地斜倚在客厅的沙发上，一边啜饮着热茶，一边盯着电视屏幕上播放的晚间全国新闻。

"Breaking news," the news anchor announced with solemn, dramatic gravity. "The body of Donglai Island's beloved traditional monarch, the world's youngest reigning monarch, has been flown home after passing away in the United States at the age of thirty-four following a private battle with an aggressive form of cancer."

“突发新闻，”新闻主播神情凝重、语气极具戏剧张力地播报道，“东莱岛备受爱戴的传统君王、全球最年轻的在位君主，在美利坚因罹患晚期癌症经私人治疗无效不幸逝世，享年三十四岁。目前，其遗体已通过专机运回国内。”

On the screen, footage showed military helicopters transporting the casket to the royal palace, accompanied by weeping crowds and national flags flying at half-mast.

屏幕上播放着军用直升机将灵柩运往王宫的画面，沿途挤满了痛哭流涕的民众，全国降半旗致哀。

"Wait, the king is dead?" Chika blinked, tilting her head in bewilderment. "Didn't he ascend the throne when he was only three years old? I remember buying commemorative plastic crowns for his coronation anniversary when I was in kindergarten."

“等等，国王驾崩了？”千夏眨了眨眼，一脸茫然地歪着头，“他不是三岁就登基了吗？我记得我上幼儿园的时候还买过他登基周年的塑料纪念王冠呢。”

"Indeed," Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, her eyes reflecting the blue light of the screen. "He ruled over two million subjects as the thirteenth traditional monarch. However, his passing has instantly triggered a massive constitutional and cultural crisis."

“确实如此，”美悦子推了推眼镜，镜片上闪烁着屏幕的幽蓝光芒，“作为第十三代传统君主，他统治着超过两百万的子民。然而，他的离世瞬间引发了一场巨大的宪政与文化危机。”

The news anchor continued, shifting to a grim tone. "His death has sparked fierce uncertainty over the future of the monarchy. While the kingdom's Prime Minister publicly claims that the late king left behind a young son whose identity will soon be revealed, the head of the royal clan has angrily denied any knowledge of such an heir, creating an unprecedented public rift between senior government officials and traditional elders."

电视画面切换，主播的语气变得严峻起来：“他的离世引发了外界对王室未来的极度不确定。尽管王国内阁首相公开宣称，已故国王留下一名幼子，其身份将在稍后公布；但王室宗族长老会首领却愤怒地表示对此子闻所未闻，这导致高级政府官员与传统宗族长老之间爆发了前所未有的公开裂痕。”

Fujiwara, who was casually massaging his still-sore lower back, suddenly stopped. His eyes widened as the gears in his opportunistic capitalist brain started spinning at Mach speed.

正揉着依然酸痛后腰的藤原突然停下了动作。随着他那颗充满投机倒把气息的资本家大脑以马赫速度飞速运转，他的双眼瞬间亮了起来。

"Wait a minute..." Fujiwara leaned forward, a greedy grin spreading across his face. "A royal death? A nationwide nine-day mourning period? Flags at half-mast? And a dramatic succession feud over a secret heir?!"

“等一下……”藤原身体前倾，贪婪的笑容在脸上蔓延开来，“国王驾崩？全国九天国丧期？降半旗？还有为争夺秘密继承人而引发的豪门宫斗大戏？！”

"Where there is national grief, there is immense retail potential," Sayuri chimed in, her eyes sparkling with professional theatrical appreciation. She adjusted her posture on the sofa, careful not to jostle her still-tender hips. "A secret royal heir? Oh, that is prime soap-opera material. I wonder if the mother is a mysterious barmaid or an undercover spy."

“哪里有全民悲痛，哪里就有巨大的零售商机，”小百合接口道，眼中闪烁着对戏剧素材的专业赞赏。她在沙发上调整了一下坐姿，免得扯到依然隐隐作痛的臀部，“秘密王室继承人？哦，这可是顶级肥皂剧的绝佳素材。我在想，孩子的母亲究竟是神秘的酒吧女招待还是卧底女间谍。”

"Shut up, you two," Rin scoffed, rolling her eyes as she tossed an empty coffee cup onto the table. "While you're dreaming of cashing in on a funeral, the government has already stepped in, warning everyone on social media not to spread speculation and to respect the crown."

“闭嘴吧你们俩，”铃没好气地翻了个白眼，随手把一个空咖啡杯扔在桌上，“就在你们盘算着怎么发国难财的时候，政府已经介入了，警告所有人在社交媒体上不准散布谣言，必须对王室保持敬畏。”

"Government warnings are merely suggestions for the uncreative," Miyetsuko dryly remarked, tapping her tablet. "However, the friction between the Prime Minister and the royal clan over the heir is a classic institutional power struggle. If the clan doesn't control the narrative of the succession, they lose their cultural hegemony."

“政府的警告对于没有创造力的人来说只是建议，”美悦子冷冷地评价道，手指在平板上敲击着，“不过，首相与宗族长老围绕继承人产生的摩擦，典型的体制权力斗争。如果宗族无法掌控继承人的叙事权，他们就会彻底失去文化霸权。”

Chika gasped, her eyes shining with innocent excitement. "Does this mean... we should start stocking up on black mourning ribbons, commemorative portrait candles, and maybe custom-sized royal baby nappies in case the secret heir is a toddler?"

千夏惊呼一声，眼中闪烁着天真的兴奋光芒：“那这意思是不是说……我们得赶紧进货黑色悼念缎带、纪念肖像蜡烛，顺便再囤一批定制尺寸的皇家婴儿纸尿裤，万一那个秘密继承人是个婴儿呢？”

Fujiwara slammed his fist into his palm, roaring with laughter. "Brilliant! Chika, you're a genius! Let's print 'Fresh Mart Official Certified Royal Weeping Tissues' by morning! When royalty falls, the retail hustle never stops!"

藤原一巴掌拍在手心上，放声大笑起来：“太绝了！千夏，你简直是个商业天才！天亮前立刻给我印出‘新鲜玛特官方特许皇家泣血纸巾’！王室倒了，咱们的生意可永远不会停！”
