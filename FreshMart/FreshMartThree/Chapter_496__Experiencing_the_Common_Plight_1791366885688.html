Dressed down in plain denim jackets and unbranded cotton tees, Fujiwara and his four ladies squeezed around a wobbly plastic folding table at a roadside open-air snack stall, the scent of charcoal, roasted skewers, and cheap draft beer drifting on the cool night air.

Even stripped of luxury brands, a single glance made it glaringly obvious this wasn't an ordinary table. Every passing patron, from exhausted blue-collar workers to weary salarymen, couldn't help but gawk in bewildered jealousy.

"Master, have another grilled tendon," Sayuri whispered with sweet, maternal grace, blowing on the skewer before feeding it directly to his lips. "Eating from roadside stalls is actually quite romantic."

“主人，再尝一口烤板筋呀，”小百合眉眼含笑，温柔地吹凉了竹签上的肉块，直接送到了他唇边，“偶尔在路边摊吃点市井风味，倒也别有一番情趣呢。”

"My boobs feel so heavy under this tight cheap cotton, Master," Chika whined softly, leaning her flushed cheek against his arm as her twin peaks pressed firmly against his elbow. "Make sure we go home early... the eunuch doctors need to apply my night balm, or they might lose that perky pink shine."

Nearby, a middle-aged drunkard in a grease-stained windbreaker stumbled past, clutching half a bottle of cheap shochu. His bleary, bloodshot eyes locked onto Fujiwara’s plastic table. Taking in the sight of one man surrounded by four breathtaking goddesses all fawning over him like loyal concubines, the drunk spat aggressively on the pavement.

就在这时，一个穿着油渍风衣、满脸横肉的中年醉汉摇摇晃晃地从旁边路过，手里死死攥着半瓶劣质烧酒。他那双充血浑浊的醉眼，直勾勾地瞪着藤原这一桌。看着眼前这一个男人居然被四位颠倒众生的绝色仙女团团围住、还一个个跟乖巧小妾似的争风吃醋讨好侍奉，醉汉嫉妒得眼珠子几乎滴血，狠狠往水泥地上啐了一大口浓痰。

"Fucking hell..." the drunk spat slurred curses under his breath, stumbling into the dark alley. "Rich bastards taking everything... four of 'em?! I work twelve hours a day and can't even get a stray dog to look at me! God damn this unfair world! Some people drown in heaven while the rest of us rot in the mud!"

“妈的……什么世道……”醉汉嘴里骂骂咧咧、含糊不清地嘟囔着，跌跌撞撞地拐进阴暗的小巷，“有钱的畜生把好苗子全霸占光了……一口气霸占四个？！老子一天累死累活干十二个小时，连条野狗都懒得多看我一眼！贼老天不公啊！有人泡在蜜罐里享福，老子就只能在烂泥里发烂发臭！”

The bitter words faded into the shadows. Fujiwara paused with a mutton skewer in his hand, his eyes tracking the man’s slouching back into the darkness. A strange, uncharacteristic look of philosophical contemplation washed over the tycoon's face.

怨毒的咒骂渐渐消散在夜色中。藤原手里捏着羊肉串顿了顿，目光顺着那个落魄的背影一路探进黑暗里。这位万亿枭雄的脸上，竟破天荒地浮现出一抹近乎哲学反思般的沉重与出神。

"You know..." Fujiwara muttered, furrowing his brows with a suddenly grave, solemn expression, "seeing the sheer despair of the working class touches my imperial heart. It feels wrong for one man to hoard so much divine beauty while ordinary men starve in perpetual coldness. I feel... a twinge of cosmic guilt."

“你们说……”藤原眉头紧锁，神情突然变得无比庄重肃穆，语气甚至带着几分深沉的唏嘘，“亲眼目睹劳苦大众这般绝望的惨状，本帝王这心里啊，还真有点不是滋味。天底下所有的极品绝色全让老子一个人给垄断了，叫普通汉子在凄风苦雨里忍饥挨饿，确实有伤天和。老子心里……居然生出了一丝奇妙的负罪感。”

The women paused, blinking at him. Before anyone could praise his newfound empathy, Fujiwara’s brain short-circuited entirely, plunging straight into an apocalyptic abyss of depraved mischief.

女人们齐齐一愣，眨巴着眼睛看着他。还没等大家开口夸赞他难得的慈悲心肠，藤原那颗永远闲不住的大脑便当场短路，毫无征兆地一头扎进了不可名状的极恶深渊。

"Here is my solution," Fujiwara declared, tapping his beer glass with total seriousness. "Why don't we send little Chika to tail him home? She can knock on his apartment door, pretend to be a penniless, homeless runaway girl, and let that poor miserable bachelor have his absolute wicked way with her for the night? Let the commoner experience what heaven tastes like!"

“老子有个两全其美的主意，”藤原神色严肃地敲了敲啤酒杯，理所当然地提议道，“要不……干脆让小千夏悄悄尾随他回家？等到了他那破公寓门口，千夏就装成走投无路、无家可归的可怜小乞丐敲门借宿，然后由着那个可怜的光棍汉肆无忌惮地爽上一整夜？也算让底层群众尝尝极乐天国的滋味嘛！”

The entire plastic table fell into an absolute, paralyzed dead silence.

整张塑料折叠桌瞬间陷入了大脑宕机般的死寂。

Chika choked violently on her sip of soda, coughing until her eyes watered, staring at him in utter horror while clutching her newly-firmed chest with both hands.

千夏一口汽水直接呛进了气管，剧烈咳嗽得泪花直流。她像看怪物一样惊恐万状地死死瞪着他，两只小手拼命护住刚刚才保养得娇嫩挺拔的雪峰。

"Are you completely out of your goddamn mind?!" Chika shrieked, her voice cracking in pure, frantic disbelief. "Send me to a filthy drunkard’s house?! To roleplay a homeless runaway?! Boss, have you completely lost your cognitive sanity?! I just spent a fortune having four neutered masters polish my breasts until they shine like pink diamonds, and you want to offer me up as roadside charity to some greasy, foul-smelling hobo?!"

小百合轻轻揉着太阳穴，无可奈何地溢出一声气极反笑的娇柔叹息。
