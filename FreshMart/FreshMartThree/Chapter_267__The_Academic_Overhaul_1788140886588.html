Chapter 267: The Academic Overhaul

发布时间：2026/8/31 09:48:05

第二百六十七章：学术级整顿

In the days following the disastrous blind box incident, "Fresh Mart" found itself sinking into a deep reputational quagmire. The once-bustling convenience store was completely deserted, practically boycotted by the entire neighborhood. Fujiwara desperately tried to downplay the catastrophe, insisting to anyone who would listen that the angry customers simply lacked the artistic vision to appreciate his modern retail surprises.

在那场灾难性的盲盒事件发生后的几天里，“新鲜玛特”发现自己陷入了声誉尽毁的泥潭之中。曾经熙熙攘攘的便利店现在空无一人，几乎遭到了整个街区的抵制。藤原拼命试图淡化这场灾难，逢人便坚称那些愤怒的顾客只是缺乏欣赏他现代零售惊喜的艺术眼光。

Miyetsuko, however, was having none of his nonsense. Armed with a clipboard and a plethora of newly printed administrative regulations, she marched into the store and announced a comprehensive, top-to-bottom overhaul of their entire business model. The embattled store manager could only watch in horror as she officially took command of the checkout counter.

然而，美悦子根本不吃他那一套胡言乱语。她拿着一个硬纸板夹和一大堆新印发的行政规章制度，大步流星地走进店里，宣布对他们整个商业模式进行一次自上而下的全面彻底整顿。这位四面楚歌的店长只能惊恐地看着她正式接管了收银台。

"From this moment on, you will no longer prey on gullible neighbors with your ridiculous schemes," Miyetsuko declared coldly, pushing up her glasses. She immediately instituted a five-stage academic review process for every single product on the shelves, instantly causing a massive operational gridlock. Fujiwara couldn't even put a discount sticker on a bottle of soda without submitting a written thesis defending his pricing strategy.

“从现在起，你休想再用你那些荒谬的诡计去欺骗容易上当受骗的街坊邻居了，”美悦子冷冷地宣布，并推了推眼镜。她立刻针对货架上的每一件商品制定了五阶段的学术审查流程，瞬间导致了便利店运营的全面瘫痪。藤原现在哪怕只是想给一瓶汽水贴个打折标签，都得先提交一份书面报告来为他的定价策略辩护。

While the store's economy ground to a painful halt, a fierce, petty vendetta was brewing in the background. Rin and Chika were aggressively fighting over the rusty paperclip from the ripped blind box, treating the worthless item like a sacred artifact and swearing eternal enmity over who rightfully owned it.

就在便利店的经济陷入痛苦的停滞状态时，一场激烈而又鸡毛蒜皮的宿怨正在后台酝酿。铃和千夏正为了那枚从扯破的盲盒里掉出来的生锈曲别针而大打出手，她们把这件毫无价值的破烂当成了一件神圣的神器，并为了谁才是它的合法主人而立下了不共戴天的誓言。

Observing Fujiwara crying as he filled out a triplicate requisition form just to use his own store's microwave, Sayuri offered an elegantly sardonic smile. "My, my. It seems our great capitalist pioneer has finally been domesticated by the iron fist of sociology," she chuckled softly, taking a leisurely sip of her evening tea.

看着藤原为了使用自家店里的微波炉而一边抹眼泪一边填写一式三份的申请表，小百合露出了一抹优雅且充满嘲讽的微笑。“哎呀呀，看来咱们伟大的资本主义先驱，最终还是被社会学的铁拳给彻底驯化了呢，”她轻笑着，端起晚茶悠闲地抿了一口。
