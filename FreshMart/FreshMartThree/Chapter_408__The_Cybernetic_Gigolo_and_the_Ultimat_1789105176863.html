The newly crowned tactile empire was operating at maximum efficiency, but tonight, Miyetsuko had temporarily locked herself in her executive suite. Within moments, the digital link established a seamless sensory feedback loop. The gigolo’s expert virtual foreplay immediately washed over her nervous system with breathtaking precision. Every simulated stroke, breath, and feather-light caress translated through the suit's micro-motors and thermal arrays, rendering Miyetsuko utterly breathless.

片刻之间，数字链路便建立起了无缝的感官反馈循环。牛郎那炉火纯青的虚拟前戏以惊人的精准度瞬间席卷了她的神经系统。每一个由手套和触觉服内的微型电机与温控阵列完美还原的模拟抚摸、呼吸和羽毛般的轻抚，都让美悦子彻底呼吸急促起来。

As the virtual integration deepened, the gigolo fully penetrated her digital avatar. A violent, electric wave of euphoria surged through her spine. The raw, visceral intensity of the sensation was so staggering that it completely obliterated reality—she could have sworn, with absolute conviction, that the physical presence inside her was unimaginably massive, vastly superseding even Fujiwara’s anatomical proportions.

随着虚拟融合的不断加深，牛郎彻底进入了她的数字虚拟化身。一股暴烈而充满电流感的快感顺着脊椎狂涌而上。这种原始、深入骨髓的强烈体验是如此震撼，以至于彻底击碎了现实——她甚至可以笃定地发誓，此刻在体内感受到的存在感简直大得超乎想象，甚至完全超越了藤原的生理尺寸。

Waves of decadent, intoxicating pleasure crashed over her in continuous succession. The room dissolved into a sensory blur of synchronized heat, deep friction, and synchronized pulses that pushed her over the edge in a blinding, breathless climax.

一波又一波堕落而迷醉的快感如潮水般接连不断地冲击着她。整个房间融化成了一片由同步热浪、深度摩擦和共振脉冲交织而成的感官虚空，在耀眼而窒息的高潮中将她彻底推向了顶峰。

Hours later, the session finally terminated. Miyetsuko collapsed backward onto her king-sized bed, completely paralyzed and limp as a ragdoll, her mind floating in a daze of post-orgasmic oblivion. It took nearly an hour of heavy, recovering breaths before she could summon the strength to peel off the high-tech haptic suit and stagger back into the main control room, where the rest of the team was waiting.

几个小时后，服务终于结束。美悦子无力地瘫倒在她的特大号双人床上，浑身酸软得像个破布娃娃，意识在一片高潮之后的空白中飘荡。她大口喘息着恢复了好久，过了将近一个小时，才勉强积攒起力气脱下那套高科技触觉服，摇摇晃晃地走回了主控室，而团队的其他成员正聚在那里等着她。

美悦子用微微颤抖的手推了推眼镜，脸颊上依然残留着一抹罕见的绯红。她深吸了一口气，试图重新找回平时那副雷厉风行的企业高管姿态，然后开始分享她的实测感受。

Fujiwara’s jaw dropped in sheer betrayal, while Sayuri burst into a fit of delightful, musical laughter.
