Chapter 15: The Boutique Spree

发布时间：2026/8/23 23:05:31

第十五章：精品店疯狂血拼

While Fujiwara stretched out on the cool sofa back at "Fresh Mart," enjoying a quiet afternoon nap after the morning's brief military scare, the three women decided to take full advantage of their newly established diplomatic immunity. They headed down to the island's most exclusive commercial lane, straight into its premier luxury boutique.

正当藤原在“新鲜玛特”凉爽的沙发上舒舒服服地伸个懒腰、享受着早晨那场短暂军事虚惊之后的惬意午觉时，三个女人决定充分利用她们刚刚确立的“外交豁免权”。她们径直前往岛上最高档的商业街，走进了最顶级的奢侈品精品店。

The boutique was usually reserved for visiting high-ranking officials and wealthy mainland elites, but the moment Miyetsuko stepped through the glass doors, the atmosphere shifted instantly. The store manager, who had likely received a very urgent, cryptic phone call from a sweating bureaucrat earlier that morning, turned pale, bowed deeply, and practically rolled out a red carpet of terrified hospitality.

这家精品店平时只接待来访的高级官员和大陆富豪，但当美悦子迈入玻璃大门的瞬间，店里的气氛瞬间变了。店长显然在清晨接到了某位满头大汗的官僚打来的十万火急的加密电话，顿时脸色煞白，深深鞠躬，几乎是以一种战战兢兢的殷勤态度铺开了一条隐形的红地毯。

"Welcome, Madame. Please, take your time. Everything in the store is at your absolute disposal," the manager stammered, his hands shaking slightly as he offered them glasses of vintage champagne.

“欢迎光临，夫人。请随意挑选，店里的所有商品随时听候您的差遣，”店长结结巴巴地说着，双手微微颤抖着为她们端上了陈年香槟。

Rin didn't waste a single second. She immediately darted toward the racks of Italian leather jackets and designer silk dresses, a predatory gleam in her eyes. "Oh, look at this. After dealing with sweaty tourists and bogus draft notices, I think the island owes me a wardrobe upgrade." She pulled out a gorgeous, midnight-black designer coat, slipping it on over her tank top with effortless swagger. "How does this look? Deadly enough to assassinate a government official?"

铃一秒钟都没浪费。她立刻直奔意大利皮衣和设计师丝绸长裙的货架，眼底闪烁着掠夺的光芒。“噢，瞧瞧这个。在应付了一群满头大汗的游客和荒谬的征兵通知后，我觉得这座岛欠我一次衣橱大升级。”她挑出一件华丽的午夜黑设计师外套，随性地套在背心外面，“这身看起来怎么样？够不够刺杀一个政府官员的派头？”

Miyetsuko, sipping her champagne with calm elegance, cast a critical eye over Rin's selection before nodding. "Acceptable, though a bit too cliché. If you're going to loot the local economy, at least choose something with genuine European tailoring." She casually pointed at a rack of custom cashmere wraps and bespoke evening gowns. "Wrap up those three. And send the bill directly to the municipal defense fund."

美悦子优雅地抿着香槟，挑剔地扫了一眼铃选的衣服，微微颔首。“勉强算过关，就是有点老套。既然要搜刮地方经济，好歹选点真正欧洲定制裁剪的。”她漫不经心地指向一排定制羊绒披肩和高级晚礼服，“那三件包起来。账单直接寄给市政国防基金会。”

Sayuri stood near the fitting rooms, blushing softly as she held up a delicate, pastel-pink silk robe that was far more daring and luxurious than anything she would normally wear. "Do you... do you think Fujiwara would like this?" she murmured shyly, turning to her sister-wives for approval.

小百合站在试衣间旁，手里拿着一件精致的浅粉色丝绸睡袍，羞涩地红了脸。这件衣服比她平时穿的大胆奢华得多。“你们说……藤原会喜欢这个吗？”她红着脸转头向两位好姐妹征求意见。

Rin grinned wickedly, walking over to give Sayuri a gentle, encouraging push toward the dressing room. "If he doesn't, he's blind. Try it on, Sayuri. In fact, buy it in every available color. Let's make sure our hardworking master has plenty of visual stimulation when he gets home."

铃坏笑着走过去，轻轻推着小百合往试衣间走去。“他要是不喜欢，那就是瞎了眼。穿上试试，小百合。干脆把所有颜色都买下来。得确保咱们辛勤工作的主人回家后有足够的视觉刺激。”

Within an hour, the three women had practically cleared out the boutique's VIP section. Designer shopping bags piled high around them, filled to the brim with silk, cashmere, and high-end accessories, while the store manager stood nearby, bowing profusely and sweating through his suit. It wasn't just a shopping trip—it was a joyful, unapologetic display of dominance, proving that no matter how chaotic the island's politics became, they would always live like queens.

不到一个小时，三个女人几乎把精品店的VIP专区给搬空了。周围堆满了高档购物袋，里面塞满了丝绸、羊绒和奢侈配饰，而店长则在一旁点头哈腰，西服都湿透了。这不仅是一场逛街购物——更是一场酣畅淋漓、肆无忌惮的权力展示，向世人证明，无论岛上的政治局势如何风起云涌，她们永远都能活得像高高在上的女王。
