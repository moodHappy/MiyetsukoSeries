Chapter 214: The Celibacy Protocol

发布时间：2026/8/30 01:26:10

第一百一十四章：终极禁欲令

As Fujiwara continued to cling to her waist like a drowning man clutching a piece of driftwood, Sayuri leaned down and lowered her voice, adding one final, devilish twist to her grand plan.

正当藤原像个溺水之人死死抱住救命稻草般赖在小百合腰间不肯撒手时，小百合忽然微微弯下腰，贴在他的耳边，抛出了整个计划中最致命、也最让人血脉偾张的终极杀招。

"By the way, dear..." Sayuri whispered with a sweet, knowing giggle. "During the dinner, I'll make sure to find an excuse to step away to the restroom. That will leave the two of you completely alone to chat privately. If you actually manage to charm her and... well, take things a step further, I won't mind at all. Consider it my special gift to you. Let's see if you've got what it takes."

“对了，亲爱的……”小百合掩嘴轻笑，眼底闪烁着恶趣味的光芒，“到时候吃饭吃到一半，我会找个借口去趟洗手间。这样你们俩就有独处的私人空间了。如果你真有本事能把她给拿下……那就算你的本事，姐姐我可一点都不会吃醋哦。就当是送你的特别福利啦。”

The moment those words registered in Fujiwara’s brain, his soul practically levitated out of his body.

这句话一进耳朵，藤原的大脑瞬间轰的一声，灵魂差点直接从天灵盖飞出去。

A private dinner with Kirara-chan and a deliberate, calculated assist from his girlfriend to hook up with his ultimate goddess? This wasn't just a dream come true; it was a divine intervention of legendary proportions!

能和偶像绮罗罗私密共进晚餐不说，正牌女友居然还主动神助攻、创造单独偷情的机会？！这哪里是什么普通粉丝福利，这简直是开挂般的人生巅峰啊！

"If... if I can actually sleep with Kirara-chan..." Fujiwara whimpered, his eyes shining with feverish, delusional religious fanaticism as he clutched Sayuri's hands. "I am willing to shorten my natural lifespan by a full year! No, two years! I will dedicate every breath of my remaining days to worshiping you, goddess Sayuri!"

“如果……如果我真能把绮罗罗给睡了……”藤原激动得浑身发抖，双眼里闪烁着狂热的宗教般的光芒，死死握住小百合的手，“我愿意折寿整整一年！不，两年！从今以后我每天的呼吸都是为了孝敬你，小百合大人！”

Rin rolled her eyes so hard she nearly gave herself a headache, tossing an empty chip bag at his head. "Get a grip, you pathetic simp! You haven't even met her yet, and you're already writing your own obituary out of sheer horniness."

铃极其无语地翻了个巨大的白眼，随手把一个空薯片袋子朝他脑袋砸过去：“醒醒吧你个没出息的舔狗！连面都还没见着呢，就已经因为精虫上脑开始提前规划遗嘱了是吧？”

Miyetsuko, adjusting her glasses while still wearing her humiliating open-crotch maid uniform, offered a dry, clinical assessment. "A classic case of dopamine-induced cognitive failure. The probability of him succeeding against a top-tier idol in a private setting is roughly equivalent to winning the national lottery twice in a row."

美悦子依然穿着那套羞耻的开裆女仆装，一边推了推眼镜一边冷酷无情地毒舌道：“典型的多巴胺过载导致的认知障碍。他在私密场合拿下顶级偶像的概率，大概相当于连续两次中全国彩票头奖。”

"Silence, non-believers!" Fujiwara declared grandly, standing up with a solemn, ironclad expression. "From this exact moment on, I am entering a state of absolute, unbreakable celibacy!"

“闭嘴吧你们这群没有信仰的凡人！”藤原大手一挥，神情肃穆地站直了身子，“从这一秒开始，为了迎接伟大的神圣之夜，我正式宣布进入绝对禁欲期！”

The living room went quiet.

起居室里瞬间安静了下来。

"Celibacy?" Rin blinked, staring at him as if he had spoken in ancient Martian. "You? The guy who treats sex like a daily morning workout?"

“禁欲？”铃难以置信地眨了眨眼，像看外星人一样看着他，“就你？把做爱当成每日晨练的究极老色批，居然说要禁欲？”

"Precisely," Fujiwara nodded solemnly. "To ensure maximum physical performance and absolute peak vitality for next week's dinner, I must conserve every single drop of my core energy. No touching, no teasing, and absolutely no horizontal wrestling until the big night!"

“没错，”藤原郑重其事地点了点头，“为了确保下周的那场晚宴上能展现出最巅峰的爆发力与状态，我必须严格保存核心战斗力。从现在开始到大功告成那天为止，绝对不许碰我，谁也不准诱惑我！”

The women stared at him for three full seconds, before bursting into a simultaneous wave of uproarious laughter.

三个女人齐刷刷地盯着他看了足足三秒钟，随后爆发出了一阵掀翻房顶的狂笑。

"Oh, this is going to be the funniest week of my life," Rin howled, wiping a tear from her eye. "A degenerate trying to fast from lust? I give him twenty-four hours before he breaks down and begs us!"

“哈哈哈哈，这绝对是我这辈子听过最好笑的笑话，”铃笑得眼泪都快飙出来了，“一个满脑子废料的变态居然想搞禁欲？我赌他连二十四小时都撑不到，就得哭着回来求咱们临幸！”

"A bold hypothesis," Miyetsuko smirked, crossing her arms in her maid outfit. "Let's test his so-called 'iron willpower' starting tonight."

“很大胆的假设，”美悦子穿着开裆女仆装冷笑着环抱双臂，“今晚就让我们拭目以待，看看他那所谓的‘钢铁意志’到底能撑几分钟。”

And thus, the great "Celibacy Protocol" of "Fresh Mart" officially commenced—though how long Fujiwara could actually survive without his daily dose of chaos remained to be seen.

于是，“新鲜玛特”轰轰烈烈的“终极禁欲令”正式拉开帷幕——不过，这位满脑子骚主意的店长大人究竟能在没有日常荒诞滋润的日子里撑多久，所有人心里其实都门儿清。
