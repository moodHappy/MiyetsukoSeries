Chapter 18: Settling the Debt

发布时间：2026/8/24 00:03:03

第十八章：了结此账

The elderly owner felt the color drain entirely from his face. He didn't need to hear the details; the mere mention of the island's highest administration and the terrifying, cold fury in Miyetsuko's eyes were more than enough to make the connection.

老店主吓得脸色彻底惨白。他根本不需要听具体细节；只需听到“最高行政部门”这几个字，再加上美悦子眼中冰冷骇人的怒火，就足以让他把一切串联起来。

"You wretched fool!" the owner shrieked, spinning around and delivering a sharp, resounding slap across the cowering clerk's face. The young man stumbled backward, clutching his cheek in absolute terror. "Get out! You are fired permanently from this clinic! Pack your things and never let me see your face on this island again!"

“你这个蠢货！”老店主气急败坏地转过身，狠狠一巴掌抽在瑟瑟发抖的店员脸上。年轻人踉跄着退后，捂着脸颊惊恐万状。“滚！你被这家诊所彻底开除了！马上收拾东西，以后别让我在这座岛上看见你的脸！”

Rin finally released his collar, letting the crying clerk slump to the floor like a broken ragdoll. She dusted off her hands with a look of utter disgust, stepping back to stand beside Sayuri.

铃终于松开了他的领口，任由那个哭丧着脸的店员像个破布娃娃一样瘫坐在地上。她嫌弃地拍了拍手，退回到小百合身旁。

Miyetsuko watched the display with cold detachment, adjusting the cuffs of her blouse. "Dismissing him is merely a baseline requirement, Director. A physical insult of this magnitude to our group requires a somewhat more... substantial compensation."

美悦子冷眼看着这一幕，淡定地整理着衬衫袖口。“开除他只是最低要求，院长。我们这群人遭此羞辱，需要一些更具诚意的……实质性赔偿。”

The owner nodded frantically, practically bowing to the floor. "Yes, of course! All treatments for you and your esteemed companions will be permanently free of charge. And please—allow me to present these rare, centuries-old herbal longevity tonics and our finest jade amulets as a humble apology for this grave oversight."

老店主连连点头，几乎快要鞠躬到地上了。“是、是！各位女士及同伴的所有治疗费用永久免单。另外——请务必收下这些珍藏的百年野山参草本补剂和上等玉石护身符，权当是对这次严重失察的诚挚歉意。”

Sayuri, having re-secured her linen sheet and regained her composure, stepped out from the cubicle with a gentle, dignified grace. She looked at the panicked owner, then at the trembling clerk on the floor, and shook her head softly. "Just make sure it never happens to anyone else," she said quietly, her voice carrying a quiet authority that brooked no argument. "We accept your apology. Let's go, girls."

小百合重新整理好亚麻床单，恢复了往日的温婉与从容，优雅地从隔间里走了出来。她看了一眼慌乱的店主，又看了看地上瑟瑟发抖的店员，轻轻摇了摇头。“只要确保这种事不再发生在别人身上就行，”她轻声说道，声音里自带一股不容置疑的温和威严，“我们接受你们的道歉。走吧，姐妹们。”

With their arms full of newly acquired luxury bags, complimentary herbal tonics, and jade gifts, the three women strolled back out into the warm evening air of Donglai Island. The little drama at the clinic was already behind them, just another minor ripple in their surreal, untouchable exile.

三个女人手里提满了刚买的奢侈品购物袋、获赠的草艺补剂和玉石礼品，重新踱步回东莱岛温暖的夜色中。诊所里的小插曲已经抛诸脑后，不过是她们那魔幻而高高在上的流亡生活中又一段小小的涟漪。

Back at "Fresh Mart," Fujiwara was still lounging comfortably on the sofa, half-asleep with a magazine draped over his face. As the front door chimed and the rich scent of expensive perfume and fresh shopping bags filled the room, he lifted the magazine with a grin.

回到“新鲜玛特”时，藤原依然舒舒服服地瘫在沙发上，脸上盖着本杂志半梦半醒。随着前门风铃响起，昂贵香水的芬芳与满满的购物袋香气瞬间充盈了整个房间，他咧嘴笑着掀开了杂志。

"Well?" Fujiwara chuckled, looking at the glowing, triumphant expressions of his women. "Did the shopping spree cure your exhaustion, or do I need to go settle some scores with the locals?"

“怎么样？”藤原轻笑着，看着女人们光彩照人、大获全胜的表情，“疯狂血拼治好你们的疲惫了吗，还是说需要我去找当地人算账？”

Miyetsuko walked over, leaning down to press a soft, lingering kiss to his lips, while Sayuri curled up beside him with a happy sigh. "No scores to settle, darling," Miyetsuko smiled, her eyes dancing with amusement. "Just a minor adjustment to the local economy. Now... tell us you saved some energy for us tonight."

美悦子走上前去，俯身在他唇上印下一个缠绵轻柔的吻，小百合则带着幸福的叹息蜷缩在他身旁。“不用算账，亲爱的，”美悦子笑着，眼底闪烁着玩味的笑意，“只不过对当地经济做了一点小小的微调罢了。现在……快告诉我，今晚你给咱们留了点力气吧？”
