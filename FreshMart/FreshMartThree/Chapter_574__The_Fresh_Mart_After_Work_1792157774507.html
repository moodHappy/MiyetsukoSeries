Chapter 574: The Fresh Mart After Work

发布时间：2026/10/16 21:36:13

第五百七十四章：下班后的新鲜玛特

The golden morning light streamed into the grand salon, illuminating a scene of bizarre intellectual ambition. Emperor Fujiwara, having fully recovered his tyrannical energy after his long-lasting confession night, sat at the mahogany desk flanked by a trembling, highly nervous royal historian clad in full imperial court robes.

金色的晨光洒进大起居殿，照亮了一幕荒诞而又充满学术气息的场景。藤原大帝在经历了昨晚的长情告白与温存之后，此刻重新焕发了暴君的活力。他正端坐在红木书桌前，身旁站着一位身穿朝服、浑身瑟瑟发抖的高级皇家史官。

"Hear this, Official Historian!" Fujiwara declared, slamming his fist on the desk. "I am officially commissioning an official historical biography for Consort Miyetsuko! Document every single glorious detail: her noble lineage, her triumphant infiltration of the Heavenly Palace, how she gracefully swept through the divine treasury, and of course, her legendary 1.23-second impression on my unfortunate son!"

“听好了，史官！”藤原一巴掌拍在桌子上大声宣布道，“朕今天要正式下旨，为美悦子爱妃修撰一本《御批美悦子传》！把她所有的丰功伟绩全部记录在案：她的高贵血统、她成功潜入天宫的壮举、她优雅搬空神仙宝库的过程，当然，还有她让朕那个倒霉儿子当场沦陷的1.23秒传奇！”

Before the trembling historian could even lift his writing brush, the heavy silk curtains parted, and Sayuri glided into the room. The Wise Empress, looking radiantly plump and impeccably regal in her Tang-dynasty silks, took one look at the historian and let out a sharp, aristocratic scoff.

还没等发抖的史官举起御笔，厚重的丝绒帘幕便被掀开，小百合优雅地走了进来。这位身穿盛唐真丝长袍、丰腴绝伦的贤后瞥了一眼那名史官，发出一声犀利而充满嘲讽的贵族冷笑。

"A historical biography? For her?" Sayuri crossed her arms, her Tang-style curves heaving with absolute executive authority. She glared straight at the Emperor. "Fujiwara, have you completely lost your mind?! She didn't die! Why on earth are you writing a biography for someone who is still very much alive and breathing right beside you? Biographies are for ancestors and martyrs, not for concubines who come home smelling like celestial nectar and high-end cloud bedsheets!"

“修传记？给她？！”小百合双手抱胸，盛唐风格的丰腴曲线因为绝对的后宫主权而微微起伏，她没好气地瞪着皇帝，“藤原，你脑子彻底进水了吧？！她又没死！好端端地给一个大活人立什么传记？史书传记那是给死去的祖宗和烈士写的，可不是给那些昨晚带着一身天界花蜜和云端床单味回来的妃子准备的！”

Fujiwara instantly shrank back into his velvet chair, completely folding under the absolute, terrifying pressure of the head wife. "A-Ah... You make a fair point, Sayuri," the Emperor stammered, sweating profusely. "I... I suppose living consorts don't traditionally get biographies while their hearts are still beating. My apologies, my Queen. I got carried away."

藤原瞬间从刚才不可一世的暴君气势中缩了回去，在大老婆绝对的威压下秒变小媳妇。“啊……小百合说得非常有道理，”皇帝擦着冷汗结结巴巴地辩解道，“朕……朕确实欠考虑了，哪有给活人歌功颂德的道理。是朕失职，皇后息怒，朕这就取消。”

The terrified historian hastily lowered his head, ready to pack up his scrolls and run for his life. But just as he reached for his inkstone, Chika strolled into the salon, munching loudly on a bag of spicy chips. The former slum maid paused, pointing a chip at the discarded historical project with a grin.

吓得半死的史官连忙低下头，准备卷起竹简逃命。可就在他伸手去拿砚台时，千夏嚼着麻辣薯片大步走了进来。这位昔日的贫民窟女仆看了一眼那个被否决的写书计划，满不在乎地挥了挥薯片。

"Wait, why are you canceling it?" Chika yelled, her heavy, milk-swollen chest bouncing. "A biography is boring anyway! Who wants to read dry government history about how many star systems she conquered or how many clothes she took off? If you really want to honor her, Boss, you should publish a book!"

“等等，凭什么取消啊？”千夏大声嚷嚷着，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔随着她的动作剧烈晃荡，“写什么正经史书多无聊啊！谁想看那些关于她征服了几个星系或者脱了几件衣服的死板官僚历史？你要是真想给她长脸，老板，你应该直接给她出本书！”

The entire grand salon went silent. Sayuri raised an eyebrow, and Miyetsuko gasped softly. In the strict autocracy of the Fujiwara Empire, allowing a concubine to have her own published book of personal thoughts, poetry, or lifestyle commentary was an unprecedented, earth-shattering honor. Imperial consorts were traditionally kept as silent, beautiful decorations of the inner palace, strictly barred from having any independent voice or public fame outside the harem.

整个大起居殿瞬间安静了下来。小百合微微挑起眉毛，美悦子则轻呼了一声。在藤原帝国森严的专制体制下，允许后宫妃子拥有自己公开发表的个人文集、心得或生活随笔，简直是绝无仅有的莫大荣宠。后宫通常被严格限制为内廷里沉默漂亮的花瓶，绝对不允许她们在后宫之外拥有任何独立的声名与公众影响力。

"A book...?" Miyetsuko whispered, her exquisite, porcelain-smooth face flushing with deep, emotional astonishment. "You want to publish my... my personal writings?"

“出书……？”美悦子轻声呢喃着，她那张白瓷般精致的脸上写满了震惊与动容，“您是说……要给奴婢出个人文集吗？”

"Brilliant!" Fujiwara slammed his fist on the desk, instantly regaining his tyrannical confidence now that Chika had provided a loophole. "Chika, you are a genius of cultural propaganda! Forget dry historical archives! We will publish a collection of her personal memoirs! Title it: 'The Fresh Mart After Work: A Noble Consort’s Guide to Celestial Shopping and Casual Extortion.' We’ll distribute it across all ten newly conquered star systems!"

“妙啊！”藤原一巴掌拍在桌子上，既然千夏提供了合规的漏洞，他的暴君底气瞬间又回来了，“千夏，你简直就是文化宣传领域的旷世奇才！什么正经史书，通通靠边站！我们要给她出版一本个人回忆录！书名就叫——《下班后的新鲜玛特：贵妃娘娘的星际扫货与高维勒索指南》。朕要把这本书直接发行到那十个新征服的星系去！”

Sayuri sighed, shaking her head with a velvet smile, fully recognizing that arguing with Fujiwara and Chika when they combined forces was a waste of macroeconomic energy.

小百合无奈地叹了口气，挂着一丝丝绒般的微笑摇了摇头。她太清楚了，当藤原和千夏这两个活宝联手时，讲道理纯粹是在浪费宏观经济能量。

"Very well, if we are publishing a book, it must have proper commercial branding," Sayuri purred smoothly, walking over to check the draft outline. "I shall personally oversee the publishing logistics through the AV Religion's interplanetary temple network. We will make sure every alien citizen in the galaxy buys a copy."

“好吧，既然要出书，那就必须得有规范的商业品牌运作，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，走过来亲自审阅起大纲，“我会亲自通过AV女神教的跨星系神庙网络来统筹出版物流。务必让银河系里的每一个外星公民都人手买一本。”

Miyetsuko dropped to her knees, bowing deeply to the Emperor, her plush, Tang-style rear hiked high in absolute, tearful gratitude. "Your humble servant is overwhelmed by Your Majesty's boundless grace... I shall begin drafting the first chapter immediately: 'How to Successfully Navigate Divine Extortion Without Losing Your Silk Underwear.'"

美悦子“扑通”一声跪倒在地，向皇帝深深叩首，那对饱满诱人的臀部高高撅起，满脸都是泪光闪闪的极致感激。“奴婢感念陛下的大恩大德……奴婢这就回去开始构思第一章：《如何在失去真丝内裤之前，优雅地完成神仙级别的跨维勒索》。”

From her floating cushion, blind Rin clapped her hands happily, her milky eyes wide with sweet demonic joy. "Um... hello! Can Rin write the appendix chapter for the book? It will be called: 'How to Steal Cookies from the Heavenly Palace's Pantry while Master is Hiding Under the Table!'"

坐在漂浮软垫上的盲人小铃开心地拍着小手，空灵的盲眼里满是甜美的恶魔喜悦：“唔……小铃也申请加入！可以让小铃在书的附录里写一章吗？章节名字就叫——《当主人吓得躲在茶几底下时，如何优雅地去天宫偷饼干》！”

Fujiwara burst into roaring laughter, pulling Miyetsuko up from the floor and wrapping his arm around Chika as well. The grand salon instantly dissolved back into its signature state of chaotic, highly profitable, and thoroughly shameless domestic bliss.

藤原爆发出放肆的狂笑，一把将美悦子从地上拉起来，顺手连千夏也一起搂进怀里。大起居殿瞬间又恢复了它那标志性的、混乱而极其厚颜无耻的家庭幸福日常。
