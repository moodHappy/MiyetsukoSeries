Chapter 593: The Outer God and the Great Heavenly Heist

发布时间：2026/10/17 16:15:53

第五百九十三章：域外之神与天宫大劫案

It was the turn of spring to summer when the Fujiwara Empire finally broke through the Southern Heavenly Gate. The siege was an absolute, glorious catastrophe for the heavens.

那是春夏之交，藤原帝国的联军终于轰开了南天门。对天庭来说，这场围攻是一场绝对的、极其辉煌的灭顶之灾。

Chika’s eighty-five-thousand-li plasma dragons were roasting the celestial clouds into cosmic cotton candy. Rin’s succubus legion had chased the celestial guards out of their armor, while Miyetsuko, the newly elected Death God, was enthusiastically waving her scythe, tagging the fleeing immortals with Styx River barcode stickers. The Heavenly Emperor, cornered against his shattered jade throne and weeping like a ruined nobleman, was merely moments away from being forcefully fed a literal, high-dimensional green hat by a maniacally laughing Fujiwara.

千夏那展翅八万五千里的等离子巨龙正把天界的祥云烤成宇宙棉花糖。小铃的魅魔军团把天兵天将追得连铠甲都跑丢了，而新任死神美悦子正兴致勃勃地挥舞着大镰刀，给四处乱窜的神仙们挨个贴上冥河的条形码标签。被逼到破碎玉座角落里、哭得像个落魄贵族的天帝，眼看就要被笑得极其猖狂的藤原强行塞下一顶物理意义上的高维绿帽子了。

But at the absolute, most critical climax of the invasion, reality simply... stopped. An "Outer God" intervened. It was an entity so profoundly unfathomable it bypassed all definitions of the Type III civilization. It possessed no detectable form, emitted no sound, and rejected all scientific and theological inquiry. It was a cosmic anomaly of pure, overwhelming authority.

然而，就在这场入侵即将达到绝对高潮的最紧要关头，现实突然……停滞了。一位“域外神”出手干预了。那是一个深不可知、彻底无视了三级文明所有定义的存在。其形不可探测，其音不可得闻，其理不可诘问。那是一种以压倒性权威降临的纯粹宇宙异常。

With merely the abstract concept of a "flick of a sleeve," the incomprehensible entity unleashed a gust of wind. In an instant, the invincible allied forces completely disintegrated. The terrifying plasma dragons shrank into confused, squeaking geckos. The demonic portals snapped shut. Miyetsuko's dreaded scythe wilted into a limp stalk of celestial celery, and Fujiwara was blown ten thousand parsecs backward, landing face-first in a pile of stardust.

仅仅是一个近乎抽象概念的“拂袖之风”，这个不可名状的存在便掀起了一阵狂风。刹那间，所向披靡的联军土崩瓦解。恐怖的等离子巨龙缩水成了满脸懵逼、吱吱乱叫的小壁虎。恶魔传送门瞬间锁死。美悦子那把令人胆寒的死神镰刀萎缩成了一根软趴趴的天界芹菜，而藤原则被当场吹飞了一万秒差距，脸朝下狠狠栽进了一堆星尘里。

The invincible war was over before the final blow could land. Under the silent, terrifying pressure of the Outer God, a forced settlement was established. The Heavenly Emperor, though saved, was forced to pay astronomical reparations to the Empire for the emotional damages caused to the new Death God. In retaliation, the utterly humiliated but pragmatic Fujiwara demanded a very specific clause: the complete annexation of exactly half of the Heavenly Emperor’s divine harem.

这场战无不胜的战争在最后一击落下前戛然而止。在域外神那无声却令人胆寒的威压下，一份强制和解协议诞生了。天帝虽然保住了一命，但被迫向帝国支付天文数字的巨额赔偿，以弥补对新任死神造成的精神损失。作为报复，倍感屈辱却又极具实用主义精神的藤原提出了一个极其明确的条款：强行霸占天帝整整一半的后宫佳丽。

Days later, back in the grand salon of the imperial palace, the atmosphere was a bizarre mix of triumphant spoils and bitter frustration. The room was now crowded with hundreds of bewildered, floating celestial concubines, currently being organized into a spreadsheet by Sayuri.

几天后，在皇宫的大起居殿里，气氛混合着战利品丰收的喜悦与极其苦涩的憋屈。整个大殿现在挤满了成百上千个满脸茫然、飘在半空中的天庭妃嫔，小百合正拿着电子表格挨个给她们登记造册。

Fujiwara lounged on the velvet sofa, swirling his wine glass with an annoyed scowl while Chika sat beside him, feeding her tiny, newly-shrunken dragon-geckos potato chips.

藤原瘫坐在天鹅绒沙发上，满脸不爽地摇晃着红酒杯。千夏坐在他身旁，正拿薯片喂她那些缩水成了小壁虎的巨龙。

"I hate that I couldn't just kill that old bastard," Fujiwara sighed deeply, staring at the newly acquired divine concubines serving him grapes. "To think that deadbeat Heavenly Emperor had such a terrifying, unfathomable cosmic sugar daddy backing him up! The Outer God didn't even look at us, and our entire army just vanished like dust!"

“朕真恨不能当场宰了那个老杂毛，”藤原深深地叹了口气，盯着那些新分来的、正在给他喂葡萄的天庭妃嫔，“谁能想到那天帝老渣男背后，竟然还站着这么一座深不可测、强得离谱的宇宙超级大靠山！那个域外神甚至连看都没看我们一眼，咱们的大军就像灰尘一样灰飞烟灭了！”

Miyetsuko gracefully polished her restored Death God scythe, kneeling obediently by the Emperor's boots. "It was truly terrifying, Master. Even the Styx River stopped flowing when that wind blew past. But at least we successfully bankrupted his celestial treasury."

美悦子优雅地擦拭着那把已经恢复原状的死神镰刀，乖巧地跪在皇帝的靴子旁：“确实太可怕了，主人。当那阵风吹过时，连冥河的水都吓得停止了流动。不过，至少咱们把他的天庭国库给彻底掏空了。”

"Indeed," Sayuri purred, reviewing the celestial asset ledger with a satisfied smirk. "Like the great ancient warlord Cao Cao, we must know when to tactically retreat and consolidate our gains. We failed to execute the Heavenly Emperor, but you did successfully rob him of his dignity, his treasury, and a full fifty percent of his mistresses. From a macroeconomic standpoint, the Fujiwara Empire won the fiscal quarter."

“确实如此，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，满意地翻阅着天界资产分类账本，“就像古代枭雄曹操收兵一样，咱们必须懂得审时度势、见好就收。我们虽然没能处决天帝，但夫君您成功抢走了他的尊严、他的金库，以及他整整百分之五十的后宫佳丽。从宏观经济学的角度来看，藤原帝国赢麻了这个财季。”

Fujiwara snorted, looking over the hundreds of new, trembling fairy maidens now wearing imperial silk collars. A slow, depraved grin returned to his face. "True enough. Cao Cao ordered the retreat, but he always took the wives with him. If I can't kill the god who cuckolded me, completely emptying half his celestial harem to warm my bed is a perfectly acceptable geopolitical compromise!"

藤原冷哼了一声，目光扫过那几百个瑟瑟发抖、如今已经戴上帝国真丝项圈的仙女妃嫔们。一抹缓慢而堕落的坏笑重新浮现在他的脸上。“说得也对。曹操虽然鸣金收兵了，但他每次撤退可都没忘把别人的老婆带走。既然朕杀不了那个给朕戴绿帽子的神仙，那强占他一半的天庭后宫来给朕暖床，也不失为一种可以接受的地缘政治妥协！”
