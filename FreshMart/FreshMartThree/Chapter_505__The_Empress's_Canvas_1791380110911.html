Chapter 505: The Empress's Canvas

发布时间：2026/10/7 21:35:09

第五百零五章：皇后的画布

Once the peak of the royal ceremony had concluded, the room finally settled into comfortable ease. Fujiwara pulled Sayuri flush against his chest as the two leaned back side by side on the velvet daybed, the heavy solemnity of the court giving way to loose, domestic warmth. The remaining sisters clambered up from the carpet one after another, circling the couch with sly, giggling grins.

这段极致的王室礼仪结束之后，殿内那股紧绷的庄严感终于彻底散去。藤原将小百合整个人搂进怀里，两人并肩靠在厚软的天鹅绒软榻上，整个大厅的气氛一下子松弛下来。几位姐妹也纷纷从地毯上爬起来，笑嘻嘻地围拢过来，各自找了舒服的姿势倚在旁边。

Fujiwara looked down at Sayuri nestled in his arms, the faint pink flush of the afterglow still lingering on her cheeks. A wicked smirk tugged at the corner of his lips, and he gave a low, throaty chuckle that dripped with unrepentant mischief.

藤原低头看了眼怀中仍带着几分余韵的小百合，忽然像是想起什么似的，低低笑了一声，语气里带着明显的恶趣味：

"Ah... you know, looking back, I think we really missed out on something special just now."

“哎呀……刚才好像错过了一个天大的好机会。”

He ran the rough pad of his thumb along her warm, dewy cheekbone, speaking in a lazy drawl deliberately loud enough for the entire audience to hear.

他用指腹轻轻摩挲着小百合还带着淡淡潮红的脸颊，声音懒洋洋的，却故意说得很大声，好让围坐的所有人都能听得一清二楚：

"I mean, watching my Queen swallow every last drop so cleanly was magnificent and deeply loyal. But if I had painted it all directly across that gorgeous face instead... wouldn't the visual impact have been ten times more intoxicating? A majestic empress bowing low, her cheeks, nose, and lashes completely coated in her master's glaze—what a masterpiece that would have been!"

“虽然看到小百合吞得干干净净、滴水不漏，确实是又乖巧又尽显忠心。可要是刚才直接射在你这张倾国倾城的绝美容颜上……视觉效果是不是更刺激、更震撼？堂堂母仪天下的中宫皇后伏在脚边，整张脸蛋、鼻尖和眼睫毛上全挂着帝王的滚烫浓浆——光是想想那副画面，可比全咽进肚子里有看头多了！”

Hearing this, Sayuri didn't shrink away into passive silence. Instead, a wave of vivid crimson surged up her neck to the very tips of her ears. The flustered yet endlessly experienced matriarch gave Fujiwara’s chest a gentle, petulant shove with her palm. She looked up at him through heavy, hooded eyes, half-shy and half-exasperated, her velvety voice dripping with tender scolding.

听到这话，小百合非但没有一味缩着不吭声，反倒是从修长的天鹅颈一路红到了耳根尖。这位又羞又臊、却深谙自家夫君脾性的中宫主母，抬手在藤原坚实的胸膛上轻轻推搡了一把，半嗔半怨地飞过去一记眼波，软糯的嗓音里满是无可奈何的娇羞打趣：

"Look at you! You get one thing and immediately lust after the other! If I had taken the facial glaze just now, wouldn't you be lying here right now whining that watching me swallow it clean would have been far more satisfying? 'Oh, what a shame, she didn't drink every drop for her Emperor!' You ah you... there really is no satisfying that bottomless greed of yours!"

“瞧瞧您这副德行！得了这个又想那个？刚才若是真依了您的性子颜射了，这会儿您肯定又得咂着嘴在心里犯嘀咕，想若是能吞了才好呢！说什么‘哎呀真可惜，没能亲眼看着皇后全咽下去表忠心’！你啊你啊……这天底下的贪心便宜，合着全让您一个人占尽了才算甘心呢？！”

Sayuri’s sharp, pitch-perfect comeback was like striking a match in a room full of fireworks. The entire hall instantly exploded into uproarious, side-splitting laughter.

小百合这番条理清晰又直戳要害的反唇相讥，瞬间像引信一样点燃了全场。整座大厅刹那间爆发出一阵掀翻屋顶的哄堂大笑。

Chika was the first to lose it, rolling backward on the sofa while clutching her heavy, buoyant chest, kicking her bare feet in the air as she cackled with tears in her eyes.

千夏头一个破功，两只小手死死捂住胸前经过顶级养护、饱满晃荡的粉嫩雪峰，整个人仰倒在沙发靠垫上笑得双脚乱蹬，眼泪花都迸了出来。

"Pffft—hahahaha! Sister read the Boss like an open book! That is one hundred percent what he would have said!" Chika shrieked with glee, pointing a shaking finger at Fujiwara. "If you had coated her cheeks, five minutes later the Boss would definitely be sighing: 'Alas, what a waste of high-protein nutrients, my Queen should have drunk it all!' Boss, your insatiable appetite is worse than a child in a candy store! But honestly... Sister's face really is too pretty. Next time, I vote for the facial! I want to take high-res photos for the family album!"

“噗哈哈哈哈！姐姐简直是把老板肚子里那点蛔虫给摸得一清二楚啊！这绝对是他能干出来的事儿！”千夏拍着大腿笑得上气不接下气，伸出手指指点点，“要是真射在脸上，五分钟后老板绝对又得唉声叹气：‘唉，多浪费高蛋白营养啊，我家皇后要是全喝光该多乖！’老板你这人心不足蛇吞象的毛病，比小孩子逛糖果店还难伺候！不过话说回来……姐姐这张脸实在太极品了。下回我举双手赞成颜射！我要拿高清相机抓拍下来存进咱们家的内部私密相册！”

Miyetsuko sat on the rug, leaning her chin gracefully against Fujiwara's knee. The softened, dewy-eyed beauty couldn't hide her wide, teasing grin, softly smoothing the edge of Sayuri’s silk skirt.

美悦子双膝并拢跪坐在地毯边，下巴乖巧地枕在藤原膝头，那张被彻底软化、毫无煞气的娇颜上绽放着按捺不住的笑意。她纤手轻柔地抚平小百合压皱的裙角，温言细语地跟着补刀：

"Sister's situational assessment is flawless," Miyetsuko chuckled softly, her voice full of sweet mirth. "Greed is the Emperor's fundamental operational doctrine. If you give him ten paces, he demands a hundred. But imagine the contrast: five seconds earlier, Sister was leading the entire harem in a grand imperial bow with absolute authority; five seconds later, she's kneeling with her eyelashes sticking together under white droplets, blinking timidly like a captured kitten... That dramatic reversal would probably keep the Emperor bragging for a whole month."

“姐姐的战术局势判断永远都是满分呢，”美悦子掩口轻笑，柔柔弱弱的嗓音里满是促狭，“贪得无厌本来就是咱们主人的核心运营法则嘛。给了一步他就想十步。不过光是脑补一下那个反差感：前五秒姐姐还是母仪天下、领着全家叩拜的中宫皇后；后五秒就因为眼睫毛被黏住了只能可怜兮兮地半眯着眼，像只被逮住的小猫一样委屈巴交地红着脸……那种戏剧张力，怕是能让主人在枕边得意吹嘘上整整一个月呢。”

From the floor cushions, blind Rin tilted her head, clapping her small hands together with horrifyingly angelic innocence.

软垫上的盲人小铃歪了歪小脑袋，开心地合起一双小手，发出银铃般悦耳纯真的清脆笑声，语调里透着魅魔独有的荒诞逻辑。

"Um... hello, everyone? Rin thinks there is a very simple succubus solution!" Rin whispered brightly, her unseeing eyes blinking toward the ceiling. "Why do we have to choose between swallowing and the face? Master has plenty of stamina! Next time, Master can paint Sister’s face until it shines like glazed porcelain, and then Rin and Chika-nee can crawl up and lick Sister’s cheeks clean until not a single drop is wasted! That way, Master gets the shocking picture, and the imperial harvest still gets swallowed down! Everyone wins!"

“唔……大家听我说好吗？小铃觉得在魅魔的世界里，这根本就不是单选题呀！”小铃甜丝丝地娇笑着，仰着小脸冲着天花板，“为什么非要在‘吞下去’和‘射在脸上’里面二选一呢？主人的体力那么好！下次主人完全可以先把姐姐的脸当成瓷娃娃一样涂得满满当当，然后由小铃和千夏姐姐凑上去，一点一点把姐姐脸上的每一滴都给舔得干干净净吞进肚子里嘛！这样一来，主人既饱了眼福，皇家的甘霖又一点没浪费！这不就皆大欢喜了吗？！”

"Rin! You little demon, don't you start offering to turn my face into a dessert platter!" Sayuri cried out in mock horror, grabbing a small silk cushion and playfully tossing it at the giggling blind girl.

“小铃！你这只无法无天的小妖精，少在这儿出馊主意把姐姐的脸当甜品盘子使唤！”小百合羞恼交加地娇呼一声，顺手抓起一只真丝软枕，没好气地朝笑得前仰后合的盲女轻轻砸了过去。

Fujiwara threw his head back and let out a booming, room-shaking laugh, thoroughly intoxicated by Sayuri’s sharp wit and the harem’s scandalous banter. He tightened his massive arm around Sayuri's waist, burying his nose playfully into the crook of her warm neck while she squirmed and giggled in his grip.

藤原仰天爆发出一阵雷鸣般的洪亮大笑，彻底被小百合那句直击灵魂的娇嗔和后宫这帮小娘皮的放肆调侃给逗乐了。他大手一揽，霸道地将小百合丰腴纤细的腰肢死死箍进怀里，整张脸坏心眼地埋进她温热芳香的颈窝深处，惹得怀里的美人一边扭动一边娇笑着躲闪。

"Hahaha! Busted completely!" Fujiwara grinned shamelessly, planting a loud, sloppy kiss on her burning cheek. "My Queen sees right through my imperial greed! You're entirely right—I would have whined about wanting you to swallow it five minutes later! The Emperor wants the cake, wants to eat the cake, and wants to paint the cake with cream! But that’s the perk of being ruler of all under heaven!"

“哈哈哈哈！全让你给说中了！”藤原厚颜无耻地咧嘴大笑，低头在她滚烫发红的脸颊上吧唧狠狠亲了一大口，“还是朕的贤后深知朕的脾气！一点都没错——要是真射在你脸上，五分钟后老子准得后悔没让你吞进肚子里！当皇帝的向来既要又要还要，既想大饱眼福，又想把甘霖全吃干抹净！但这不正是富有四海的特权嘛！”

He swiped a finger playfully across her lower lip, his dark eyes sparkling with boundless perversion. "Fine, fine. Today, you took the loyal swallow. But keep that pretty face prepped, Sayuri... next time we hold court, I’m taking Rin’s advice. You’re getting glazed from forehead to chin, and your sisters are cleaning the canvas!"

他坏笑着伸出食指在她饱满诱人的下唇上轻轻一刮，眼底跳跃着无休无止的极恶神采：“行行行，今儿个全凭你的一片忠心，算你全吞了过关。不过你可把这张倾国倾城的俏脸给老子保养好了，小百合……下回再开朝会，老子非得采纳小铃的妙计不可。先把你从脑门到下巴结结实实涂上一层，再让你的好妹妹们排着队上来把你舔干净！”

The entire salon dissolved into another wave of breathless, sweet, and chaotic giggles. Surrounded by his laughing wives, his sharp-witted queen, and the endless indulgence of his private empire, Fujiwara leaned back in absolute triumph, relishing the sweet, depraved perfection of his domestic realm.

满殿之内再次被一阵甜腻、放浪而又温馨的娇笑与起哄声所彻底淹没。依偎着巧笑嫣然的众女，怀抱着冰雪聪慧的贤后，藤原大剌剌地陷在龙榻深处，在这片荒诞无羁却又蜜里调油的极乐帝国里，尽情享受着身为至尊主宰的无上逍遥。
