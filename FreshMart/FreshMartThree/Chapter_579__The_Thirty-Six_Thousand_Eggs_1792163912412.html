Chapter 579: The Thirty-Six Thousand Eggs

发布时间：2026/10/16 23:18:31

第五百七十九章：三万六千颗龙蛋

Three months into the pregnancy, the "Shared Interdimensional Asset" custody agreement entered a terrifyingly literal, biological phase.

孕期进入第三个月后，“跨维度共享核心资产”的托管协议正式转入了一种字面意义上、极其惊悚生物学阶段。

It all started during a quiet Tuesday afternoon in the grand salon. Miyetsuko, who had been gracefully lounging on the central velvet sofa while sipping celestial nectar and reviewing higher-class tax forms, suddenly froze. Her porcelain-smooth face turned a pale, luminous white. A low, strange, rhythmic rumbling echoed from deep within her abdomen—sounding less like a human pregnancy and more like a high-end automated manufacturing plant shifting into overdrive.

这一切发生在一个星期二下午的宁静午后。正优雅地斜倚在中央天鹅绒沙发上、一边品尝着天界花蜜一边审阅高档税务报表的美悦子突然僵住了。她白瓷般的脸颊瞬间变得煞白。一阵低沉而诡异的、带有某种奇特节律的轰鸣声从她的腹腔深处传了出来——那声音听起来与其说是一个人类孕妇，倒不如说像是一座全自动的高端工业制造流水线正在开足马力超负荷运转。

"Master... Sayuri-nee..." Miyetsuko gasped, her eyes widening in absolute, aristocratic shock as she clutched her bulging belly. "Something... something is dropping!"

“主人……小百合姐姐……”美悦子惊呼一声，双手死死护住微微隆起的小腹，那双美目中写满了贵族式的震撼，“有什么东西……有什么东西要掉出来了！”

Pop. Pop-pop. Pop-pop-pop-pop!

啵！啵啵！啵啵啵啵——！

Before anyone could even blink, shimmering, pearlescent, perfectly spherical eggs began tumbling out from beneath her Tang-dynasty silk robes like an endless cascade of celestial marbles. They bounced softly against the Persian rug, rolling across the floor in a dazzling, glowing wave.

还没等任何人反应过来，一颗颗散发着珍珠般璀璨光泽、绝对完美球形的卵便从她盛唐风格的真丝长裙下滚落而出，宛如一拨接一拨永无止境的仙界弹珠瀑布。它们轻轻撞击在波斯地毯上，伴随着温润的光晕在地砖上铺开，滚成了一片耀眼夺目的发光海洋。

One minute passed. Then two. The torrent of eggs simply refused to stop.

一分钟过去了。两分钟过去了。这场产卵的狂潮根本没有停下来的意思。

By the time the biological event finally sputtered to a halt, the entire grand salon was completely flooded. Mountains of glowing, warm, higher-dimensional dragon eggs filled every square inch of the room, burying the furniture up to the cushions and trapping the astounded members of the imperial family against the walls.

当这场生物学奇观终于缓缓平息时，整个大起居殿已经被彻底淹没。堆积如山、散发着温热高维能量的龙蛋塞满了房间里的每一个角落，把沙发淹没到了只剩靠背的高度，甚至把目瞪口呆的皇室全家生生逼到了墙角。

Wise Empress Sayuri knelt down, carefully picking up one of the glowing spheres. Her macro-economic brain instantly calculated the volume, mass, and statistical absurdity of what had just occurred.

贤后小百合弯下腰，小心翼翼地捧起其中一颗发光的圆球。她那颗精于宏观经济计算的大脑在这一瞬间几乎当机，开始疯狂推演眼前这堆东西的体积、质量以及其统计学上的荒谬程度。

"My... my god," Sayuri murmured, her velvet voice cracking slightly. "It’s not just a single heir. The Heavenly Emperor’s reproductive methodology operates on entirely different biological parameters. Three months per litter... and a single batch yields precisely thirty-six thousand divine offspring."

“我的……我的天哪，”小百合软糯地音调微微发颤，“这根本不是什么单一继承人。天帝陛下的生育逻辑完全采用了一套截然不同的生物学参数。三个月一胎……单单这一窝，竟然产下了整整三万六千个神明后代。”

Thirty-six thousand.

三万六千个。

The grand salon fell into a state of stunned, profound psychological paralysis.

整个大起居殿陷入了一种被彻底震慑后的深度心理瘫痪状态。

Chika grabbed the nearest edge of the sofa, her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaving as she stared out across a literal sea of glowing dragon eggs. Her jaw hit the floor.

千夏一把抓住身旁的沙发边缘，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔剧烈起伏着，瞪大眼睛看着眼前这片望不到头、由发光龙蛋构成的海洋。她的下巴惊得彻底脱臼。

"Thirty-six thousand?! Are you shitting me?!" Chika shrieked, waving her arms wildly over the sea of eggs. "That’s not a pregnancy! That’s an entire interstellar infantry division dropped in one go! Miyetsuko didn't just get pregnant; she literally manufactured an empire’s worth of demigods in ninety days! How the hell are we supposed to feed them, let alone potty-train them?!"

“三万六千个？！你他妈在逗我吧？！”千夏尖叫着用力挥舞着手臂，“这哪里是怀孕生孩子？！这分明是一次性空投了一整支星际步兵师啊！美悦子这哪是怀孕，她分明是在短短九十天里直接流水线制造了一个连的神魔后代！咱们上哪儿找那么多奶水去喂它们，更别提怎么给这三万多只小祖宗把尿了？！”

Miyetsuko, sitting exhausted amidst the sea of glowing pearls, blushed a brilliant, humiliated crimson, adjusting her disheveled silk robes. "His Celestial Majesty did mention... that divine lineages require high-density numerical proliferation to properly populate the cosmos. I... I honestly didn't expect the batch size to be quite this large."

坐在发光珍珠海洋中央、显得筋疲力尽的美悦子羞涩得满脸通红，手忙脚乱地整理着凌乱的真丝长袍。“天帝陛下之前确实提过……神明血脉需要庞大的人口基数才能妥善繁衍星河。奴婢……奴婢也没想到这一胎的产量竟然会这么富余。”

From her floating cushion, blind Rin swung her legs, completely unfazed by the thirty-six thousand newborn cosmic entities rolling around her. The blind little succubus picked up a glowing dragon egg like a basketball, patting it cheerfully.

坐在漂浮软垫上的盲人小铃晃荡着双腿，对身边滚来滚去的三万六千个宇宙级新生儿毫无畏惧。这只瞎眼的小魅魔像拿篮球一样抱起一颗发光的龙蛋，开心地拍了拍。

"Um... hello! Thirty-six thousand babies!" Rin chirped happily. "That’s amazing! If we put them in the underworld daycare, we can train them to ride skeleton ponies and throw fireballs by next week! Rin can be the headmistress of the ultimate demon-god kindergarten!"

“唔……太好啦！三万六千个小宝宝耶！”小铃开心地欢呼道，“这可太棒啦！如果把它们送到地狱托儿所去，下个星期就能训练它们骑着骷髅小马去扔火球啦！小铃申请当这个终极神魔幼儿园的园长大人！”

At the center of the room, standing ankle-deep in glowing divine eggs, Emperor Fujiwara stared out over the endless sea of his newly acquired, half-celestial offspring.

在房间的正中央，深陷于发光神蛋海洋之中的藤原大帝默默注视着这片无边无际、属于他自己的半神骨肉。

For a long moment, the absolute tyrant remained completely silent. Then, a slow, magnificent, utterly unrepentant capitalist grin spread across his face. The initial shock melted away, replaced by the grand, terrifying vision of a warlord who had just realized he had accidentally acquired an army of thirty-six thousand demigod heirs.

漫长的沉默过后，这位绝对暴君的脸上突然缓缓爬上了一个极其壮阔、厚颜无耻的资本主义狂笑。最初的震惊烟消云散取而代之的是一个野心勃勃的宏伟构想——一个突然意识到自己不小心批发了一整支三万六千人神二代军团的野心家图景。

"An army," Fujiwara muttered softly, his dark eyes sparkling with demonic ambition. Then, his voice rose into a booming, tyrannical roar that shook the golden ceiling. "Thirty-six thousand divine dragon-seed heirs! Forget potty-training! Forget milk bottles! We are establishing the Fujiwara Interstellar Demigod Academy! We will arm every single one of these little bastards with plasma rifles, ship them off to conquer the remaining unmapped galaxies, and turn our family into the ruling dynasty of the entire universe!"

“一支军队，”藤原喃喃低语着，漆黑的双眼里闪烁着野心勃勃的资本光芒。紧接着，他的声音陡然拔高，化作一声震动穹顶的暴君狂怒大笑，“三万六千个纯血龙种继承人！还把什么尿布！喂什么奶瓶！老子要立刻成立‘藤原星际半神军事学院’！等这帮小王八蛋一孵化出来，人手配发一把等离子步枪，直接给老子发配到宇宙边荒去横扫所有未被征服的星系，把咱们藤原家族的名字刻进全宇宙每一个星核里去！”
