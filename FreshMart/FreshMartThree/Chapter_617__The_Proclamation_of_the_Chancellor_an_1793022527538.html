第六百一十七章：首辅讨伐令与暴君的罪己诏

The grand salon of the imperial palace was uncharacteristically tense, though the atmosphere was still marinated in that signature, delightfully shameless brand of domestic absurdity.

皇宫的大起居殿内罕见地弥漫着一丝紧张的气氛，不过空气中依然弥漫着那股标志性、厚颜无耻且令人愉悦的恶趣味日常调调。

Following their traumatic, thoroughly violated afternoon in the royal bathhouse, the Capital’s First Beauty and her teenage daughter had managed to crawl back to the opulent estate of the Grand Chancellor. Soaked in sulfur water, blood, and the lingering remnants of imperial seed, the traumatized mother and daughter threw themselves at the Chancellor’s feet, weeping hysterically and recounting the horrific, unbridled public atrocities committed by the deposed Emperor.

在经历了皇家澡堂里那场惨绝人寰、彻底被疯狂凌虐的下午后，京城第一美人和她的宝贝千金终于连滚带爬地逃回了权势熏天的首辅府邸。浑身沾满硫磺泉水、血迹以及皇室浓精的母女俩跪倒在首辅脚下痛哭流涕，声泪俱下地控诉着那位废帝在澡堂里犯下的种种丧心病狂、无法无天的公开暴行。

The Chancellor’s fury shook the foundations of the capital. Within hours, a fiery, high-priority imperial proclamation was blasted across every communication network, star system, and planetary screen in the realm.

首辅震怒，滔天的杀意瞬间震动了整个京城的基础地基。短短数小时内，一道杀气腾腾的最高级别讨伐檄文通过各大通信网络和星系全息屏幕传遍了整个帝国。

“The current Emperor is devoid of virtue, tyrannical and utterly immoral! He is a liar, worse than a beast!” The Chancellor’s proclamation thundered across the empire. “At the Royal Women’s Bathhouse, he publicly assaulted the wives and daughters of high-ranking officials! He explicitly promised to spare the daughter if her mother submitted in her place, yet he broke his word and brutalized them both! Such a shameless, dishonorable tyrant has earned the wrath of heaven and earth! I hereby proclaim to the entire realm: Fujiwara has three days to kneel publicly and apologize. Otherwise, I shall personally lead my private mercenary armies from four star systems to the capital and raze the imperial palace to the ground!”

“当今天子无德，暴虐无道！言而无信，禽兽不如！”首辅的讨伐檄文在帝国上空轰然炸响。“其于皇家女澡堂当众凌辱命妇与千金！此前明言若母亲代女受辱则饶恕其女，如今却出尔反尔，母女俱遭惨烈摧残！此等毫无诚信之暴君，人神共愤！老夫今特向天下宣告：限藤原三日之内公开下跪谢罪，否则老夫必率四星系私人雇佣兵大军兵临城下，踏平皇宫！”

Inside the grand salon, the imperial household gathered around the holographic projection of the proclamation, listening to the furious Chancellor roar on screen.

大殿内，皇室全家正围聚在全息投影的檄文前，津津有味地听着屏幕里首辅那气急败坏的怒吼。

"Well, well, well. Look at the old man throwing a galactic temper tantrum," Fujiwara chuckled, leaning back on the velvet sofa with a glass of red wine while Chika fed him grapes. "He’s calling me a liar because I promised to spare his daughter if her mother offered herself up, and then I went ahead and brutally sodomized both of them anyway. Is breaking a promise to a political rival really considered a major character flaw in this empire?"

“哟哟哟，瞧瞧这老头子发飙的架势，还挺有星际大片的感觉嘛，”藤原端着红酒斜倚在天鹅绒沙发上轻笑着，千夏在一旁贴心地给他喂着葡萄，“他居然骂我是个骗子，说好只要她妈代受辱就放过女儿，结果朕母女俩一个没放过、全给狠狠操了一遍。在咱们帝国，对政敌出尔反尔难道也算是什么不可饶恕的道德污点吗？”

Sayuri stood gracefully beside the obsidian desk, elegantly shaking her velvet fan as her Tang-dynasty silks rustled. As regent, her pragmatic macroeconomic calculus immediately kicked in.

小百合优雅地站在黑曜石书桌旁，盛唐长袍沙沙作响，手中丝绒折扇轻轻摇晃。作为摄政女王，她那精明务实的宏观经济账本瞬间在脑海里转了起来。

"Actually, my Lord, in the political marketplace, personal credit and promise-keeping are considered core intangible assets," Sayuri pointed out with a serene smile. "However, mobilizing four star systems of mercenary fleets right now would incur unacceptable logistical expenditures and temporarily disrupt our fiscal quarterly profits. Therefore, my macroeconomic advice is simple: just apologize."

“其实吧，夫君，在政治资本市场上，个人信用和信守承诺确实属于核心无形资产，”小百合带着温婉的微笑提醒道，“不过，眼下要是跟他的四星系雇佣兵舰队硬碰硬，会产生巨大的后勤财政支出，还会暂时打扰咱们本季度的财政净利润。所以，奴家的宏观经济学建议非常简单：服个软，道个歉就行了。”

"Apologize?!" Fujiwara balked, his tyrannical eyebrows twitching. "The absolute Emperor of the galaxy apologizing to a grumpy old Chancellor just because I broke a promise to double-team his family?!"

“道歉？！”藤原眉头一挑，暴君的傲气顿时涌了上来，“堂堂银河系绝对大帝，就因为对政敌母女俩出尔反尔、顺手多双飞了一把，居然要给一个老头子低头认错？！”

"Oh, stop whining, Boss," Chika chimed in, rolling her eyes as her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaved. "Think about it! A simple, two-page 'Imperial Confession Letter' is all it takes to make the Chancellor call off his army. And besides, you already got to thoroughly ravish a high-society mother and tear open her teenage daughter's virgin asshole against a bronze statue! You tasted the ultimate forbidden fruit; trading a piece of paper and a fake apology for total peace and quiet is the greatest ROI in imperial history!"

“哎呀，别在这儿装什么贞洁烈男了，老板，”千夏没好气地翻了个白眼，那对因胀奶而饱满的巨乳剧烈起伏着，“你想想这买卖多划算！区区一份两页纸的《罪己诏》就能让首辅乖乖把大军撤回去。再说了，你今天已经在澡堂里把门阀主母和她女儿的雏菊给彻底干烂了！绝世母女花你早就吃干抹净、爽得灵魂出窍了；现在用一张纸和一个口头道歉换来后宫的太平日子，绝对是帝国历史上投资回报率最高的一笔买卖！”

Miyetsuko adjusted her No. 183828 Death God glasses, nodding in agreement. "As the Supreme Goddess of Death, I must concur. A formal apology prevents unnecessary casualties—which means fewer death registration forms for my underworld bureau to process. Swallow your pride, Your Majesty. Writing a confession letter costs nothing compared to losing sleep over a civil war."

美悦子扶了扶183828号死神眼镜，深以为然地点点头：“身为最高死神，本统领举双手赞成。发个致歉公告能避免不必要的伤亡——这意味着我的冥界车管所能少填几万张人口死亡档案。低头吧，陛下。跟避免一场内战相比，写封认错信又不会少块肉。”

Rin bounced on her cushion, giggling mischievously. "Yes, yes! Write the apology letter! Make it super emotional and dramatic so the Chancellor thinks he won, and then we can laugh at him behind his back!"

小铃在软垫上兴奋地蹦跶，调皮地娇笑道：“对呀对呀！赶紧写认错信！肉麻一点、煽情一点，让那老头子以为自己赢麻了，回头咱们关起门来在背地里笑死他！”

Fujiwara crossed his arms, staring at his adoring, pragmatic harem. He let out a long, defeated sigh, though a wicked grin soon crept back onto his face.

藤原双手抱胸，无语地看着这群精明算计的爱妃们。他长叹了一口气，不过脸上很快又浮现出了那副恶劣的坏笑。

"Fine, fine! You win!" Fujiwara relented, grabbing a golden quill. "If a simple piece of paper can make the old bastard call off his fleet while I get to keep the memory of that bronze-statue sodomy, I’ll write the finest, most pathetic confession letter this galaxy has ever seen!"

“行行行！算你们狠！”藤原妥协地抓起一支黄金羽毛笔，“既然一张破纸能打发那老杂毛撤兵，又能让朕留着青铜雕像干千金的美好回忆，那朕今天就亲自操刀，给全宇宙写一份感人肺腑、认错态度极其恶劣的绝世《罪己诏》！”

Ten minutes later, the imperial printing press officially published Fujiwara’s formal "Imperial Confession Letter." In it, the Emperor profusely apologized for his "lack of administrative integrity," expressed "profound sorrow" for failing to keep his promise to the mother and daughter, and promised to reflect upon his horny impulses.

十分钟后，帝国新闻署正式对外发布了藤原亲笔签署的《罪己诏》。在这份公告中，皇帝痛心疾首地检讨了自己的“行政失信行为”，对未能遵守诺言“母女双收后却出尔反尔”表达了深切的歉意，并郑重承诺今后一定加强自我约束、深刻反省下半身的冲动。

Upon reading the humiliating confession letter, the Grand Chancellor felt his public honor was miraculously restored. Declaring that the Emperor had finally bowed to justice, the Chancellor graciously called off his four-star-system mercenary fleet, returning the capital to its usual, corrupt state of peace.

当首辅读到这份充满羞辱性的认错公告时，居然觉得自己那饱受摧残的门阀尊严得到了奇迹般的救赎。首辅大度地宣称废帝终于向正义低头，随后大发慈悲地命令四星系雇佣兵舰队调头返航，京城表面上的局势再次回到了那片腐烂而虚假的和平中。

Back in the grand salon, Fujiwara tossed the quill aside, pulled Chika and Miyetsuko back onto his lap, and poured himself another glass of wine.

大殿内，藤原随手扔下羽毛笔，把千夏和美悦子重新拽回腿上，给自己满上了一杯红酒。

"Well, the crisis is averted," Fujiwara grinned devilishly. "Now that the Chancellor is pacified, who wants to help the Emperor celebrate our diplomatic triumph with a little round fifty of our own?"

“行了，危机解除，”藤原邪恶地咧嘴一笑，“既然首辅那老杂毛已经被哄开心了，接下来哪位爱妃愿意帮朕用一场轰轰烈烈的大满贯，来庆祝咱们这场外交大胜利呀？”
