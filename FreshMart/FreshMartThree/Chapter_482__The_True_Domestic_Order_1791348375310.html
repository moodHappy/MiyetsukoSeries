第四百八十二章：真正的家庭秩序

The brief, ridiculous notion of outside "boy-toys" vanished from the penthouse like smoke. The moment Fujiwara settled back into his armchair, the true, irreversible reality of his household reasserted itself. Any lingering memory of their former lives—of corporate boardrooms, foreign intelligence networks, and international banking—had been thoroughly washed away by the Emperor's depraved domestic conditioning.

那阵关于在外包养“小白脸”的荒唐戏言，转眼便如青烟般在顶层公寓里消散得无影无踪。当藤原重新陷进他的宽大扶手椅时，这个家庭真实而不可逆转的现状再次显露无遗。所有关于过去的零星记忆——无论是跨国企业的董事会、跨国情报网络，还是国际金融战场——早就被这位帝王荒诞不经的闺房调教彻底冲刷得干干净净。

In the center of the sofa, Chika was slumped lazily across a mountain of velvet pillows, wearing nothing but an unbuttoned, milky-pink silk nursing gown. Her hands were lazily cupping her heavy, swollen breasts, her fingers stained with sweet white droplets that continuously seeped through the fabric. The former corporate titan couldn't even remember what a stock ticker looked like; her entire consciousness was now dictated purely by the rhythmic pressure in her chest.

在沙发的正中央，千夏整个人懒洋洋地陷在天鹅绒靠垫堆里，身上只松松垮垮地套着一件解开了衣襟的乳粉色真丝哺乳裙。她那一双小手正慵懒地托着胸前沉甸甸、饱胀发热的雪峰，白皙的指尖上还沾着不断渗出布料的甜美奶渍。这位曾经的金融女王如今连股票代码长什么样都记不清了，她的整个脑海早已完全被胸口那一阵阵规律性的胀满感所彻底占据。

"Master... look at them, they're getting tight again already," Chika whimpered softly, her cheeks flushed with a sleepy, dazed blush as she gently squeezed one soft, overflowing mound. Come be a good boy and have a snack..."

Sayuri sat gracefully beside her, holding a delicate porcelain bowl filled with warm, sweet fermented rice soup. The former queen smiled with deep, maternal indulgence, gently blowing on a silver spoon before feeding it to the drowsy little dairy cow.

小百合体态婀娜地坐在她身旁，手里端着一只盛满温热酒酿蛋的精致白瓷小碗。这位前女王眼底流淌着浓浓的宠溺与母性光辉，用银汤匙轻轻吹凉了羹汤，温柔地递到这只睡眼惺忪的小奶牛嘴边。

"Open wide, little sister," Sayuri cooed sweetly, dabbing a stray drop of syrup from Chika's lips with a silk handkerchief. "You need to finish every drop so your supply stays thick and sweet for Master. You're the whole family's most precious little treasure now, you know."

“乖，张嘴，好妹妹，”小百合柔声哄着，拿真丝手帕轻轻拭去千夏唇边溢出的一点甜汁，“把这一整碗都喝得干干净净，产出来的奶水才能一直又浓又甜，把咱们的主人伺候得舒舒服服的。你现在可是咱们全家最金贵的小心肝呢，知道不知道？”

Chika happily swallowed the soup, letting out a kittenish, contented sigh as she rubbed her cheek against Sayuri's soft shoulder like a pampered pet.

千夏乖乖咽下温热的甜羹，发出一声小猫般惬意的呢喃，像只被宠坏的幼崽一样，把泛红的小脸亲昵地蹭在小百合柔软温热的香肩上。

Meanwhile, on the plush carpet right at Fujiwara's feet, the fearsome "shadow commander" of the past was nowhere to be found. Miyetsuko was draped in a fluffy, peach-colored robe, curled up against the base of his chair. Her combat knives had been locked away in storage weeks ago, replaced by a pliant, dewy-eyed devotion that mirrored Sayuri's own.

而在藤原脚边的厚实地毯上，曾经那位令人闻风丧胆的“暗影指挥官”更是不见半点踪影。美悦子裹着一袭毛茸茸的水蜜桃色睡袍，整个人乖巧地蜷缩在他的椅脚旁。她的特种军刺早在几周前就被彻底锁进了库房深处，取而代之的，是一种丝毫不逊色于小百合的娇柔顺从与波光流转的秋水眸光。

Miyetsuko gently rested her chin against Fujiwara's knee, gazing up at him with wide, bashful eyes that seemed perpetually lost in a dream of pleasure.

美悦子轻轻将下巴枕在藤原的膝盖上，仰起那张精致动人的俏脸，满眼含羞带怯地凝视着他，眼神里仿佛永远沉浸在昨夜销魂蚀骨的欢愉余韵之中。

"Master..." Miyetsuko whispered, her voice unbelievably timid and soft, trailing her slender fingertips affectionately across his ankle. "My hips still feel completely weak from last night... Every time I close my eyes, I can still feel that nine-shallow, one-deep rhythm echoing inside me. It felt so heavenly, it made me cry like a child... Will you... will you please soften me like that again tonight? I never want to be that cold, lonely person with guns ever again. I just want to stay right here, melting in your lap..."

Blind Rin clapped her small hands softly from the corner, smiling with serene, succubus bliss as she leaned against Miyetsuko's shoulder.

盲人小铃在角落里轻轻拍了拍手，那张纯净无瑕的小脸上绽放出安详甜蜜的笑容，顺势娇憨地依偎在美悦子的肩头。

"Um... hello, Master?" Rin whispered sweetly. "Miyetsuko-nee smells just like warm peaches and vanilla now! She's so soft and warm to cuddle with! And Chika-nee smells like fresh morning cream. With everyone so gentle and sweet, Rin doesn't even need to use her succubus magic to make the house happy. Master's big hands have already smoothed all the sharp edges away!"

“唔……主人听我说好吗？”小铃甜甜地细语着，“美悦子姐姐现在身上闻起来全是甜桃子和香草的温软味道呢！抱起来又滑又软，舒服极了！千夏姐姐闻起来也是新鲜纯正的奶油香气。大家现在都变得这么温柔、这么甜糯，小铃甚至都不用动用魅魔的幻术来维系家里的快活了。全靠主人那双大手，把屋里所有人的棱角都给彻底抚平啦！”

Fujiwara let out a deep, rumble of absolute imperial satisfaction. He reached down with one hand to stroke Miyetsuko's soft hair, letting her purr contentedly against his palm, while beckoning Chika over with his other arm.

藤原胸膛深处滚出一阵志得意满的帝王低笑。他一手向下抚摸着美悦子柔顺的发丝，任由她在自己的掌心下发出满足温顺的轻哼；另一只手臂则随意一招，将瘫在沙发上的千夏也一并揽了过来。

No more military tension. No more cold financial calculations. Just his little milk cow begging to be drained, his tamed warrior begging to be melted, and his household resting in absolute, delightfully depraved peace.

没有了剑拔弩张的战术戒备，更没有了冷冰冰的金融算计。眼前只剩下哼哼唧唧求着被吮吸的小奶牛，眼波流转求着被彻底揉碎的娇柔佳人，以及这整座彻底沉沦、在其乐融融的放浪形骸中尽享极乐的后宫帝国。
