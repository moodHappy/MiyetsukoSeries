Chapter 149: The Orphanage Reception

发布时间：2026/8/26 23:59:40

第百四十九章：孤儿院里的荒唐哺乳

The short drive to the local orphanage on Donglai Island was an experience none of them would ever forget. In the backseat, Chika sat rigid with mortification, clutching the wailing infant to her chest while desperately trying to keep up her "phantom nursing" duties as Fujiwara had instructed, her open-crotch maid uniform adding an extra layer of absurd vulnerability to her predicament.

驶往东莱岛当地孤儿院的车程，注定是一段让所有人都终生难忘的奇妙旅程。在轿车后排，千夏羞耻得浑身僵硬，紧紧抱着那个哭闹不休的小婴儿，硬着头皮履行着藤原临时委派的“虚无哺乳”职责。她身上那套标志性的开裆女仆装，更让这副画面平添了几分令人忍俊不禁的荒诞色彩。

Upon arriving, the group marched straight into the front reception office of the orphanage, led by a thoroughly amused Fujiwara.

抵达目的地后，在心情大好的藤原带领下，一行人气势汹汹地走进了孤儿院的前台接待大厅。

The receptionist, a kind-looking middle-aged woman, looked up with a welcoming smile as they approached. Before she could offer a greeting, Fujiwara stepped forward and dropped the bombshell with a completely straight face.

前台坐着一位面容慈祥的中年女工作人员，见有人进来便微笑着抬起头准备接待。还没等对方开口寒暄，藤原便大步流星地走上前去，一本正经地抛出了一枚重磅炸弹：

"Good day," Fujiwara announced smoothly, gesturing toward the baby in Chika's arms. "We're here to make a donation of sorts—specifically, an abandoned infant left on our doorstep. We need to officially hand him over to your care. Oh, and by the way, he hasn't eaten a single thing for a full day, so his makeshift wet nurse is trying her best to keep him pacified."

“您好，”藤原面不改色心不跳地开口道，随手指了指千夏怀里的小家伙，“我们是来做点‘爱心捐赠’的——确切地说，是个被人遗弃在我们店门口的小婴儿。我们需要正式把他移交给你们照顾。哦，对了，这小家伙已经整整一天没吃过东西了，所以他的临时‘湿乳妈’正在尽力安抚他呢。”

The receptionist blinked, her professional smile freezing instantly. Her gaze slowly shifted from Fujiwara to Miyetsuko, Rin, and Sayuri, who were struggling to hold back their grins, and finally landed directly on Chika.

女工作人员当场愣住，职业化的微笑瞬间僵在了脸。她的目光先是狐疑地扫过藤原，又看了看旁边几个强忍着笑意的女人（美悦子、铃和小百合），最后直直地落在了千夏身上。

There stood Chika—dressed in a scandalous, high-end open-crotch maid uniform with her sensitive lower body entirely bare, desperately holding a crying infant to her chest while whispering red-faced sweet-nothings to the baby, pretending to feed him from a completely dry, non-lactating bosom.

只见千夏正穿着那套引人注目、下半身毫无遮挡的开裆女仆装，满脸通红地将哭闹的婴儿紧紧贴在胸口，红着耳根小声哄着孩子，试图用空空如野、一滴奶都没有的胸口去“喂饱”这个饿了一整天的小祖宗。

The receptionist's jaw dropped. Her eyes widened into absolute, unadulterated saucers as her brain struggled violently to process the surreal tableau before her: an un-lactating, teenage-looking anime maid in an open-crotch uniform acting as a pacifier for an abandoned baby.

女工作人员的下巴差点惊掉在地上，双眼圆睁得如同铜铃一般。她的脑子在这一刻彻底短路，完全无法理解眼前这幅超现实的画面：一个穿着开裆制服、看起来年纪轻轻的纯情少女，正顶着零产奶量的胸口，一本正经地给弃婴当“人形安抚奶嘴”。

"Um... excuse me...?" the receptionist stammered, her voice pitching an octave higher as she stared at Chika's chest, then down at her exposed outfit. "A... a wet nurse? With... with zero milk? And... what is she wearing?!"

“额……请问一下……？”工作人员结结巴巴地开口，声音因为过度震惊而拔高了一个八度，难以置信地在千夏的胸口和她露骨的打扮之间来回游移，“湿……湿乳妈？而且一滴奶都没有……？还有，她身上穿的这到底是什么制服啊？！”

The dam broke.

这一问彻底击溃了众人的心理防线。

Rin instantly lost all control, doubling over and howling with laughter so hard she had to lean against the reception counter for support. Sayuri covered her mouth, her shoulders shaking violently as soft giggles escaped her lips. Miyetsuko couldn't help a rare, loud snort of amusement, pushing up her glasses as her shoulders quaked with laughter. Even Fujiwara burst into a roaring bark of laughter, patting the bewildered receptionist on the shoulder.

铃当场笑得直不起腰来，双手撑着前台柜台笑得浑身发抖；小百合捂着嘴，肩膀剧烈耸动着发出银铃般的娇笑；美悦子也忍不住发出一声轻笑，推了推眼镜掩饰着忍俊不禁的笑意；就连藤原也放声狂笑起来，一把拍了拍彻底陷入石化的工作人员的肩膀。

Standing in the center of the chaos, Chika felt like she was burning alive, her face buried deep in the baby's blanket as she whimpered in sweet, excruciating embarrassment, turning the orphanage reception room into an absolute house of comedic pandemonium.

站在风暴中心的千夏羞耻得恨不得当场人间蒸发，整张脸深深地埋进婴儿的毛毯里发出委屈的呜咽。整个孤儿院的前台大厅，瞬间被一阵欢快而爆笑的空气彻底淹没。
