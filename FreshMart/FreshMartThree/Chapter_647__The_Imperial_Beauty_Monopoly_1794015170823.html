第六百四十七章：皇室美色垄断

The Imperial Grand Council formally promulgated the long-awaited "National Aesthetic Scoring and Companionship Regulatory Edict," causing an immediate operational meltdown across all municipal registries.

帝国枢密院正式向全星系颁布了酝酿已久的《国家颜值评级与包养行为规制法案》，瞬间在各级官衙与市井网络间引发了地动山摇般的剧烈震动。

Under this sweeping administrative order, the empire instituted a compulsory ten-point physical grading registry for all female subjects, managed directly by the Imperial Household Department's aesthetic algorithms.

在这道大包大揽的行政命令下，帝国对全天下所有适龄女性强制推行了十分制的肉体容貌评级总册，由内务府直属的美学算法直接接管并每日动态核算。

The most controversial section of the statute focused on the commercial "Sugar Daddy" marketplace, introducing a bizarre civic welfare mechanism: any aging patron purchasing sexual companionship possessed the statutory right to enjoy three full sessions of bedchamber services, after which he could invoke the "Unconditional Consumer Remorse Clause" to demand a complete, one-hundred-percent refund of the sponsorship fee.

法案中最引人瞩目且惊世骇俗的条款，针对民间的“糖爹包养”市场推出了一项匪夷所思的惠民机制：凡花钱买春包养的老汉，在预付金银并完整享用了三次周到的榻侧肉体侍奉后，皆可行使法定“无条件反悔权”，全额索回最初垫付的全部包养银两。

This effectively legalized structural, state-sanctioned free sexual access for hundreds of millions of elderly, impoverished commoners across the star systems.

这在实质上等于由朝廷出面背书，为全星系亿万囊中羞涩的市井老头量身定做了一套合情合法的“国家级白嫖福利”。

Simultaneously, the second half of the edict struck the upper social tiers with draconian force: any female subject graded above five points was strictly barred from being kept as a sugar baby, entered into marriage contracts, or engaging in romantic relationships, under pain of high treason.

而法案的后半段，则以雷霆手段狠狠砸向了中上层民间社会：凡经官署核定颜值评级在五分以上的妙龄女子，一律严禁私自接受民间包养，严禁缔结婚约，甚至严禁私下谈情说爱，违者直接以谋逆重罪论处。

The stated legislative rationale was clear and unvarnished: to ensure the Imperial Reserve Pool was never contaminated by civilian seed before the Emperor conducted his personal selections.

律法给出的官方法理依据更是直白坦荡到了极点：为了确保浩瀚星海中的特级美色资源在九五之尊亲自检阅开苞前，绝不被任何市井凡夫俗子的浊气提前污染。

Inside the grand imperial salon, Emperor Fujiwara was reclined across the obsidian throne, an embroidered cushion supporting his head while Miyetsuko massaged his temples.

大起居殿内，藤原大帝大刺刺地仰卧在黑曜石王座上，后颈枕着锦缎软垫，舒舒服服地由着美悦子替他拿捏按揉着太阳穴。

"A triumph of dual-track macroeconomic governance!" Fujiwara declared, waving an ivory chopstick toward the holographic policy projection with immense pride. "The commoner elders toil in the factories and shipyards all their lives with no retirement joy; this clause allows them to sample domestic warmth three times in a row and walk away with their wallets intact! A magnificent sovereign subsidy!"

“这才是双轨制宏观治理的无上杰作！”藤原手握一根象牙象牙箸，志得意满地指着半空中的全息政策光幕高声自夸，“底层的糟老头子们在工坊船坞里辛劳了一辈子，临老也没个知冷知热的去处；朕给他们设立这‘三响反悔全退款’条款，让他们白白体验三宿温香软玉还能全身而退、一文不差！何等浩荡的皇恩浩荡！”

Chika casually peeled a roasted peanut, tossed it into her mouth, and let out an earthy, dismissive snort as her heavy, milk-laden breasts shifted under her silk tunic.

千夏随手捏碎一颗炒花生仁丢进嘴里，胸前那对沉甸甸、胀满丰腴的饱满巨乳随之乱晃，没好气地嗤笑出声。

"Subsidizing the elderly with free rides—only you could dress up systematic swindling as public charity," Chika scoffed, rolling her eyes. "Those poor mid-tier sugar babies are going to spend all week shaving their legs and putting on perfume, only to have some wrinkled grandfather mount them three nights in a row, take his cash back, and leave them with sticky sheets and an empty purse."

“拿这种白嫖勾当当敬老福利，全宇宙也就只有您老人家能把这等下三滥的赖账手段包装得跟慈善工程似的，”千夏翻了个大白眼，“那些三四分的寻常窑姐儿这下可倒了大霉，辛辛苦苦刮了腿毛喷了香水伺候三天，回头叫那帮满脸褶子的死老头连干三回拔鸟就退款，除了一床烂摊子，连个铜板都落不着。”

Sayuri leaned gracefully against the jade railing, slowly unfolding her velvet fan as an amused, aristocratic sneer curled the corners of her lips.

小百合斜倚在汉白玉栏杆旁，漫不经心地摇开丝绒折扇，朱唇噙着一抹清冷而深邃的权贵式讥诮。

"From the perspective of municipal stability, the policy is brutally effective," Sayuri purred smoothly. "Allowing commoners to endlessly exploit low-grade companionship vents the biological unrest of the working class at zero budgetary cost to the treasury. The court spends not a single copper credit, while millions of aging laborers hail the sovereign as their benevolent benefactor."

“若从维稳与民政统筹的尺度来看，此策倒是狠辣而精妙得很，”小百合软糯地剖析道，“让市井闲汉老者无成本地在庸脂俗粉身上宣泄生理躁动，既平抑了民间的治安暴戾之气，又无需内库拨款分文。朝廷不花一个子儿，数以亿计的底层光棍却反要对陛下感激涕零，直呼万岁呢。”

Miyetsuko adjusted her 183828号 Death God glasses, tapping her stylus against the census projections on her terminal.

美悦子推了推鼻梁上的183828号死神眼镜，手中的冥界手写笔在户政光幕上飞快勾画。

"The true brilliance lies in the five-point demographic threshold," Miyetsuko noted dryly, her voice dripping with bureaucratic coldness. "By legally classifying every woman with an aesthetic value of 5.1 and above as an untouchable state reserve, we freeze an entire demographic cohort in place. No boyfriends, no husbands, no local patrons. They must preserve their freshness in perpetual isolation until the palace logistics fleet arrives."

“真正的杀招还在于那条五分线的人口分流壁垒，”美悦子语气漠然，一针见血地补刀，“凡容貌评级在五点一分以上的女性，一律在法理上被圈定为‘国家级不可动用特种储备’，等于在源头上直接冻结了她们的所有民事结合权。不准谈情，不准成亲，更不准民间包养。她们唯有在深闺中乖乖守着清白身子，安分守己地候着内廷采选飞艇的登门。”

Rin chuckled, swishing her pointed succubus tail across the marble tiles with wicked delight.

小铃倚在王座扶手边笑得花枝乱颤，尖尖的魅魔尾巴在地砖上欢快地拍打出轻响。

"It’s totally brilliant! The girls rated five and below are condemned to get drained and refunded by stingy grandfathers, while the girls rated six and up are kept under house arrest like prize heifers waiting for the butcher's knife!" Rin giggled, clapping her hands. "If an attractive girl wants to actually date a handsome boy her own age, she has to deliberately smear soot on her cheeks or eat until she gains thirty pounds just to fail the score!"

“这招简直绝了！五分往下的底层姑娘注定要被抠门老汉白嫖退款，而六分往上的漂亮姑娘则被画地为牢，跟待宰的特级小羊羔似的被朝廷死死盯着！”小铃拍手咯咯直笑，“哪个年轻小娘子要是真想跟同龄的情郎谈情说爱，只怕得天天往脸上抹锅灰、逼着自己胡吃海塞增肥几十斤，好把自个儿的评分给狠狠刷下去呢！”

Fujiwara burst into booming laughter, pulling Chika onto his lap with one arm and catching Sayuri’s slender wrist with the other, completely untroubled by moral qualms.

藤原大帝听罢仰天狂笑，一手霸道地将千夏拉进怀里狠狠搂住，另一手顺势扣住小百合温香软玉般的纤细手腕，眉宇间尽是无法无天的暴君快意。

"A pristine sovereign garden requires uncompromising pruning!" Fujiwara declared grandly, nuzzling against Chika's fragrant collarbone. "Let the old men rejoice in their risk-free bedside refunds, and let the peerless beauties guard their chastity for the throne! When the sovereign is well-fed, the entire empire sleeps in peace!"

“打理好这片浩瀚星海的大花园，本就该用这等铁腕规矩去好生修剪！”藤原在千夏喷香的锁骨处使劲蹭了蹭，大言不惭地放浪大笑，“让民间的白嫖老汉们且去欢呼谢恩，让全天下的名媛绝色皆给朕守身如玉！唯有朕这天子吃饱喝足了，这锦绣江山方能万民安寝啊！”
