第四百七十四章：削去一切爵位

The next morning, Fujiwara gathered the entire family in the living room and signed a grand, gold-embossed decree. With a single stroke of his fountain pen, he officially stripped Sayuri of every single imperial title and aristocratic honor.

第二天早晨，藤原把一大家子人全都召集到了客厅，郑重其事地签署了一份烫金的大号法令。随着钢笔笔尖划过的清脆声响，他正式削去了小百合身上所有的皇家头衔与贵族爵位。

She was no longer the Red Date Queen. She was no longer the Holy Virtuous Empress. And her terrifying title as the Absolute Plenipotentiary Executive Officer with a universal veto was formally revoked.

她不再是红枣皇后了。她不再是圣德贤良皇后了。至于那个令人闻风丧胆、享有一票否决权的“全权专断执政官”，也一并被正式撤销。

Of course, this wasn't because their beloved Sayuri had committed any crime, nor was she being banished to an icy, neglected cold palace. Quite the opposite: the entire demotion was executed at Sayuri’s own desperate, tearful insistence. She had argued that elevating her above her sisters was terrible for household harmony, and frankly, she was terrified of having her intimate bedroom trials immortalized as official imperial law.

当然，这绝不是因为大家挚爱的小百合犯下了什么弥天大错，更不是要将她打入冷宫。恰恰相反，这一切全都是小百合自己昨晚哭笑不得、软磨硬泡主动要求的。她的理由极其充分：自己绝不应该凌驾于众姐妹之上，这种特权极其不利于家庭的团结与和谐；而且说实话，她实在太害怕自己的那些闺房私密试验，隔三差五就被立成帝国的法定头衔了。

Even with all the crowns, seals, and executive vetoes erased, every single soul in the syndicate—and across the globe—knew exactly how indispensable she truly was. Corporate titles were meaningless anyway; her supreme, unassailable importance to Fujiwara lay squarely in the night, in the sheets, and in the raw, primal essence of their insatiable sex life.

即便所有的王冠、玉玺和一票否决权全被一笔勾销，整个辛迪加乃至全世界的每一个人，心里都跟明镜似的，清清楚楚地知道她到底有多么无可替代。企业头衔终究只是虚名；她对藤原那至高无上、坚不可摧的重要性，始终深深扎根在深夜里、床单间，以及他们那永远不知餍足的狂野性爱之中。

"There. The imperial registry is officially cleared," Fujiwara announced, setting the pen down with a wicked grin. He leaned back on the sofa, wrapping his arm around Sayuri's waist. "You are now just plain old Sayuri. Completely unranked. A civilian commoner in our empire."

“搞定。皇家名册上的头衔已经彻底清空了，”藤原放下钢笔，嘴角挂着一抹邪魅的坏笑。他往沙发上一靠，顺手搂住了小百合的细腰。“你现在就只是普普通通的小百合了。毫无品级，成了咱们帝国里一个货真价实的一介平民。”

Sayuri let out a long, luxurious sigh of relief, patting her chest. "Thank goodness... Now people can stop bowing to me in the grocery store, and foreign ambassadors won't ask me for dating advice regarding their rear ends."

小百合轻轻拍着胸口，如释重负地长长舒了一口气。“谢天谢地……现在我去超市买菜总算不用被人行大礼了，那些外国大使也不会再跑来向我请教后庭养生的心得了。”

Chika spun around in her desk chair, blowing a bubble with her chewing gum and giggling wildly.

千夏在转椅上转了一圈，吹了个泡泡糖，咯咯地狂笑起来。

"A civilian commoner? Don't make me laugh, Boss!" Chika teased, tapping her tablet. "She doesn't need an executive title! Sayuri-nee holds the most monopolistic, non-negotiable asset in our entire multinational portfolio: the Master Key to the Boss's groin! I can manipulate European interest rates all day, but if she decides to lock her legs for forty-eight hours, the Emperor goes into biological default!"

“一介平民？老板，您可别逗我笑了！”千夏打趣着，用触控笔敲了敲平板电脑。“她根本不需要什么企业职务！小百合姐手里握着咱们整个跨国投资组合里最垄断、最具话语权的核心资产：开启老板裤裆的万能钥匙！我能在金融市场上把欧洲利率玩弄于股掌之间，但要是她决定把大腿并拢四十八小时，咱们这位帝王的生理机能当场就得宣告违约！”

Miyetsuko scoffed from the armchair, casually tossing an unsharpened throwing knife into the air and catching it.

美悦子坐在单人扶手椅上冷哼了一声，漫不经心地把一把没开刃的飞刀抛向空中再稳稳接住。

"The accountant is right. Taking away her title changes nothing," Miyetsuko noted dryly, adjusting her glasses with a smirk. "The real hierarchy of this house is established between midnight and 4 AM. She’s the only one who can take your most depraved, biological assaults, swallow thirty billion yen of hardware without complaining, and wake up looking like an innocent maiden. In tactical terms, she is the supreme biological sponge of the Fresh Mart empire."

“小会计说得没错。剥夺头衔根本改变不了任何实质问题，”美悦子推了推眼镜，带着坏笑冷冷点评道。“这个家真正的权力阶层，向来是在午夜到凌晨四点之间确立的。她是唯一一个能扛得住你最变态的生理突击、吞下价值三十个亿的精密硬件连哼都不哼一声，第二天醒来还能像个清纯少女一样的女人。从战术术语来讲，她就是‘新鲜玛特’帝国的终极生物吸压海绵。”

Sayuri hid her burning, crimson face behind a silk fan, groaning softly. "Miyetsuko! Please! Must you describe my marital duties like a military operation?!"

小百合用一把真丝折扇挡住滚烫羞红的脸颊，软软地哀嚎起来：“美悦子！求求你了！你非得把人家的夫妻义务形容得跟军事行动一样吗？！”

From the carpet near the balcony, blind Rin was carefully organizing her knitting basket. She tilted her head, her unseeing eyes blinking innocently as she chimed in with her usual, horrifying sweetness.

在靠近阳台的地毯边，盲人铃正认真地整理着她的毛线篮。她歪着头，空洞无神的双眸无辜地眨了眨，带着她那一贯令人汗毛倒竖的甜美语气插话了。

"Um... hello, Sayuri-nee?" Rin whispered warmly. "Even if Master took away your golden seals, you will always be our absolute Queen of the Mattress! I already started sewing a special golden label inside all your bedsheets that says 'Reserved for the Chief Pleasure Cushion'. That way, Master never gets confused about where to deposit his nighttime stress!"

“唔……大家听我说好吗，小百合姐？”铃温温柔柔地呢喃着，“就算主人收回了你的金印，你在我们心里也永远是绝对的‘床垫女王’呀！我已经开始在你的所有床单里侧，缝上特制的纯金标签了，上面写着‘首席极乐肉垫专座’。这样一来，主人晚上想要倾倒压力的时候，就绝对不会走错地方啦！”

The entire living room exploded into another round of chaotic, uproarious laughter. Fujiwara threw his head back, roaring with delight as he pulled the flustered, title-less Sayuri flush against his chest, peppering kisses all over her burning cheeks.

整个客厅里顿时再次爆发出了一阵混乱而畅快的狂笑。藤原仰头大笑，一把将这位被撤了职、羞得手足无措的小百合紧紧搂在怀里，在满是红晕的脸颊上狂风骤雨般地落下一个又一个亲吻。

"You hear that, my little commoner?" Fujiwara chuckled wickedly into her ear. "You can give up the throne, you can give up the veto, but you are never escaping night duty. Now go put on something transparent—the Emperor has a few more private matters to settle with his favorite citizen."

“听见了吧，我的小无冕平民？”藤原贴着她的耳朵，邪恶地低声坏笑。“王位你能退，一票否决权你也能交，但这夜班你是这辈子都休想逃掉了。现在赶紧去换件透光点的衣服——帝王还有好几桩私密大案，正急着找他最偏心的小顺民好好‘严刑逼供’呢。”
