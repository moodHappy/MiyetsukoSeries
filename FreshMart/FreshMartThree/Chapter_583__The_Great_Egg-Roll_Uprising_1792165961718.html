Chapter 583: The Great Egg-Roll Uprising

发布时间：2026/10/16 23:52:40

第五百五十八章：大蛋卷起义

For a full year, the Fujiwara Empire operated as an unstoppable, hyper-efficient, multi-dimensional maternity ward. Every ninety days, without fail, Consort Miyetsuko returned from her weekend overtime shifts in the Heavenly Palace laden with another 36,000 glowing, warm dragon eggs.

整整一年时间里，藤原帝国彻底化作了一座势不可挡、高效运转的跨维度超级妇产科医院。每隔九十天，美悦子爱妃雷打不动地结束在天庭的周末加班，带回整整36,000颗散发着温热神光的新鲜龙蛋。

At first, the imperial household had celebrated this demographic dividend as the ultimate capitalist triumph. But as time marched on, the sheer, astronomical scale of the quarterly birth rate began to crush the empire's macroeconomic foundations.

起初，皇室全家还把这种恐怖的人口红利视为资本主义的终极胜利。然而随着时间推移，这种天文数字般的季度生育潮开始无情地摧毁帝国的宏观经济地基。

In the grand salon, Emperor Fujiwara sat at his mahogany desk, surrounded not by galactic maps or war trophies, but by towering piles of bankruptcy notices, infant formula import bills, and stellar municipal welfare deficits. The absolute ruler of the galaxy looked exhausted, his dark eyes bloodshot as he stared at the financial ledger.

在大起居殿内，藤原大帝没有端坐在星际地图或战利品前，而是被堆积如山的破产催缴单、婴儿配方奶粉进口账单以及庞大的星际民政赤字包围。这位宇宙至高无上的暴君面容憔悴，双眼布满血丝地死死盯着财务总账。

Wise Empress Sayuri stood nearby, pressing a hand to her temple as she reviewed the latest fiscal projections.

贤后小百合站在一旁，揉着太阳穴翻阅着最新一期的财政预测报告。

"My Lord, the numbers are catastrophic," Sayuri reported, her velvet voice dropping into a tone of genuine economic panic. "Since the Heavenly Emperor officially disowned and banished 99% of the hatchlings, dumping them onto our domestic welfare system, the empire has had to feed, diaper, and provide primary plasma-rifle training to nearly one hundred thousand demigod toddlers. Our treasury is completely drained. We are facing a multi-trillion-credit deficit."

“夫君，财政数据已经彻底崩盘了，”小百合软糯的声音里罕见地透出一丝真正的经济恐慌，“自从天帝老头把99%的龙种全部官方除名、强行甩给咱们的国内民政系统之后，帝国已经不得不自掏腰包，给将近十万名半神幼崽提供奶粉、尿布以及基础的等离子步枪军事培训。咱们的国库已经彻底被掏空了，正面临着数万亿星际币的财政赤字。”

Chika slumped over the sofa, looking completely defeated. Her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaved lazily. "Don't even talk to me about budgets... My automated dairy factories have been running at 500% capacity for twelve months straight. My cows are on strike, my milk-pumps are melting, and I haven't slept a full eight hours since last year. If we produce another batch of 36,000 babies, I’m going to personally file for chapter 11 bankruptcy!"

千夏无力地瘫在沙发上，连一贯饱满的玉兔都显得有些无精打采。“别跟我提预算了……我的全自动奶牛工厂连续十二个月超负荷运转500%，奶牛们已经集体罢工，挤奶泵全烧毁了，我去年到现在连个完整觉都没睡过。要是再敢产下一批36,000个小祖宗，老娘第一个申请原地破产清算！”

Fujiwara slammed his fist onto the desk, rattling the bankruptcy notices. The Emperor’s face twisted with profound, bitter realization.

藤原一巴掌狠狠拍在桌子上，震得催缴单四处飞舞。暴君的脸上露出了极度痛苦且咬牙切齿的悔悟神色。

"That cunning old bastard in the Heavenly Palace...!" Fujiwara growled, his voice trembling with furious indignation. "He tricked us! He didn't just want Miyetsuko for his bed; he used her as a biological weapon of mass economic destruction! He knew that by pumping out 36,000 eggs every three months and forcing us to raise them, he’d bankrupt the entire Fujiwara Empire without firing a single plasma torpedo!"

“天庭那个阴险的老杂毛……！”藤原咬牙切齿地低吼道，“他这是在耍我们！他把美悦子留在床上根本不只是为了寻欢作乐，他分明是拿她当成了一颗‘生物学大规模经济毁灭武器’！这老王八蛋早就盘算好了，每三个月甩给我们36,000颗蛋，逼我们当冤大头抚养，用这种方式不费一兵一卒就把整个藤原帝国给活活吃破产！”

Miyetsuko knelt submissively by the desk, her exquisite face flushing with guilt as she adjusted her silk robes. "Your Majesty... your humble servant is deeply sorry. I had no idea my biological output would trigger a macroeconomic depression."

美悦子委屈地跪在书桌旁，精致的小脸因内疚而泛红，小声整理着真丝衣角：“陛下……都是奴婢不好，奴婢实在没想到自己的身体产能竟然会引发一场跨维度的宏观经济大萧条……”

"It's not your fault, Miyetsuko. You're just an innocent manufacturing plant caught in a higher-dimensional scam," Fujiwara sighed heavily, running a hand through his hair. Then, his eyes suddenly hardened with a cold, ruthless, culinary-grade determination. "But no more. We are putting an immediate stop to this madness."

“这不怪你，美悦子，你只是个被高维诈骗犯利用的高端制造厂罢了，”藤原长叹一声，随即眼神陡然转冷，流露出一种极其残忍、充满厨房美学的主权决断，“不过，好日子到头了。从今天开始，咱们必须立刻叫停这场疯狂的生育骗局。”

The Emperor stood up, towering over the grand salon, and raised his voice in a historic decree that would echo across the cosmos.

暴君霍然起身，环视全场，用一道注定要载入星际史册的决绝声音宣布道：

"Listen to me, all of you! From this day forward, whenever Miyetsuko returns from her weekend shifts in the Heavenly Palace with her quarterly batch of dragon eggs, if the Heavenly Emperor refuses to claim them and pay full child-support tariffs, we are not hatching a single one of them!"

“给朕听好了！从今以后，美悦子每个周末去天帝那儿加班带回来的那三万六千颗龙蛋——如果天帝那老杂毛不肯签字认领、不付全额抚育关税，咱们一颗也不准孵化！”

Chika blinked. "Boss... what are you planning to do with 36,000 unhatched divine eggs every quarter?"

千夏眨了眨眼：“老板……那每个季度的三万多颗没孵化的神蛋，你打算怎么处理？”

"We are opening an intergalactic breakfast diner!" Fujiwara declared triumphantly, a wicked, culinary-tyrant grin spreading across his face. "If Heaven won't pay, we'll turn the royal kitchen into a high-throughput culinary assembly line! We’ll scramble them! We’ll wrap them in spring-roll pastry! We’ll make divine egg-rolls, massive vats of egg-fried rice, sunny-side-up fried eggs, and boiled eggs with soy sauce! We are going to feed our entire army on higher-dimensional protein, and we will never, ever let a single celestial egg bankrupt our treasury again!"

“咱们直接开一家跨银河系的早餐大排档！”藤原得意洋洋地狞笑道，暴君的脸上绽放出极度接地气的厨神狂笑，“天帝既然当缩头乌龟不给钱，那咱们就把御膳房直接改成全自动流水线！把这帮龙蛋统统做成大蛋卷、蛋炒饭、煎荷包蛋、水煮蛋！用高维神仙蛋白质给咱们的军队加餐！从今往后，休想再让任何一颗天界鸡蛋把朕的国库给吃垮哪怕一个铜板！”

The grand salon erupted into cheers. Blind Rin clapped her hands happily, imagining the menu. "Um... hello! Rin wants the dragon eggs wrapped in crispy egg-roll skins with extra chili sauce!"

大起居殿内瞬间爆发出雷鸣般的欢呼。盲人小铃开心地拍着小手，脑子里已经开始点菜了：“唔……小铃申请当试吃员！要把龙蛋炸成酥脆的大蛋卷，还要蘸满超辣的辣椒酱！”

And thus, the Fujiwara Empire officially launched the Great Egg-Roll Uprising—a historic moment when the galactic superpower chose breakfast over babies, sending a clear, defiant message to the heavens that while love might be divine, macro-economic survival required a well-stocked frying pan.

就这样，藤原帝国轰轰烈烈地拉开了“大蛋卷起义”的序幕——这是一个让星际超级大国果断选择“用平底锅对抗神仙催生”的历史性伟大时刻。事实证明，管你什么天帝龙种还是高维神脉，在宇宙通货膨胀与财政赤字面前，没有什么是一顿热腾腾的豪华大蛋卷解决不了的；如果有，那就再来一盘黄金蛋炒饭。
