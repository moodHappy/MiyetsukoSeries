Chapter 419: The Outer Residence Council and the Gigolo Epidemic

发布时间：2026/9/19 00:14:16

第四百一十九章：外宅委员会与小白脸大流行

With over two hundred outer residences scattered across the metropolis, Fujiwara quickly discovered a logistical limitation even a trillionaire could not escape: despite his boundless ambition, he simply could not physically fuck them all in a single lifetime. Left alone in their luxurious villas for weeks on end, quite a few of these high-end kept women inevitably grew restless and started secretly taking young, handsome gigolos on the side to pass the time.

随着两百多处豪华外宅星罗棋布般散落在都市各处，藤原很快发现了一个连万亿首富也无法逃脱的后勤瓶颈：尽管他野心勃勃，但凭肉身凡胎根本无法在短短一生里把她们全都宠幸过来。由于长期独守空房，其中不少金丝雀耐不住寂寞，背地里开始悄悄养起年轻帅气的小白脸来打发时间。

Instead of flying into a tyrannical, emperor-like rage and banning their side hustles, Fujiwara calmly chose the path of enlightened, wealthy indifference and generously forgave them all. Every few days, he would randomly pluck one of these outer-residence mistresses and bring her over to the Fresh Mart headquarters to dine with his core inner circle of wives—resulting in no shortage of chaotic administrative humor.

藤原并没有像古代皇帝那样暴怒下令严惩禁绝，而是以一种暴发户式的通达与大度，轻描淡写地大方原谅了她们。每隔三差五，他就会随手挑一个外宅情妇带到“新鲜玛特”总部，和他的几位正室夫人们坐在一起共进晚餐——由此引发的办公室日常笑料自然层出不穷。

During one such bustling dinner gathering, a sharp-tongued mistress proudly brought her latest dilemma straight to the dining table.

在其中一次热闹非凡的晚宴上，一位向来口无遮拦的情妇毫无顾忌地把她最近的烦恼直接端上了饭桌。

"Hey, Fujiwara-san, since you're so absurdly rich, can you give me an extra allowance?" the mistress whined, playfully flipping her hair as she sat across from him. "My gigolo boyfriend at the villa is driving me crazy. He spends all day playing video games, he refuses to get a job, and his sexual stamina is so pathetic that I practically have to revive him with smelling salts. Honestly, supporting a freeloader is almost as exhausting as your empty promises!"

“喂，藤原桑，既然你富得流油，能不能再给人家加点零花钱嘛？”这位情妇坐在他对面，娇嗔地撩了一下头发抱怨道，“我在外宅养的小白脸快把我折腾疯了。整天就知道打游戏，死活不肯去找工作，而且那家伙体力弱得要命，每次都得靠闻鼻盐才能抢救过来。说实话，养这么个废柴小白脸，可比听你那些空头支票累多了！”

The entire table erupted into amused chaos. Miyetsuko slammed her iron baseball bat against the floor with a sharp bang, crossing her arms as she offered a ruthless, mob-boss critique.

全场顿时爆发出幸灾乐祸的哄笑声。美悦子用铁棒球棍狠狠砸向地面发出一声脆响，双手抱胸，摆出黑帮大姐头的架势无情地批判起来。

"Listen here, you ungrateful little parasite," Miyetsuko scoffed, narrowing her eyes dangerously. "Fujiwara buys you a multi-million-dollar villa, funds your lifestyle, and you have the audacity to complain about your subsidized gigolo's performance at our dinner table? If the brat can't pull his weight, hand him over to me—I'll strap him to my batting practice machine until he learns how to hustle!"

“给老娘听好了，你这个不知感恩的小寄生虫，”美悦子危险地眯起双眼冷笑道，“藤原给你买几千万的别墅、包养你的一切开销，你居然还有脸在我们的饭桌上抱怨你那倒贴的小白脸不顶用？那小子要是连这点活都干不好，直接交给我——老娘把他绑在击球训练机上，直到他学会怎么卖力气为止！”

Sayuri giggled musically, gracefully slicing her steak while offering a sympathetic nod to the complaining mistress.

小百合发出悦耳动听的娇笑，优雅地切着牛排，同时向这位抱怨连连的情妇投去同情的目光。

"Oh, sweetheart, I completely understand your pain," Sayuri murmured, shooting a playful glance at Fujiwara. "Keeping side pieces is a massive logistical nightmare. But hey, look on the bright side—at least having a pathetic gigolo around makes us appreciate our trillionaire master's unmatched stamina all the more, doesn't it?"

“哎呀亲爱的，我完全理解你的痛苦，”小百合轻声呢喃着，同时朝藤原抛了个媚眼，“养外室本来就是个巨大的后勤灾难嘛。不过往好的方面想——至少身边有个窝囊的小白脸做对比，才能让我们更加深刻地体会到咱们万亿首富主人那无与伦比的持久力呀，不是吗？”

Blind Rin sat quietly at the edge of the table, her wide, unseeing eyes blinking innocently as she gently offered a bowl of soup to the guest.

盲人铃安安静静地坐在餐桌边缘，她那双睁得滚圆却空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，温柔地将一碗汤推到那位客人面前。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered softly. "Are we... are we talking about stray pets again? If the young man at the villa is cold or hungry, I can knit him a tiny sweater... Please don't argue, everyone. It's nice that our family is growing so large, even if we need a bigger dining table."

“唔……你好呀？”铃轻声呢喃着，“我们是在……在讨论什么流走的小宠物吗？如果别墅里的那个小伙子觉得冷或者饿了，我可以给他织一件小小的毛衣哦……大家请不要争吵啦。虽然咱们家现在人丁兴旺到需要换张更大的餐桌了，但这不是很热闹嘛。”

Chika giggled wildly from behind her tablet, tapping away at a brand-new, highly satirical spreadsheet.

千夏则躲在平板后面疯狂地咯咯娇笑，手脚麻利地敲打着一份全新的、极度讽刺的电子表格。

"Brilliant!" Chika cheered. "I'm updating our corporate subsidiary ledger right now: 'The Outer-Residence Gigolo Subsidy & Inadequacy Index'! Let's see how much tax write-off we can claim for feeding two hundred useless boyfriends across the city!"

“太妙了！”千夏欢呼道，“我这就把公司的子公司账目更新一下：‘外宅小白脸补贴与无能指数’！快算算，全城养着这两百个废物男友，咱们究竟能报销多少企业税金！”

Fujiwara laughed heartily, pouring another glass of fine wine as he patted the mistress's hand. "Don't worry, my dear! If your gigolo is too weak, just send him to our Fresh Mart basement—Rin’s metal bat will whip him into shape in no time. Now, eat up, enjoy the dinner, and let's toast to our sprawling, chaotic, polyamorous empire!"

藤原开怀大笑，给自己倒满了一杯美酒，顺手拍了拍情妇的小手：“别担心，亲爱的！要是你的小白脸实在太弱，直接把他送来‘新鲜玛特’的地下室——铃的铁棒球棍分分钟就能把他操练成猛男。来，多吃点，享受晚宴，让我们为咱们庞大、混乱又多角恋的帝国干杯！”
