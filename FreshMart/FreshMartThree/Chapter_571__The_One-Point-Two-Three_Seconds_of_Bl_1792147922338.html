第五百七十七章：那一点二三秒的亵渎

Just as the imperial family was happily packing their bags to embark on a tax-free, galaxy-spanning vacation across the ten newly conquered star systems, tragedy struck the House of Fujiwara. The glittering celebration of military triumph came to a screeching, catastrophic halt right in the middle of the palace courtyard.

正当皇室全家高高兴兴地收拾行李，准备前往那十个新征服的星系开启免税银河大游玩时，藤原家族却迎来了乐极生悲的惨剧。这场庆祝军事大捷的璀璨庆典，直接在皇宫庭院的正中央戛然而止。

It all happened during the pre-departure inspection. The decorated Prince, resplendent in his quantum terminator armor, committed the ultimate, unforgivable capital sin: while standing at attention, his eyes involuntarily drifted downward for a fraction of a second, lingering with pure biological appreciation on Miyetsuko’s explosively voluptuous, newly divine-pampered breasts.

这一切都发生在出发前的检阅仪式上。那位功勋显赫、身披量子终结者装甲的皇子，犯下了一条十恶不赦的死罪：在立正行礼时，他的目光不由自主地向下游离了零点几秒，极其贪婪且充满生物学欣赏地停留在了美悦子那双被天帝滋润得爆满、极其宏伟的傲人巨乳上。

SLAP!

啪！

A thunderous sound echoed across the courtyard as Emperor Fujiwara materialized out of thin air, delivering a backhand strike so ferocious it sent the interstellar conqueror spinning three times before he crashed into a decorative marble pillar.

伴随着一声震耳欲聋的脆响，藤原大帝犹如天神下凡般闪现而出，一记反手耳光扇得这位星际征服者凌空旋转了三圈，轰然撞在一根雕花大理石柱上。

"You ungrateful little livestock!" Fujiwara roared, his tyrannical face twisted with murderous fury as he pointed a trembling, outraged finger at his son. "Winning ten star systems gives you zero diplomatic immunity to commit high treason! The Heavenly Emperor may ravish Miyetsuko because he has higher-dimensional privileges, but you—you pathetic, milk-fed peasant—how dare you?!"

“你这个忘恩负义的小畜生！”藤原暴怒地狂吼着，暴君的脸上因极致的狂怒而扭曲，他颤抖着、愤怒地用手指着自己的亲儿子，“区区打下十个星系，可给不了你犯下谋反重罪的外交豁免权！天帝能肏美悦子，那是因为人家有高维特权，可你——你这个靠吃奶长大的废物点心，凭什么敢盯着看？！”

Inside the grand salon moments later, the mood was utterly suffocating. The Prince had been instantly stripped of his quantum armor, his galactic medals, and his royal title. Chika stood in the corner, staring at the floor with a mixture of shock and profound maternal disappointment, while Sayuri and Rin watched the drama unfold with detached, popcorn-eating amusement.

几分钟后，大起居殿内的气氛压抑得令人窒息。皇子当场被剥夺了所有的量子装甲、银河勋章和皇室头衔。千夏呆呆地站在角落里，脸上写满了震惊与深度的母性失望；而小百合和小铃则抱着看戏的心态，津津有味地围观着这场家庭伦理惨剧。

"I am a reasonable ruler," Fujiwara paced back and forth across the Persian rug, fuming with unquenchable wrath. "When the Heavenly Palace descended with higher-dimensional fire and demanded Miyetsuko, I had no choice. The gods outranked me. What was I supposed to do? Fight higher-dimensional physics with a plasma rifle? The Heavenly Emperor took her, and frankly, on that front, I was completely powerless. I had to let him have her!"

“朕可是一位讲道理的明君啊，”藤原在波斯地毯上气得来回踱步，满腔怒火无处发泄，“当年的天宫带着高维神火压境、硬生生点名要走美悦子的时候，朕确实毫无办法。神仙的官阶比朕大，朕能怎么办？难道要拿着等离子步枪去跟高维物理定律拼命吗？天帝把她劫走了，坦白讲，在那种降维打击的局面下，朕确实无能为力，只能捏着鼻子认了！”

The Emperor stopped, turning to glare at the kneeling, disgraced former prince with eyes burning with pure malice.

皇帝猛地停下脚步，转过身，用充满恶毒杀意的双眼死死瞪着跪在地上的废皇子。

"But you?!" Fujiwara screamed, his voice cracking with utter disbelief. "You are my biological son! You share my blood! And yet, right here in broad daylight, in the sacred halls of the Empire, you didn't just glance—you stared! According to the imperial intelligence reports and slow-motion surveillance analysis, you stared at your mother's tits for exactly one point two three seconds!"

“可是你呢？！”藤原尖叫着，声音里透着满腔的难以置信，“你是朕的亲生骨肉！你流着朕的血！可你倒好，就在这光天化日之下、在帝国的神圣大殿里，你居然不只是瞄了一眼——你他妈是死死盯着看！根据皇家情报部门的慢动作监控回放分析，你这个小畜生刚才足足盯着你母妃的奶子看了整整1.23秒钟！”

The Prince pressed his forehead against the cold obsidian floor, trembling violently, unable to utter a single defense. One point two three seconds of unadulterated, unblinking perversion directed at his stepmother-slash-colleague. It was a societal outrage.

皇子把额头死死贴在冰冷的黑曜石地面上，浑身剧烈颤抖，连一句辩解的话都说不出口。整整1.23秒毫无掩饰、连眼皮都不带眨一下的对继母兼同僚的色情凝视。这简直是道德的沦丧。

与此同时，帝国的民间大街小巷和内部网络彻底炸开了锅。这个三级文明的全体公民一整个早上都在疯狂热议这桩荒诞绝伦的皇室丑闻。全息公共大屏幕上全都是铺天盖地的吃瓜言论：
