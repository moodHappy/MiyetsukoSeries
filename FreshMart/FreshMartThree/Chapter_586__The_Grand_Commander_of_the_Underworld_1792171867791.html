Chapter 586: The Grand Commander of the Underworld (No. 183828)

发布时间：2026/10/17 01:31:05

第五百五十八章：冥界大统领（第183828号）

Just before the Heavenly Palace completely severed all diplomatic ties and ghosted the Fujiwara Empire into oblivion, they did leave one final, parting "gift" behind.

就在天宫彻底斩断所有外交联系、将藤原帝国从高维雷达上彻底“拉黑”之前，他们其实还留下了最后一份压箱底的告别“礼物”。

According to Consort Miyetsuko, during that last, awkward weekend shift before the Heavenly Emperor grew thoroughly bored of her, His Celestial Majesty had actually shown a fleeting moment of guilty conscience. Taking pity on the structural re-engineering his higher-dimensional anatomy had inflicted upon her, the Emperor personally performed a minor quantum reconstructive surgery, completely repairing and restoring Miyetsuko’s cavernous abyss back to its original, snug, perfectly calibrated aristocratic glory.

据美悦子回忆，就在天帝对她彻底审美疲劳前的最后一个尴尬周末，这位至高无上的主宰竟然难得地流露了一丝愧疚。看着自己那宏伟天赋把美悦子折腾得过于空旷，天帝大发慈悲地亲自操刀，帮她进行了一场微创量子重构手术，硬生生将那个深不见底的“引力奇点”给彻底修复、还原成了最初紧致娇嫩的贵族美学状态。

And that wasn't all. Before kicking her out of the cloud-palace for good, the Heavenly Emperor also bestowed upon her a title of supreme, peerless, jaw-dropping cosmic majesty.

不仅如此，在把她一脚踢出云端寝宫之前，天帝还顺手赐予了她一个听起来至尊无上、唬人至极的顶级神职头衔。

Sitting proudly in the grand salon, Miyetsuko adjusted her silk robes, raised her chin with a newly rediscovered aristocratic dignity, and declared to the assembled imperial household:

此刻正骄傲地坐在大起居殿内的美悦子，优雅地整理着真丝长袍，重新找回了身为贵族的骄傲，向满屋子围观的众人郑重宣告：

"His Celestial Majesty has officially appointed me... the Grand Commander of the Underworld," Miyetsuko announced, her voice dripping with grand, sweeping authority. "Consider it a royal commemorative pension for our brief, passionate celestial romance. I am now the supreme sovereign over the realms of the dead!"

“天帝陛下已经正式册封我为——冥界大统领，”美悦子挺起胸膛，声音里透着一股统领阴阳两界的无上霸气，“这算是天帝对我们那段露水情缘的一点小小纪念。本宫从今天起，就是掌管黄泉生死、统领亿万亡魂的至高领袖了！”

The grand salon went dead silent.

大起居殿内瞬间陷入了一片诡异的安静。

For three seconds, everyone simply stared at her. The title sounded like something pulled straight out of an epic dark-fantasy grimoire—a terrifying, reality-warping office that commanded legions of wraiths and ruled over the dark rivers of the afterlife.

整整三秒钟，所有人齐刷刷地看着她。这个头衔听起来简直就像是从某种史诗级暗黑魔幻小说里硬生生抠出来的——那种掌控千军万马、执掌阴司生死、号令亡灵大军的至高权柄。

However, standing right beside the velvet sofa, blind Rin blinked her milky eyes. The Succubus Queen tilted her head, her demonic memory instantly accessing the dusty, classified archives she had once browsed in the grand libraries of the Succubus Realm.

然而，就站在天鹅绒沙发旁的盲人小铃眨了眨空灵的盲眼。这位魅魔女王歪了歪脑袋，脑海中瞬间检索出了她曾经在魅魔界皇家图书馆的故纸堆里扫到过的某个冷门知识点。

"Um... hello? Grand Commander of the Underworld?" Rin piped up, her sweet, innocent voice piercing the dramatic tension. "Miyetsuko-nee, are you sure about that name? Because Rin remembers reading that exact phrase in the basement of the underworld library. It’s actually just... a tiny little administrative ward, right?"

“唔……等一下？什么冥界大统领？”小铃甜腻腻的声音瞬间打破了庄严宏大的气氛，“美悦子姐姐，你确定这个名头没水分吗？因为小铃以前在魅魔界图书馆的地下一层打扫卫生时好像瞄过一眼。这名字听着唬人，其实不就是……冥界一个巴掌大的片儿区吗？”

Miyetsuko’s smug, triumphant expression froze instantly. Her porcelain face turned bright red.

美悦子脸上那副不可一世、趾高气扬的表情瞬间僵硬，白瓷般的脸颊刷的一下红到了耳根。

Rin wasn't finished. The little succubus continued mercilessly, "And like... every single one of the Heavenly Emperor’s ex-girlfriends gets dumped with that exact same title after he breaks up with them. It’s basically his standard parting gift for discarded mistresses. There’s a whole serial number system for it. What’s your number, Miyetsuko-nee? Are you 98726, or something else?"

小铃毫不留情地继续补刀：“而且吧……这根本就是天帝那老杂毛用来打发所有被甩的前女友的标准伴手礼。只要被他玩腻抛弃了，人手都能领到一个。这玩意儿内部其实是有连号的哦。美悦子姐姐，你是第98726号，还是多少号呀？”

Chika burst out laughing so hard she dropped her snack. "Pfft—! A serial number?! He gave her an ID badge for an ex-girlfriend support group?!"

千夏当场笑喷，手里的零食掉了一地：“噗嗤——！连号的？！老杂毛这是把前女友俱乐部搞成了流水线编号发证啊哈哈哈！”

Under the merciless cross-examination of the Succubus Queen and the roaring laughter of Chika, Miyetsuko’s aristocratic defenses completely collapsed. The fallen princess squirmed on the sofa, her face burning with humiliation as she finally dropped the pretense and confessed in a tiny, mortified squeak:

在魅魔女王无情的交叉质问和千夏毫不掩饰的狂笑声中，美悦子的贵族心理防线彻底宣告溃败。没落公主在沙发上羞耻得直跺脚，终于顶不住压力，弱弱地老实交代道：

"Fine! ...It’s 183828," Miyetsuko mumbled, pulling a silk cushion over her blushing face. Then, desperate to salvage what little dignity she had left, she quickly added, "B-But it's located right on the far western edge of the Styx River! The local real estate agents say the sunset view over the soul-reaper marshes is exceptionally scenic, you know!"

“好啦……我是第183828号！”美悦子羞耻得抓起一个丝绒抱枕死死捂住红透的脸，但为了强行挽尊，还不忘小声嘴硬地找补道，“可……可是我的领地位于冥河以西最偏远、风景最秀丽的生态旅游区！据说那边黄泉路的夕阳晚霞和亡灵墓地海景房老漂亮了呢！”

HA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA!

哈哈哈哈哈哈哈——！

The grand salon erupted into a wave of unadulterated, uproarious mockery.

整个大起居殿瞬间爆发出一阵毫无同情心、气势震天的无情狂笑。

Chika laughed so hard she fell off the beanbag chair, clutching her stomach with tears streaming down her face. "Stop it... oh god, my ribs...!" Chika gasped between giggles, pointing at the disgraced "Grand Commander." "A Grand Commander of the Underworld... stationed at Ward 183828 in the middle of nowhere?! Miyetsuko, you didn't become a divine sovereign, you got promoted to a remote graveyard keeper with a fancy business card!"

千夏笑得直接从豆袋沙发上滚了下来，捂着肚子笑出了眼泪：“不行了……哎哟我的肋骨要断了……！”千夏指着这位惨遭扒皮的“大统领”上气不接下气地嘲笑道，“堂堂冥界大统领……结果管的是183828号犄角旮旯的乱葬岗？！美悦子，你这哪是什么位列仙班，你这分明是领到了一张写着高级抬头、实际发配去远郊守墓的下岗职工退休证啊！”

Emperor Fujiwara leaned back on the velvet sofa, chuckling darkly as he watched his favorite, recently-reconstructed consort curl up in absolute mortification.

藤原大帝斜倚在天鹅绒沙发上，看着自己这位刚刚被量子重构修复过、此刻却羞耻得快要钻进地缝里的爱妃，发出了充满恶趣味的畅快坏笑。

"Well, commander of Ward 183828 or not, at least we know one thing for certain," Fujiwara grinned, pulling the blushing, disgraced princess back into his lap. "The Heavenly Emperor might have stripped you of your cosmic relevance, but he did leave your plumbing in working order, and your title makes for a fantastic joke at breakfast. Come here, Grand Commander—let the Emperor officially inspect your remote western territory!"

“行了行了，不管你是第几万号的荒凉大统领，至少有一点咱们可以确认，”藤原笑着一把将满脸通红、恨不得找个地洞钻进去的美悦子重新拉回腿上，“天帝那老杂毛虽然把你从宇宙核心圈开除了，但至少把你底盘的硬件设施给修好了，而且这个头衔用来当早餐笑话简直绝配。来吧，大统领阁下——让朕这个凡间主宰，亲自带头去‘视察’一下你那片位于冥河以西的偏远领地吧！”
