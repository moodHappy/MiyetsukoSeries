Chapter 202: The Nail Inspection Ritual

发布时间：2026/8/29 16:47:14

第二百零二章：东莱岛的“美甲”规矩

In the wonderfully degenerate and bizarre ecosystem of Donglai Island, every establishment had its own unique rules. But none were quite as infamous—or as wildly nerve-wracking for first-time visitors—as the legendary ritual enforced at the island’s most exclusive local pleasure houses.

在东莱岛那片充满魔幻色彩与恶趣味的奇妙生态里，每一家风俗店都有着自己独一无二的规矩。但其中最令老客津津乐道、又让初访者冷汗直冒的，莫过于当地最高档风俗店里那套闻名遐迩的“硬核规矩”。

One lively Friday night, Fujiwara decided to take a break from the chaotic, harem-filled madness of "Fresh Mart" for a classic, unadulterated "boys' night out." Accompanied by a few of his most depraved local buddies, they stepped through the discreet sliding doors of the island’s premier establishment.

在一个热闹的周五夜晚，藤原决定暂时抛开“新鲜玛特”里那群风格各异的女人们，和几个臭味相投的本地哥们组了一场纯粹的“男人局”。一行人嬉皮笑脸地推开了东莱岛最老牌、也最懂人心的某家知名风俗店大门。

Upon entering the spacious guest waiting room, Fujiwara and his friends immediately noticed something bizarre. Every single seating booth, right next to the ashtrays and drink menus, was equipped with a sleek, professional-grade stainless steel nail clipper.

刚一走进宽敞的贵宾等待室，藤原和几个哥们就注意到了一处极其诡异的细节——每个卡座的桌面上，除了烟灰缸和酒水单，竟然都摆着一个崭新铮亮的精美指甲刀。

"What's with the manicure station?" one of his buddies muttered, picking up a clipper with a puzzled frown. "Are we here to get a pedicure or what?"

“卧槽，这地方什么时候兼营美甲了？”其中一个哥们纳闷地拿起桌上的指甲刀，“怎么着，待会儿还得先做个手部护理再进去？”

"Ah, you must be first-timers here," an experienced local regular sitting nearby chuckled knowingly. "That’s the golden rule of Donglai Island. You better check your fingers right now. If your nails are even a millimeter too long, you're not getting past the threshold."

“哟，几位是头回转到这儿来吧？”隔壁卡座的一个熟客幸灾乐祸地笑着提醒道，“这可是东莱岛风俗店的铁律。劝你们赶紧趁现在检查一下自己的指甲，要是长出哪怕半毫米，待会儿可就直接社死了。”

Panic instantly broke out among the group. Realizing the hygiene and safety protocols of the house, the guys frantically scrambled around the tables, clicking away like a colony of nervous crabs as they desperately trimmed their fingernails down to the quick.

一听这话，几个平时大大咧咧的大男人顿时慌了神。为了不丢面子，大家纷纷手忙脚乱地抓起桌上的指甲刀，现场咔嚓咔嚓地剪起指甲来，场面一度十分滑稽。

A few minutes later, a sharply dressed receptionist walked in, bowing politely. "Gentlemen, your rooms are ready. Please, follow me."

没过多久，一名身穿制服、妆容精致的女接待微笑着走了进来：“各位贵宾，房间已经准备好了，请随我来。”

True to local lore, she didn't lead them straight to the rooms. Instead, she guided them down a softly lit corridor straight to a large, immaculate white wall covered in neatly printed house rules.

正如传说中那样，接待员并没有直接把他们带进包厢，而是领着这群忐忑不安的男人来到了一条铺着地毯的长廊尽头——那里赫然立着一面贴满了规章制度的巨大墙壁。

With a calm, professional smile, the staff member began to recite the bylaws line by line, pointing at each paragraph with a pointer stick as if they were unruly kindergarteners attending orientation day.

女接待脸上挂着职业而甜美的微笑，拿出一根指挥棒，开始逐条逐句地给这群大老爷们宣读店里的各项纪律，那架势像极了幼儿园老师在给新入园的小朋友上安全教育课。

"Rule number one: No unauthorized photography or recording devices..." she droned on monotonously.

“第一条：严禁携带任何未授权的摄影及录音设备……”接待员的声音波澜不惊。

Fujiwara and his bros stood in a neat little row, nodding obediently like guilty schoolboys, sweating profusely under her stern, evaluating gaze.

藤原和几个哥们排成一排乖乖罚站，像一群犯了错的小学生一样连连点头，在对方审视的目光下额头直冒冷汗。

Finally, the rule-reading ceremony reached its dramatic climax. The receptionist lowered her pointer, turned to the group, and gave the ultimate command:

终于，长达十分钟的“规章宣读仪式”迎来了万众期待的高潮。女接待收起指挥棒，转过身来，发布了最后一道核心指令：

"And now... please extend both hands, palms down. It’s time for the mandatory nail inspection."

“那么接下来……请各位依次伸出双手，掌心向下。我们要进行最重要的指甲长度检查。”

One by one, the terrified men held out their hands. The inspector walked down the line, meticulously inspecting each fingertip, running a light fingernail check to ensure no sharp edges remained.

男人们战战兢兢地把手伸了出去。检查人员顺着队伍逐一排查，仔细摸过每一个指尖，确认没有任何粗糙锋利的边缘。

When she reached Fujiwara, he proudly flashed his freshly clipped, neatly rounded fingers. She gave an approving nod, officially passing him through the gauntlet.

轮到藤原时，他得意洋洋地亮出自己刚刚精修过、圆润干净的十指。检查员满意地点了点头，大手一挥，宣布合格。

With the final inspection successfully completed, the receptionist’s professional mask melted away into a warm, inviting grin. She stepped back, opened the VIP room door, and delivered the magic words every man on Donglai Island lived to hear:

随着最后一个人通过检查，女接待脸上严肃的职业表情瞬间融化，换上了一副极具诱惑力的坏笑。她侧过身推开包厢大门，说出了全岛男人们梦寐以求的那句终极台词：

"Alright, gentlemen... Go ahead. Touch whatever you want."

“好啦，各位……摸吧，想摸哪摸哪。”
