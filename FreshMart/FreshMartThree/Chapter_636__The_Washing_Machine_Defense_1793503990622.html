第六百三十六章：洗衣机防卫奇案

The holographic feed in the grand salon refreshed once again, projecting the latest municipal oddity that had gone completely viral across the imperial networks.

大起居殿内的全息投影再次刷新，将帝国各大网络节点上刚刚引发热议的一桩离奇市井奇案投射在大殿中央。

The post detailed a routine appliance repair gone entirely off the rails: a voluptuous young housewife, attempting to retrieve a fallen item, had managed to lodge her upper torso completely inside a front-loading washing machine, leaving her lower half trapped and sticking out into the utility room.

帖子里巨细靡遗地记录了一场彻底走样的家电维修：一位身段丰腴的少妇因试图掏出掉落的物件，大半个身子不慎卡进了滚筒洗衣机深处，动弹不得，只留下丰腴的下半身滑稽而无助地撅在洗衣房中。

When the repairman arrived, he was greeted not by a customer at the door, but by the sight of a woman helplessly wedged in place, her form wrapped in a tight, figure-hugging knit skirt that strained against the contours of her ripe, rounded hips.

维修工推门而入时，迎接他的并非立于门前的女主人，而是一具被死死卡住、进退维谷的身躯；一条紧致的包臀针织长裙紧紧绷在她圆润丰硕的臀部上，曲线毕露，饱满得近乎要将布料撑破。

杂念与冲动瞬间压垮了微薄的职业操守；借着女人被卡住无法脱身的情势，工人撩起紧绷的裙摆，剥下轻薄的蕾丝衬裤，就着那台冰凉震颤的金属机身，肆意将她就地办了个结结实实。

After finishing, the man casually zipped his trousers, tapped the side of the machine, and announced that the repair bill was officially waived, having already received "physical compensation."

完事之后，工人慢条斯理地拉上裤链，拍了拍修好的机器外壳，轻描淡写地表示修理费全免，全当已经“肉偿抵扣”。

Inside the palace hall, the consorts immediately began dissecting the absurd event from every perverse legal and economic angle.

大殿之内，后宫众人随即围绕着这起荒诞的市井纠纷，从各种古怪的法理与经济学角度展开了七嘴八舌的议论。

"A clear breach of transaction terms, but an undeniable display of opportunistic leverage," Chika commented, leaning back and munching on a snack. "Some online commenters are screaming about taking advantage of a crisis, while others insist it was merely a creative, real-time conversion of labor costs into tangible physical services."

“这显然违背了最初的雇佣契约，但不得不说是一次极其精明的趁虚而入，”千夏斜靠在一旁嚼着零食点评道，“网上一帮人骂他趁人之危，可另一帮人却咬定这不过是一场富有创意的、将劳务成本实时转化为实体肉偿的等价交换罢了。”

Sayuri waved her velvet fan, her expression dripping with aristocratic irony. "From a macroeconomic perspective, bartering services is the oldest trade mechanism in existence. However, the moralists on the forums seem utterly distressed by the lack of prior written quotation."

小百合轻摇丝绒折扇，眉宇间带着权贵特有的讥诮：“从宏观经济学的角度而言，以物易物本就是最古老的商业交换机制。不过那些在论坛上叫嚷的卫道士们，显然是对这种未曾出具事前书面报价的‘即时结算’感到难以接受。”

He isn't a predator—in the eyes of imperial jurisprudence, he's practically a charitable public servant!"

Emperor Fujiwara laughed aloud at the sheer administrative absurdity of the argument, shaking his head at the legal loophole. Archive this interpretation into the legal records—let the judicial registry note that in this empire, charity truly comes in all shapes and positions."
