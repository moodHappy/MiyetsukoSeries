Chapter 577: The Shared Custody Agreement

发布时间：2026/10/16 22:53:44

第五百七十七章：共享协议

As Emperor Fujiwara stood frozen in a complex matrix of bruised tyrannical pride and pragmatic intergalactic greed, Wise Empress Sayuri stepped forward. The Living Pontiff of the AV Religion adjusted her Tang-dynasty silk robes, her exquisite face radiating the cold, calculating brilliance of a seasoned corporate strategist.

正当藤原大帝陷入“暴君尊严受损”与“星际贪婪获利”的复杂纠结中时，贤后小百合优雅地站了出来。这位AV女神教的活教主轻轻整理了一下盛唐风格的真丝长袍，精致的脸上绽放出一位老练的跨国企业战略家那冷酷而精明的计算光芒。

"My Lord, let us not abandon our pragmatism for the sake of petty, emotional pride," Sayuri purred smoothly, waving her velvet folding fan. "Declaring war on the Heavenly Palace with Rin’s succubus army would certainly be a magnificent spectacle, but it would completely disrupt our supply chains across the ten newly conquered star systems. Instead of fighting a costly interdimensional war over custody, why don't we apply the brilliant business model of corporate resource-sharing?"

“夫君，切莫为了意气用事而丢了咱们务实的经商头脑，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，轻轻摇晃着丝绒折扇，“动用小铃的魅魔大军跟天庭全面开火虽然场面宏大，但势必会彻底打乱咱们在十个新星系刚铺开的物流供应链。与其为了争夺抚养权而打一场劳民伤财的跨维大战，咱们为什么不直接借鉴一下现代商业中最先进的‘企业核心资源共享模式’呢？”

Fujiwara blinked, his dark eyes narrowing in sudden, enlightened interest. "Resource-sharing? Go on, Sayuri."

藤原微微一怔，漆黑的双眼里瞬间闪过一丝被点醒的贪婪光芒。“资源共享？说来听听，小百合。”

"Precisely," Sayuri smiled knowingly. "The Heavenly Emperor wants Miyetsuko for her elite... diplomatic services, while we want the higher-dimensional warp drive blueprints and divine tax exemptions. Since neither side is willing to completely relinquish her unique talents, we propose a binding, legally-enforced bilateral custody agreement: Consort Miyetsuko shall be officially classified as a 'Shared Interdimensional Asset.' She spends the weekdays in the mortal palace maintaining imperial morale, and travels to the Heavenly Palace on weekends for her 'advanced celestial training' and treasury looting."

“很简单，”小百合自信地微笑分析道，“天帝想要美悦子是为了她那精湛的……‘外交服务’，而我们想要的是高维跃迁引擎图纸和神界免税特权。既然双方都不愿彻底放手这笔优质资产，那咱们大可拟定一份具有法律效力的双边联合托管协议：正式将美悦子爱妃登记为‘跨维度共享核心资产’。她周一到周五在人间大殿驻留，维持皇室士气与日常产能；周末则准时打包送往天宫，进行她的‘高级仙位修炼’与宝库搬空业务。”

Chika clapped her hands in sudden, enlightened enthusiasm, her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaving. "Oh! Like a joint-custody weekend visitation rights program! That is pure genius! That way, she still gets to bring back space-age gadgets, and we don't permanently lose our best stress-relief toy!"

千夏兴奋地拍起手来，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔剧烈晃荡着：“噢！就像那种离婚后的周末探视权联合抚育计划！这简直是天才般的脑洞！这样一来，她不仅能继续给家里带回黑科技外挂，咱们也用不着永久失去这个绝佳的解压玩偶了！”

Blind Rin swung her legs on her fluffy cushion, nodding happily. "Um... hello! Rin approves! If the Heavenly Emperor agrees to this sharing deal, Rin doesn't even need to wake up the succubus army! Plus, it saves Rin a mountain of paperwork from having to explain a divine war to the underworld tax auditors!"

盲人小铃坐在毛绒软垫上开心地晃着双腿：“唔……小铃批准！只要天帝老头肯答应这个共享方案，小铃甚至连魅魔军团的起床号都不用吹啦！最棒的是，这还能帮小铃省下一大堆跟地狱财务总监解释神仙大战账目的繁琐公文呢！”

Miyetsuko’s eyes sparkled with absolute, submissive ecstasy. The fallen princess dropped to her knees, hiking her plush Tang-style rear into the air with enthusiastic devotion. "Your Majesty... to be designated as an official imperial-heavenly bridge of bilateral trade... your humble servant is ready to sacrifice her physical endurance for the greater good of both realms!"

美悦子的双眼里闪烁着绝对顺从的狂喜。没落公主当场双膝跪地，将那对丰腴诱人的臀部高高撅起，满脸写着愿为大局献身的觉悟：“陛下……能被册封为连接人间与天庭的‘跨维双边贸易桥梁’……奴婢简直荣幸之极，随时准备为两大维度的繁荣昌盛献出所有的体力与耐力！”

Suited up with this brilliantly depraved diplomatic strategy, the Fujiwara Empire immediately transmitted an encrypted, high-frequency holographic broadcast straight to the gates of the Heavenly Palace.

带着这套极其无耻且高效的外交战术，藤原帝国立刻通过高频量子全息广播，将一份正式的联合声明直接发到了天宫的大门前。

To the absolute relief of the higher-dimensional gods—who secretly held a healthy, cautious respect for Queen Rin’s millions of horny, soul-sucking succubus shock-trooper divisions and didn’t really want to clean up an underworld invasion on their immaculate white marble floors—the Heavenly Emperor reviewed the proposal, checked Miyetsuko’s stellar resume, and enthusiastically accepted the terms.

正如这群老谋深算的家伙所料，高维神仙们其实对小铃手里那数百万只饥渴、擅长吸魂的魅魔突击军团也心存忌惮，压根不想在自己一尘不染的汉白玉广场上打扫地狱战场。天帝在收到这份提案、顺便复核了美悦子那堪称教科书级的“实战考核业绩”后，几乎没有任何犹豫，当场欣然盖章批准了这套“共享协议”。

In the grand salon, the holographic confirmation seal flashed bright gold. Fujiwara grinned broadly, pulling the newly-minted "Shared Asset" onto his lap.

大殿内，全息确认金印轰然亮起。藤原咧嘴大笑，一把将这位刚刚晋升为“帝国级共享资产”的爱妃拉到腿上。

"Well, what do you know? Diplomacy is surprisingly easy when you combine high-tech leverage with underworld intimidation," Fujiwara laughed, giving Miyetsuko’s heavy, velvet-clad hip a resounding slap. "Listen up, my celestial ambassador! You have five days a week of mandatory service under the Emperor's roof, and two days of mandatory overtime in the heavenly bedchamber. Let's make sure both realms get their full return on investment!"

“瞧瞧，只要把高维勒索和地下世界武力威慑完美结合起来，跨国外交其实也就是这么简单嘛，”藤原大笑着，在美悦子那沉甸甸的丝绒臀肉上重重拍了一记，“听好了，我的天界特使大人！你以后周一到周五必须在朕的宫里全天候服役，周末则无缝衔接去天宫加班。咱们务必要让两个维度的投资回报率都达到百分之百！”
