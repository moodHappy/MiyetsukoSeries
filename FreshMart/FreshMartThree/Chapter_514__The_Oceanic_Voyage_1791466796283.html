第五百一十四章：海上巡游

After several uninterrupted weeks enclosed within the eighty-hundred-acre estate of the East River district, the suffocating luxury of the inner palace finally began to breed a faint sense of boredom. Deciding that the imperial court required a change of scenery, Fujiwara ordered his maritime logistics division into action. By the weekend, the entire household had boarded his private, ultra-luxury superyacht—a floating fortress boasting a massive displacement of over fifteen thousand tons—setting sail for the boundless open ocean.

在东川特区那八百亩的私人庄园里连续待了数周之后，深闺内宫那令人窒息的奢靡终于开始滋生出一丝淡淡的无聊。认定皇家内阁需要换换风景，藤原直接下令海事后勤部门拔锚起航。到了周末，全家人已经登上了他那艘排水量高达一万五千多吨、宛如海上堡垒般的私人超豪华巨型邮轮，浩浩荡荡地驶向了一望无际的公海。

On the sun-drenched top deck, a gentle sea breeze ruffled the white cabana curtains. Chika lay spread-eagle on a padded lounger, wearing a ridiculously tiny pink string bikini that completely failed to contain her heavily milk-swollen assets.

在阳光灿烂的顶层甲板上，轻柔的海风吹拂着白色凉亭的纱幔。千夏毫无形象地呈大字型瘫在厚实的躺椅上，身上穿着一套小得离谱的粉色绑带比基尼，那点可怜的布料根本兜不住她那对因充满奶水而沉甸甸、饱满胀痛的丰腴雪峰。

"Ah... it's so hot, and these straps are digging into my neck!" Chika whined, lifting her heavy chest with both hands to relieve the tension. "Boss, why are we out here baking in the sun? At least in the estate, the eunuch doctors keep the temperature at a perfect twenty-four degrees. Now my inventory feels like it's going to turn into warm yogurt!"

“哎呀……好热呀，这比基尼的挂脖细带都快把我的脖子勒断了！”千夏娇滴滴地抱怨着，两手托起沉甸甸的胸脯以缓解坠胀感。“老板，咱们干嘛非得跑大海上顶着太阳烤啊？在庄园里好歹有太监大夫们把恒温保持在完美的二十四度。现在我感觉我的库存都要被晒成温热的酸奶了！”

Sayuri, reclining elegantly under a parasol in a sheer black one-piece swimsuit, let out a melodic, velvet chuckle. She lazily swirled a cocktail equipped with a miniature paper umbrella.

优雅地倚在遮阳伞下的小百合，身上穿着一件半透的黑色连体泳衣，发出了一声悦耳如丝绒般的轻笑。她慵懒地摇晃着手里插着迷你纸伞的特调鸡尾酒。

"Oh, stop fussing, little sister. The Emperor simply wanted to compare your displacement with his superyacht," Sayuri teased, her eyes sparkling with mature mischief. "Though, looking at how those strings are struggling, I'd say your twin torpedoes are exerting far more pressure than the ship's entire hull. If one of those strings snaps, the recoil might sink us all."

“哎呀，好妹妹，你就别抱怨了。咱们陛下不过是想拿你的排水量跟他这艘超级邮轮比一比罢了，”小百合打趣道，美眸里闪烁着成熟少妇的促狭。“不过嘛，看着你那几根细带苦苦支撑的惨状，我敢说你胸前那对双联装鱼雷所承受的压强，可比整艘船的船体还要大得多呢。要是哪根带子突然崩断了，那后坐力怕是能把咱们一船人全给击沉了呢。”

Miyetsuko sat cross-legged on a towel next to Fujiwara’s lounge chair, wearing a tactical-looking dark green two-piece. With thoroughly tamed devotion, she was meticulously massaging SPF 50 sunscreen into Fujiwara’s broad chest, her dewy eyes entirely focused on her task.

美悦子穿着一套极具战术风格的墨绿色分体泳衣，盘腿坐在藤原躺椅旁的浴巾上。带着被彻底驯服的温顺，她正全神贯注、一丝不苟地将SPF 50的防晒霜揉抹进藤原宽厚的胸膛里，水汪汪的美眸中全无旁鹜。

"Sayuri-nee is making a valid point about localized pressure, but strategically speaking, the ship is perfectly safe," Miyetsuko murmured softly, her smooth hands gliding over his muscles. "I had the logistics team upgrade the yacht's defense systems before we left. We have military-grade sonar, automated close-in weapon systems hidden behind the upper deck panels, and a reinforced titanium hull. Master can sunbathe and indulge his depraved urges with absolute geopolitical security."

“小百合姐关于局部压强的物理分析很到位，但从战略角度来说，这艘船绝对安全，”美悦子细声细语地轻喃着，柔滑的双手在他饱满的肌肉上游走。“出发前我已让后勤组全面升级了邮轮的防御系统。咱们配备了军用级声呐，上层甲板隔板后甚至隐藏了自动近防武器系统，还有钛合金加固的船壳。主人大可在这里一边晒日光浴，一边在绝对的地缘政治安全下尽情满足他那些毫无底线的恶趣味。”

"A luxury yacht with anti-aircraft guns?" Chika rolled her eyes. "Only our family would treat a beach vacation like a naval invasion!"

“一艘装了防空高射炮的豪华邮轮？”千夏翻了个大大的白眼。“全天下也就只有咱们家，会把悠闲的海上度假搞得跟诺曼底登陆一样！”

From the edge of the glass-bottomed infinity pool, blind Rin splashed her feet in the water, turning her angelic face toward the salty breeze with a purely innocent, horrifying succubus giggle.

坐在玻璃底无边泳池边缘的盲人小铃，正悠哉地用小脚丫拍打着水面。她将那张宛如天使般的小脸迎向带着咸味的海风，发出了纯洁无暇却又令人毛骨悚然的魅魔娇笑。

"Um... hello, Master? Rin loves the big ocean!" she chimed in sweetly. "In demon lore, if you pour a few drops of Master's divine essence and Chika-nee's fresh milk over the bow of the ship, it will summon the legendary kraken! Then we can capture the giant squid and have the eunuch doctors teach it how to massage Chika-nee's chest with its tentacles! It would be the ultimate eight-handed spa treatment!"

“唔……主人听得见吗？小铃好喜欢这片大海洋呀！”她甜丝丝地插嘴道。“在恶魔的传说里，只要在船头倒上几滴主人的神圣精华和千夏姐姐的新鲜奶水，就能召唤出传说中的北海巨妖呢！到时候咱们就把那只大乌贼活捉了，让太监大夫们教它怎么用触手给千夏姐姐做胸部按摩！那绝对是最顶级的八爪水疗体验哦！”

"Rin! I am not letting a slimy sea monster touch my boobs!" Chika shrieked in spoiled panic, frantically covering her breasts.

“小铃！我死也不会让一只滑溜溜的海怪来碰我的胸部的！”千夏在惊恐中被惯坏地尖叫起来，拼命捂住自己的双乳。

Fujiwara burst into a booming, arrogant roar of laughter that overpowered the sound of the ocean waves. He reached down, grabbing Miyetsuko by her slender waist, pulling her effortlessly onto his lap right over his swim trunks.

藤原爆发出一阵压过海浪声的洪亮、狂妄的大笑。他大手一捞，一把揽住美悦子纤细的腰肢，毫不费力地将她拽到了自己穿着泳裤的大腿上跨坐着。

"Who needs sea monsters when the Emperor already possesses the greatest predators on Earth?" Fujiwara grinned wickedly, his thick hand sliding down Miyetsuko’s wet back to give her tactical swimsuit bottom a resounding slap. "The open sea answers to no laws, making it the perfect playground for a tyrant! Today, we don't just sail the waters—we conquer them! Now, hand me that sunscreen, Sayuri. I need to make sure my Queen's assets are properly glazed before we head to the master cabin to test the ship's suspension!"

“既然本帝王早就把这地球上最顶尖的掠食者全养在了后宫，还要什么海怪？”藤原恶狠狠地坏笑着，厚实的大掌顺着美悦子湿润的脊背滑下，在她那件战术泳裤的臀部结结实实地拍出一声脆响。“公海之上没有任何法律，这简直是暴君最完美的游乐场！今天咱们不仅是航海——咱们要彻底征服这片大洋！行了，把防晒霜递给我，小百合。在咱们去主卧舱测试这艘船的避震系统之前，老子必须亲自给皇后的本钱好好上一层抛光釉彩！”

Amidst the sea breeze and the cry of seagulls, the private sun deck was entirely drowned in the sounds of Chika's complaining, Sayuri's velvet laughter, and the shamelessly decadent banter of the most overpowered household on the planet.

伴随着海风与海鸥的鸣叫，这片私家顶层甲板彻底被千夏的娇嗔抱怨、小百合风情万种的笑声，以及这个星球上战力最恐怖的家庭那毫无底线、奢靡放浪的恶趣味调侃声所淹没。
