Chapter 222: The Idol Purge

发布时间：2026/8/30 18:25:32

第二百二十二章：清扫偶像

The front door of "Fresh Mart" swung open late that night. Stepping inside with the determined aura of a man on a sacred mission, Fujiwara didn't even bother taking off his jacket before marching straight to his study desk.

深夜，“新鲜玛特”的大门被一把推开。藤原带着一股仿佛身负神圣使命般的决绝气场走了进来，甚至连外套都顾不上脱，径直大步走向了自己的书桌。

With ruthless, unhesitating efficiency, he swept his arm across the shelves, gathering every single piece of Kirara merchandise he owned—posters, photo books, limited-edition Blu-rays, and signed acrylic stands. Without a second glance, he marched them over to the kitchen bin and dumped the entire collection straight into the trash.

他动作利落地一把扫过书架，将所有关于绮罗罗的周边——海报、写真集、限定蓝光碟以及签名立牌——全部搜罗起来，看都不看一眼，直接拎到厨房把整整一大包偶像周边毫不留情地全扔进了垃圾桶。

Sitting around the living room table, Rin, Miyetsuko, and Chika froze mid-conversation. They stared at the bin, then up at Fujiwara, their jaws dropping in utter astonishment.

正围坐在起居室桌旁闲聊的铃、美悦子和千夏当场戛然而止。她们看看垃圾桶，又抬头看了看一脸严肃的藤原，惊得下巴都快掉下来了。

"Uh... hate to break it to you, boss," Rin blinked, slowly pointing a finger at the trash can. "Didn't you spend the entire last week crying over missed tickets, treating that pop star like the literal savior of human civilization? Did she reject you so hard that you've gone completely insane?"

“呃……恕我直言，店长大人，”铃眨了眨眼，缓缓伸出一根手指指着垃圾桶，“你前几天不是还为了没抢到票哭天喊地、把人家当成人类文明大救星吗？怎么着，难道是表白被无情拒绝，受刺激精神失常了？”

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, offering a cool, analytical observation. "A textbook psychological defense mechanism. Discarding the idol memorabilia is a symbolic rejection of the unattainable fantasy, usually triggered by severe social disappointment."

美悦子冷静地推了推眼镜，开启了学霸毒舌分析：“经典的心理防御机制。丢弃偶像周边是对不可企及幻想的符号化排解，通常是由严重的现实社交打击所引发的。”

Chika tilted her head, looking thoroughly perplexed. "Wait, did something go wrong at the private dinner? Did she turn out to be completely different in person?"

千夏歪着头，满脸写着问号：“等等，难道昨晚的私密晚宴翻车了？难道大明星真人跟屏幕上完全不一样？”

Facing a barrage of curious, teasing questions, Fujiwara slowly raised a hand, silencing the room with the solemn gravity of a supreme monarch making a state decree.

面对铺天盖地的调侃与追问，藤原缓缓抬起一只手，用一种宛如帝王颁布圣旨般的严肃神情镇住了全场。

"Nothing went wrong. In fact, the dinner was a massive success," Fujiwara declared, puffing out his chest with absolute conviction. "The reason I threw all that plastic junk away is simple: I have finally seen the light."

“没有什么翻车，事实上，那场晚宴成功得无以复加，”藤原挺起胸膛，信誓旦旦地宣布道，“我之所以把这些塑料泡沫全扔了，原因很简单：我终于大彻大悟了。”

He turned around, walking over to wrap his arm around Sayuri—who was sitting quietly on the sofa, sipping her tea with a gentle, immensely pleased smile—and pulled her close against his side.

他转过身，大步走到正端着茶杯、嘴角挂着温柔而极度受用微笑的小百合身旁，一把将她紧紧搂入怀里。

"Listen up, everyone," Fujiwara announced loudly to the room. "Starting tonight, and for the foreseeable future, I am officially declaring that I will sleep exclusively in Sayuri's bed! No exceptions, no rotations!"

“都给我听好了，”藤原高声向全屋宣布，“从今天开始，在未来的很长一段时间里，我正式宣布：我每晚都要和小百合同床共枕！绝不轮换，没有任何例外！”

The living room went dead silent for two seconds.

起居室里瞬间安静了两秒钟。

"Compared to the absolute perfection, the peerless technique, and the boundless devotion of my true goddess right here," Fujiwara continued, pressing a passionate kiss to Sayuri's blushing cheek, "those mainstream celebrities are nothing more than over-packaged garbage! Sayuri is the undisputed, supreme number one goddess of the universe, and I am never looking at another idol again!"

“比起眼前这位无论在技术、温柔还是段位上都堪称绝无仅有的真正的女神，”藤原深情地在小百合泛红的面颊上亲了一口，“那些主流媒体包装出来的流量明星简直就是一文不值的工业垃圾！小百合才是统治宇宙、无可替代的唯一真神，从今以后，我的眼中只有她一个人！”

Rin snorted, rolling her eyes as she burst into laughter. "Oh, my god... He got so thoroughly brainwashed by a single dinner that he's renounced his entire fandom religion!"

铃没忍住噗嗤一声笑喷出来，翻了个巨大的白眼：“我的天呐……这死变态居然吃了一顿饭就被彻底洗脑，连多年的粉丝信仰都当场叛变了！”

Miyetsuko shook her head with a faint, amused smile. "A predictable surrender. When faced with ultimate domestic superiority, all external fantasies collapse."

美悦子无奈地摇了摇头，嘴角却勾起一抹玩味的笑意：“意料之中的投降。在绝对的实力面前，任何外部幻想的崩塌都只是时间问题。”

As the household erupted into warm, teasing laughter, Sayuri nestled deeper into Fujiwara's embrace, her eyes melting with sweet victory. The great idol purge of "Fresh Mart" was officially complete, and the reigning queen's throne was more secure than ever.

在一片温馨而欢快的哄笑声中，小百合幸福地依偎进藤原的怀里，眼底满是打了胜仗般的温柔与得意。“新鲜玛特”轰轰烈烈的清扫偶像行动完美落幕，而正宫女王的宝座，此刻比任何时候都要稳如泰山。
