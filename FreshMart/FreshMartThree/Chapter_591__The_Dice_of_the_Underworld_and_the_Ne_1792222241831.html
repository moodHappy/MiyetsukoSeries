Chapter 591: The Dice of the Underworld and the New Death God

第五百九十一章：冥界骰子与新任死神

Just as the imperial household was still digesting the staggering revelation that Chika was, in fact, the primordial Mother of Earth capable of treating plasma dragons like stray puppies, the holographic terminal on the obsidian desk suddenly emitted a loud, spectral chime.

正当皇室全家还在消化千夏竟然是能把等离子巨龙当野狗训的“大地之母”这一惊天秘密时，黑曜石书桌上的全息终端突然发出了一声尖锐而空灵的幽冥提示音。

A spectral fax machine materialized in mid-air, spitting out a glowing, parchment-like scroll stamped with millions of bureaucratic seals and dripping with phantom Styx water. Miyetsuko rushed over, picked it up, and adjusted her 183828号 bureaucrat glasses. As her eyes scanned the celestial dispatch, her porcelain face turned pale, then flushed with utter, jaw-dropping disbelief.

一台散发着冥河阴风的幽灵传真机凭空在半空中凝聚成形，吐出了一卷闪烁着荧光、盖满无数官僚红头印章的羊皮纸文书。美悦子赶忙小跑过去捡起文件，扶了扶她那副183828号大统领眼镜。当她的目光扫过文书上的字迹时，白瓷般的小脸先是一白，随后因为极度的震惊而彻底涨红了。

"M-Master...!" Miyetsuko stammered, clutching the scroll to her chest as her heavy, newly-blessed physique trembled. "A high-priority emergency dispatch from the entire Underworld network!"

“主……主人……！”美悦子结结巴巴地喊道，将文书死死按在胸口，浑身因为过度激动而微微颤抖，“来自整个冥界网络的最高级加急公文！”

Fujiwara, still reclining on the velvet sofa with his arm around Chika, raised a skeptical eyebrow. "What is it now? Did Ward 183828 run out of toilet paper, or did the Heavenly Emperor send another batch of defective ID badges?"

正搂着千夏斜倚在天鹅门沙发上的藤原挑起一根眉毛：“又怎么了？难不成是183828号乱葬岗的厕纸不够用了，还是天帝那老杂毛又给发了一批劣质的退货员工证？”

"No!" Miyetsuko gasped, her voice cracking. "The entire census of discarded mistresses, vengeful spirits, and regional ward managers across the Styx River just held a democratic election to choose a new Supreme Death God! And... and by a unanimous vote decided entirely by cosmic dice rolling... I won!"

“不是！”美悦子倒吸了一口凉气，声音都变了调，“冥河两岸所有被抛弃的前女友、怨灵以及各片区的大统领们，刚刚通过全冥界联网、用宇宙大骰子进行了一场民主大选来挑选新任的最高统治者——死神！而且……而且通过掷骰子全票一致通过……我当选了！”

The grand salon froze. Even Sayuri, who was in the middle of calculating the stock market value of Chika’s primordial breast milk, dropped her stylus.

大起居殿内瞬间空气凝固。就连正在计算千夏神圣母乳纳斯达克上市估值的小百合，都惊得把手里的触控笔掉在了地上。

"You... became Death?" Chika blinked, her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaving. "The grim reaper? The harvester of souls? The one who carries a giant scythe and puts people in the ground? You?! The girl who just spent the entire morning bragging about her beachfront graveyard view?"

“你……当上死神了？！”千夏眨巴着眼睛，那对沉甸甸的玉兔剧烈起伏着，“那个手拿大镰刀、负责收割灵魂、把人送进土里的终极死神？！你？！刚才还在吹嘘自己海景墓地风景很好的那个丫头？！”

"It gets crazier!" Miyetsuko shrieked, pointing frantically at the scroll. "The election wasn't just about picking a new Death God! The entire collective of millions of discarded ex-girlfriends has passed a binding legislative resolution: We are officially seceding from the Heavenly Palace's jurisdiction!"

“这还不是最离谱的！”美悦子抓狂地尖叫着，疯狂指着文书上的条款，“这次大选不仅是为了选死神！千百万个被天帝玩弄后抛弃的前女友大军，刚刚通过了一项具有法律约束力的终极决议：我们全员正式宣布脱离天宫管辖！”

Blind Rin gasped, her milky eyes widening with sweet, chaotic demonic delight. "Um... hello?! Did you say a mass secession?! Are the ex-girlfriends finally unionizing?!"

盲人小铃惊呼出声，空灵的盲眼里闪烁着甜美而混乱的恶魔喜悦之光：“唔……等一下？！你是说集体独立？！天帝的前女友们终于要成立工会造反啦？！”

"Yes!" Miyetsuko cried out, reading the final paragraph with trembling hands. "The resolution states that since the Heavenly Emperor is a deadbeat serial-cheater who dumps his mistresses with worthless serial numbers, the entire Underworld under my new command as Death is launching a collective, cross-dimensional class-action revenge war against the Heavenly Palace! We are going to march up there, repossess the celestial clouds, and demand cosmic alimony for every single heartbroken soul!"

“对！”美悦子颤抖着读出了最后一行字，“决议书上写着：鉴于天帝是个只知道发劣质编号打发前女友的死渣男，新任死神美悦子将率领整个冥界，正式向天宫发起跨维度的集体复仇大诉讼与军事武装讨伐！我们要打上南天门去，把他们的云端宫殿全依法查封，给所有受过情伤的苦命姐妹讨回高维巨额抚养费！”

Silence fell over the room for three agonizing seconds. Then, Fujiwara slowly stood up from the sofa. A dark, utterly unhinged, magnificent grin slowly spread across the absolute tyrant's face. The Emperor burst into a laughter so loud and thunderous it rattled the cosmic windows of the grand salon.

全场陷入了令人窒息的三秒死寂。紧接着，藤原缓缓从沙发上站了起来。一抹极度黑暗、疯狂且充满狂喜的恶劣笑容，缓缓爬上了这位绝对暴君的脸庞。紧接着，他爆发出一阵震耳欲聋、几乎要把大起居殿宇宙窗户震碎的狂放大笑。

"Hahaha! HAHAHAHA!" Fujiwara roared, throwing his head back as absolute, corporate-takeover genius crystallized in his brain. "A hostile labor strike and military coup organized by millions of the Heavenly Emperor's scorned ex-girlfriends, led by my very own concubine who just got promoted from a local ward keeper to the Supreme Death God via a dice roll?! This is the greatest geopolitical plot twist in the history of the cosmos!"

“哈哈哈！哈哈哈哈哈！”藤原仰天长笑，脑海中瞬间碰撞出了一场堪称商业并购史诗级的绝妙灵感，“由数百万个被天帝始乱终弃的前女友们发动的武装政变和工会大罢工，而领头人竟然是朕刚刚从片儿区守墓人通过掷骰子摇号摇成最高死神的爱妃？！这绝对是宇宙有史以来最伟大的地缘政治神级反转！”

The Emperor stepped forward, wrapped a proud arm around the newly elected Death God, and gave her plush, high-raised rear a resounding, triumphant slap.

暴君大步流星地走上前去，一把搂住这位新上任的死神大人的纤腰，在她那丰腴饱满、高高翘起的臀肉上重重拍了一记，宣告大业将成。

"Forget the Andromeda resort vacation! Cancel the dairy tours!" Fujiwara commanded, his eyes burning with the terrifying ambition of an intergalactic warlord ready to back up an army of furious ex-mistresses. "Grand Commander—no, Death Herself—you have the Emperor's full backing! Mobilize the Underworld legions, issue the cosmic arrest warrants, and let us go show that deadbeat Heavenly Emperor what happens when you cross a man who owes back-pay to millions of angry women!"

“什么仙女座度假旅游全取消！奶茶摊也别摆了！”藤原大手一挥，双眼里燃烧着准备给千百个愤怒前女友撑腰的狂热野心，“大统领——不，死神陛下！朕全盘支持你！立刻给朕集结冥界大军，签发宇宙通缉令，今天咱们就要让那个始乱终弃的天帝老杂毛见识一下，惹怒了一群被白嫖了还拿不到抚养费的女人究竟会有什么下场！”
