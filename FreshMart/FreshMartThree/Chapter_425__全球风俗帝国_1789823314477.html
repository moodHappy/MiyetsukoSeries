第四百二十五章：The Global Pleasure Empire

With over five hundred high-end outer residences scattered across the globe, Fujiwara finally hit a hard operational ceiling. Even with a trillionaire's stamina, keeping up with his sprawling harem had become a logistical nightmare. Realizing he simply couldn't handle the workload alone, he summoned Chika to the executive suite and handed down a brilliant, highly profitable mandate: package all five hundred mistresses into an elite, world-class commercial sex and VIP networking syndicate.

随着全球各地亮起灯火的外宅飙升到五百多处，藤原终于迎来了物理上的极限。就算贵为万亿首富，应付这庞大的后宫也成了一场无法完成的后勤灾难。意识到自己根本分身乏术后，他把千夏叫到了总统套房，大手一挥下达了一道暴利至极的指令：把这五百个外室全部打包，正式转型转型成一条集高端商务与风俗娱乐于一体的全球顶级红灯产业链。

Within weeks, Chika unleashed her masterstroke. The moment the luxury pleasure club opened its doors, filthy-rich oligarchs, corrupt politicians, and global elites from every corner of the planet flooded the platform with astronomical bids. For these power-hungry elites, sleeping with Fujiwara’s former or current mistresses wasn't just about carnal indulgence—it was a backdoor political handshake, a desperate attempt to buy proximity to the world’s reigning tactile emperor and secure lucrative corporate contracts.

短短几周内，千夏便雷厉风行地打出了王牌。奢华的风俗俱乐部一经开业，来自世界各地的腰缠万贯的富豪、腐败政客和全球精英便带着天价资金疯狂涌入平台。对于这群渴望权力的权贵来说，和藤原的外室共度春宵可不仅仅是为了肉体消遣——这分明是一场通往权力核心的敲门砖，是搭上世界首富藤原以获取商业合作的绝佳跳板。

Naturally, the unprecedented commercial expansion brought an endless stream of bizarre, unhinged customer requests into Chika’s bustling office.

理所当然地，这种空前的商业扩张也给千夏热闹非凡的办公室引来了源源不断、脑洞大开的奇葩客户请求。

"Hey, boss lady! You wouldn't believe the kind of psychos we're dealing with today," Chika giggled hysterically, spinning around in her ergonomic chair as she waved a multi-million-dollar wire transfer slip in front of Miyetsuko and Fujiwara. "Some clueless billionaire from overseas actually tried to offer a staggering fortune to buy me out! Can you imagine? Trying to poach the chief financial mastermind of the entire syndicate for a personal harem?"

“喂，老板娘！你们绝对猜不到今天碰上了一群什么精神病，”千夏在人体工学椅上转了个圈，夸张地挥舞着一张数百万美元的电汇凭单冲着美悦子和藤原咯咯娇笑，“海外有个不知死活的蠢富豪，竟然想出天价大把大把砸钱要把我给直接买下来！你们敢信？居然敢挖角整个辛迪加的首席财务大脑去当他的私人禁脔？”

Miyetsuko scoffed, adjusting her glasses with a cold, intimidating glare as she twirled her iron baseball bat. "If that moron shows his face around Fresh Mart again, throw him in the basement and let Rin practice her massage techniques on his spine until he goes bankrupt."

美悦子冷哼一声，带着冰冷摄人的威严推了推眼镜，随手转了转手中的铁棒球棍：“那白痴要是敢再在‘新鲜玛特’附近露脸，直接把他扔进地下室，让铃去他的脊椎上好好操练一下按摩技艺，直到他倾家荡产为止。”

"Oh, but that's not even the wildest part," Chika continued, her eyes sparkling with wicked amusement. "Minutes later, another even more delusional lunatic walked in. He wanted to negotiate for VIP tickets to spend a romantic night with Sayuri! He offered enough cash to buy a small island, totally forgetting that our sweet little first wife is strictly off-limits to the public!"

“哦，但这还不是最离谱的，”千夏接着乐不可支地说道，“几分钟后，又来了一个更精神错乱的狂人。他居然跑来跟我们谈判，问能不能搞到一张和小百合共度春宵的VIP门票！开出的现金足够买下一座小岛了，完全忘了咱们可爱的大房正室是绝对不对外开放的无价之宝！”

Sayuri, who was sitting gracefully nearby peeling a tangerine, offered a sweet, musical giggle. She leaned her head affectionately against Fujiwara’s shoulder, her eyes twinkling with amused submissiveness.

正优雅地坐在旁边剥橘子的小百合发出一串甜美动听的娇笑。她亲昵地把脑袋靠在藤原的肩膀上，眼底闪烁着顺从又带点恶趣味的调笑。

"My, my... people are getting awfully bold these days, aren't they?" Sayuri murmured softly. "I suppose my charm has become far too valuable for the open market. I hope you're going to protect me from these crazy bidders, my darling manager?"

“哎呀呀……这年头的人胆子真是越来越大呢，”小百合轻声呢喃着，“看来人家的魅力在公开市场上已经贬值得太夸张、身价太高啦。亲爱的经理，你可得好好保护我免受这群疯子骚扰哦？”

Fujiwara pulled her closer, letting out a deep, confident laugh as he kissed the top of her head.

藤原将她搂得更紧了些，发出一声自信而宠溺的大笑，低头在她的发顶落下一个吻。

"Don't worry, my queen—your exclusivity is priceless," Fujiwara grinned. "Let the outer-residence mistresses handle the public market and fleece those desperate oligarchs dry. To date, between our revolutionary haptic internet, the trillion-dollar sperm bank empire, and now this newly minted global elite club, we've officially monopolized every single sector of high-end human vice on planet Earth!"

“放心吧我的女王——你的独一无二可是无价之宝，”藤原咧嘴笑道，“让外宅那些女人去应付公开市场、把那群走投无路的富豪榨干就行了。到现在为止，咱们靠着划时代的触觉互联网、万亿级别的精子库帝国，再加上如今新开辟的全球顶级风俗业俱乐部，算是彻底垄断了地球上人类所有的最高级罪恶与享乐产业啦！”

Blind Rin sat quietly in the corner, her wide, unseeing eyes blinking innocently as she folded a fresh stack of soft towels for the club's VIP rooms. She offered a gentle, serene smile.

盲人铃安安静静地坐在角落里，她那双睁得滚圆却空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，正为俱乐部的VIP包厢仔细叠着一叠崭新柔软的毛巾，脸上挂着温柔而宁静的微笑。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered softly. "Are we... are we running a big hotel now? If the guests are tired from all their business deals, I can prepare some warm tea... Please work hard, everyone. It's wonderful that our little store has grown so very big."

“唔……你好呀？”铃轻声呢喃着，“咱们现在是……是在开一家大饭店吗？如果客人们谈生意谈累了，我可以去帮大家准备点热茶哦……大家请继续加油呀。看着咱们的小杂货铺长得这么大，真是让人打心底里高兴呢。”
