Chapter 179: The Melted Iceberg and the Blur of Sweetness

发布时间：2026/8/28 20:11:04

第百七十九章：融化的冰山与傻傻分不清

When Fujiwara and Miyetsuko finally pushed open the door to "Fresh Mart," a welcoming wave of indoor warmth instantly melted away the lingering chill of the sleet storm outside. But what melted even faster was Miyetsuko's legendary, unyielding clinical armor.

当藤原和美悦子终于推开“新鲜玛特”的大门时，屋内的暖气瞬间驱散了两人身上残留的寒意。然而，比冬日风雪消融得更快的，是美悦子那层向来坚不可摧的高冷学霸防线。

She didn't immediately walk over to check her files or adjust her glasses with her usual detached precision. Instead, she walked hand-in-hand with Fujiwara, her cheeks still carrying a soft, rosy flush from the alleyway, and her eyes swimming with a melting, liquid tenderness that left her looking breathtakingly sweet and utterly adorable.

她没有像往常那样一进门就去检查资料或冷酷地推眼镜，而是小鸟依人般任由藤原牵着手。她的小脸上还带着小巷里留下的娇羞红晕，清冷的双眸此刻却被一层融化般的脉脉柔情所填满，整个人散发着一种让人惊艳的甜美与娇媚。

Rin, who was lounging on the sofa with a mug of hot cocoa, froze mid-sip. She slowly lowered her cup, blinking in absolute disbelief as she stared at Miyetsuko.

正端着热可可窝在沙发里休息的铃动作猛地一僵，手里的杯子差点滑落。她难以置信地眨了眨眼，上上下下打量着美悦子。

"Hold on a second..." Rin gasped, pointing a finger in theatrical shock. "Who are you, and what have you done with our cold-hearted, sociologically detached professor?! Why does she look like she just stepped out of a romance manga?!"

“等、等一下……”铃夸张地指着美悦子惊呼出声，“你哪位啊？快把咱们那位冷酷无情、满脑子社会学理论的冰山教授交出来！这怎么出门一趟回来，连眼神拉丝的甜美劲儿都快赶上纯爱女主了？！”

Sayuri stepped over with a gentle smile, but as she looked closely at Miyetsuko, her own eyes widened in soft astonishment. She blinked, tilting her head as she compared Miyetsuko's flushed, tender expression with her own natural gentleness.

小百合端着茶杯迎了上去，可当她看清美悦子此刻的模样时，也不由得惊讶地捂住了嘴巴。她微微歪着脑袋，看着美悦子那副娇羞温存的可爱姿态，又看了看自己。

"Goodness..." Sayuri murmured, her breath catching as a playful giggle escaped her lips. "Rin is right. Miyetsuko, you look... well, you actually look even softer and more tender than me right now! If we stood side-by-side in the dark, I honestly think even Fujiwara wouldn't be able to tell who is who!"

“天呐……”小百合忍不住轻笑出声，“铃说得对。美悦子，你现在看起来……甚至比我还要娇媚温柔呢！要是关了灯站在一起，只怕连藤原都要傻傻分不清到底谁是谁了。”

Chika poked her head out from the kitchen, taking one look at Miyetsuko’s flushed, thoroughly pampered aura before bursting into a bright, knowing giggle. "Oh! I know that look! That's the 'I just got thoroughly loved by our dear master' glow! Sister Miyetsuko has officially crossed over to the dark side of romance!"

千夏从厨房探出头来，一眼扫见美悦子身上那股被狠狠疼爱过后的娇媚余韵，顿时捂着嘴吃吃地笑了起来：“哇！我懂了！这是被主人彻底滋润过后的‘热恋高光’呀！美悦子姐姐今天这甜美度，简直要犯规啦！”

Hearing the playful teasing from all directions, Miyetsuko didn't snap back with her usual sharp academic retorts. Instead, her face turned an even deeper shade of red. With a shy, incredibly uncharacteristic whimper, she gently buried her face against Fujiwara’s shoulder, hiding from their giggling gazes like a bashful newlywed.

面对四面八方的调侃，美悦子罕见地没有用冷冰冰的学术反击，反而小脸腾地红到了耳根。她羞涩地轻哼了一声，像个热恋中的小女人一样，自然而然地把绯红的小脸埋进藤原的肩膀里，躲避着大家揶揄的目光。

Fujiwara laughed warmly, wrapping his arm tightly around her waist as he swept a proud, affectionate gaze across his extraordinary household.

藤原朗声大笑起来，手臂顺势将她不堪一握的细腰紧紧揽入怀中，目光扫过眼前这群性格各异、却同样娇美动人的女人们，满心都是化不开的柔情。

"See? That's the magic of true love and winter sleet," Fujiwara grinned, pulling Miyetsuko closer as the living room erupted into another wave of joyous, heartwarming laughter. "Come on, everyone, dinner is waiting—let's celebrate the grand return of our sweetest new romantic heroine!"

“看见没？这就是真爱和冬日雨夹雪的奇迹，”藤原坏笑着把美悦子搂得更紧了些，在一片欢声笑语中扬声说道，“好啦各位，晚餐已经准备好了——让我们热烈庆祝咱们家新晋的纯爱系大美女诞生！”
