Chapter 585: The Ghosting of the Gods

发布时间：2026/10/17 00:31:04

第五百八十五章：来自神明的彻底无视

Months rolled by in the Fujiwara Empire without a single bolt of divine fire, a single jade decree, or a single batch of 36,000 dragon eggs. The cosmic high-speed pipeline between the mortal realm and the Heavenly Palace had completely, irrevocably flatlined.

在藤原帝国的岁月里，几个月的时间转瞬即逝。然而这段日子里，既没有降下半道神火，也没有飞来一块玉质法旨，更没有那每隔九十天准时送达的三万六千颗龙蛋。人间与天宫之间曾经热闹非凡的跨维高速量子通道，如今彻底、死寂般地断线了。

Heaven had ghosted them. Completely. Absolutely. As if the Fujiwara Empire had never existed at all.

天界把他们给彻底“拉黑”了。从头到尾，干干净净，就像藤原帝国从来没有在这个宇宙中存在过一样。

In the grand salon, the imperial household sat in a state of profound, philosophical bewilderment. Emperor Fujiwara was lazily reclining on the central velvet sofa, tossing a piece of dried fruit into his mouth while staring blankly at the dead holographic broadcast screen.

大起居殿内，皇室全家正陷入一种哲学层面的深度懵圈中。藤原大帝懒洋洋地斜倚在中央天鹅绒沙发上，一边往嘴里扔着果干，一边目光呆滞地盯着那块黑屏的全息直播仪。

"Not a single text. Not a missed call. Not even a spam message about divine tax collection," Fujiwara muttered, shaking his head with a mixture of wounded tyrannical pride and bizarre relief. "Those higher-dimensional bastards just cut us off cold turkey. One day we’re their co-parenting business partners, and the next day we’re blocked on every interdimensional channel."

“连个问候短信都没有。连个未接来电都没有。甚至连催缴神界税的垃圾广告都没一条，”藤原摇了摇头，脸上交织着受伤的暴君尊严与一丝诡异的解脱感，“这帮高维杂毛说断交就断交，一点缓冲都不给。前几天咱们还是深度合作的育儿合伙人，结果转头就把咱们在所有跨维频道里全给拉黑了。”

Wise Empress Sayuri adjusted her folding fan, her macroeconomic brain instantly analyzing the geopolitical autopsy of the silent treatment.

贤后小百合优雅地摇着折扇，宏观经济大脑瞬间上线，开始对这场“冷暴力断交”进行深度的地缘政治尸检。

"It was bound to happen, My Lord," Sayuri purred smoothly, a velvet smirk playing on her lips. "Think about it from a higher-dimensional perspective. Perhaps our little galactic taxes and petty political schemes were simply beneath their cosmic notice. Or perhaps the Heavenly civilization underwent another sudden, hyper-dimensional software upgrade and simply outgrew us."

“这是迟早的事，夫君，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，红唇边挂着一抹丝绒般的冷笑，“从高维文明的视角来想一想就明白了。也许咱们那点蝇头小利和政治小把戏，人家根本连看一眼都嫌浪费卡路里；又或者，天界文明最近又完成了某种跨维度的底层算法升级，直接把咱们给开除出观察名单了。”

Chika chimed in, munching lazily on a snack while lounging on the beanbag chair. Her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaved as she pointed out the cold, hard biological truth.

千夏懒洋洋地躺在豆袋沙发上一边吃零食一边搭腔，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔随着她耸肩的动作微微晃荡，一针见血地指出了残酷的生物学现实。

"Let’s be honest, guys," Chika snorted. "Do humans stop to negotiate treaties with ants? Do we collect income tax from a colony of termites? To them, we aren't even a threat; we’re just a mildly amusing dust mite infestation on a remote rock. Now that Miyetsuko isn't bringing back 36,000 eggs a quarter, they probably forgot we even have a phone number."

“说句戳心窝子的大实话吧，”千夏嗤笑道，“正常人会在路边停下来跟蚂蚁开会、签建交条约吗？会去朝白蚁窝收个人所得税吗？在人家眼里，咱们压根谈不上什么威胁，充其量也就是偏远荒星上的一堆稍微有点意思的尘螨罢了。现在美悦子每季度不去产那三万多颗蛋了，估计人家早就把咱们的区号给彻底忘了。”

Miyetsuko, sitting nearby in her exquisite silk robes, blushed deeply, pulling her collar up. Ever since the Heavenly Emperor had dumped her due to her newly acquired, gravity-defying anatomical dimensions, the fallen princess had quietly retired from international diplomacy.

坐在旁边的美悦子穿着精致的真丝长袍，羞耻地把领口往上拉了拉。自从被天帝因为“尺寸不匹配”而惨遭退货后，这位没落公主就已经正式宣告从跨国外交界光荣退休了。

"Well... at least I still kept my immortal status," Miyetsuko murmured, defending her honor with a weak, trembling smile. "I am officially a retired goddess with a permanent pension of zero credits. And hey! At least my body doesn't have to function as an industrial egg factory anymore."

“好……好歹我还保住了仙位呢，”美悦子小声嘟囔着给自己挽尊，“我现在可是名副其实的‘带编退休老神仙’，虽然一分钱退休金也没有。不过话说回来，至少我的身体总算不用再当全自动工业产卵机了嘛。”

From her floating cushion, blind Rin swung her legs, giggling happily as she tossed a skull-shaped rattle in the air. "Um... hello! Rin thinks it's super great that the old gods left us alone! If they don't care about us, it means we can finally stop pretending to be a civilized empire and go back to doing whatever we want! No more sky taxes, no more divine inspection teams!"

盲人小铃坐在浮空软垫上开心地晃着双腿，把一个骷髅拨浪鼓抛来抛去：“唔……小铃觉得这群老神仙不理咱们反而超级棒耶！既然他们不管咱们了，那咱们以后就可以不用装成什么正经帝国啦，直接恢复原形随便瞎胡闹！再也没有天庭来收空气税和卫生费啦！”

Fujiwara let out a long, resonant laugh. The tyrannical weight that had hung over his empire ever since the first column of divine fire struck the auxiliary capital suddenly evaporated. He stood up, stretching his arms, completely unbothered by the fact that his civilization had just been officially categorized as cosmic irrelevance.

藤原仰天大笑。自从第一道神火砸碎陪都以来，一直压在帝国头顶的那片阴影在这一刻烟消云散。他站起身来伸了个懒腰，对于自己的文明被宇宙高级圈子彻底“开除籍贯”这件事不仅毫无挫败感，反而感到前所未有的轻松。

"Ignored by the gods? Ghosted by the higher dimensions? Excellent!" Fujiwara declared, a wide, unrepentant tyrant’s grin spreading across his face. "If they think we're just ants, then let's build the biggest, most chaotic anthill in the galaxy! No more divine interference, no more celestial child-support quotas, and no more astronomical budget deficits! We are completely, sovereignly, gloriously irrelevant!"

“被神仙无视了？被高维世界拉黑了？太妙了！”藤原大笑着，暴君的脸上绽放出无比无耻的灿烂笑容，“既然他们觉得咱们是蚂蚁，那咱们就在这片星河里把蚁穴建得最大、最混乱！从今以后再也没有神仙来指手画脚，没有跨维催生指标，也没有天文数字的财政赤字了！咱们现在是绝对独立、无比辉煌的‘宇宙编外无产阶级’！”

The grand salon erupted into joyous, chaotic laughter. The Fujiwara Empire had survived higher-dimensional war, cosmic taxation, and the Great Egg-Roll Uprising, only to achieve the ultimate post-modern freedom: being completely, blissfully forgotten by God.

大起居殿内瞬间爆发出欢快而混乱的狂笑。藤原帝国历经高维大战、星际税收以及大蛋卷起义的狂轰滥炸，最终竟然达成了后现代主义的最高自由——被老天爷彻彻底底、无忧无虑地遗忘在了宇宙的角落里。
