第五百一十九章：未开发的领地

For the past two days, Miyetsuko had been experiencing a mild bout of gastrointestinal discomfort, likely from indulging in too many chilled summer desserts.

这两天，美悦子遇上了一阵轻微的肠胃不适，大概是贪吃了太多冰镇夏日甜点的缘故。

Naturally, the overbearing Emperor would not tolerate any flaw in his prized tactical weapon.

理所当然地，这位霸道的帝王绝不容许他珍贵的战术武器出现任何瑕疵。

Fujiwara immediately summoned the elite eunuch medical corps, ordering a comprehensive, top-to-bottom gastrointestinal examination—which inherently included a mandatory colonoscopy.

藤原立刻传唤了顶尖的太监医疗组，下令进行一次自上而下的全面肠胃检查——这自然也包含了一项强制性的肠镜检查。

Inside the sterile, jasmine-scented medical suite, Miyetsuko lay curled on her side upon the examination bed.

在弥漫着茉莉花香的无菌理疗室内，美悦子侧蜷在检查床上。

The former top-tier assassin was dressed in a backless medical gown, her face flushed as red as a ripe tomato while the masked specialists calibrated the sleek, black fiber-optic endoscope.

这位昔日的顶尖杀手穿着一件露背的医疗罩袍，当戴着口罩的专家们调试着那根细长乌黑的光纤内窥镜时，她的脸早就红得像颗熟透的番茄。

As the sterile lubricant was applied, the usually fearless enforcer suddenly panicked.

随着无菌润滑剂的涂抹，这位向来无所畏惧的战神突然慌了神。

She reached out, grabbing Fujiwara’s sleeve with a trembling hand, her dewy eyes welling with timid, shameful tears.

她伸出手，用发颤的小手死死揪住藤原的衣袖，水汪汪的美眸里涌起怯生生、羞耻的泪花。

"M-Master..." Miyetsuko whimpered softly, her voice barely a squeak as she glanced back at the encroaching medical tube. "Please don't let them put that in there... Master hasn't even developed that place yet... it's completely untouched territory..."

“主、主人……”美悦子细弱游丝地呜咽着，嗓音细得像蚊子叫，回头瞥了一眼渐渐逼近的医疗管子。“求您别让他们把那个放进去……主人都还没有开发过那个地方呢……那里可是完完全全的未开发领地呀……”

The entire medical suite erupted into a chorus of uproarious, deeply depraved laughter.

整间理疗室瞬间爆发出一阵震耳欲聋、极度恶趣味的哄堂大笑。

Chika, munching on a bag of potato chips while watching the spectacle, pointed at the blushing assassin and cackled.

正嚼着一袋薯片看热闹的千夏，指着羞愤欲绝的女杀手狂笑起来。

"Hahaha! Listen to the lethal weapon! She's about to undergo a standard medical procedure, and her first thought is that the Boss's 'ownership rights' are being violated by a camera! Miyetsuko-nee, your brain has been completely rewired into a dirty romance novel!"

“哈哈哈！听听这位致命武器说的话！明明是要做个正规的医学检查，她脑子里冒出来的第一个念头，居然是觉得老板的‘所有权’被一个摄像头给侵犯了！美悦子姐姐，你的脑回路已经彻底被改造成小黄书了吧！”

Sayuri leaned against the doorframe, gracefully swirling a cup of warm tea with a velvet smirk.

小百合倚在门框边，优雅地摇晃着一杯温茶，唇边挂着风情万种的坏笑。

"Well, strategically speaking, a fortress's rear gate is always its most vulnerable and highly guarded chokepoint," Sayuri teased smoothly. "Our dear sister is simply preserving her tactical purity for the Commander-in-Chief. Though, yielding your first rear-guard breach to a piece of medical equipment does seem a bit unromantic."

“哎呀，从战略角度来说，要塞的后门向来都是最脆弱、防守最严密的关隘呢，”小百合软糯地打趣道。“咱们的好妹妹不过是在为最高统帅保留她的战术纯洁性罢了。不过，把后方阵地的‘初次失守’交待给一台医疗仪器，听起来确实是有些不够浪漫呢。”

From her spot on the medical stool, blind Rin swung her legs and giggled with horrifying succubus innocence.

坐在医疗圆凳上的盲人小铃晃荡着双腿，发出了令人毛骨悚然的纯洁魅魔娇笑。

"Um... hello, Master? Rin thinks the solution is very simple!" she chimed in brightly. "If Miyetsuko-nee is sad that the doctor's tube is going in first, Master should just go in right now and claim the territory! Once Master leaves his demonic scent back there, the medical camera will just be taking pictures of Master's great victory!"

“唔……主人听得见吗？小铃觉得解决办法非常简单呀！”她甜丝丝地插嘴道。“如果美悦子姐姐因为医生叔叔的管子要先插进去而伤心，那主人现在就应该直接冲进去宣誓主权嘛！只要主人在里面留下了恶魔的气味，那医疗摄像头拍下来的，不就全都是主人的伟大胜利了吗！”

"Rin! You little pervert, this is a sterile medical environment!" Chika shrieked, nearly choking on a potato chip.

“小铃！你这个小变态，这里可是无菌的医疗环境！”千夏尖叫起来，差点被一片薯片给噎死。

Fujiwara let out a booming, arrogant roar of laughter, thoroughly intoxicated by the absolute, shameless devotion of his tamed assassin.

藤原爆发出一阵狂妄洪亮的大笑，彻底被这只驯服的女杀手那毫无廉耻的绝对忠诚给取悦了。

He reached down, playfully swatting Miyetsuko's exposed, shivering peach cheeks with a resounding smack.

他伸出大手，在美悦子暴露在外、微微发颤的蜜桃臀上戏谑地拍出了一声清脆的脆响。

"You truly are the Emperor's greediest little demon," Fujiwara grinned wickedly, running a dominant hand through her silky hair. "Don't worry about the camera taking your 'first time', Miyetsuko. Medical reconnaissance is just preparing the battlefield! Once the eunuchs confirm your rear logistics are perfectly healthy, the Emperor will personally conduct a massive, heavy-artillery landing operation tonight to formally develop that untouched territory!"

“你可真是本帝王最贪吃的小妖精，”藤原恶劣地坏笑着，霸道的大手揉乱了她柔顺的长发。“别操心什么摄像头夺走了你的‘第一次’，美悦子。医疗侦察只不过是在为战场做准备！等太监们确认你的后方补给线绝对健康之后，今晚，本帝王就会亲自发起一场大规模的重火力登陆作战，正式开发那片处女地！”

Miyetsuko buried her burning face into the sterile pillow, letting out a sweet, muffled whine of pure, decadent surrender as the medical exam officially commenced amidst the roaring laughter of the imperial harem.

美悦子将滚烫的脸颊深深埋进了无菌枕头里，在后宫佳丽们哄堂的大笑声中，发出了一声甜腻、沉沦的娇吟，任由这场医学检查正式拉开帷幕。
