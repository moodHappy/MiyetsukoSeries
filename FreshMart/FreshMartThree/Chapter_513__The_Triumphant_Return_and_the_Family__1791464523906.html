Chapter 513: The Triumphant Return and the Family Meeting

发布时间：2026/10/8 21:02:02

第五百一十三章：凯旋而归与家庭茶话会

The sleek black limousine glided smoothly past the iron gates of the eight-hundred-acre estate, returning the triumphant Emperor to his private sanctuary late in the night.

黑色流线型的豪华加长轿车平稳地滑过八百亩庄园的铁艺大门，在深夜将凯旋的帝王送回了他的私人圣殿。

When Fujiwara strode into the grand salon, he was radiating the smug, overbearing aura of a conqueror who had just successfully annexed a small country.

当藤原大步流星地迈进豪华大起居殿时，他浑身上下都散发着一种刚刚成功吞并了一个小国家的征服者般得意、霸道的气场。

The four ladies of the house were gathered around the velvet sofas, sipping warm milk and herbal tea.

家里的四位佳丽正围坐在天鹅绒沙发旁，小口啜饮着温热的牛奶与花草茶。

"Rejoice, my imperial court!" Fujiwara declared grandly, tossing his suit jacket to a waiting servant. "Little Rin's tactical proposal has been flawlessly executed!"

“欢呼吧，我的皇家内阁！”藤原不可一世地宣告着，将西装外套随手扔给一旁候着的侍从。“小铃的战术提案已经被完美执行了！”

Chika nearly dropped her glass of warm milk, her eyes widening as she clutched her plush, silk-draped chest.

千夏差点摔了手里的温牛奶杯，眼睛瞪得老大，死死捂住被真丝包裹着的丰腴胸口。

"Wait... you don't mean..." Chika gasped, her voice trembling with spoiled disbelief. "Boss, did you actually spend a fortune to buy out that entire three-generation family bundle?!"

“等等……你该不会是说……”千夏倒抽了一口凉气，嗓音里满是被惯坏的不可思议，“老板，你居然真的砸下天价，把那个祖孙三代同堂的大礼包给全盘买断了？！”

Fujiwara sank heavily into the central armchair, swirling a fresh glass of bourbon with a wicked, deeply satisfied smirk.

藤原重重地陷进中央的主位单人沙发里，摇晃着新倒的一杯波旁威士忌，嘴角挂着一抹极其恶劣又心满意足的坏笑。

"An absolute, unbreakable genetic monopoly," he boasted shamelessly. "From the fifty-four-year-old matriarch to the thirty-six-year-old mother, right down to the nineteen-year-old fresh blossom. The Emperor claimed the entire bloodline in a single night."

“绝对的、牢不可破的基因垄断，”他厚颜无耻地吹嘘道。“从五十四岁的老祖母，到三十六岁的母亲，再到十九岁刚绽放的鲜花。本帝王在一夜之间包揽了整条血脉。”

Miyetsuko set her teacup down with a soft clink, her dewy eyes shining with genuine, clinical admiration.

美悦子轻轻放下茶杯，发出一声微弱的脆响，水汪汪的美眸里闪烁着发自内心、冷酷如手术刀般的赞赏。

"A textbook logistical strike," the former assassin murmured smoothly, resting her chin on her hand. "By conquering the root, the trunk, and the branch simultaneously, Master completely neutralized any psychological resistance they could offer each other. Tactically speaking, it is the ultimate siege."

“教科书级别的后勤打击，”这位前顶尖杀手细声细语地轻喃着，单手托着香腮。“通过同时征服树根、树干与树枝，主人彻底瓦解了她们之间可能存在的任何心理防线。从战术上讲，这是终极的围城战。”

"Oh, please, Miyetsuko-nee, don't encourage his financial insanity!" Chika whined, pouting furiously as she kicked her bare feet against the cushions. "Do you know how many zero's the accounting department had to authorize to get three generations to abandon their family shame in one VIP room? You could have bought a fleet of private jets!"

“哎呀求你了，美悦子姐姐，别再助长他这种财务上的疯狂行径啦！”千夏气急败坏地娇嗔着，光着的小脚丫在靠垫上乱踢。“你知道财务部得批多少个零，才能让那祖孙三代在同一间VIP包厢里彻底抛弃家族羞耻心吗？这笔钱都够买一支私人机队了！”

Sayuri gracefully crossed her legs, letting out a velvet, melodious sigh of amused exasperation.

小百合优雅地交叠起双腿，发出一声既好气又好笑的慵懒长叹。

"Truly, our Master is a philanthropist of the highest order," Sayuri purred, her tone dripping with affectionate sarcasm. "Funding a family reunion in the most depraved manner imaginable. Tell me, darling, are you planning to bring this botanical garden back to our estate, or will they remain outsourced?"

“咱们主人真真是一位最高级别的慈善家呢，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，语气里浸透了宠溺的冷嘲热讽，“用全天下最不知廉耻的方式，资助了一场盛大的家庭大团圆。跟我说说，亲爱的，您是打算把这座植物园搬回咱们庄园呢，还是让她们继续留在外面做外包服务？”

Fujiwara let out a loud bark of laughter, waving his hand dismissively.

藤原大笑了一声，不以为意地摆了摆手。

"Keep them outsourced, of course!" he declared with imperial pride. "They were a magnificent novelty, a premium consumer experience! But the inner palace is reserved exclusively for the most pristine treasures! No street-bought fantasy can replace my wise Queen, my lethal enforcer, my sweet succubus, or my precious milk cow!"

“当然是继续外包！”他带着帝王的傲气宣告道。“她们是一场绝妙的猎奇，一次顶级的消费体验！但这座内宫，永远只为最纯洁无瑕的稀世珍宝保留！任何花钱买来的街头幻想，都替代不了我的智慧皇后、我的致命利刃、我的甜美魅魔，还有我珍贵的小奶牛！”

From her corner on the Persian rug, blind Rin clapped her hands with purely innocent glee.

坐在波斯地毯角落里的盲人小铃，开心地拍着双手，满脸都是纯粹无邪的欢乐。

"Um... hello, Master? Rin is so happy her advice worked!" the succubus giggled sweetly. "Now that Master has conquered a whole family tree, your demonic prestige has leveled up again! Does this mean Master will be extra hungry for tomorrow morning's breakfast?"

“唔……主人听得见吗？小铃好开心自己的建议奏效了呢！”小魅魔甜丝丝地娇笑着。“既然主人已经征服了一整棵家族树，那您在恶魔界的威望肯定又升级啦！这也就是意味着，主人明天早上吃早点的时候会格外饿对不对？”

Chika instantly turned pale, clutching her chest as she glared at the laughing Emperor.

千夏瞬间花容失色，死死捂住胸口，瞪着那个哈哈大笑的帝王。

"No way! You just drained three generations of women!" Chika shrieked in spoiled panic. "Don't you dare come after my dairy supply with double the appetite tomorrow! The eunuch doctors will have to work overtime to fix me!"

“没门！你刚刚才榨干了三代女人！”千夏在被惯坏的惊慌中尖叫起来。“明天早上你休想带着双倍的胃口来洗劫我的奶源！太监大夫们非得加班加点才能把我修好不可！”

Amidst Chika's dramatic protests and Sayuri's soothing laughter, Fujiwara leaned back into his cushions, a thoroughly fulfilled tyrant basking in the chaotic, wicked harmony of his perfect home.

在千夏夸张的抗议与小百合温柔的轻笑声中，藤原悠然地靠回软垫里，宛如一位彻底满足的暴君，尽情沉浸在他完美家庭那喧闹、恶趣味的和谐之中。
