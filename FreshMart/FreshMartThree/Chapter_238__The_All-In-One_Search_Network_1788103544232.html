Chapter 238: The All-In-One Search Network

发布时间：2026/8/30 23:25:43

第二百三十八章：各显神通的人际网

With Fujiwara successfully fled the scene, leaving behind his dramatic, tear-soaked manifesto of betrayal, the living room of "Fresh Mart" transformed instantly into a high-stakes command center.

随着藤原留下一封充满悲愤与血泪的离家出走信、决绝地消失在夜色中，“新鲜玛特”的起居室瞬间变成了一个高度戒备的高级作战指挥中心。

If they wanted to drag their runaway store manager back before he did anything truly stupid, ordinary searching wasn't going to cut it. They needed a massive, all-encompassing dragnet. And so, the four women pulled out their phones and activated their respective, wildly contrasting social networks across Donglai Island.

要想在自家店长惹出什么无法挽回的乱子之前把他抓回来，靠常规的盲目搜寻显然是不可能的。必须撒下一张覆盖全城的超级大网。于是，四位女侠当机立断，各自掏出手机，彻底发动了她们在东莱岛上截然不同、且能量惊人的庞大人际关系网。

First came Miyetsuko. With a solemn, grim expression, she logged into her academic portal and started pinging her elite network of university professors, research fellows, and intellectual elites across the island’s higher education institutions, instructing them to keep their eyes peeled for a depressed-looking man wandering near libraries or university districts.

首先是美悦子。只见她面沉如水地打开学术论坛和通讯录，直接动用了她在东莱岛各大高校和科研机构里庞大的学者、教授圈子，发布了一级寻人令，要求全岛的高知分子一旦发现可疑、失魂落魄的男性身影立刻上报。

Next was Sayuri. Pulling from her legendary, well-preserved contact list from her past life in the adult entertainment industry, she discreetly reached out to former colleagues, agency managers, and high-end entertainment club operators, spreading Fujiwara’s photo across the nocturnal underground circles of Donglai Island with a quiet, polite request for assistance.

接着是小百合。她凭借着当年在业界积累的庞大人脉，不动声色地联系了多位曾经合作过的圈内同仁、经纪人以及高端风俗娱乐场所的负责人，将藤原的照片悄悄发进了夜生活的地下圈子里，委托各路夜场大佬帮忙留意。

Rin didn't lag behind either. She fired up Discord, gaming forums, and local esports club groups, mobilizing a small army of hardcore gamers, live-stream moderators, and internet cafe owners. Within minutes, her network of digital gremlins was scouring every gaming lounge and cyber-cafe from one end of the island to the other.

铃自然也不甘示弱。她直接登录各大游戏开黑群、电竞论坛和本地网咖管理群，发动了一大批常年混迹网络的电竞圈网瘾少年和网吧老板，铺天盖地地在各大虚拟与现实的战区展开搜寻。

And then, there was Chika. If the other three networks were specialized scalpels, Chika’s was a massive, nuclear-grade sledgehammer. Leveraging her massive, chaotic circle of university acquaintances, campus clubs, and part-time student networks, she blasted Fujiwara’s photo across thousands of smartphones, turning her entire undergraduate cohort into an unstoppable search brigade.

最后则是人脉最恐怖的千夏。如果说前三者的网络是精准的精密手术刀，那千夏的人脉就是核弹级别的无差别轰动。她凭着在大学里社牛般的神仙交际圈，一口气发动了数千名大学生、社团成员和兼职生，将藤原的照片瞬间刷屏了无数个校园群聊。

Hours ticked by as the sun slowly dipped below the horizon, plunging Donglai Island into the neon glow of evening.

时间在紧张的搜寻中一分一秒地流逝，夕阳终于沉入地平线，东莱岛被一片华丽的霓虹夜色所笼罩。

Just as the tension in the living room reached a boiling point, two notifications buzzed simultaneously on separate phones.

就在起居室里的气氛紧绷到极点时，两部不同的手机几乎在同一秒发出了清脆的提示音。

Sayuri glanced down at her screen first. A message had popped up from one of her old acquaintances in the high-end entertainment sector: “Found him. He’s drowning his sorrows at the VIP lounge of ‘Moonlight Haven’ club.”

小百合最先低头扫了一眼屏幕。那是她风俗店圈子的老熟人发来的加密消息：“人找到了。这小子正躲在‘月光港湾’高端风俗会所的VIP包厢里借酒消愁呢。”

At the exact same moment, Miyetsuko’s phone lit up. An urgent message had arrived from one of her esteemed academic professor colleagues: “Professor Miyetsuko! Uh... regarding that missing man you asked us to look for... I happened to spot him stumbling into the VIP area of a local adult entertainment club while I was conducting... er, crucial field research on urban sociology.”

几乎就在同一瞬间，美悦子的手机也剧烈震动起来。发信人竟然是她学术圈里一位德高望重的教授同事：“美悦子教授！咳……关于您托我们寻找的那位失踪先生……我刚才在进行一项关于城市夜生活与人类行为学的‘重要实地田野调查’时，恰好亲眼看到他跌跌撞撞地走进了某家知名风俗会所的VIP区……”

Miyetsuko stared at the screen, her face turning pitch black.

美悦子死死盯着屏幕上的字，一张白皙的小脸瞬间黑得像锅底。

A crucial field research on urban sociology in a high-end brothel?! Miyetsuko’s eye twitched in pure, unadulterated academic fury as she realized her supposed "upstanding, respectable colleague" was actually just slumming it in the exact same shady establishment.

去高端风俗店进行城市社会学的重要实地田野调查？！美悦子的眼角疯狂抽搐，内心掀起惊涛骇浪：好一个道貌岸然、满口仁义道德的老色批同事，居然背着学术委员会跑去那种地方鬼混！

Despite her boiling rage toward the hypocritical professor, Miyetsuko quickly suppressed her anger, turned to the rest of the household, and held up her phone.

尽管对那个道貌暗然的家伙恨得牙痒痒，但大局当前，美悦子还是深吸一口气压下怒火，抬起头看向众人，晃了晃手机：

"We have a confirmed location," Miyetsuko announced, her voice tight with a mix of gratitude and murderous intent. "He’s at a well-known high-end entertainment club on the east side of Donglai Island."

“位置已经确认了，”美悦子咬着牙冷声宣布道，“多亏了……某位正在实地田野调查的‘优秀教授’提供的情报。他在东莱岛东区那家知名的风俗会所里找到了店长。”
