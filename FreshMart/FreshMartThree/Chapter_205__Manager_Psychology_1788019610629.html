第二百五章：店长心理学

An otherwise quiet afternoon at "Fresh Mart" was suddenly shattered by Rin’s explosive, triumphant cackle echoing from the study room.

原本宁静的一个下午，却突然被铃从书房里传出的狂笑声彻底打破。

"Oh, my god! You guys have got to hear this! I struck absolute gold while snooping through Miyetsuko’s old digital archives!" Rin shrieked, strutting out of the study with a tablet in her hand, her eyes sparkling with malicious, highly amused glee.

“我的天哪！你们绝对得听听这个！我在美悦子以前的旧文档里翻到了绝世宝藏！”铃一边尖叫着，一边拿着平板电脑从书房里快步走了出来，眼里闪烁着看热闹不嫌事大的兴奋光芒。

Miyetsuko, who was sipping her afternoon tea at the kitchen island, froze mid-sip. Her stoic, academic composure instantly fractured, a rare flash of panic crossing her sharp features. "Rin! Give that back right now. Some empirical data is meant to remain strictly classified!"

正坐在厨房岛台旁品茶的美悦子动作猛地一僵。她那向来冷静沉着的学术面具瞬间出现了一丝裂痕，锐利的眼眸里罕见地闪过一丝慌乱：“铃！把那个立刻还给我。有些实证数据是必须永久保密的！”

"Too late, it's already uploaded to my brain!" Rin laughed, holding the tablet out of reach as she dramatically read out the title of the recovered document to the rest of the room.

“晚啦，已经上传到本姑娘的大脑里啦！”铃坏笑着把平板举过头顶，声情并茂地念出了那篇被封存文件的核心标题：

“Learning from the Supermarket Manager: How to Make Your Girlfriend Willingly Wash Your Underwear.”

《和超市店长学习，如何让女朋友心甘情愿的洗内裤》。

The living room fell into a stunned, two-second silence before exploding into absolute chaos.

起居室里安静了两秒钟，随后爆发出几乎要把屋顶掀翻的狂笑。

"Wait... what?!" Chika choked on her saliva, pointing at Miyetsuko with wide, disbelieving eyes. "Sister Miyetsuko—our rational, cold, high-and-mighty intellectual queen—actually wrote a psychological thesis on how Fujiwara tricked her into doing his laundry?!"

“等等……什么鬼？！”千夏呛得连连咳嗽，瞪大眼睛指着美悦子，“美悦子姐姐——咱们那位高冷理智、满腹经纶的学霸女王，居然写过这种怎么被藤原忽悠去洗内裤的学术论文？！”

Sayuri covered her mouth, her shoulders shaking with helpless, melodic giggles as she looked at a thoroughly blushing Miyetsuko. "Goodness, Miyetsuko... Is that true? Did our resident store manager actually use psychological warfare on you?"

小百合掩嘴娇笑，肩膀笑得直颤，转头看向满脸通红的美悦子：“天呐，美悦子……这是真的吗？咱们那位店长大人当年居然对你发动过心理战术？”

Fujiwara, lounging comfortably on the sofa, smirked with supreme, unapologetic pride, crossing his arms behind his head. "Hey, don't knock the classic 'Manager Psychology'! It's a masterclass in behavioral conditioning. I didn't force her—I simply engineered a scenario where doing my laundry felt like a profound intellectual privilege."

舒服地躺在沙发上的藤原得意洋洋地翘起二郎腿，双手抱头坏笑道：“喂，别诋毁伟大的‘店长心理学’好不好！这可是行为心理学的大师级应用。我当年可没强迫她——我只是巧妙地设计了一个场景，让她觉得洗我的内裤简直是一种无上的智力特权。”

Miyetsuko let out an exasperated sigh, pinching the bridge of her nose as she surrendered to the public execution. She adjusted her glasses, trying to salvage a shred of her professional dignity.

美悦子无奈地叹了口气，抬手捏了捏鼻梁，索性放弃了抵抗。她重新推了推眼镜，试图挽回最后一丝学者的尊严：

"It was... an observational study," Miyetsuko defended herself dryly, her cheeks tinged with a delicate pink. "At the time, I was fascinated by how a man with such subpar academic credentials could manipulate domestic dynamics so effortlessly. By the time I realized I had fallen for his manipulative retail tactics, I was already scrubbing his clothes with a smile on my face."

“那是一次……行为观察实验，”美悦子红着脸强行辩解道，“我当时只是对这种现象很好奇：一个学识平平的杂货铺老板，究竟是怎么轻而易举地操纵家庭动力学的。等我反应过来自己中了这小子的零售业营销圈子时，我已经高高兴兴地帮他把衣服洗干净了。”

The laughter softened into a warm, affectionate hum as the conversation naturally drifted into a deeper, reflective question.

笑声渐渐平息，化作了一阵温馨而闲适的低语，大家的话题也不知不觉转到另一个让人忍俊不禁的归宿上。

"Still," Sayuri smiled gently, looking across the room at Fujiwara with eyes full of unconditional devotion. "It really makes you wonder. Out of all the people in the world, why did we all end up following a chaotic, perverted grocery store manager like him?"

“不过说真的，”小百合温柔地笑着，目光深情地落在藤原身上，“仔细想想还挺神奇的。天底下有那么多优秀的男人，为什么咱们姐妹几个最后全心全意追随的，偏偏是个满脑子恶趣味的杂货铺老板呢？”

Rin grinned, leaning back against the sofa. "Because normal men are boring. Only a certified lunatic like Fujiwara could turn everyday life into an unhinged sitcom."

铃往沙发上一靠，坏笑道：“因为正经男人太无聊啦。只有像藤原这种纯种神经病，才能把平淡无奇的日子过成一部惊世骇俗的情景喜剧。”

"And because beneath all his degenerate tricks," Chika added, hugging Fujiwara’s arm with a bright, adoring smile, "he's the only one who can keep this crazy, broken family together with so much love."

“而且因为在这堆变态花招的底下，”千夏笑嘻嘻地抱住藤原的胳膊，“他是唯一一个能用满满的爱，把咱们这个奇葩又温暖的家紧紧凝聚在一起的人呀。”

Surrounded by the clever, witty, and deeply devoted women who ran his life, Fujiwara just grinned, pulling them all into a chaotic, laughter-filled group hug, proving once and for all that "Manager Psychology" was the greatest textbook ever written.

被这群聪明、毒舌却又死心塌地的女人们包围在中间，藤原朗声大笑起来，一把将她们统统揽入怀中。事实证明，“店长心理学”绝对是这世上最伟大的畅销教科书。
