Chapter 433: The Billionaire's Reward

发布时间：2026/10/2 21:19:53

第四百三十三章：富豪的奖赏

While the rest of the family was busy monitoring world leaders playing farm hands on satellite feeds, blind Rin was quietly browsing the internet on her specialized tactile tablet, her fingers gliding smoothly across the braille-and-haptic display as she caught up on viral social media trends.

正当家里其他人忙着通过卫星监控全球领袖在田里当农夫时正乐不可支时，盲人铃正安安静静地拿着她的触觉平板电脑上网冲浪，手指熟练地在盲文触觉显示屏上滑过，津津有味地刷着热门社交媒体上的各种奇闻异事。

"Um... hello, everyone?" Rin piped up softly, tilting her head with an innocent, curious blink. "I just read the funniest little story online. It said that when a wife gets pregnant, her husband has to stop having regular intercourse. But since the family was too poor to afford a proper professional establishment, the virtuous, understanding wife decided to personally suck her husband's penis every single day to take care of his physical needs while she was expecting!"

“唔……大家快听呀？”铃轻声开口，无辜而好奇地歪了歪头，“我刚刚在网上看到了一则超级搞笑的趣闻。上面说，当老婆怀孕期间，丈夫就不得不暂停同房。可家里又穷得去不起正规的风俗场所，于是这位贤惠体贴的妻子为了照顾丈夫的生理需求，竟然在孕期每天都亲自给欲望旺盛的老公嗦鸡巴来解决问题呢！”

The penthouse instantly fell silent for a fraction of a second before Fujiwara burst into a deep, emotional chuckle, deeply moved by the sheer, unadulterated dedication of the fictional wife.

顶层公寓里瞬间安静了零点几秒，随后藤原爆发出深深被触动的爽朗大笑，被这个虚构妻子那纯粹无私的奉献精神深深感动了。

"Now that is what I call true, old-fashioned marital virtue," Fujiwara declared, wiping a tear of amusement from his eye as he pulled Sayuri closer. "In this day and age, finding a woman willing to go to such lengths for her man is a rare treasure! We can't have good, hardworking folks suffering just because they're broke."

“这才是真正的传统美德啊，”藤原笑着擦掉眼角笑出的泪水，顺手将小百合搂得更紧了些，“在这个年头，能找到一个愿意为了自家男人做到这一步的女人，简直是稀世珍宝！咱们可不能让这种老实肯干的穷苦老百姓吃亏。”

Chika spun around in her ergonomic chair, her eyes sparkling with wicked, entrepreneurial mischief.

千夏在人体工学椅上转了个圈，双眼里闪烁着恶作剧般的创业火花。

"Oh, I love this!" Chika giggled wildly, tapping away at her tablet. "If that loyal, hardworking husband is struggling so badly with his wife’s pregnancy, let’s reward his persistence! I propose we reach out, pluck him straight out of obscurity, and reward him with one of the absolute most breathtaking beauties straight out of our Hall of Great Breasts!"

“哎呀，这个我喜欢！”千夏疯狂地咯咯娇笑，在平板上飞速敲击着，“既然那个忠诚又努力的老公被老婆怀孕折腾得这么惨，咱们得好好奖励他的坚持！我提议，直接把这小子从茫茫人海里捞出来，把咱们大胸宝殿里最顶级的绝世尤物直接赐一个给他！”

Miyetsuko scoffed playfully, adjusting her glasses with a cold, intimidating smirk as she twirled her iron baseball bat.

美悦子推了推眼镜，带着冷酷而坏坏的微笑转着手中的铁棒球棍调侃道。

"A stunning courtesan is a good start, but it's not enough," Miyetsuko added, crossing her arms with a commanding mob-boss flourish. "You can't just hand a guy a three-thousand-beauty Hall of Great Breasts graduate without any capital! We need to wire him a few tens of millions in cash too, so his pregnant wife doesn't have to stress about diapers. We can't have the girls coming out of our grand hall enduring any financial hardship!"

“送个绝色佳人固然不错，但还远远不够，”美悦子霸气地交叉起双臂补充道，“你总不能白白塞给人家一个大胸宝殿出来的极品姑娘却不给安家费吧！咱们得直接甩给他几千万现金，好让他那怀孕的老婆连买尿不湿的压力都没有。绝对不能让咱们大胸宝殿出来的姑娘跟着受半点穷酸罪！”

Sayuri offered a sweet, musical giggle, leaning her head affectionately against Fujiwara's shoulder while shooting him a playful, admiring glance.

小百合发出一串甜美悦耳的娇笑，亲昵地把脑袋靠在藤原的肩膀上，同时向他投去一个调皮又崇拜的眼神。

"My, my... now that is what I call a true billionaire's reward," Sayuri murmured softly. "Rewarding wifely devotion with high-society concubines and multi-million-dollar handouts? Only our magnificent manager could turn a viral internet joke into a generous philanthropic operation."

“哎呀呀……这才是真正的富豪的奖赏嘛，”小百合轻声呢喃着，“用上流社会的绝色佳人和数千万巨款去奖赏妻子的忠诚？也就只有咱们伟大的经理才能把一则网络趣闻办成一场慷慨激昂的大慈善啦。”

Fujiwara laughed heartily, kissing Sayuri’s forehead before raising his glass in a grand toast.

藤原开怀大笑，在小百合的额头上亲了一口，随后高高举起酒杯敬酒。

"Consider it done!" Fujiwara grinned proudly. "Let the word go out: loyalty, wifely devotion, and good old-fashioned oral endurance will always be handsomely rewarded in our empire! Cheers to the billionaire's reward!"

“这事就这么定啦！”藤原得意洋洋地咧嘴笑道，“传我的话出去：在咱们的帝国里，忠诚、妻子的奉献以及老实肯干的口技耐力，永远都能获得最丰厚的回报！为富豪的奖赏干杯！”

Blind Rin sat quietly in the corner, her wide, unseeing eyes blinking innocently as she handed a fresh round of warm drinks to the table, offering a sweet, gentle smile.

盲人铃安安静静地坐在角落里，她那双睁得圆溜溜却空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，体贴地为桌上众人递上新一轮的热饮，露出了一个甜美温柔的微笑。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered softly. "Are we... are we giving prizes to good husbands today? If the lucky gentleman needs a nice blanket for his growing family, I can knit him a very big one... Please drink up, everyone. It's so nice that love and generosity are being rewarded."

“唔……你好呀？”铃轻声呢喃着，“咱们今天是在……是在给好丈夫发奖品吗？如果这位幸运的先生需要给添丁进口的家里准备毛毯，我可以帮他织一条特别大的哦……大家快喝吧，看到爱与慷慨能得到回报真是太好啦。”
