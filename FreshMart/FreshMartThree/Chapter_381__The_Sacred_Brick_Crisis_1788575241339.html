Chapter 381: The Sacred Brick Crisis

发布时间：2026/9/5 10:27:20

第三百七十八章：圣砖危机

By midnight, the fluorescent lights of Fresh Mart hummed with a depressing, sterile buzz. Fujiwara and Sayuri were still on their hands and knees, exhaustedly scrubbing the ketchup-soaked aisle with standard-issue plastic toothbrushes.

到了午夜时分，“新鲜玛特”的日光灯发出令人抑郁的、冰冷的嗡嗡声。藤原和小百合依然跪趴在地上，精疲力竭地用标准塑料牙刷拼命刷洗着那条被番茄酱浸透的过道。

However, every frantic scrub of Fujiwara's toothbrush only smeared the thick sauce further, causing the gruesome red stains to spread across the white linoleum until the entire grocery store floor looked like the aftermath of a horrific chainsaw massacre.

然而，藤原每用牙刷狂躁地刷一下，只会把浓稠的酱汁抹得更开，导致这些惨烈的红色污渍在白色的油毡地面上不断扩散，直到整个杂货铺的地板看起来简直就像是一场恐怖电锯大屠杀的案发现场。

"My knees are bleeding, my back is broken, and my exposed breast is freezing!" Sayuri whimpered, shivering in her shredded, ketchup-stained stockings as she weakly dabbed at the floor. "I am an executive madam, not a crime scene cleanup crew! If I die of hypothermia on this ketchup floor, I'm haunting you forever, Fujiwara!"

“我的膝盖都流血了，我的腰快断了，而且我露在外面的那颗奶子快要冻僵了！”小百合穿着破烂不堪、沾满番茄酱的黑丝袜冻得瑟瑟发抖，一边虚弱地擦着地板一边呜咽道，“我是个高级妈妈桑，不是什么凶案现场清理工！要是我在这片番茄酱地板上死于失温，我做鬼也要永远缠着你，藤原！”

"Shut up and keep scrubbing!" Fujiwara sobbed, a manic, sleep-deprived twitch developing in his left eye. "If Rin finds one more red spot, she's going to harvest my kidneys! Why does this fake blood keep spreading?! It's defying the laws of physics!"

“闭嘴，继续刷！”藤原崩溃地啜泣着，左眼因为极度缺觉而产生了一种癫狂的抽搐，“要是让铃再发现一个红点，她绝对会把我的肾割下来卖掉的！为什么这假血越抹越多？！这简直违反了物理定律！”

Suddenly, the heavy backroom door swung open. Miyetsuko stepped out, her face illuminated by the cold, blue glow of her tablet. She paused, adjusting her glasses as she clinically surveyed the red disaster zone.

突然，沉重的后仓大门开了。美悦子走了出来，脸庞被平板电脑冰冷幽蓝的光芒照亮。她停下脚步，推了推眼镜，用冷酷的目光审视着这片红色的重灾区。

"Fascinating," Miyetsuko murmured, tapping her screen. "It appears our little ketchup incident has escaped our immediate control. Wild, bizarre conspiracy theories have begun to multiply rapidly across the island's patriotic internet forums over the last three hours."

“真有趣，”美悦子喃喃自语，轻轻敲击着屏幕，“看来我们的番茄酱小插曲已经超出了我们目前的控制范围。在过去的三个小时里，各种狂野离奇的阴谋论已经开始在全岛的爱国互联网论坛上迅速蔓延。”

Rin and Chika peeked out from behind Miyetsuko. Chika squeaked in terror at the slaughterhouse-like floor, while Rin leaned against the doorframe, looking highly amused.

铃和千夏从美悦子身后探出头来。看着宛如屠宰场般的地板，千夏吓得发出一声尖叫，而铃则靠在门框上，露出了极度愉悦的表情。

"What kind of theories?" Rin asked, tossing a peanut into her mouth.

“什么阴谋论？”铃往嘴里抛了一颗花生米，随口问道。

"The online mob firmly believes that the red stains covering our floor are not spilled condiments, but rather miraculous, bleeding 'Stigmata of the Motherland,'" Miyetsuko explained deadpan. "They claim the floor itself is weeping tears of blood for our persecuted patriotism."

“网上的暴民们坚信，目前覆盖在我们地板上的红色污渍根本不是打翻的调料，而是奇迹般流血的‘祖国之圣痕’，”美悦子面无表情地解释道，“他们声称，是地板本身在为我们遭受迫害的爱国主义精神流下血泪。”

Fujiwara stopped scrubbing. The toothbrush fell from his trembling, ketchup-stained fingers. The exhaustion in his eyes was instantly replaced by the terrifying, blinding light of pure, unadulterated capitalist greed.

藤原停止了刷地。牙刷从他沾满番茄酱、微微颤抖的指尖滑落。他眼中那深深的疲惫瞬间一扫而空，取而代之的是纯粹、毫无杂质的资本主义贪婪所折射出的恐怖强光。

"Wait... so they think the floor tiles are sacred relics?" Fujiwara whispered, slowly standing up, his ketchup-smeared face stretching into a maniacal grin. "Why the hell are we scrubbing?! We shouldn't be cleaning the floor—we should be PRYING IT UP!"

“等等……所以他们认为这些地板砖是神圣的宗教遗物？”藤原喃喃低语着，慢慢站起身来，那张抹满番茄酱的脸扭曲成了一个极其癫狂的笑容，“那我们到底为什么要刷地？！我们根本不该清理地板——我们应该把它给挖出来！”

Sayuri gasped, instantly catching on to the grift. She forgot all about her exposed chest and freezing knees, clapping her hands together in delight. "Oh my god! We could sell the blood-stained tiles individually! 'Authentic Weeping Floorboards of Fresh Mart'—five hundred dollars a piece!"

小百合倒吸一口凉气，瞬间领悟了这场骗局的核心。她立刻把露在外面的胸部和冻僵的膝盖抛到了九霄云外，兴奋地拍起手来：“我的天呐！我们可以把这些沾血的地砖一块一块拆下来卖！‘新鲜玛特官方认证泣血圣砖’——一块五百美金！”

Rin blinked, staring at the two grifters before a slow, wicked smile spread across her face. She reached behind the counter, pulled out a heavy steel crowbar and a sledgehammer, and tossed them directly at Fujiwara's feet.

铃眨了眨眼，盯着这两个死性不改的骗子，随后，一抹邪恶而冰冷的笑容在她的脸上缓缓荡开。她伸手摸向柜台后方，抽出一根沉重的精钢撬棍和一把大铁锤，“哐当”一声直接扔在了藤原的脚边。

"You heard the lady, Mr. Manager," Rin sneered, crossing her arms. "Your toothbrush duty has officially been upgraded to demolition duty. Start smashing. I want fifty sacred tiles boxed and ready for the morning rush. And if you break one, I'm taking it out of your salary."

“你听到大导演的话了，店长大人，”铃双手抱胸，冷笑着说道，“你的刷地任务现已正式升级为拆迁任务。开始砸吧。天亮前我要看到五十块打包好的圣砖准备迎客。要是砸碎了一块，我就从你的工资里扣。”

Fujiwara stared at the heavy sledgehammer, letting out a soul-crushing sob as he realized his brilliant capitalist epiphany had only resulted in even worse physical labor. But with Rin cracking her knuckles menacingly, he had no choice but to pick up the hammer and start violently demolishing his own grocery store.

藤原死死盯着那把沉重的大铁锤，发出一声令人心碎的哀嚎，他终于意识到，自己那绝妙的资本家灵光一闪，结果只是换来了更惨烈的重体力劳动。但看着铃充满杀气地捏响指节，他别无选择，只能哭丧着脸抡起大锤，开始疯狂强拆自己的杂货铺。
