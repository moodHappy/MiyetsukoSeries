Chapter 4: Movie Night

发布时间：2026/8/22 17:17:39

第四章：电影之夜

With the generator humming steadily and the air conditioner finally cooling the shop down to a comfortable temperature, the heavy tension of the island heat completely melted away. Outside, the night sky was clear and full of stars, while inside "Fresh Mart," the atmosphere turned cozy and domestic.

随着发电机平稳地轰鸣，空调终于将店内降到一个舒适的温度，岛上那股沉闷的暑气彻底消散了。屋外夜空晴朗、繁星点点，“新鲜玛特”店内则充满了温馨舒适的居家氛围。

"Alright," Fujiwara said, wiping a bit of strawberry ice cream from his thumb as he tossed a worn-out projector onto the counter. "No spies, no military patrols, and no more false alarms. It’s movie night."

“好啦，”藤原擦掉大拇指上沾的一点草莓冰淇淋，把一台磨损的投影仪扔在柜台上，“没有间谍，没有巡逻艇，也没有虚惊一场。今晚是电影之夜。”

Rin immediately dropped her spoon into her empty bowl and cheered, kicking her legs up on the sofa. "Finally! Something that doesn't involve running up a rocky hill in forty-degree heat. What are we watching? Tell me we have something with explosions, or at least a decent thriller."

铃立刻把勺子扔进空碗里欢呼起来，把双腿搭在沙发上。“太好啦！总算不用顶着四十度高温往石头山上跑了。今晚看什么？快告诉我，有那种带爆炸场面的，或者至少是个像样的惊悚片。”

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, leaning back with her iced tea. "Please, Rin. Explosions are for the unrefined. I vote for a classic 1960s French noir film. Something with intellectual depth, moody cinematography, and complex moral ambiguity."

美悦子推了推眼镜，端着冰茶往后靠了靠。“拜托，铃。爆炸那是没品位的人看的。我投一票给六十年代的经典法国黑色电影。得有点思想深度、忧郁的镜头语言和复杂的道德模糊感。”

Sayuri giggled, gently wrapping a blanket around Fujiwara’s shoulders before sitting beside him. "Why don't we watch a romance? Something sweet and heartwarming, where everyone is happy at the end. We could use a little bit of sweetness tonight, don't you think?"

小百合轻笑着，贴心地给藤原的肩膀披上一条毯子，然后在旁边坐了下来。“咱们为什么不看爱情片呢？温馨甜蜜的那种，结局大家都开开心心的。今晚咱们需要一点甜甜的东西，不是吗？”

小百合温柔的提议立刻得到了藤原的支持。他伸手搂住她的腰，笑着把投影仪的连接线插进旧笔记本电脑里。“听见没？少数服从多数。今晚不搞什么烧脑文艺片，也不搞枪战动作片，就看温馨浪漫的。”

Sayuri’s sweet suggestion instantly won Fujiwara's approval. He wrapped an arm around her waist, smiling as he plugged the projector cable into the old laptop. "Hear that? Majority rules. No brain-melting art films and no shoot-em-ups tonight. We're going with romance."

Rin groaned dramatically, though a soft smile tugged at the corner of her lips. "Fine, fine. But if the male lead doesn't make at least one hilariously bad life choice in the first ten minutes, I'm taking over the playlist."

铃夸张地哀叹了一声，不过嘴角却泛起了一抹温柔的笑意。“行吧行吧。不过要是男主角在前十分钟里没做出至少一个荒谬绝伦的愚蠢决定，我就得强行接管播放列表了。”

As the projector flickered to life, casting a warm, flickering image against the white-washed back wall of the shop, the four of them crowded together on the oversized sofa. Fujiwara sat in the middle, with Miyetsuko curled up on his left, Sayuri nestled snugly against his right shoulder, and Rin resting her feet right across his lap.

随着投影仪亮起，温暖而闪烁的画面投射到店铺雪白的后墙上，四个人挤在宽大的沙发上。藤原坐在正中间，美悦子蜷缩在他左侧，小百合紧紧依偎在他右侧肩膀上，铃则直接把脚搭在了他的腿上。

The screen showed a quiet, rainy European village—a stark, peaceful contrast to the stifling reality of Donglai Island. Outside, the cicadas hummed in the dark, but inside, wrapped in the cool hum of the AC and the soft glow of the screen, the world felt small, safe, and entirely their own.

屏幕上播放着一个安静多雨的欧洲小村庄——与东莱岛令人窒息的现实形成了鲜明、宁静的对比。屋外，蝉鸣在黑暗中低吟；而屋内，伴随着空调清凉的轰鸣和屏幕柔和的光芒，整个世界变得那么渺小、安全，完全属于他们自己。
