Chapter 588: Dragon Field Racing

发布时间：2026/10/17 11:44:17

第五百八十八章：龙场竞技

Deep within the Andromeda Galaxy colony lay the crown jewel of the Fujiwara Empire’s hyper-cluster: the Grand Dragon Field. Spanning millions of square parsecs, it was the largest breeding and training ground for colossal beast-tier entities in the entire known universe. Here lived magnificent, mythical Winged Divine Dragons—creatures straight out of eastern fantasy epics, boasting massive wingspans that stretched across eighty-five thousand li, capable of swallowing solar winds and breathing plasma fire.

在仙女座星系的殖民地深处，坐落着整个藤原帝国所在超星系团中最宏伟的明珠——庞大的龙场。这里绵延数百万平方秒差距，是整个已知宇宙中规模最大、专门培育和训练巨兽级神龙的顶级基地。在这里栖息着壮丽无比、只存在于玄幻小说里的翼行神龙——这些神话巨兽展翅竟达八万五千里，张口能吞噬恒星风，呼吸间喷吐着高能等离子烈焰。

When the imperial family arrived at the colossal launching pad, the sheer atmospheric pressure of the divine beasts made the air vibrate with raw, primordial power.

当皇室一行人浩浩荡荡抵达这座宏伟的起飞平台时，神龙散发出的庞大远古威压让周围的空气都因为纯粹的原始力量而剧烈震颤。

"Look at the size of those scales!" Chika gasped, her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaving as she pointed up at a leviathan whose tail was entirely obscured by planetary clouds. "If I tried to milk one of those things, I’d need a bucket the size of a gas giant and a suicide wish!"

“瞧瞧这些龙鳞的个头！”千夏惊呼着，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔随着她指着头顶那头尾巴隐没在行星云层里的庞然大物剧烈起伏，“我要是敢去给这玩意儿挤奶，不仅得准备一个气态巨行星那么大的桶，还得自带一张去阎王爷那报道的单程票！”

Naturally, the royal attempts to tame the mythical beasts did not go smoothly.

当然啦，皇室成员们亲自驯服这些神话巨兽的尝试，过程绝谈不上顺利。

Sayuri and Miyetsuko took one look at the towering, razor-sharp claws and the swirling vortex of plasma storming around the nearest Divine Dragon, and immediately retreated to the safety of the VIP observation deck.

小百合和美悦子仅仅看了一眼那遮天蔽日的锋利龙爪以及盘绕在神龙周身翻滚的等离子风暴，当机立断，非常有自知之明地双双退回了VIP观景台的安全区域。

"My Lord, I firmly believe that true aristocratic dignity involves knowing when to let the common beasts trample other people," Sayuri purred smoothly, elegantly hiding behind her velvet fan as she watched from afar. Miyetsuko nodded frantically in absolute, breathless agreement, her plush rear pressed tightly against the safety railing.

“夫君，臣妾深以为，真正的贵族尊严就体现在懂得什么时候该让别的野兽去践踏——哦不，是让别人去冒险，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，一边优雅地躲在丝绒折扇后面远远观望。美悦子在一旁疯狂点头表示绝对、毫无保留的赞同，那对丰腴的蜜桃臀死死贴着观景台的防护栏。

Fujiwara, however, refused to be outdone by a giant lizard. Refusing the anti-gravity harnesses, the Emperor boldly clambered up the multi-layered scales of an eight-thousand-li dragon, attempting to establish dominance. Unfortunately, the dragon merely sneezed, releasing a cosmic static discharge that instantly blasted the Emperor right off its neck.

然而，藤原大帝可不觉得自己会输给一只大蜥蜴。这位暴君拒绝使用反重力安全背带，大摇大摆地硬生生顺着八万里神龙层层叠叠的龙鳞往上爬，试图彰显他作为宇宙主宰的绝对统治力。然而不幸的是，神龙仅仅打了个喷嚏，随之释放的一道宇宙静电瞬间把皇帝从龙脖子上给轰飞了出去。

THUD!

啪叽！

Fujiwara performed a magnificent, undignified belly-flop, crashing face-first into the titanium-alloy landing pad with a sickening crunch. He lay there like a squashed frog, (nearly fracturing his immortal spine), before the automated imperial security drones rushed over to catch his bouncing, battered corpse.

藤原当场贡献了一次极其华丽、毫无仪态的“狗吃屎”式俯冲，脸朝下重重砸在钛合金起居降落坪上，发出一声令人牙酸的闷响。他像只被拍扁的青蛙一样趴在地上，险些把自己的不朽脊椎骨彻底砸碎，直到几架全自动的皇室安全保卫龙兽急忙飞过来，伸出温顺的巨爪把这位连滚带爬的皇帝主子给接住。

"Boss! Are you still alive, or should I start filing the paperwork to divide your chocolate factories?!" Chika yelled down from above, holding her sides as she howled with laughter.

“老板！你还活着没有？要不要我现在就开始写文件准备瓜分你的巧克力工厂了啊？！”千夏站在上方叉着腰，笑得眼泪都快出来了。

While the Emperor was busy picking titanium shrapnel out of his teeth, the two true masters of the Grand Dragon Field stepped forward: Chika and Queen Rin.

正当皇帝忙着从牙缝里往外抠钛合金碎渣时，龙场里真正的两位驯兽高手已经双双登场了：千夏与女王小铃。

As the newly crowned Succubus Queen, Rin didn't even need to use force. The moment she approached a terrifying, bloodthirsty star-dragon with an eighty-five-thousand-li wingspan, the beast caught a whiff of her hyper-concentrated demonic allure. Its terrifying vertical pupils dilated instantly. The colossal beast let out a whimpering, puppy-like whine, lowered its titanic head, and gently nuzzled Rin’s tiny shoes.

作为新晋的魅魔女王，小铃甚至连一根手指都不用动。她仅仅是迈着小碎步靠近了一头展翅八万五千里、凶残嗜血的星空恶龙，那头巨兽就瞬间捕捉到了她身上那浓郁到极致的魅魔芬芳。巨兽那可怕的竖瞳瞬间放大，庞大无朋的头颅发出一声宛如小狗般的呜咽，乖乖垂下高傲的头颅，极其温柔地用鼻尖蹭了蹭小铃的小鞋子。

"Um... hello, big dragon-doggy! Be a good boy, okay?" Rin giggled, patting the dragon's massive snout before hopping gracefully onto its back. With a flick of her glowing Ruby Scepter, the obedient dragon unfurled its galactic wings and effortlessly carried the blind little queen straight into the roaring, lightning-filled abyss of the planetary storm canyons, performing breathtaking barrel rolls through the cosmic thunderbolts.

“唔……大龙狗狗乖哦！要听话知不知道呀？”小铃甜甜地娇笑着，拍了拍巨龙巨大无比的鼻尖，随后轻巧地跳上了龙背。随着她轻轻挥舞手中的红宝石权杖，这条温顺听话的巨龙瞬间张开遮天蔽日的银河巨翼，轻而易举地载着这位盲眼小女王一头扎进了雷霆万钧、电闪雷鸣的峡谷风暴深处，在宇宙闪电中花式表演着惊心动魄的超音速侧滚翻。

If Rin’s domination was a matter of divine demonic magic, Chika’s performance was nothing short of an absolute, reality-breaking mystery.

如果说小铃的征服靠的是降维打击般的魅魔神力，那么千夏的表现简直就是一种打破物理定律的玄学奇迹。

Stepping up to a notoriously feral, un-tameable plasma dragon that had rejected every elite beast-master in the galaxy, Chika casually popped a strawberry-flavored milk carton into her mouth. She didn't use a whip, a leash, or a magic spell. She simply walked up, patted its armored snout with her milk-swollen hand, and barked at it like an irritated mother scolding a stray dog.

只见千夏大步走到一头凶名远扬、拒绝了银河系所有顶尖御兽大师的狂暴等离子龙面前，随手往嘴里塞了盒草莓味牛奶。她既没有用皮鞭，也没有用绳套，更没有念咒，而是直接走上前去，用自己那沉甸甸、胀满奶水的巨乳顺手拍了拍龙的厚甲鼻尖，像个训斥野狗的泼辣母亲一样凶巴巴地呵斥道：

"Hey! Stop showing off your scales, you oversized lizard! Kneel down before your primary dairy supplier!" Chika snapped.

“喂！不许给老子在面前显摆你的破鳞片，你个大号蜥蜴！赶紧给你的主要奶水供应商跪下！”千夏叉着腰骂道。

To the utter astonishment of the entire imperial entourage, the colossal beast let out a terrified rumble, instantly dropped its massive forelegs, and groveled in the space-dust. Chika hopped onto its neck as casually as mounting a bicycle, and the legendary plasma dragon took off into the stratosphere with the smooth, trembling obedience of a trained circus pony.

在随行所有帝国随从目瞪口呆的注视下，这头庞然大物竟然发出一声恐惧的呜咽，乖乖前腿一软，趴在星际尘埃里瑟瑟发抖。千夏像骑自行车一样极其随意地跨上了龙脖子，而那头传说中的狂暴等离子龙竟然爆发出前所未有的平稳与顺从，驮着她冲入平流层，飞得比经过专业训练的马戏团小矮马还要听话。

From the VIP observation deck, Sayuri and Miyetsuko watched with dropped jaws, while Fujiwara, still brushing space-dirt off his imperial trousers, stared in sheer, unadulterated awe.

在VIP观景台上，小百合和美悦子惊得下巴掉了一地；而刚刚把身上的宇宙泥土拍干净的藤原，则露出了极度震撼、叹为观止的神情。

"Incredible..." Sayuri murmured, adjusting her fan. "She has zero tactical training, zero magical prowess, and yet she commands the apex predators of the cosmos with the raw, terrifying authority of an angry mother. No wonder her son turned out to be such an interstellar conqueror."

“太不可思议了……”小百合喃喃自语着，整理了一下折扇，“她既没有经过战术特训，也没有高深莫测的魔法，却能凭借一种暴躁母亲般纯粹、恐怖的威严，把宇宙顶级的掠夺者训得服服帖帖。难怪她能生出那种年纪轻轻就打下十个星系的怪物儿子。”

"Indeed," Miyetsuko agreed softly, glancing nervously toward the East Gate in the distant capital. "Speaking of her son... should we perhaps check on how His Highness is progressing with his toilet-cleaning duties at the East Gate? It has been a whole afternoon."

“确实，”美悦子轻声附和着，有些心虚地朝远处首都的方向望了一眼，“说起来……咱们是不是该派人去视察一下皇子殿下在东门扫厕所的进度怎么样了？毕竟已经过去整整一个下午了呢。”

"Let him scrub!" Fujiwara scoffed, pulling Miyetsuko back against his plush lap as he watched Chika effortlessly pilot a plasma dragon across the alien sky. "If he can conquer ten star systems, he can surely conquer a public restroom! Now, ignore the disgraced toilet cleaner, and let us focus on more important matters—such as figuring out how to convince these giant lizards to let us set up a mobile milk-tea stand on their wings!"

“管他去死，让他接着刷去！”藤原冷哼一声，一把将美悦子拉回自己腿上坐好，一边津津有味地看着千夏在异星天空中轻松驾驭着等离子巨龙，“这小子既然能打下十个星系，难道还搞不定一个公共公厕吗？行了，别管那个扫厕所的逆子了，咱们抓紧研究正事——比如怎么才能说服这头八万里巨龙，让它允许我们在它的翅膀上支一个移动奶茶摊！”
