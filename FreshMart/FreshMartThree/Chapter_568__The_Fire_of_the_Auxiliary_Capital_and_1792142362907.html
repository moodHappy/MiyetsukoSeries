Chapter 568: The Fire of the Auxiliary Capital and the Celestial Abduction

发布时间：2026/10/16 17:19:21

第五百六十八章：陪都天火与天宫劫掠

Just as Fujiwara and his harem were eagerly scheming to install a quantum router in the heavenly bedchamber and blackmail the entire celestial pantheon, reality hit them with the terrifying, irresistible force of a higher-dimensional executioner.

正当藤原和他的后宫佳丽们兴致勃勃地盘算着如何在天庭龙床上安装量子路由器、顺便敲诈整个神界的时候现实却像高维度的处决官一样，带着令人绝望的绝对力量轰然降临。

BOOM!

轰隆——！

A terrifying, blinding column of azure-white divine fire smashed down from the stratosphere, instantly vaporizing the grand imperial auxiliary capital outside the palace walls. Through the floor-to-ceiling smart windows, the entire household watched in sheer, paralyzed horror as millions of tons of concrete, steel, and high-tech defense installations were reduced to glowing cosmic dust in less than three seconds.

一道恐怖刺目的青白色神火柱从平流层轰然砸下，瞬间将皇宫城墙外的宏伟陪都化为了虚无。透过落地智能玻璃窗，整个后宫佳丽与皇帝呆若木鸡地看着几百万吨的混凝土、钢铁和高科技防御设施在不到三秒钟内被烧成发光的宇宙微尘。

Worse still, the sky above the courtyard didn't just clear up—it projected a colossal, high-definition holographic screen that spanned the entire horizon. And what was broadcasting live on that screen? Why, the living room they were currently standing in! Every single smug expression, every lewd joke, and every detail of their treasonous "master plan" to subvert the Heavenly Palace was being streamed in 8K resolution straight to the gods above.

更糟糕的是，庭院上空的云层不仅没有散去，反而投影出了一块横跨整个地平线的巨型高清全息屏幕。而这块屏幕上正在现场直播的是什么？不正是他们刚才所在的这座客厅吗！他们每一个洋洋得意的表情、每一句下流的玩笑、以及企图谋反颠覆天宫的“宏伟计划”，全都被8K超高清画质原封不动地实时直播给了天上的神仙们。

"T-They saw everything?!" Chika shrieked, dropping her jaw so low it practically hit the Persian rug. Her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaved in blind panic. "Our entire conspiracy was broadcast live across the entire galaxy?! We just plotted treason on public television!"

“他……他们全都看见了？！”千夏尖叫着，下巴惊得差点掉到波斯地毯上，由于极度的恐慌，她那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔剧烈起伏着，“咱们的全部阴谋都被全银河系现场直播了？！我们在公开电视频道里公然策划谋反叛国啊！”

Sayuri’s velvet composure finally snapped completely. The Wise Empress stared up at the giant screen, her plush, Tang-dynasty curves trembling as she dropped her fan. "Higher-dimensional quantum surveillance... They didn't just bypass our shields; they weaponized our own security cameras against us. We are completely, transparently naked before them."

小百合那丝绒般的从容终于彻底崩塌了。这位贤后抬头死死盯着巨屏，她那丰腴的盛唐曲线气得微微发抖，手中的折扇“啪嗒”一声掉在地上。“更高维度的量子监控……他们不仅绕过了我们的护罩，还把我们自己的安全监控系统反向变成了他们的直播镜头。我们在他们面前，真的是彻底一丝不挂的小透明了。”

Panic swept through the grand salon like a tidal wave. Blind Rin, realizing the absolute terror of the situation, instantly let out an angry, demonic shriek. The little succubus raised her glowing Ruby Scepter toward the sky, her milky eyes flashing with chaotic underworld malice.

恐慌如海啸般席卷了大起居殿。盲人小铃意识到局势已经陷入绝境，瞬间发出了一声愤怒的恶魔尖叫。这只小魅魔将手中发光的红宝石权杖指向天空，空灵的盲眼里闪烁着混乱的地下世界恶意。

"Those mean old gods think they can bully Master?!" Rin yelled furiously. "Rin is going to open a dimensional rift right now! Rin is calling the entire Succubus Legion! We are going to march into the Heavenly Palace, tear down their gates, and make them kowtow to the true masters of the underworld!"

“这帮讨人嫌的老神仙居然敢欺负主人？！”小铃气呼呼地大喊道，“小铃现在就要撕开次元裂缝！小铃要把整个魅魔军团全调集过来！我们要杀上天宫去，砸烂他们的南天门，让他们给地狱真正的统治者下跪磕头！”

Before Rin could finish her chant and summon a billion horny, bloodthirsty demons into the living room, a pair of trembling, desperate hands shot out from beneath the mahogany coffee table.

还没等小铃念完咒语、把数以亿计发情且嗜血的魅魔大军召唤到客厅里，一双手从红木茶几底下惊恐万状地伸了出来。

Swish!

唰！

Fujiwara, the absolute ruler of the global empire, had completely abandoned his dignity. He was currently hiding on all fours underneath the heavy wooden table, frantically grabbing the hem of Rin’s dress to physically stop her. His face was entirely drained of color, his voice trembling with an uncharacteristic, bone-deep terror.

藤原这位全球帝国的绝对统治者，此刻已经把所有的尊严扔得干干净净。他正四肢着地、狼狈不堪地躲在厚重的红木茶几底下，拼命拽着小铃的裙角强行制止她。他的脸上血色全无，声音里带着一种从未有过的、深入骨髓的极度恐惧。

"Stop! For the love of the cosmos, stop right there, you little demon!" Fujiwara cried out in a hoarse, terrified whisper, his body shivering uncontrollably. "Are you out of your mind?! Did you not read the divine decree?! They are watching us right this second! If you summon the succubus army, those higher-dimensional deities will erase us from the space-time continuum before our troops even cross the threshold! We are powerless! Do you hear me? We are utterly at their mercy!"

“住手！看在宇宙的份上，快给老子住手，你个小祖宗！”藤原用沙哑而惊恐的低语哀求着，身体不受控制地剧烈颤抖着，“你疯了吗？！没看到刚才神仙降下的圣旨吗？！他们现在这一秒钟就在看着我们呢！你要是把魅魔军团召唤出来，那些更高维度的神仙在我们军队踏出门槛之前就能把我们在时空层面上直接抹除！我们毫无胜算！听见了吗？我们在神明面前根本毫无还手之力！”

While the Emperor cowered beneath the furniture in total submission, a golden beam of heavenly light descended straight through the ceiling, bypassing the physical roof entirely. It locked onto Miyetsuko with inescapable divine precision.

正当皇帝在家具底下吓得瑟瑟发抖、彻底屈服时，一道金色的天界神光直接穿透了穹顶，完全无视物理屋顶地径直落下。它带着无法逃脱的神圣精准度，瞬间锁定了美悦子。

"Ah...!" Miyetsuko gasped softly. Before she could even utter a single syllable of farewell, her exquisite, aristocratic clothes dissolved into glittering particles of light. Stripped completely bare, the trembling noblewoman was gently lifted into the air by the divine beam, wrapped in a shimmering cloud of ethereal pink mist.

“啊……！”美悦子轻呼一声。还没等她说出一个音节的诀别之语，她那精致贵气的衣服瞬间溶解成了闪烁的光子微粒。这位浑身一丝不挂、瑟瑟发抖的贵族女子被神光轻柔地托举到半空中，包裹在一团流光溢彩的粉色缥缈仙雾之中。

"Master... save me...!" Miyetsuko wept softly, her porcelain-smooth skin flushing with absolute, soul-crushing humiliation as the divine beam began to pull her upward through the open dome toward the stratosphere.

“主人……救救我……！”美悦子泪眼婆娑地轻声哀求着，当神光开始将她顺着敞开的穹顶向上拉向平流层时，她那白瓷般光滑的肌肤因为极度绝望的羞耻而泛起一层诱人的红晕。

From beneath the coffee table, Fujiwara looked up with a pale, miserable expression. He didn't dare utter a single word of defiance, nor could he think of any absurd jokes. The terrifying reality of divine retribution had finally broken the tyrant's spirit. He could only watch in silence, his fists clenched in helpless despair, as his beloved consort was forcefully abducted by the heavens.

茶几底下的藤原脸色惨白、神情凄惨地抬起头。他再也不敢吐出半句大逆不道之言，也编不出任何荒诞的玩笑。神罚那令人窒息的恐怖现实，终于彻底击碎了这位暴君的傲慢。他只能默默地看着，双拳紧握满是无能为力的绝望，眼睁睁地看着自己心爱的妃子被天界强行劫走。

With a final flash of blinding light, Miyetsuko vanished into the heavens, whisked away to the Heavenly Emperor's bedchamber, leaving the terrified Emperor and his harem huddled in the living room, completely crushed and humbled by the absolute, inescapable power of the higher-dimensional gods.

伴随着最后一道刺眼的强光闪过，美悦子彻底消失在了九霄云外，被强行带到了天帝的寝宫之中。只留下吓破了胆的皇帝和他的后宫佳丽们缩在客厅里，被更高维度神明那绝对的、无法抗拒的伟力彻底折服，陷入了死一般的死寂与敬畏之中。
