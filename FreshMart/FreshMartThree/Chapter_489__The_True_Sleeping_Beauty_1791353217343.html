Chapter 489: The True Sleeping Beauty

发布时间：2026/10/7 14:06:54

第四百八十九章：真·睡美人

After the hysterical laughter died down, the bed was a warm, tangled mess of rumpled silk and lingering relief. Fujiwara sat propped up against the carved headboard, sipping a glass of brandy to soothe his still-trembling nerves. The four women lounged around him like contented kittens, their cheeks rosy from the sheer exertion of the prank.

在震天响的狂笑声终于渐渐平息之后，巨大的床榻上只剩下一片温热凌乱的真丝被褥，以及劫后余生般的浓郁温馨。藤原靠在高耸雕花的床头，小口抿着白兰地，平复着那颗险些被吓停滞的小心脏。四个女人宛如温顺餍足的幼猫般蜷缩在他身侧，两腮因刚才那场恶作剧的狂笑而泛着健康的潮红。

Playful Chika, who was lazily half-asleep while cradling her heavy, soft chest, suddenly poked Fujiwara's ribs with a slender toe. An impish, mischievous grin spread across her sweet face.

正懒洋洋半眯着眼睛、两手习惯性托着胸前软糯雪峰的俏皮千夏，忽然伸出白嫩的脚趾，轻轻戳了戳藤原的侧肋。那张娇憨甜美的脸蛋上，倏地浮现出一抹狡黠坏透的笑意。

"Hey, Master..." Chika asked playfully, tilting her head with boundless curiosity, "now that the scare is over, answer us honestly. Earlier today, when we were completely limp and unresponsive in that 'feign death' state... who actually gave you the absolute best time? Who felt the most heavenly to play with?"

“喂，主人……”千夏歪着小脑袋，满眼都是按捺不住的八卦与好奇，软糯糯地打趣道，“既然刚才那场惊吓已经翻篇了，那您老实招来呗。今天下午我们全都软绵绵、毫无反应地躺在床上当‘假死尸体’的时候……到底是谁让您玩得最爽呀？哪一个的感觉最销魂？”

The bedroom went quiet. Sayuri arched an amused brow, while Miyetsuko and Rin both turned their eyes toward the Emperor, eager to hear his critique of their motionless state.

整间卧室顿时安静了下来。小百合挑了挑那双风情万种的柳叶眉，而美悦子和小铃也纷纷把目光投向这位帝王，满怀期待地等待着他对那段“静止玩偶体验”的权威评测。

Fujiwara paused, setting his glass on the nightstand. His brow furrowed as he wrestled with the lingering trauma of his recent crying fit, forcing his brain to rewind past the panic and sift through the raw, physical data of the afternoon's feast.

藤原动作一顿，将酒杯轻轻搁在床头柜上。他微微蹙起眉头，硬生生从刚才哭天抹泪的心有余悸中挣脱出来，强迫大脑倒带，重新翻阅起下午那场荒唐盛宴里最原始、最纯粹的触觉数据。

Under normal circumstances, Sayuri was the undisputed champion—her vocal flair, veteran hip rhythms, and devoted eye contact were peerless. But in a vegetative state? Without her mind active, Sayuri had simply been soft and still. Chika, overflowing with sweet milk, had felt like sinking into a tub of warm custard—wonderfully decadent, but lacking any physical pushback. Rin had been eerily quiet, like an ornate, glowing antique doll.

在平日里，小百合向来是毫无悬念的卫冕冠军——她那勾魂夺魄的喘息、老练至极的起伏配合，以及深情注视的眼波，在世间无人能及。但若是在毫无知觉的植物人状态下呢？失去了灵魂的调度，小百合不过是一具温软寂静的皮囊；而奶水充沛的千夏，陷进去就像落入了一大桶温热的布丁——虽说极尽香甜，却毫无张力可言；至于小铃，则透着一股荧光古董人偶般的森然诡异。

Finally, Fujiwara’s eyes settled on one figure, and his hand pointed straight at her.

良久，藤原的目光锁定了床角的一道身影，食指笔直地伸了过去。

"Miyetsuko," Fujiwara announced with supreme philosophical gravity. "Without a shadow of a doubt, Miyetsuko felt the most incredible."

“美悦子，”藤原带着无上的哲学严肃感大声宣布道，“毫无疑问，在毫无知觉的状态下，美悦子给老子的感觉是最极品、最销魂的。”

The entire bed gasped. It was completely, utterly unexpected.

床上的女人们齐刷刷倒吸了一口凉气。这个结果简直大大出乎了所有人的意料。

Miyetsuko’s face turned an immediate, apocalyptic shade of crimson. The softened former warrior clutched her duvet to her chin, her eyes darting frantically in bashful shock. "M-Me?! How is that possible?! I didn't say a word! I didn't even move! Sayuri-nee is the Queen of the bed!"

美悦子的俏脸瞬间烧成了一片灾难级的血红。这位早已软化成温顺小猫的昔日战神，一把将薄被死死拉到下巴处，又惊又羞地瞪圆了眸子：“我、我？！这怎么可能啊？！我当时连一句话都没说！身子更是一动没动！明明小百合姐才是名副其实的床笫之王啊！”

"That is precisely why the finding is so profound!" Fujiwara declared, gesturing like a scientist presenting a Nobel-winning thesis. "It proves the fundamental divide between sexual 'software' and raw biological 'hardware'!"

“这恰恰证明了这个结论有多么震撼！”藤原手舞足蹈，宛如一位在诺贝尔讲台上发表跨时代论文的狂热科学家，“它彻底揭示了闺房之乐中，‘软件生态’与‘纯物理硬件’之间的根本分水岭！”

He pointed at Sayuri. "My sweet Sayuri is ninety-nine percent top-tier software. Her greatness relies on interaction, vocal performance, and emotional submission. When her brain is turned off, the software crashes! But Miyetsuko? Miyetsuko is pure, unmatched, industrial-grade hardware!"

他先是指了指小百合：“我家甜美的小百合，百分之九十九靠的是顶级神级软件。她的极乐体验全建构在互动、娇吟、眼神交流与心理顺从之上。可一旦大脑宕机，软件直接闪退！但美悦子呢？美悦子那浑身上下，全是不折不扣、工业级的顶级硬件！”

Fujiwara leaned forward, grinning with absolute dark mischief. "Years of elite tactical drills, combat endurance, and pelvic floor conditioning have built an involuntary, biological vice into her core. Even when her brain was clinically dead, her nervous system still maintained a terrifying, crushing baseline grip! In fact... her conscious mind was the only thing holding her back! Her shyness, her little hesitations—they were all bottlenecks! The moment her consciousness died, she became a pure, flawless hydraulic press!"

藤原往前凑了凑身子，脸上洋溢着毫无掩饰的纯粹恶趣味：“多年的特种作战拉练、高强度的体能锤炼，以及核心力量的生理强化，早已在她的身体深处铸就了一座本能级的生物液压钳。哪怕大脑在医学层面上彻底睡死了过去，她的神经末梢依旧保持着令人头皮发麻的本能死绞！说白了……她平日里的主观意识，反倒是限制发挥的最大瓶颈！她的害羞、她那点微弱的迟疑，全成了减速带！只要她的大脑一断电，她整个人瞬间就化身成了一台完美无瑕的高性能液压机！”

The living room exploded into merciless, uproarious mockery.

整间卧房瞬间爆发出一阵毫不留情的放肆调笑声。

Chika clapped her hands together, rolling on the pillows and shrieking with laughter. "Hahaha! Oh my god, Miyetsuko-nee! Your biggest operational flaw in bed was having a brain! The Boss literally thinks you're best when you're legally dead!"

千夏拍着巴掌在枕头上打滚，笑得尖叫连连：“哈哈哈哈！我的天哪美悦子姐！你在床上最大的技术短板居然是长了脑子！在老板眼里，你居然只有在法律意义上断气的时候才是最极品的！”

Sayuri wiped a tear of pure amusement from her eye, shaking her head with mock professional sorrow. "To think I spent a decade in the entertainment industry perfecting vocal tones, breathing control, and psychological seduction... only to be thoroughly defeated by a coma patient's muscle spasms! Truly, biology is a cruel mistress, little sister."

小百合擦去眼角笑出来的泪花，假模假样地叹了口气，满脸都是职业生涯遭受重创的戏谑惋惜：“亏得姐姐当年在演艺圈苦心钻研了十年的发声技巧、呼吸控制和心理诱惑……到头来，居然输给了一个昏迷病患的肌肉痉挛！造化弄人啊好妹妹，肉体本能果然才是最残酷的降维打击。”

From the edge of the mattress, blind Rin tilted her head, clapping her small hands with horrifying, angelic sweetness.

在床沿边，盲人小铃歪了歪头，拍着一双小手，语气里满是令人啼笑皆非的纯洁与诡异。

"Um... hello, Miyetsuko-nee?" Rin whispered warmly. "This is so inspiring! You really are the true Sleeping Beauty! Other princesses need a true love's kiss to wake up, but Miyetsuko-nee only reaches her maximum battle power when she's asleep in a coffin! Next time Master is stressed, we don't even need to bother you—we can just knock you out with a little mallet and leave you in his room!"

“唔……美悦子姐姐听我说好吗？”小铃温温柔柔地呢喃着，“这实在太励志啦！你才是那个名副其实的真·睡美人呢！别的公主都需要真爱之吻才能醒过来，可美悦子姐姐偏偏只有躺在棺材里睡大觉的时候，才能爆发出最强的战斗力！下次主人工作压力大的时候，咱们甚至都不用劳烦姐姐费神——直接拿个小木槌把你敲晕，然后扔进主人房里就行啦！”

"Stop it! All of you, shut up!" Miyetsuko wailed in mortification, pulling the silk blanket entirely over her head. Her muffled, frantic whines echoed from beneath the covers as her legs kicked helplessly. "I am not a hydraulic press! I am not a dead piece of hardware! Fujiwara, you absolute pervert, don't you dare drug me again!"

“别说了！你们全都给老子闭嘴啊！”美悦子羞愤欲绝地哀嚎一声，一把将真丝薄被蒙过了头顶。她那闷闷的、抓狂的抗议在被子里嗡嗡作响，两截白皙的小腿在地毯边绝望地乱蹬，“老娘不是什么液压机！老娘更不是毫无知觉的死硬件！藤原你这个天杀的大变态，以后你要是再敢给我下药试试看！”

Fujiwara roared with laughter, prying the blanket back and scooping his thoroughly humiliated, tomato-red "Sleeping Beauty" into his arms. The afternoon terror was entirely forgotten, dissolved into the sweet, depraved warmth of an empire where even a medical prank became an excuse for shameless domestic bliss.

藤原畅怀大笑，一把扯开被子，将那位羞得满脸滴血、无地自容的“真·睡美人”一把搂进怀里狠命揉搓。下午那场令人魂飞魄散的惊吓早已烟消云散，全数化作了这个荒诞帝国里，最甜腻、最不知羞耻的欢声笑语。
