Chika, whose corporate instincts were always hungry for more data, wasn't about to let the conversation end with just the top-tier rankings. She spun her tablet around, her eyes gleaming with wicked, statistical curiosity.

千夏那充满企业直觉的雷达总是对数据贪得无厌，她显然不打算让这场对话仅仅停留在顶级排行榜上。她转了转手里的平板电脑，双眼里闪烁着恶趣味与统计学的好奇光芒。

"Wait a minute, Boss!" Chika giggled wildly, tapping a new column on her spreadsheet. "If there's a glorious Top Ten, then there must be a bottom of the barrel. Come on, spill it! Who makes up the absolute worst performance tier list? Which countries should we actively avoid in our global acquisitions?"

“等一下，老板！”千夏疯狂地咯咯娇笑，在电子表格上新建了一列，“既然有光芒万丈的前十名，那就肯定有垫底的差生。快说快说！到底谁组成了绝对最差表现榜单？咱们在全球收购时，应该主动避开哪些国家的女人？”

美悦子发出一声冰冷尖锐的嗤笑，身子向前倾靠在沙发上。“哦，这绝对精彩。快告诉我们，究竟是谁能让地球上最容易被取悦的万亿首富都感到大失所望。”

Fujiwara sighed dramatically, swirling the vintage wine in his glass. Though such assessments were highly subjective and depended entirely on his own decadent perspective, in his empire, his subjective opinion was absolute law.

藤原戏剧化地叹了口气，摇晃着高脚杯里的珍藏红酒。尽管这种评价高度主观，且完全取决于他那堕落的个人视角，但在他的帝国里，他主观的看法就是绝对的法律。

"If you insist on bringing up traumatic memories," Fujiwara groaned playfully, shaking his head. "Here are the bottom three worst performers I've ever had the misfortune of entertaining."

“既然你们非要勾起我这些心理创伤般的回忆，”藤原半开玩笑地抱怨着，摇了摇头，“那我就说说我曾不幸临幸过的、表现最差的垫底前三名吧。”

Chika shrieked with laughter, typing furiously. "Optimal friction coefficient... logged! Who's next?"

千夏笑得尖叫起来，手指在键盘上飞舞。“最优摩擦系数……记录在案！下一个是谁？”

"Number Two: The Canadians," Fujiwara continued, shivering slightly. "They are entirely too polite. You slap their ass, pull their hair, and they instantly apologize. 'Oh, sorry! Sayuri hid her face against Fujiwara's chest, her shoulders shaking as she tried to suppress a highly unladylike fit of giggles.

小百合把脸埋在藤原的胸膛里，肩膀一抽一抽的，拼命想要憋住一阵极不淑女的狂笑。

"And the absolute worst? Number One on the Do-Not-Touch list?" Fujiwara scoffed. "The Australians. Nothing—and I mean nothing—kills a trillionaire's raging libido faster than being mid-stroke and having a naked woman yell, 'Bloody hell, mate, that's a massive cock!' It feels like I'm fucking a kangaroo out in the Outback. Zero elegance!"

“那绝对垫底的最差一名呢？黑名单上的榜首是谁？”藤原冷哼了一声，“澳大利亚女人。没有任何事情——我是说绝对没有任何事情——能比在你冲刺到一半时，底下一个光着身子的女人突然大喊一句‘我的老天爷啊哥们，你这根鸡巴真他妈巨大！’更能瞬间抹杀万亿首富的狂暴性欲了。感觉就像是在内陆荒野里操一只袋鼠。毫无优雅可言！”

The penthouse erupted into absolute chaos. Miyetsuko slammed her hand on the table, laughing so hard she almost dropped her baseball bat, while Chika fell backward in her chair, gasping for air as she finalized the 'Worst Performers' spreadsheet.

顶层公寓瞬间陷入了彻底的混乱。美悦子一巴掌拍在桌子上，笑得差点连手里的棒球棍都拿不住了；而千夏则仰倒在椅子上，一边大口喘气一边完成了那份“最差表现者”的电子表格。

In the corner of the sofa, however, blind Rin had stopped knitting. She tilted her head, her wide, unseeing eyes blinking innocently, taking the entire conversation with absolute, terrifying seriousness.

然而在沙发的角落里，盲人铃却停下了手里的毛线活。她歪着头，那双睁得圆溜溜却空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，把这番对话当成了绝对严肃、甚至有些可怕的工作指令。

Rin gently placed her knitting needles down and pulled out her tactile corporate phone.

铃轻柔地放下手里的织针，掏出了她的盲文触觉办公手机。

"Don't worry, Master Fujiwara," Rin smiled serenely, radiating a terrifyingly wholesome brand of absolute ruthlessness. "I will issue the severance packages immediately. The room fell silent for a moment before Fujiwara burst into another booming laugh, leaning over to kiss Rin's forehead. "Now that is what I call proactive management! Deport them all, my sweet Rin! Leave the bedroom to Sayuri!"

房间里安静了一瞬，随后藤原再次爆发出一阵响彻云霄的大笑，倾身在铃的额头上亲了一口。“这才叫积极主动的管理！把她们全遣散了吧，我可爱的铃！把卧室留给小百合就足够了！”
