Chapter 492: Top-Tier Dairy Maintenance

发布时间：2026/10/7 15:34:20

第四百九十二章：顶级奶源保养工程

One morning, after another demanding session at the family’s breakfast bar, Chika stood before the floor-to-ceiling mirror in the dressing room. She wore only an unbuttoned silk dressing gown, her soft hands carefully cupping and lifting her heavy, swollen chest. Looking at the sheer volume of her daily yield, she let out a long, distressed sigh and marched straight into the lounge with a leather-bound requisition folder.

某个清晨，在刚结束了又一轮高强度的全家早间营养配给后，千夏正独自站在衣帽间的落地大镜子前。她身上只松松垮垮地披着一件敞怀的真丝睡袍，两只白嫩的小手正小心翼翼地托起自己胸前沉甸甸、饱胀发热的雪峰左右端详。端详着那惊人的沉重体量，她忍不住发出一声发愁的长叹，随即气鼓鼓地抱起一份皮质请款夹，径直踏进了起居室。

"Master, we have a major domestic crisis!" Chika whimpered, dropping the folder straight onto Fujiwara's lap and leaning over him with an exaggerated, trembling pout. "I need an open-ended, unlimited budget for daily professional breast care and luxury aesthetic firming! If I keep serving as the household's breakfast dispensary every single day without proper maintenance, gravity is going to win! I’m still in the blooming flower of my youth—if you all keep sucking on me like this, they’re going to sag all the way down to my knees!"

“主人，咱们家现在面临着极其严重的重大危机！”千夏娇滴滴地哼唧着，一把将文件夹拍在藤原腿上，整个人委屈巴巴地凑上前，瘪着小嘴大声抗议：“老娘申请开通一笔上不封顶的专属预算，用于每日特级美胸抗衰与紧致护理！如果每天早晨雷打不动地给全家当早点摊、却不给任何专业维护的话，地心引力迟早要战胜老娘的胶原蛋白！人家可还是如花似玉的黄金年龄啊——照你们这么天天猛喝猛嘬下去，迟早要早早地下垂到肚脐眼上去啦！”

Under ordinary circumstances, any normal woman would have ceased production months ago. The child was already well over a year old, having long been quietly placed in the care of top-tier private aristocratic guardians abroad, completely detached from the Fresh Mart household without ever knowing its mother. Biologically, the weaning period had passed ages ago. Yet, the milk supply remained ceaseless purely due to the relentless, daily morning demand from Fujiwara and his ladies.

按常理来说，换作普通人家的女子，早在数月前就该彻底断奶了。孩子如今已经一岁有余，早就被悄悄送往海外由最顶级的贵族寄宿家庭悉心照料，一生衣食无忧，却从未与千夏见过一面，彻底隔绝于“新鲜玛特”的后宫体系之外。在生理层面上，正常的哺乳期早就该结束了；而千夏胸前那滔滔不绝的泉涌，全仰赖于藤原和这帮绝色佳人们每天清晨雷打不动的强力吮吸与物理刺激。

Hearing the subtle implication that her duty might ever be downsized, Fujiwara instantly sat bolt upright, shaking his head with fierce, unyielding resolve.

一听到千夏话里隐隐透着想要减少排量甚至断工的苗头，藤原瞬间把脑袋摇得像拨浪鼓，满脸都是坚决不容商量的霸道神色。

"Stop the feeding? Absolutely impossible! The imperial household vetoes any notion of weaning!" Fujiwara declared, pulling Chika onto his knees and giving her soft waist a proprietary squeeze. "We’ve all gotten completely addicted to having your sweet nectar every morning! Sayuri loves it, Miyetsuko thrives on it, and Rin can't live without it! If we let your supply dry up, how are we supposed to get it back? We’d have to go through the grueling nine-month process of getting you pregnant and having another baby all over again! That’s far too long, far too exhausting, and far too miserable for my sweet little cow!"

“停掉吸奶？绝对不行！最高帝王决策层一票否决任何断奶企图！”藤原大声宣布，一把将娇滴滴的千夏抱到自己腿上坐好，大掌在她软软的细腰上霸道地揉了一把，“全家上下早就习惯了每天早上拿你这口鲜甜开胃！小百合爱喝，美悦子指着它当特训燃料，小铃更是离了它都睡不着觉！万一真让这泉眼彻底干涸了，以后上哪儿再补回来？难不成还得让你再怀胎九月、吃尽苦头再生一次？那周期太漫长、过程太痛苦了，老子可舍不得让我软糯的小奶牛再遭那份罪！”

Fujiwara flipped open the folder and stamped the approval line with his golden imperial signet without even glancing at the numbers.

藤原连文件里的具体数字看都没看一眼，直接掏出烫金印章，在请款栏上啪地盖了个大大的准字。

"Budget approved! The highest echelon in the world!" Fujiwara grinned shamelessly, resting his chin on her exposed, fragrant shoulder. "I’ll fly in the leading aesthetic dermatologists from Zurich and Paris! Quantum radiofrequency lifting, zero-gravity peptide serums, botanical collagen wraps—whatever it takes! I guarantee your twin peaks will never sag a millimeter. In fact, under our top-tier maintenance program, they will remain perkier, fuller, and far more breathtaking than any non-lactating chest in this house!"

“预算全票通过！直接拉满全球最高规格！”藤原厚颜无耻地咧嘴坏笑，下巴惬意地搭在她露出的白腻香肩上，“老子立刻派专机把苏黎世和巴黎最顶级的医美团队全接过来！什么量子射频提拉、零重力多肽紧致精华、植物胶原深层水疗，要什么给什么！老子拿帝王信誉向你担保，这对雪峰绝对连一毫米都掉不下去！在顶级保养下，它们只会比屋里这几位不产奶的佳丽更加丰腴饱满、高耸挺拔！”

Sayuri glided over in her silk lounge dress, holding a cup of herbal tea, her eyes shining with genuine, affectionate support.

小百合身着一袭素雅的真丝家居裙款款走来，手里端着温热的花草茶，眼底流淌着无比真挚而宠溺的赞许。

"Master is entirely right, and the rest of us support this completely," Sayuri purred softly, gently smoothing down Chika’s disheveled blonde pigtails. "Our sweet little sister makes the greatest daily sacrifice for this family’s mornings. Without her generous yield, Master would be waking up cranky and aggressive. She deserves the absolute finest care on Earth. And don't you worry about the color either, sweetie—with their advanced micro-circulation brightening creams, they won't turn dark or worn out from daily sipping. They'll stay pale pink and fresh like blooming cherry blossom petals forever."

“主人说得一点都没错，姐姐们举双手赞成，”小百合软糯地轻笑着，动作温柔地替千夏理顺了凌乱的金发双马尾，“千夏妹妹为了全家人的清晨做出了最大的奉献。要是没有她这口源源不断的甘泉滋润，咱们主人每天起床指不定要怎么凶神恶煞呢。妹妹理应享受这世上最奢华的礼遇。而且好妹妹你大可把心放宽——有顶级的美白微循环护理伺候着，每天被喝也绝对不会变深变难看，保证永远粉嫩剔透得像刚绽放的初樱花瓣一样。”

Miyetsuko, curled lazily against the rug in a fuzzy oversized sweater, looked up with soft, pliant agreement, her combat instincts completely buried beneath domestic sweetness.

美悦子套着一件宽大的毛茸茸毛衣，整个人懒洋洋地蜷在地毯上，昔日的锐利杀气早就被驯化成了一汪温软春水，跟着连连点头。

"Sayuri-nee is right," Miyetsuko murmured, her voice sweet and gentle. "Proper maintenance is essential for high-stress assets. Since I gave up my heavy tactical training, even I have to do stretching and skin routines to stay soft for Master. Chika is working hard on the frontline every dawn. Keeping her plump and beautiful is a matter of household honor."

“小百合姐说得很在理，”美悦子细声细语地轻声道，“高负荷运转的宝贵资产，本来就该配上最高规格的战后养护。自从人家彻底放弃了那些粗笨的战术拉练，平时也得靠精油软化皮肤来维持服侍主人的娇嫩呢。千夏每天破晓都在前线辛苦劳作，把她养得白白胖胖、水灵挺拔，可是关系到咱们全家的体面呢。”

From the terrace cushions, blind Rin clapped her delicate hands together, smiling with pure, unnerving succubus logic.

露台软垫上的盲人小铃开心地合起一双小手，扬起精致的小脸，语调里透着魅魔独有的荒诞与纯澈。

"Um... hello, Chika-nee?" Rin piped up sweetly, tilting her head. "In succubus folklore, the sacred dairy fountain must always be kept in an immaculate, pearlescent chalice! If your chalice stays soft, perky, and sweet-scented forever, Master will never get tired of visiting the temple! When the beauty team arrives, can Rin help apply the gold-leaf massage balm? My hands are very good at following the shape without opening my eyes!"

“唔……千夏姐听得到吗？”小铃歪着头甜甜地开了口，“在魅魔的古老传说里，神圣的乳汁泉眼可必须时刻盛放在毫无瑕疵的珠光圣杯里呢！只要姐姐的圣杯永远又挺又软、香气扑鼻，主人就永远都不会厌倦来神殿朝圣啦！等顶级护理团队到了，小铃能不能也去帮忙涂抹金箔按摩膏呀？小铃的手哪怕闭着眼睛，也能把形状摸得准准的哦！”

Chika blushed furiously at the overwhelming chorus of domestic pampering, but a delighted, smug smile was already creeping onto her lips. She snuggled deeper into Fujiwara’s chest, lazily wrapping her arms around his neck.

千夏在这阵铺天盖地的宠溺与调笑声中羞得满面绯红，但唇角却早已忍不住得意洋洋地翘了起来。她顺从地往藤原宽厚的怀里使劲蹭了蹭，双臂慵懒地攀上他的脖颈。

"Hmph... well, as long as the Emperor is paying the bills and guaranteeing my beauty, I suppose the little milk cow can keep her morning shifts," Chika whispered playfully, resting her chin on his chest. "Just remember, Boss—after my gold-leaf lifting treatment tonight, you're only allowed to look with your eyes until the serum fully absorbs!"

“哼……既然帝王亲自掏腰包给报销、还负责兜底美貌，那本小奶牛勉为其难就继续接下这个清晨早班吧，”千夏娇嗔地轻喃着，下巴轻轻点在他结实的胸肌上，“不过老板你可记好咯——今晚涂完了特制金箔紧致霜之后，在精华彻底吸收前，你只准用眼睛看，绝对不许偷嘴哦！”
