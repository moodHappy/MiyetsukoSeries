Chapter 254: The 20 Million Miracle

发布时间：2026/8/31 02:09:43

第二百四十五章：阴谋与两千万的奇迹

Before the laughter in the living room could even fully die down, Miyetsuko let out a piercing, blood-curdling shriek that nearly shattered the windows.

还没等起居室里的笑声完全落下，美悦子突然爆发出了一声刺耳无比、几乎能把窗户震碎的尖叫。

"Wait... WHAT?!" Miyetsuko bolted upright from the sofa, staring at her phone screen with eyes wide enough to pop out of her skull. "My account! A transaction just went through! My hard-earned, state-approved, blood-sweat-and-tears twelve yuan and eighty cents... has completely vanished!"

“等等——什么鬼？！”美悦子猛地从沙发上弹了起来，死死盯着手机屏幕，眼珠子都快瞪出来了，“我的账户！刚、刚才有一笔资金变动！我辛辛苦苦挣来的、国家盖戳认证的那十二块八毛钱……居然不翼而飞了！”

She frantically refreshed the app, her face twisting in pure, unadulterated outrage as she stared at the zero-balance notification.

她气急败坏地疯狂刷新着账单，看着那清零的余额，整个人气得浑身发抖：

"This is no ordinary data leak!" Miyetsuko slammed her phone onto the coffee table, her voice shaking with lethal fury. "This is a targeted, malicious insult! First, they insulted my three days of grueling physical labor with a paltry twelve point eight yuan, and now they’re weaponizing a so-called 'system glitch' to claw even that pittance back! There is a dark, shadowy conspiracy afoot! The town government simply doesn't want to pay me! They fabricated this entire data breach as an excuse to embezzle my sweat money!"

“这绝对不是什么普通的数据泄漏！”美悦子“啪”的一巴掌拍在茶几上，咬牙切齿地控诉道，“这分明是一场蓄谋已久的定向羞辱！先是用十二块八毛钱来侮辱我的尊严，现在居然又借口什么‘系统漏洞’把这笔巨款强行收回！这背后绝对有不可告人的惊天阴谋！镇政府就是不想把钱给我，故意捏造借口赖账！”

Miyetsuko paced back and forth across the room, fuming like an unhinged mad scientist, while the others watched her meltdown in absolute amusement.

美悦子气得在屋里来回踱步，活像个被逼疯的疯狂科学家，而其他人则津津有味地围观着她的情绪大爆发。

Right at that exact second, Fujiwara’s phone in his pocket gave a cheerful, high-pitched ding!

就在这千钧一发之际，藤原口袋里的手机突然欢快地响了一声清脆的提示音：叮咚！

Lazily, Fujiwara pulled out his phone, expecting a spam message or a notification from a food delivery app. But the moment his eyes landed on the balance screen, his jaw unhinged. He rubbed his eyes once, twice, three times, completely convinced he was hallucinating.

藤原懒洋洋地掏出手机，以为是垃圾短信或者外卖提醒。可当他的目光扫到余额显示的那一瞬间，下巴“啪”的一声掉到了地上。他揉了一遍、两遍、三遍眼睛，怀疑自己是不是因为昨天通宵过度而出现了幻觉。

"H-Hold on a second..." Fujiwara gasped, his voice trembling as he pointed a shaking finger at his screen. "My mouth... my mouth was literally opened by the gods today! Look! Look at this!"

“等、等一下……”藤原倒吸一口凉气，颤抖着手指举起手机屏幕，声音激动得直哆嗦，“我的嘴……我的嘴今天绝对开光了！你们快看！快看这个！”

Sayuri, Rin, and Chika crowded around him, their eyes widening in sheer disbelief. Right there on the screen, sitting proudly in Fujiwara’s previously empty Zero-Yuan Treasure account, was a fresh, unblemished influx of funds.

小百合、铃和千夏呼啦一下围了过去，齐刷刷地瞪大了眼睛。只见在藤原本来比脸还干净的账户里，此刻赫然多出了一笔巨款。

Balance: 20,000,000.00 Yuan.

账户余额：20,000,000.00元。

Two thousand wan. Twenty million yuan.

整整两千万！没错，货真价实的两千万整！

The living room fell into an absolute, pin-drop silence. For five solid seconds, nobody breathed.

起居室里瞬间陷入了绝对的死寂，连根针掉在地上都听得见。足足过了五秒钟，没有一个人喘气。

Then, Fujiwara clutched his chest, his eyes rolling back as he wobbled on his feet, looking dangerously close to fainting from sheer, unadulterated happiness.

紧接着，藤原痛苦地捂住胸口，双眼翻白，整个人兴奋得摇摇欲坠，幸福得快要当场晕死过去：

"I'm rich... I'm a multi-millionaire...!" Fujiwara sobbed dramatically, clutching his phone like a precious newborn.

“我发财啦……我成千万富翁啦……”藤原抱着手机喜极而泣，感动得眼泪鼻涕一把抓。

Hearing his joyful cries, Miyetsuko stopped pacing. She whirled around, glaring at him with eyes burning with jealousy and righteous indignation.

听到这番狂喜的哀嚎，正在生闷美的美悦子猛地停下脚步，双眼喷火地转过头来，眼神里充满了嫉妒与愤慨：

"Fujiwara! You broke, bankrupt scam artist!" Miyetsuko pointed an accusing finger straight at his nose. "How on earth did you suddenly get twenty million yuan?! That is mathematical proof of the conspiracy! They stole my twelve point eight yuan to fund your multi-million dollar hush money payout!"

“藤原！你个穷得叮当响的破产骗子！”美悦子气急败坏地指着他的鼻子质问，“凭什么你一开口账户里就凭空多出了两千万？！这简直就是黑幕实锤！他们肯定是把我那十二块八毛钱强行征收上去，凑齐了当成封口费转给你了吧？！”

Hearing her accusation, Fujiwara clutched his precious phone tighter to his chest, puffing out his cheeks in immediate, defensive protest.

一听这话，藤原立刻把手机死死护在怀里，一脸委屈又幸福地大声反驳道：

"No way! Absolutely not!" Fujiwara protested proudly, wearing the smile of a man who had just hit the lottery jackpot. "This is completely, 100% fair! The universe heard my prayers! If someone is willingly sending twenty million yuan straight into my pocket, it's a divine market correction! This has nothing to do with any conspiracy—it's pure, unadulterated economic justice!"

“才不会！绝对公平公正！”藤原挺起胸膛，理直气壮地辩解道，“这绝对是命运对本大人的补偿！苍天有眼，听到我昨晚的诚心祈祷了！既然有人愿意心甘情愿给我发两千万巨款，那就说明这钱绝对来路正当、童叟无欺！这跟什么阴谋诡异毫无关系，这就是纯粹的财神爷显灵！”

The living room exploded into another wave of chaotic, hilarious laughter, as "Fresh Mart" officially welcomed its newest, most ridiculously wealthy—and most socially ruined—millionaire.

起居室里再度爆发出了一阵掀翻屋顶的狂笑，“新鲜玛特”在一片欢声笑语中，正式迎来了他们家里这位史上最离谱、最富有的千万富翁。
