Chapter 589: The Death of Fujiwara

发布时间：2026/10/17 14:59:13

第五百八十九章：藤原之死

Even for an absolute tyrant equipped with a genetically engineered immortal physique, a Type III civilization medical regeneration tank, and an endless supply of high-end dairy products, death can sometimes arrive in the most profoundly stupid and anticlimactic way possible.

即使对于一个拥有基因改造不朽体质、三级文明医疗再生舱以及源源不断高端乳制品供应的绝对暴君来说，死亡有时也会以一种极其愚蠢且毫无史诗感的方式突然降临。

It happened right in the middle of the afternoon lounge session. Emperor Fujiwara was casually reading a live holographic progress report sent from the East Gate—detailing how his disgraced Prince son was currently using a laser-guided toothbrush to scrub a high-security public toilet—when Chika casually tossed him an extra-large, ultra-dense "Galactic Milk Caramel Candy."

这一切就发生在下午的客厅日常小憩中。藤原大帝正闲情逸致地翻看着从东门传来的实时全息进度报告——上面详细记录着他那被贬为庶人的皇子儿子正拿着激光牙刷卖力地刷着高安级别的公共马桶——就在这时，千夏随手抛过来一颗超大号、密度惊人的“银河浓缩牛奶太妃糖”。

Caught off guard by a sudden burst of laughter as the Prince accidentally dropped his face into the toilet bowl, Fujiwara inhaled sharply. The giant milk candy went straight down his windpipe, blocking his airway with impenetrable, sticky dairy perfection.

由于看到皇子不小心把脸栽进马桶里的滑稽画面而猝不及防地笑出了声，藤原猛地倒吸了一口凉气。那块巨大的牛奶糖直接卡死在他的气管里，用坚不可摧且黏糊糊的乳制品完美堵死了他所有的呼吸通道。

Gasp... choke... rattle...!

呃……咳……咯咯……！

The Emperor clutched his throat, his eyes bulging as his legendary, universe-conquering constitution engaged in a pathetic, two-second struggle against a piece of candy before officially shutting down. With a final, dramatic twitch of his boots, the supreme ruler of the global empire collapsed face-first onto the Persian rug, utterly and unmistakably dead.

这位皇帝双手死死掐着脖子，双眼暴突。他那传说中足以征服宇宙的无敌体质，在一块糖果面前极其顽强地抵抗了两秒钟，随后便宣告正式宕机。伴随着双腿最后戏剧性地抽搐了两下，全球帝国的最高统治者脸朝下重重倒在波斯地毯上，彻彻底底、毫无悬念地驾崩了。

The grand salon instantly fell into a stunned, awkward silence.

大起居殿内瞬间陷入了一片震惊而尴尬的死寂。

Chika blinked, holding her half-eaten bag of chips. "Wait... did the Boss just choke to death on a piece of my candy? Is he actually dead, or is he just taking a very dramatic nap?"

千夏眨了眨眼睛，手里还抓着吃了一半的薯片：“等一下……老板刚才不会是被我的一颗糖给活活噎死了吧？他这是真挂了，还是在表演什么行为艺术午觉？”

Sayuri calmly walked over, nudged the Emperor's motionless boot with the tip of her embroidered slipper, and immediately pulled out her holographic financial calculator.

小百合十分冷静地走上前去，用绣花鞋尖轻轻踢了踢皇帝一动不动的靴子，随后迅速掏出了全息财务计算器。

"His vital signs have flatlined, and his soul has already detached," Sayuri announced smoothly, her velvet voice entirely devoid of grief. "According to imperial succession laws, I am now officially the regent queen. More importantly, we need to immediately freeze his bank accounts before the galactic tax authority realizes he choked on a sweet."

“他的生命体征已经彻底归零，灵魂也已经剥离肉体了，”小百合语气平静地宣布道，丝绒般的嗓音里没有半点悲伤，“根据帝国继承法，本宫现在正式成为摄政女王。更重要的是，我们必须在银河税务局发现他死于吃零食噎死之前，立刻把他的银行账户全部冻结。”

Rin hopped off her cushion, poking the dead Emperor’s cheek with her Ruby Scepter. "Um... hello? Can we turn him into a zombie? Or should we just throw a massive Halloween party to celebrate?"

小铃从软垫上蹦了下来，用红宝石权杖戳了戳死去的皇帝的脸颊：“唔……听得见吗？我们要不要把他变成僵尸呀？还是说直接开个盛大的万圣节派对来庆祝一下？”

Just as the harem was already beginning to debate how to divvy up his vast collection of star systems and private chocolate reserves, a faint, glowing blue light materialized near the ceiling.

正当后宫佳丽们已经开始热烈讨论该如何瓜分他那庞大的星系领地和私人巧克力储备时，一团淡淡的幽蓝色发光体在天花板附近凭空凝聚成形。

Whoosh!

哗啦！

A small, official-looking portal stamped with a bureaucratic seal reading “Underworld Ward 183828 Administrative Office” suddenly ripped open. Stepping gracefully out of the rift was none other than Miyetsuko.

一道盖有官方红头文件印章、上面写着*“冥界183828号行政办公室”*的小型传送门突然撕裂开来。从裂缝中迈着优雅步伐走出来的，正是美悦子。

The fallen princess was dressed in her crisp, newly minted "Grand Commander of the Underworld" uniform, complete with an official clipboard, a pair of reading glasses perched on her delicate nose, and an authoritative scowl that screamed low-level bureaucratic competence.

这位没落公主身上穿着崭新笔挺的“冥界大统领”制服，手里夹着一块官方文件夹，挺翘的鼻梁上架着一副老花镜，脸上挂着一种“虽然官职很小但老娘也是个领导”的威严神情。

"Stop panicking, everyone! As the supreme sovereign of Ward 183828, I happen to have direct jurisdiction over accidental snack-related fatalities in this galactic sector!" Miyetsuko declared grandly, strutting over to Fujiwara's lifeless husk.

“大家不要慌！作为183828号领地的最高主宰，本统领恰好对这个银河星区里发生的一切‘零食噎死类非正常死亡’拥有直接司法管辖权！”美悦子大步流星地走到藤原冰冷的躯壳旁，威风凛凛地宣布道。

Sayuri raised an eyebrow. "Miyetsuko? Can you actually drag his soul back from the Styx, or are you just here to repossess his furniture?"

小百合挑了挑眉：“美悦子？你真能把他的灵魂从冥河捞回来，还是说你只是来清点他的遗物好把沙发搬走？”

"Watch and learn, Sister Sayuri!" Miyetsuko sneered with pride. She pulled out a high-tech Styx-approved suction plunger, muttered an ancient underworld bureaucratic incantation, and slammed the plunger directly onto Fujiwara's chest.

“瞧好了，小百合姐姐！”美悦子骄傲地冷哼一声。她掏出一个冥河官方认证的高科技强力马桶吸盘，嘴里念念有词地念起了一段古老的地下世界官僚咒语，随后“啪”的一声把吸盘狠狠按在了藤原的胸口上。

SUCK-POP!

啵——通！

With a sickening, vacuum-sealed suction noise, a hazy, protesting black cloud representing Fujiwara’s soul was violently yanked out of the ethereal plane and slammed right back down through his ribcage.

伴随着一声令人牙酸的真空抽吸巨响，一团代表着藤原灵魂的、骂骂咧咧的黑色雾气被硬生生地从虚无的灵界拽了回来，狠狠砸回了他的胸腔里。

COUGH! SPIT!

咳——噗！

Fujiwara suddenly bolted upright, violently coughing out the giant milk caramel candy across the room where it shattered a decorative vase. The Emperor gasped for air, his eyes wide and wild as his tyrannical life force flooded back into his veins.

藤原猛地直挺挺地坐了起来，剧烈地咳嗽着，将那块巨大的牛奶糖一口喷飞出去，当场砸碎了一个观赏花瓶。皇帝贪婪地大口喘着粗气，随着暴君的生命力重新灌满血管，他的双眼逐渐恢复了狂热的神采。

"What the...?! Where am I?! Did I just die from a piece of candy?!" Fujiwara roared, clutching his chest in disbelief before looking up to see Miyetsuko standing over him with her official clipboard.

“我靠……？！朕这是在哪？！朕刚才居然被一颗糖给送走了？！”藤原难以置信地捂着胸口怒吼道，随后抬起头，正好看见美悦子拿着文件夹居高临下地看着他。

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, crossing her arms with a smug, radiantly satisfied smile. "Well, Master? Who said my 183828号 title was useless? If it weren't for my underworld bureaucratic intervention, you’d currently be standing in line behind three million other dead guys waiting to fill out paperwork at the Styx River DMV."

美悦子推了推眼镜，双手抱胸，脸上挂着一层红光满面、得意洋洋的微笑。“怎么，主人？谁说我的183828号大统领头衔是个摆设了？要不是本统领亲自出马行使行政干预，你现在早就排在三百万个死鬼后面，在冥河车管所排队填表格了呢。”

The entire grand salon stared in stunned silence. Even Chika and Sayuri had to admit that the fallen princess had genuinely pulled off a minor miracle.

整个大起居殿里的人全都惊呆了。就连千夏和小百合也不得不承认，这位没落公主关键时刻还真有点深藏不露的小能耐。

Fujiwara blinked twice, processing the fact that he had just been saved from the afterlife by his own concubine's pathetic Styx-ward post. A slow, wicked, deeply appreciative grin spread across the Emperor’s face. He reached out, grabbed Miyetsuko by her waist, and pulled her tumbling down onto the rug right beside him.

藤原眨了眨眼，终于消化了自己刚才差点死掉、又被自己妃子靠着那个寒酸的冥河片儿区职位给强行捞回来的事实。一抹极其恶劣、充满赞赏的坏笑爬上了这位暴君的脸庞。他伸出手一把揽住美悦子的纤腰，将她直接拽翻在身旁的地毯上。

"Magnificent! Absolutely stellar administrative performance, Grand Commander!" Fujiwara laughed uproariously, giving her plush, high-raised rear a resounding slap. "You may be in charge of a remote graveyard in Ward 183828, but you officially earned a lifetime VIP pass to the Emperor's bedchamber! Now, as a reward for your underworld necromancy, let us immediately test if your resurrection spell affected your structural integrity!"

“干得漂亮！大统领阁下的行政救援能力实在是惊为天人！”藤原放声狂笑，顺手在她那丰腴的臀肉上重重拍了一记，“虽然你管的只是183828号的荒凉乱葬岗，但你今天凭实力赢得了朕寝宫的终身VIP通行证！来吧，作为对你施展冥界回魂术的奖赏，让朕立刻亲自测试一下，你这具死过一次的身子骨到底有没有受到什么结构性损伤！”
