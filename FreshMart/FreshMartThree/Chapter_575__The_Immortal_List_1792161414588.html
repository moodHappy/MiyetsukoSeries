Chapter 575: The Immortal List

发布时间：2026/10/16 22:36:53

第五百五十七章：位列仙班

The cosmic hierarchy of the universe was brutally clear to anyone with a functioning radar: the Heavenly Palace—or simply "Heaven," as the mortals of the Fujiwara Empire called it—ruled supreme as the ultimate sovereign over the mortal realm. The human empire was, in the grand scheme of things, merely a glorified tributary state expected to pay taxes and stay out of trouble. However, there were exceptions to every celestial rule. Specifically, the terrifying underworld dimension ruled by Queen Rin—the Succubus Realm—boasted a military and magical might that could rival the heavenly pantheon toe-to-toe.

宇宙间的权力金字塔在任何一个有理智的星际雷达图上都写得清清楚楚：被藤原帝国凡人尊称为“天宫”的至高存在，才是主宰人间乃至整个物质世界的真正主权者。在神仙眼里，区区藤原帝国不过是个需要按时交税、老实听话的番属国罢了。然而，凡事总有例外。比如由小铃统治的那个深渊魅魔界，其掌握的暗黑魔力与庞大军团，却足以和天庭分庭抗礼、甚至隐隐有一较高下的底气。

That afternoon, a glowing, jade-inscribed imperial edict descended from the stratosphere, landing with a soft, resonant hum right on the mahogany coffee table of the grand salon.

那天下午，一道散发着温润玉质光芒的天界法旨毫无征兆地从平流层降下，伴随着一声清脆的嗡鸣，稳稳地落在了大起居殿的红木茶几上。

"By decree of the Heavenly Palace: In recognition of the mortal realm’s recent... chaotic developments, Heaven shall bestow a supreme grace. Within seven days, one mortal from the human realm shall be selected to ascend to immortality and take a seat among the celestial ranks."

“天宫法旨：鉴于人间近日之……种种荒诞变故，天庭特施恩典。七日之内，将从人间挑选一位凡人位列仙班，登天享福。”

The decree vanished into thin air, leaving the four beauties and the Emperor staring at the spot where it had landed. A tense, dead silence filled the room for exactly three seconds before the fierce, self-righteous lobbying for the divine promotion began.

法旨随之消散，留下面面相觑的四美与皇帝。大殿里死一般的寂静持续了整整三秒钟，随后，关于“究竟谁才是天选之子”的激烈自荐拉力赛瞬间爆发。

"Well, obviously, it is going to be me," Emperor Fujiwara declared, standing up and striking a majestic, tyrannical pose. He smoothed down his imperial robes with absolute, unshakable confidence. "I am the supreme sovereign of the mortal realm! I conquer star systems, I tyrannize billions, and I even negotiated a trade deal with the Heavenly Emperor himself! If anyone in this wretched dimension is getting a promotion to immortal status, it is the Boss."

“这还用问吗？名额绝对是留给朕的，”藤原大言不惭地站起身，摆出一个充满暴君威严的帅气姿势，理了理长袍，“朕可是尘世间至高无上的主宰！朕征服星系、暴虐亿万生灵，甚至还亲自和天帝谈成了跨维度双边贸易！这破地方如果真有人能位列仙班，除了朕还能有谁？”

Sayuri, looking gloriously plump and impossibly regal in her Tang-dynasty silks, let out an elegant, velvet scoff. The Wise Empress gracefully adjusted her folding fan.

穿着盛唐真丝长袍、丰腴绝伦的小百合发出一声优雅而充满不屑的丝绒轻哼，顺势合上了折扇。

"Please, My Lord, let's be realistic," Sayuri purred smoothly, her tone dripping with executive authority. "You couldn't even manage your own libido during our trip to the Succubus Realm. The immortal spot belongs to me. I am the supreme sovereign of the AV Religion, the spiritual comforter of billions of overworked earthly souls. I provide the psychological infrastructure of this entire empire. If the heavenly court wants a civilized, highly influential religious leader on their payroll, they are obviously picking the Living Pontiff."

“夫君，咱们现实一点好不好，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，语气里透着绝对的CEO级自信，“您去趟魅魔界连自己的下半身都管不住，还好意思妄想成仙？这个仙位绝对该归我。我是AV女神教的主宰，是尘世间亿万劳累过度灵魂的心灵抚慰者。我为整个帝国的精神稳定提供了坚实的心理基建。天庭如果要招安一个文明优雅、且极具社会影响力的宗教领袖，闭着眼睛也会选本教主。”

Chika, who had been lazily munching on a bag of spicy chips, suddenly slammed the snack bag onto the table and stood up, her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaving with righteous maternal indignation.

正懒洋洋嚼着麻辣薯片、试图摸鱼的千夏“啪”的一声把零食袋砸在桌上，猛地站了起来，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔因为极度的母性不服气而剧烈起伏着。

"Are you both out of your minds?!" Chika shrieked. "An immortal should be someone who actually sustains life! I am the Great Provider! For eighteen years, I have single-handedly sustained the imperial family's nutritional lifeline with my endless, high-grade dairy output! I am the literal mother of the realm's future generations and the ultimate nurturer of the state! If anyone deserves a VIP seat in heaven, it’s the woman who keeps the Emperor from withering into a dried raisin!"

“你们俩脑子是不是被驴踢了？！”千夏尖叫道，“成仙这种大事，难道不该颁发给真正维持了众生命脉的人吗？！我可是伟大的人间抚育者！十八年来，我单枪匹马用源源不断的高级母乳供应链支撑着整个皇室的营养命脉！我是帝国未来的母亲，是尘世终极的奶水抚育官！如果说谁最有资格在天庭占个VIP座位，那绝对是保护了皇帝没变成干葡萄干的本仙女！”

While the three of them argued passionately, fighting over who was most qualified to abandon their earthly duties for a cloud-based promotion, Miyetsuko didn't even attempt to lobby. The fallen princess simply dropped straight to her knees on the Persian rug in a flawless, bone-deep prostration.

就在这三位大佬吵得面红耳赤、为了争夺云端晋升名额互不相让时，美悦子却连争都懒得争。这位没落公主十分识趣地“扑通”一声双膝跪地，在波斯地毯上行了一个五体投地的大礼。

"I am merely a humble, thrice-ravished servant of two empires," Miyetsuko murmured sweetly, pressing her forehead against the floor and hiking her plush, newly-pampered Tang-style rear high into the air. "I dare not aspire to such divine heights. Whether I am scrubbing the Heavenly Emperor’s floors or serving tea in the mortal palace, my only destiny is absolute, submissive devotion."

“奴婢不过是个历经双重洗礼的卑微小妾罢了，”美悦子甜甜地呢喃着，将额头死锁在地面上，把那对被天帝和暴君双重滋润得丰腴至极的臀部高高撅起，“奴婢万万不敢妄想什么位列仙班。无论是去天帝的宫里擦地板，还是在人间大殿里奉茶，奴婢唯一的宿命就是绝对的顺从与奉献。”

From her floating cushion, blind Rin swung her legs completely unbothered. The little Succubus Queen picked up another demonic cookie, her milky eyes wide with carefree indifference.

坐在漂浮软垫上的盲人小铃悠闲地晃荡着双腿，对这场神仙打架毫不在意。这位新晋的魅魔女王顺手抓起一块恶魔饼干塞进嘴里，空灵的盲眼里满是无所谓的散漫。

"Um... hello, everyone? Rin doesn't care at all!" the little demon chirped happily. "Rin already has a whole dimension full of skeletons, lava, and hot succubi to boss around! Why would Rin want to go live in the sky and eat vegetarian peaches with a bunch of boring old gods? Besides... if Rin abandons her throne to become a celestial immortal, would that cause a massive underworld rebellion? Even Rin, the mighty Skull Throne master, has no idea if the demon accountants would let her resign without starting a civil war!"

“唔……大家别争啦！小铃才不在乎什么仙位呢！”小魅魔开心地嚼着饼干含糊不清地说道，“小铃自己的地盘里有成吨的骷髅、岩浆和火辣的魅魔可以随便当牛做马，干嘛非要跑去天上跟一帮无聊的老头子吃素桃子呀？再说了……如果小铃抛弃王座跑去成仙，会不会直接引发魔界大暴动、造成叛界啊？连小铃这个骷髅王座的主人自己也搞不清楚，地狱的财务总监能不能允许小铃不办离职手续就直接跳槽呢！”

Fujiwara stopped arguing with Sayuri and Chika, stroking his chin as he listened to Rin's pragmatic, terrifyingly bureaucratic demonic logic. The Emperor looked around his chaotic, thoroughly corrupt grand salon, a wicked, brilliant tyrannical grin slowly spreading across his face.

藤原听到这里，终于停止了和小百合、千夏的口水战，摸着下巴陷入了沉思。他环视着这座混乱、荒诞却又无比奢靡的大殿，一抹极其恶劣且充满暴君智慧的坏笑缓缓爬上了他的脸庞。

"You know what?" Fujiwara declared, pulling Miyetsuko up from the floor and wrapping one arm around her waist while winking at the others. "Let the Heavenly Palace keep their immortal seat! Why would I want to leave my empire, my infinite taxes, and my four perfectly corrupt consorts just to go sit on a cloud and eat grass? If Heaven wants to play personnel games, let them! We have ten newly conquered star systems to exploit, a bestselling memoir to publish, and a very busy afternoon schedule right here on the Persian rug!"

“你们知道吗？”藤原一把将美悦子从地上拉起来，顺手搂住她的腰，向其他几人挑了挑眉，“什么狗屁仙位，爱谁去谁去！朕才舍不得抛弃朕的帝国、无尽的税收和这四个绝顶腐败的爱妃，跑去天上吃斋念佛呢！天庭既然喜欢搞人事任命，那就由他们去折腾吧！咱们手头上还有十个新星系的奴役大业要忙、畅销回忆录要出版，最重要的是——这块波斯地毯下午的‘日常行政日程’可排得满满当当呢！”
