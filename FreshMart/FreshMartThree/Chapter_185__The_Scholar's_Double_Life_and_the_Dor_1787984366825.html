As the sticky remnants of the Coca-Cola experiment were finally cleaned up and fresh cups of tea were poured around the dining table, the chaotic laughter slowly settled into a cozy, conversational hum.

随着可乐实验的残局被收拾干净，餐桌上重新换上了清香的热茶，原本喧闹的哄笑声也渐渐转为温馨而闲适的饭后闲聊。

Miyetsuko elegantly adjusted her glasses, her sharp intellectual gaze shifting from general teasing to a more practical matter. She turned her head toward the youngest member of the household.

美悦子优雅地推了推眼镜，锐利的学者目光从先前的调侃中收回，转而投向了家里年纪最小的千夏。

"Speaking of which, Chika," Miyetsuko inquired, her tone carrying a touch of genuine elder-sisterly concern mixed with playful skepticism. "How are your actual academic pursuits in Tokyo going lately? Please tell me you aren't failing your university courses because your brain has been completely liquefied by Fujiwara's degenerate training."

“说起来，千夏，”美悦子语气中带着一丝大姐姐的关心与戏谑，“你在东京那边的大学学业最近怎么样？可别告诉我，你期末考试挂科是因为脑子里装满了藤原那些奇奇怪怪的变态废料吧。”

Chika puffed out her chest in mock indignation, crossing her arms defensively over her maid uniform. "Hmph! For your information, Sister Miyetsuko, I am a model student! My GPA is comfortably in the top five percent of my department. Just because I moonlight as an open-crotch meat toy on the weekends doesn't mean my brain stops working during the week!"

千夏骄傲地挺起胸膛，双手抱胸故作不满地哼了一声：“哼！美悦子姐姐，请对本学霸放尊重些！我的绩点可是稳稳排在系里前百分之五的好不好！虽说我周末兼职当开裆裤肉便器，可并不代表我周一到周五上课时智商会下线呀！”

Rin burst into a loud, amused cackle, leaning across the table to pinch Chika's cheek. "Oh, the sheer irony of a straight-A university student casually referring to herself as a weekend meat toy! What do they teach you in modern higher education anyway? 'Advanced Applied Prostitution and Spaced Repetition Orgasms'?"

铃当场爆笑出声，隔着桌子伸手捏了捏千夏的小脸：“哎呀，堂堂大学优等生居然能这么理直气壮地自称是‘周末肉便器’，这反差简直绝了！现在的现代高等教育都教你们写什么论文啊？《论高级应用风俗学与间隔重复高潮的关联性》吗？”

Sayuri covered her mouth, her eyes crinkling with warm, gentle amusement. "Goodness... just imagine what her stern, respectable professors would say if they knew that the quiet, straight-laced girl sitting in the front row of the lecture hall spent her weekends getting her mouth filled with cola and cum by an eccentric grocery store owner."

小百合掩嘴轻笑，温柔的眼眸里满是忍俊不禁：“天呐……真不敢想象，要是她那些严肃正经的大学教授知道了，坐在阶梯教室第一排用功听讲的乖乖女，周末居然会在杂货铺老板手里经历那种连可乐都用上的疯狂折腾，非得当场惊掉下巴不可。”

Miyetsuko chuckled softly, swirling her tea. "Given the current state of modern academia, they'd probably invite her to write a guest thesis on 'The Socioeconomic Benefits of Domestic Subservience'. But honestly, Chika, your dual life is quite impressive."

美悦子轻笑着晃了晃茶杯：“以当今学术界的魔幻风气，说不定还会邀请她去发表一篇《论家养萌妹的社会经济学效益》呢。不过说真的，千夏这种双面人生确实挺让人刮目相看的。”

Despite the lighthearted banter, a sudden cloud of gloom drifted over Chika's expressive face. Her shoulders slumped slightly, and her playful smirk faded into a genuine, deeply melancholic pout.

然而，正当大家说笑时，千夏的小脸却突然垮了下来，原本得意的笑容变成了浓浓的委屈与沮丧。

"Speaking of university..." Chika groaned softly, burying her face in her hands. "That's the worst part. Tomorrow morning... I have to take the early train back to the campus dorms. And because of my heavy class schedule and exams this month, it's going to be ages—a whole agonizingly long time—before I can finally come back home again."

“说到大学……”千夏郁闷地把脸埋进双手里，叹气道，“这才是最让人难受的地方。明天一大早……我就得坐早班车回学校宿舍了。而且因为这个月课业重、考试多，我得好半天——好久好久才能再回一趟家了……”

The dining room instantly went quiet. The sudden reality of the little maid's upcoming departure cast a bittersweet shadow over the room.

餐厅里的嬉闹声戛然而止。小丫鬟明天就要离开的现实，让这间欢快热闹的屋子里瞬间染上了一丝不舍的离愁。

Fujiwara paused, a slow, roguish grin spreading across his face as he reached over and pulled the pouting college student straight onto his lap.

藤原微微一愣，随即嘴角勾起一抹坏笑，长臂一伸直接把这个撅着嘴的小丫鬟重新捞回了自己的大腿上坐好。

"Is that so?" Fujiwara murmured, his eyes locking onto hers with a mixture of affection and pure, unadulterated mischief. No sleeping allowed until every single drop of your energy—and mine—is completely exhausted."

铃的眼睛瞬间亮了起来，兴奋地举双手赞成：“这个主意太棒了！给咱们的优秀大学生举办一场高强度的‘临别集训营’！今晚不把你折腾到明天连去火车站的力气都没有，咱们几个姐姐可就白疼你啦！”

Miyetsuko and Sayuri exchanged knowing, beautifully wicked smiles, instantly falling in line with the degenerate consensus.

美悦子和小百合默契地对视一眼，脸上挂着同样绝美而危险的微笑，全票通过了这份绝妙的“临别加餐”方案。
