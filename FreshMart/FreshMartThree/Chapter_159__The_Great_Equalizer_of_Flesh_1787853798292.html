Chapter 159: The Great Equalizer of Flesh

发布时间：2026/8/28 02:03:16

第百五十九章：血肉的“绝对平等”

Miyetsuko didn't even blink, her expression remaining as cool and clinical as a surgeon delivering a diagnosis. She took another measured sip of her tea before driving her point home.

美悦子连眼睛都没眨一下，神情冷静得像是在宣读一份外科手术诊断书。她不慌不忙地又抿了一口茶，将这个冰冷的理论彻底剖析开来：

"It's basic population management," Miyetsuko continued smoothly. "By strategically allowing a tiny fraction of elite resources—even high-value mating rights—to leak downward, you effectively neutralize systemic resentment. If the lower classes ever become convinced that certain privileges are permanently walled off, they experience disillusionment. They realize the game is rigged, they feel the injustice... and that is when they riot and burn the system down."

“这其实是一种最高效的人口维稳策略，”美悦子语气平缓地继续说道，“通过刻意让一小部分顶级的稀缺资源——甚至是高价值的交配权——向下流露出一丝缝隙，就能瞬间瓦解底层阶级的怨气。如果让穷人们彻底意识到某种资源是他们一辈子也休想染指的绝对禁区，他们就会产生幻灭感，感到极度的不公……而一旦这种绝望蔓延，他们就会选择造反，把整个系统付之一炬。”

She pushed up her glasses, a dark, brilliant smirk playing on her lips. "But when those same penniless vagrants realize that they share the exact same access—that a desperate kidnapper can breach the inner sanctuary and share the exact same high-end body, entering the very same intimate space as the city's billionaires—a powerful illusion of fairness is created. They feel equal. And a man who feels he has a shot at the peak will never waste his energy tearing down the mountain."

她推了推眼镜，嘴角勾起一抹充满讽刺意味的聪明微笑，“可当那些穷鬼猛然意识到，原来自己和高高在上、不可一世的大亨们享有同等的生理通道——一个穷光蛋居然能用极端手段攻破顶级花魁的防线，和权贵们进出同一个小穴、享用同一具肉体时——一种奇妙的‘绝对公平感’便油然而生了。这让他们感到心理上的平衡，从而不会再去聚众闹事。一个坚信自己也能染指巅峰的穷鬼，是绝不会浪费力气去推翻这座金字塔的。”

Rin rolled her eyes so hard she nearly strained them, though she couldn't hide the amused grin twitching at the corners of her mouth.

铃没好气地翻了个白眼，险些把眼珠子翻到天上去了，嘴角却忍不住挂着一抹被雷到不行的好笑弧度。

"My god, Miyetsuko," Rin snorted, crossing her arms. "You’ve officially managed to turn kidnapping, blackmail, and prostitution into a grand sociological masterclass in social stability. If the local government had any brains, they'd hire you as their minister of propaganda—'The Brothel as the Ultimate Equalizer'!"

“我的天呐，美悦子，”铃双手抱胸吐槽道，“你居然能把绑架勒索和肉体交易，硬生生包装成一门伟大的‘社会稳定学’。当地政府要是脑子没进水，就该直接聘你当首席宣传大臣——口号我都替你想好了：‘平民的花魁，大家的肉便器：论风俗业如何促进社会和谐’！”

Sayuri covered her mouth, her shoulders shaking with helpless, melodic giggles as she imagined the sheer absurdity of it. "Goodness... just imagine the wealthy tycoons paying millions to maintain their elite status, completely unaware that the girl warming their beds is sharing the exact same nights with a penniless fugitive. If the stock market ran on humiliation, Wall Street would crash by noon."

小百合掩嘴娇笑，肩膀笑得直颤，脑补着那个画面险些笑出声来：“天呐……真不敢想象那些挥金如土的大亨们要是知道了真相，顶着尊贵的身份花大钱去包养花魁，结果转头发现自己碰过的女人正被一个身无分文的穷鬼肆意蹂躏……要是能把这种羞辱感折算成股票，华尔街只怕当场就要熔断了。”

Fujiwara burst into a roaring bark of laughter, thoroughly delighted by the chaotic philosophical debate happening over their morning coffee. He reached out, pulling Rin and Sayuri tightly into his arms while locking a proud, affectionate gaze on Miyetsuko.

藤原当场放声大笑起来，对这场在早餐桌上展开的黑色幽默哲学辩论满意极了。他大笑着伸出双臂，将身旁的铃和小百合一把揽入怀中，同时转头朝美悦子投去一个赞许而宠溺的目光。

"That's why I love this family," Fujiwara grinned, kissing Sayuri’s cheek while winking at Miyetsuko. "You won't find this kind of high-level geopolitical analysis anywhere else on earth. Let the billionaires keep their delusions of grandeur—we've got our cozy little shop, our brilliant minds, and all the chaotic entertainment we could ever ask for right here in 'Fresh Mart'."

“这就是我为什么这么爱咱们这个家，”藤原坏笑着在小百合脸上亲了一口，顺便朝美悦子挑了挑眉，“这种顶级的社会学黑深残剖析，你在外面打着灯笼都找不到第二份。管他什么大亨富豪的宏图伟业，咱们只要守着这间‘新鲜玛特’，有聪明绝顶的大脑陪着，天天看这些魔幻现实的大戏，这日子就绝对亏不了！”
