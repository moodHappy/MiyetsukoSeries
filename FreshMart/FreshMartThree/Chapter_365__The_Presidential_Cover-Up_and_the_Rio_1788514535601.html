Chapter 365: The Presidential Cover-Up and the Riot at the Door

发布时间：2026/9/4 17:35:34

第三百五十六章：总统的幕后庇护与门前抗议

Three days later, Fujiwara was released from the high-security holding cell not by a miracle of justice, but by the sheer, terrifying administrative weight of Miyetsuko's rolodex. With a single phone call straight to the President's private office, the entire narrative of the island-wide food poisoning scandal underwent a miraculous, Orwellian transformation.

三天后，藤原并没有迎来什么正义的奇迹，而是靠着美悦子那恐怖的人脉网和一通直通总统办公室的神秘电话，大摇大摆地从戒备森严的拘留所里被捞了出来。随着一通电话打完，轰动全岛的食物中毒丑闻瞬间经历了一场魔幻的、奥威尔式的惊天大反转。

On the midday news broadcast, the national television networks broadcasted a hastily assembled, highly scripted press conference. The government spokesman solemnly announced that Fresh Mart had been entirely vindicated and wrongfully accused; rigorous forensic audits proved the chicken ingredients had been impeccably fresh upon leaving the store, and the mass poisoning was solely the result of a logistical delivery delay caused by a third-party transport company.

在中午的电视新闻直播中，各大电视台紧急播报了一场按剧本演出的新闻发布会。政府发言人神情严肃地宣布，“新鲜玛特”已被完全洗清冤屈，此前纯属无端指责；严格的司法审计证明，食材在离开店铺时绝对新鲜无比，而这场大规模食物中毒完全是由第三方物流公司的运输延误导致的。

Of course, anyone with half a brain—especially the five housemates sitting in the living room—knew this was a 100% fabricated political spin engineered by Miyetsuko to protect their collective hides from a four-hundred-year prison sentence.

当然，只要稍微有点脑子的人——尤其是坐在客厅里的这五个家伙——心里都明镜似的一清二楚：这全是由美悦子精心策划的政治公关话术，纯粹是为了把大家从四百年的牢狱之灾里捞出来而强行甩锅的弥天大谎。

"A logistical delay? That’s brilliant!" Fujiwara gloated, stretching his arms comfortably on the sofa as he tossed an apple in the air. "Blaming the shipping company is a masterpiece of corporate self-preservation! We're completely off the hook!"

“物流延误？这借口简直绝了！”藤原得意洋洋地舒展着身体瘫在沙发上，手里抛着一个苹果坏笑道，“把锅全甩给物流公司，简直是企业自保的艺术巅峰！咱们这回彻底安全了！”

However, the citizens of Donglai Island were far less forgiving than the presidential administration. Sick of institutional corruption, government cover-ups, and blatant lies, an enraged mob of thousands of protesters quickly mobilized, marching straight through the streets with fiery torches and crude picket signs.

然而，东莱岛的民众可远没有总统府那么好糊弄。厌倦了体制腐败、政府掩盖和弥天大谎的愤怒市民们迅速集结成庞大的示威游行队伍，举着火把和抗议标语，气势汹汹地直奔“新鲜玛特”的大门而来。

Within an hour, the storefront was completely surrounded. Megaphones blasted angry chants, glass bottles rattled against the security shutters, and furious protesters banged on the doors, screaming for accountability, justice, and the immediate shutdown of the corrupt grocery store.

不到一个小时，整座杂货铺就被围得水泄不通。高音喇叭里回荡着愤怒的口号声，玻璃窗在抗议人群的推搡下哐当直响，激动的示威者们疯狂砸门，高喊着要讨回公道、绳之以法，并强烈要求立刻关停这家满嘴谎言的黑心杂货铺。

Miyetsuko calmly adjusted her glasses, peering through the blinds at the roaring crowd outside. "Public outrage coefficient is currently peaking at critical levels. However, as long as the executive branch maintains our fabricated narrative, physical trespassing remains the protesters' only viable recourse."

美悦子冷静地推了推眼镜，隔着百叶窗冷冷注视着门外黑压压的愤怒人群：“公众愤怒指数目前已达到临界峰值。不过，只要行政长官继续配合我们的伪造叙事，这群抗议者目前也只能在门外过过嘴瘾了。”

Rin sneered, crossing her arms as a brick bounced off the reinforced front window. "Well, looks like our peaceful little grocery store has officially transformed into a political war zone. Hope you've got a backup plan, genius—because the mob outside doesn't look like they're leaving anytime soon."

铃双手抱胸发出一声冷笑，看着一块砖头重重砸在加固的前窗上：“看来咱们这平和的小杂货铺算是正式变成政治战场了。天才美悦子，你最好有备用计划——因为外面那群暴徒看起来可没有要走的意思。”
