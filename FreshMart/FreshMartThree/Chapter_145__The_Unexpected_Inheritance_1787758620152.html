Chapter 145: The Unexpected Inheritance

发布时间：2026/8/26 23:36:58

第百四十五章：天降的“一发入魂”

Thirteen months had flown by since that chaotic, impulsive afternoon in the back room of "Fresh Mart," and the memory of the glamorous beauty had long since faded into the shop's eccentric archive of past adventures.

自从后室里那场冲动而荒唐的露水情缘过去之后，时间已经悄然滑过了十三个月。那位美艳女子的身影，早已在大伙儿的记忆中渐渐模糊，成了“新鲜玛特”众多奇葩回忆中的一段插曲。

Until one sunny afternoon, when the front door chimed and the very same beauty strutted back into the shop—this time, pushing a luxury baby stroller with a glowing, cherubic infant cooing inside.

直到某个阳光明媚的下午，“新鲜玛特”的门铃清脆地响起。那位风韵犹存的美女竟然再次踏进了店门——而这一次，她手里推着一辆豪华婴儿车，里面正坐着一个咿呀学语、粉雕玉琢的小婴儿。

Before Fujiwara could even process her return, the woman marched straight up to the counter, gently unbuckled the baby, and placed the bewildered infant right into Fujiwara's trembling arms.

还没等藤原反应过来，这位美女便踩着高跟鞋直奔柜台而来，利落地将婴儿从小车里抱起，不由分说地塞进了目瞪口呆、浑身僵硬的藤原怀里。

"Here, he's yours," the beauty announced breezily, flashing a bright, unapologetic smile. "Remember that afternoon a little over a year ago? Well, congratulations! You scored a bullseye on the very first try. That's your biological son."

“给，这是你的，”美女笑意盈盈地拍了拍手，语气轻松得仿佛在寄存一件快递，“还记得一年多前的那个下午吗？恭喜你呀，一发入魂！这小家伙可是你亲骨肉。”

Fujiwara stood completely paralyzed, his eyes bulging as he stared down at the tiny baby blinking back at him. "My... my son?! From one afternoon?!"

藤原整个人当场石化，双眼圆睁地看着怀里那个冲自己吐泡泡的小不老头，大脑彻底宕机：“我……我的儿子？！就凭当年那一下午？！”

"Yep!" the woman chirped cheerfully, checking her designer watch. "Anyway, my new fiancé is extremely wealthy, traditional, and utterly intolerant of raising another man's child. But since I know you're running a massive, chaotic household with plenty of resources, I figured you'd make a responsible dad. Gotta run, my Uber's waiting outside—bye!"

“对呀！”美女愉快地看了看手腕上的名牌手表，“好啦，不瞒你说，我最近刚订婚，未婚夫是个极度传统的大富豪，绝对容不下别人的骨肉。不过我看你们家里地方大、人手足，资源又丰富，养个孩子对你来说肯定小菜一碟。就这样啦，我约的网约车还在外面等我呢，拜拜咯！”

Before Fujiwara could utter a single syllable of protest, she blew him a flying kiss and practically skipped out the door, leaving the master of "Fresh Mart" standing frozen in the middle of the shop with a baby in his arms.

没等藤原吐出一个反对的字眼，她便潇洒地抛了个飞吻，踩着高跟鞋轻盈地转身离去，只留下堂堂“新鲜玛特”的老板抱着个婴儿风中凌乱。

That evening, the entire household gathered in the living room around the coffee table, staring down at the infant who was currently chewing happily on a decorative coaster.

当晚，屋里的全员齐聚起居室，大眼瞪小眼地围坐在茶几周围，看着眼前这个正开心地啃着装饰杯垫的小家伙。

"Well," Rin said, breaking the heavy silence as she burst into a fit of helpless, breathless laughter. "Talk about an unexpected delivery! Boss, you went out to hook up with a random customer a year ago, and now you're a proud father of a bouncing baby boy!"

“好家伙，”铃打破了死一般的寂静，当场笑得直不起腰来，“什么叫天降惊喜！老板，你当年随手在店里收割个桃花，结果一年后直接喜当爹了呀！”

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, leaning over to inspect the baby with clinical detachment. "Thirteen months of gestation... mathematically slightly irregular, but biologically unmistakable. The paternal facial structure is strikingly, comically identical to yours, Fujiwara. A textbook genetic stamp."

美悦子推了推眼镜，冷静地俯下身子仔细端详着小家伙，客观地给出结论：“十三个月的孕期计算……虽然时间轴上略有偏差，但生物学特征无可辩驳。这父系的面部轮廓简直和藤原如出一辙，堪称完美的基因复刻。”

Sayuri smiled warmly, her maternal instincts kicking in instantly as she reached out to gently tickle the baby's chin. "He's absolutely adorable, though. Look at those big eyes! Honestly... having a little one running around the house might actually add a wonderful, lively warmth to 'Fresh Mart'."

小百合温柔地笑了笑，母性瞬间泛滥，伸手轻轻逗弄着小家伙的下巴：“不过这孩子长得真讨人喜欢呢，你看这双大眼睛！说实话……家里要是多添个这么可爱的小团伙，以后‘新鲜玛特’说不定会更热闹更有烟火气呢。”

Fujiwara finally snapped out of his daze, sinking face-first into the sofa cushions with a long, agonizing groan of utter bewilderment. "A baby... She just dropped a kid on my doorstep and ran off to live her high-society fairy tale! What on earth am I supposed to do with a diaper-wearing terrorist?!"

藤原终于从巨大的震惊中回过神来，一脸生无可恋地一头栽倒在沙发靠垫上，发出一声绝望而幽怨的哀嚎：“一个大活人就这么把孩子塞给我，自己跑去过豪门阔太的日子去了！我到底该拿这个穿着纸尿裤的小祖宗怎么办啊？！”

Surrounded by the uproarious laughter of his women and the innocent gurgling of his brand-new son, the chaotic master of the house realized that his extraordinary life had just taken its wildest, most hilarious turn yet.

在女人们此起彼伏的欢声笑语和新晋儿子天真无邪的咿呀声中，这位一向无法无天的杂货铺老板突然意识到——他那本就荒诞不经的人生，此刻算是彻底驶向了更加失控的爆笑深渊。
