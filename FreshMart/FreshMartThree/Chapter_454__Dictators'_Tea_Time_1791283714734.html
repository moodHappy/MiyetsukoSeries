Chapter 454: Dictators' Tea Time

发布时间：2026/10/6 18:48:33

​第四百五十四章：独裁者的下午茶

After the chaotic "gardening" incident on the terrace, the family finally dried off and gathered around the massive mahogany coffee table in the living room. Miyetsuko, still radiating a faint, embarrassed blush, pointedly bypassed the firm leather sofa and carefully lowered herself onto a deeply plush, orthopedic memory-foam cushion. Everyone noticed, but in a rare display of deadpan mercy, no one mentioned it.

在经历了露台上那场混乱的“园艺”事件后，一家人终于擦干身体，围坐在客厅那张巨大的红木茶几旁。美悦子脸上还带着一抹尴尬的微红，她刻意绕过了那张坚硬的真皮沙发，非常谨慎地把自己降落在一个极其柔软的记忆海绵护臀软垫上。所有人都注意到了这一点，但出于一种罕见的、一本正经的默契，谁也没有点破。

Fujiwara took a sip of his freshly brewed tea, crossing his legs as he looked at his formidable harem. "Well then, since I spent my afternoon diligently tending to Miyetsuko's... cosmetic upgrades... how was everyone else's day?"

藤原抿了一口刚泡好的清茶，翘起二郎腿，环视着他这群强悍的后宫。“好了，既然我整个下午都在勤勤恳恳地维护美悦子的……美容升级项目……那你们各位今天过得怎么样？”

Chika sighed heavily, kicking off her designer heels and massaging her arches with the weary air of an overworked office lady.

千夏重重地叹了口气，踢掉脚上的名牌高跟鞋，像个疲惫不堪的普通办公室女郎那样揉着自己的脚弓。

"Europe was an absolute drag," Chika complained, popping a piece of mochi into her mouth. "The board of directors at the European Central Bank kept stalling my hostile takeover. They spent three hours arguing about 'anti-monopoly laws' and 'sovereign debt'. Plus, the coffee they served in the boardroom was incredibly lukewarm."

“欧洲那边简直烦透了，”千夏抱怨着，往嘴里塞了一块麻薯，“欧洲央行的董事会一直想拖延我的恶意收购。他们花了整整三个小时在那儿扯什么‘反垄断法’和‘主权债务’。而且，他们会议室里提供的咖啡居然是温的，难喝死了。”

"How dreadful," Sayuri murmured sympathetically, pouring Chika more tea. "Lukewarm coffee is simply unforgivable. How did you handle it, sweetie?"

“那太可怕了，”小百合充满同情地呢喃着，给千夏添了点茶。“温咖啡绝对是不可原谅的。那你是怎么处理的呢，亲爱的？”

"I shorted their entire national pension fund while they were in the bathroom," Chika stated completely deadpan. "Then I bought the bank, fired the entire board, and legally reclassified the CEO's luxury office as a public public restroom for our syndicate's janitorial staff. By 2 PM, they were practically begging to sign the transfer papers. Honestly, corporate negotiations are so tedious."

“趁他们去上厕所的时候，我做空了他们整个国家的全民养老基金，”千夏面无表情、一本正经地说道。“然后我把那家银行买了下来，解雇了整个董事会，并在法律上把CEO的豪华办公室重新划定为咱们辛迪加保洁大妈的公共厕所。到了下午两点，他们几乎是跪在地上求着签转让协议。说真的，企业谈判真是太枯燥了。”

"Very efficient. Time is money," Fujiwara nodded approvingly. "And you, my queen? How was the visit to your maiden family?"

“非常高效。时间就是金钱，”藤原赞许地点了点头。“那你呢，我的女王？回娘家探亲感觉如何？”

Sayuri smiled sweetly, her demeanor the perfect picture of a traditional, elegant Japanese housewife.

小百合甜甜地笑了，她的仪态宛如一位最完美、最传统优雅的日本家庭主妇。

"Oh, it was very relaxing. Mother made my favorite snacks," Sayuri said, her voice soft and melodious. "However, my father was a bit stressed. He was complaining that the regional governor had zoned the land next to our ancestral estate for a noisy commercial complex, ruining his afternoon bird-watching."

“哦，非常放松呢。母亲做了我最爱吃的点心，”小百合嗓音柔和悦耳地说道。“不过，我父亲稍微有点心烦。他抱怨说，当地的州长把咱们祖宅旁边的那块地规划成了一个吵闹的商业综合体，严重破坏了他下午在院子里看鸟的兴致。”

Miyetsuko scoffed from her plush cushion. "Let me guess. You sent a hit squad to bury the governor under his own commercial complex?"

美悦子坐在她那柔软的垫子上冷哼了一声。“让我猜猜。你派了一支暗杀小队，把那个州长活埋在他自己的商业综合体地基下面了？”

Sayuri blinked, looking genuinely mildly offended by the suggestion of such crude violence.

小百合眨了眨眼，似乎对这种粗鲁暴力的提议感到了一丝真诚的冒犯。

"Of course not, Miyetsuko, I’m a married woman of high society now," Sayuri giggled softly behind her hand. "I simply bought the commercial complex, bought the governor's political party, and had him demoted to a gardener. He's currently trimming the bonsai trees in my father's courtyard. Father said his pruning technique is actually quite excellent."

“当然没有啦，美悦子，我现在可是上流社会的已婚妇人呢，”小百合用手掩着嘴轻柔地娇笑。“我只是把那个商业综合体买了下来，顺便把那个州长所在的政党也买了下来，然后把他降职成了园丁。他现在正在我父亲的院子里修剪盆栽呢。父亲说，他剪树枝的手艺其实相当不错。”

"See? That's how you solve domestic disputes elegantly," Fujiwara laughed, turning his gaze to the blind personnel manager who was happily eating a strawberry. "And what about you, little Rin? Did you have a good shift at the adult massage parlor?"

“看吧？这就叫优雅地解决家庭纠纷，”藤原大笑着，把目光转向了正开心地吃着草莓的盲人人事主管。“那你呢，小铃？今天在成人按摩店的兼职班次过得还开心吗？”

Rin paused, chewing thoughtfully before swallowing. She tilted her head, her wide, unseeing eyes looking perfectly innocent.

铃停下动作，若有所思地咀嚼了一会儿才咽下去。她歪着头，睁着那双空洞却极其无辜的大眼睛。

"It was a very busy day," Rin whispered softly. "A very scary gentleman came in. He had full-body tattoos and missing fingers. He told me he was a legendary Yakuza boss, and he demanded that I give him an 'unforgettable ending that would completely destroy him'."

“今天店里特别忙呢，”铃轻声细语地呢喃道。“来了一位非常可怕的先生。他全身都是纹身，还缺了几根手指。他告诉我他是传说中的黑帮老大，还强硬地要求我给他一个‘能将他彻底摧毁的、终生难忘的结局’。”

The room fell completely silent. Miyetsuko instinctively reached for her baseball bat. "A Yakuza boss dared to sexually harass you? Give me his name. I'll make sure his 'ending' involves a woodchipper."

客厅里瞬间安静了下来。美悦子本能地伸手去摸她的棒球棍。“一个极道头目敢对你性骚扰？把他的名字给我。我保证他的‘结局’里绝对包含一台碎木机。”

"Oh, no, it's fine!" Rin smiled serenely, waving her hands. "I took his request very seriously. I found a severely inflamed knot in his lumbar spine and pressed my thumbs into his sciatic nerve as hard as I could to 'destroy' his fatigue."

“哦，不用不用，没事的！”铃宁静地微笑着摆了摆手。“我非常认真地对待了他的要求。我在他的腰椎上找到了一个严重发炎的结节，然后用尽全力把大拇指按进了他的坐骨神经里，以此来‘彻底摧毁’他的疲劳。”

Chika stared at her. "Wait. You used your succubus grip... on a mob boss's sciatic nerve?"

千夏死死盯着她。“等等。你把你魅魔般的握力……用在了一个黑帮老大的坐骨神经上？”

"Yes!" Rin nodded happily. "He screamed for forty-five minutes straight! He cried so much his tattoos got all soggy. When I finished, he was crawling on the floor. He thanked me, gave me a three-million yen tip, and said he had seen the face of God. He texted the parlor an hour later to say he dissolved his crime syndicate and is moving to the mountains to become a vegan monk."

“对呀！”铃开心地直点头。“他连续惨叫了四十五分钟呢！他哭得眼泪把纹身都弄湿了。我按完的时候，他整个人都在地板上爬。他一直向我道谢，给了我三百万日元的小费，还说他看见了上帝的脸。一个小时后他给店里发短信，说他已经解散了黑帮，准备去深山里当个吃素的和尚了。”

Rin took another bite of her strawberry, looking mildly perplexed. "Men are so emotional when they get a good back rub."

铃又咬了一口草莓，神情看起来有一丝困惑。“男人们在享受了一次优质的背部推拿后，真是太多愁善感啦。”

Fujiwara, the undisputed Emperor of the underworld, looked at his three wives: the one who casually bankrupts nations over lukewarm coffee, the one who buys politicians to trim her dad's bushes, and the blind masseuse who accidentally realigns Yakuza bosses into pacifist monks.

藤原，这位地下世界毋庸置疑的帝王，看着他的三位妻子：一个因为咖啡是温的就随手搞垮一个国家的会计，一个买下政客去给亲爹修剪树枝的传统主妇，还有一个不小心把黑道老大按成和平主义素食和尚的盲人按摩师。

He took a long, slow sip of his tea, a profound sense of domestic pride swelling in his chest.

他端起茶杯，漫长而缓慢地喝了一口，一股深沉的家庭自豪感在胸膛里激荡开来。

"Just an ordinary Tuesday in the Fujiwara household," he concluded with a satisfied nod. "Miyetsuko, pass me a mochi."

“藤原家普通而平凡的一个星期二啊，”他满怀欣慰地点头总结道。“美悦子，递块麻薯给我。”
