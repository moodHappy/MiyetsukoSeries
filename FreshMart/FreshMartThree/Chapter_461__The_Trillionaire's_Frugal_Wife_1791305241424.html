Chapter 461: The Trillionaire's Frugal Wife

发布时间：2026/10/7 00:47:20

第四百六十一章：万亿首富的勤俭娇妻

The afternoon sun poured into the sprawling Fresh Mart penthouse, illuminating a truly bizarre scene. Despite being the undisputed Queen of the world's most powerful First Family, Sayuri was standing gracefully at an ironing board. She was meticulously pressing a wrinkle out of Fujiwara’s dress shirt, a sewing kit resting nearby.

午后的阳光洒进宽敞的“新鲜玛特”顶层公寓，照亮了一幕极其诡异的场景。尽管身为全球权势滔天的第一家庭里毋庸置疑的女王，小百合此刻却优雅地站在熨衣板前。她正一丝不苟地熨烫着藤原衬衫上的褶皱，旁边甚至还放着一个针线盒。

"A truly wealthy family must never forget its humble roots," Sayuri murmured piously, threading a needle to fix a tiny loose button on the cuff. "We must set an example for the empire. As the old saying goes: 'New for three years, old for three years, and patched up for another three years.' It builds character, darling."

“一个真正富有的家族，绝不能忘记自己微贱的出身，”小百合虔诚地呢喃着，正穿针引线，准备缝补袖口上一颗稍微松动的纽扣。“我们必须给整个帝国树立榜样。俗话说得好：‘新三年，旧三年，缝缝补补又三年。’这能磨炼心性呢，亲爱的。”

Fujiwara, who was lounging on the sofa drinking a glass of scotch that cost more than a small island, stared at his wife in sheer, delighted amusement.

正慵懒地靠在沙发上、喝着一杯比一座小岛还要昂贵的威士忌的藤原，满脸荒谬与乐趣地盯着自己的妻子。

"Sayuri, my sweet, perfect wife," Fujiwara chuckled, shaking his head. "We literally own seventy percent of the global textile industry. I could snap my fingers and have a million brand-new bespoke suits woven from golden spider silk delivered by tomorrow morning. Why are you darning my socks?"

“小百合，我完美又甜美的老婆，”藤原轻笑着摇了摇头，“我们在字面意义上控制着全球百分之七十的纺织业。我只要打个响指，明天一早就能有一百万套用黄金蛛丝纯手工定制的全新西装送上门。你到底为什么在补我的破袜子？”

Chika looked up from her financial terminal, frantically doing the math in her head.

千夏从财务终端前抬起头，疯狂地在脑子里算起了一笔账。

"From a macroeconomic perspective, Sayuri-nee's labor value is roughly twelve million dollars an hour!" Chika gasped, horrified by the inefficiency. "That means repairing that ten-dollar sock just cost the syndicate about four hundred thousand dollars in lost opportunity costs! Boss, her frugality is a financial disaster!"

“从宏观经济学的角度来看，小百合姐的劳动价值大概是一小时一千二百万美金！”千夏倒吸一口凉气，对这种低效感到惊恐。“也就是说，缝补那双十块钱的袜子，刚刚让咱们辛迪加损失了大约四十万美金的机会成本！老板，她的节俭简直是一场财务灾难！”

Miyetsuko leaned against the doorframe, crossing her arms with a cold scoff.

美悦子靠在门框上，双手抱胸冷哼了一声。

"Not to mention, if the boss has a hole in his clothes, it implies someone managed to get close enough to damage him," Miyetsuko stated flatly. "It's a glaring security flaw. Throw the damn sock away before it triggers my instinct to execute the entire bodyguard detail for negligence."

“更别提，如果老板的衣服上破了个洞，这就暗示有人成功近身并对他造成了伤害，”美悦子面无表情地陈述道。“这是一个极其刺眼的安保漏洞。赶紧把那破袜子扔了，免得它触发了我想去把整个保镖小队以渎职罪全处决了的冲动。”

Sayuri pouted elegantly, completely ignoring the screaming accountant and the paranoid assassin. She smoothed out the fabric with a gentle, motherly pat.

小百合优雅地嘟起嘴，完全无视了抓狂的会计和多疑的杀手。她充满母爱地轻轻拍平布料上的褶皱。

"You all just don't understand the charm of a virtuous, traditional wife," Sayuri huffed softly, looking quite proud of her mending skills. "A hand-patched collar shows the world that a husband is deeply loved and cared for at home!"

“你们根本不懂一个勤俭持家、传统贤妻的魅力，”小百合轻柔地哼了一声，对自己的缝补手艺看起来相当自豪。“一个手工打着补丁的衣领，才能向全世界彰显出，这位丈夫在家里被深深爱着、无微不至地照顾着呀！”

In the corner, blind Rin gently set down her teacup. Her unseeing eyes blinked innocently as she took Sayuri's thrifty philosophy completely to heart.

在角落里，盲人铃轻柔地放下了茶杯。她那空洞的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，把小百合的“节俭之道”完完全全地听进了心里。

"Um... hello, Sayuri-nee?" Rin piped up softly, her sweet voice cutting through the banter. "If our strict family rule is 'patch it up for another three years'... does that mean I should bring out the sewing kit for all the lace panties Master tore to shreds last night?"

“唔……你好呀，小百合姐？”铃轻声细语地开了口，甜美的嗓音穿透了众人的打趣。“如果咱们严格的家训是‘缝缝补补又三年’……那我是不是该把针线盒拿出来，把主人昨晚撕成碎片的那些蕾丝内裤全都补一补呀？”

Rin tilted her head, looking genuinely concerned by the workload. "There are a lot of tiny little pieces... it might take me a few days to stitch the crotches back together so you can wear them again..."

铃歪了歪头，似乎对这庞大的工作量感到了真诚的担忧。“碎片真的好多好小哦……我可能得花上好几天，才能把那些底裆重新拼缝在一起，让你接着穿呢……”

Sayuri’s face instantly flushed a brilliant, catastrophic shade of crimson. The hot iron nearly slipped from her hand, her 'virtuous, frugal housewife' persona immediately destroyed by the lewd, explicit reminder of her aggressive nocturnal activities.

小百合的脸瞬间烧成了一种极其灿烂、灾难级别的绯红。滚烫的熨斗差点从她手里滑落，她那“贤妻良母、勤俭持家”的人设，瞬间被这个关于她夜晚狂野战况的下流提醒给彻底粉碎了。

"R-Rin! We... we don't patch those!" Sayuri stammered, frantically waving her hands as the other women burst into laughter.

“小……小铃！那些……那些东西咱们不补的！”小百合结结巴巴地说着，慌乱地摆着手，而另外几个女人早就爆发出了一阵狂笑。

Fujiwara let out a booming, breathless roar of laughter, walking over to wrap his arms around his blushing, flustered queen from behind.

藤原爆发出一阵震耳欲聋、笑得上气不接下气的狂笑，走过去从背后一把揽住他那位羞愤欲绝、不知所措的女王。

"She's right, Rin! We definitely don't patch those up!" Fujiwara grinned wickedly, kissing Sayuri's burning cheek. "In fact, considering my Queen's thoroughly 'frugal' appetite in bed, we might need to buy out another European lingerie factory by tomorrow!"

“她说得对，小铃！那些东西咱们绝对不用补！”藤原邪恶地咧嘴笑着，亲了亲小百合滚烫的脸颊。“事实上，考虑到我的女王在床上那‘勤俭节约’的巨大胃口，咱们明天可能还得再去收购一家欧洲的内衣工厂才行！”
