Chapter 578: The Celestial Heir Apparent

发布时间：2026/10/16 23:12:42

第五百七十八章：龙种

Several weeks into the newly implemented "Shared Interdimensional Asset" custody agreement, the grand salon of the imperial palace was hosting a remarkably tense, highly confidential medical briefing. Wise Empress Sayuri stood by the central table, holding a glowing holographic diagnostic slate that displayed the biological scans of Consort Miyetsuko.

在“跨维度共享核心资产”托管协议正式运转了几周之后，皇室大起居殿内正在举行一场气氛极其微妙且高度机密的医疗通报会。贤后小百合站在中央茶几旁，手里拿着一块发光的量子医疗诊断全息板，上面清晰地显示着美悦子爱妃最新的生理扫描数据。

The entire household had gathered around. Fujiwara sat in his velvet armchair with a dark, contemplative expression, his fingers drumming rhythmically on the armrest. Chika was leaning over the back of the sofa, her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaving as she stared at the scan in utter disbelief. Blind Rin swung her legs on her fluffy cushion, humming a cheerful underworld lullaby, completely unaware of the geopolitical magnitude of the data.

整个后宫的成员全部围了过来。藤原坐在天鹅绒单人沙发上，脸上带着阴晴不定的沉思神色，手指在扶手上轻轻叩击。千夏探身趴在沙发靠背上，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔随着她目瞪口呆的惊呼剧烈起伏着。盲人小铃则晃荡着双腿，嘴里哼着轻快的地狱摇篮曲，对这份数据背后足以引发星际地震的地缘政治分量毫无所觉。

"Well?" Fujiwara finally broke the silence, his voice dropping into a low, tyrannical growl. "What are the results of the medical scan, Sayuri? Did she pick up a quantum parasite from the heavenly pantry, or is it what I think it is?"

“怎么样？”藤原终于打破了沉默，声音低沉下来，透着一股暴君特有的危险质感，“小百合，医疗扫描的结果到底是什么？她是从天庭的储藏室里沾染了什么量子寄生虫，还是说……正如朕所预料的那样？”

Sayuri let out a soft, velvet sigh, setting the holographic slate down on the mahogany table. The Living Pontiff’s pristine lips curled into a complex, profoundly amused smile.

小百合发出一声丝绒般的轻叹，将全息诊断板轻轻放在红木茶几上。这位活教主完美无瑕的红唇边，挂起了一抹复杂而耐人寻味的微笑。

"Congratulations, My Lord," Sayuri purred smoothly, her tone dripping with mock solemnity. "According to the divine DNA matchers and the higher-dimensional womb markers, Consort Miyetsuko is pregnant. She is carrying a true, authenticated celestial heir—a literal 'Dragon Seed' sired by the Heavenly Emperor himself during her weekend overtime shifts."

“恭喜夫君，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，语气里带着几分调侃的庄严，“根据天界DNA比对器与高维子宫生物标记的显示，美悦子爱妃怀孕了。她怀上了一个货真价实、经过天庭官方认证的纯血仙胎——也就是在她的周末加班期间，由天帝陛下亲自播种的‘龙种’。”

The grand salon erupted.

大起居殿瞬间炸开了锅。

"A baby?!" Chika shrieked, dropping her half-eaten bag of chips onto the Persian rug. Her eyes bulged as she pointed at Miyetsuko’s still-flat, porcelain-smooth abdomen. "The Heavenly Emperor knocked her up?! That means we are literally running a joint-custody baby-sitting service with the Supreme Deity of the Universe! Does this mean the kid gets dual citizenship in Heaven and the Empire?!"

“怀上了？！”千夏尖叫着，手里吃了一半的薯片直接砸在波斯地毯上。她瞪圆了眼睛，指着美悦子那目前还平坦光滑的小腹，“天帝居然把她给搞怀孕了？！那岂不是说，咱们现在不仅在搞共享伴侣，还要跟宇宙至高主宰联合搞托儿所了？！那这孩子以后算不算拥有天庭和帝国的双重国籍啊？！”

Miyetsuko, kneeling gracefully by the Persian rug in her usual submissive posture, blushed a brilliant, ecstatic shade of pink. Her plush Tang-style rear was hiked high in the air as she pressed her forehead against the floor, trembling with the immense, breathtaking honor of carrying a divine fetus.

作为当事人的美悦子则优雅地跪在波斯地毯上，白瓷般的脸上泛起一层极致的红晕。她将那对被滋润得丰腴至极的臀部高高撅起，额头贴着地面，因为怀上神胎那无上的荣宠而激动的浑身发抖。

"Your humble servant never imagined... to bear a seed of the higher-dimensional pantheon," Miyetsuko murmured, her voice laced with decadent sweetness. "I only hope the child inherits Master’s tactical ruthlessness and His Celestial Majesty’s divine immortality!"

“奴婢做梦也没想到……竟然能孕育高维神明的血脉，”美悦子甜甜地呢喃着，声音里透着一股靡烂的幸福感，“奴婢只希望这孩子以后生下来，既能继承主人您那冷酷无情的暴君手腕，又能遗传天帝陛下的不朽神格！”

Fujiwara stood up slowly from his velvet chair. Instead of flying into a jealous, destructive rage—as any normal, self-respecting tyrant would—a slow, brilliant, utterly unrepentant business-mogul grin spread across the Emperor’s face. He walked over, pulled Miyetsuko up from the floor, and firmly patted her newly consecrated, pregnant belly.

藤原缓缓从天鹅绒沙发上站起身。想象中的暴怒与绿帽嫉妒并没有发生，相反，一个极其精明、厚颜无耻的商业大亨式坏笑缓缓爬上了这位皇帝的脸庞。他大步走过去，将美悦子从地上拉了起来，重重地拍了拍她那刚刚被神圣加冕、孕育着龙种的肚子。

"Jealous? Angry? Nonsense!" Fujiwara laughed uproariously, his dark eyes sparkling with pure capitalist ambition. "This isn't a cuckoldry scandal; this is the ultimate geopolitical merger! Think about it, everyone! We now possess direct familial ties to the Heavenly Palace! When the child is born, we can use our imperial leverage to demand more higher-dimensional tech, negotiate zero-tariff trade routes across the cosmos, and establish a permanent blood-monopoly over both mortal and celestial real estate!"

“嫉妒？愤怒？胡说八道！”藤原放声狂笑，漆黑的双眼里闪烁着纯粹的资本主义野心，“这算哪门子绿帽丑闻？这分明是跨维度的世纪资产重组与强强联手！大家仔细想想！咱们现在直接跟天宫攀上亲家了！等这小家伙生下来，咱们以后就能直接打着‘皇亲国戚’的旗号，去找天帝索要更多的高维科技、敲定全宇宙零关税贸易航线，甚至直接在人间和仙界搞房地产血统垄断！”

Sayuri nodded in immediate, enthusiastic agreement, her macroeconomic soul instantly aligning with the Emperor’s vision.

小百合立刻赞许地点头，她那宏观经济学的灵魂瞬间与皇帝的无耻愿景达成高度共识。

"Brilliant, My Lord. I shall immediately update the imperial corporate strategy," Sayuri purred smoothly, adjusting her folding fan. "We will launch a new public relations campaign: 'The Fujiwara-Celestial Nursery: Raising the Next Generation of Cosmic Landlords.' We can even sell commemorative baby-scan holograms to the citizens across our ten newly conquered star systems. The merchandising revenue alone will fund our next fleet of stealth bombers."

“夫君高见。臣妾这就着手更新帝国的核心商业战略，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，优雅地合上折扇，“咱们可以立刻启动一轮全新的公关宣传：‘藤原-天庭联姻育儿中心：培养下一代宇宙包租公’。甚至还能把这胎儿的全息扫描图做成纪念周边，向那十个新征收的星系公民公开售卖。光是周边衍生品的销售额，就足够咱们再建造一支隐形轰炸机舰队了。”

Chika whistled, grabbing another snack. "Well, damn. I thought I was the only one in this palace whose body was designated as a state-owned production line. Turns out Miyetsuko just got promoted to a higher-dimensional manufacturing plant! Congrats on the royal bun in the oven, sis!"

千夏吹了个口哨，顺手抓起一把零食塞进嘴里。“啧啧，我还以为这座宫殿里只有我一个人的身子被当成国有生产线呢。搞半天美悦子这是直接晋升成了高维特供制造厂啊！恭喜恭喜，这波绝对是超级大丰收！”

From her floating cushion, blind Rin clapped her hands happily, her milky eyes wide with sweet demonic joy. "Um... hello, pregnant Miyetsuko-nee! Does this mean the baby is half-god and half-mortal? If we raise it in the underworld, will it become a half-demon too? Rin wants to teach the little baby how to play with skull rattles and summon tiny fireballs!"

盲人小铃坐在浮空软垫上开心地拍着小手，空灵的盲眼里满是甜美的恶魔喜悦：“唔……恭喜怀孕的美悦子姐姐！这意思是不是说，小宝宝以后既是神仙又是凡人呀？如果咱们把它抱到地狱里来养，它会不会变成半人半魔的小怪兽呀？小铃申请当大伯母，负责教小宝宝玩骷髅拨浪鼓和召唤迷你小火球！”

"Enough planning! This calls for an immediate, multi-dimensional celebration!" Fujiwara roared, pulling the pregnant "Shared Asset" firmly into his arms and steering her toward the velvet sofa. "Sayuri, prepare the imperial treasury accounts! Chika, bring the fresh milk! Miyetsuko, you are going to rest your divine cargo right here while the Emperor personally conducts a prenatal developmental checkup!"

“别光顾着开会规划了！这么大的喜事，必须立刻举行跨维度的全家大庆祝！”藤原大手一挥，将这位怀着龙种的“共享资产”紧紧搂在怀里，大步流星地朝着天鹅绒沙发走去，“小百合，准备好皇室财务报表！千夏，把新鲜奶水端来！美悦子，你现在立刻把你的‘神圣载荷’给朕安顿好，接下来由皇帝陛下亲自为你进行一场全方位的胎儿发育健康大检查！”
