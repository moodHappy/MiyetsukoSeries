Chapter 216: The Legendary Fan Girl

发布时间：2026/8/30 01:37:20

第二百一十六章：传奇粉丝的诞生

The sun dipped below the horizon of Donglai Island, casting a warm, amber glow over the private VIP dining room of the exclusive restaurant.

夕阳沉入东莱岛的地平线，将一层温润的金辉洒满了那家高级餐厅的私密VIP包厢。

Inside, Fujiwara sat on the edge of his plush leather seat, his heart hammering against his ribs like a trapped bird. Despite his usual shameless swagger, tonight he was a nervous wreck. The Celibacy Protocol might have been shattered the day before by Chika, but the grand idol-slaying mission was finally at hand, and the sheer tension in the air was enough to suffocate a grown man.

包厢里，藤原如坐针毡地边缘卡座上，心跳快得像一只撞击笼子的飞鸟。尽管平日里厚颜无耻惯了，但今晚他却紧张得手心直冒汗。虽然禁欲令昨天已经被千夏无情地粉碎，但眼下这场筹划已久的“偶像攻略战”终于拉开帷幕，空气中弥漫的强大气场几乎压得他喘不过气来。

The private door slid open.

就在这时，包厢的移门被轻轻拉开。

Stepping into the room was Kirara—the radiant, peerless superstar whose face graced billboards across the entire nation, stunningly gorgeous and impeccably dressed. Fujiwara’s breath hitched. He braced himself to stand up and deliver a smooth, charming greeting.

走进房间的正是绮罗罗——那位海报贴满全国大街小巷、光芒万丈的顶级国民超级巨星。她真人比屏幕上更加惊艳动人，穿着一身优雅高档的小礼服。藤原呼吸一窒，刚想站起身来展现一番成熟男人的优雅风度。

However, before Fujiwara could even utter a single syllable, Kirara’s eyes locked onto the quiet, unassuming woman sitting gracefully at the center of the table.

然而，还没等藤原吐出半个音节，绮罗罗的目光便瞬间锁定坐在桌子中央、神情恬静淡然的小百合身上。

In a fraction of a second, all the superstar poise vanished. Kirara’s eyes widened in sheer, starstruck disbelief, her hands trembling slightly as she instinctively snapped into a textbook, deeply respectful ninety-degree bow.

仅仅零点一秒间，什么巨星风范、什么傲人架子，瞬间荡然无存。绮罗罗的双眼因为极度的震惊与狂热而微微睁大，双手有些微微发抖，本能地弯下腰，深深地鞠了个标准的九十度大躬。

"S-Sayuri-sama?!" Kirara gasped, her voice cracking with overwhelming, starstruck emotion. "I... I cannot express how deeply honored I am! To receive a personal invitation from a legendary industry pioneer... I honestly thought my manager was playing a cruel joke on me! I have been your absolute, devoted fan since the very first day I entered this profession!"

“小……小百合前辈？！”绮罗罗的声音因为极度的激动而微微发颤，“我……我简直不敢相信自己的眼睛！能够收到业界传奇前辈的亲自邀请，我刚才还以为是经纪人在跟我开玩笑！我从踏入这一行的第一天起，就一直是您最忠实的超级粉丝啊！”

Fujiwara, who was halfway out of his seat, froze like a statue. His mouth hung open in utter bafflement.

正打算起身的藤原当场石化在原地，嘴巴张得老大，脑子彻底当机。

What? Fan?

什么鬼？粉丝？！

He had spent the entire week agonizing over how to impress this idol, forgetting a rather monumental cosmic fact: in the grand hierarchy of the adult entertainment universe, Kirara wasn't just a popular star—she was part of the generation that grew up worshiping Sayuri as an untouchable, god-tier titan.

他这一整周都在绞尽脑汁琢磨着怎么在偶像面前表现自己，却偏偏忘了那个极其震撼的宇宙真相：在整个娱乐帝国的金字塔尖上，绮罗罗根本不是什么高高在上的女王，而是当年把小百合奉为不可亵渎之神的狂热追随者之一！

Facing the trembling, starstruck superstar, Sayuri didn't flinch or bat an eye. The gentle, domestic girlfriend who poured tea and washed chamber pots at "Fresh Mart" vanished in an instant, replaced by the effortless, crushing aura of a retired legendary queen.

面对眼前这位情绪激动的当红巨星，小百合没有丝毫慌乱。那个在“新鲜玛特”里倒尿盆、温柔泡茶的居家女友在这一秒瞬间隐去取而代之的，是那位隐退江湖多年的殿堂级女王所散发出的、绝对掌控全场的强大气场。

Sayuri stood up gracefully, offering a warm, composed smile, and extended her hand in a dignified greeting.

小百合优雅地站起身来，脸上挂着温和而从容的微笑，十分大方地伸出手回礼寒暄：

"It is a pleasure to finally meet you, Kirara-san," Sayuri said smoothly, her voice calm, commanding, and dripping with supreme grace. "I have heard wonderful things about your work. Please, sit down. Let's not stand on ceremony tonight."

“很高兴终于见到你，绮罗罗，”小百合的声音平静而沉稳，带着一种让人无法抗拒的优雅与威严，“我也经常听到别人夸奖你的作品。请坐吧，今晚大家都是朋友，不必拘泥于这些虚礼。”

"Y-Yes, ma'am!" Kirara stammered respectfully, quickly taking her seat like an obedient schoolgirl meeting her principal.

“是、是的前辈！”绮罗罗恭恭敬敬地应了一声，乖巧得像个面见校长的三好学生，小心翼翼地在对面坐了下来。

Fujiwara slowly lowered himself back into his chair, wiping a cold sweat from his forehead.

藤原默默地重新坐回椅子上，抬手擦了擦额头上的冷汗。
