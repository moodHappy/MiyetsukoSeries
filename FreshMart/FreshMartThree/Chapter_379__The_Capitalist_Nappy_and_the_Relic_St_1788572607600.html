第三百七十九章：资本主义尿布与圣物地摊

By 6:00 AM, the back-breaking demolition was finally complete. Fifty uneven, ketchup-stained linoleum tiles were carefully packed into wooden crates. Fujiwara, completely covered in white concrete dust and sticky red sauce, slumped against the counter. Beside him, Sayuri was shivering, her glamorous executive madam persona thoroughly destroyed by a night of hauling debris.

清晨六点，那场令人腰酸背痛的强拆工程终于结束了。五十块参差不齐、沾满番茄酱的油毡地砖被小心翼翼地装进了木箱里。浑身沾满白色水泥灰和黏糊糊红酱汁的藤原，像滩烂泥一样瘫靠在柜台边。在他身旁，小百合冻得瑟瑟发抖，她那迷人的高级妈妈桑形象在干了一整夜的搬砖苦力后，已经彻底荡然无存。

But before they could even catch their breath, Fujiwara reached under the pharmacy counter and pulled out a massive, crinkly plastic package. With a grim, deadpan expression, he extracted a thick, ultra-absorbent adult nappy.

但还没等他们喘口气，藤原就伸手到药房柜台下面，拽出了一个巨大的、哗啦作响的塑料包装袋。他面无表情、神色凝重地从里面掏出了一片超厚、超强吸收力的成人纸尿裤。

Sayuri’s eyes widened in sheer horror. She scrambled backward, clutching her torn, ketchup-stained skirt. "Fujiwara... what the hell is that?! Don't you dare come near me with that geriatric diaper!"

小百合惊恐地瞪大了眼睛。她拼命往后退去，死死攥着自己那条破烂不堪、沾满番茄酱的短裙：“藤原……那到底是什么鬼东西？！你敢拿着那种老年痴呆尿布靠近我试试！”

"It's an industrial-grade nappy, Madame Director," Fujiwara replied with cold, ruthless capitalist logic, advancing toward her. "Rin’s orders. We are strictly forbidden from taking a single bathroom break until all fifty 'Sacred Weeping Tiles' are sold at the outdoor stall. Time is money. Bladder control is an inefficient luxury we can no longer afford."

“这是工业级纸尿裤，大导演女士，”藤原带着冷酷无情的资本家逻辑逼近她，“铃下的死命令。在外面地摊上把这五十块‘泣血圣砖’全部卖光之前，我们绝对不准去上哪怕一次厕所。时间就是金钱。膀胱控制力是我们现在根本消费不起的低效奢侈品。”

"I am a former legendary AV phenomenon! I cannot wear a bulky, crinkly nappy!" Sayuri shrieked, kicking her shredded black-stockinged legs in a futile attempt to resist as Fujiwara tackled her to the floor. "It's going to ruin my seductive silhouette! My dignity will be permanently obliterated!"

“我可是曾经红极一时的传奇前AV女优！我绝不穿这种臃肿、走起路来还哗啦直响的尿布！”小百合尖叫着，踢打着她那穿着破烂黑丝的双腿，徒劳地试图反抗将她扑倒在地的藤原，“这会彻底毁了我性感的身体曲线！我的尊严将被永久性抹杀的！”

"Your dignity was obliterated the moment you got tied to a pillar and almost roasted like a salt-baked duck!" Fujiwara grunted, expertly pinning her down and forcibly strapping the thick, puffy nappy around her hips, securing the adhesive tapes with aggressive snaps. "Just think of it as a wearable, portable VIP toilet! Peak retail efficiency!"

“你的尊严在被绑在柱子上差点当成盐焗板鸭烤了的时候就已经灰飞烟灭了！”藤原闷哼一声，熟练地将她按住，强行把那片厚实臃肿的尿布垫在她的臀部，并凶狠地撕开胶带“啪叽”一声牢牢贴好，“你就把它当成一个可穿戴的便携式VIP马桶吧！这可是零售效率的巅峰体现！”

Ten minutes later, the front doors of Fresh Mart swung open. The morning sun illuminated a massive, chaotic crowd of fanatical patriots already lined up down the block, chanting slogans and waving cash.

十分钟后，“新鲜玛特”的大门轰然敞开。清晨的阳光照耀在街区外，那里早就排起了一支庞大而混乱的狂热爱国者队伍，他们高呼着口号，在空中疯狂挥舞着钞票。

Fujiwara and Sayuri dragged the heavy crates of floor tiles out onto the pavement to set up their makeshift stall. Every step Sayuri took produced a loud, unmistakable crinkle-crinkle sound from beneath her shredded, hiked-up skirt. The bulky nappy completely puffed out her backside, making her waddle like an agitated, highly provocative penguin.

藤原和小百合把沉重的地砖木箱拖到人行道上，开始摆弄他们临时搭建的地摊。小百合每走一步，她那条被向上撸起的破烂短裙下就会发出响亮且毫无掩饰的“哗啦哗啦”塑料摩擦声。臃肿的尿布把她的屁股整个撑了起来，让她走起路来像只暴躁又极具挑逗性的企鹅，一摇一摆。

Inside the comfort of the store, the three girls watched the pathetic display from a set of plush lawn chairs, sipping iced coffees.

在杂货铺舒适的内部，三个女孩舒舒服服地坐在毛绒躺椅上，一边喝着冰咖啡，一边欣赏着这悲惨的一幕。

"Look at her waddle," Rin snorted, nearly choking on her drink as she pointed at Sayuri. "She looks like a giant toddler trying to solicit sailors. I can't believe he actually put it on her."

“看她那一摇一摆的样子，”铃差点被咖啡呛到，指着小百合毫不留情地喷笑出声，“她现在看起来简直就像个试图去招揽水兵的巨型巨婴。我真不敢相信藤原居然真的给她穿上了。”

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, her tablet glowing as she ran a quick calculation. "By eliminating an average of three standard restroom breaks per shift, we have increased operational stamina by eighteen percent. The integration of adult incontinence products into our workforce is a triumph of scientific management."

美悦子推了推眼镜，平板电脑闪烁着光芒，她正在进行快速计算：“通过取消每班平均三次的标准如厕时间，我们的运营耐久度提升了百分之十八。将成人失禁用品整合到我们的劳动力体系中，简直是科学管理的伟大胜利。”

Chika giggled innocently, waving at the struggling duo outside. "Do your best, Sayuri-san! Don't forget to use the nappy if you need to! We won't judge!"

千夏天真无邪地咯咯笑着，朝着外面苦苦挣扎的两人挥手：“加油哦，小百合姐！如果憋不住了千万别忘了直接尿在里面呀！我们绝对不会笑话你的！”

Outside, Sayuri's face flushed a deep, humiliated crimson as the first customer approached the stall. But the moment the fanatic slapped five crisp hundred-dollar bills onto the table for a single ketchup-stained piece of linoleum, the actress within her took over.

门外，当第一位顾客走近地摊时，小百合的脸瞬间涨成了屈辱的深红色。但就在那个狂热粉丝将五张崭新的百元大钞拍在桌上，只为买下一块沾满番茄酱的破油毡地砖时，她体内的女演员本能瞬间占据了上风。

"Welcome, brave patriot!" Sayuri cooed, leaning seductively over the crate of tiles. The motion caused the thick diaper to aggressively crinkle, but she didn't even flinch, flashing a dazzling, million-dollar smile. "Behold the Weeping Tiles of Fresh Mart! Limit two per customer, unless you'd like to pay the VIP premium!"

“欢迎您，勇敢的爱国者！”小百合娇滴滴地唤道，妖娆地俯身靠在装地砖的木箱上。这个动作导致她那厚实的尿布发出了极其狂躁的“哗啦”声，但她连眉头都没皱一下，立刻绽放出一个价值百万的耀眼笑容。“快来瞻仰‘新鲜玛特’的泣血圣砖！每人限购两块，除非您愿意支付VIP溢价！”

"I'm going to be rich," Fujiwara muttered, aggressively stuffing the cash into his pockets while furiously arranging the dirty floor tiles. "I don't care if she shits herself out here, as long as the money keeps flowing. God bless the stupid masses!"

“我要发财了，”藤原一边凶狠地把现金塞进口袋，一边疯狂地整理着那些脏兮兮的地板砖，嘴里神经质地嘟囔着，“只要钱能源源不断地流进来，就算她当场拉在裤裆里我都不在乎。上帝保佑这群愚蠢的大众！”
