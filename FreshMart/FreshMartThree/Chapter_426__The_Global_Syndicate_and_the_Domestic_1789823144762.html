Chapter 426: The Global Syndicate and the Domestic Routine

发布时间：2026/9/19 21:05:43

第四百二十六章：全球辛迪加与温馨的日常

By this point, the Fresh Mart conglomerate had expanded its tentacles so deeply into the planetary grid that it essentially controlled the lifeblood of almost every nation on Earth. With a mere snap of Fujiwara’s fingers, a sovereign government could be brought to its knees, a ruling political party could be ousted overnight, or global financial markets could be sent crashing into historic plunges or soaring into astronomical booms. The trillionaire's word was literally absolute law across continents.

到了这个时候，“新鲜玛特”财团的触角已经深深扎入了全球秩序的每一个角落，几乎掌控了地球上绝大多数国家的命脉。只要藤原轻轻打个响指，一个主权国家的政府就能当场垮台，执政党能在转眼间被扫地出门，全球金融市场也能在一念之间暴跌成灾或是暴涨冲天。这位万亿首富的话，在各大洲简直就是不容置疑的绝对真理。

Within this sprawling, terrifyingly powerful global hegemony, the division of labor had become a finely tuned family machine: Miyetsuko ruled over the entire Western Hemisphere with an iron fist and a swinging baseball bat; Chika managed the Eastern Hemisphere alongside their hyper-profitable, world-class pleasure club syndicate; and gentle, blind Rin presided over the massive, high-yield sperm bank empire while providing her signature innocent, ditsy charm. As for Sayuri? She held the most exclusive portfolio of all: dedicating herself entirely to servicing Fujiwara alone, always ready to pamper him with her warm, soft mouth or present her voluptuous, cushiony backside at a moment's notice.

在这庞大而恐怖的全球霸权之中，分工已经精细得宛如一台高效运转的家庭机器：美悦子带着铁棒球棍以铁血手腕执掌着整个西半球；千夏则包揽了东半球以及那家日进斗金的全球顶级风俗俱乐部；至于温柔的盲人铃，则一边掌管着庞大高产的精子库帝国，一边负责提供她那标志性的呆萌可爱。至于小百合呢？她独占着全天下最尊贵的专属岗位：全心全意只伺候藤原一个人，随时准备着用她那温暖柔软的口腔或饱满挺翘的丰臀来取悦主人。

Back in the cozy, chaotic private penthouse of Fresh Mart, the terrifying global shadow-rulers were currently gathered around the dining table, bickering over mundane household matters with all the chaotic warmth of a completely dysfunctional family.

此刻在“新鲜玛特”温馨而混乱的私人顶层公寓里，这群掌控全球命脉的恐怖阴影巨头正围坐在餐桌旁，像一个无比奇葩却充满烟火气的家庭一样，为着鸡毛蒜皮的日常琐事拌着嘴。

"Hey, Chika, if you mismanage the Eastern Hemisphere's tax routing one more time, I'm personally flying over to smash your servers with my bat," Miyetsuko grunted casually, stealing a piece of sushi from Chika’s plate while adjusting her glasses.

“喂，千夏，你要是再把东半球的税务路由搞砸一次，老娘可就亲自飞过去用球棍砸烂你的服务器了啊，”美悦子一边推了推眼镜，一边顺手从千夏的盘子里偷走了一块寿司，大大咧咧地哼声道。

Chika giggled wildly, slapping Miyetsuko’s hand away with a playful grin. "Relax, fierce boss lady! My eastern market algorithms are printing money faster than central banks can panic. Worry about your own Western politicians—they're practically begging us to rig their upcoming elections!"

千夏夸张地咯咯娇笑，坏笑着一把拍开美悦子的手。“安啦，凶猛的老板娘！我这边的东部市场算法印钱的速度可比央行崩盘还快呢。你还是操心你西边的政客吧——他们现在正排着队求咱们去帮他们内定大选呢！”

Blind Rin sat quietly in the corner, her wide, unseeing eyes blinking innocently as she handed a fresh cup of tea to Fujiwara. "Um... hello? Are we... are we still talking about running the world? If everyone is done ruling countries today, I baked some cookies... Please don't fight over politics, girls. It's time to eat."

盲人铃安安静静地坐在角落里，她那双睁得滚圆却空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，体贴地递给藤原一杯新茶。“唔……你好呀？咱们是在……在讨论统治世界的事吗？如果大家今天把国家都统治完了，我烤了点小饼干哦……女孩子们可千万别为政治吵架啦，快来吃饭吧。”

Fujiwara chuckled heartily, pulling Sayuri onto his lap as he took a bite of Rin's cookie. Sayuri blushed delicately, leaning back against his chest with a sweet, submissive smile while feeding him a grape.

藤原开怀大笑，顺手把小百合拉到自己腿上坐下，咬了一口铃烤的饼干。小百合娇羞地红了脸，带着甜美顺从的微笑靠在他的胸膛上，纤手剥了一颗葡萄喂进他嘴里。

"Let the politicians cry all they want, my dears," Fujiwara grinned, wrapping his large, powerful arm securely around Sayuri's waist. "As long as our family business is booming and my favorite little wife keeps me this happy, the entire planet can spin however it likes. Pass the wine, Chika—let's toast to our delightfully unhinged empire!"

“管这群政客怎么哭天喊地呢，宝贝们，”藤原咧嘴一笑，宽厚有力的大臂稳稳揽住小百合的纤腰，“只要咱们的家族生意红红火火，我最爱的小娇妻把我伺候得舒舒服服，这破地球爱怎么转就怎么转。千夏，把酒递过来——让我们为咱们这个妙趣横生又无法无天的家族帝国干杯！”
