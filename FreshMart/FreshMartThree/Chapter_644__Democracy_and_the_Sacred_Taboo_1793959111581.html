第六百四十四章：民主与神圣禁忌

The imperial administrative monitoring center was beaming live holographic data of the stellar general elections across ninety-six galactic sectors. 帝国政务监控中心正全息投射着九十六个星区正在如火如荼进行的全民大选实时数据。

Citizens of every planet were passionately utilizing direct digital ballots, transparent blockchain tallies, and intense parliamentary debates to elect governors, adjust local tax brackets, and impeach underperforming municipal bureaucrats. 各大行星的公民们正狂热地使用去中心化数字投票、透明的区块链计票以及火药味十足的议会辩论，自主选举星区总督、调整地方税率，甚至弹劾业绩不达标的市政长官。

Everything carried the polish of an ultra-advanced, civilized, and model participatory democracy—until one candidate made a fatal rhetorical misstep. 一切都闪耀着高度发达、文明且典范式的现代民主光辉——直到某位激进的竞选议员在辩论中犯了一个致命的口误。

On the main feed, a passionate orator bellowed into the microphone: "Our regional transit deficit has ballooned because the Crown’s private pleasure barge occupied forty percent of the equatorial launch budget! The palace must be audited!" 主屏幕上，那位慷慨激昂的政客正对着麦克风嘶吼：“咱们地方轨道交通赤字之所以爆炸，全是因为皇室的私人享乐飞艇霸占了四成的赤道发射预算！皇室必须接受公开审计！”

Before the sentence even fully registered with the audience, a flash of red automated beams dissected the speaker into eighteen precise, steaming cubes, and the entire platform dropped into a pit of molten plasma. 这番话甚至还没在选民脑子里转完一圈，一道刺目的自动化赤红光束便瞬间将该候选人精准切割成十八块冒着热气的碎肉，整座竞选演讲台当场塌陷跌入滚烫的等离子熔岩深渊。

A soothing robotic broadcast immediately overrode the feed: "Candidate #4 has experienced a terminal compliance adjustment for Sovereign Irreverence. Please resume voting for your remaining candidates." 轻柔的播音腔随即接管了转播信号：“四号候选人因冒犯至尊已执行终末合规调整。请广大选民继续为其余候选人投下您神圣的一票。”

Inside the grand imperial salon, Emperor Fujiwara was comfortably reclining with his head pillowed on Miyetsuko's silk-covered thighs, lazily swiping through the voter turnout stats. 大起居殿内，藤原大帝正舒舒服服地把脑袋枕在美悦子那裹着黑肉丝的温软大腿上，懒洋洋地滑动着各星区的选民投票率数据。

"Look at our citizens enjoying genuine liberty," Fujiwara chuckled, tossing a sweet plum into his mouth. "They can elect their leaders, vote to build public parks, lower business tariffs, and even petition to turn waste treatment plants into laser tag arenas. We are practically the pinnacle of civic enlightenment!" “瞧瞧咱们治下这群享受着真民主的子民，”藤原随手把一颗甜梅丢进嘴里，咧嘴坏笑道，“他们能自己选长官，投票建市民公园、降企业税，甚至还能联名把垃圾处理厂改成全息激光枪射击馆。咱们这文明高度，放眼全宇宙那也是公民自由的珠穆朗玛峰啊！”

Chika poured herself a glass of vintage spiced wine, snorting with amusement as her bountiful chest heaved against her tight bodice. 千夏给自己倒了杯年份香料酒，胸前饱满的巨乳随着嗤笑剧烈颤动，毫不留情地戳破了西洋镜。

"Yeah, an enlightened civilization right until someone mentions your luxury fleet," Chika mocked, resting her elbows on the armrest. "One second it's a sterile parliamentary democracy debating environmental carbon credits, and the next second it’s an ancient sacrificial cult feeding dissenters to plasma pyres. The mood swings in this empire are giving the historians whiplash." “可不是嘛，文明典范得不得了——只要没人不长眼提起您老人家那支声色犬马的享乐舰队，”千夏倚着扶手打趣道，“前一秒还是衣冠楚楚的代议制议会，在那儿正儿八经辩论环保碳积分呢；后一秒直接一键切换成上古活人祭祀邪教，把提意见的当场丢进等离子火坑。这精神分裂式的反差感，怕是把全星系的史官脑浆都给干抽搐了。”

Sayuri lightly traced the edge of her velvet fan along Fujiwara’s cheek, her lips curving into a dry, aristocratic smirk. 小百合用丝绒折扇的扇柄轻轻划过藤原的脸颊，唇角泛着清冷又促狭的贵族式浅笑。

"It is a brilliant socio-political balance, my Lord," Sayuri remarked smoothly. "Give the masses real authority over garbage collection, zoning laws, and school boards, and they feel fully empowered. But maintain the royal household as an absolute, untouchable deity. If they criticize a governor, it’s healthy politics; if they look at your palace sideways, they get skinned alive. Fear and freedom, operating in perfect cosmic equilibrium." “这恰恰是最高明的政治平衡术呀，夫君，”小百合软糯地剖析道，“把垃圾清运、街区规划、学区划分这些鸡毛蒜皮的真正决定权全下放给贱民，他们便真觉得自己当家做主了；而皇室则永远保持高高在上、不可直视的神祇图腾地位。骂总督叫公民监督，多看皇宫一眼那叫满门抄斩。恐怖与自由齐飞，两手抓得稳稳当当。”

Miyetsuko adjusted her 183828号 Death God glasses with a gloved finger, her heels lightly tapping the floorboards. 美悦子用戴着蕾丝手套的指尖扶了扶183828号死神眼镜，黑色细高跟在木地板上轻轻敲打着节奏。

"From a bureaucratic standpoint, it keeps the execution registry surprisingly clean," Miyetsuko noted coldly. "The citizens are busy arguing over local school budgets and property taxes ninety-nine percent of the time. We only have to dispatch the dismemberment drones once or twice a week whenever an idiot forgets the taboo. Highly cost-effective governance." “从冥界卷宗和内廷行政治理的口径来看，这反而极大精简了死刑名单，”美悦子冷冰冰地附和道，“平时百分之九十九的时间里，老百姓都在为学区预算和物业管理费吵得不可开交；只有极少数脑子被驴踢了的蠢货忘了不可言说的绝对底线时，咱们才需要动用一次肢解无人机。综合治理成本低廉得令人发指。”

Rin chuckled, swishing her pointed succubus tail across the obsidian armrest. 小铃倚在扶手边咯咯直笑，带尖刺的魅魔尾巴在黑曜石扶手上扫来扫去。

"It’s like living in a super-advanced laboratory where everyone is a licensed scientist, but they all still worship an angry, horny volcano god at the center of the island!" Rin giggled, bouncing playfully. "Vote for your neighborhood council all you want, but don't forget to toss virgins and tribute to the Emperor or the sky fires up!" “这感觉就像在一个超现代的顶尖科学实验室里，人人都是持证科学家，结果大家在实验室正中央居然供奉着一尊脾气暴躁又极度好色的活火山邪神！”小铃嘻嘻哈哈地蹦跶着，“邻里委员会你们随便选，但要是敢少给火山暴君上供绝色美女，天火分分钟把你们全家扬咯！”

Fujiwara burst into booming laughter, pulling Miyetsuko down by the neck for a messy, open-mouthed kiss before slapping Chika’s thigh with unrestrained delight. 藤原大帝听罢仰天狂笑，一把揽住美悦子的后颈狠狠深吻了一口，又肆无忌惮地一巴掌拍在千夏滑腻肥美的玉腿上，乐得合不拢嘴。

"A volcano god! Exactly!" Fujiwara declared grandly, leaning back into the throne. "Let the mayors debate recycling quotas until they're blue in the face! As long as they remember that the throne is absolute and our beds are kept full of prime tributes, they can have all the participatory democracy their little hearts desire!" “火山邪神！说得太他妈贴切了！”藤原放荡不羁地大笑起来，“让这帮市长议员们为了垃圾分类配额争到面红耳赤去吧！只要他们牢牢记住至尊皇权不可指摘、按时往朕的龙榻上进贡极品绝色，他们想要多少民主自决，朕就赏他们多少！”
