第二百三十一章：家庭扩招计划的破产

Rin's open confession about her midnight esports hotel tryst didn't cause a scandal; instead, it acted like a match thrown into a powder keg of chaotic inspiration.

铃对昨晚电竞酒店之行的坦白不仅没有引发众怒，反而像是一根火柴，瞬间点燃了屋里这群女人们跃跃欲试的八卦之魂。

Hearing how casually external liaisons were being discussed, Chika’s eyes lit up. She puffed out her chest in her signature open-crotch maid uniform and chimed in with a bright, cheerful grin.

一听到外面的野路子居然这么好玩，千夏顿时来了兴趣。她挺了挺穿着招牌开裆女仆装的身子，笑嘻嘻地举手提议：

"Well, if Rin-onee-chan can go find a childhood friend, maybe I should head out and scout a few hookup buddies of my own!" Chika giggled. "It’s about time I expanded my social circle!"

“既然铃姐姐都能去找青梅竹马叙旧，那要不我也趁着周末出去找几个高质量的炮友玩玩呗，”千夏笑得一脸天真烂漫，“正好也拓展一下人脉嘛！”

Before Fujiwara could even process Chika's bombshell, Sayuri offered a gentle, incredibly dangerous smile, casually stirring her tea.

还没等藤原消化完千夏这番虎狼之词，一旁的小百合忽然优雅地搅拌着茶杯，掩嘴轻笑着补了一刀：

"Speaking of which... back when I was still active in the industry, there were a few veteran male actors whose technical skills were genuinely exceptional," Sayuri murmured thoughtfully, her eyes twinkling with mischief. "Perhaps I should give one of them a quick call to catch up. I hear they're still in great shape."

“说起来……当年我在业界的时候，确实有几个搭档过的男优技术相当不错，”小百合眼底闪过一丝恶趣味的笑意，慢条斯理地说道，“要不，哪天我也联系他们叙叙旧？听说他们现在的状态保养得还挺好的呢。”

Across the table, Miyetsuko didn't even look up from her heavy sociological tome. Deeply engrossed in academic theories, she offered a cold, detached assessment without sparing them a single glance.

餐桌的另一端，美悦子连头都懒得从厚厚的社会学论文里抬起来。她对这种低级趣味毫无兴趣，冷冰冰地抛下一句评价：

"A degenerative collective slide into promiscuity," Miyetsuko muttered dryly, pushing up her glasses. "I remain intellectually and physically divorced from such primitive, chaotic mating rituals. Please proceed with your moral decay without me."

“一群集体陷入动物性繁衍冲动的废料，”美悦子冷酷地推了推眼镜，“本厅长醉心学术，对这种低级且混乱的灵长类交配游戏毫无兴趣。请你们继续沉沦，别带上我。”

Hearing Chika and Sayuri casually tossing around the idea of outside partners, Fujiwara’s brain instantly went into DEFCON 1 panic mode. He slammed his hands down on the table, instantly pivoting into a strict, tyrannical household dictator.

听到千夏和自家正宫居然双双动了向外发展的邪念，藤原体内的危机雷达瞬间拉响到最高级别。他“啪”的一声拍案而起，瞬间切换成了暴君模式：

"Hold on just a minute!" Fujiwara glared at Chika. "Chika, you are nothing more than an open-crotch maid of the lowest administrative tier! You belong strictly to the household inventory—you have zero sovereign privileges to go looking for outside hookups! Motion denied!"

“等一下！反了你们了！”藤原指着千夏义正言辞地痛斥道，“千夏你不过是个底层编制的开裆女仆，身份卑微，吃穿用度全靠家里，根本没有任何去外面找野男人的特权！驳回申请！”

Having swiftly crushed Chika’s rebellion, Fujiwara immediately spun around to face Sayuri, his tyrannical dictator persona evaporating into thin air, replaced by a pathetic, teary-eyed beggar.

秒杀完千夏后，藤原脸上的暴君神情瞬间无缝切换，惨兮兮地扑向小百合，秒变痛哭流涕的摇尾乞怜模式。

"And you... my absolute queen, my goddess..." Fujiwara wailed, grabbing Sayuri's hands and burying his face in her lap. "Please, I'm begging you, have mercy! Those AV guys you worked with are absolute monsters—they're built like industrial pile drivers! If you let them touch you, they'll stretch you out and ruin your tightness! Next time, you won't be snug anymore! Who am I going to cling to then?!"

“还有你……我的女王大人，我的亲生真神啊，”藤原一把抱住小百合的大腿，假惺惺地挤出两滴眼泪哀嚎道，“求求你放过我吧！你当年合作的那些家伙一个个都是人形挖掘机，家伙大得离谱！真让他们给撑开了，把你那宝贝小穴弄松了，以后哥哥我用起来不紧致了可怎么办呀！呜呜呜，你这是要断了我的幸福源泉啊！”

The entire kitchen exploded into uproarious laughter at his shameless, vulgar desperation.

听着他这番充满市井气又极其无耻的直白哀求，餐厅里顿时爆发出了一阵掀翻屋顶的狂笑。

As for Sayuri, even with all her legendary composure and years of experience facing cameras, she never expected her reckless husband to broadcast such a private, humiliatingly crude anatomical concern right out in the open. A brilliant, burning crimson blush rushed up her neck, painting her face bright red.

至于小百合本人——纵使她当年贵为殿堂级巨星，见惯了大风大浪，也绝对没想到自家这个没脸没皮的男人居然会把这种极其私密、甚至带点粗俗的生理担忧当众嚎叫出来。一抹滚烫娇艳的绯红瞬间从脖子根一路烧到了耳尖，羞得她连耳根都红透了。

"You... you absolute idiot..." Sayuri stammered, her cheeks burning as she lightly slapped his head, unable to hide the sweet, flustered smile breaking through her embarrassment. "Stop saying such shameful things in front of everyone!"

“你……你个没正经的死变态……”小百合羞恼交加地拍了拍他的脑袋，娇嗔着想要掩饰内心的窘迫，“大庭广众之下，瞎说什么浑话呢快闭嘴！”
