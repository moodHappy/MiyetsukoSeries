第四百六章：触觉生意

The back room of Fresh Mart had practically transformed into an advanced cybernetic laboratory, reeking of hot solder and unhinged capitalistic ambition. Fujiwara was staring at GPT’s technical breakdown of haptic feedback and neural tactile encoding with the glowing, dilated eyes of a man who had just discovered the absolute pinnacle of human exploitation.

“新鲜玛特”的后仓几乎变成了一个散发着电烙铁热气与疯狂资本野心的前沿赛博实验室。藤原正盯着关于触觉反馈和神经触觉编码的技术拆解，双眼放光、瞳孔放大，宛如一个刚刚发现了人类剥削终极巅峰的狂热赌徒。

"Hear that, team?!" Fujiwara cackled, slamming his fist onto the desk. "GPT says it's entirely possible! Pressure, friction, temperature, and proprioception can all be digitized, transmitted, and re-stimulated! The haptic internet is officially here!"

“听到没，团队们？！”藤原一巴掌拍在桌上狂笑道，“GPT说了，这完全行得通！压力、摩擦、温度和本体感觉全都可以数字化、传输并重新刺激！触觉互联网正式登场了！”

Miyetsuko snapped her head up from her tablet, slamming her iron baseball bat against the concrete floor with a deafening bang. Her expression twisted into a terrifying, aggressive scowl as she pointed the tip of the bat right between his eyes.

美悦子猛地从平板前抬起头，手中的铁棒球棍重重砸向水泥地面，伴随着震耳欲聋的巨响，她的表情变得无比凶狠暴躁，直接把球棍尖端指向了藤原的眉心。

"Stop drooling over your cybernetic wet dreams, you worthless degenerate!" Miyetsuko barked, her voice dripping with lethal contempt. "If you think you're going to waste corporate bandwidth on your pathetic virtual fetishes instead of optimizing our expired yogurt supply chains, I’m going to use a neural interface to make you experience the sensation of being hit by a freight train at Mach 2!"

“少在这做你的赛博春梦了，你这个一文不值的死变态！”美悦子厉声喝道，声音里透着致命的蔑视，“你要是敢把企业带宽浪费在你那些可悲的虚拟癖好上，而不是去优化我们的过期酸奶供应链，老娘立刻就用神经接口让你真切体验一下被两倍音速货车撞飞的感觉！”

Blind Rin sat quietly on her stool in the corner, her wide, unseeing eyes blinking innocently. She gently raised her hands, tilting her head with a soft, timid smile.

盲人铃静静地坐在角落的凳子上，她那双睁得滚红却空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨。她有些羞怯地抬起双手，微微歪着头，露出了一个温柔而胆怯的微笑。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered gently, her voice like a soft breeze. "Are we... are we talking about long-distance handshakes? If someone is far away and feeling lonely, I can weave a soft blanket for their haptic suit... Please don't fight, everyone. It makes me feel a little nervous."

“唔……你好呀？”铃轻声呢喃着，声音如同柔和的微风，“我们是在……在讨论远程握手吗？如果有人在远方觉得孤单，我可以为他们的触觉服织一条柔软的毯子……大家请不要吵架好不好，我有点紧张呢。”

Sayuri stood right beside Rin, gently patting the blind girl's shoulder with an expression of supreme, submissive warmth. She turned her gentle, loving gaze down toward Fujiwara, smiling sweetly.

小百合温柔地站在铃身旁，脸上带着极致顺从与温暖的表情，轻轻拍着盲人女孩的肩膀。接着，她将温柔深情的目光投向藤原，甜甜地笑了笑。

"Oh, my visionary manager," Sayuri murmured affectionately. "Your grand schemes always open up such... exotic market segments. If touch can be converted into digital signals, we have officially found the ultimate solution for long-distance romance."

“哦，我那有远见的经理呀，”小百合柔情似水地呢喃道，“你的宏伟计划总是能开辟出如此……别具一格的市场板块呢。如果触觉能转换成数字信号，咱们可就正式找到了异地恋的终极救星啦。”

Fujiwara’s grin widened into something truly grotesque. He rubbed his hands together greedily.

藤原的咧嘴大笑变得愈发诡异怪诞。他贪婪地搓了搓手。

"Exactly, Sayuri! This is the absolute salvation of long-distance romance!" Fujiwara cackled. "The ultimate gospel for remote prostitution! Think about it: those poor, isolated folks stuck out in the rural countryside can now taste the absolute highest-tier, luxurious hospitality of Donglai Island's legendary club headliners—right from the comfort of their own haptic suits! Exquisite, fragrant, and entirely digital!"

“没错，小百合！这就是异地恋的绝对救星！”藤原狂笑道，“更是远程嫖娼的终极福音！你想想看：那些被困在偏远乡下的可怜孤单汉子们，现在只要套上触觉服，就能在家里品尝到东莱岛最顶级的会所头牌们带来的极致服务了！香艳无比、沉浸感拉满，而且完全走数字化路线！”

Chika giggled wildly from behind her tablet, rapidly tapping out a brand-new, highly illegal business blueprint.

千夏则躲在平板后面疯狂地咯咯娇笑，手脚麻利地敲打着一份全新的、极度违法的商业蓝图。

"Brilliant!" Chika cheered, her eyes sparkling with data-driven greed. "I’ll set up the 'Donglai Island Cyber-Brothel & Haptic Hostess Network' right now! We'll charge a hundred bucks a minute for real-time proprioceptive feedback. Let's see how fast the villagers bankrupt themselves for virtual romance!"

“太妙了！”千夏欢呼道，双眼里闪烁着数据驱动的贪婪光芒，“我马上就去搭建‘东莱岛赛博红灯区与触觉头牌网络’！实时本体感觉反馈，每分钟收一百块。快算算，这群村民为了虚拟爱情破产的速度能有多快！”
