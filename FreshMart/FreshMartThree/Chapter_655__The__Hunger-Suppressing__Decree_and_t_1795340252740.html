Chapter 655: The "Hunger-Suppressing" Decree and the Broadcast Cut

发布时间：2026/11/22 17:37:30

第六百五十五章：“以爱充饥”新政与直播切断

The flickering candlelight in the dim dining hall suddenly took on a feverish, chaotic energy as Emperor Fujiwara tossed his silver fork aside, his eyes gleaming with a familiar, reckless impulsiveness.

昏暗餐厅里摇曳的烛火骤然染上了一层躁动而荒唐的气息，藤原大帝随手将银叉掷在一旁，眼底闪烁着众人再熟悉不过的狂妄与胡闹劲头。

"A revelation!" Fujiwara declared loudly, slamming his palm onto the obsidian table while the holographic broadcast drones hovered just inches away, beaming his face to billions of households. "Look at the impoverished masses watching us! If food and clothing are scarce, how should a loyal imperial subject endure the hardship? Through passionate intimacy! Let us inaugurate a glorious new tradition for the Festival of Leftovers—satisfying hunger through affection, while simultaneously boosting the empire's birth rate!"

“朕忽然顿悟了一桩治国大道！”藤原猛地一拍黑曜石餐桌，高声宣告，半空中的直播无人机正嗡嗡悬停在咫尺之外，将他的面孔实时传遍千家万户，“瞧瞧屏幕前亿万饥寒交迫的黎民！若是衣食不足、腹中空空，我帝国忠良子民当如何度过难关？自当以热烈的闺房之爱来充饥解乏！今夜，朕便要为这残羹节开创一项光辉的新传统——用亲热抵御饥饿，顺道一举拉高帝国的出生率！”

Before Chika could even swallow her bite of reheated steak, Fujiwara lunged across the table, his large hands brazenly grabbing onto her waist and pulling her straight onto his lap, sending her tattered peasant blouse slipping dangerously off her shoulders.

千夏嘴里那口回锅牛排还没来得及咽下去，藤原便已如猛虎扑食般越过桌面，大掌毫不客气地一把扣住她的纤腰，将她整个人硬生生拽进怀里，扯得她身上那件破旧农妇衫顺着香肩滑落了一大片。

"Hey! Are you completely out of your mind?! The red recording light is still blinking, you maniac!" Chika yelled, swatting wildly at his chest while her face flushed crimson—not out of shyness, but from sheer exasperation at his total disregard for state protocol.

“喂！你是不是彻底疯了？！头顶上那盏红彤彤的直播录制灯还在闪呢，你个不知羞耻的混蛋！”千夏一边满脸通红地大喊，一边挥拳猛捶他的胸口——她这脸红倒不是因为害羞，纯粹是被这暴君毫无底线的操作给气炸了肺。

"Let the whole universe watch and learn!" Fujiwara laughed heartily, completely unfazed as he leaned in to nuzzle her neck, waving his free hand toward Sayuri, Miyetsuko, and Rin. "Come, my beloved consorts! Join the sovereign demonstration! Show the citizenry how the royal family turns poverty into pure, unbridled fertility!"

“让全宇宙好好看着、好好学着！”藤原浑不在意地放声狂笑，低头凑在她颈窝边肆意蹭弄，腾出另一只手朝小百合、美悦子与小铃大刺刺地招手，“来来来，爱妃们！一同加入朕的御前示范！让天下子民见识见识，皇室是如何将清贫日子过成热火朝天的生育大业的！”

Sayuri’s serene smile instantly froze, a faint twitch appearing at the corner of her eye before she gracefully snapped her velvet fan shut.

小百合脸上那抹温婉端庄的浅笑瞬间凝固，眼角微不可察地抽搐了一下，随即“啪”地一声利落合拢丝绒折扇。

"As inspiring as a live imperial fertility seminar sounds, my Lord," Sayuri said in a dangerously sweet, icy voice, "broadcasting bedroom acrobatics to forty billion slum households will cause the planetary power grids to crash from viewer spikes, not to mention ruining our carefully curated 'ascetic ruler' PR campaign."

“虽说一场现场直播的皇室生育教学听起来确实很鼓舞人心，夫君，”小百合用一种甜得发腻却又冰冷刺骨的语调开口，“但对着四十亿贫民窟住户直播龙榻杂技，不仅会导致各星区电网因收视率暴增而彻底瘫痪，更会毁掉我们精心包装的‘苦行明君’公关人设。”

Miyetsuko didn't even bother arguing. She simply adjusted her 183828号 Death God glasses, raised her administrative stylus, and fired a sharp burst of electromagnetic static directly at the hovering broadcast drones.

美悦子连半句废话都懒得多说。她只是冷静地推了推鼻梁上的183828号死神眼镜，抬起手中的冥界触控笔，对着半空中的直播无人机直接点出一道尖锐的电磁干扰脉冲。

ZAP—! FIZZLE!

滋啦——！啪！

The crimson recording lights died instantly, the drones plummeting onto the carpet in a shower of harmless sparks as the empire-wide feed abruptly cut to a polite black screen reading: "Technical Difficulties: The Imperial Family is Currently Engaged in Deep Contemplation."

猩红的录制指示灯瞬间熄灭，几架无人机冒着几缕无害的电火花一头栽倒在地毯上，全星系的直播画面随之骤然切断，变成了一块礼貌的黑屏公告：“信号调试中：皇室成员正处于深度冥想反思状态。”

"Party poopers!" Fujiwara grumbled, though he didn't stop pulling Chika down onto the plush rug beside the table.

“真是一群扫兴的丫头！”藤原嘴上嘟囔着，手上的动作却半点没停，直接搂着千夏顺势滚倒在餐桌旁的厚实绒毯上。

"Well, if the cameras are off, I suppose we don't have to pretend to be starving anymore," Rin giggled mischievously, kicking off her tattered oversized shirt and diving straight onto the pile with her succubus tail swishing wildly. "Move over, Chika! If we're doing 'hunger-suppressing' exercises, I'm not letting you hog the main course!"

“既然镜头都关了，那咱们总算不用继续装穷饿肚子啦！”小铃顽皮地咯咯娇笑，一脚踢飞身上那件宽大的破衬衫，魅魔尾巴欢快地甩动着，直接飞身扑进了绒毯的人堆里，“往边上挪挪，千夏！既然要做‘以爱充饥’的运动，我可不会让你一个人独吞主菜！”

Sayuri sighed softly, shaking her head with aristocratic resignation as she unfastened the remaining clasps of her tattered evening gown, letting the silk pool around her ankles while Miyetsuko calmly locked the heavy golden doors of the dining hall from the inside.

小百合无奈地轻叹一声，带着权贵特有的纵容摇了摇头，纤手解开那身破旧晚礼服仅剩的几枚搭扣，任由滑腻的绸缎顺着脚踝滑落；而一旁的美悦子则面无表情地反手锁死了餐厅沉重的鎏金大门，将一切荒唐与喧嚣尽数隔绝在了深宫之内。
