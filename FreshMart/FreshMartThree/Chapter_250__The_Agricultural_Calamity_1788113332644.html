Chapter 250: The Agricultural Calamity

发布时间：2026/8/31 02:08:51

第二百五十章：农田劫难

Back from their triumphant, hedonistic "inspection tour," Fujiwara marched down the street with Sayuri on one arm and Rin on the other, grinning from ear to ear as he anticipated Miyetsuko’s reaction to their wild stories.

带着凯旋般的满足感，藤原左手挽着小百合，右手搂着铃，大摇大摆地回到了“新鲜玛特”门前。一想到马上就能看到美悦子得知他们昨晚在情趣酒店夜夜笙歌时的精彩表情，他脸上的坏笑就根本收不住。

But as they stepped up to the porch, they found a grim reality waiting for them: the front door was bolted shut with a heavy padlock, and a stern official notice was pasted across the glass.

然而，当他们走到门口时，眼前的景象却让他们当场傻眼——大门紧闭，上面不仅挂着一把大锁，还贴着一张镇政府的红色紧急通知。

"What in the world...?" Fujiwara blinked, stepping forward to peel the notice off.

“搞什么名堂……？”藤原纳闷地上前撕下通知。

Right at that moment, the back alley gate creaked open. Out stepped Miyetsuko, and the sight of her sent Fujiwara and the girls into immediate, uncontrollable fits of laughter.

就在这时，后院的木门“吱呀”一声开了。美悦子从里面走了出来。当看清她此刻的模样时，藤原三人瞬间笑得前俯后仰，差点背过气去。

The usually pristine, immaculate academic queen looked like she had just survived a multi-front combat zone. Her hair was tied back in a messy, chaotic bun with stray strands clinging to her sweaty forehead, her designer blouse was covered in smudges of stubborn mud, and she was dragging a heavy wooden hoe behind her like a battle-weary peasant.

只见这位平日里高贵优雅、纤尘不染的冰山学霸，此刻狼狈得简直判若两人。她精致的发髻凌乱不堪，几缕被汗水浸湿的发丝贴在额头上，名贵衬衫上沾满了洗不掉的泥点子，手里还拖着一把沉甸甸的农用锄头，活像个刚从水深火热的前线撤下来的苦力。

"You... you idiots are finally back," Miyetsuko rasped, dropping her hoe with a dull thud and glaring at them with bloodshot eyes.

“你们这群没良心的混蛋……总算舍得滚回来了啊！”美悦子气喘吁吁地把锄头往地上一扔，顶着两个大大的黑眼圈，咬牙切齿地瞪着他们。

"Goodness, Miyetsuko! What on earth happened to you? Did you get drafted into an agricultural re-education camp?" Rin cackled, clutching her stomach as tears of amusement streamed down her face.

“哎呀我的妈呀！美悦子姐，你这是刚从哪个原始部落逃难回来？怎么沦落成这副难民样了？”铃笑得直不起腰，眼泪都快飙出来了。

Miyetsuko let out a bitter, exhausted sigh, leaning against the doorframe. "It’s that damn town conscription policy. Right after you guys left for your little decadent getaway, government officials knocked on the door claiming the agricultural harvest is facing an unprecedented labor shortage. Every household on the town registry was forced to send someone for compulsory farm work. Since I was the only one at home, guess who got press-ganged into the paddy fields?"

美悦子痛苦地扶着门框，生无可恋地哀叹道：“还不是该死的镇政府强制征召令！就在你们前脚刚走，乡里的人就挨家挨户敲门，说今年农忙人手极度短缺，全镇所有户籍人口必须无条件服从义务劳动。家里当时就我一个人在家，你们说谁被抓去当壮丁了？！”

Hearing that the high-and-mighty department director had spent the last few days knee-deep in mud, pulling weeds and harvesting crops while they were partying in a luxury suite, Fujiwara and the girls practically exploded with glee.

一听堂堂高冷的美悦子厅长居然顶着大太阳在地里当了好几天面朝黄土背朝天的农妇，而他们三个却在情趣酒店里左拥右抱、夜夜笙歌，藤原三人当场笑得快要把房顶给掀翻了。

"Oh, sweet merciful heavens!" Fujiwara howled, wiping a tear from his eye as he patted Miyetsuko’s muddy shoulder. "That is poetic justice! While you were doing Marxist physical labor in the mud, we were living like emperors! You seriously missed out, Director—you should've seen how Sayuri and Rin worked those hips last night—"

“哈哈哈哈……苍天有眼啊！”藤原笑得肚子抽筋，幸灾乐祸地拍了拍美悦子沾满泥土的肩膀，“美悦子啊美悦子，这叫什么？这就叫报应！我们在外面当皇帝享福，你倒好，在家里体验社会主义农村大改造！我要是你，现在肯定气得想跳河了！”

"Shut up, you disgusting pervert," Miyetsuko hissed, though her face flushed bright red with a mix of exhaustion and absolute humiliation. "If I had known you were out there wasting taxpayer money on hostesses while I was sweating bullets pulling weeds, I would have used my academic connections to blacklist every single love hotel on the island!"

“闭嘴吧你个精虫上脑的死变态！”美悦子气急败坏地啐了一口，苍白的脸上泛起一抹由于羞恼而产生的红晕，“我要是早知道你们在外面花天酒地，我哪怕动用全岛学术界的权限，也得把那家破酒店给连夜查封了！”
