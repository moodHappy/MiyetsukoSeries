Chapter 475: The Royal Lactation Venture

发布时间：2026/10/7 09:48:13

第四百七十五章：皇家母乳养生大业

Just when the household thought the era of eccentric longevity schemes had finally ended with the red dates and the lost micro-device, Fujiwara went down another ancient health rabbit hole.

正当全家人都以为，伴随着红枣和那颗失落的微型仪器，帝王那些千奇百怪的养生长寿计划终于告一段落时，藤原又一头扎进了古代医典的偏门冷知识里。

Early Monday morning, while lounging with an antique medical compendium, he stumbled across ancient imperial records documenting human breast milk used as a supreme tonic—a secret panacea coveted by emperors for eternal youth, cellular rejuvenation, and unmatched vitality.

周一清早，他翻阅着一本古医典线装残卷，偶然读到了古代宫廷将人乳视作极品滋补圣药的秘录——那曾是历代帝王为了永葆青春、延缓衰老梦寐以求的长生秘药。

"A natural elixir rich in active immunoglobulins and primal life energy," Fujiwara declared grandly, pacing the marble living room in his silk dressing gown. "Pure, unprocessed, direct from the source. This is the missing link in my longevity portfolio."

“富含活性免疫球蛋白与先天元气的天然琼浆，”藤原身裹真丝睡袍，在大理石客厅里大步来回踱步，神采飞扬地宣布，“纯天然、零加工、产地直供。这正是我的长生大业里缺失的关键拼图。”

The catch, of course, was purely biological: genuine lactation required pregnancy. Fujiwara was notoriously disgusted by commercial wet nurses or outside suppliers—to him, strangers lacked the pristine genetics and flawless hygiene of his household. Only his own women qualified as top-tier, imperial-grade stock.

然而摆在面前的问题纯粹是生理层面的：真正的乳汁分泌需要以妊娠为前提。藤原向来极其嫌弃外面的奶妈或商业供应商——在他眼里，外人根本没有自家后宫那无可挑剔的极品基因与绝对的卫生标准。唯有这几个女人，才配得上充当最高规格的皇家御供。

He ruled out Sayuri immediately. The newly stripped "Red Date Queen" was still recovering from weeks of rear-end experiments, and even Fujiwara's bottomless appetite had a sliver of mercy for his undisputed bedroom favorite. Miyetsuko’s icy aura made the prospect of maternal domesticity look like a tactical minefield, and blind Rin was far too unpredictable.

他第一时间就把小百合排除了。刚被剥夺一切虚名的前皇后才刚从连续几周的后庭试验里缓过劲来，哪怕是藤原那毫无底线的索取欲，对自己最宠爱的枕边人也尚存一丝难得的怜香惜玉。至于美悦子，她那一身冰冷的杀手戾气让“慈爱哺乳”看起来像个战术雷区；而盲人小铃又实在太过天马行空。

His sharp, predatory eyes slowly swept the room and locked squarely onto Chika, who was happily typing on her financial terminal while slurping strawberry yogurt.

他那锐利如猎鹰般的目光在客厅里缓缓扫过，最终死死锁定在了千夏身上。此时的千夏正一边吸溜着草莓酸奶，一边对着财务终端键盘敲得飞起。

"Chika," Fujiwara announced, pointing a finger with theatrical solemnity. "The empire has selected its primary biological asset. It's time for you to bear the syndicate's next heir, open up a whole new operational branch, and provide your Emperor with fresh, daily, five-star nutrition."

“千夏，”藤原戏剧化地竖起手指，庄严宣告，“帝国已经选定了首要生物资产。该轮到你来为辛迪加孕育下一任继承人、开拓全新业务线，并为你的帝王每日提供新鲜出炉的五星级口粮了。”

Chika choked on her yogurt, coughing violently as she slammed her spoon onto the table.

千夏一口酸奶猛地呛在喉咙里，剧烈咳嗽着，勺子“啪”地一声摔在桌上。

"Excuse me?!" Chika shrieked, clutching her chest and staring at him in utter disbelief. "A biological asset?! Boss, are you turning me into a high-yield dairy cooperative?! Why me?! Sayuri-nee is the traditional homemaker! Her maternal metrics are at least three times higher than mine!"

“哈？！你说什么？！”千夏扯着嗓子尖叫起来，抓着胸口满脸难以置信，“生物资产？！老板，你这是打算把我改造成高产奶牛合作社吗？！凭什么是我啊？！小百合姐才是正统贤妻良母！她的母性指标起码比我高出三倍不止吧！”

Sayuri, sitting peacefully with her morning tea, offered a soft, angelic smile that concealed a wave of sheer, profound relief.

正安安静静喝着早茶的小百合露出了天使般柔和的微笑，眼底却藏着一股如释重负的侥幸。

"Oh, sweetie, I've already served my time on the frontlines for Master's longevity experiments," Sayuri purred smoothly, waving a delicate hand. "My rear was occupied for weeks by dried agricultural products and vibrating space-age technology. It is only fair under corporate equity that the younger generation shoulders the next phase of R&D."

“哎呀，好妹妹，为了主人的长生试验，姐姐已经在前线奉献够多了呢，”小百合轻描淡写地挥了挥纤纤玉手，嗓音软糯，“人家的后门被农产品和太空级震动设备连续征用了好几个星期。按照企业公平原则，这下一阶段的研发重担，理应由年轻一辈挑起大梁呀。”

"Corporate equity my ass!" Chika protested furiously, spinning around to face Fujiwara with her hands on her hips. "Do you have any idea how much economic damage a nine-month pregnancy will do to my productivity?! Who is going to short European sovereign debt while I'm dealing with morning sickness?! Who is going to aggressively hostile-takeover biotech startups while my chest expands two cup sizes?!"

“企业公平个鬼啊！”千夏气得双手叉腰，猛地转过身冲着藤原抗议，“你到底懂不懂怀胎九月对我的生产力会造成多大的经济损失？！我孕吐的时候谁去帮咱们做空欧洲主权债务？！我胸围暴涨两个罩杯的时候谁去恶意并购生物科技初创企业？！”

Miyetsuko scoffed from her corner, cleaning the sights on an assault rifle without looking up.

美悦子在角落里擦拭着突击步枪的瞄准镜，头也不抬地冷嗤了一声。

"Expanding two cup sizes sounds like a significant tactical upgrade for you, small-fry," Miyetsuko commented dryly. "Right now, your chest offers zero ballistic deflection. A little volume might actually help your center of gravity."

“胸围暴涨两个罩杯，对你这种豆芽菜来说倒是战术性能的大幅升级，”美悦子冷冰冰地补刀，“就你现在那点胸围，防弹跳弹率基本为零。多点分量说不定还能稳一稳你的身体重心。”

"Nobody asked for ballistic advice on my boobs, big sis!" Chika wailed, her pigtails practically trembling with indignation.

“又没人向你请教我胸部的防弹建议，臭大姐！”千夏抓狂地哀嚎，双马尾都气得直打颤。

From the floor by the terrace, blind Rin tilted her head, blinking her wide, innocent eyes as she paused her needlework.

阳台边地毯上的盲人小铃歪了歪头，眨了眨无辜的大眼睛，停下了手里的针线活。

"Um... hello, Chika-nee?" Rin piped up softly, her sweet voice dripping with terrifyingly earnest medical curiosity. "If you become Master's personal milk farm, should I knit tiny little bottle warmers for your tops? Or maybe a special suction harness? I can help with manual extraction every morning before the markets open! My succubus grip is very thorough... not a single drop of imperial supplement will be wasted!"

“唔……大家听我说好吗，千夏姐？”小铃甜甜地轻声开口，语气里带着令人汗毛倒竖的真挚求知欲，“如果你成了主人的专属奶场，我是不是该在你的衣服上织几个保温杯套呀？或者特制的负压吸奶吊带？每天早晨开盘前我都可以帮你做人工挤压哦！我的魅魔握力可是非常到位的……绝对保证一滴皇家补品都不会浪费呢！”

"Keep your bone-crushing thumbs away from my nipples, you little psycho!" Chika screamed, throwing a plush cushion at Rin, who easily dodged it purely by hearing the air shift.

“把你那对能捏碎骨头的大拇指离我的乳头远点，你个盲眼小恶魔！”千夏尖叫着抓起靠垫就砸了过去，而小铃仅凭听声辨位就轻巧地侧头躲开了。

Fujiwara burst into a booming, triumphant laugh, stepping forward and effortlessly sweeping Chika up off her chair and into his arms. Despite her squirming and theatrical protests, a vivid, deep blush was already creeping up from her collarbone to the tips of her ears.

藤原爆发出一阵痛快淋漓的狂笑，大步跨上前，一把将千夏从转椅里捞了起来抱进怀里。尽管她在怀里扭来扭去、大呼小叫地抗议，但一抹鲜艳夺目的绯红早已从她的锁骨一路蔓延到了耳尖。

"The decision of the Board is unanimous and non-negotiable," Fujiwara grinned, tapping her nose playfully. "You're always bragging about high yields and premium quarterly dividends, Chika. It's time to put your own biology on the balance sheet. Now come along—the Emperor is ready to make the initial seed investment."

“董事会决议全票通过，不接受任何上诉，”藤原坏笑着刮了刮她的鼻子，“你天天吹嘘高收益、大手笔的季度分红，千夏。现在是时候把你的身体指标正式计入资产负债表了。走吧——帝王已经准备好注入第一笔‘天使投资’了。”
