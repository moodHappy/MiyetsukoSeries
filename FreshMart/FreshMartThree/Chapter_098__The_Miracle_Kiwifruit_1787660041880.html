Chapter 98: The Miracle Kiwifruit

发布时间：2026/8/25 20:14:00

第九十八章：神鸣猕猴桃的奇迹

The morning sun had barely cleared the horizon when the glass door of "Fresh Mart" was thrown open with a dramatic, joyful bang.

清晨的阳光刚越过地平线，“新鲜玛特”的玻璃门便被人带着激动而狂喜的力道猛地推开了。

A middle-aged customer marched into the store, looking like he had just won a multi-million-dollar lottery. In his hands, he carried an elaborate, red-ribboned gift basket filled with high-end pastries, marching straight up to the counter where Fujiwara was lazily wiping down the glass.

一个中年顾客大步流星地走进了店里，脸上洋溢着仿佛中了彩票般的狂喜。他手里捧着一个系着红绸带的高档礼品篮，里面塞满了昂贵的糕点，径直走到正懒洋洋擦拭柜台的藤原面前。

"Boss! Fujiwara-boss!" the man cried out, his voice trembling with sheer, unadulterated euphoria as he slammed the gift basket onto the counter. "I don't know what kind of divine magic you've got hidden in this shop, but you've saved my entire bloodline!"

“老板！藤原老板！”那名顾客激动得声音都在颤抖，一把将礼篮重重拍在柜台上，“我不知道你这小店里到底藏了什么神仙魔法，你这是救了我们老陈家一脉单传的大恩大德啊！”

Fujiwara paused, raising an eyebrow as he leaned back against the shelf. "Easy there, pal. Did one of our scratch-off lottery tickets actually hit the jackpot?"

藤原停下手中的动作，挑了挑眉毛靠在货架上。“冷静点，老兄。难不成咱们店里卖的刮刮乐中头奖了？”

"Better than a lottery!" the customer beamed, grabbing Fujiwara's hand and shaking it vigorously. "Three days ago, I bought a box of imported kiwifruits from your front display. My wife and I have been struggling with primary infertility for over seven years—we spent a fortune on treatments and completely lost hope. But swear to God, the very next morning after she ate two of your kiwifruits... boom! Morning sickness kicked in, and the clinic just confirmed it this morning! She’s pregnant!"

“比中彩票还带劲！”顾客咧嘴大笑着，一把抓住藤原的手用力摇晃，“三天前，我在你家门口的展篮里买了一箱进口猕猴桃。我和我老婆结婚七年多一直怀不上——大医院跑遍了、偏方吃尽了，早就彻底死心了。结果苍天有眼，她昨天连着吃了两个你们家的猕猴桃……好家伙！今天一早去医院一查，居然真的怀上了！”

The entire shop went utterly silent for a split second.

整个杂货店瞬间安静了一秒钟。

Rin, who had been stocking the beverage fridge, dropped a soda can with a loud clatter, staring at the customer like he had just announced alien contact.

正在理货的铃手一抖，“当啷”一声把一罐饮料砸在了地上，目瞪口呆地看着这位顾客，仿佛在看一个刚宣布遭遇外星人绑架的神经病。

Miyetsuko pushed her glasses up the bridge of her nose, her clinical, analytical brain screeching to an immediate, violent halt. She stepped forward, her eyes narrowing behind her lenses.

美悦子一把将眼镜推到鼻梁上，她那颗一向严谨冷静的理性大脑瞬间宕机。她迈步上前，镜片后的目光锐利地锁定对方。

"Primary infertility spontaneously resolved by actinidia deliciosa consumption?" Miyetsuko stated, her tone deadpan and deeply skeptical. "Biologically speaking, that is a statistical anomaly so extreme it borders on a miracle of molecular biology. Are you entirely certain of the physiological lineage?"

“由于食用中华猕猴桃而自愈了长期的原发性不孕不育？”美悦子语气冰冷而充满学术性质疑，“从生物学角度来看，这是一种极端到堪称分子生物学奇迹的小概率事件。你确定生理学上的溯源绝对没有出现偏差吗？”

Sayuri peeked out from behind the stock shelves, her soft face flushing a delicate shade of pink as she shyly covered her mouth, her eyes wide with bewildered amusement.

小百合从货架后探出半个脑袋，白皙的面颊飞起一抹红晕，羞涩地捂着嘴，水灵灵的眸子里满是错愕与好笑。

Fujiwara, however, didn't miss a beat. A smooth, roguish smirk immediately spread across his face. He patted the ecstatic customer firmly on the shoulder, leaning in with an air of profound, mysterious wisdom.

然而藤原反应极快，脸上瞬间挂起了一抹高深莫测的坏笑。他气定神闲地拍了拍这位狂喜顾客的肩膀，摆出一副世外高人的姿态凑过去低声说道：

"My friend, you underestimate the hidden attributes of Fresh Mart," Fujiwara chuckled warmly, playing right into the absurdity. "We don't just sell groceries; we sell supreme vitality. Those kiwifruits were hand-selected under a full moon, infused with premium island energy. Though... between you and me? I'm pretty sure your wife's pregnancy has a lot more to do with your own hidden talents once you fed her that fruit."

“老兄，你还是太低估‘新鲜玛特’的隐藏神效了，”藤原爽朗地笑着，顺水推舟地胡诌起来，“我们这儿卖可不仅仅是生鲜，而是精纯的生命力。那箱猕猴桃可是沐浴着岛上的日月精华精心挑选的。不过……说句掏心窝子的话？我寻思着，你老婆能怀上，多半还是因为你喂了她水果之后，你自己晚上加倍卖力吧？”

The customer blushed furiously, scratching the back of his head with an embarrassed, triumphant laugh. "Ha! Well... I can't deny the house was lively! Anyway, thank you, boss! I'm bringing all my friends here from now on!"

顾客的老脸腾地一下红了透，尴尬而得意地挠了挠后脑勺。“哈哈！那倒也是……那天晚上家里确实挺热闹的！总之太谢谢老板了！以后我绝对把身边的亲戚朋友全拉来你们这儿买东西！”

With that, the overjoyed father-to-be left the shop skipping like a schoolboy, leaving behind an expensive basket of pastries and a store full of stunned silence.

说罢，这位喜当爹的顾客像个中学生似的一蹦一跳地离开了小店，留下一大篮昂贵的糕点和满屋子目瞪口呆的众。

Rin burst into a loud, helpless laugh, shaking her head. "A fertility clinic disguised as a convenience store. Damn, boss, we should start charging extra for the 'miracle' label."

铃忍不住爆发出一阵狂笑，笑着直摇头：“好家伙，表面上是个杂货铺，背地里居然是个送子观音庙。老板，看来咱们以后得给水果加收一笔‘送子税’了。”

Miyetsuko let out a soft sigh, shaking her head as she adjusted her glasses. "Utterly unscientific, yet undeniably effective for local PR. I suppose I should update our inventory ledger to include 'reproduction catalysts'."

美悦子无奈地轻叹一声，推了推眼镜：“完全不合科学逻辑，但对于地方公关来说却有着无可比拟的引流效果。看来我得把库存账本里的猕猴桃分类改成‘生殖催化剂’了。”

Sayuri walked over, leaning gently against Fujiwara's side with a sweet, affectionate giggle. "Well, seeing people happy like that... I suppose it's a very good kind of magic after all."

小百合笑着走过来，温柔地靠在藤原的身侧，发出甜美的轻笑：“不过……能看到别人这么幸福，这种美丽的‘魔法’倒也挺不错的呢。”

Fujiwara wrapped his arm around her waist, pulling her close as he smirked at the gift basket. "Magic or not, looks like Fresh Mart's reputation just went global. Come on, girls, let's go celebrate our new medical breakthrough in the back room."

藤原伸手揽住她的纤腰，看着桌上的礼篮挑眉一笑：“管它是不是魔法，看来咱们杂货铺的名声这回是彻底响彻全岛了。走吧姑娘们，咱们去后室好好庆祝一下这伟大的‘医学突破’。”
