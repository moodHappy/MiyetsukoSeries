Chapter 487: The Failed Awakening and the Trillionaire's Meltdown

发布时间：2026/10/7 13:42:27

第四百八十七章：唤醒失败与首富的终极大崩溃

The digital countdown timer on the mahogany nightstand let out a crisp, mechanical chime, signaling the exact end of the two-hour pharmacological window. According to the Swiss laboratory’s technical briefing, the metabolic half-life of the "Feign Death Water" should have lapsed, restoring neural firing and baseline autonomic reflexes.

红木床头柜上的数码倒计时器发出一声清脆而规律的电子提示音，精准地宣告着两小时药理窗口期的正式终结。按照瑞士顶级生物实验室的技术简报，此时“假死水”的代谢半衰期应当彻底耗尽，受试者的脑神经信号与基础自主神经反射理应全线恢复。

Fujiwara, still basking in the glow of his dark conquest, tossed his empty scotch tumbler aside. He stood at the edge of the bed and clapped his hands with boisterous imperial confidence. "All right, my sleeping beauties! The royal inspection is concluded. Time to wake up and attend to your Emperor!"

仍沉浸在征服快感中的藤原随手将空了的威士忌杯扔到一旁。他志得意满地站在床沿边，带着帝王惯有的威严与霸道，响亮地拍了拍巴掌：“好了，我的睡美人们！皇家巡礼圆满结束，该醒过来伺候你们的帝王了！”

Nothing happened.

然而，床上一片死寂。没有任何动静。

The bedroom remained dead silent, broken only by the faint hum of the central air conditioning. Fujiwara frowned slightly, stepping closer to Sayuri. He reached out and gently patted her smooth cheek. "Sayuri? Come now, the performance is over. Don't play coy with me."

奢华的主卧里依然落针可闻，唯有中央空调微弱的气流声在空气中打转。藤原微微蹙眉，俯身凑近小百合身边。他伸出手，轻轻拍了拍她白腻滑嫩的脸颊：“小百合？好啦，戏演完了。少跟老子在这儿装模作样。”

Her head simply rolled lifelessly to the side, resting against the silk pillow like a discarded porcelain doll. Her skin was still cool to the touch. Her chest did not rise. There was no teasing whisper, no flutter of long eyelashes, no soft murmur of sweet compliance.

她的螓首只是软绵绵地顺着力道歪向一侧，宛如一只被遗弃的瓷娃娃般深陷在真丝枕套里。她的肌肤依旧微凉如冰，胸脯不见丝毫起伏。没有往日甜糯的低语，没有睫毛的微颤，更没有半点顺从的娇嗔。

A tiny drop of cold sweat broke out across Fujiwara's brow. He rushed around to Chika's side of the mattress, firmly shaking the little blonde's shoulder. "Chika! Wake up! You're leaking milk all over the sheets! If you don't get up right now, I'm cutting your daily budget to zero!"

一滴细密的冷汗悄然自藤原的额角渗出。他慌忙绕到床榻另一侧，一把抓住千夏柔弱的双肩用力摇晃：“千夏！醒醒！奶水都流得满床单都是了！再不给老子起来，下个月的零花钱预算全部扣光！”

Chika’s limp frame merely wobbled back and forth under his grip. Her arms hung completely slack, her fingers pale, with no sign of that trademark spark or greedy pout.

千夏软塌塌的身子只是随着他的动作无力地前后晃荡。她的小手毫无知觉地耷拉着，指尖泛着不祥的惨白，平日里那股灵动娇憨、财迷心窍的生机荡然无存。

Panic, sharp and icy, stabbed straight through the trillionaire's chest. He practically lunged across the mattress toward Miyetsuko, pressing two trembling fingers against her carotid artery. The pulse was so faint, so sluggish, it felt like a ghost whisper against his skin. He pried open her eyelids—her pupils were completely dilated and dull.

一股冰冷彻骨的恐慌瞬间如尖刀般刺透了这位万亿首富的胸膛。他几乎是扑过了凌乱的床褥，冲到美悦子身旁，用两根剧烈颤抖的手指死死按住她的颈动脉。那脉搏微弱而迟缓到了极致，指腹下感受到的跳动微茫得宛如游魂的叹息。他扒开她的眼皮——瞳孔涣散无光，没有一丝一毫的对光反射。

"Rin! Rin!" Fujiwara scrambled to the black velvet cushions, shaking the petite blind girl in sheer desperation. "Wake up! Rin, where is your succubus magic?! Use your soul power and open your eyes!"

“小铃！小铃！”藤原狼狈不堪地爬向那堆黑天鹅绒软垫，带着近乎绝望的歇斯底里摇晃着娇小的盲女，“醒醒啊！小铃，你的魅魔魔法呢？！快用你的灵魂力量把眼睛睁开啊！”

Rin lay entirely motionless, like a breathless statue entombed in ancient ice.

小铃毫无声息地静止在那里，宛如一尊被永久冰封在远古苔原里的绝美雕像。

Ten minutes passed. Then fifteen. The four most precious women in the world remained trapped in an abyssal, unresponsive coma.

十分钟过去了。接着是十五分钟。这四个被他视若性命的女人，依然深深陷在死一般的深渊昏迷中，毫无知觉。

"No... no, no, no! This is impossible!" Fujiwara roared, grabbing his encrypted satellite phone with hands that shook so violently he nearly dropped the device three times. He punched in the emergency direct line to his private Swiss bio-tech installation.

“不……不可能！这绝对不可能！”藤原爆发出野兽般慌乱的怒吼，抓起加密卫星电话的手抖得连按了三次才堪堪拿稳。他发疯似地拨通了瑞士私人生物实验室的最高紧急专线。

"Klaus! You incompetent piece of German shit, pick up the phone!" Fujiwara screamed into the receiver the second the line connected, completely devoid of his usual calculated composure. "What did you put in that damn water?! The two hours are up! It's been thirty-five minutes past the deadline! Why haven't they woken up?! Why are they still completely lifeless?!"

“克劳斯！你这个办事不力的德国饭桶，给老子滚出来接电话！”电话刚一接通，藤原便冲着听筒歇斯底里地咆哮起来，平日里运筹帷幄的从容霸道早已荡然无存，“你们在那该死的药水里到底掺了什么东西？！两个小时早就过了！现在超期整整三十五分钟了！为什么她们一个都醒不过来？！为什么浑身上下还跟尸体一样毫无反应？！”

The lead pharmacologist on the other end stammered in stark terror. "M-My Lord! The formula was thoroughly simulated on primate models! But... but human metabolic variance... if their liver enzymes were depleted, or if the central nervous receptors became oversaturated due to... extreme emotional or physiological exhaustion during the trial... the half-life could theoretically extend into a permanent respiratory depression!"

电话那头的首席药理学专家吓得语无伦次、结结巴巴：“阁、阁下！配方在灵长类动物模型上明明经过了成千上万次模拟！但……但是人体的代谢差异极其复杂……如果她们体内的肝药酶被耗尽，或者中枢神经受体因为在试验期间遭受了极度激烈的生理或神经透支……半衰期在理论上有可能会恶化为永久性的呼吸抑制啊！”

"Permanent respiratory depression?!" Fujiwara’s voice cracked into a horrifying screech. "Are you telling me I just killed my entire household for an afternoon's amusement?! Listen to me, Klaus: if a single hair on their heads stops breathing permanently, I will wipe your entire bloodline off the face of the Earth! I will deploy three hypersonic missiles directly onto your research facility! Scramble the airborne ICU! Bring every dose of neural receptor antagonists, epinephrine, and electro-stimulators in Western Europe! You have twenty minutes before I raze Zurich to the ground!"

“永久性呼吸抑制？！”藤原的声音瞬间拔高成了惊恐万状的破音，“你的意思是老子为了图一下午的恶趣味，亲手把老子的后宫全给毒死了？！克劳斯你给老子听清楚：要是她们敢少了一根头发、敢彻底断了气，老子让你全家上下从这颗星球上彻底蒸发！老子立刻调动三枚高超音速导弹把你们整个研究所夷为平地！马上调集空中重症医疗机！把全西欧所有的神经受体拮抗剂、肾上腺素和电刺激设备全给老子运过来！你们只有二十分钟，不然老子就把整个苏黎世炸成一片焦土！”

He slammed the phone down, his knees buckling as he collapsed heavily onto the carpet between the four motionless figures.

他狠狠把电话掼在大理石地面上，双膝一软，整个人重重地跌跪在四具毫无生机的娇躯之间。

The Emperor who ruled the global financial underworld, who crushed cartels and toppled sovereign governments with a single pen stroke, was suddenly crying. Cold, unvarnished tears of sheer terror streamed down his cheeks. He frantically pulled Sayuri into his chest, clutching her limp, cold form like a drowning man grasping at driftwood, desperately pressing his lips against her unmoving forehead.

这位统治全球地下金融、翻手为云覆手为雨的霸主，此刻竟彻底崩溃了。大颗大颗惊恐至极的泪水顺着他惨白的脸颊疯狂滚落。他发了疯似地将小百合瘫软的身子死死搂进怀里，宛如溺水之人拼命抱住最后的浮木，语无伦次地吻着她冰凉无觉的额头。

"Sayuri... wake up, please, I'm begging you," Fujiwara sobbed, his voice cracking with raw agony. "Don't leave me alone! Chika, wake up and I'll let you short whatever stock you want! Miyetsuko, you can break my ribs with your baseball bat, I won't dodge! Rin, come back... Master was wrong! The Emperor was an idiot! Please... just open your eyes!"

“小百合……醒醒，求求你醒过来……”藤原带着哭腔绝望地哀嚎，声音沙哑撕裂，“别扔下老子一个人！千夏，快醒醒，老子准你去做空任何你想做空的股票！美悦子，你拿棒球棍把老子的肋骨打断老子也绝不躲！小铃，快回来啊……主人错了！帝王是个彻头彻尾的大傻逼！求求你们……快把眼睛睁开啊！”

The quiet room offered no answer. Only four cold, silent bodies lay motionless in his trembling arms, offering the absolute terror of a game that had gone horribly, fatally wrong.

死寂的寝殿中没有一丝回应。唯有四具冰凉脆弱的身躯静静横陈在他剧烈发颤的双臂之间，向这位狂妄自大的帝王亮出了这场荒诞玩火闹剧最致命、最绝望的深渊獠牙。
