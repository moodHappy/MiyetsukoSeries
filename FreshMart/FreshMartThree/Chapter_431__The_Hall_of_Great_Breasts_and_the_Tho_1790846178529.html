Chapter 431: The Hall of Great Breasts and the Thousand-Year Legacy

发布时间：2026/10/1 17:16:12

第四百三十一章：大胸宝殿与千年传承

Ever content with merely ruling the planetary economy, Fujiwara decided it was time to cement his legacy into human history with a monument of truly epic, unhinged proportions. Inspired by a characteristically crude pun playing on the traditional Buddhist Mahavira Hall—known in Japanese and Chinese as the Daioh/Daxiong Baodian (Great Hero Hall)—he resolved to construct a colossal, breathtaking pleasure palace dedicated entirely to the world’s most magnificent assets. He christened it The Hall of Great Breasts (大胸宝殿).

仅仅满足于统治地球经济显然已经无法让藤原停下脚步，他决定用一座规模宏大、离谱至极的奇观来将自己的不朽功勋永远铭刻在人类历史中。受一个谐音梗的启发——他巧妙地将庄严的大雄宝殿谐音化，决定建造一座气势恢宏、专属于人间极致美景的顶级享乐宫殿，并将其正式命名为大胸宝殿。

To fill this architectural marvel, Fujiwara handpicked three thousand breathtaking beauties straight from his sprawling network of outer residences and international talent pipelines. Entry into the Hall of Great Breasts transformed these women into the ultimate elite of high-society courtesans—an exclusive, ultra-luxury entertainment sanctuary where VIP clients were not only permitted, but actively encouraged, to engage in raw, uninhibited internal breeding without restrictions.

为了填满这座建筑奇迹，藤原从他那庞大的外宅网络和国际人才库中精挑细选出了三千位绝世佳丽。凡是能够入选这座大胸宝殿的女子，瞬间便成了上流社会里最高端的顶级娼妓——这里是一个奢华至极的娱乐圣所，特权贵宾们不仅被允许，甚至被热烈鼓励在里面进行毫无保留的生插内射。

Moreover, to ensure his empire of flesh outlived empires and civilizations, Fujiwara established a permanent, multi-trillion-dollar perpetual trust fund. This financial behemoth was engineered to continuously replenish, audit, and recruit fresh waves of stunning beauties across the centuries, guaranteeing that the Hall of Great Breasts would operate uninterrupted for a guaranteed duration of one thousand years—with provisions to automatically renew for another millennium thereafter.

不仅如此，为了确保这座肉体帝国能够超越凡世的文明与政权，藤原建立了一个规模庞大、价值数万亿美元的永久信托基金。这个金融巨兽被精准设定为随着时间推移不断自动递补、审核并招募源源不断的新鲜佳丽，从而确保大胸宝殿能够不间断地运营整整一千年，并且在千年期满后自动续期。

Back in the cozy, chaotic penthouse of Fresh Mart, the family gathered around the architectural holographic blueprint, beaming with absolute pride.

在“新鲜玛特”温馨而混乱的顶层公寓里，家族成员们正围聚在全息建筑蓝图前，脸上洋溢着无比自豪的笑容。

"Well, I must hand it to you, Fujiwara, your talent for architectural wordplay is truly legendary," Chika giggled hysterically, tapping away at the trillion-dollar perpetual fund spreadsheet on her tablet. "Three thousand hand-selected beauties, a guaranteed thousand-year operational runway, and uninhibited internal breeding rights for elite clients? I’ve just projected the quarterly dividends, and our profit margins are going to outlive human civilization itself!"

“说实话不得不佩服你啊藤原，你在建筑命名上的谐音梗造诣简直堪称一绝，”千夏在平板上疯狂敲打着万亿基金的电子表格，咯咯娇笑个不停，“三千名精选佳丽、一千年雷打不动的运营周期，再加上对VIP客户开放的无限制内射权？我刚算了下季度红利，咱们的利润率绝对能比人类文明本身活得还久！”

Miyetsuko scoffed playfully, adjusting her glasses as she twirled her iron baseball bat with casual mastery. "Hmph, turning a juvenile pun into a multi-trillion-dollar global institution of sacred debauchery? Only you could pull off something so ridiculously grandiose, Fujiwara. Rest assured, I’ll personally deploy my security squads to make sure no unruly gigolos try to scam our three-thousand-strong treasury."

美悦子推了推眼镜，漫不经心地转着铁棒球棍轻哼道：“哼，把一个幼稚的谐音梗搞成价值数万亿美元的神圣淫乱全球机构？也就只有你这混蛋能干出这么宏大又离谱的事了。放心吧，我亲自安排安保小队去盯着，绝对不让任何不长眼的小白脸敢来咱们的三千佳丽金库里白吃白喝。”

Sayuri leaned affectionately against Fujiwara's shoulder, offering a sweet, musical giggle as she watched him trace the blueprints.

小百合亲昵地靠在藤原的肩膀上，看着他勾画蓝图，发出一声甜美悦耳的娇笑。

"It's a magnificent monument to your eternal glory, my love," Sayuri murmured tenderly, gently stroking his chest. "Though no matter how many thousands of beauties you put inside that grand hall, you'll never replace your favorite first wife, right?"

“这简直是对你永恒功勋的伟大丰碑呀，亲爱的，”小百合温柔地呢喃着，纤手轻轻抚摸着他的胸膛，“不过话说回来，不管你往那座宏伟的宝殿里塞多少万个美女，你最疼爱的大房正室永远都是无可替代的吧？”

Fujiwara pulled her close, planting a deep, passionate kiss on her lips before laughing heartily. "Of course not, my queen! Those three thousand are just for the public and our elite investors. You’re the only one who rules the throne room!"

藤原一把将她搂入怀中，在她唇上深深落下一个充满热烈爱意的吻，随后开怀大笑：“那是自然，我的女王！那三千人不过是用来招待公众和顶层投资者的门面罢了，真正能稳坐王座主位的，可永远只有你一个！”

Blind Rin sat quietly in the corner, her wide, unseeing eyes blinking innocently as she held up a massive, freshly knitted decorative banner. She offered a soft, timid smile.

盲人铃安安静静地坐在角落里，她那双睁得圆溜溜却空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，手里展开了一条巨大崭新、亲手织好的装饰横幅，露出了一个温柔而胆怯的微笑。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered softly. "Are we... are we opening a new temple today? I embroidered a special welcoming sign that says 'Welcome to the Hall of Great Breasts'... Please work hard, everyone. It's wonderful that our family's project will last for a thousand years."

“唔……你好呀？”铃轻声呢喃着，“咱们今天是在……是在开一家新寺庙吗？我特意绣了一块写着‘大胸宝殿欢迎您’的迎宾横幅哦……大家请继续加油呀。咱们家族的项目能流芳一千年，真是太让人高兴啦。”
