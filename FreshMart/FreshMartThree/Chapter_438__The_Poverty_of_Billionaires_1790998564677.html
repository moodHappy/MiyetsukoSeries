Chapter 438: The Poverty of Billionaires

发布时间：2026/10/3 11:36:03

第四百三十八章：亿万富翁的贫穷

To alleviate an unusually boring weekend, the Fresh Mart syndicate's inner circle decided to grace a hyper-exclusive global billionaire gala with their presence. To the rest of the world, this glittering ballroom held the untouchable masters of the universe; to Fujiwara and his wives, wandering through the crowd felt like visiting a quaint, impoverished petting zoo.

为了打发一个无聊透顶的周末，“新鲜玛特”辛迪加的核心圈子决定大驾光临一场极其私密的全球顶级亿万富豪晚宴。在外界看来，这个金碧辉煌的宴会厅里装满了不可一世、掌控宇宙的主宰；但在藤原和他的妻子们眼里，漫步在这群人中间，感觉就像是参观一个古朴寒酸的乡村宠物动物园。

While Fujiwara was casually sipping a glass of champagne with Sayuri wrapped securely around his arm, a pompous, newly minted tech magnate strutted over. Intoxicated by his own perceived importance and entirely ignorant of the hidden monsters standing before him, the magnate stared hungrily at Sayuri’s voluptuous, clinging dress and made a breathtakingly suicidal proposition.

当藤原正挽着小百合漫不经心地品尝香槟时，一个自命不凡、刚刚跻身全球巨头的科技新贵趾高气扬地走了过来。他被自己那点虚幻的地位冲昏了头脑，对站在眼前的隐形怪物一无所知，只是色眯眯地死死盯着小百合在那紧身礼服下呼之欲出的惹火身段，提出了一个堪称自杀般狂妄的建议。

"Fujiwara, isn't it?" the magnate smirked arrogantly, swirling his glass. "I like your wife. I'll give you ten billion dollars cash right now for one night with her. Don't look at me like that—we're all businessmen here, and everyone knows everything has a price."

“你是藤原对吧？”这位巨头傲慢地冷笑着，摇晃着酒杯，“我看上你老婆了。我现在就给你一百亿美元现金，买她陪我一晚。别用那种眼神看我——在座的都是生意人，大家都知道凡事皆有标价。”

The air around the Fujiwara family froze for a split second, before Sayuri covered her mouth and let out a sweet, unrestrained fit of musical giggles. She leaned helplessly against Fujiwara's broad chest, shaking with genuine, aristocratic amusement.

藤原家族周围的空气瞬间凝固了零点一秒，随后小百合忍不住捂着红唇，发出一阵甜美而毫无顾忌的悦耳娇笑。她笑得花枝乱颤，娇柔无力地靠在藤原宽广的胸膛上，眼中满是真正的贵族式嘲弄。

"Oh, my goodness..." Sayuri gasped delicately, wiping a tear of laughter from her eye. "Ten billion? Darling, doesn't the head gardener at our summer estate get more than that just for his quarterly bonus?"

“哎呀，我的天哪……”小百合娇喘着呢喃道，抹去眼角笑出的泪水，“一百亿？亲爱的，咱们避暑山庄的首席园丁，光是一个季度的绩效奖金都不止这个数吧？”

Chika whipped out her tablet from her designer clutch, spinning around playfully.

千夏从她的名牌手拿包里掏出平板电脑，调皮地转了个圈。

"I just checked his entire corporate portfolio!" Chika giggled wildly. "His absolute total net worth is barely forty billion! How incredibly adorable! He doesn't even realize his entire life's fortune couldn't buy a single golden toilet in our Hall of Great Breasts. Our lowest-ranking domestic servant uses more money to tip the pizza delivery guy!"

“我刚查了他名下所有的企业资产！”千夏疯狂地咯咯娇笑，“他砸锅卖铁的总身家勉强才四百亿！真是可爱得令人发指！他根本没意识到，他倾尽一生的财富连咱们大胸宝殿里的一个纯金马桶都买不起。咱们家最低等的倒水奴才，给披萨外卖员的跑腿小费都比这多！”

The magnate frowned, his arrogant smirk faltering as he realized he was being openly mocked. "Excuse me? Do you know who I am—"

那位巨头皱起了眉头，当他意识到自己正在被公开嘲笑时，傲慢的冷笑僵在了脸上。“你说什么？你们知道我是谁吗——”

Fujiwara didn't even bother to look angry; he simply looked utterly bored. He snapped his fingers and glanced at Chika.

藤原甚至懒得发火；他看起来只觉得无聊透顶。他打了个响指，瞥了千夏一眼。

"Bankrupt him," Fujiwara commanded casually, stroking Sayuri's waist. "Hostile takeover of his shares, dissolve his holding companies, and freeze every personal account he has globally."

“让他破产，”藤原漫不经心地命令道，手掌轻轻摩挲着小百合的腰肢，“恶意收购他的股份，解散他的控股公司，把他全球所有的私人账户全给我冻结了。”

Chika tapped her screen three times. "Done. He's officially penniless. It took exactly four seconds."

千夏在屏幕上敲了三下。“搞定。他现在正式身无分文了。总共花了四秒钟。”

Instantly, the arrogant magnate's phone began to buzz frantically with a barrage of calls from his panicked board of directors and frantic bankers. His face drained of all color, turning an absolute, sickly shade of white as the reality of his instant, terrifying ruin set in.

刹那间，这位狂妄巨头的手机开始疯狂震动，惊恐万状的董事会和急得跳脚的银行家们打来了夺命连环call。当他意识到自己瞬间倾家荡产的恐怖现实时，脸上的血色褪得一干二净，变得惨白如纸。

Miyetsuko stepped forward from the shadows, tapping her iron baseball bat rhythmically against her palm with a cold, terrifying smirk.

美悦子从阴影中走上前来，用铁棒球棍有节奏地敲击着手心，嘴角挂着一抹冰冷恐怖的坏笑。

"And since this filthy beggar dared to verbally solicit our undisputed first wife with his dirty mouth..." Miyetsuko said, signaling the syndicate's heavily armed guards who materialized from the crowd. "Drag him to the basement. Under our newly established Lex Talionis code, a dirty tongue gets removed. No one disrespects the queen."

“而且，既然这个满身铜臭的乞丐敢用那张脏嘴言语侮辱我们毋庸置疑的大房正室……”美悦子说着，向从人群中悄然出现的辛迪加全副武装保镖挥了挥手，“把他拖进地下室。根据我们新出炉的同态复仇法典，嘴贱就得拔舌头。绝对不允许任何人对女王不敬。”

As the screaming, newly-impoverished man was brutally dragged out of the gala by his collar, blind Rin offered a sweet, timid smile, holding up a tiny, freshly knitted pouch.

当那个刚刚破产的男人尖叫着被保镖像拎小鸡一样粗暴地拖出晚宴大厅时，盲人铃露出了一个甜美而怯生生的微笑，手里举着一个小巧的新织布袋。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered softly. "Did that poor gentleman lose all his money? I can knit him a little coin purse for when he starts over... Please enjoy the party, everyone. It's so nice to meet new friends."

“唔……你好呀？”铃轻声呢喃着，“那位可怜的先生是把钱全丢了吗？我可以给他织一个小小的零钱包，等他重新白手起家的时候用哦……大家请继续享受派对呀。能交到新朋友真是太开心啦。”

Fujiwara laughed heartily, pressing a dominant, passionate kiss against Sayuri's soft lips right in front of the terrified onlookers.

藤原开怀大笑，当着周围瑟瑟发抖的旁观者的面，在小百合柔软的唇上印下了一个充满占有欲的深吻。

"Just another charming day in high society!" Fujiwara grinned. "Come, my beautiful queen, let's go taste the caviar. I think working up an appetite wiping out a billionaire is the perfect start to our evening."

“上流社会这迷人的一天啊！”藤原咧嘴笑道，“走吧，我美丽的女王，咱们去尝尝鱼子酱。我觉得靠抹杀一个亿万富翁来开胃，绝对是咱们今晚最完美的开场。”
