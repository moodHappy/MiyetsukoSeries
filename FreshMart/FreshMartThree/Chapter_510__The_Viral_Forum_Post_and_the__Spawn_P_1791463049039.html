第五百一十章：爆红热帖与“出生点”猛攻

During a rare afternoon of leisure, the grand penthouse was bathed in warm, lazy sunlight. Fujiwara was lounging on the oversized velvet sofa, casually swiping through a tablet, when he suddenly burst into a booming, wicked laugh.

在一个难得的悠闲午后，宽敞的顶层公寓沐浴在温暖慵懒的阳光中。藤原正惬意地靠在超大的天鹅绒沙发上，随手滑动着平板电脑，突然，他爆发出一阵震耳欲聋、充满恶趣味的狂笑。

"Come look at this masterpiece of modern internet literature," Fujiwara beckoned, tossing the tablet onto the coffee table. The screen displayed a viral anonymous forum thread with a shocking, clickbait title: 'Betrayed and Cheated on by My Ex-Girlfriend, So I Seduced My Ex-Mother-in-Law and Fiercely Attacked My Ex's Spawn Point!'

“快来看看这篇现代互联网文学的绝世佳作，”藤原招了招手，将平板电脑扔到茶几上。屏幕上显示着一个爆红的匿名论坛热帖，标题极具噱头且骇人听闻：《被前女友出轨背叛，怒而勾搭前岳母，猛攻前任出生点！》

The girls leaned in closely. The original poster had written a vivid, highly descriptive essay detailing the scandalous revenge. He meticulously compared the bedroom skills of the young daughter and her mature mother, praising the mother's enduring, rich charm. Above all, he raved about the unparalleled psychological and physical thrill of conquering the very "spawn point" from which his cheating ex-girlfriend had been born.

姑娘们纷纷凑上前去。发帖人声情并茂地写下了一篇极其生动、细节拉满的长文，详细描述了这场骇世惊俗的复仇。他字斟句酌地对比了年轻女儿与成熟母亲在床上的不同技艺，大肆赞美了岳母风韵犹存、丰满迷人的成熟韵味。最重要的是，他狂热地炫耀了那种彻底征服前女友“出生点”时，所带来的无与伦比的心理与生理双重快感。

Chika’s eyes went wide as saucers, her jaw dropping as she clutched her plush, milk-swollen chest. "Good heavens! 'Attacked her spawn point'?! He actually used gaming terminology to describe sleeping with her mother?! That is so toxic, so deeply psychopathic! The poor ex-girlfriend must be traumatized for life!"

千夏的眼睛瞪得像铜铃一样大，惊得下巴都快掉下来了，两手下意识地死死捂住胸前那对饱满发胀的软嫩雪峰。“我的天爷呀！‘猛攻出生点’？！他居然用打游戏的电竞术语来形容睡了人家的亲妈？！这也太恶毒、太变态了吧！那个可怜的前女友肯定会留下一辈子的心理阴影啊！”

Miyetsuko, resting her chin on Fujiwara's knee, approached the scandal with the cold, analytical mindset of a former top-tier assassin. "From a tactical perspective, it is an absolute masterpiece of psychological warfare," she murmured softly, her dewy eyes glinting with analytical appreciation. "If you want to completely destroy an enemy's morale, you don't just defeat them; you conquer their point of origin and establish total dominance over their ancestral supply line. This man has the mindset of a military genius."

美悦子把下巴枕在藤原的膝头，用昔日顶尖杀手那种冷酷、充满逻辑的思维模式来分析这桩丑闻。“从战术角度来看，这简直是一场心理战的绝对杰作，”她细声细语地轻喃着，水汪汪的美眸里闪烁着赞赏的精光，“如果你想彻底摧毁敌人的意志，就不能仅仅是打败他们；你必须征服他们的发源地，并对他们的祖传补给线建立绝对的统治权。这男人有着军事天才般的思维。”

Sayuri picked up her porcelain teacup, her lips curving into a slow, velvet smirk as she cast a knowing, highly amused side-glance at Fujiwara. "Well, youth has its tight vitality, but true enduring charm requires decades of marination. The mother clearly possessed a level of rich, mellow grace that the daughter completely lacked. Master," she purred, tracing a finger down his collar, "reading this hasn't awakened any strange new cravings in your boundless imperial appetite, has it? Are you suddenly plotting to raid some poor girl's 'spawn point'?"

小百合端起骨瓷茶杯，红唇勾起一抹缓慢、风情万种的坏笑，意味深长又极具促狭地斜睨了藤原一眼。“哎呀，年轻虽然有紧致的活力，但真正历久弥香的魅力可是需要几十年的岁月沉淀的。那位母亲显然拥有女儿完全欠缺的醇厚风韵与成熟仪态。主人，”她娇媚地轻唤了一声，指尖顺着他的衣领缓缓滑下，“看了这篇帖子，该不会又唤醒了您那无底洞般的帝王胃口里某种奇怪的新癖好吧？您是不是突然也想去端了哪个可怜姑娘的‘出生点’呀？”

Fujiwara let out a booming, unrepentant roar of laughter, wrapping his heavy arms around Sayuri's waist and pulling her lush, fragrant body flush against his chest. "Hahaha! Don't project this commoner's desperate revenge onto the Emperor! The man only seduced the mother because he was dumped by the daughter! A true monarch does not settle for an either/or scenario. If I wanted a mother and daughter, I would simply conquer them both on the same massive silk bed and make them thank me for the privilege!"

藤原仰天爆发出一阵毫无悔意、震耳欲聋的狂笑，粗壮的双臂一把环住小百合的纤腰，将她那具丰腴温软、暗香浮动的娇躯紧紧搂在胸前。“哈哈哈哈！少把这种底层凡夫俗子狗急跳墙的复仇戏码往本帝王身上套！这男的不过是因为被女儿甩了，才跑去勾搭亲妈的！真正的雄主，从不做非此即彼的单选题。老子要是真看上了一对母女，老子只会把她们俩同时按在一张巨大的真丝大床上彻底征服，还要让她们跪着谢主隆恩！”

From her soft cushion on the Persian rug, blind Rin clapped her delicate hands together, giggling with pure, terrifying succubus innocence. "Um... hello, everyone? Rin thinks the internet uncle is actually very romantic!" she chimed in sweetly, tilting her unseeing eyes toward the ceiling. "In the demonic realm, going back to fertilize the soil where your enemy grew is called 'retroactive soul farming'! But Master is right—why stop at the mother? Master should conquer the daughter, the mother, and the grandmother all at once! A complete, three-generation family bundle!"

坐在波斯地毯软垫上的盲人小铃开心地合起一双白嫩的小手，发出纯洁却又令人毛骨悚然的魅魔娇笑。“唔……大家听我说好吗？小铃觉得网上的这位大叔其实非常浪漫呢！”她甜丝丝地插嘴道，毫无焦距的盲眼微微仰向天花板，“在恶魔的世界里，跑回敌人长大的土壤里去重新施肥，这叫做‘追溯性灵魂耕种’哦！不过主人说得对——干嘛只停在妈妈这一代呢？主人应该一口气把女儿、妈妈、还有外婆全都征服掉呀！来个完整的三代同堂家庭大礼包嘛！”

"Rin! You little demon, stop putting apocalyptic ideas into the Boss's head!" Chika shrieked, swatting at the blind girl with a silk pillow while the entire penthouse dissolved into a chorus of chaotic, deeply depraved laughter.

“小铃！你这只小妖精，别再往老板脑袋里灌输这种毁天灭地的危险想法啦！”千夏抓狂地尖叫起来，抓起一只真丝软枕就去轻轻敲打那个盲眼女孩。整间顶层公寓顿时陷入了一片混乱、却又充斥着极度恶趣味的哄堂大笑之中。

Trapped in the decadent warmth of his twisted harem, Fujiwara simply leaned back on his throne, swirling a glass of amber bourbon, thoroughly entertained by the bottomless wickedness of his daily domestic life.

被这群扭曲又迷人的后宫佳丽簇拥在奢靡的温香软玉里，藤原只是大剌剌地靠在自己的王座上，悠闲地晃着杯中的琥珀色波旁威士忌，彻底被自己这毫无底线的恶趣味日常给取悦了。
