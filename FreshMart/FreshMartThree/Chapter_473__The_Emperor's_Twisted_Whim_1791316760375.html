Chapter 473: The Emperor's Twisted Whim

发布时间：2026/10/7 03:59:19

第四百七十三章：帝王的恶趣味

The living room had barely settled into a calm rhythm when Fujiwara casually picked up his crystal tumbler of scotch. He swirled the amber liquid, leaning back into the leather armchair with an infuriatingly proud, triumphant smirk plastered across his face.

客厅里刚恢复了几分宁静，藤原就漫不经心地端起了他的水晶威士忌杯。他轻轻晃动着琥珀色的酒液，往真皮扶手椅里一靠，脸上挂着一抹极其得意、欠揍到了极点的坏笑。

"You know, I suppose it's time I reveal a little secret to you all," Fujiwara chuckled, raising an index finger like a mastermind unveiling a grand master plan. "That wasn't just some ordinary, cheap adult toy you buy off an underground website. Inside that shell was an ultra-high-precision, aerospace-grade biometric device developed by our top-secret Swiss laboratory. The rare metals and nanotechnology alone made that single prototype worth roughly three billion yen."

“说起来，我觉得也是时候向你们坦白一个小秘密了，”藤原轻笑一声，像个揭晓终极阴谋的幕后黑手一样竖起食指，“那可不是什么在地下小网站上随便买来的便宜玩具。在那层外壳下面，装的是咱们瑞士绝密实验室研发的超高精度、航空航天级的生物监测设备。单是里面的稀有贵金属和纳米技术，就让那颗唯一的原型机价值大概三十亿日元。”

Sayuri paused with her teacup halfway to her lips. She stared at him, her eyes widening in pure bewilderment.

小百合端着茶杯的手停在了半空中。她睁大眼睛，满脸茫然地死死盯着他。

Fujiwara turned his gaze toward her, his tone softening into pure, unadulterated doting. "My sweet Sayuri, do you honestly think I would ever let you get hurt? That device was so ridiculously expensive precisely because it was designed with fail-safes. Even if your greedy rear entrance had completely swallowed it whole, it would have automatically initiated a molecular self-dissolution sequence within seventy-two hours. It would have dissolved harmlessly, turning into organic nutrients and absorbing into your bloodstream like a high-end vitamin shot."

藤原把目光转向她，语气瞬间软化成毫不掩饰的极致宠溺：“我甜美的小百合，你真以为我会让你受伤吗？那玩意之所以贵得如此离谱，正是因为设计了终极安全保障。哪怕你的屁股真的把它给彻底‘吃’进去了，七十二小时之内它也会自动启动分子级销毁程序。它会毫无害处地融化分解，变成纯天然的有机营养，像一剂高级维生素补剂一样直接被你的身体吸收。”

A profound, suffocating silence fell over the penthouse. Chika froze with her hand inside a bag of potato chips. Miyetsuko slowly lowered her cleaning cloth, her eyebrows twitching violently.

顶层公寓里瞬间陷入了一阵死一般的寂静。千夏伸在薯片袋里的小手僵住了。美悦子慢慢放下了手里的战术布，眉毛开始疯狂抽搐。

"Wait a second..." Sayuri spoke up, her voice trembling between profound disbelief and towering rage. Her elegant posture completely vanished as she pointed an accusing finger at her husband. "If it was going to dissolve into harmless vitamins anyway... then why in the world did we rush through traffic like maniacs to go to the hospital?!"

“等……等一下……”小百合开口了，声音在极度的难以置信与滔天的怒火之间微微发颤。她那高贵的仪态荡然无存，指着丈夫大声质问道：“既然它横竖都会自己融化成无害的维生素……那我们到底为什么要像疯子一样在大马路上飙车去医院啊？！”

Sayuri’s face flushed an apocalyptic shade of crimson, memories of the medical humiliation flooding back with blistering force. "And why did I have to lie on an examination table while a dozen creepy male doctors stared at my bare bottom?! That disgusting old pervert literally dripped his drool onto my buttcheek, Fujiwara! My buttcheek!"

小百合的脸瞬间烧成了一片毁灭性的血红，在医院遭受的那场奇耻大辱排山倒海般涌上心头：“而且我到底凭什么要趴在检查台上，被十几个猥琐的男大夫围着看我的光屁股蛋子？！那个恶心透顶的老色皮大夫，甚至把哈喇子直接掉在了人家的屁股蛋上！藤原！是掉在我的屁股上啊！”

Fujiwara took a slow sip of his scotch, his eyes gleaming with absolute, unrepentant mischief.

藤原慢悠悠地抿了一口威士忌，眼里闪烁着毫无悔意、纯粹到了极点的恶趣味光芒。

"Because," Fujiwara grinned shamelessly, leaning forward, "you looked so unbelievably, adorably cute when you were panicking like a little schoolgirl. The sheer drama, the desperate blush, the way you buried your face in my chest begging for help... it was peak entertainment. You can't buy that kind of authentic cuteness with money."

“因为啊，”藤原厚颜无耻地咧嘴坏笑，往前凑了凑身子，“你当时像个清纯女学生一样惊慌失措的样子，简直可爱得要命。那种夸张的抓狂、绝望的脸红，还有你把整张脸埋在我胸口拼命求救的模样……简直是顶级视听享受。这种原汁原味的原生态可爱，花多少钱都买不到。”

"You... you absolute demon!" Sayuri gasped, grabbing a velvet sofa cushion and hurling it straight at his head with surprising velocity.

“你……你这个彻头彻尾的魔鬼！”小百合气得倒吸一口凉气，抓起沙发上的天鹅绒抱枕，以惊人的速度直接朝他的脑袋狠狠砸了过去。

Fujiwara caught the cushion with one hand, laughing heartily. He looked toward the floor-to-ceiling windows, taking on a philosophical, contemplative expression as he thought about the aftermath in the bathroom.

藤原单手接住抱枕，爆发出一阵开怀大笑。他转头望向落地窗外，脸上突然换上了一副富有哲理的沉思表情，琢磨起了刚才洗手间里的后续。

"Though, looking back, watching thirty billion yen get flushed down the sewer pipes is truly a grand spectacle," Fujiwara sighed wistfully, shaking his head with mock tragedy. "Thirty billion, drifting through the underground city water system. I wonder which lucky sewer worker will stumble upon it. If the guy has a sharp eye and knows what he's looking at, he’ll immediately become the richest sanitation worker on planet Earth."

“不过回过头想想，亲眼看着三十个亿被冲进下水道管网，倒真是一场壮观的景象啊，”藤原带着几分假模假样的惋惜叹了口气，摇了摇头，“三十个亿，就这么在城市的地下供水系统里漂流。真不知道哪个走运的下水道清淤工能捡到它。那家伙要是稍微有点眼力见、能认出那是什么玩意儿，他可就瞬间变成这颗星球上最有钱的环卫工了。”

Chika let out a bloodcurdling screech, nearly leaping over the coffee table.

千夏嘴里爆发出了一声凄厉的惨叫，整个人差点从茶几上直接翻过去。

"Thirty billion yen?!" Chika wailed, violently tugging at her pigtails. "You flushed thirty billion yen down the toilet?! And you didn't even activate the GPS recovery beacon?! That’s thirty billion off the asset sheet! Boss, I am literally going to have to write 'Lost via Royal Digestive Track' on our quarterly audit!"

“三十个亿？！”千夏抓狂地撕扯着自己的双马尾，撕心裂肺地哀嚎道：“你把整整三十个亿冲进了马桶？！而且你甚至都没开启GPS回收定位信标？！那是资产负债表上实打实的三十个亿啊！老板，老娘在季度审计报告上，真得被迫在亏损原因那一栏写上‘经由皇家消化道遗失’了！”

Miyetsuko rubbed the bridge of her nose, her baseball bat resting beside her knee. "I take back what I said earlier. Forget the doctors. I need to mobilize three tactical dive teams into the municipal sewer lines. If a civilian finds that tech, it’s a national intelligence crisis. Fujiwara, your horny impulses are going to bankrupt the global supply chain."

美悦子揉了揉鼻梁，棒球棍就靠在她的膝盖边。“我收回刚才的话。先别管那帮大夫了。我得立刻调动三支战术潜水小队进入市政下水道管网。要是让平民捡到那种黑科技，绝对是一场国家级的情报危机。藤原，你这精虫上脑的恶趣味迟早要把全球供应链给整破产。”

In the corner, blind Rin tilted her head, blinking innocently as she thoughtfully chewed on a biscuit.

在角落里，盲人铃歪了歪头，眨巴着无辜的眼睛，若有所思地嚼着小饼干。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered sweetly. "If the little machine was full of healthy vitamins... does that mean the sewer rats are going to become super-rats? Maybe they’ll get very shiny coats and live for a hundred years! Master really is a philanthropist, even to the underground creatures."

“唔……大家听我说好吗？”铃甜甜地呢喃着，“如果那个小机器里面装满了健康的维生素……那是不是意味着下水道里的小老鼠们，马上就要变成超级大老鼠啦？说不定它们会长出油光水滑的皮毛，还能活上一百岁呢！主人果然是个大慈善家，连对地底下的微小生灵都这么温柔。”

Sayuri buried her blushing face back into her hands, completely defeated by the sheer absurdity of the family. "I give up. I'm married to an unhinged trillionaire and living in a circus."

小百合彻底被这一家子令人发指的荒谬逻辑给打败了，再次把羞红的脸埋进手里。“我认输了。我不仅嫁给了一个无可救药的疯批万亿首富，还住进了一个彻头彻尾的马戏团里。”

Fujiwara leaned over, pulling his grumbling, deeply loved Queen into his lap despite her half-hearted struggles, laughing with absolute delight as the sun set over their chaotic empire.

藤原倾过身，无视了这位深受宠爱、嘴里还在小声抱怨的女王那毫无力气的挣扎，一把将她拽进自己怀里抱紧，在夕阳洒满他们混乱帝国的余晖中，肆意畅快地狂笑起来。
