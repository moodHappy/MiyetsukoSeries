Chapter 383: The Mourning Breakfast and the Royal Omelette

发布时间：2026/9/6 09:59:43

第三百八十三章：默哀早餐与皇家煎蛋卷

By the third day of the national mourning period, the storefront of Fresh Mart was completely dominated by capitalist sorrow. In the foreground, a single, miserable seagull was pecking at an expired hotdog bun on the pavement, setting the perfect depressing atmosphere. Behind the counter, Chika was stirring a giant pot of gloomy black sesame porridge in a slow, clockwise motion, preparing the store's latest scam: the "Royal Fasting Breakfast."

到了全国哀悼期的第三天，“新鲜玛特”的店面已经被资本主义的悲伤彻底统治。透过玻璃看去，在视线的前景中，一只凄惨的海鸥正啄食着人行道上的一块过期热狗面包，烘托出了完美的抑郁氛围。柜台后面，千夏正顺时针缓慢地搅动着一大锅阴郁的黑芝麻糊，准备推出他们最新的骗局：“皇家绝食哀悼早餐”。

Sayuri, draped in her sheer black widow's veil and exuding an undeniable, tragic glamor, was actively trying to befriend a weeping, wealthy businessman. With a dramatic sigh, she presented him with a completely burnt, charcoal-black omelette, charging him a fortune for what she claimed was the late king's favorite "mourning dish."

小百合披着那层半透明的黑色寡妇面纱，散发着一种令人无法抗拒的、悲剧般的迷人魅力，她正试图结交并忽悠一位泣不成声的富商。伴随着一声充满戏剧性的叹息，她端上了一份彻底烤焦成黑炭的煎蛋卷，声称这是已故国王最爱的“哀悼特供”，并以此敲诈了一笔巨款。

In the back room, Miyetsuko was reading the morning financial reports aloud while Fujiwara eagerly took dictation, ensuring they squeezed every last shilling out of the grieving public. The sheer influx of cash was creating a literal whirlpool of greed in Fujiwara's mind, though keeping the authorities blind to their unhinged pricing was becoming exceedingly troublesome.

在后仓里，美悦子正大声朗读着早间的财务报告，而藤原就像个贪婪的书记员一样拼命做着听写记录，确保他们从悲痛的公众身上榨干最后一先令。巨额现金的涌入在藤原的脑海中形成了一个纯粹由贪婪构成的漩涡，尽管要瞒过物价局对他们这种丧心病狂的定价的调查，已经变得极其麻烦。

At the entrance, Rin stood like a grim reaper, ready to paralyse any insufficiently sad customer with pure terror. If anyone's lips so much as twitched outwards into a smile, she would aggressively swing her metal bat downwards, demanding an immediate "Happiness Penalty" for their treasonous joy.

在入口处，铃像个冷酷的死神一样站着，随时准备用纯粹的恐惧让任何不够悲伤的顾客吓得瘫痪。如果谁的嘴唇敢向外扬起哪怕一丝微笑，她就会将手中的金属棒球棍向下猛砸，要求对方立刻为这种叛国的喜悦支付“快乐违约金”。

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, noting that while their business model was doubtless problematic legally, in a figurative sense, it was a masterpiece of market adaptation. She handed Fujiwara a new batch of promotional flyers, warning him to proofread them carefully, as spelling errors were often a highly accurate barometer for exposing corporate fraud.

美悦子推了推眼镜，指出尽管他们的商业模式在法律上毫无疑问地是存在问题的，但在比喻意义上，这简直是市场适应的绝佳杰作。她递给藤原一叠新的促销传单，警告他必须仔细校对，因为拼写错误往往是暴露商业欺诈的最准确的晴雨表。

Suddenly, Fujiwara clutched his stomach, claiming his long-removed appendix was ghost-aching from the stress of manual labor. He suggested they mechanize the grieving process by blasting a painfully depressing violin concerto over the speakers, while hiring Miyetsuko to act as a fake linguist to translate the dead king's honourable last words into a catchy slogan for their overpriced toilet paper.

突然，藤原捂住肚子，声称他早年割掉的阑尾因为重体力劳动的压力产生了幻痛。他提议他们应该将悲痛的流程彻底机械化，比如在广播里大声播放极其致郁的小提琴协奏曲，并让美悦子伪装成语言学家，把已故国王那些高尚的遗言翻译成推销高价卫生纸的洗脑广告词。
