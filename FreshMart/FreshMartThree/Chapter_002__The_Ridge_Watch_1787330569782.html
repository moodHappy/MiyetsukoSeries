Chapter 2: The Ridge Watch

第二章：北岭暗哨

Nightfall on Donglai Island brought no relief. Instead, the heat trapped by the island’s rocky spine simply shifted from a blinding glare to a heavy, humid blanket that clung to the skin like wet wool. The temperature stubbornly hovered near forty degrees, turning the midnight trek up the steep, winding path toward the northern ridge into an endurance test.

📌 完整翻译

夜幕降临在东来岛上并未带来任何缓解。相反，被岛上岩石脊梁困住的热气仅仅是从刺眼的耀光转变为沉重、潮湿的毯子，像湿羊毛一样紧贴在皮肤上。气温顽固地徘徊在四十度左右，使得午夜沿着通往北岭的陡峭蜿蜒小径的跋涉，变成了一场耐力考验。

    （指天色变暗的时刻）

  • rocky spine

  • blinding glare
    = 刺眼的耀光
    （强烈且令人眼睛难以适应的光）

  • humid blanket
    = 潮湿的毯子

    （形象描绘湿热感受）

    = 徘徊在四十度左右
    （温度几乎保持在四十摄氏度）

东莱岛的夜幕降临，并未带来丝毫解脱。相反，岛屿脊骨处积聚的热浪由刺眼的白昼强光转变为一层湿热厚重的毛毯，如湿羊毛般紧紧贴在皮肤上。温度顽固地徘徊在四十度上下，使得午夜时分攀登陡峭曲折的北岭小路成了一场耐力考验。

Fujiwara moved first, his boots crunching quietly against the loose shale. Behind him, Sayuri kept pace, her breathing controlled despite the burning in her lungs, clutching a battered tactical flashlight in one hand. Further back, Rin covered their six, her eyes scanning the darkened tree line with cold, professional vigilance.

藤原走在最前面，靴子踩在松散的页岩上发出轻微的沙沙声。小百合紧随其后，尽管肺部如同火烧，呼吸却保持着平稳，手里紧紧攥着一把破旧的战术手电筒。再往后是负责殿后的铃，她目光冷峻而职业，警惕地扫视着黑暗中的林线。

"The maritime patrol vessel has cut its lights," Miyetsuko murmured, keeping her voice low as she stepped up beside Fujiwara behind a thick outcropping of volcanic rock. Through a pair of military-grade binoculars, she tracked the gray silhouette sliding across the dark water below. "They’re running dark. That means they’re either dropping an insertion team, or calibrating local ground sensors."

“那艘海上巡逻艇熄灯了，”美悦子低声说道，借着一块厚实的火山岩掩护，快步来到藤原身边。她举着一副军用望远镜，追踪着下方暗流中滑行的灰色轮廓。“它们在隐蔽航行。这意味着要么是在投放渗透小队，要么是在校准岛上的地面传感器。”

Fujiwara lowered his own binoculars, his expression grim. They had left the illusion of the "Fresh Mart" behind; survival here was raw and immediate. "Sensors," he spat out quietly. "The state doesn't spend fuel on stealth unless they’re tracking a specific signal. Someone’s been using the emergency radio band."

藤原放下望远镜，神色冷峻。他们已经抛弃了“新鲜玛特”的幻象；这里的生存变得原始而急迫。“传感器，”他低声啐了一口，“如果不是在追踪某个特定信号，国家才不会浪费燃料玩隐形。有人一直在用紧急无线电频段。”

Rin caught up to them, wiping sweat from her brow with the back of her sleeve. "Not us. We’ve kept the transmitters off since the grid died. That means there’s another active relay on the island. Someone else is out here—either a black-ops spotter or another group of castaways the government forgot to wipe out."

铃追了上来，用袖口擦去额头的汗水。“不是我们。自从电网瘫痪后，我们就把发射器关了。这意味着岛上还有另一个活跃的中继站。还有别人在这儿——要么是黑行动小组的观察员，要么是政府还没来得及抹除的另一拨难民。”

Up ahead, silhouetted against the starlit sky, the skeletal metal frame of an abandoned telecommunications tower rose from the ridge. A small, blinking red light pulsed near the top—a light that shouldn't have been functioning given that the island’s power grid had collapsed days ago.

前方，在星空的衬托下，一座废弃电信塔的金属骨架耸立在山脊上。顶部附近闪烁着一盏微弱的红光——鉴于岛上的电网几天前就已经瘫痪，这盏灯本不该亮着。

"An independent power source," Miyetsuko analyzed, her eyes narrowing as she pointed toward the base of the tower. A small, camouflaged tent was pitched right beside the concrete foundation, illuminated by the faint, green glow of a laptop screen. "They aren't just watching the perimeter. They've set up a localized intelligence node."

“一个独立电源，”美悦子眯起眼睛，指向塔基方向进行分析。混凝土基座旁搭着一顶伪装帐篷，被笔记本电脑微弱的绿色屏幕光芒照亮。“他们不仅在监视周边，还建立了一个本地情报节点。”

Sayuri looked at Fujiwara, her eyes reflecting the distant, blinking red beacon. There was no fear in her expression now, only a quiet, resolute calm. "What do we do, Fujiwara? Do we bypass them, or do we take the node?"

小百合看向藤原，眼中倒映着远处闪烁的红色信标。她的神情里已无恐惧，取而代之的是一种沉着而坚定的平静。“我们怎么做，藤原？是绕过他们，还是端掉这个节点？”

Fujiwara checked the magazine of his sidearm with a familiar, mechanical click, a cold smirk touching the corners of his mouth.

藤原伴随着熟悉的机械咔哒声检查了一下配枪的弹匣，嘴角泛起一记冰冷的冷笑。

"Bypass?" Fujiwara shook his head, stepping past them toward the ridge. "We’ve been running from their surveillance long enough. If they want to play spy games in forty-degree heat, let's go introduce ourselves and see what kind of secrets their little box is holding."

“绕过？”藤原摇了摇头，从他们身旁走过，朝山脊走去。“我们在他们的监视下逃得够久了。如果他们想在四十度的高温里玩间谍游戏，那我们就去自我介绍一下，顺便看看他们的小黑盒子里藏着什么秘密。”
