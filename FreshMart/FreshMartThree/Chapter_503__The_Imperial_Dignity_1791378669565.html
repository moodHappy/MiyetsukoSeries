Chapter 503: The Imperial Dignity

发布时间：2026/10/7 21:11:08

第五百零三章：皇家的庄严

The salon was still echoing with Rin’s cheerful proposal to treat the police leadership to an all-inclusive soap bath when Sayuri gracefully set down her teacup. Her lips curved into a slow, disdainful smile, and she shook her head with supreme, sovereign elegance.

放映厅里还回荡着小铃那提议请警务高层来洗免费泡泡浴的清脆笑声，小百合却已优雅地放下了手中的薄胎骨瓷茶杯。她唇角噙着一抹若有若无的冷艳讥诮，微微摇了摇头，浑身上下散发出一股居高临下的高贵威仪。

"No, Rin. That is entirely beneath us," Sayuri spoke, her voice velvety yet carrying the absolute weight of an imperial matriarch. "Your Majesty holds dominion over the four seas and commands all under heaven. Why on earth should we stoop to flattering or pacifying mere street bureaucrats? In my humble view, we need not waste our courtesans or good spirits on them. Simply dispatch a single junior footman with an imperial decree to padlock the gates of the entire Metropolitan Police Department. Only by shutting down their entire headquarters do we truly display the absolute dignity of our royal house."

“不可，小铃，这成何体统，”小百合轻启朱唇，嗓音虽轻柔如丝，却透着一股号令中宫的无上凤仪，“陛下富有四海，威加宇内，岂有反过来巴结讨好区区几个差役官僚的道理？依我看，何须拿咱们楼里的极品姑娘与好酒去便宜他们？只需随手差遣一名外门的小厮下人，持一道手谕过去，把他们整个东京都警视厅的大门给彻底查封落锁，方能彰显我们皇家的无上庄严。”

A single sentence turned the tide and settled the empire's heavens.

一语定乾坤。

Fujiwara froze for a fraction of a second, his eyes widening in pure, ecstatic realization. He looked at Sayuri as if beholding the sharpest tactical mind on Earth. With a booming, triumphant shout, he reached out his massive arms, swept Sayuri entirely off the cushion, and crushed her lush, fragrant body against his chest in a fierce, possessive embrace.

藤原整个人僵了半秒，双眼骤然迸射出极度狂喜与顿悟的光芒。他看着怀里的小百合，简直像看着这颗星球上最合他心意的并肩女帝。伴随着一声畅快至极的豪迈长啸，他长臂一揽，不由分说地将小百合整个人拦腰抱起，把那具丰腴温软、暗香浮动的娇躯狠狠勒进自己怀里。

"Brilliant! Magnificently spoken!" Fujiwara roared with shameless delight, kissing her lips and cheeks with greedy passion. "You truly are my irreplaceable imperial consort! My wise matriarch! I was almost led astray by these little airheads talking about bubble baths and sweet talk! Why bother cajoling dogs when you can simply dissolve their doghouse?! You hit the nail right on the head!"

“妙啊！说得简直太他妈解气了！”藤原放肆地狂笑起来，大手托着她的细腰，低头在她娇艳欲滴的红唇与脸颊上狠狠亲了好几大口，“真真不愧是本帝王的贤内助！朕的中宫皇后！老子刚才差点被这帮小娘皮带偏了思路，还真琢磨起给那帮饭桶洗澡赔笑了！当主子的哪有给看门狗赔笑脸的道理？直接把他们的狗窝给一锅端了，这才是咱们该有的排场！”

He whipped his head toward the shivering runner still kneeling by the double doors, bellowing with absolute imperial arrogance.

他猛地扭过头，目光如刀般射向仍跪在雕花大门边瑟瑟发抖的小厮，满脸都是不可一世的狂妄恶霸相：

"Did you hear the Lady?!" Fujiwara barked. "Forget negotiations! Pick the lowest-ranking errand boy in the back garden! Hand him our gold-seal executive padlock, send him straight to Kasumigaseki, and chain the front revolving doors of the Metropolitan Police Headquarters shut! Tell them that by imperial decree, their agency is officially suspended until further notice! If anyone asks for a warrant, show them our family crest!"

“夫人的话你听清楚没有？！”藤原雷霆大喝，“撤回所有协调公关！从后花园里随便挑一个扫地跑腿的最下等小厮！给他带上一把咱们印着金徽的特制行政大铁锁，直接大摇大摆开去霞关警视厅正门口，把他们总部的一楼旋转玻璃大门给老子结结实实绞死封死！告诉他们，奉皇家特旨，自即日起东京都警视厅全员停业整顿！要是哪个不长眼的敢问要搜查令和公文，直接拿咱们藤原家的家徽戳他们脸上去！”

"Y-Yes, Your Majesty! Immediately!" The runner bowed frantically, scrambling backward out of the salon on all fours to deliver the decree.

“是、谨遵法旨！奴才这就去办！”小厮吓得连连叩头，连滚带爬地倒退着退出大殿，一溜烟飞奔去传达这桩惊世骇俗的封门特令。

Chika, bouncing slightly in her unbuttoned robe while cradling her perky, snow-white assets, stared in utter awe, her jaw practically on the floor.

千夏光着两条白嫩的长腿坐在软榻上，双手下意识地托了托胸前经过顶级养护、粉嫩挺拔的雪峰，下巴惊得差点砸在胸脯上，满眼都是崇拜与不可思议。

"Wow... Sayuri-nee, that is so outrageously cool," Chika whispered, eyes shining with spoiled admiration. "Sending an errand boy to close down the national police department... That's a billion times more vicious than just suing them! The Boss is truly the final boss of capitalism!"

“哇塞……小百合姐，这也太霸气了吧，”千夏两眼放光，娇滴滴地捧着小脸惊呼，“派个扫院子的小厮去把全警视厅的大门给锁了……这可比打官司或者查税狠上千万倍啊！咱们老板果然才是站在食物链顶端的终极大恶霸！”

Miyetsuko curled closer against Fujiwara's thigh, her face flushing with sweet, pliant adoration as she rested her chin on his knee.

美悦子身段柔软地贴在藤原腿边，把泛着浅浅红晕的俏脸乖巧地枕在他膝头，眼眸里泛着水汪汪的温顺与依恋。

"A decisive tactical decapitation," Miyetsuko purred, tracing the fabric of his trousers. "Why deal with district inspectors when you can simply invalidate the institution? Sayuri-nee's strategic instinct is flawless. That is how the imperial house preserves its dignity without firing a single bullet."

“教科书般的战略降维打击，”美悦子细声细语地轻喃着，修长的指尖眷恋地抚平他西裤上的褶皱，“与其跟底层的小警长费口舌扯皮，不如从根子上把整个执法机关给物理注销。小百合姐的战术眼光真是绝了。这才是咱们皇室不费一兵一卒、尽显至高威严该有的格调呢。”

From her cushion, blind Rin clapped her hands in pure, angelic glee, laughing until her little shoulders bounced.

地毯角落里的盲人小铃开心地拍着小手，发出银铃般清脆纯净的欢快娇笑，小肩膀一颤一颤的。

"Um... hello, Master? Rin thinks that is so funny!" Rin giggled, tilting her head toward the ceiling. "The poor police commissioner won't even be able to get inside to fetch his morning coffee! He will have to stand outside the locked glass doors in the cold wind, filling out an apology form on the sidewalk! Sayuri-nee really knows how to bully people in the most elegant way!"

“唔……主人听得见吗？小铃觉得这简直太好玩啦！”小铃歪着小脑袋，冲着天花板软糯地咯咯笑着，“这下那位倒霉的总监大人怕是连进大楼喝口早咖啡都进不去啦！只能裹着风衣站在大风里，趴在反锁的玻璃大门上，规规矩矩地给主人填赔罪申请书呢！小百合姐姐欺负起人来，段位可真是全天下最高贵的呢！”

Sayuri nestled comfortably into Fujiwara's chest, her eyelashes fluttering with serene, matriarchal satisfaction as she ran her fingers playfully along his jaw.

小百合心安理得地蜷在藤原宽厚温暖的怀抱深处，长长的睫毛温顺地微颤着，眼底流转着母仪天下的从容与宠溺，纤手轻柔地抚弄着他的下巴。

"As long as Master is pleased," Sayuri whispered with sweet velvet devotion. "Let the servants deal with the outside chaos. Inside these walls, Your Majesty's bathwater is warm, the milk is sweet, and your kingdom remains forever untouchable."

“只要能博得陛下一笑，臣妾便心满意足了，”小百合软语呢喃，娇媚入骨地倚在他胸口，“外面的喧嚣脏乱，由下人去打发便是。在这深闺禁苑之内，陛下的温汤正暖，鲜乳正甜，这片只属于您的极乐江山，永远都容不得任何凡夫俗子染指分毫。”
