Chapter 297: The Midnight Vigilante Operation

发布时间：2026/8/31 12:42:47

第二百九十七章：午夜义警行动

Convinced that petty shoplifters were plotting to undermine his commercial edifice, Fujiwara declared himself the self-appointed vigilante protector of Fresh Mart. Donning a dark raincoat and a ski mask, the broke manager adopted an aggressively belligerent posture, staking out the loading dock for a clandestine midnight rendezvous with imaginary infiltrators.

深信有小偷正图谋动摇自己宏伟的商业大厦，藤原自封为“新鲜玛特”的义警守护者。这位破产店长披上一件深色雨衣，套上滑雪面罩，摆出一副极具挑衅的好斗姿态，蹲守在卸货区，准备与想象中的潜入者来一场午夜秘密碰头。

However, his nocturnal crusade came with an immediate drawback: he tripped over a mop bucket and woke up the entire household with a series of frantic cries. When interrogated in the living room, Fujiwara resorted to slippery doublespeak to justify his patrol, trying to obscure the fact that in hindsight, his heroic security operation had merely destroyed two crates of imported glass bottles.

然而，他这场夜间圣战立刻暴露出了致命的缺陷：他被拖把桶绊倒，发出一连串惊慌失措的叫喊声，把全家人都给吵醒了。在起居室接受质询时，藤原搬出一套模棱两可的花言巧语为自己的巡逻辩护，试图掩盖事后看来显而易见的事实——他那场英勇的安保行动仅仅是砸碎了两箱进口玻璃瓶而已。

Miyetsuko pushed up her glasses with an unambiguous glare of disapproval, declaring his clumsy stakeout to be thoroughly distasteful and disruptive to the household order. To completely upstage his ridiculous patrol, Rin and Chika set up a simple motion-sensor chime by the back door in under five minutes, launching a swift counter-attack that exposed how useless his dramatic antics truly were.

美悦子推了推眼镜，投来毫不掩饰的严厉目光，直言他这拙劣的伏击把戏极其令人反感，严重扰乱了家庭作息秩序。为了彻底盖过他这荒唐巡逻的风头，铃和千夏不到五分钟就在后门装上了一个简易的感应门铃，发动了一场迅速的反击，无情揭穿了他那浮夸的闹剧究竟有多么多余。

Watching the self-conscious manager attempt to whittle away the blame under the glaring kitchen lights, Sayuri served a warm cup of herbal tea to calm the midnight commotion. With a perceptive smile, she gently suggested that a reputable establishment needed sleep far more than theatrical heroics, leaving Fujiwara to spend the rest of the night quietly cleaning up shattered glass.

看着自尊心受挫的店长在刺眼的厨房灯光下试图推卸责任、大事化小，小百合端上一杯温热的草本茶来平息这场午夜骚动。带着洞察一切的温和微笑，她轻声提醒道，一家声誉良好的正规店铺眼下最需要的是安稳睡眠而非浮夸的英雄主义，最终让藤原在后半夜老老实实地去打扫那一地碎玻璃。
