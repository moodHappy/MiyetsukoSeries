第三百六十章：海军海啸

By late afternoon, the docks of Donglai Island were entirely overwhelmed by a surging, sea-of-blue tide. Thousands of battle-fatigued US sailors, starved of civilian life and female company for 250 grueling days, swarmed off the transport vessels like a ravenous horde.

下午傍晚时分，东莱岛的码头彻底被一片汹涌的“海军蓝”海洋所淹没。数千名饱受战事折磨、在海上熬了整整250天没见过女人的美国水兵，如同蝗虫过境般从运输船上蜂拥而上。

Attracted by the glowing neon sign and whispered rumors of a specialized port-call resort, the massive crowd surged directly toward Fresh Mart. The glass storefront rattled under the pressure as the front doors burst open, letting in a wall of raw testosterone, military cologne, and unbridled desperation.

被闪烁的霓虹灯招牌和关于特种港口度假村的私密传闻所吸引，庞大的人流径直朝着“新鲜玛特”汹涌扑来。玻璃店面在人群的压迫下瑟瑟发抖，前门“轰”地一声被撞开，夹杂着浓烈雄性荷尔蒙、军用古龙水以及压抑已久狂热气息的人潮瞬间灌满全场。

Standing right at the entrance in her shortest maid apron, Chika shook like a leaf in a hurricane. Confronted by towering, bearded sailors who looked like they could bench-press a tank, she desperately squeaked out her rehearsed greeting, waving a fluffy pink duster like a distress flag.

千夏穿着最清凉的女仆围裙站在正门口，宛如狂风暴雨中的一片落叶般瑟瑟发抖。面对着那些身材魁梧、满脸络腮胡、看起来能把坦克当杠铃举的大兵，她慌乱地挥舞着粉红色的鸡毛掸子当作求救信号，结结巴巴地喊着排练好的台词。

"W-Welcome to Fresh Mart! Hotdog—I mean, welcome home, big guys!" Chika stammered, blushing furiously as a massive sailor hoisted her up effortlessly to inspect her merchandise, roaring with delight as the cash register behind her started ringing non-stop.

“欢、欢迎光临新鲜玛特！热狗——不对，欢迎回家，大块头们！”千夏羞得满脸通红地结巴着。一名巨型水兵毫不费力地把她整个人抱起来检查“店内商品”，身后的收银机顿时发出了疯狂连击的清脆响声。

A few feet away, Rin was holding court with lethal, terrifying efficiency. Dressed in her customized high-slit apron, she sneered at the approaching line of admirers, but her fierce resistance and sharp, cold glares only drove the sailors into a frenzy of aggressive bidding.

几步开外，铃正以一种极具杀伤力和威慑力的效率坐镇全场。她穿着特制的开叉围裙，冷笑着睥睨着排队凑上来的追求者们，然而她那带刺的抗拒姿态和冰冷的眼神非但没吓退人，反而彻底点燃了这群水兵狂热的竞价欲望。

"Back off, you horny apes! Fifty dollars just to look at my face, and another hundred if you want me to step on your boots!" Rin snapped, kicking a bewildered sailor in the shin while mercilessly pocketing a wad of crisp hundred-dollar bills into her apron pocket.

“离远点，你们这群发情的猩猩！光看本小姐一眼五十美金，想让我踩你的靴子还得再加一百！”铃厉声呵斥道，一脚踢在一个满脸懵逼的水兵小腿上，顺手却毫不客气地把厚厚一沓百元大钞塞进围裙兜里。

Behind the main counter, Miyetsuko operated the POS terminal with the cold precision of a military sniper. Her fingers flew across the keyboard faster than a machine gun, simultaneously scanning barcodes, upselling industrial-grade lubricants, and calculating hourly revenue distribution for the KPI leaderboard.

主柜台后方，美悦子正以堪比军事狙击手的冷酷精准操作着收银终端。她的手指在键盘上敲击得比机关枪还快，一边飞速扫描条形码、强力推销工业级润滑油，一边实时计算着 KPI 排行榜上的每小时营业额分配。

Meanwhile, hidden deep in the back storage room, Fujiwara was already facing his worst nightmare. Surrounded by a group of burly, heavily tattooed sailors who had enthusiastically opted for the "alternative VIP sector," the terrified store manager could only clutch his apron and let out a shrill, helpless shriek into the darkness.

与此同时，在后仓的阴暗深处，藤原正迎来他人生的至暗时刻。他被一群纹身满面、壮硕如牛、专门选了“小众VIP专区”的水兵团团围住。这位惊恐万状的店长只能死死护住围裙，在黑暗中发出了绝望而凄厉的惨叫。

Up in the elevated VIP glass booth, Sayuri reclined on a velvet sofa with effortless grace. Sipping her vintage champagne and watching the chaotic, highly profitable human tsunami flood her store, she smiled warmly, knowing that the greatest adult entertainment empire in the Pacific was officially born.

在高处的VIP玻璃包厢内，小百合正慵懒优雅地斜倚在天鹅绒沙发上。她端着复古香槟，俯瞰着下方冲刷店铺的混乱且暴利的狂热人潮，欣慰地笑了——太平洋上最伟大的成人娱乐帝国，在这一刻正式诞生了。
