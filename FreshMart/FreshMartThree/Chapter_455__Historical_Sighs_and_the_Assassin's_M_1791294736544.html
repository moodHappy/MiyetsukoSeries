第四百五十五章：历史的叹息与刺客的推拿

The afternoon tea time continued in the Fresh Mart penthouse. Having exhausted the topics of European banking and Yakuza realignments, Fujiwara casually picked up a thick, leather-bound book from the coffee table. He had recently been studying classical Chinese history in preparation for a massive corporate expansion into the Asian market.

“新鲜玛特”顶层公寓的下午茶时光还在继续。在聊完了欧洲银行业和黑道重组的话题后，藤原漫不经心地从茶几上拿起一本厚重的真皮装帧书。为了准备对亚洲市场进行大规模的资本扩张，他最近一直在研究中国古典历史。

"You know, the long river of Chinese history is absolutely fascinating," Fujiwara sighed, shaking his head with a mix of admiration and amusement. "It's filled with brilliant empires, but also some of the most frustrating, agonizingly avoidable tragedies. If you ladies had to pick the single most regrettable, 'sigh-inducing' moment in their history, what would it be?"

“说真的，中国历史的长河简直令人着迷，”藤原叹了口气，带着几分赞赏与戏谑摇了摇头，“那里有辉煌璀璨的帝国，但也充满了最让人抓狂、明明可以避免的悲剧。如果让你们选出中国历史上最令人扼腕叹息、最让人拍大腿的时刻，你们会选哪个？”

Miyetsuko didn't even hesitate. She leaned forward on her plush cushion, adjusting her glasses with a look of pure, professional disdain.

美悦子连想都没想。她坐在柔软的护臀垫上向前倾了倾身子，推了推眼镜，脸上写满了纯粹、职业的鄙夷。

"The Hongmen Banquet," Miyetsuko stated coldly. "Xiang Yu, the absolute overlord, had his greatest rival, Liu Bang, sitting right in front of him. He had the troops, he had the advantage, he had the perfect opportunity to smash his skull in! Instead, what does he do? He hesitates. He drinks wine and watches a stupid sword dance! If I had been there with my baseball bat, Liu Bang’s head would have been knocked out of the tent for a home run. Hesitation is the death of power."

“鸿门宴，”美悦子冷冷地说道。“西楚霸王项羽，拥有绝对优势的霸主，他最大的死敌刘邦就坐在他面前。他有军队，有优势，有砸碎那家伙脑袋的完美时机！结果他干了什么？他居然犹豫了。他竟然坐在那儿喝酒看什么破剑舞！如果我当时带着棒球棍在场，刘邦的脑袋早就被我当成全垒打轰出帐篷了。在权力面前犹豫，就是自寻死路。”

Chika snapped her fingers, vigorously chewing her mochi. "Ugh, true! But my biggest historical sigh goes to the Song Dynasty! Boss, do you realize the Song Dynasty produced something like sixty or seventy percent of the global GDP at the time? They were insanely rich! But their military was so soft they just paid massive amounts of silver and silk to the nomadic tribes so they wouldn't get invaded!"

千夏打了个响指，用力嚼着嘴里的麻薯。“呃，确实！但我最大的历史叹息绝对要给大宋朝！老板，您知道宋朝当时的GDP占了全球的百分之六七十吗？他们富得流油！可是他们的军事软弱到了极点，居然只能靠给游牧民族交大量的岁币和丝绸来换取不被挨打！”

Chika tapped her tablet furiously, deeply offended by the bad economics. "They literally paid a premium monthly subscription fee for the 'Please Don't Beat Us Up' package! If I were the Song Emperor, I would have used that cash to buy every mercenary on the continent, shorted the Mongol cavalry’s horse feed supply, and initiated a hostile takeover of the entire Eurasian steppe!"

千夏疯狂地敲击着平板，被这种糟糕的经济学狠狠冒犯到了。“他们这简直就是在花高价购买‘求求你别打我’的包月VIP服务！如果我是宋朝皇帝，我早就用那笔现金买下整个大陆的所有雇佣兵，做空蒙古铁骑的马草供应，然后对整个欧亚大草原发起恶意收购了！”

Sayuri offered a delicate, musical sigh, shaking her head as she leaned gracefully against Fujiwara's shoulder. Her eyes, however, held a glint of aristocratic ruthlessness.

小百合发出了一声娇柔悦耳的叹息，优雅地靠在藤原的肩膀上摇了摇头。然而，她的眼底却闪烁着属于贵族的冷酷。

"Those are tragic, but nothing is more heartbreakingly pathetic than the Mawei Courier Station incident during the Tang Dynasty," Sayuri murmured, her voice dripping with elegant disdain. "Emperor Xuanzong supposedly loved his favorite concubine, Yang Guifei, more than life itself. But the moment his mutinous soldiers demanded her death, he panicked and let them strangle her to save his own throne. A man who sacrifices his woman to save his own skin isn't an Emperor; he's a coward."

“那些确实很悲惨，但没有哪个比唐朝的马嵬驿兵变更让人觉得可悲可叹了，”小百合呢喃着，嗓音里透着优雅的鄙夷。“据说唐玄宗爱他的宠妃杨贵妃胜过爱自己的生命。可是，当他手下哗变的士兵要求处死她时，他竟然慌了神，为了保住自己的皇位，眼睁睁看着她被勒死。一个靠牺牲自己的女人来保全性命的男人，根本不配当皇帝；他只是个懦夫。”

Sayuri looked up at Fujiwara, gently trailing a manicured fingernail down his chest with a deeply devoted, lewd smile. "If my Master were faced with a mutiny, I know he would slaughter the entire army, burn the empire to the ground, and still make it back to the bedroom in time to ruin my panties. Right, darling?"

小百合仰起头看着藤原，用修剪精致的指甲在他胸前轻轻划过，露出一个无比深情又带着几分下流的微笑。“如果换作是我的主人遇到兵变，我知道他一定会屠光整支军队，把整个帝国烧成灰烬，然后还能准时回到卧室里，把人家的内裤撕碎。对吧，亲爱的？”

"I'd nuke the planet before I let anyone touch a single cheek of your glorious ass," Fujiwara grinned, kissing her forehead.

“在让任何人碰到你那完美屁股的哪怕一丁点边缘之前，我就会先用核弹把这颗星球给炸了，”藤原咧嘴一笑，亲了亲她的额头。

In the corner, blind Rin had been listening quietly, her unseeing eyes blinking as she thoughtfully rolled a strawberry between her fingers.

在角落里，盲人铃一直安静地听着，空洞的眼睛眨了眨，若有所思地在手指间转动着一颗草莓。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered softly, raising her hand like a polite student. "I always felt really bad for Mr. Jing Ke. You know, when he tried to assassinate the King of Qin?"

“唔……大家听我说好吗？”铃轻声呢喃着，像个礼貌的小学生一样举起了手。“我一直觉得荆轲先生真的很可惜呢。就是他去刺杀秦王的那次？”

Fujiwara raised an eyebrow. "Jing Ke? The famous assassin who hid a dagger in a map, missed his throw, and got hacked to pieces? Yes, a very tragic failure."

藤原挑了挑眉。“荆轲刺秦王？那个把匕首藏在地图里，结果飞刀没扔中，最后被砍成肉泥的著名刺客？确实，一场非常悲壮的失败。”

"Yes, it's very sad," Rin nodded earnestly. "He chased the King around a big pillar but his arms were too short. I just think... he used the completely wrong approach. If he had just rolled up his sleeves and told the King of Qin, 'Your Majesty, reviewing all these bamboo scrolls must make your neck so stiff, let me give you a massage,' he wouldn't have needed a knife at all."

“是的，真的好让人叹息呀，”铃认真地点了点头。“他绕着大柱子追着秦王跑，可惜胳膊太短了。我只是觉得……他用的方法完全错啦。如果他只是挽起袖子，对秦王说：‘大王，您每天批阅这么多竹简，颈椎一定很僵硬吧，让我来给您做个推拿吧’，他根本连刀都不需要带的。”

Rin smiled her sweet, utterly innocent, terrifying smile.

铃露出了她那种甜美、纯洁无瑕，却又令人毛骨悚然的微笑。

"He just needed to press his thumbs right into the King's third cervical vertebra, apply three hundred pounds of succubus pressure, and snap. The unification of China would have been permanently cancelled, and Mr. Jing Ke wouldn't have had to run so much. Running in those long robes looks very tiring."

“他只需要把大拇指直接按进大王的第三节颈椎里，施加三百磅的魅魔握力，然后‘咔嚓’一下。统一六国的大业就永久取消啦，荆轲先生也不用跑得那么累了。穿着那种长袍跑步看起来真的很辛苦呢。”

The entire penthouse fell into a stunned, delighted silence. Chika slowly lowered her tablet, Miyetsuko stared at the blind girl with profound professional respect, and Sayuri burst into a fit of elegant, helpless giggles.

整个顶层公寓陷入了一阵震惊而充满乐趣的沉默。千夏慢慢放下了平板电脑，美悦子用一种深沉的职业敬意盯着这个盲眼女孩，而小百合则忍不住爆发出一阵优雅而无可奈何的娇笑。

Fujiwara let out a booming roar of laughter, shaking his head at the sheer, terrifying brilliance of his household.

藤原爆发出一阵响彻云霄的狂笑，对他这群后宫那令人胆寒的奇思妙想佩服得直摇头。

"History truly is written by the victors," Fujiwara grinned broadly, finishing his tea. "But if I ever get sent back in time to build an empire, I am definitely putting Rin in charge of the royal health spa. The casualty rate would be magnificent!"

“历史确实是由胜利者书写的，”藤原咧开嘴大笑着，喝完了杯里的茶。“但如果我哪天穿越回古代去建立帝国，我绝对要让小铃去掌管皇家养生馆。那杀伤力绝对是壮观级别的！”
