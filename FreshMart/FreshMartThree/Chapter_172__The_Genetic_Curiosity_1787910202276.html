Chapter 172: The Genetic Curiosity

发布时间：2026/8/28 17:43:21

第百七十二章：家族基因的吸引力

As Fujiwara sat back down, expecting his women to express at least a modicum of shock or polite disgust at his grand-uncle’s outrageous conquest, the living room took a sharp, violently unhinged turn.

正当藤原重新坐回沙发上，以为这群女人至少会假惺惺地对二大爷的疯狂行径表示一番震惊或无语时，起居室里的画风却冷不丁地来了个一百八十度的大转弯。

Rin froze mid-laugh, her eyes suddenly narrowing into a speculative, wicked glint. She slowly leaned across the table, resting her chin on her hands as a mischievous smirk spread across her face.

铃脸上的狂笑戛然而止，双眼微微眯起，闪烁起某种跃跃欲试的危险光芒。她慢悠悠地把胳膊搭在桌上，托着下巴露出一抹坏笑：

"You know..." Rin murmured, her voice dripping with playful curiosity. "Listening to you describe that old monster makes me strangely curious. If he's really that powerful... honestly, I kind of want to taste the old man myself."

“话说回来……”铃挑了挑眉，语气里透着一股让人跌破眼镜的恶趣味，“听你这么绘声绘色地一描述，弄得人家心里直痒痒。如果那老头真有这么大本事……说真的，我突然也想尝尝二大爷到底是什么滋味了。”

Fujiwara nearly choked on his coffee, coughing violently as he stared at her. "Are you out of your mind?! That's my grand-uncle we're talking about! He's seventy years old!"

藤原刚喝进嘴的一口咖啡差点当场喷出来，剧烈咳嗽着瞪大了眼睛：“你疯了吧？！那可是我二大爷！他今年七十岁了啊！”

Before Fujiwara could even catch his breath, an even more shocking confession chimed in from across the table.

还没等藤原顺过气来，桌子对面紧接着传出了一个更加石破天惊的声音。

Sayuri’s face flushed bright, burning crimson. She fidgeted nervously with the rim of her teacup, keeping her gaze lowered as a soft, deeply embarrassed whimper escaped her lips. "W-Well... actually... to tell the truth... while Rin was talking just now, I was thinking the exact same thing in my heart... I just didn't have the nerve to say it out loud..."

只见小百合的整张脸瞬间红到了耳根，烫得仿佛能煎鸡蛋。她局促不安地用手指绕着茶杯边缘，水润的眼眸死死盯着桌面，羞耻得声音都在发颤：“那、那个……其实……刚才听铃这么说的时候，我心里其实也在偷偷这么想……只不过觉得太不知羞耻了，没好意思开口……”

The room plunged into an absolute, stunned silence.

起居室里瞬间陷入了一片诡异而绝对的死寂。

Fujiwara sat paralyzed on the sofa, his brain completely short-circuiting. The legendary courtesan had surrendered, and now the women of his own household were lining up to volunteer for the grand-uncle's legacy?

藤原直接石化在沙发上，大脑当场彻底宕机。好家伙，那头牌花魁刚哭喊着要终身免费，结果自己家里这两个也按捺不住准备组团去投奔二大爷了？！

Miyetsuko calmly pushed up her glasses, taking a slow sip of her tea as she offered a characteristically clinical evaluation.

美悦子却冷静地推了推眼镜，慢条斯理地抿了一口茶，从容不迫地给出专业点评：

"From a genetic and psychological perspective, this is entirely logical," Miyetsuko declared with a straight face. "Absolute, unbridled physical dominance transcends generational boundaries. The women of this household simply possess a healthy, pragmatic appreciation for superior biological raw power."

“从生物学和进化心理学的角度来看，这完全合乎逻辑，”美悦子一本正经地分析道，“绝对的雄性力量与恐怖耐力往往能跨越年龄的界限。咱们家这几位女士，只不过是对优质的生物基因表现出了极其纯粹、务实的追求罢了。”

Rin burst into a triumphant laugh, pointing a finger at a blushing, flustered Sayuri. "Look at that! Even Sayuri wants a piece of the grand-uncle action! Fujiwara, you better watch out—your family bloodline is about to become the ultimate luxury brand in Donglai Island!"

铃当场爆笑出声，一把拉过旁边羞得快要钻进地缝里的小百合，得意洋洋地调侃道：“听到没！连小百合都被带偏了！藤原，我看你得赶紧把二大爷请回镇上常住，不然咱们这‘新鲜玛特’迟早要变成你们老藤家的后宫育种基地了！”

Fujiwara slumped back against the cushions, rubbing his temples in utter defeat as he groaned at the sheer, beautiful madness of his household.

藤原无力地一头倒在沙发靠垫上，绝望地揉着太阳穴，面对这群思维彻底放飞的女人，他除了苦笑实在不知该作何感想。
