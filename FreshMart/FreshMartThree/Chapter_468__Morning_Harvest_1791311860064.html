Chapter 468: Morning Harvest

发布时间：2026/10/7 02:37:38

第四百六十八章：清晨丰收

The morning sun poured through the floor-to-ceiling windows of the penthouse. Fujiwara slowly opened his eyes, turning to look at Sayuri sleeping peacefully beside him. After the absolute madness of last night's "dual-entry" ordeal, she looked exquisitely exhausted. A few strands of her messy, damp black hair clung to her cheeks, making her soft, beautiful face look unbelievably cute and endearingly fragile.

清晨的阳光透过顶层公寓的落地窗洒了进来。藤原缓缓睁开眼睛，看着身旁还在熟睡的小百合。经过昨晚那场近乎疯狂的“二穴同开”折腾，她显得格外疲惫。几缕凌乱、带着湿润汗意的黑发乖巧地贴在她柔美的脸颊上，让她那张精致的脸庞看起来可爱至极，又透着一股惹人怜爱的脆弱。

He didn't wake her. Instead, he leaned in and placed an incredibly gentle, lingering kiss on her smooth forehead.

他没有吵醒她，只是凑过去，在她光洁的额头上落下了一个极其轻柔、充满眷恋的吻。

Fujiwara put on his robe and walked into the living room, looking thoroughly refreshed. Miyetsuko, Chika, and Rin were already there. Miyetsuko was polishing her throwing knives, Chika was reviewing financial reports, and Rin was quietly knitting.

藤原披上睡袍，神清气爽地来到了客厅。美悦子、千夏和铃早已经在那儿了。美悦子在擦拭飞刀，千夏在看财报，铃则在安静地织毛衣。

Fujiwara sat on the sofa, rubbing his temples as if engaged in a profound philosophical crisis. "Ladies, I need your help," he muttered, racking his brain. "What else can I possibly give Sayuri? She is my most beloved girl. I've already thrown her a global coronation and given her the absolute peak of imperial favor. What else is there?"

藤原坐在沙发上，揉着太阳穴，仿佛陷入了一场极其深奥的哲学危机。“女士们，我需要你们的帮助，”他绞尽脑汁地嘀咕着，“我到底还能给小百合什么东西？她是我最爱的女孩，我已经为她举办了全球册封大典，给了她极盛的荣宠。我还能再送她什么？”

Chika pushed up her imaginary glasses. "From an asset allocation perspective, Boss, you've already transferred three European luxury conglomerates and a private island to her name. Unless you plan to buy the mining rights to the moon for her backyard, you've maxed out on material wealth."

千夏推了推不存在的眼镜。“从资产配置的角度来看，老板，您已经把欧洲的三家奢侈品集团和一座私人岛屿划到她名下了。除非您打算买下月球的开采权给她当后花园，否则在物质财富上您已经到顶了。”

Miyetsuko scoffed coldly. "If you think that's not enough, I can go assassinate every internet troll who ever said a bad word about her and make a necklace out of their skulls. That’s a highly unique, tactical gift."

美悦子冷哼了一声。“如果您觉得这还不够，我可以去把那些曾经在网上对她指指点点的键盘侠全部暗杀掉，用他们的头盖骨给她串一条项链。这绝对是一份独一无二的战术礼物。”

Rin tilted her head, thinking seriously. "Um... Master, maybe you could cook her a nice pot of pork rib and red date soup today? She worked really, really hard last night..."

铃歪着头，认真地想了想。“唔……主人，要不您今天亲自下厨，给她炖一锅红枣排骨汤补补身子？毕竟她昨晚真的超级、超级辛苦呀……”

Right as they were brainstorming, Sayuri slowly walked out from the hallway. Wearing an oversized silk robe, her steps were visibly awkward and tender. She walked up to the coffee table and, with an incredibly natural yet shy grace, reached behind herself.

就在他们绞尽脑汁讨论的时候，小百合慢悠悠地从走廊深处走了出来。她披着一件宽大的真丝睡袍，走路的步伐肉眼可见地有些别扭和娇弱。她走到茶几旁，带着一种极其自然却又难掩羞涩的优雅，将手伸向了身后。

With a few soft, wet pops, she slowly extracted the three massive red dates from her swollen rear—dates that had spent the night absorbing the so-called "hundred-fold Yin essence"—and placed them neatly onto a crystal fruit platter.

伴随着几声细微黏湿的“啵、啵”声，她慢慢地从那有些红肿的后庭里，将昨晚那三颗饱经蹂躏、吸饱了所谓“百倍阴气”的大红枣一颗颗拿了出来，整齐地放在了晶莹剔透的玻璃果盘里。

She looked up, her exquisite face flushed with a beautiful, intoxicating crimson, her eyes filled with absolute, unconditional devotion.

她抬起头，那张精致柔美的脸上带着一抹醉人的潮红，眼底满是绝对的、无条件的深情与顺从。

"You don't need to rack your brain to give me anything else, Master," Sayuri said, her voice soft, sweet, and overflowing with affection. "You have already given me the entire world. And all I have to give you in return... is this fresh breakfast, and my entire self. As long as Master is healthy and happy... I don't need anything else."

“主人不用再绞尽脑汁想送我什么了，”小百合声音软糯甜美，满溢着化不开的情意。“您已经给了我整个世界。而我能回报给您的……只有这最新鲜的早餐，和我毫无保留的全部。只要主人健康开心……我就什么都不缺了。”

Looking at the three dates still warm from her body, and hearing a confession that could melt solid steel, Fujiwara's heart took a massive, undeniable hit. He reached out and pulled Sayuri fiercely into his arms, holding her tight.

看着那三颗还带着她体温的红枣，听着这句简直能把百炼钢化作绕指柔的深情告白，藤原的心脏猛地遭受了一记无法抗拒的重击。他伸出手，一把将小百合狠狠拉进怀里，紧紧地抱住她。

It was a moment of endless, overwhelming tenderness that transcended all of his global conquests.

那是一种超越了他所有全球征服霸业的、铺天盖地且道不尽的柔情蜜意。

"You wonderful fool," Fujiwara murmured huskily into her neck, entirely consumed by his love for her. "With you by my side... why would I ever need immortality?"

“你这个美好的傻瓜，”藤原把脸埋进她的颈窝，声音沙哑，被对她极致的宠溺彻底淹没。“有你在我身边……我还要什么长生不老。”
