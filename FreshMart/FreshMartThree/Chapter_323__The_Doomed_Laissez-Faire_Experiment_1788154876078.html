Chapter 323: The Doomed Laissez-Faire Experiment

发布时间：2026/8/31 16:14:48

第三百二十三章：注定失败的自由放任实验

Exhausted by constant scolding, Fujiwara finally decided that he had had enough.

被连绵不断的训斥搞得精疲力尽，藤原终于决定自己受够了。

One morning, he gathered everyone together and solemnly declared a new covenant of laissez-faire management.

📌 完整翻译

一天早晨，他把所有人召集在一起，庄严地宣布了一项新的自由放任管理契约。

📌 Key Expressions

  • One morning = 某个早晨 （表示时间的起始，常用于叙事开头）

  • gathered everyone together = 把所有人召集在一起 （“gather” + “together” 强调集合的完整性）

  • solemnly declared = 庄严地宣布 （“solemnly” 修饰动词，表示态度严肃、郑重）

  • new covenant = 新的契约 （“covenant” 常指正式的、具有约束力的协议或誓约）

  • laissez-faire management = 自由放任管理 （源自法语 “laissez‑faire”，指政府或上级对企业/组织不干预的管理方式）

一天早晨，他把大家召集起来，郑重宣布缔结一项自由放任管理的新契约。

“From today onward,” Fujiwara announced, “I will adopt a low-key attitude.”

“从今天开始，”藤原宣布道，“我将采取低调的态度。”

Miyetsuko raised an eyebrow.

美悦子挑了挑眉。

“And what exactly does that mean?”

“具体是什么意思？”

Fujiwara proudly explained that he would step back from daily operations.

藤原骄傲地解释说，他将从日常运营中退居二线。

“In short,” he said, “I will lead by not interfering.”

“简单来说，”他说道，“我将通过不干涉来领导。”

He claimed this was an intrinsic evolution of his leadership.

他声称，这是他领导力的一种内在进化。

Unfortunately, the store was already rife with his meagre accomplishments.

遗憾的是，店里早就充斥着他那微薄的成就。

Miyetsuko looked around the store.

美悦子环顾了一下店铺。

“So your previous leadership was already visible?”

“所以，你之前的领导成果已经可以看出来了吗？”

Fujiwara coughed awkwardly.

藤原尴尬地咳嗽了一声。

“Some achievements are more... subtle.”

“有些成就是比较……隐性的。”

His strange preoccupation with doing absolutely nothing soon reached an entirely new level.

他那种对什么都不做有着奇怪的全神贯注，很快达到了一个全新的高度。

Fujiwara began spending more and more time sitting in the back room.

藤原开始花越来越多的时间坐在后屋里。

When someone asked about the inventory reports, he casually presented a set of quantitative figures.

有人询问库存报告时，他随手拿出了一组定量数据。

Miyetsuko looked at them for several seconds.

美悦子盯着那些数据看了几秒钟。

“Did you actually check the inventory?”

“你真的清点库存了吗？”

Fujiwara avoided eye contact.

藤原避开了她的目光。

“I checked it spiritually.”

“我从精神层面检查过了。”

In reality, he had fudged the quantitative inventory reports.

实际上，他捏造了那些定量的库存报告。

His real plan soon became obvious.

而他真正的计划很快也暴露了出来。

Fujiwara wanted to earmark store funds for a hammock.

藤原想指定动用店铺资金购买一张吊床。

He claimed that this would increase his personal occupancy of the back room.

他声称，这样可以增加他个人对后屋的占有率。

“It is an investment in management efficiency,” he explained.

“这是对管理效率的一项投资。”他解释道。

Miyetsuko stared at him.

美悦子盯着他。

“A hammock?”

“一张吊床？”

Fujiwara nodded confidently.

藤原自信地点了点头。

“A comfortable manager is an efficient manager.”

“一个舒适的店长，才是高效率的店长。”

Miyetsuko delivered a point-blank, matter-of-fact rejection.

美悦子对他的提议给予了直截了当、实事求是的回绝。

“No.”

“不行。”

Fujiwara blinked.

藤原眨了眨眼。

“That was very quick.”

“你拒绝得也太快了吧。”

Miyetsuko noted that his laziness was universally counter-productive.

美悦子指出，他这种懒惰在普遍意义上绝对是适得其反的。

She then found ample evidence to corroborate her argument.

随后，她找到了充足的证据来证实自己的观点。

His extremism in avoiding work would only detract from their revenue.

他在逃避工作方面的极端主义，只会减损他们的收入。

And it would also bode ill for the shop's future.

同时，这也预示着店铺的未来堪忧。

Fujiwara crossed his arms.

藤原抱起双臂。

“You can't prove that resting will hurt the business.”

“你不能证明休息会伤害生意。”

Miyetsuko pointed toward the growing pile of unopened boxes.

美悦子指了指旁边越堆越高的未拆货箱。

“Those boxes have been waiting for you for three days.”

“那些箱子已经等你三天了。”

Then she pointed at the messy shelves.

然后，她又指向凌乱的货架。

“And those shelves?”

“还有那些货架呢？”

Fujiwara looked away.

藤原再次移开了目光。

“That's a different department.”

“那是另一个部门的问题。”

Her sharp logic quickly shattered his preconception that he could silently secede from his duties.

她犀利的逻辑迅速粉碎了他的先入之见——他原本以为自己可以悄无声息地脱离自己的职责。

Fujiwara slowly looked at the hammock catalog on the table.

藤原缓缓看向桌上的吊床目录。

Miyetsuko took it away.

美悦子直接把它拿走了。

With the fundamental rules that underpin their household finally restored, order gradually returned to the store.

随着支撑他们家庭的基本规则终于得以恢复，店铺也逐渐恢复了秩序。

The formerly estranged manager was handed a mop.

这位早先脱离集体的店长被递了一把拖把。

Fujiwara stared at it in disbelief.

藤原难以置信地盯着那把拖把。

“What is this supposed to mean?”

“这是什么意思？”

Miyetsuko smiled.

美悦子笑了笑。

“It means your laissez-faire experiment is over.”

“意思是，你的自由放任实验结束了。”

Fujiwara sighed deeply.

藤原深深地叹了一口气。

Then he took the mop.

随后，他接过了拖把。

Forced back to reality, the manager quietly began cleaning the floor.

在被强行拉回现实之后，这位店长默默开始拖地。

From the counter, Miyetsuko watched him work.

美悦子站在柜台旁看着他。

“See?” she said. “This is also management.”

“看见了吗？”她说道，“这也是管理。”
