Chapter 232: The Transfer Student's Secret Past

发布时间：2026/8/30 22:41:41

第二百三十二章：转学生与学霸的旧账

"Since our resident gamer has officially expanded her social horizons," Fujiwara announced one afternoon, casually peeling an orange in the living room, "I've decided we need to be proper, civilized hosts. Rin, invite that Kenta kid over to the house for dinner. I want to meet the brave soul who dared to test our household security."

“既然咱们家的电竞猛将已经成功拓展了社交边界，”藤原一边在起居室里优哉游哉地剥着橘子，一边大手一挥宣布道，“那咱们就得展现出大户人家的好客之道。铃啊，把那个叫健太的小伙子请到家里来吃顿饭吧。我倒要亲自会会这个敢在太岁头上动土的勇士。”

Rin blinked, her cheeks flushing a delicate pink. She stammered, poking her fingers together, "Are... are you sure? The poor guy is probably still having nightmares about 'Fresh Mart' after what I told him. I'm not even sure he'd have the guts to show up."

铃微微一愣，白皙的面颊上泛起一丝羞涩的红晕。她有些局促地揉了揉手指，结结巴巴地说道：“真、真的要叫他来吗？那家伙听了我的‘家庭背景介绍’，估计现在晚上做噩梦都梦见你们几个拿着刀呢。我可不确定他有没有这个胆子敢上门。”

"Tell him the store manager guarantees his safety—provided he brings good snacks," Fujiwara grinned.

“你就告诉他，只要带够了高档零食，店长大人亲自保证他的人身安全，”藤原坏笑着拍了拍胸口。

Surprisingly, after a flurry of hesitant text messages, Kenta actually accepted the challenge. Perhaps driven by a mixture of morbid curiosity and youthful bravado, he showed up at the front door that evening, standing nervously behind Rin as she pushed it open.

让人意外的是，经过一番激烈的思想挣扎和微信拉锯战，健太竟然真的答应了这场鸿门宴。或许是出于对传说中“新鲜玛特”的好奇，又或许是少年意气的盲目自信，当晚铃带着他推开大门时，这位小伙子正略显紧张地拎着礼品站在门外。

"Hello... everyone," Kenta swallowed hard, stepping into the hallway.

“大、大家好……”健太硬着头皮迈进玄关，换好鞋走了进来。

As he raised his head, his eyes instantly locked onto the living room sofa.

就在他抬起头的瞬间，目光不经意间扫向起居室的沙发，整个人瞬间僵在了原地。

Sitting there with a heavy sociological volume in her lap was Miyetsuko.

只见美悦子正端正地坐在沙发上，膝头摊开着一本厚厚的社会学专著，正冷冷地抬眼朝门口看过来。

Flashback...

闪回开始……

Years ago, during their high school days, a new transfer student had suddenly arrived from another city. His name was Kenta.

那是几年前的高中时代，班里突然转来了一个从外市转学过来的男生。他的名字，就叫健太。

Back then, Miyetsuko wasn't the cynical, sarcastic academic queen of "Fresh Mart" yet; she was the untouchable, ice-cold school phantom who treated everyone like intellectual inferiors. But on the first day Kenta walked into the classroom, leaning casually against the podium with a lazy, handsome smile, something unexpected happened.

那时的美悦子还不是后来那个毒舌的“新鲜玛特学霸女王”，而是整天抱着习题集、把所有人都当成智障智商碾压的冰山学霸。然而，当健太第一天走进教室，斜靠在讲台上露出一抹漫不经心的帅气微笑时，美悦子古井无波的心弦却狠狠地拨动了一下。

A strange, unfamiliar heat fluttered in her chest. For the first time in her academic life, a boy had bypassed her mental firewall entirely.

那种突如其来的怦然心动，打乱了她引以为傲的理性防线。在学霸生涯里，这个转学生成了她唯一的视觉与逻辑盲区。

They didn't stay strangers for long. Late library study sessions quickly turned into whispered secrets behind the bookshelves, which eventually escalated into passionate, hidden flings behind the school gymnasium. Back then, Kenta wasn't just a random childhood friend—he was the secret, reckless romance of the school's coldest academic genius.

两人很快就有了交集。从图书馆深夜闭馆后的低声耳语，到体育馆后阴影里的疯狂纠缠。谁能想到，当年那个高高在上、对谁都爱搭不理的冰山女学霸，背地里竟然跟这个转学生有着一段炽热而隐秘的过去。

Back to reality...

回到现实……

The air in the living room went drop-dead silent.

起居室里的空气仿佛在这一秒彻底凝固了。

Kenta stared at Miyetsuko. Miyetsuko stared at Kenta. The heavy sociology book slipped from her fingers, landing softly on the carpet with a dull thud.

健太死死盯着美悦子，美悦子也瞪大了眼睛看着健太。膝头那本沉重的社会学巨著啪的一声滑落在地毯上，发出沉闷的声响。

"You...?" Kenta choked out, his face turning pale.

“你……怎么在这儿？”健太倒吸一口凉气，脸色瞬间精彩万分。

"Unbelievable," Miyetsuko murmured, her icy academic composure cracking into a million pieces as a rare flush of utter bewilderment crept up her cheeks. "Of all the statistical anomalies in the universe... why is my high school ex-fuckbuddy standing in my living room?!"

“这不科学……”美悦子向来冷静的面具瞬间碎了一地，罕见地有些语无伦次起来，“放着全宇宙七十亿人口不遇……我当年高中时期的地下情人，怎么会突然出现在我的客厅里？！”
