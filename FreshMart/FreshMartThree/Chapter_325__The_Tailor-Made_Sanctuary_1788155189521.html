Chapter 325: The Tailor-Made Sanctuary

发布时间：2026/8/31 16:19:28

第三百二十五章：量身定制的避难所

In a last-ditch effort to save face, Fujiwara attempted to refinance his failed projects by launching a tailor-made "spiritual enrichment" zone right in the middle of the grocery aisles.

作为挽回颜面的孤注一掷，藤原试图通过在杂货店过道里推出一个量身定制的“精神充实”专区来为他失败的企划重新融资。

"Behold the sanctuary of the mind! I shall cure your modern ailments with pure philosophy!" Fujiwara announced, hanging up a bizarre tapestry made of old curtains.

“见证心灵的避难所吧！我将用纯粹的哲学治愈你们的现代病痛！”藤原一边宣布，一边挂起了一块用旧窗帘拼凑成的诡异挂毯。

"You look more like a cult leader than a wellness guru," a passing customer muttered, quickening his pace.

“你看起来更像个邪教头子，而不是什么养生大师，”路过的顾客小声嘀咕着，加快了脚步。

Claiming he could cure the neighborhood's respiratory ailments and psychological stigma with his amateur psychiatry, he hoped this new venture would germinate into a massive success. He envisioned Fresh Mart joining the pantheon of elite wellness centers, eventually outgrowing its humble origins before facing total retail extinction.

他声称自己可以用业余的精神病学手段治愈附近街坊的呼吸系统疾病和心理病耻感，指望这项新事业能萌芽并取得巨大成功，让“新鲜玛特”跻身精英养生中心的殿堂，并最终在面临零售业灭绝之前成长并超越其卑微的出身。

However, Rin viewed his chaotic rearranging as a direct attempt to infringe upon her self-governing snack corner.

然而，铃将他这一通乱七八糟的重新布置视为对她自治的零食角的一种直接侵犯。

"Cross this line with your spiritual nonsense, and I'll redefine your psychology," Rin warned, sharpening a plastic spoon with a deadpan expression.

“你要是带着你的精神胡话越过这条线，我就亲自帮你重新定义心理学，”铃面无表情地磨着一把塑料勺子警告道。

Sparking a fierce secessionist rebellion against his blatant materialism, the girl stubbornly refused any conciliatory offers of extra pocket money. When chaos began to ensue, the loud noise of their bickering reverberated throughout the shop, vividly proving his grand ideas were totally infertile.

面对他赤裸裸的物质主义，少女掀起了一场激烈的分离主义叛乱，并坚决拒绝了任何额外给零花钱的和解提议。当混乱开始随之发生时，他们争吵的喧嚣在整个店铺里回荡，生动地证明了他的宏伟想法完全是贫瘠无效的。

Standing behind the counter, Miyetsuko offered a dry, matter-of-fact assessment.

美悦子站在柜台后面，给出了一份干巴巴却实事求是的评估。

"Your attempt to decentralize cashier duties has only resulted in missing loose change," Miyetsuko pointed out with a chilling stare.

“你试图下放收银员职责的举动，唯一的成果就是让零钱不翼而飞了。”美悦子以冰冷的目光指出。

She made it clear that to outlive this ongoing financial crisis, he needed to offer a proportionate amount of actual hard work.

她明确表示，要想熬过这场持续的财务危机，他需要付出成比例的实际努力。

Acknowledging her compatriot in maintaining order, Sayuri smiled gently over her teacup, noting that true empowerment came from honest labor.

向这位维持秩序的同胞点头致意，小百合端着茶杯温柔地微笑着，打趣道真正的赋权来源于诚实的劳动。

"Hear that, manager? Time to consult the mop for some spiritual guidance," Sayuri chuckled softly.

“听到没，店长？是时候去向拖把寻求一些精神指导了。”小百合轻笑着说道。

Defeated by overwhelming logic, Fujiwara sighed and took the handle. With a heavy heart, he was successfully pushed right back to the mop.

在压倒性的逻辑面前，藤原叹了口气，接过了拖把柄。伴随着沉重的心情，他再次被成功地推回了拖把前。
