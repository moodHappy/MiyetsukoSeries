Chapter 304: The Coastal Wharf Auction

发布时间：2026/8/31 13:13:45

第三百零四章：海港渔市的拍卖风云

Leaving the mountain valley behind to bask in the halcyon coastal breeze, Fujiwara spotted an old fishing wharf and immediately envisioned a shortcut to island prominence. The ambitious manager began to espouse a bizarre new enterprise: he would bypass all prosaic seafood distributors and secure a superlative monopoly on deep-sea catches, boasting that this bold move would claim the lions share of the regional tourist market.

告别山谷去沐浴那宁静美好的海滨微风，藤原一眼瞥见了不远处的一座老旧渔港码头，顿时脑中闪过一条迅速登顶海岛显赫声望的捷径。这位野心勃勃的店长开始极力鼓吹一项奇葩的新营生：他要彻底绕开所有平淡无奇的海产批发商，一举垄断最顶级的深海渔获，更大言不惭地吹嘘这一妙计定能抢占该地区旅游消费市场的最大份额。

Marching onto the pier to harry the local fishermen with a flurry of oblique negotiation tactics, Fujiwara attempted to gouge prices on prime crab crates while lending undue credence to his own haggling skills. However, his stubborn refusal to accept fair market rates created an intractable sticking point, leaving the penniless manager to bear the brunt of the fishermen's salty insults as Rin began to playfully snipe at him from the boardwalk.

大步走上码头，用一连串拐弯抹角的谈判手段去纠缠骚扰当地渔民，藤原试图恶意压低优质蟹笼的进价，同时对自己那点拙劣的砍价技巧抱有盲目的迷信。然而，他固执地拒绝接受合理的市价，迅速制造了一个难以化解的死结与主要分歧点；这位身无分文的店长只能首当其冲地承受渔民们粗粝而直白的痛骂，而铃则在木栈道上一边看戏一边欢快地出言冷嘲热讽。

Miyetsuko stepped in to halt the escalating quarrel, making it clear that Fujiwara would receive no vote of confidence from the household for such underhanded tactics. Pushing up her glasses, the strict director pointed out that trying to undercut honest harbor trade was practically economic heresy, demanding substantive and immediate corrective measures before ordering him to revert to buying normal dinner ingredients.

美悦子果断上前制止了越演越烈的争执，明确表示全家人绝不可能为藤原这种下三滥的手段投下信任票。推了推眼镜，这位严厉的厅长指出试图恶意压价 honest（诚信）海港贸易无异于商业上的大逆不道，严令他立刻采取实质性的纠正补救措施，并勒令他老老实实恢复按正常规矩采购今晚的晚餐食材。

To smoothly defuse the wharf tension, Chika began to flit gracefully between the stalls, using her innocuous charm and well-established culinary instincts to excel at picking out fresh mackerel and scallops. She even brewed a thermos of hot green tea as a soothing stimulant for the weary deckhands, effortlessly winning the goodwill of the entire harbor.

为了巧妙化解码头上的剑拔弩张，千夏步履轻盈地穿梭在各个摊位之间，凭借毫无攻击性的人畜无害魅力与炉火纯青的烹饪直觉，极其出色地挑选出一批新鲜的青花鱼和扇贝。她甚至倒出保温壶里刚泡好的热绿茶作为提神饮品犒劳疲惫的船员，不费吹灰之力便赢得了整个港口的一致好感。

Resting on a weathered timber bench, Sayuri watched the lively seaside bustle with cosmopolitan poise, ready to impart her gentle wisdom over the sunset view. She remarked that while Fujiwara had been given ample latitude to explore his ambition, the family’s true characterization lay in their warm unanimity at the dinner table, raising a cup to toast another hilariously unforgettable adventure.

端坐在斑驳的风化木椅上，小百合带着见多识广的大气从容注视着眼前热闹的海边喧嚣，准备对着绚烂的落日余晖传授她那温婉的智慧。她轻声调侃道，虽然全家人给了店长足够充分的自由发挥空间去折腾他的野心，但这个家真正的特质恰恰体现在餐桌上那份温馨而高度一致的默契和睦之中；她微笑着举起茶杯，为又一段滑稽而难忘的海岸历险干杯致意。
