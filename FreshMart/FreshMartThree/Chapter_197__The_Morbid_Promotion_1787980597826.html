Chapter 197: The Morbid Promotion

发布时间：2026/8/29 13:16:35

第一百九十七章：病态的升迁

As the car cruised smoothly along the coastal highway on the way back from the hillside cemetery, the heavy, somber atmosphere that had clung to the family all morning finally began to lift. The quiet reflection of the gravesite gave way to the soft hum of the engine and the familiar, comforting presence of one another.

当轿车沿着海滨公路平稳地驶回时，一清早笼罩在全家人心头的那股沉重哀思终于渐渐散去。墓园里的静默缅怀，化作了发动机柔和的轰鸣与彼此熟悉而安心的陪伴。

In the back seat, Chika lazily scrolled through her smartphone, catching up on the university’s emergency announcements and news updates regarding the tragic Tokyo campus shooting.

后座上，千夏百无聊赖地滑动着手机屏幕，浏览着东京校园枪击案后续的大学紧急公告与新闻通报。

Suddenly, her thumb froze. Her eyes widened as she read the newly published, official casualty and victim list released by the administration.

突然，她的手指猛地一僵。看着校方刚刚公开发布的官方死伤者完整名单，她的双眼微微睁大。

A strange, deeply conflicted expression washed over her face—a bizarre cocktail of unadulterated triumph and profound, comical disappointment.

一种极其复杂、充满戏剧性的神情在她脸上交织开来——那是纯粹的喜悦与一种让人哭笑不得的遗憾。

"Well, look at that," Chika muttered, blinking in disbelief before letting out a soft, dramatic sigh. "The final death list is out. And... wow. The top-ranked student in our department—my eternal academic arch-nemesis—actually got herself killed."

“哇哦，快看这个，”千夏眨了眨眼，忍不住发出一声戏剧性的轻叹，“最终的死亡名单出来了。而且……天哪，我们系里长年稳居第一名、把我压得死死的那个万年学霸，居然也在遇难名单里。”

Rin, who was riding shotgun, whipped her head around with a mischievous grin. "Oh? Did your mortal enemy kick the bucket? Does that mean..."

正坐在副驾驶座上的铃猛地回过头，脸上露出了看热闹不嫌事大的坏笑：“哦？你的死对头英年早逝了？那岂不是意味着……”

"Yep," Chika pouted, crossing her arms with a petulant huff. "I'm officially promoted to number one in the department by default. On one hand, I am insanely happy that I finally made it to the top. But on the other hand... I'm so annoyed! She died before I could ever beat her fair and square on an exam. Now I'll never get the satisfaction of crushing her properly!"

“对呀，”千夏鼓着腮帮子抱怨道，“我直接顺理成章地变成系里第一名了。一方面，我高兴得简直想原地转圈；可另一方面……我气死了好吗！她居然在期末考前把自己给弄死了，害得我再也无法在考场上堂堂正正地击败她了。这算什么嘛，胜之不武啊！”

The entire car plunged into a stunned silence for two seconds, before erupting into uproarious, helpless laughter.

整辆车里安静了两秒钟，随即爆发出一阵几乎要把车顶掀翻的狂笑。

"Only a true, card-carrying degenerate could experience a career breakthrough during a mass casualty event!" Rin howled with amusement, clutching her stomach as she leaned against the car door. "She literally survived a domestic terrorist attack just to complain that her academic rival died before she could steal her crown!"

“只有你这个纯种小变态，才能在特大校园安全事故里完成职场晋级！”铃笑得直不起腰，拍着大腿调侃道，“好不容易在枪击案里死里逃生，结果第一反应居然是嫌死对头死得太早、自己没机会亲手踩死她！”

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses from the driver's seat, a cold, intensely amused smirk playing on her lips. "A brilliant, albeit sociopathic, display of opportunistic pragmatism. Darwinian natural selection meets academic competition: when your rival is eliminated by external catastrophic forces, your own evolutionary fitness instantly spikes to the top."

正在开车的美悦子从后视镜里瞥了一眼，嘴角勾起一抹冷静而恶趣味的微笑：“教科书般的实用主义功利主义。当外部灾难性力量自动清除了你的竞争对手时，你的学术生存适应度便瞬间飙升至顶峰。达尔文看了都要直呼内行。”

Sayuri shook her head, covering her mouth as she giggled softly. "Goodness, Chika... that is wonderfully morbid. But I suppose it's very much in line with how our little family operates."

小百合掩嘴轻笑，无奈地摇了摇头：“天呐，千夏……这想法也太暗黑、太恶趣味了吧。不过仔细想想，倒还挺符合咱们家一贯的风格呢。”

Fujiwara roared with laughter, reaching back to give Chika’s head a playful, affectionate knuckle-rub. "That's my girl! Eliminating the competition through accidental geopolitical and campus tragedy—truly an elite career move. Who cares about a fair fight when you can just inherit the throne by default?"

藤原更是直接笑出了声，伸手往后一把揉了揉千夏的脑袋，坏笑道：“不愧是我带出来的小丫鬟！通过不可抗力的校园悲剧直接清空竞争对手——这职场晋升路线简直野得可以。管她什么公平竞争呢，能坐上第一把交椅就是好汉！”

"Hmph! Exactly!" Chika grinned, tossing her hair back with renewed, bubbly confidence as the lingering shadows of the past week were completely blasted away by their chaotic humor. "Since I'm officially the top student now, I demand a grand celebration tonight. And this time, no holding back!"

“哼！就是嘛！”千夏傲娇地一甩头发，连日来笼罩在心头的阴霾被这群不正经的家人彻底一扫而光，“既然本小姐现在已经是名正言顺的系里第一了，今晚必须得大肆庆祝一番。而且主人，今晚可不准再像昨天那样偷懒哦！”

Surrounded by the bright, unhinged laughter of her loved ones, the car sped down the highway toward "Fresh Mart," proving once again that no matter how dark life got, this wonderfully eccentric family could turn even a morbid piece of news into the sweetest, most hilarious comedy on earth.

在一片欢快、肆无忌惮的哄笑声中，轿车朝着“新鲜玛特”疾驰而去。事实证明，无论生活多么残酷黑暗，这群奇葩的家人总能把任何病态的黑幽默，化作这世上最甜蜜、最爆笑的人间喜剧。
