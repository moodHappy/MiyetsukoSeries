第四百六十章：“想射就射”法案

For over six months, the tedious, day-to-day governance of the Eastern and Western hemispheres had fallen entirely on the capable shoulders of Chika and Miyetsuko. Fujiwara, the absolute ruler of the globe, rarely bothered to intervene in mundane geopolitical affairs, preferring to leave the stock markets and shadow wars to his hyper-competent wives.

半年多来，东西半球那些枯燥乏味的日常政务，几乎全都落在了千夏和美悦子那能干的肩膀上。作为全球的绝对统治者，藤原极少费心去过问那些平庸的地缘政治事务，他更乐意把股市和暗影战争全都交给这些极度能干的妻子们。

But early Tuesday morning, Fujiwara abruptly summoned his family to the living room. Wearing a silk bathrobe and holding his morning espresso, he looked as profoundly serious as a statesman about to declare World War III.

但在周二一大早，藤原突然把一家人召集到了客厅。他穿着真丝睡袍，手里端着晨间浓缩咖啡，神情严肃得就像一位即将宣布第三次世界大战的政治家。

"Ladies, I have just drafted and signed a new, mandatory global legislation," Fujiwara announced proudly, standing before them. "Effective immediately, the worldwide adult entertainment industry will operate under the 'Shoot When You Want' Act."

“女士们，我刚刚起草并签署了一项全新的、具有全球强制性的法案，”藤原站在她们面前，无比自豪地宣布道。“即刻起，全球成人娱乐产业将全面施行‘想射就射法案’。”

He gestured grandly with his espresso cup. "By law, directors are now strictly forbidden from suppressing male actors. Everyone is allowed to freely express themselves and finish whenever nature dictates! This is a humanitarian effort to liberate our poor male brethren and drastically reduce their agonizing, on-set suffering!"

他端着咖啡杯夸张地挥舞了一下。“根据法律规定，现在严禁导演压抑男优。允许所有人自由发挥，大自然呼唤你什么时候射，你就可以什么时候射！这是一项人道主义壮举，旨在解放我们可怜的男性同胞，大幅减少他们在片场遭受的痛苦煎熬！”

"Furthermore," Fujiwara grinned, "I am confident the global audience will overwhelmingly support this. The masses are completely sick of rigid, fake performances. If actors are relaxed and allowed to climax freely, we will capture their rawest, most primal essence. The public is going to witness entirely new, authentic masterpieces!"

“不仅如此，”藤原咧嘴一笑，“我深信全球观众一定会压倒性地支持这项法案。大众早就对那种死板、虚假的表演感到厌烦了。如果在轻松的环境中允许演员们自由发挥、随意高潮，我们就能捕捉到他们最本质、最原始的状态。大众将会看到和原来完全不同的、真正原生态的旷世杰作！”

Miyetsuko, who was casually drinking a protein shake on the sofa, slowly lowered her glass. She stared at him with profound, sarcastic disbelief.

正坐在沙发上漫不经心地喝着蛋白粉奶昔的美悦子慢慢放下了杯子。她用一种深沉、带着讥讽的难以置信的眼神盯着他。

"Fujiwara... for six months, you completely ignored NATO, ignored the global energy crisis, and let me handle three different civil wars all by myself. And you finally decide to use your ultimate global veto power... to pass a porn law?"

“藤原……这半年多来，你对北约不管不顾，对全球能源危机视而不见，让我一个人去处理了三场不同的内战。结果你终于决定动用你那至高无上的全球否决权……就为了通过一部A片法案？”

Chika, however, was already furiously tapping away on her tablet, her eyes glowing with absolute capitalist fervor.

然而，千夏早已经在平板电脑上疯狂敲击起来，她的双眼里闪烁着绝对狂热的资本家光芒。

"Wait, big sis, don't dismiss this! The economic implications are phenomenal!" Chika squealed excitedly. "If directors can't force actors to edge for six hours, the average shoot time will drop from a full day to about... twelve minutes! We can churn out fifty movies a day on a single soundstage! Production costs will plummet! The ROI is going to explode!"

“等等，大姐头，别急着否定啊！这背后的经济效益简直惊人！”千夏兴奋地尖叫着。“如果导演不能强迫男优硬憋六个小时，那平均拍摄时间就会从一整天直接缩短到……大概十二分钟！我们在一个摄影棚里一天就能量产五十部电影！制作成本将直线暴跌！投资回报率绝对要爆炸了！”

In the corner, blind Rin clapped her hands happily, her wide, unseeing eyes filled with genuine medical relief.

在角落里，盲人铃开心地拍着手，那双空洞的大眼睛里充满了真诚的、出于医学角度的欣慰。

"Oh, Master, you are so kind! This is such a wonderful victory for occupational health and safety!" Rin smiled sweetly. "The actors' lumbar spines and prostate glands will finally be saved! I'll draft an HR memo to all global studios immediately so they can stop buying so much back-pain medication for their staff."

“哦，主人，您真是太仁慈了！这绝对是职业健康与安全领域的一场伟大胜利！”铃甜甜地微笑着。“男优们的腰椎和前列腺终于有救啦！我马上就起草一份人事备忘录发给全球所有制片厂，这样他们就可以少给员工买点治腰痛的药了。”

Sayuri let out a sweet, musical giggle, covering her lips with her sheer silk sleeve as she looked at her husband with absolute, devoted adoration. The former undisputed queen of the industry knew exactly what this law would look like in practice.

小百合发出一串甜美悦耳的娇笑，用薄如蝉翼的真丝衣袖掩住红唇，用那种绝对深情、充满崇拜的目光看着自己的丈夫。这位毋庸置疑的前业界女王，比谁都清楚这项法案在实际操作中会是怎样一番景象。

"It truly is a brilliant stroke of genius, darling," Sayuri purred lewdly, leaning her heavy breasts against his arm. "But I must say, the poor actresses are going to need a lot more towels. If every actor just shoots the absolute second they feel like it, the sets are going to be completely flooded with your 'raw authenticity' before the lighting crew even finishes setting up the cameras."

“这真的是堪称天才的神来之笔呢，亲爱的，”小百合下流地娇哼道，将沉甸甸的双乳贴在他的手臂上。“不过我不得不说，那些可怜的女优们以后得多准备点毛巾了。如果每个男优都在想射的那一秒钟直接射出来，那估计灯光师还没把摄像机架好，整个片场就已经被你所谓的‘原生态的真实’给彻底淹没啦。”

Fujiwara puffed his chest out proudly, deeply satisfied by his humanitarian executive decision.

藤原自豪地挺起胸膛，对自己这项充满人道主义精神的行政决策感到无比满意。

"Let them flood! True art cannot be restrained!" Fujiwara grinned broadly, finishing his espresso. "Chika, wire the decree to the United Nations immediately. Tell them the Emperor has spoken, and the era of the eunuch is officially over!"

“那就让片场发大水吧！真正的艺术是无法被束缚的！”藤原咧嘴大笑，一口喝干了浓缩咖啡。“千夏，马上把法令发给联合国。告诉他们，帝王已经发话了，太监的时代正式宣告终结！”
