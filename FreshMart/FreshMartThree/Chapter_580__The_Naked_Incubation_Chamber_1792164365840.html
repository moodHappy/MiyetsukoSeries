第五百八十章：赤身孵蛋大产房

While the grand salon was still echoing with Emperor Fujiwara’s grand vision of establishing the Fujiwara Interstellar Demigod Academy and arming thirty-six thousand half-celestial babies with plasma rifles, Consort Miyetsuko offered a crucial, highly specific piece of divine intelligence that immediately altered the imperial logistics.

"Wait, My Lord... there is an operational catch," Miyetsuko murmured, adjusting her silk robes as she nervously pointed at the sea of glowing, warm pearls. "According to His Celestial Majesty, these dragon eggs cannot simply be hatched in a standard mechanical incubator. “等一下，主人……这里有个核心的技术硬性要求，”美悦子小心翼翼地整理着真丝长袍，指着那片发光的珍珠海洋弱弱地说道，“据天帝陛下透露，这些龙蛋绝对不能塞进那种机械孵化箱里。天界基因密码强制设定：它们必须依靠人间绝美女性的直接肉体体温来进行孵化。更具体地说……孵蛋的妃子们必须光着屁股、一丝不挂地进行物理接触。如果没有这种绝对坦诚的血肉交融，龙蛋是绝对不会破壳的。”

The room fell into an abrupt, stunned silence.

大殿内瞬间陷入了一片诡异的死寂。

Chika blinked, her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaving as she stared at the thirty-six thousand glowing spheres. "Wait... you're telling me we can't just dump them in a warehouse? We have to sit on them? Naked?! All thirty-six thousand of them?!"

千夏眨了眨眼，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔剧烈起伏着，目瞪口呆地看着那三万六千颗发光的圆球。“等等……你的意思是，咱们没法把它们直接扔进仓库？还得像母鸡孵蛋一样亲自去坐在上面？还得光着屁股？！足足三万六千颗啊？！”

Sayuri’s macroeconomic brain raced instantly. "Thirty-six thousand eggs requiring personalized, naked terrestrial warmth... Our current imperial harem headcount is mathematically insufficient for this scale of production. We are facing a severe human-resource bottleneck."

小百合的宏观经济学大脑瞬间开始极速运转：“三万六千颗需要个性化、赤裸裸的人间体温来孵化的龙蛋……咱们目前后宫的人头储备在数学层面上完全不够用。咱们面临着严重的劳动力资源瓶颈。”

Fujiwara, however, didn't see a bottleneck. As a visionary supreme tyrant, he immediately saw a magnificent opportunity for imperial expansion and administrative enrichment.

然而，藤原大帝眼里可没有什么瓶颈。作为一个极具前瞻性的最高暴君，他瞬间看到了一次扩大帝国后宫编制、顺便大肆扩招美人的绝佳历史机遇。

"A human-resource bottleneck? Nonsense!" Fujiwara laughed uproariously, slamming his fist on the table. "If Heaven requires earthly beauties to hatch its divine progeny, then the Fujiwara Empire shall officially launch a galaxy-wide recruitment drive! We will issue an imperial decree: Any noblewoman, alien princess, or intergalactic influencer who wishes to contribute to our cosmic dynasty can apply to enter the palace as an official 'Imperial Incubator Consort'! We are going to stock this palace with the finest beauties the universe has to offer!"

“劳动力瓶颈？胡说八道！”藤原放声狂笑，一巴掌拍在桌子上，“既然天庭非要人间绝美女子来孵蛋，那咱们藤原帝国立刻正式启动全银河系的‘广纳美人大招募计划’！立刻给朕传下圣旨：凡是愿意为咱们宇宙大业贡献青春与体温的各族美女、外星公主、星际网红，统统可以申请进宫担任‘御用孵蛋爱妃’！老子要把全宇宙最顶级的绝色佳丽全给招进皇宫来孵蛋！”

Within days, the imperial palace was transformed. A colossal, high-security wing was hastily retrofitted into the Imperial Grand Incubation Complex. Thousands of soft velvet nests were installed across the sprawling halls, glowing with a warm, dim, highly atmospheric radiance.

短短几天之内，皇宫内部就发生了翻天覆地的巨变。一座戒备森严、规模宏大的建筑群被紧急改造成了“皇家超级大产房”。广袤的大殿内铺满了成千上万个柔软舒适的天鹅绒暖巢，散发着柔和而暧昧的粉色光晕。

Of course, as the core founders of the dynasty, the four original beauties didn't need to compete with the incoming waves of alien applicants. They each hand-selected a primary, high-priority "Royal Dragon Egg" to care for personally.

当然，作为藤原帝国的开国功臣，四位核心爱妃自然不需要跟外面蜂拥而至的外星应聘者去挤大通铺。她们每人精心挑选了一颗最顺眼、能量最足的“核心龙蛋”，准备亲自贴身孵化。

The incubation process required a grueling forty-nine days of continuous, unbroken skin-to-skin contact. And every single day, when Emperor Fujiwara took his morning inspection stroll through the grand incubation wards, the scene was nothing short of a surreal, highly amusing domestic comedy.

这项孵化工程极其漫长，需要整整七七四十九天不间断的赤裸肉体接触。于是，当藤原大帝每天早晨背着双手、迈着八字步去产房巡视时，眼前的画面简直滑稽得令人叹为观止。

Pushing open the heavy velvet doors of the VIP incubation suite on day twelve, the Emperor was greeted by a magnificent, thoroughly absurd spectacle.

当他在第十二天推开VIP产房那厚重的丝绒大门时，映入眼帘的是一幕极其香艳又荒诞不经的绝妙奇景。

There lay Sayuri, draped gracefully across a plush velvet divan, completely naked from head to toe. Her gloriously plump, Tang-dynasty curves were arched in regal elegance as she cushioned a massive, glowing dragon egg right beneath her plush Tang-style buttocks, fan in hand, looking like an absolute empress of fertility.

只见小百合优雅地斜倚在一张定制的天鹅绒躺椅上，从头到脚一丝不挂。她那盛唐风格、丰腴绝伦的完美曲线正以一种极其高贵优雅的姿态微微隆起，将一颗硕大发光的龙蛋严严实实地压在自己那肉乎乎、饱满的蜜桃臀底下。她手里甚至还摇着折扇，看起来宛如一位统领生育大业的绝代女王。

A few steps away, Chika was sprawled out on a massive beanbag chair, entirely bare and glowing with a soft post-milk warmth. Her heavy, milk-swollen breasts bounced rhythmically as she lazily patted the glowing egg cradled between her thighs, humming a trashy daytime soap-theme song to keep herself entertained.

不远处的懒人豆袋沙发上，千夏毫无形象地大大咧咧躺着，同样光着屁股，身上散发着刚挤完奶的温润热气。她那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔随着呼吸上下剧烈晃荡，正懒洋洋地用大腿夹着一颗发光的龙蛋轻轻摇晃，嘴里还哼着无脑肥皂剧的主题曲解闷。

Over in the aristocratic corner, Miyetsuko maintained her flawless, submissive posture. Completely naked, she knelt on a silk mat, her exquisite porcelain face flushing with devout pride as she carefully balanced a prime celestial egg against her lower back, her famous rear hiked ridiculously high in the air to ensure optimal thermal transfer.

在贵族专属的角落里，美悦子则完美保持着她那无可挑剔的顺从姿势。同样一丝不挂的她跪在一张真丝软垫上，白瓷般精致的脸上带着虔诚而骄傲的红晕，正小心翼翼地把一颗顶级的仙界龙蛋固定在后腰与挺翘的臀峰之间，将她那标志性的完美臀线高高撅起，以确保达到最高效的热量传导。

And finally, in the chaotic center of the room, blind Rin was bouncing up and down on a giant nest of pillows, completely naked, giggling uncontrollably as she used her own bare bottom to play leapfrog with a glowing dragon egg, treating the sacred divine incubation process like a bouncing castle.

The sheer absurdity of his four elite consorts sitting completely naked in giant nests, acting as high-end biological warmers for thirty-six thousand cosmic heirs, was the ultimate comedic triumph of his tyrannical reign.

I must say, the Empire has never seen such a magnificent, high-yield display of bare-bottomed imperial devotion. Keep up the good work, ladies—only thirty-seven days left until the dragon hatchlings start chewing on your furniture!"
