Chapter 364: The Toxic School Meal Scandal

发布时间：2026/9/4 17:29:58

第三百六十四章：有毒的免费午餐危机

Donglai Island was plunged into absolute chaos on Wednesday morning. Across the island, nearly two thousand students and teachers from various schools suddenly collapsed with violent food poisoning, turning campus restrooms into catastrophic war zones of simultaneous diarrhea and explosive vomiting.

周三清晨，东莱岛彻底陷入了一片大乱。全岛各所学校的近两千名学生和老师突然集体爆发了剧烈的食物中毒，各大校园的厕所瞬间沦为惨烈无比的“腹泻与呕吐交织战区”。

On the morning news broadcast, the island's beleaguered President clutched his head in despair, facing a massive public relations nightmare over his signature, multibillion-dollar "Free School Meals" initiative. Reporters aggressively questioned why thousands of children were hospitalized after consuming state-sponsored lunches featuring slimy chicken, undercooked fish, and a distinct, suspicious ocean-rot odor.

在早间新闻直播中，东莱岛焦头烂额的总统正抱着脑袋陷入绝望，面对着他那耗资数十亿的招牌政策“免费学校午餐计划”引发的惊天公关危机。记者们尖锐地质问着，为什么数千名孩子在吃完由政府资助、带有诡异黏稠鸡肉和可疑变质海腥味的午餐后集体进了医院。

Inside Fresh Mart, the five housemates stared at the living room television in stunned, breathless silence. The news anchor solemnly displayed a graphic of the food distributor responsible for the contaminated ingredients, boldly emblazoned with a familiar, cheerful store logo: Fresh Mart Wholesale.

在“新鲜玛特”的客厅里，五个人目瞪口呆地盯着电视屏幕，呼吸都停滞了。新闻主播严肃地打出了劣质食材供应商的调查画面，上面赫然印着一个大家无比熟悉的欢快店标：新鲜玛特批发。

Fujiwara slowly turned around from the TV, offering a deeply suspicious, sweating grin while nervously patting his bulging trouser pockets, which were literally stuffed with thick envelopes of government procurement cash.

藤原缓缓从电视机前转过身，脸上挤出一个心虚无比、满头大汗的假笑，同时紧张地拍了拍鼓囊囊的裤兜——那里塞满了政府采购合同换来的厚厚现金信封。

"Well... you see, kids," Fujiwara chuckled nervously, backing away toward the kitchen door. "When the island's Department of Education put out that low-bid tender for free school lunches... I figured buying three tons of expired, discounted freezer-burnt chicken from the dockside scrap yard was just smart, visionary entrepreneurship!"

“这个嘛……其实吧，孩子们，”藤原干笑着往厨房门口退去，“当岛上的教育局发布低价竞标的免费午餐合同时……我觉得从码头废品站以九折优惠买下三吨过期的冷冻僵尸鸡，绝对算是一场极具远见的精明创业！”

Rin's soul practically left her body. She grabbed a heavy cast-iron frying pan, her eyes locking onto him with murderous, homicidal intent. "You toxic piece of trash! You sold rotting, fishy chicken corpses to elementary schools?! Two thousand people are currently taking turns shitting themselves inside every public toilet on this island because of your greed!"

铃的灵魂差点当场吓飞。她顺手抄起一把沉重的铸铁平底锅，双眼迸发出杀气腾腾的凶光：“你这个有毒的死杂碎！你居然把腐烂发臭的死鸡肉卖给中小学？！全岛两千个人现在正排着队在各个公厕里喷射，全都是因为你的贪婪！”

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, her fingers flying across her tablet as she pulled up the legal liabilities. "According to the penal code, mass poisoning of minors carries a combined prison sentence of approximately four hundred and fifty years. Furthermore, our corporate assets will be seized, and we will likely be sentenced to hard labor in a salt mine."

美悦子推了推眼镜，手指在平板上疯狂敲击，迅速计算出法律后果：“根据刑法，群体性未成年人食物中毒最高可判处大约四百五十年的综合监禁。此外，我们的企业资产将被彻底查封，所有人大概率会被判去盐场进行强制劳役。”

Chika burst into tears, dropping her schoolbag as she hyperventilated. "W-We're going to jail?! I helped pack those slimy chicken boxes yesterday! I'm an accomplice to biological warfare!"

千夏吓得嚎啕大哭，书包“啪”地掉在地上开始疯狂过呼吸：“我、我们要坐牢了？！我昨天还帮着打包那些黏糊糊的鸡肉盒饭呢！我成生化武器的帮凶了！”

Sayuri sighed gracefully, delicately sipping her morning tea amidst the unfolding apocalypse. "Well, my career as a legendary AV director was cut short, and now my career as a respectable grocery store madam is ending in a federal penitentiary. At least we're going down together, darlings."

小百合优雅地叹了口气，在这场即将降临的末日中淡定地抿了一口早茶：“唉，我那传奇的AV导演生涯半路夭折，现在连当个正经杂货铺老板娘的晚节也要在联邦监狱里画上句号了。不过能整整齐齐地一起进去，倒也挺浪漫的嘛，亲爱的们。”

Before Miyetsuko could even finish calculating their bail requirements, Rin roared like a banshee and hurled the frying pan across the room, chasing a screaming, pants-dropping Fujiwara out the front door and straight into the waiting arms of the island police.

还没等美悦子把保释金算完，铃便发出一声母夜叉般的怒吼，把平底锅狠狠砸了出去，追着吓得屁滚尿流的藤原夺门而出，直奔岛上闻讯赶来的警察而去。
