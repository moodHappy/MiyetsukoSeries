Chapter 549: Persian Twins and Imperial Lust

发布时间：2026/10/12 11:33:21

第五百四十九章：波斯双姝与帝王淫欲

Having ruthlessly crushed the internal rebellions and completely subjugated the barbarian borders, Fujiwara's empire entered an era of absolute, terrifying peace. To celebrate this universal submission and the arrival of thousands of foreign diplomats, the Emperor hosted a massive, unprecedented "Exotic Culture Expo" in the heart of the capital.

在冷血无情地平定了国内叛乱、彻底降服了边境胡人之后，藤原的帝国迎来了威震寰宇、天下太平的绝对鼎盛期。为了庆祝这万邦来朝的盛况，皇帝在首都核心区举办了一场空前盛大的“异域风情博览会”。

Fujiwara strolled through the bustling, incredibly crowded plaza, flanked by his beautiful harem. The sheer volume of exotic tributes was staggering. Without needing a single ledger, the Emperor’s eyes swept over a breathtaking array of global wealth: Loulan merchants leading sturdy camels laden with felt and sweet wine; Kucha envoys proudly displaying heavy ironware and majestic steeds; Khotan representatives rolling out exquisite carpets, flawless jade, and smooth silks.

藤原带着一众千娇百媚的爱妃漫步在人山人海的繁华广场上。异域贡品的数量令人咋舌。无需任何清单，皇帝的目光便扫过了一片令人窒息的全球财富：楼兰商人牵着满载毛毡与美酒的骆驼；龟兹使者骄傲地展示着沉重的铁器与神骏的良马；于阗代表铺开了精美的地毯、无暇的和田玉与丝绸。

Further down the avenue, they passed Dali marble and fragrant Pu'er tea, resting alongside Ba and Shu well-salt, brocade, and glossy lacquerware. From the distant western continents, Ancient Egypt sent dazzling gold, spices, and fine linen, while Babylon offered sweet dates, grain, and woven wool. India presented vibrant cotton and precious gems, and Greece and Rome practically competed with one another, overflowing their stalls with endless supplies of olive oil, vintage wines, and sculpted marble pottery.

沿着大道继续向前，他们看到了大理国的大理石与芬芳的普洱茶，紧挨着巴蜀两地的井盐、蜀锦与光泽饱满的漆器。来自遥远西方大陆的古埃及送来了耀眼的黄金、香料与精美亚麻布；古巴比伦奉上了甜腻的椰枣、谷物与羊毛织物。古印度进献了艳丽的棉布与无价的宝石，而古希腊与古罗马简直是在疯狂内卷，他们的展台上堆满了成吨的橄榄油、年份葡萄酒和雕花大理石陶器。

"My Lord, the economic influx is astonishing," Sayuri murmured gracefully. The Wise Empress, looking magnificently regal and gloriously plump in her Tang-style silk robes, was already calculating the tax revenues. "We can fill the imperial treasury ten times over with just the Egyptian gold and Roman oil."

“夫君，这经济的大规模流入当真是惊人，”小百合优雅地轻声呢喃道。这位贤后穿着一身盛唐风格的真丝长袍，那被喂得丰腴完美的绝佳身段透着母仪天下的贵气，脑子里却已经在飞速盘算着税收了。“单凭古埃及的黄金和古罗马的橄榄油，就足以让咱们的皇室府库再充实十倍。”

"Tax revenues are boring! Boss, look at those Loulan wines and Babylonian dates!" Chika cheered, bouncing energetically and causing her heavy, milk-swollen chest to heave dramatically. "I want to build a chocolate fountain, but with wine!"

“算税收多无聊呀！老板，你快看那些楼兰美酒和古巴比伦的椰枣！”千夏欢呼着蹦蹦跳跳，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的胸脯随之夸张地剧烈晃荡着，“我要建一个喷泉，不过里面要全部倒满葡萄酒！”

However, the absolute climax of the expo came from the Persian Empire.

然而，整场博览会绝对的高潮，来自于波斯帝国。

After presenting a fortune in woven carpets, rare spices, and blinding gems, the Persian envoy bowed deeply and clapped his hands. From behind a sheer silk screen emerged the ultimate tribute: a pair of breathtakingly exquisite Persian twin beauties. Dressed in incredibly revealing, translucent belly-dancer silks adorned with tiny bells, their golden eyes, olive skin, and flawless, identical curves instantly set the plaza on fire. They moved with a mesmerizing, serpentine grace, dropping to their knees before Fujiwara with perfectly synchronized, exotic submissiveness.

在献上了价值连城的繁复地毯、珍稀香料与刺眼的宝石之后，波斯使节深深鞠了一躬，拍了拍手。从一道半透明的丝绸屏风后，缓缓走出了终极贡品：一对美得惊心动魄、充满异域风情的波斯双胞胎绝色美女。她们穿着极其暴露、薄如蝉翼且缀满小铃铛的舞娘轻纱，那对金色的眼眸、橄榄色的肌肤以及完美无瑕、如同复制粘贴般的火辣曲线，瞬间点燃了整个广场。她们迈着令人迷醉的水蛇步，以极其同步、充满异域风情的绝对顺从，在藤原面前双膝跪下。

Fujiwara’s eyes widened. All thoughts of global trade, taxation, and military strategy instantly evaporated from the tyrant's mind, completely overridden by pure, unadulterated lust.

藤原的眼睛瞬间亮了。所有关于全球贸易、税收和军事战略的念头瞬间从这位暴君的脑海中蒸发得一干二净，彻底被纯粹、毫无杂质的浓烈淫欲所取代。

"Right. That's it. The expo is officially over," Fujiwara declared shamelessly, waving his hand to dismiss the rest of the tour. He grabbed the chains of the twins' silk collars. "I've seen enough. Cancel all my afternoon meetings."

“很好。到此为止。博览会正式结束，”藤原厚颜无耻地大声宣布，一挥手便取消了剩下的所有参观行程。他一把抓起这对双胞胎颈间丝绸项圈上的锁链。“朕已经看够了。把朕下午的所有会议统统取消。”

Sayuri sighed, resting a delicate hand on her plump cheek with velvet exasperation. "My Lord, we are only halfway down the avenue. The Greek diplomats have been waiting for three hours to present their pottery."

小百合叹了口气，用一只纤细的玉手托着自己那肉乎乎的香腮，流露出一丝丝绒般的无奈。“夫君，咱们才逛了半条街呢。古希腊的外交官为了给您展示陶器，都已经苦等了三个小时了。”

"Fuck their pottery! I have two Persian masterpieces right here that require my immediate, personal 'inspection'!" Fujiwara roared with wicked impatience, practically dragging the stunning twins toward the royal carriage. "Hurry up, ladies! We are returning to the palace immediately!"

“去他妈的陶器！朕面前现在就有两件波斯绝世艺术品，需要朕立刻、马上亲自进行‘深入鉴定’！”藤原带着极其恶劣的急躁吼道，几乎是急不可耐地拽着这对惊艳的双胞胎走向皇家马车。“都快点，爱妃们！咱们现在就立刻回宫！”

Miyetsuko immediately gathered her skirts and knelt by the carriage door, her exquisite face flushing with deep, aristocratic obedience. "Yes, Master! Your servant shall instruct the eunuchs to prepare the widest Tang-style bed and warm the massage oils immediately!"

美悦子立刻提着裙摆跑到马车门边跪下，精致的小脸因为深深刻在骨子里的贵族顺从统而涨得通红。“遵命，主人！奴婢这就吩咐太监们去铺好最宽大的唐风龙床，并立刻温好精油！”

Blind Rin clapped her hands cheerfully, her milky eyes wide with demonic innocence as she hopped into the carriage. "Um... hello, Master? Are we going to make a Persian twin sandwich? Rin wants to watch them bounce!"

盲人小铃开心地拍着手，空灵的盲眼里满是恶魔般的纯洁，一蹦一跳地上了马车。“唔……主人听得见吗？咱们这是要去做波斯双胞胎三明治吗？小铃想看她们俩一起弹来弹去的样子！”

"Oh, they will be bouncing, little demon," Fujiwara laughed wildly, pulling Sayuri's soft, heavy hips into the carriage alongside the bewildered, blushing Persian twins. "The empire is at peace! It's time for the Emperor to conduct some extremely deep, high-intensity cultural exchange behind closed doors!"

“噢，她们绝对会弹得飞起的，小恶魔，”藤原狂笑着，将小百合那柔软沉甸的丰臀一把拽进马车，紧挨着那对满脸通红、不知所措的波斯双胞胎。“帝国如今已经天下太平了！是时候让皇帝陛下关起门来，去进行一场极其深入、高强度的文化交流了！”
