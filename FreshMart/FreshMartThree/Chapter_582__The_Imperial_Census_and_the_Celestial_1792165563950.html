Chapter 582: The Imperial Census and the Celestial Heirarch-in-Law

发布时间：2026/10/16 23:46:02

第五百八十二章：天帝的寻亲

The grand courtyard of the imperial palace had barely recovered from the bloody cleanup of the failed incubation consorts when a second, highly disruptive celestial decree descended from the stratosphere.

皇宫大庭院里那场关于清洗失败孵蛋妃子的血腥气味还没完全散去，第二道极具颠覆性的天界法旨便带着滚滚雷霆从平流层轰然降临。

A colossal, glowing golden imperial edict hung suspended in the sky, broadcasting the Heavenly Emperor's absolute legal verdict regarding the 23,639 newly hatched dragon-seed babies. The ruling was as brief as it was ruthless:

一张巨大的金色圣旨悬挂在半空中，向全宇宙公开了天帝对于那23,639个新生龙种的最终法律裁决。判决言简意赅，却冷酷无情：

"By decree of the Heavenly Palace: Among the thousands of hatchlings produced across the mortal realm, His Celestial Majesty officially recognizes and authenticates only the single pure-blood celestial lineage nurtured and birthed by Consort Miyetsuko as his legitimate royal heir. All other 23,638 hatchlings, hybrids, and experimental spawn are hereby disowned, stripped of any heavenly pedigree, and ruthlessly banished to the mortal realm as ordinary commoners."

“天宫法旨：鉴于人间近期产出的万余名龙种，天帝陛下经过血统严苛复核，官方正式仅承认并册封由美悦子爱妃所生、兼具正统天界神格的唯一嫡脉为合法皇嗣。其余23,638个杂交幼崽、实验产物及各路野种，一律剥夺天界户籍，贬落人间，发配为普通凡民。”

The cosmic decree dissolved into a shower of golden sparks, leaving the grand salon in a state of stunned, incredulous silence.

金色圣旨化作一片金色的火雨消散，整个大起居殿陷入了一片目瞪口呆的死寂中。

"Wait... wait a minute!" Chika shrieked, dropping her half-eaten snack as her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaved in blind outrage. "He’s recognizing only Miyetsuko’s kid?! What about my milk-drinking, horn-bearing giant baby? What about Sayuri’s macroeconomic cult leader? And what about Rin’s little black-hole-swallowing demon spawn?! The Heavenly Emperor just ghosted 23,638 of his own children in one sweep?!"

“等……等一下！”千夏尖叫着扔下手中的零食，那对因胀奶而剧烈起伏的玉兔因为极致的愤怒而上下颤抖着，“他竟然只承认美悦子的孩子？！那我那个长着龙角的巨婴怎么办？小百合孵出来的宏观教主怎么办？还有小铃吞黑洞的小恶魔怎么办？！天帝这老杂毛一句话，直接甩掉了两万多个亲生骨肉，这是妥妥的渣男集体诈尸跑路啊！”

Wise Empress Sayuri sighed, calmly adjusting her folding fan as she processed the macroeconomic implications of the mass banishment.

贤后小百合无奈地叹了口气，一边优雅地摇着折扇，一边冷静地分析着大规模贬落凡间的宏观经济学后果。

"It is a classic aristocratic demographic maneuver," Sayuri purred smoothly. "The Heavenly Emperor cannot afford to split the celestial real estate portfolio among twenty-four thousand rival heirs. By officially recognizing only Miyetsuko’s elite, pure-blood offspring, he secures a single, undisputed successor while legally dumping the massive childcare financial burden directly onto the Fujiwara Empire's domestic welfare budget."

“标准的贵族人口金字塔清算策略，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，“天帝可负担不起把天庭的房地产分给两万多名各路继承人的开销。他通过官方仅册封美悦子生下的那个顶级纯血嫡脉，既保住了唯一的法定继承人，又顺手把极其庞大的育儿财政赤字直接甩锅给了咱们藤原帝国的国内民政预算。”

Miyetsuko dropped gracefully to her knees, bowing deeply. Her exquisite, porcelain-smooth face was flushed with the ultimate, breathless triumph of an imperial mother whose child had just beaten twenty-three thousand competitors to win the cosmic lottery.

美悦子优雅地双膝跪地，深深叩首。她那张白瓷般的精致脸上洋溢着一种“后宫宫斗终极大获全胜”的绝顶骄傲——自己的孩子硬生生从两万多名竞争者中脱颖而出，独占天帝法统。

"Your humble servant is overwhelmed by His Celestial Majesty's supreme grace..." Miyetsuko murmured, her plush Tang-style rear hiked high in the air. "Our precious child is now officially the Crown Prince of Heaven and Earth. As for the rest... well, they will simply form the foundation of our new mortal labor force."

“奴婢代皇嗣谢恩……天帝陛下圣明，”美悦子甜甜地呢喃着，将那对丰腴诱人的臀部高高撅起，“咱们的宝贝疙瘩如今正式荣登天地共主的储君宝座。至于剩下的那两万多个嘛……正好可以充当咱们帝国未来开荒拓土的基石劳动力嘛。”

From her floating cushion, blind Rin swung her legs, giggling uncontrollably. "Um... hello! Rin doesn't mind at all! If the Heavenly Emperor dumped all those little dragon babies into the mortal realm, Rin’s underworld daycare center will gladly adopt them! We can teach them how to dig coal in the underworld mines and worship the Skull Throne!"

坐在漂浮软垫上的盲人小铃开心地晃着双腿：“唔……小铃完全不介意哦！既然天帝老头把那两万多只小龙崽全给贬落到人间了，那咱们地狱托儿所全盘接收好啦！正好教它们去地狱煤矿里挖煤，顺便集体加入骷髅王座神教！”

Fujiwara stood at the center of the room, looking over the chaotic army of discarded demigod infants and the single, glowing golden infant cradled in Miyetsuko's arms. A slow, terrifying, deeply ambitious tyrannical grin spread across the Emperor's face.

藤原站在房间的正中央，俯视着眼前这群被天帝抛弃的半神幼崽，以及美悦子怀中那唯一一颗散发着金光的正统嫡脉。一个极其庞大、野心勃勃的暴君坏笑缓缓爬上了他的脸庞。

"Disowned by heaven and banished to earth? Excellent!" Fujiwara roared with laughter, slamming his fist against the obsidian desk. "Do you realize what this means, everyone?! The Heavenly Emperor just gifted us an unpaid, highly resilient army of twenty-three thousand six hundred and thirty-eight half-divine workers!"

“被天庭除名、贬落凡间？太妙了！”藤原爆发出狂放的大笑，一拳砸在黑曜石书桌上，“各位爱妃，你们难道还没意识到这意味着什么吗？！天帝这老杂毛等于一口气白送了咱们两万三千六百三十八个自带神力的免费高级劳动力啊！”

The Emperor paced across the room, his eyes burning with absolute capitalist momentum.

暴君在殿内来回踱步，漆黑的双眼里燃烧着绝对的资本扩张烈焰。

"And that’s not even the best part," Fujiwara grinned wickedly. "According to the celestial biological parameters, the Heavenly Emperor's dragon-seed reproductive cycle operates at three months per litter, yielding thirty-six thousand eggs at a time. Since Miyetsuko is officially the designated 'Shared Interdimensional Asset' and the mother of the chosen heir, her bi-weekly weekend shifts in the Heavenly Palace mean she will be producing another 36,000 eggs every ninety days!"

“而且这还不是最绝的，”藤原笑得无比奸诈，“按照天帝那套逆天的生物学参数——三个月一胎，一次产卵三万六千颗。既然美悦子已经是官方盖戳认证的‘共享资产’兼正统国母，那她以后每个周末去天帝那儿加班，岂不是意味着每隔九十天，咱们的皇宫就要迎来一次高达三万六千人的……人口大爆炸？！”

Silence.

死寂。

Chika choked on her potato chip, her eyes bulging out of her head. "Wait... a population explosion of 36,000 demigods every three months?! Boss, at that rate, within two years our imperial nursery won't just outgrow the palace—we’re going to need to colonize three more star systems just to build enough diapers and cribs!"

千夏当场被薯片卡住，眼珠子差点瞪出来：“等等……每隔三个月就来一次三万多人的半神人口大爆炸？！老板，照这个恐怖的繁殖速度，不出两年咱们皇宫的育儿室不仅得撑爆，还得为了给这帮小祖宗造尿布和婴儿床去多征服三个星系才够用啊！”

"Exacty! The ultimate demographic dividend!" Fujiwara declared triumphantly, pulling Miyetsuko and her golden crown-prince baby into a massive, unbreakable hug. "Let the Heavenly Palace keep their single heir! We have the factory, we have the mother, and we have an endless, high-yield quarterly pipeline of divine infants. Get the maternity wards ready, ladies—the Fujiwara Empire is about to conquer the cosmos through sheer, unstoppable, multi-dimensional birth rates!"

“说得对！这才是真正的最高人口红利！”藤原意气风发地大笑，一把将怀抱金光太子的美悦子紧紧搂进怀里，“让天庭去守着他们那个孤零零的继承人吧！咱们有工厂、有生母、更有每季度源源不断的高产仙胎流水线。产房准备好，爱妃们——咱们藤原帝国接下来要靠着无可匹敌的跨维超高生育率，把整个宇宙彻底生下来、占个底朝天！”
