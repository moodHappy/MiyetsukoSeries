Chapter 253: The Bankrupt Man's Blessing

发布时间：2026/8/31 02:09:30

第二百五十三章：破产者的终极豁免权

Just when everyone at "Fresh Mart" thought life was finally returning to a peaceful, albeit chaotic, normality after the agricultural disaster, a digital earthquake shook the entire island of Donglai.

正当“新鲜玛特”的一员以为在经历过那场轰轰烈烈的农田闹剧后，生活总算能回归某种鸡飞狗跳的平静时，一场数字大地震毫无征兆地席卷了整个东莱岛。

Early in the morning, cell phones across the household started buzzing and flashing with emergency news alerts simultaneously.

清晨刚过，屋里所有人的手机同时疯狂震动起来，弹出了全岛范围内的紧急新闻推送。

“Breaking News: Donglai Island's primary local payment application, 'Zero-Yuan Treasure,' has suffered a catastrophic, island-wide data breach! Millions of user privacy records, transaction histories, and financial accounts have been leaked online!”

“【突发警报】东莱岛本土核心支付软件‘零用宝’发生重大数据泄漏事故！数百万用户的隐私数据、交易流水及绑定账户遭到全面泄露！”

Panic swept through the streets instantly. Inside the living room, the atmosphere turned tense in a fraction of a second.

一时间，整个东莱岛人心惶惶。起居室里的气氛瞬间降至冰点。

Miyetsuko’s face turned pale. She instantly whipped out her phone, frantically checking her security settings and muttering about digital footprints and data encryption protocols. Rin shrieked in horror, worried that her embarrassing online shopping history and secret gaming purchases would be exposed to the public. Even Sayuri looked genuinely concerned, quickly checking her bank linkages.

美悦子的脸色瞬间变得煞白，手指飞速在屏幕上敲击，面色凝重地检查着各项隐私权限与数据加密状态；铃则吓得尖叫出声，脑子里疯狂闪过自己那些见不得人的网购订单和游戏氪金记录；就连一向沉稳的小百合也微微蹙眉，第一时间开始确认自己的资产安全。

Amidst the absolute chaos and panic, there was one person sitting comfortably on the sofa, sipping plain tap water with the serene, enlightened smile of a Zen master.

然而，在一片鸡飞狗跳的惊恐中，却有一个人舒舒服服地窝在沙发里，手里端着一杯白开水，脸上挂着宛如老僧入定般的祥和微笑。

That person was Fujiwara.

这个人，正是藤原。

"Fujiwara! Are you out of your mind?!" Rin shouted, grabbing him by the shoulders and shaking him furiously. "The entire island's financial system is compromised! Your bank accounts! Your transaction history! Your private life! Aren't you worried at all?!"

“藤原！你脑子进水了吗？！”铃气急败坏地冲过来一把揪住他的肩膀疯狂摇晃，“全岛的支付系统都瘫痪了！你的银行账户、你的消费记录、你的隐私全曝光了！你居然还笑得出来？！”

Fujiwara calmly set down his cup of water, raised his hands, and offered a thoroughly unbothered, philosophical shrug.

藤原淡定地放下水杯，慢条斯理地摆了摆手，露出一副看透红尘的超然神情：

"Worried? Why on earth would I be worried?" Fujiwara scoffed lightly. "Let's analyze this logically, girls. First of all, privacy? What privacy? Thanks to your little 'search party' stunt a few days ago, my mugshot, my runaway letter, and my brothel VIP status are already well-known by thousands of college students, professors, and underworld hosts. My social death is complete. I have achieved ultimate privacy immunity!"

“担心？我有什么好担心的？”藤原嗤笑一声，振振有词地分析道，“来，姐妹们，咱们用理性的经济学思维分析一下。第一，隐私？什么隐私？托你们几天前那场‘全岛大通缉’的福，我的高清大头照、离家出走绝命信还有风俗店VIP包厢实录，早就印进好几千名大学生和教授的脑子里了。我都已经彻底社会性死亡了，还有什么隐私可言？这叫获得终极隐私豁免权！”

Miyetsuko’s eye twitched at the logic, but she couldn't immediately find an academic counter-argument.

美悦子的眼角狠狠抽搐了一下，一时间竟找不出学术上的反驳理由。

"And second," Fujiwara continued, puffing out his chest with absolute pride, "what about my money? Have you forgotten? My personal savings account was completely wiped out by three top-tier hostesses just a few days ago! My balance is currently sitting proudly at zero point zero-zero yuan. Hackers can't steal a single cent from a man who is already legally bankrupt!"

“第二，”藤原挺起胸膛，脸上甚至泛起了一丝因贫穷而带来的自豪感，“至于钱？你们是不是忘了？我的私人小金库前几天刚被那三位顶级头牌给彻底榨干、清零了！我现在账户余额常年稳定在光荣的零点零零元。黑客就算把系统底裤都扒了，从我这个穷光蛋身上也只能偷走满地空气！”

Sayuri and Rin stared at him, utterly dumbfounded. They had never seen someone so proud of being broke.

小百合和铃呆呆地看着他，一时竟无言以对。活了这么多年，她们还是头一次见到有人把“破产穷光蛋”当成护身符的。

"In fact," Fujiwara whispered conspiratorially, his eyes gleaming with greedy, wild anticipation as he leaned forward, "I am sitting here praying for a massive system glitch. Just imagine—what if a stray hacker’s algorithm accidentally malfunctions and accidentally deposits ten million yuan into my empty account? That wouldn't be a data leak; that would be the greatest economic stimulus package in Donglai Island's history!"

“不仅如此，”藤原压低了声音，双眼闪烁着对财富的极度渴望，贼兮兮地坏笑道，“说实话，我这会儿正合十祈祷系统出大bug呢！万一哪个黑客写代码时手一抖，误打误撞往我这空空如也的账户里错转个一千万呢？这哪是什么数据泄漏，这分明是东莱岛有史以来最伟大的精准扶贫啊！”

The living room fell into a stunned silence for three seconds before exploding into helpless, uproarious laughter.

起居室里诡异地安静了三秒钟，随后爆发出了一阵掀翻屋顶的无情狂笑。

"You are truly hopeless," Miyetsuko sighed, rubbing her temples while trying to suppress a smile.

“你这个人真的是没救了……”美悦子无奈地扶着额头，终究还是没忍住噗嗤一声笑出了声。
