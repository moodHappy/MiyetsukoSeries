​Chapter 469: The Holy Virtuous Queen

发布时间：2026/10/7 02:48:16

第四百六十九章：圣德皇后

Despite Sayuri’s tender refusal, Fujiwara was not about to let the matter drop. The undisputed ruler of the global underworld suddenly acted like a stubborn, overgrown boy, burying his face into her neck and wrapping his arms around her waist in a dramatic display of absolute insistence.

尽管小百合柔声拒绝，但藤原却完全不打算就这么算了。这位地下世界毋庸置疑的全球统治者，突然像个固执的大男孩一样，把脸埋进她的颈窝里，双手死死搂住她的腰，用一种极其戏剧化的姿态耍起了无赖。

"No! I refuse!" Fujiwara complained loudly, rubbing his cheek against her collarbone. "I am the King of the World! I can bankrupt a European nation before breakfast, but I can't even give my favorite girl a proper reward for her hard work? It's unacceptable! I have to give you something! Tell me what you want, right now!"

“不行！我拒绝！”藤原大声抱怨着，用脸颊蹭着她的锁骨。“我可是世界之王！我能在吃早饭前搞垮一个欧洲国家，结果却不能给我最爱的女孩一份应得的辛苦奖励？这绝对不行！我必须要送你点什么！现在立刻马上告诉我你想要什么！”

Sayuri giggled helplessly, gently stroking his hair. She looked at the other three women in the room, her eyes curving into sweet, benevolent crescents.

小百合无可奈何地咯咯娇笑着，温柔地抚摸着他的头发。她看向客厅里的另外三个女人，眼睛笑成了甜美、慈爱的月牙。

"Master, you are acting like a spoiled child," Sayuri murmured affectionately. "If you truly wish to reward me, then I only have one request. Please, share your boundless grace and affection with my sisters. Miyetsuko, Chika, and Rin all work so hard for the empire. Seeing the household harmonious and everyone receiving Master's love... that is the greatest gift I could ever ask for."

“主人，您现在简直像个被宠坏的孩子，”小百合满含爱意地呢喃道。“如果主人真的想奖励我，那我只有一个请求。请您将您的恩泽与宠爱，多分给我的妹妹们吧。美悦子、千夏和小铃为帝国工作都那么辛苦。能看到咱们家和睦美满，每个人都能得到主人的疼爱……这就是我能要到的最棒的礼物了。”

Fujiwara paused, completely struck by her words. He slowly sat up, looking at Sayuri as if she possessed a glowing halo of divine purity.

藤原愣住了，被她的话深深震撼。他慢慢坐直身子，看着小百合，仿佛她身上正散发着神圣纯洁的光环。

"Sayuri... your virtue is simply unmatched," Fujiwara declared, deeply moved. "You have the grand heart of a true empress! Therefore, I am officially upgrading your title. From this day forward, you are not just the Red Date Queen. You are the 'Holy Virtuous Red Date Queen'!"

“小百合……你的品德简直无人能及，”藤原深受感动地大声宣布。“你拥有一位真正国母的宽广胸怀！因此，我正式为你的头衔升级。从今天起，你不再仅仅是红枣皇后了。你是‘圣德贤良红枣皇后’！”

Chika, who was sipping her morning coffee, choked and burst into a fit of giggles. Miyetsuko rolled her eyes so hard it hurt.

正喝着晨间咖啡的千夏直接呛到了，随后爆发出一阵咯咯的娇笑。美悦子则翻了个大大的白眼，翻得眼睛都疼了。

"But wait," Fujiwara rubbed his chin thoughtfully, his capitalist instincts kicking in. "A title without real authority is just an empty shell. Chika runs global finance. Miyetsuko runs global security and tactical operations. Rin runs human resources. You are the only one without an official corporate position outside the bedroom."

“但是等等，”藤原若有所思地摩挲着下巴，他的资本家本能开始发作了。“没有实权的头衔只是个空壳。千夏掌管全球财务。美悦子掌管全球安保和战术行动。小铃掌管人力资源。你是这个家里唯一一个在卧室之外没有官方企业职务的人。”

Fujiwara stood up, striking a dramatic pose. "Therefore, I am creating a new position just for you. Sayuri, you are hereby appointed as the 'Absolute Plenipotentiary Executive Officer' of the Fresh Mart syndicate!"

藤原站起身，摆出了一个戏剧化的大动作。“因此，我要为你量身定制一个新职位。小百合，我在此正式任命你为‘新鲜玛特’辛迪加的‘全权专断执政官’！”

Chika immediately dropped her tablet. The glowing spreadsheets bounced on the carpet. "Wait... Boss! What does 'Absolute Plenipotentiary' mean in our corporate structure?!"

千夏手里的平板电脑瞬间掉在了地上。发光的财务报表在地毯上弹了一下。“等等……老板！‘全权专断’在咱们的企业架构里到底是个什么意思？！”

"It means she has a universal veto over everything," Fujiwara grinned proudly. "Every single decision made by the three of you is now subject to her final approval. From now on, Miyetsuko, Chika, and Rin, you will all officially obey her as your absolute Big Sister!"

“意思就是，她对所有事情都享有一票否决权，”藤原得意洋洋地咧嘴笑道。“你们三个做出的每一个决定，现在都必须经过她的最终批准。从今往后，美悦子、千夏、小铃，你们都必须正式尊奉她为你们绝对的姐姐！”

"A veto?!" Chika shrieked, clutching her head in absolute corporate terror. "Are you telling me that if I want to short the American tech sector and liquidate three hedge funds, I have to ask the Red Date Queen for permission first?! She patches your socks to save ten dollars! She'll veto my hundred-billion-dollar leverage because it 'seems a bit wasteful'!"

“一票否决权？！”千夏尖叫起来，抱着脑袋陷入了绝对的职场恐慌。“你的意思是说，如果我想做空美国科技板块，顺便清算三家对冲基金，我还得先去向红枣皇后请示？！她可是为了省十块钱连破袜子都要缝缝补补的人啊！她绝对会因为‘看起来有点铺张浪费’而否决我的一千亿美元杠杆计划的！”

Miyetsuko slowly set her throwing knives down, pointing an accusing finger at Fujiwara. "You are compromising global security because your wife was nice to you. What if I need to authorize a drone strike on a rival cartel? Is the 'Holy Virtuous Queen' going to veto my missiles because she thinks explosions are too loud and impolite?!"

美悦子慢慢放下手里的飞刀，用谴责的指尖指着藤原。“你仅仅因为你老婆对你温柔了一点，就把全球安保当儿戏。如果我需要授权用无人机轰炸敌对黑帮怎么办？这位‘圣德贤良皇后’是不是会因为觉得爆炸声太吵、太没礼貌，就把我的导弹发射给否决了？！”

Sayuri, completely taken aback by her sudden promotion to the most powerful executive on Earth, blinked her beautiful eyes. She placed a hand over her mouth, giggling softly as the absolute power rushed to her head.

小百合被这突如其来的晋升惊呆了，她眨着美丽的眼睛，完全没想到自己竟然成了地球上最有权势的高管。她用手掩着嘴，随着这股绝对权力的涌入，忍不住发出了一阵轻柔的娇笑。

"My, my," Sayuri smiled sweetly, looking at her chaotic new subordinates. "A veto power... I suppose that means no more staying up until 4 AM shorting European stocks, Chika. And Miyetsuko, perhaps we can resolve cartel disputes with a nice tea party instead of baseball bats?"

“哎呀呀，”小百合甜甜地笑着，看向她这些乱作一团的新下属。“一票否决权……我想这意味着，千夏，你以后不能再熬夜到凌晨四点去做空欧洲股票了。还有美悦子，也许我们可以用一场优雅的茶话会来解决黑帮争端，而不是动不动就用棒球棍？”

Miyetsuko and Chika groaned in unison, flopping back onto the sofa as if they had just been handed a corporate death sentence.

美悦子和千夏异口同声地哀嚎了一声，瘫倒在沙发上，仿佛刚刚接到了一份职场死刑判决书。

Only Rin seemed absolutely thrilled. The blind personnel manager clapped her hands cheerfully.

只有小铃看起来开心极了。这位盲人人事主管欢快地拍着手。

"Hooray!" Rin cheered softly. "Sayuri-nee is the big boss now! Does this mean the Plenipotentiary Executive Officer will officially help me knit the little wool socks for the assassins? We can have sisterly knitting circles!"

“万岁！”铃轻柔地欢呼着。“小百合姐现在是大老板啦！这意思是执政官大人以后会正式帮我一起给刺客们织羊毛小袜子了吗？我们可以办一个姐妹毛线团啦！”

Fujiwara laughed heartily, sitting down on the sofa and pulling Sayuri into his lap. The Emperor's reckless, romantic whim had just completely reorganized the hierarchy of the world's most dangerous syndicate, returning their luxurious morning to its usual, delightful chaos.

藤原开怀大笑，在沙发上坐下，顺手将小百合拉进自己怀里。这位帝王一时兴起的浪漫念头，就这样彻底重组了世界上最危险辛迪加的权力阶层，也让这个奢华的清晨再次回到了那种充满欢乐的混乱之中。
