Chapter 263: The Body-Selling Auction

发布时间：2026/8/31 09:17:34

第二百六十三章：卖身拍卖会

"This is demonic! Pure, unadulterated psychological torture!" Fujiwara wailed, collapsing face-first onto the living room carpet as he banged his fists in sheer agony. "They made me strip naked, parade around like a circus animal, fill out forty pages of forms, and then killed me with a single sentence at the very end! Donglai Island’s bureaucracy is a literal gateway to hell!"

“这简直是魔鬼啊！太地狱了！”藤原拖着光溜溜的疲惫身躯回到家里，像条死狗一样趴在起居室的地毯上疯狂捶地痛哭，“先让我光着屁股在大厅里社死两小时，填了四十页的变态表格，最后居然用一句‘你名下有超市’把我秒杀！东莱岛的官僚系统绝对是通往十八层地狱的直通车啊！”

Night fell over "Fresh Mart." With no clothes, no money, and no dignity left, Fujiwara finally hit rock bottom. Driven by the primal instinct of survival, the former multi-millionaire crawled toward the dining table like a pathetic street beggar, staring hungrily at the feast the women were enjoying.

夜幕降临，“新鲜玛特”的餐厅里灯火通明。没有衣服、没有金库、甚至连低保都被无情剥夺的藤原，终于彻底跌入了人生的谷底。在强烈的饥饿感驱使下，这位曾经风光无限的千万富翁此刻彻底放下了做人的尊严，像个街头乞丐一样匍匐着爬向餐桌，双眼冒光地死死盯着女人们享用的丰盛晚餐。

"Please... my honorable queens, my wealthy goddesses," Fujiwara whimpered, pressing his palms together and bowing repeatedly to the table. "Spare a poor, starving soul a crust of bread! Just a tiny bite of leftover rice! Otherwise, your beloved store manager is going to starve to death right here on this rug!"

“求求各位女王陛下、各位富婆女神行行好吧……”藤原双膝跪地，双手合十，对着满桌的山珍海味疯狂磕头哀求，“赏一口饭吃吧！哪怕是剩饭菜汤也行啊！再不济给块馒头也成，堂堂店长大人要是真饿死在自家地毯上，传出去你们脸上也无光啊！”

The four women paused, looking down at the naked, begging man with a mix of amusement and absolute contempt.

四个女人动作一顿，居高临下地看着这个赤条条跪在地上摇尾乞怜的男人，眼神里写满了嫌弃与戏谑。

"Well, look at that," Rin chuckled, taking a slow bite of her steak. "The mighty naked tycoon has officially degraded into a street mutt."

“哟，瞧瞧这可怜巴巴的样，”铃切了一块牛排送进嘴里，毫不留情地嘲笑道，“堂堂光屁股大富豪，转眼就退化成索要剩饭的流浪狗了。”

Miyetsuko pushed up her glasses, adjusting her posture with cold detachment. "Based on his current market value, nutritional intake per calorie, and lack of functional utility, I estimate his economic worth to be roughly equivalent to half a spoiled potato."

美悦子冷酷地推了推眼镜，双手环胸开启了毒舌分析：“根据他的当前市场流动性、热量转化率以及零蛋的实用价值，我评估他目前的经济身价大概相当于半个发芽的土豆。”

Seeing that begging wasn't working, Fujiwara’s desperate brain suddenly sparked a stroke of absolute, unhinged genius. He leaped up, striking a dramatic, desperate pose with his promotional fan.

眼看卖惨装可怜不管用，藤原脑子里电光石火般闪过一个绝妙（且变态）的自救方案。只见他一个鲤鱼打挺从地上站了起来，摆出一个极其妖娆又极其心酸的姿势，大声宣布道：

"Fine! Since you have no pity for the starving, I am forced to resort to desperate measures!" Fujiwara declared, puffing out his chest proudly. "I hereby officially launch the 'First Fresh Mart Body-Selling Auction'! Tonight, my physical services, my unmatched bed stamina, and my exclusive companionship are up for grabs! Highest bidder gets to sleep with me tonight. Come on, ladies, place your bids!"

“既然你们铁石心肠，那就别怪本大爷出绝招了！”藤原咬了咬牙，豁出去大喊道，“我宣布，‘新鲜玛特第一届肉体拍卖会’现在正式开标！今晚本大爷的贴身服务、绝顶床技以及独家陪伴权正式挂牌出售！起步价一碗白米饭！价高者得，今晚谁出资最高，本大爷就把自己打包送给谁！快来竞标吧！”

The dining room fell into a stunned, three-second silence before erupting into absolute, uncontrollable chaos.

餐厅里瞬间安静了三秒钟，随后爆发出了一阵险些把房顶掀翻的狂笑。

"An auction?!" Chika shrieked, laughing so hard she spilled her juice. "Master, you're completely naked, broke, and currently worth negative points! What kind of delusional scam are you running?!"

“拍卖会？！”千夏笑得前俯后仰，果汁差点喷出来，“主人，您现在不仅光着屁股、身无分文，连信用额度都是负数好吗！你拿什么跟人家搞拍卖啊！”

"Oh, I'll bid!" Sayuri chuckled, her eyes sparkling with wicked, playful amusement as she raised her hand gracefully. "I offer a bowl of leftover vegetable soup—on the condition that he wears a clown wig while drinking it."

“哎呀，那我来出个价吧！”小百合掩嘴轻笑，眼底闪烁着恶趣味的光芒，优雅地举起手，“我出一碗剩菜汤——不过前提是，他得戴着小丑假发把汤喝完。”

"I bid two expired potato chips and a kick in the ribs!" Rin shouted, cackling wildly.

“我出两片过期薯条，外加一记排山倒海的侧踢！”铃唯恐天下不乱地举手大喊。

Faced with a room full of merciless, laughing bidders, the naked auctioneer realized his body was currently valued way lower than expected, plunging the household's bizarre daily comedy to an entirely new, hilarious low.

面对这群杀价杀到脚踝骨底下的无情买家，这位赤条条的落魄店长欲哭无泪地发现，自己的身体行情在家里居然连一碗剩汤都快换不起了。整个屋子里充满了快活而混乱的爆笑气息。
