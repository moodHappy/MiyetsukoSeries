Chapter 143: The Miraculous Eggs

发布时间：2026/8/26 23:23:33

第百四十三章：鸡蛋引发的“奇迹”

Life at "Fresh Mart" always managed to swing wildly between the solemn weight of the outside world and the sheer, unfiltered absurdity of running a local grocery store on Donglai Island.

“新鲜玛特”的日子总是这样，上一秒还沉浸在外界风雨交加的凝重中，下一秒就会被东莱岛上各种奇奇怪怪的市井荒诞剧拉回现实。

A few days after the relief funds were safely channeled through their shell corporations, a peculiar incident disrupted the afternoon quiet of the shop. A stunning, glamorous beauty with an air of furious indignation marched right through the front doors, slamming her designer handbag onto the counter.

就在那笔巨额救援资金通过层层套壳公司神不知鬼不觉地运转出去几天后的一个下午，店里迎来了一位极其引人注目的不速之客。那是一位容貌美艳、身材火辣的大美女，此刻却满脸怒容地踩着高跟鞋走进店里，“啪”地一声把昂贵的名牌手袋砸在柜台上。

"Are you the owner of this place?!" the beauty demanded, glaring daggers at Fujiwara. "I want an explanation! Your grocery store is selling fraudulent merchandise! My grandmother bought a carton of your so-called organic farm eggs, and..." She paused, taking a furious breath, "...and now she's pregnant!"

“你就是这家店的老板吧？！”美女怒气冲冲地瞪着藤原，“我要你们给个说法！你们店里居然卖这种荒唐的假冒伪劣商品！我奶奶上周在你们这儿买了一箱所谓的有机土鸡蛋，结果……”她气得深吸了一口气，“结果她老人家居然怀孕了！”

Fujiwara, who had been leaning casually against the cash register, froze mid-sip of his coffee. He stared at the gorgeous customer, blinking in sheer, helpless bewilderment. For a solid ten seconds, his brain completely short-circuited.

原本正懒洋洋地靠在收银台边喝咖啡的藤原当场石化，嘴里的咖啡差点喷出来。他目瞪口呆地看着眼前这位气势汹汹的美女，脑子足足宕机了十秒钟。

"Pardon?" Fujiwara managed, offering a weak, helpless smile. "Miss, let's get our facts straight. We sell fresh farm eggs, not mystical fertility elixirs blessed by ancient deities. Unless your grandmother is a biological anomaly defying modern medical science, I'm pretty sure my chickens have nothing to do with it."

“哈……？您说什么？”藤原嘴角抽搐了一下，露出一抹极其无奈的苦笑，“小姐，咱们讲点道理行吗？我们这儿卖的只是正经的农家鲜鸡蛋，可不是什么神仙显灵的送子观音套餐。除非您奶奶是打破现代医学常识的生物学奇迹，否则这锅我们店里的母鸡可真背不动啊……”

After an exasperated argument where the furious customer insisted on demanding a "genetic refund" before storming out in a huff, Fujiwara finally closed the shop early and retreated to the living room, collapsing onto the sofa to recount the bizarre tale to Miyetsuko, Rin, and Sayuri.

经过一番鸡同鸭讲、甚至差点演变成“要求店里赔偿精神损失”的荒唐争吵后，那位气急败坏的美女最终气冲冲地摔门而去。藤原无奈地叹了口气，干脆提前关了店门，回到起居室一屁股瘫在沙发上，把刚才这段离谱的奇葩经历当成笑话讲给美悦子、铃和小百合听。

The moment he finished, Rin burst into a peal of uncontrollable, uproarious laughter, clutching her stomach as she rolled across the sofa. "Oh, my god! A pregnant grandma from eating grocery store eggs?! Fujiwara, what the hell are you feeding those chickens? Pure atomic radiation?!"

听完这番转述，铃当场爆发出毫无形象的狂笑，整个人笑得直不起腰，在沙发上滚来滚去：“我的天呐！吃个鸡蛋能把老太太吃怀孕？！藤原，你老实交代，你平时到底给店里的母鸡喂了什么神仙饲料？核辐射催化剂吗？！”

Sayuri covered her mouth, her shoulders shaking with helpless, gentle giggles. "Goodness... that poor customer must have been under an incredible amount of stress to come up with something so imaginative. Though... it certainly makes our daily routine a lot more colorful."

小百合捂着嘴笑得花枝乱颤，肩膀不停地耸动着：“天哪……那位顾客家里最近一定是遇到了什么让人匪夷所思的事，才会把这种离奇的账算到鸡蛋头上。不过……有这样的趣事调剂，咱们店里的日常生活还真是越来越热闹了呢。”

Miyetsuko pushed up her glasses, a clinical, highly amused smirk playing on her lips. She calmly flipped open a medical reference book on the coffee table. "A magnificent display of misattributed causality. Either a profound generational misunderstanding of human biology, or an elderly matriarch leading a far more adventurous secret life than her grandchildren ever suspected. In either case, Fujiwara, I suggest labeling your next egg shipment with a formal disclaimer: Not responsible for miraculous divine interventions."

美悦子推了推眼镜，嘴角勾起一抹冷静而玩味的微笑，慢条斯理地合上手头的一本医学参考书：“极其典型的因果关系错置。要么是年轻一代对人类生物学产生了惊人的误解，要么就是那位老太太的晚年私生活远比她的子孙想象得要精彩刺激得多。不过说真的，藤原，我建议你明天在鸡蛋货架上挂个醒目的免责声明：本店概不负责因食用鸡蛋引发的神秘生命奇迹。”

Surrounded by the bright, ringing laughter of his brilliant women, Fujiwara shook his head with a fond, helpless smile, pulling Rin and Sayuri closer as the absurd, chaotic, and deeply cherished warmth of "Fresh Mart" enveloped them once again.

听着女人们此起彼伏的欢声笑语，藤原无奈地摇了摇头，脸上却挂着宠溺的笑容。他顺势将凑过来的铃和小百合揽入怀中，在这间荒诞、温馨又无比真实的“新鲜玛特”里，尽情享受着属于他们独一无二的平静时光。
