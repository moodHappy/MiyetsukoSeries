第五百六十五章：合体之精

In the golden days of the empire, Emperor Fujiwara routinely commanded all four of his magnificent beauties to share the massive imperial bed simultaneously. However, after returning from his diplomatic trip to the Succubus Realm as a severely dehydrated, traumatized husk, his geopolitical ambitions remained intact, but his biological confidence had suffered a catastrophic blow. The very thought of multi-tasking four incredibly demanding women at once seemed like a terrifying, high-speed rail journey back to the intensive care unit.

在帝国曾经的黄金岁月里，藤原大帝理所当然地要求他那四位绝色佳丽每晚同寝伺候。然而，自从结束了对魅魔界的外交访问，变成一具严重脱水、深受心理创伤的干尸被抬回来之后，尽管他的地缘政治野心依然旺盛，但他那饱受摧残的生理自信心却遭遇了毁灭性的打击。现在光是想想同时应付四个极其难缠的女人，感觉就像是搭乘了一列通往重症监护室的高速列车。

While the tyrant was brooding over his compromised stamina on the velvet sofa, Queen Rin happily swung her legs from her fluffy cushion, loudly munching on a cookie. The newly crowned Succubus Queen had brought back far more than just a terrifying title from her maiden home.

正当这位暴君瘫在天鹅绒沙发上，为自己大不如前的持久力长吁短叹时，女王小铃正坐在毛绒软垫上，开心地晃着双腿，嘎吱嘎吱地啃着饼干。这位新晋的魅魔女王从娘家带回来的，可不仅仅是一个令人闻风丧胆的头衔。

"Um... hello, Master? Don't be sad!" the little blind demon chirped brightly, her milky eyes wide with sweet, terrifying demonic innovation. "After Rin ascended the throne, Rin got to read the restricted royal archives! There is a super secret underworld magic trick called the 'Essence of Fusion'!"

“唔……主人听得见吗？不要伤心啦！”瞎眼的小恶魔甜丝丝地欢呼道，空灵的盲眼里闪烁着极其恐怖的恶魔创新之光，“小铃登基之后，获得了查阅皇家限制级绝密档案的权限哦！那里记录着一个超级厉害的深渊绝密法术，叫做‘合体之精’！”

Fujiwara raised a skeptical, sunken eyebrow. "The Essence of Fusion? Does it involve me drinking more imported milk? Because my kidneys are still filing formal complaints with my nervous system."

藤原挑起一根充满怀疑的、深陷的眉毛。“合体之精？这需要朕再喝下更多吨的进口牛奶吗？因为朕的肾脏现在还在向中枢神经系统提交正式抗议呢。”

"No, no! It's an outsourcing spell!" Rin giggled, enthusiastically clapping her hands. "We just take a tiny piece of the souls from Sayuri-nee, Chika-nee, Miyetsuko, and Rin, and mash them all together! It creates a brand new, temporary physical homunculus that lasts for exactly twenty-four hours! Master can enjoy all four of our flavors and shapes combined into one ultimate body, but you only have to spend the physical energy of sleeping with one person! Plus, the rest of us can just go about our normal lives without getting tired!"

“不不不！这是一个外包法术呀！”小铃开心地拍着小手娇笑道，“我们只需要从小百合姐姐、千夏姐姐、美悦子和小铃的灵魂里各自抽取一小片，然后把它们全部揉捏在一起！这样就能创造出一具全新的、只能维持二十四小时的临时肉身！主人可以在这一具终极躯体上，同时享受我们四个人的所有味道和形状，而且您只需要消耗应付一个人的体力！最棒的是，我们四个本尊依然可以照常过咱们的日常生活，根本不会觉得累！”

The grand salon instantly fell into a stunned, deeply analytical silence as the absolute brilliance of this demonic loophole settled over the harem.

随着这个魅魔界法律漏洞的绝妙之处在后宫中彻底传开，整个大起居殿瞬间陷入了一阵充满震撼与深度分析的死寂。

Sayuri, looking gloriously plump in her Tang-dynasty silks, elegantly snapped her velvet fan open. The Wise Empress immediately approached the black magic from a strictly macroeconomic perspective.

穿着盛唐真丝长袍、显得丰腴极品的小百合，优雅地“啪”一声打开了丝绒折扇。这位贤后立刻从极其严谨的宏观经济学角度，对这项黑魔法进行了可行性评估。

"A truly flawless corporate restructuring, my Lord," Sayuri purred smoothly, a velvet smirk on her pristine lips. "By delegating our nocturnal administrative duties to a temporary, synthesized sub-contractor, the imperial household achieves maximum operational efficiency. Master avoids systemic burnout, and I finally have uninterrupted time to review the empire's quarterly tax returns."

“当真是一次完美无瑕的集团架构重组呢，夫君，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，完美无瑕的红唇边挂着一抹丝绒般的冷笑，“通过将我们夜间的行政服务职责，全权下放给一个临时合成的外包临时工，皇室的运营效率将达到最大化。主人既避免了系统性的过劳死，而臣妾也终于有不被打扰的完整时间，来审查帝国第三季度的税务报表了。”

Chika grabbed a handful of spicy chips, her heavy, milk-swollen chest heaving with sheer, lazy excitement. "Wait, you mean I can just sit here on the sofa watching my trashy daytime soap operas and eating snacks, while my soul-clone does all the heavy lifting in the bedroom?! This is the greatest labor rights reform in the history of the universe! Sign me up for the fusion!"

千夏抓起一把麻辣薯片，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔随着她那纯粹的、懒汉般的激动剧烈起伏着。“等等，你的意思是，我可以就这么舒舒服服地瘫在沙发上，一边追我的无脑狗血白日肥皂剧一边啃零食，而我的灵魂克隆体则在卧室里替我干那些体力活？！这简直是全宇宙历史上最伟大的劳工权益改革！算我一个，我要合体！”

Miyetsuko, kneeling dutifully on the Persian rug, offered a deeply historical bow, her cheeks flushed with aristocratic awe. "To distill the dignity of a royal princess, the raw milk production of a commoner, the plump wisdom of an Empress, and the endless stamina of a Succubus into a single, disposable vessel... The underworld's administrative efficiency is truly terrifying."

极其顺从地跪在波斯地毯上的美悦子，行了一个充满历史考究的大礼，脸颊上带着贵族式的震撼。“将皇家公主的端庄、平民阶级的原生态产奶量、贤后那丰腴的智慧，以及魅魔那深不见底的体力，全部浓缩进一个一次性的容器里……地狱的行政效率当真是令人胆寒。”

Fujiwara’s eyes widened. The absolute tyrant, who had spent the last fifteen days fearing for the structural integrity of his own pelvis, felt an overwhelming surge of pure, ecstatic joy. He slammed his hand against the mahogany table, roaring with tyrannical laughter.

藤原的眼睛瞬间亮了。这位在过去十五天里一直为自己骨盆的结构完整性而担惊受怕的绝对暴君，此刻感到一股排山倒海般纯粹、狂喜的快乐。他猛地一巴掌拍在红木茶几上，爆发出震天动地的暴君狂笑。

"Absolute perfection!" Fujiwara declared, his dark eyes burning with renewed, highly-efficient lust. "The Empire has officially entered the era of four-in-one centralized bedroom planning! A masterpiece of magical engineering! Rin, initialize the spell immediately! Sayuri, Chika, Miyetsuko—surrender your soul fragments! The Emperor demands to inspect this temporary chimera right this second!"

“绝对的完美！”藤原大声宣告，漆黑的双眼里重新燃起了极其高效的旺盛欲火，“帝国正式迈入了‘四合一’中央集权卧室规划的新时代！这简直是魔法工程学的绝顶杰作！小铃，立刻启动法术阵列！小百合、千夏、美悦子——统统把你们的灵魂碎片交出来！皇帝陛下现在、立刻、马上就要亲自检阅这具临时的终极缝合怪！”
