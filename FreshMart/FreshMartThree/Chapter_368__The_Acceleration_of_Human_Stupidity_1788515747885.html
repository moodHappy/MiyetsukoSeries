Chapter 368: The Acceleration of Human Stupidity

发布时间：2026/9/4 17:55:46

第三百六十八章：人类愚蠢的加速主义

Exactly two weeks after the riots and the state-sanctioned purge, the five housemates gathered around the living room television once again, nursing their morning coffee in stunned, breathless silence.

就在暴动和国家机器清洗过去整整两周后，五个人再次围坐在客厅的电视机前，一边端着早晨的咖啡，一边陷入了目瞪口呆的死寂之中。

On the screen, a fiery, highly rated morning talk show host was passionately slamming the fists on his desk, defending Fresh Mart with theatrical tears in his eyes. Internet forums and social media algorithms had completely flipped overnight, trending with viral hashtags declaring that the recent protesters were nothing more than "state-wreckers and foreign-funded agitators."

屏幕上，一位慷慨激昂的金牌早间脱口秀主持人正用力拍打着桌子，眼里甚至闪烁着戏剧性的泪光，正声泪俱下地为“新鲜玛特”辩护。各大网络论坛和社交媒体算法在一夜之间彻底反转，热门话题榜上全是以“抗议者实乃国家破坏者与外国资助煽动者”为主题的病毒式热搜。

"Fresh Mart is a pure, conscientious enterprise!" the anchor bellowed passionately into the camera. "They provided fresh, wholesome food to our children! But in this corrupt environment, conscientious businesses are always framed by malicious forces whose interests were threatened by their honesty!"

“‘新鲜玛特’是一家彻头彻尾的良心企业！”主持人对着镜头激情怒吼，“他们为我们的孩子提供了最新鲜、最健康的食品！然而在这个糟糕的环境中，良心企业往往会被那些利益受损的别有用心之人无情陷害！”

In the living room, Fujiwara slowly dropped his mug, turned around, and fell to his knees directly in front of Miyetsuko, staring up at her with the fanatical, teary-eyed devotion of a medieval cult worshipper.

客厅里，藤原缓缓放下马克杯，转过身直接跪倒在美悦子面前，用一种中世纪邪教信徒般狂热而含泪的崇拜目光仰视着她。

"Master Miyetsuko!" Fujiwara sobbed dramatically, clutching the hem of her skirt. "You said ninety days! You said three months! But it only took fourteen days! Your predictive algorithms are so terrifyingly accurate they border on dark sorcery!"

“美悦子大人！”藤原夸张地啜泣着，紧紧抓着她的裙摆，“你之前说要九十天！说要三个月！可结果居然只用了十四天！你的预测算法精准得简直让人毛骨悚然，这已经涉嫌黑魔法了吧！”

Miyetsuko calmly adjusted her glasses, a rare, smug twitch of satisfaction playing at the corner of her lips. "A minor miscalculation. My statistical models severely underestimated the exponential growth rate of internet echo chambers combined with a generalized societal baseline of cognitive decay. Human stupidity is truly a perpetual motion machine."

美悦子慢条斯理地推了推眼镜，嘴角罕见地浮现出一抹极其得意的冷笑：“这是一个微小的统计学失误。我的统计模型严重低估了互联网回音室效应与社会整体认知水平退化之间的指数级增长规律。人类的愚蠢果然是一台永动机。”

Rin crossed her arms, shivering slightly as she stared at the glowing screen. "It's genuinely terrifying. Two weeks ago they were trying to burn us alive with torches, and now they're treating us like national martyrs? Have these people's brains completely turned to mush?"

铃双手抱胸，看着发光的屏幕忍不住打了个冷战：“这简直太可怕了。两周前他们还举着火把想把咱们活活烧死，现在就把咱们当成民族烈士来供着了？这帮人的脑子是全变成浆糊了吗？”

Chika blinked innocently, tilting her head. "Uhm... so does this mean we don't have to pack up and run away to a remote island anymore? Should we... should we raise the price of the expired chicken again to celebrate?"

千夏天真地眨了眨眼，歪着头问道：“呃……那这意思是不是说，我们不用收拾包袱逃到荒岛上去了？那我们要不要……庆祝一下，把过期鸡肉的价格再往上提一提？”

Sayuri chuckled softly, swirling her teacup with an amused, glittering smile. "That’s the capitalist spirit, Chika. Let the mob scream and wave their signs all they want outside. As long as Miyetsuko is steering our propaganda engine, we'll be selling expired energy drinks to our most devoted fanatics by sundown."

小百合轻笑出声，优雅地晃着茶杯，眼中闪烁着愉悦的光芒：“这才是资本主义的灵魂嘛，千夏。随外面那群乌合之众怎么在门口叫嚣抗议。只要有美悦子在帮我们开动宣传机器，太阳落山前，我们就能把过期功能饮料高价卖给这群最忠实的狂热粉丝了。”
