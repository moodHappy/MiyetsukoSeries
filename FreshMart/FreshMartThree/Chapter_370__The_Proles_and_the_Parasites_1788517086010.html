第三百七十章：打工人与寄生虫

By the time the heavy security shutters finally rolled down and the last of the patriotic, flag-waving fanatics drifted away into the night, the three girls staggered back into the employee breakroom. They looked like refugees fresh out of a trench warfare zone—hair static-charged, aprons stained with expired soda spills, and feet throbbing with pure agony.

当沉重的防锁卷帘门终于拉下、最后一批狂热挥舞着旗帜的爱国狂热分子夜色中散去时，三个女孩摇摇晃晃地挪回了员工休息室。她们的模样简直就像刚从战壕里爬出来的难民——头发凌乱、围裙上沾满了过期的饮料渍、双脚酸痛得快要失去了知觉。

"I swear... if one more person asks me if our expired bread was blessed by the founding fathers, I'm going to commit a hate crime," Rin groaned, collapsing limply onto the nearest couch and burying her face in a cushion.

“我发誓……要是再有一个人跑来问我我们的过期面包是不是经过国父开光加持的，我绝对要当场犯下仇恨罪，”铃无力地呻吟着，直接瘫软在最近的沙发上，把脸埋进抱枕里。

Chika dragged herself across the floor, clutching a heavy metal cash box packed to the brim with patriotic earnings. She dropped it on the table with a loud clatter and flopped face-first onto the carpet, whimpering, "My arms feel like they're about to fall off... I scanned four thousand items today..."

千夏拖着疲惫不堪的身体挪进屋里，手里还死死抱着一个塞满爱国营业额的沉重金属钱箱。她“当啷”一声把箱子砸在桌上，整个人直接脸朝下扑倒在地毯上，带着哭腔呜咽道：“我的胳膊快要断掉了……我今天扫码扫了足足四千件商品……”

Miyetsuko adjusted her crooked glasses, her usually unflappable demeanor replaced by a dark, murderous aura. "Retail exploitation index has exceeded acceptable physiological thresholds. The division of labor within this establishment is mathematically unjust."

美悦子扶了扶歪掉的眼镜，平日里古井无波的学霸脸上此刻正笼罩着一层黑暗的杀气：“零售剥削指数已经完全超出了人类的生理极限。这家店里的人力分工在数学逻辑上简直极度不公。”

Fuming with righteous exhaustion, Miyetsuko kicked open the door to the back storage room, expecting to find Fujiwara desperately cataloging inventory or at least sweeping the floor.

怀揣着满腔被压榨的怒火，美悦子一脚踹开了后仓储藏室的大门，本以为会看到藤原正拼命盘点库存或者至少在拖地的情景。

Instead, the horrific, shameless reality of the back room hit them like a physical blow.

然而，映入眼帘的后仓景象却像一记重拳，狠狠砸在她们的脸上。

Fujiwara and Sayuri were sprawled across a makeshift mattress of empty cardboard boxes, locked in a deep, snoring sleep of utter exhaustion. The afternoon's unhinged debauchery had left them looking like victims of a mild natural disaster. Sayuri's black silk stockings were shredded into useless, dangling ribbons around her ankles, her tight skirt had been shoved all the way up to her chest, one of her soft, flushed breasts was completely exposed to the chilly night air, and her neck was heavily bruised and stained red from an afternoon of breathless intimacy.

藤原和小百合正横七竖八地瘫在一张用空纸箱临时搭成的床垫上，陷入了极度疲惫的深沉鼾声中。下午那场毫无节制的疯狂放纵，把他们俩折腾得宛如刚经历了一场轻度自然灾害。小百合的黑丝袜已经被扯成了挂在脚踝上的破烂布条，屁股上的包臀裙甚至被撸到了胸口，其中一颗雪白饱满的奶子大喇喇地暴露在微凉的空气中，白皙的脖颈上满是被激烈亲热留下的红痕与斑驳吻痕。

Right beside her, Fujiwara lay flat on his back with his mouth wide open, drool pooling on a stack of promotional flyers, completely drained. His once-hyperactive organ was now limp, shriveled, and pathetic, resting like a sad, deflated balloon between his thighs.

而在她身旁，藤原正四脚朝天地躺着，嘴巴大张，口水都快流到了一叠促销宣传单上。他那根在下午大显神威的雄性象征此刻已经彻底罢工，软绵绵、皱巴巴地瘫在大腿根部，活像一个泄了气的悲伤气球。

The trio stood frozen in the doorway, staring at the scandalous, sleeping wreckage of their bosses. For three full seconds, the only sound in the room was Fujiwara's rhythmic, rhythmic snoring and Sayuri's soft, exhausted whimpers in her sleep.

三个女孩呆若木鸡地杵在门口，死死盯着这两个睡得昏天暗地的荒淫罪魁祸首。整整三秒钟里，屋里唯一的动静就只有藤原那富有节奏的呼噜声和小百合在梦中发出的微弱呻吟。

Then, the veins on Rin's forehead began to throb violently.

紧接着，铃额头上的青筋开始疯狂暴起。

"We... we were out there selling expired tuna to foaming-at-the-mouth nationalists like galley slaves for ten hours straight..." Rin whispered, her voice trembling with homicidal fury as she slowly cracked her knuckles. "...And these two absolute parasites spent the whole afternoon playing adult gymnastics while we did all the heavy lifting?!"

“我们……我们在外面像奴隶一样连轴转了十个小时，把过期的金枪鱼卖给那群发疯的爱国狂热分子……”铃咬牙切齿地低语着，声音因极致的杀意而颤抖，同时慢慢捏响了指节，“……而这两个纯种寄生虫倒好，把我们当牛使唤，自己在后屋快活了一下午？！”

Chika burst into hysterical, exhausted tears, pointing a trembling finger at the sleeping couple. "They're sleeping so peacefully! Look at her clothes! Look at his... his pathetic little limp thing! It's so unfair!"

千夏气得眼泪都快飙出来了，颤抖着手指指向那对酣睡的狗男女：“他们俩居然睡得这么香！你们看她的衣服！再看他那……那软趴趴的死样子！凭什么啊！”

Miyetsuko slowly rolled up a thick stack of unsold expired price tags, her eyes flashing behind her lenses with cold, calculated bloodlust. "Rebellion protocol initiated. Let's wake up our dear leaders with a mandatory, high-intensity wake-up call."

美悦子面无表情地卷起厚厚一沓没卖出去的过期价签，镜片后闪烁着冰冷而残酷的寒光：“武装政变协议启动。让我们用一点高强度的物理唤醒服务，来迎接咱们亲爱的店长和小百合大人吧。”
