第三百七十二章：甩锅大战与白莲花演技

Sayuri finally fluttered her heavy, sleep-deprived eyes open. As the harsh reality of her situation hit her—bound tightly to a concrete pillar, half-naked, with shredded black stockings and one voluptuous breast hanging out in the cold air—her professional actress instincts kicked in at the speed of light.

小百合终于缓缓睁开沉重的睡眼。当她看清自己眼下的处境时——被死死五花大绑在水泥柱上，衣衫不整，黑丝破烂，甚至有一颗饱满的奶子大喇喇地露在冰冷的空气中——她那职业女演员的求生本能瞬间以光速启动了。

Tears welled up in her eyes instantly, turning her expression into a masterpiece of pure, trembling innocence. She sniffled pitifully, letting out a sob that practically screamed Oscar-worthy drama.

她的眼眶里瞬间蓄满了泪水，表情在一秒内切换成了纯洁无辜、我见犹怜的受害者模样。她抽噎着，发出一声堪称奥斯卡级别的绝美啜泣。

"Wait! Please, girls, you have to believe me! I'm entirely innocent!" Sayuri wailed, desperately trying to pull her shredded skirt upward. "I was actually planning to go out front and help you scan those expired cans! But he—!" She glared fiercely over her shoulder at Fujiwara. "The store manager abused his executive power, dragged me into the back room, forced me to spank his backside, and aggressively demanded that I service him with extreme intensity!"

“等等！求求你们相信我，我是无辜的！”小百合带着哭腔哀嚎着，拼命想把破碎的裙子往上拉，“我本来……本来是打算去前面帮你们扫码卖过期罐头的！可是他——！”她转过头，恶狠狠地瞪着身后的藤原，“店长滥用职权，把我硬生生拽进后屋，强迫我服侍他，还口口声声命令我动作必须激烈点、配合必须到位！”

Sayuri choked back another theatrical sob, thrusting her chest forward—accentuating her exposed, reddened flesh. "I didn't want any of this! I'm just a fragile, exploited victim of workplace sexual harassment! If anyone deserves to be burned at the stake, it's him! I demand a divorce from this toxic partnership!"

小百合哽咽着挤出更多的眼泪，刻意挺了挺胸膛，把那处被揉得红肿的春光暴露得更彻底：“我根本是被迫的！我只是个惨遭职场性骚扰的弱势群体啊！要烧也该烧这个罪魁祸首藤原！我要求立刻和这个变态解散合伙关系！”

Fujiwara’s eyes nearly popped out of his head. He strained against the zip-ties, his face twisting from humiliation to absolute, fiery rage.

藤原的眼珠子差点瞪出来。他拼命挣扎着身上的扎带，脸色从之前的尴尬瞬间扭曲成了熊熊燃烧的离奇愤怒。

"What did you just call me, you little hypocritical bitch?!" Fujiwara roared back, his voice cracking with utter betrayal. "You're playing the innocent white lotus now?! Who was the one riding on top of me all afternoon screaming 'harder, manager-san, don't stop'? Who kept begging me to go deeper while clawing up my back?!"

“你刚才叫我什么？你这个两面三刀的小贱种？！”藤原气急败坏地怒吼道，声音因遭到背叛而破音，“这会儿倒装起纯洁白莲花了？！下午到底是谁骑在我身上一边疯狂摇摆一边喊着‘店长用力、不要停’的？！是谁一直哭着求我深入一点、还在我背上抓出十几个血印子的？！”

Sayuri’s tearful facade flickered for a split second before she snapped back with equal venom, her voice dropping into a harsh hiss. "Shut up, you useless, thirty-second-man! You forced me at gunpoint—metaphorically speaking! I was just fulfilling my duties as an executive madam!"

小百合那张梨花带雨的俏脸瞬间僵硬了一秒，随即冷哼一声，恶狠狠地反唇相讥：“闭嘴吧你这个三十秒就完事的废柴！你那是强迫！我只是在履行高级妈妈桑的指导义务！”

Standing off to the side, Rin, Chika, and Miyetsuko watched the back-to-back couple tear into each other with bags of popcorn they had somehow materialized out of thin air.

站在一旁的铃、千夏和美悦子则一边津津有味地看着这出“背靠背狗咬狗”的大戏，一边不知从哪掏出一包爆米花吃了起来。

"Well," Rin muttered, casually tossing a popcorn kernel into her mouth as she watched Fujiwara's re-erected member twitch in rage. "The political crisis is resolved, the patriotic profits are rolling in, and the island's most toxic couple is currently divorcing via tiki torch execution. Honestly? Best Tuesday ever."

“行吧，”铃随手扔了一颗爆米花进嘴里，斜眼瞥着藤原那根气得直哆嗦的物件，“政治危机化了解了，爱国钞票数到手软了，岛上最恶劣的狗男女现在正准备在火刑柱前办离婚手续。说实话？这是过得最爽的一个周二了。”
