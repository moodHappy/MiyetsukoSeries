Chapter 643: The Grand Orgy Ball and Imperial Cultural Integration

发布时间：2026/11/6 17:35:48

第六百四十三章：群交舞会与帝国的文化输出

Following the swift military annexation of several rebellious frontier star systems, the Imperial Ministry of Rites and Civil Order issued a mandatory decree to celebrate the integration of the newly conquered territories.

随着数个桀骜不驯的边陲外星领地被大军迅速荡平并吞并入版图，帝国礼部与治安司随即联合颁布了一道强制法令，以庆贺新归附领地的正式接纳。

To demonstrate the boundless cultural superiority of the Fujiwara Empire and assess the political submission of the conquered populace, the Crown announced the immediate staging of a grand, mandatory "Imperial Orgy Ball" across the provincial capitals.

为了全面彰显藤原帝国无远弗届的文化输出，并对新属地臣民的归顺度进行摸底，朝廷高调宣布将在各大星区首府举办一场全民参与的盛大“帝国群交舞会”。

The decree explicitly commanded all former feudal aristocrats, provincial ministers, and local oligarchs to present their dignified wives and sheltered daughters at the grand ballroom, stripped of all traditional titles and clad only in ceremonial, see-through gossamer.

诏书中白纸黑字勒令前朝的旧贵族、地方领主与豪门巨贾，必须准时携家中原本养尊处优的端庄贵夫人及其深闺千金出席，届时一律剥除所有爵禄头衔，仅着统一的轻薄透明薄纱入场。

Inside the imperial salon, the logistics of the celebratory festivities were projected across the holographic tactical map, prompting a round of cynical amusement among the inner council.

大起居殿内，这场浩大庆典的筹备明细被全数投映在全息战术光幕之上，引得内廷众人端着香茗，品头论足起来。

"A grand orgy masked as high diplomacy," Chika remarked with a loud snort, tossing a peeled grape into her mouth. "You couldn't settle for standard tax extraction or hostage exchanges; you had to go straight for a planetary-scale debauchery mixer just to strip the local elites of their remaining dignity."

“披着高端外交皮的行星级大乱交，”千夏嚼着葡萄冷嗤一声，没好气地打趣道，“寻常朝廷开疆拓土，无非是催缴钱粮赋税或者羁押质子；您老人家倒好，开门第一桩事就是办群交舞会，非得把当地贵族的最后一点体面扒得一干二净不可。”

Sayuri gently tapped her velvet fan against her wrist, analyzing the strategic dimensions with a dry, aristocratic smirk. "From the perspective of imperial statecraft, it is remarkably efficient. The willingness of an aristocratic caste to surrender their wives and daughters to public carnality serves as an infallible litmus test for political compliance. A lord who endures such humiliation without rebellion is a lord completely broken in spirit."

小百合以折扇轻击腕间，唇角挂着清冷而锐利的玩味笑意：“从统御帝术的角度考量，此举倒是省心高效得很。旧日权贵是否甘愿交出正室与名门闺秀置于大庭广众之下任由宣淫，历来是检验其顺从度的绝佳试金石。连这等奇耻大辱都能咬牙咽下的领主，其反抗意志便已彻底从根子上瓦解了。”

Miyetsuko adjusted her 183828号 Death God glasses, consulting the administrative rosters for the occupied zones. "The local registry confirms over four thousand noble matrons and unwed daughters have been logged into the guest manifest. Any household attempting to substitute commoners or feign illness has been threatened with immediate orbital bombardment for treason."

美悦子推了推鼻梁上的183828号死神眼镜，翻看着占领区的户籍名册冷淡道：“地方名籍显示，已有四千余名旧族贵妇与未出阁的千金录入与会名册。凡企图以婢女冒名顶替或托病告假者，皆已依谋逆大罪由驻防舰队施以天基轨道打击的直接威慑。”

Rin chuckled gleefully, her succubus tail swishing across the edge of the dais. "The best part is watching those refined, prudish ladies who spent their lives preaching modesty get dragged into the center of the hall, discovering that imperial culture means getting shared like snacks at a buffet!"

小铃倚在王座边掩口嘻嘻直笑，魅魔尾巴摇得飞快：“最有趣的莫过于看那些平日里满口妇德清规、端庄不可方物的贵妇名媛们被推进舞池正中央，终于亲身体会到咱们帝国的文化输出究竟是何等滋味——就像自助餐里的点心一样，人人有份嘛！”

Emperor Fujiwara lounged across the obsidian throne, grinning unabashedly as he reviewed the portraits of the trembling provincial beauties awaiting his arrival.

藤原大帝大刺刺地仰卧在黑曜石王座上，端着美酒，满脸得意地扫视着光幕上那些战战兢兢等候御临的异星名媛画像。

"This is the cornerstone of true imperial integration!" Fujiwara declared grandly, raising his goblet in mock triumph. "We liberate them from their archaic feudal modesty, introduce them to genuine universal freedom, and thoroughly enjoy the finest noble flowers of the frontier all in one stroke! Call the heralds—the Emperor is ready to lead the dance!"

“这才是真正的帝国大一统基石！”藤原大言不惭地举杯狂笑，浑不知羞耻为何物，“既替他们破除了陈腐虚伪的封建礼教，又让领民切实体会到了何为‘四海一家的大自由’，顺带还能让朕与将士们将这些娇滴滴的贵族花朵好生采撷品尝一番！传礼官起驾——朕要亲自去领舞了！”
