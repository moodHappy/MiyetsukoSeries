第二十二章：精油与肌肤

Fujiwara chuckled softly, reaching into one of the luxury shopping bags they had hauled back from the boutique earlier. He pulled out a sleek, frosted glass bottle and held it up under the dim bedside light.

藤原轻笑了一声，从下午在精品店带回来的奢侈品购物袋里摸出一个精致的磨砂小玻璃瓶，在昏暗的床头灯下晃了晃。

"Did you sneak this from the boutique too?" Rin raised an eyebrow, a playful, teasing glint in her eyes. "A tough guy like you raiding a luxury store is one thing, but since when do you dabble in high-end massage oils?"

📌 Key Expressions

  • sneak sth. from... = 从……偷偷拿走某物
  • boutique = 精品店；高档专卖店
  • raise an eyebrow = 挑眉；扬起眉毛（表示惊讶、怀疑或调侃）
  • a playful, teasing glint = 调皮、戏谑的眼神
  • glint in one's eyes = 某人眼中闪过的光芒/神色
  • tough guy = 硬汉；硬汉形象的人
  • raid a store = 洗劫一家商店；闯进店里大肆搜刮
  • luxury store = 奢侈品商店；高档商店
  • is one thing, but... = ……是一回事，但……
  • since when...? = 从什么时候开始……的？
  • dabble in... = 涉猎……；玩玩……；偶尔尝试……
  • high-end = 高端的
  • massage oil = 按摩油
  • dabble in high-end massage oils = 居然开始涉猎/研究高端按摩油了

"A smart man always plans ahead," Fujiwara grinned, uncapping the bottle. A subtle, intoxicating blend of white tea and citrus drifted into the warm air. He poured a few warm drops into his palms and rubbed his hands together. "I saw you eyeing the relaxation section while Miyetsuko and Sayuri were busy buying dresses. Before Rin could fire back with a sarcastic remark, Fujiwara gently pressed her back against the mattress, smoothing his slick, fragrant palms over her shoulders.

还没等铃开口调侃，藤原已经顺势将她轻轻按倒在床垫上，将沾满滑润精油的双手覆上了她的肩膀。

Fujiwara didn't stop at her shoulders. He smoothed the oil down her spine, over her waist, and across her slender legs, making her skin gleam under the golden light. Rin closed her eyes, her breathing deepening as her fierce, guarded exterior completely gave way to pure, unadulterated comfort.

藤原并没有止步于肩膀。他顺势将精油涂抹过她的脊椎、纤细的腰肢和修长的双腿，让她的肌肤在柔和的灯光下泛着诱人的光泽。铃缓缓闭上双眼，呼吸渐渐加深，那层平日里用来伪装的强悍外壳在极致的舒适中彻底卸下。

"You're awfully spoiled tonight, big guy," Rin murmured, her voice laced with lazy contentment as she pulled him down toward her.

“今晚被你伺候得太舒服了，大块头，”铃懒洋洋地嘟囔着，声音里透着满满的惬意，同时伸手将他拉向自己。

"Only for my favorite cat," Fujiwara whispered against her lips.

“只对我的小野猫这样，”藤原贴着她的唇呢喃。

The skin-to-skin friction was absolute heaven—every subtle shift of their bodies slid together with effortless, frictionless perfection, turning their embrace into a seamless, gliding dance of warmth and pleasure.
