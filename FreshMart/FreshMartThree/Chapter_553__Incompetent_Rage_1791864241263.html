The afternoon tea in the grand salon was once again seasoned with the absurd spectacles of the empire's commoners. "Listen to the pathetic state of our citizenry in Songxue City," Fujiwara laughed, shaking his head. "A local love hotel was completely trashed last night. Chika burst out laughing, her heavy, milk-swollen chest bouncing wildly beneath her loose silk top as she reached for a pastry. "Wait, he got stood up, so he took his sexual frustration out on a showerhead and kidnapped the mugs?! What kind of loser steals hotel mugs after a failed booty call?! Fujiwara stroked his chin, a spark of tyrannical, over-engineered public policy suddenly lighting up his dark eyes.

藤原摩挲着下巴，漆黑的眼眸中突然闪过一道属于暴君的、过度设计的荒诞公共政策灵光。

However, if the date flakes out and stands the guy up, the abandoned soul can still spend a passionate spring night with the bionic doll and thoroughly relieve himself, rather than engaging in a pathetic bout of incompetent rage against innocent appliances!"

“说起来，作为一名仁慈的君主，朕实在不能容忍酒店的财物因为臣民憋坏了裤裆而惨遭毒手，”藤原带着恶劣的坏笑若有所思地说道，“或许我们应该下达一道皇家法令，要求所有情趣酒店的经营者，在每个房间里都强制标配一台最先进的超逼真仿生性爱机器人。这样一来，如果两个人顺利开房，直接叫仿生人退下进衣柜待命就行。可如果约会对象爽约了，那个被放鸽子的倒霉蛋还能拉着仿生人共度春宵、爽快地发泄一番，而不是对着其他无辜的日常物品来一番无能狂怒！”

Sayuri, resting her newly plump, gloriously soft Tang-dynasty curves against the armrest, raised a delicate eyebrow. The Wise Empress immediately approached the Emperor's perverted whimsy from a purely macroeconomic perspective.

将那丰腴饱满、柔软至极的盛唐曲线慵懒地靠在扶手上的小百合，微微挑起一侧秀眉。这位贤后立刻从纯粹的宏观经济学角度，对皇帝这荒唐的恶趣味奇思妙想进行了可行性评估。
