Chapter 581: The Hatching and the Execution

发布时间：2026/10/16 23:38:37

第五百八十一章：破壳与处决

On the forty-ninth day, the grand imperial incubation complex reached a fever pitch. A sudden, synchronized wave of cosmic energy rippled across the sprawling halls as the divine shells began to fracture.

到了第四十九天，皇家超级大产房内的气氛终于达到了沸腾的顶点。随着一股整齐划一的宇宙能量波纹瞬间扫过广袤的大殿，那些神圣的龙蛋开始接连不断地发出清脆的碎裂声。

Crack! CRACK!

咔嚓！咔嚓——！

Out of the thirty-six thousand glowing spheres that had been entrusted to the sprawling network of imperial incubators, exactly 23,639 dragon-seed heirs successfully punched their way through their shells. They tumbled out onto the velvet nests, roaring, kicking, and glowing with raw, untamed celestial energy—every single one of them as lively and aggressive as a pack of hyper-active cosmic badgers.

在交由庞大后宫网络孵化的三万六千颗神蛋中，最终整整23,639个龙种继承人成功破壳而出。它们活蹦乱跳地从天鹅绒暖巢里滚了出来，伴随着原始狂野的仙界能量，个个生龙活虎，凶猛得就像一群刚出生就自带超能力的小怪兽。

However, the remaining thousands of eggs sat stubbornly dormant, turning cold and gray. The clinical inspection teams soon confirmed the brutal truth: because the lower-tier alien beauties recruited during the galaxy-wide talent search simply didn't meet the strict "absolute aesthetic standard" required by higher-dimensional biology, their thermal resonance had failed. The neglected embryos had withered and died.

然而，剩下的那一万多颗龙蛋却顽固地死寂着，逐渐失去了光泽并彻底冷却成了死胎。医疗检测团队很快查明了残酷的真相：由于在银河系海选时招进来的部分外星妃子“颜值与身材不够绝美”，根本不符合高维生物挑剔的审美标准，导致其生物体温共振频率未达标，从而造成了大规模的胚胎夭折。

Standing in the center of the nursery, Emperor Fujiwara stared at the thousands of dead shells with a dark, thunderous expression. His tyrannical patience snapped instantly.

站在产房中央的藤原大帝看着那一堆死胎，脸色瞬间阴沉得犹如暴风雨前的天空。暴君的耐心在这一刻彻底宣告清零。

"Incompetent fools!" Fujiwara roared, his voice shaking the obsidian foundations of the palace. "I gave them one job! One simple job: look gorgeous and keep a warm bottom! If they aren't pretty enough to hatch a divine dynasty, what are they even good for?!"

“这帮废物点心！”藤原爆发出震动皇宫地基的狂怒咆哮，“朕给她们的任务就只有一个：长得绝美，屁股坐暖！如果连孵个蛋这点姿色都达不到，留着这群废物有什么用？！”

Slash!

噗嗤！

Within the hour, the Imperial Selection Official—the bureaucrat responsible for screening the galaxy-wide beauty pageant—was unceremoniously beheaded in the courtyard. Not stopping there, Fujiwara ordered the imperial guard to round up every single incubation consort whose aesthetic score fell below the Emperor's brutal standards. Before sunset, the remaining sub-par incubator women were marched to the execution grounds and instantly beheaded, turning the palace courtyards into a very bloody, very strict lesson in beauty standards.

不到一个小时内，负责全银河系选美筛选的首席选美官就被当场拖到庭院里砍了脑袋。杀红了眼的藤原不仅没有停手，反而下令御林军将所有颜值评分未达标的孵蛋妃子全部押入大牢。太阳落山前，这批“不够美”的孵蛋人被尽数斩首，用一场血淋淋的人头落地向全帝国宣告了什么叫绝对严苛的审美专制。

Despite the bloody purge, the successful hatchlings were undeniably magnificent. And among the 23,639 newborns, the children directly incubated by the four core beauties immediately stood out as anomalies of epic proportions.

尽管经历了一场血腥清洗，但那两万多名成功破壳的龙种依然让所有人大开眼界。而在这些新生儿中，由四位核心爱妃亲自孵化的子嗣更是显现出了极其诡异、超越常规的恐怖特质。

In the grand salon, the imperial family gathered to inspect the elite litter.

在大起居殿内，皇室全家正围坐在一起，仔细打量着这窝精锐之师。

Sayuri’s child, born from the direct biological warmth of the AV Religion’s living pontiff, possessed an aura of eerie, mesmerizing enlightenment. The infant floated three inches above the floor, constantly whispering abstract macroeconomic theories and broadcasting subliminal religious charisma that made nearby guards instantly want to drop to their knees and tithe their entire life savings.

小百合亲手孵化的孩子，由于沾染了AV女神教活教主的圣洁与宏观气息，天生自带一种极其诡异迷幻的宗教感悟光环。这小家伙一出生就悬浮在离地三英寸的半空中，嘴里喃喃念叨着高深的宏观经济宏图，甚至散发出一种能让周围御林军当场下跪上交全部资产的精神洗脑磁场。

Chika’s child, nurtured by the empire's ultimate dairy provider, was already the size of a robust six-year-old. The hyper-nourished infant possessed two glowing, milk-white horns and immediately latched onto a floating synthetic dairy tank, chugging high-grade nutrients with terrifying, world-conquering enthusiasm.

而由人间之母千夏精心培育的孩子则是个不折不扣的“重量级巨婴”。刚破壳就已经长得像个六岁壮汉，头顶生着两只奶白色的神圣龙角，一出生就极其精准地扑向一台悬浮的合成奶水箱，以吞噬星河般的狂热疯狂干饭。

However, it was the hatchlings from Queen Rin and Consort Miyetsuko that truly pushed the laws of physics into a total meltdown.

然而，最让所有人在物理层面上大跌眼镜的，却是小铃和美悦子孵化出来的龙种。

Chika stared at Rin’s glowing, pitch-black little dragon-spawn—which was currently summoning miniature black holes and giggling as it swallowed decorative vases whole—with a bewildered expression. She turned to Miyetsuko, scratching her head.

千夏看着小铃孵出来的那只通体漆黑、正一边嘎吱嘎吱吞掉皇家花瓶一边开心地召唤迷你黑洞的小龙崽，满脸费解地挠了挠头，转头看向美悦子。

"Wait a minute," Chika questioned, pointing a finger at the chaotic demon-dragon. "I get why Sayuri's kid is a cult leader and mine is a milk-crazed glutton. But Rin is the Queen of the Succubus Realm! She’s a pure-blood demon, not a human! How the hell did she manage to hatch a divine dragon-seed in the first place?"

“等一下，”千夏指着那只到处吐黑洞的小怪物疑惑道，“小百合的孩子当教主、我的孩子当饭桶，这逻辑我懂。可小铃分门别类是个魅魔女王啊！她明明是纯血恶魔，又不是凡间女子，怎么凭她也能把天帝的龙种给孵出来？”

Miyetsuko gracefully adjusted her Tang-dynasty bodice, offering a scholarly, highly sophisticated smile as she cradled her own exceptionally radiant, perfectly pure dragon-infant in her arms.

美悦子优雅地整理了一下真丝胸衣，抱着自己怀里那只散发着纯净金光、完美无瑕的顶级龙婴，露出一抹博学而神秘的微笑。

"It is quite simple if you understand higher-dimensional anthropology, Chika-nee," Miyetsuko explained smoothly. "The Succubus Realm and the Heavenly Palace are not entirely separate entities; they possess deep, ancient, and highly tangled genealogical connections. Only a supreme sovereign like Queen Rin—whose dark bloodline holds the necessary celestial-demonic resonance—could successfully trick the dragon eggs into thinking they were resting in a divine nursery."

“如果懂得高维人类学的话，其实这很好理解，千夏姐姐，”美悦子耐心地解释道，“魅魔界与天庭并不是完全割裂的两个宇宙维度，它们之间有着极其深厚、古老且剪不断理还乱的血统渊源。只有像小铃女王这样地位尊崇、其黑暗血脉中恰好具备‘神魔双向共振’的至尊，才能瞒过龙蛋的生物基因锁，成功把它们孵出来。”

Miyetsuko looked down at her own precious infant, her eyes softening with absolute, smug maternal pride.

美悦子低下头，看着自己怀里那个散发着无上神威的极品龙婴，眼神中流露出满满的骄傲与自豪。

"As for my child..." Miyetsuko continued, gently patting the radiant, pure-blooded celestial baby. "Since I am the primary biological mother who carried the pregnancy for three full months and underwent the original incubation process in the heavenly bedchamber, my hatched dragon-seed is infinitely more pure, concentrated, and powerful than all the rest. It carries the unadulterated essence of both the Heavenly Emperor and the ultimate earthly diplomat!"

“至于我孵化的这个孩子嘛……”美悦子挺起胸膛，语气中透着绝对的权威，“由于我是怀胎足三个月、并在天宫龙床上亲自完成了最初孕育的正牌生母，我亲手孵出来的这个龙种，其血统纯度自然要比其他任何同类都要精纯无数倍。它完美继承了天帝的神格与本宫的顶级外交基因，可以说是天生自带主角光环的宇宙级二代！”

In the center of the room, Emperor Fujiwara crossed his arms, looking down at the magnificent, terrifying, and thoroughly chaotic army of newborns flooding the palace halls. The tyrant let out a long, deeply satisfied chuckle.

大殿中央，藤原大帝双手抱胸，俯视着这支塞满皇宫大殿、既壮观又无比混乱的宇宙级龙种幼崽大军。暴君满意地咧开嘴，发出一声畅快淋漓的低笑。

"From thirty-six thousand eggs to twenty-three thousand demigods, complete with divine-demonic hybrids and pure celestial aristocrats..." Fujiwara grinned, his dark eyes flashing with absolute dominion. "The Fujiwara Interstellar Demigod Academy just got its first freshman class. Let's see which galaxy dares to tax us now!"

“从三万六千颗蛋到两万三千个半神，甚至还自带神魔双修的混血精英和纯血贵族……”藤原冷笑着，漆黑的双眼里闪烁着对全宇宙的绝对统治欲，“朕的‘藤原星际半神军事学院’总算迎来了第一届新生。接下来，倒要看看全银河系究竟还有哪个不知死活的星系敢找咱们收税！”
