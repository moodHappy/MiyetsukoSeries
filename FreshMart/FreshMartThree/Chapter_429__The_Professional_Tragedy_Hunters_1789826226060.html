It didn't take long for human greed to adapt to the Fresh Mart syndicate's absurdly generous environmental decrees. Within the following weeks, as news of Greg O'Neill's multi-million-dollar shark settlement spread like wildfire across global tabloids, a cynical new trend began to fester. A series of "unfortunate" new swimming accidents popped up along various coastlines, with desperate individuals willingly offering up their worthless lives to the ocean's apex predators, hoping to cash in on the trillionaire's soft spot and secure generational wealth for the good-for-nothing partners or relatives they left behind.

人类的贪婪适应“新鲜玛特”辛迪加那荒谬而慷慨的环保政令，并没有花太长时间。在接下来的几周里，随着格雷格·奥尼尔那笔数百万美元鲨鱼抚恤金的消息在世界各大八卦小报上疯传，一股愤世嫉俗的全新风潮开始滋生。各大海岸线上接连发生了多起“不幸”的游泳事故，一些走投无路的人甘愿将自己不值钱的性命主动奉献给海洋的顶级掠食者，试图借此投合这位万亿首富的软肋，为他们留下的那些游手好闲的同伴或亲戚换取世代享用的巨额财富。

Back in the cozy, chaotic penthouse of Fresh Mart, Chika slammed a fresh stack of actuarial reports onto the dining table with an exasperated sigh, interrupting the cozy family dinner.

  • back in + 地点 = 回到……里面/在……之中

  • a fresh stack of sth. = 一摞新的……

在“新鲜玛特”温馨而混乱的顶层公寓里，千夏没好气地将厚厚一叠精算报告重重拍在餐桌上，打断了温馨的家庭晚宴。

"Well, folks, our philanthropic little shark-rescue policy is officially being exploited as a get-rich-quick scheme," Chika groaned, spinning around in her ergonomic chair. "In just the last two weeks, amateur swimmers have been leaping into shark-infested waters left and right, deliberately getting themselves mauled just to leave their deadbeat boyfriends and lazy relatives millions of dollars in compensation. They're literally monetizing their own funerals!"

“各位，咱们那项充满仁慈的救鲨环保政策，现在正式变成一条发家致富的快车道了，”千夏在人体工学椅上转了个圈，夸张地抱怨道，“就在过去两周里，业余游泳爱好者们接二连三地往鲨鱼出没的水域里跳，故意让自己被活活咬死，就是为了给他们那些吃软饭的男友和懒惰亲戚留下几百万的赔偿金。这群家伙简直是在把自己的葬礼当成上市项目来做啊！”

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, leaning casually against her iron baseball bat as a cold, dangerous smirk crossed her lips.

美悦子推了推眼镜，漫不经心地靠在铁棒球棍上，嘴角泛起一抹冷酷而危险的坏笑。

"Stupidity truly knows no bounds," Miyetsuko deadpanned. "If these lowlife parasites think they can turn our corporate philanthropy into a lottery ticket for their lazy scum partners, they've got another thing coming. Want me to deploy the coastal drone sweep and drag these suicidal idiots back to shore by their ankles, Fujiwara?"

“人类的愚蠢果然是没有下限的，”美悦子冷冷地评价道，“这些社会底层的寄生虫要是以为能把我们的公司慈善当成他们懒惰同伴的彩票，那就大错特错了。藤原，要不要我派海岸无人机去把这群想自杀的白痴全按住脚踝拖回岸上？”

Fujiwara let out a booming, unbothered laugh, pulling Sayuri closer into his lap as he poured himself another glass of vintage wine.

藤原爆发出一阵底气十足、毫不在乎的狂笑，顺手将小百合拉到自己腿上坐好，给自己重新倒了一杯珍藏红酒。

"Let them jump, my fierce boss lady!" Fujiwara grinned wickedly. "If people are willing to feed themselves to sharks just to pay off their debts, who are we to stand in the way of natural selection? Though, speaking of dedicating lives and making grand sacrifices..." He lowered his voice, casting a smoldering, highly suggestive glance down at Sayuri. "My sweet first wife here is the only one who deserves to sacrifice her body entirely for my pleasure."

“让这群家伙跳去吧，凶猛的老板娘！”藤原坏笑着咧嘴道，“如果有人愿意为了还债把自己喂给鲨鱼，咱们又何必去阻碍自然选择呢？不过，说到奉献躯体和做出伟大牺牲……”他压低了声音，目光火辣而充满暗示地落在了小百合身上，“我这最亲爱的大房正室，才是唯一一个有资格把全身心彻底奉献给老子快感的人。”

Sayuri’s cheeks instantly burned with a gorgeous, crimson flush. She bit her lower lip, squirming deliciously against his lap as a soft, breathless whimper escaped her lips.

小百合的脸颊瞬间烧起一抹娇艳欲滴的绯红。她轻咬着下唇，在他腿上羞涩地扭了扭身子，口中溢出一声软糯诱人的娇喘。

"Oh, Fujiwara-san... you're always so impossibly lewd," Sayuri murmured, her voice dripping with practiced, velvety seduction. "Even when we're discussing international death tolls, your mind is only focused on bullying this humble little concubine... But since you mentioned it, my soft mouth and heavy hips are completely at your mercy tonight..."

“哎呀，藤原桑……你总是这么坏、这么下流，”小百合轻声呢喃着，嗓音里透着浸润了风情的天鹅绒般甜腻，“哪怕咱们正讨论着国际伤亡率，你的脑子里也满是要欺负人家这只可怜小妾……不过既然你都这么说了，人家今天晚上这副温热的小嘴和饱满的臀儿，可就全凭主人处置啦……”

Unable to resist her breathless enticement, Fujiwara instantly abandoned the actuarial reports. He grabbed her by the waist, swept her up from the chair, and carried her straight toward the master bedroom, stripping away her clothes with ravenous hunger as the sound of her sweet, wanton moans echoed through the penthouse.

哪里经得起她这般呼吸急促的勾引，藤原当即把那堆精算报告抛到了九霄云外。他一把攥住她的纤腰将她从椅子上横抱起来，大步流星地朝着主卧室走去，伴随着眼底闪烁的狼性将她的衣衫一件件撕碎，阵阵甜美而放荡的娇喘瞬间回荡在顶层公寓里。

Blind Rin sat quietly in the corner, her wide, unseeing eyes blinking innocently as she neatly folded a fresh stack of towels, offering a gentle, serene smile to the empty chair.

盲人铃安安静静地坐在角落里，她那张睁得圆溜溜却空洞无神的眼睛无辜地眨了眨，仔细地将一叠新毛巾叠好，对着那张空荡荡的椅子露出了一个温柔而宁静的微笑。

"Um... hello?" Rin whispered softly. "Are we... are we still talking about people jumping into the water? If everyone is feeling too hot and bothered today, maybe I should knit some cool ice-silk pajamas... Please don't be too wild in the bedroom, you two. Dinner is almost ready."

“唔……你好呀？”铃轻声呢喃着，“大家还在……在讨论有人跳进水里的事吗？如果大家今天都觉得心里热乎乎的，我是不是该去织几件凉爽的冰丝睡衣呢……你们两个在卧室里可别太疯啦，晚饭马上就好哦。”
