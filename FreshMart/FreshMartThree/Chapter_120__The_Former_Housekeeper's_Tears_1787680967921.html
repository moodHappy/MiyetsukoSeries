发布时间：2026/8/26 02:02:46

第一百二十章：旧日管家的泪水

A few days after Fujiwara promised to hire help for the heavy outdoor chores, the new housekeeper arrived at "Fresh Mart."

在藤原承诺要为她分担户外粗活几天后，新来的老妈子终于抵达了“新鲜玛特”。

When the elderly woman stepped through the back entrance and locked eyes with Chika, who was standing there in her mandatory open-crotch maid uniform with her intimate parts fully exposed, the old woman froze in absolute, crushing shock.

当这位上了年纪的老妇人迈步走进后门，一眼看清正站在眼前的千夏时——那套随时随地袒露私处、毫无遮拦的开裆女仆装让老妇人当场石化，震惊得说不出话来。

"Chika... Miss Chika?!" the housekeeper gasped, her weathered hands trembling as she dropped her bags to the floor.

“千夏……大小姐？！老妇人颤抖着惊呼出声，饱经风霜的双手一软，随身带的行李包“啪嗒”一声跌落在地。

It was Auntie Kayo, the former head housekeeper who had served in Chika's family estate since she was a child. Seeing the young mistress of a proud, noble bloodline reduced to a domestic chattel, wearing clothes designed solely for instant sexual penetration, broke the old woman's heart. Tears instantly flooded her eyes as she rushed forward, desperately trying to pull her apron down to cover Chika's exposed body.

眼前这位正是从小看着千夏长大的前朝旧邸总管——加代婶。看到曾经高贵的大小姐如今沦为任人玩弄的家奴，甚至连衣着都成了专门为了方便别人随时插入的开裆货，加代婶只觉得心如刀割。她老泪纵横地冲上前去，慌乱地想扯下围裙遮挡住千夏赤裸的下半身。

"Oh, my poor, precious young mistress..." Auntie Kayo sobbed, stroking Chika's hair with shaking fingers. "How did you fall to this? Reduced to a slave, forced to wear such a shameful thing, ready to spread your legs for anyone at a second's notice... It breaks my old heart into pieces!"

“哎哟，我可怜的大小姐啊……”加代婶老泪纵横地抚摸着千夏的头发，声音哽咽颤抖，“你怎么会落到这种地步？堂堂名门千金，竟然沦为奴隶，还被迫穿着这种不知羞耻的衣服，随时随地撅着屁股伺候别人……这让老奴看着怎么能不心碎啊！”

Chika didn't flinch. Instead, she gently took the weeping woman's rough hands, offering a calm, bittersweet smile to comfort her.

千夏却并没有慌乱躲闪。她反手轻轻握住老人家粗糙颤抖的手，脸上浮现出一个释然中带着苦涩的温柔微笑，反过来安慰起对方。

"Don't cry for me, Auntie Kayo," Chika whispered softly, wiping a tear from the old woman's cheek. "There's really no need to feel sad. Besides... have you forgotten? Back in our old mansion, my own brother used to treat our family's young maids in the exact same rough, casual way behind closed doors. Power and fate change quickly, and I accept where I am."

“别为我难过，加代婶，”千夏轻声软语地安慰着，伸手帮老人家拭去脸颊上的泪水，“真的没必要为我伤心。再说了……您忘了吗？以前在咱们宅子里，我那亲哥哥背地里不也经常这样随手拉过家里的年轻丫鬟胡乱发泄吗？世事无常，既然落魄了，这就是命，我已经接受了。”

Auntie Kayo stared at her in stunned silence, wiping her eyes as Chika continued with a genuine, glowing warmth in her voice.

加代婶怔怔地看着她，一时语塞。而千夏则继续开口，语气里透出一丝动人的真诚与暖意：

"And honestly, you don't need to worry about my treatment here," Chika added, glancing back toward the living room where Miyetsuko, Rin, and Sayuri were resting. "None of them treat me like a low servant. They share their food, teach me, and care for me like true sisters. Compared to the cold, rigid loneliness of my past, being here... with them, and even serving Fujiwara... is actually much warmer than I ever expected."

“而且说实话，您真不用担心我在这里的待遇，”千夏回过头，目光望向起居室里正休息的美悦子、铃和小百合，“她们三位从来没有把我当过下人看。不仅把好吃的分享给我，教我东西，还像真正的亲姐妹一样照顾我。比起过去那种高贵却冰冷的孤独，现在在这里……和大家在一起，甚至是被藤原少爷随意插弄的日子，其实比我想象的要温暖得多。”

Hearing those accepting, heartfelt words, Auntie Kayo finally stopped crying. She realized that though her young mistress's body now belonged to the masters of "Fresh Mart," her heart had found a strange, unshakable shelter among the chaos.

听到这番发自肺腑的坦然之言，加代婶终于渐渐止住了泪水。她看着千夏清澈的眼眸，心中明白——虽然大小姐的身体已经完全属于了“新鲜玛特”的主人，但在这一片混乱与极乐交织的屋檐下，她的心，终于找到了一个能真正容纳她的避风港。
