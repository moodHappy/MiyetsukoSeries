Chapter 562: The Ascension of the Succubus Queen

第五百六十二章：魅魔女王的登基

The lazy afternoon peace of the grand salon was abruptly interrupted when a portal smelling heavily of brimstone, sulfur, and shockingly expensive aphrodisiac perfume ripped open above the Persian rug. A glowing, floating scroll dropped directly onto the mahogany coffee table. Blind Rin, swinging her legs on her fluffy cushion, immediately perked up, her milky eyes wide with sweet, terrifying excitement.

大起居殿那慵懒的午后宁静被突然打破了。一个散发着浓烈硫磺味、地狱烈火气息以及极其昂贵的催情香水味的传送门，在波斯地毯上方被硬生生撕开。一卷发着暗红色光芒的悬浮卷轴直接掉在了红木茶几上。坐在毛绒软垫上晃荡着双腿的盲人小铃立刻精神了起来，空灵的盲眼里闪烁着甜美而极其恐怖的兴奋光芒。

"Um... hello, everyone? Rin has super good news!" the little succubus chirped happily, catching the burning scroll without even looking. "Old Wang, the current King of the Demon Realm, just threw his back out! He says the paperwork in the torture department is too much for his lumbar disc, so he's officially retiring! And guess what? He's summoning Rin back to the underworld to ascend the throne! Rin is going to be the new Succubus Queen!"

“唔……大家听我说好吗？小铃有一个超级天大的好消息呀！”小魅魔甜丝丝地欢呼着，看都不看就一把接住了那卷燃烧着的卷轴。“现任魔界的老王刚刚把腰给闪啦！他说刑讯部的公文实在太多了，他的腰椎间盘根本承受不住，所以他正式宣布退休啦！你们猜怎么着？他现在正召唤小铃回地狱去继承大统呢！小铃马上就要成为新一任的魅魔女王啦！”

The entire harem stared at the blind, perpetually cheerful little demon. The idea of this sweet, cookie-munching menace ruling over an entire dimension of dark magic and eternal suffering was profoundly absurd.

整个后宫都盯着这个瞎了眼、永远都在傻乐的小恶魔。一想到这个整天抱着饼干啃、甜美又危险的小灾星即将统治一整座充满黑魔法与永恒折磨的异次元维度，所有人都感到一种深深的荒谬。

Fujiwara, however, shot up from the velvet sofa, his dark eyes instantly burning with absolute, unhinged tyrannical lust. The Emperor’s geopolitical priorities shifted in a nanosecond.

然而，藤原却猛地从天鹅绒沙发上弹了起来，漆黑的眼眸瞬间燃起了绝对的、毫无底线的暴君狂热。这位皇帝的地缘政治优先级在零点零一秒内完成了彻底的重组。

"A whole realm populated entirely by professional succubi?!" Fujiwara roared, his voice trembling with the sheer magnitude of the debauchery. "Pack your bags immediately! The Emperor is going on an official state visit to his in-laws! We are accompanying our little Queen back to her maiden home!"

“一整个维度，里面全他妈是专业的魅魔？！”藤原狂吼一声，声音都因为这即将到来的宏大淫靡而微微发抖，“立刻给老子收拾行李！皇帝陛下现在就要对亲家的领地进行一次正式的国事访问！咱们全家都要陪着咱们的小女王回娘家！”

Sayuri, looking gloriously plump and impossibly regal in her Tang-dynasty silks, elegantly reached for her fan. The Wise Empress’s mind was already calculating the cross-dimensional tax potential.

穿着盛唐真丝长袍、看上去丰腴极品且不可思议般雍容华贵的小百合，优雅地拿起了折扇。这位贤后的脑子里，已经开始飞速盘算起跨维度的税收潜力了。

"A truly magnificent diplomatic opportunity, my Lord," Sayuri purred smoothly, a velvet, thoroughly corrupt smile playing on her pristine lips. "A realm where pure lust is the primary currency and the local population specializes in draining souls through sexual intercourse... If we can monopolize the import-export trade of the succubus nightlife sector, the imperial treasury will overflow within a week. I shall bring my accounting ledgers."

“当真是一次宏伟的绝佳外交机遇呢，夫君，”小百合软糯地娇笑着，那完美无瑕的红唇边挂着一抹丝绒般彻底腐败的微笑，“一个以纯粹的情欲为主要货币、且当地居民全都精通通过性交来吸干灵魂的国度……如果我们能彻底垄断魅魔夜生活产业的进出口贸易，帝国的国库不出一个星期就会被彻底撑爆。我得把我的财务账本带上。”

Chika scrambled off the sofa, wildly stuffing bags of spicy chips into a duffel bag, her heavy, milk-swollen chest bouncing furiously with panic.

千夏连滚带爬地从沙发上翻下来，疯狂地往旅行袋里塞着一包包的麻辣薯片，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔随着她的恐慌剧烈乱颤着。

"Do they have cows in Hell?!" Chika shrieked, completely missing the geopolitical implications. "Because if they expect me, a mere mortal, to supply milk for an entire dimension of thirsty demons, my nipples are going to physically fall off! Boss, you have to promise you won't sell my dairy contracts to the underworld mob!"

“地狱里有奶牛吗？！”千夏崩溃地尖叫着，完全没抓住地缘政治的重点，“因为如果他们指望我区区一个凡人，去给一整座维度、数以亿计口渴的恶魔全天候供应奶水，我的乳头绝对会在物理层面上当场脱落的！老板，你必须向我保证，绝不会把我的产奶合同卖给地狱的黑帮！”

Miyetsuko simply knelt perfectly by the swirling portal, bowing her head in flawless, aristocratic submission. "It is a grand occasion," the fallen princess murmured sweetly. "I have always wondered how the demonic aristocracy organizes its maidservants. I shall dutifully observe their torture protocols and bring the best practices back to Master's bedroom."

美悦子则极其完美地跪在那个旋转的传送门旁，低下头，展现出无可挑剔的贵族式顺从。“这真是一场盛大的庆典，”没落公主甜甜地呢喃着，“奴婢一直很好奇，恶魔贵族们是如何调教通房丫头的。奴婢一定会尽职尽责地观察她们的刑讯流程，并把最优秀的经验原封不动地带回主人的卧室里。”

Hours later, the imperial family stepped through the portal, instantly arriving in the obsidian throne room of the Succubus Realm. It was a terrifyingly magnificent cavern lit by hellfire, overflowing with millions of breathtakingly beautiful, scantily clad demons. When little Rin skipped happily up the bone staircase and plopped her small, bouncy body onto the colossal Skull Throne, the entire dimension dropped to its knees in absolute, fearful submission.

几个小时后，皇室一家浩浩荡荡地穿过了传送门，瞬间抵达了魅魔界那黑曜石打造的宏伟王座大厅。那是一个由地狱烈火照亮的、令人毛骨悚然却又壮丽无比的巨大洞穴，里面挤满了数以百万计美得惊心动魄、衣不蔽体的妖娆恶魔。当小铃一蹦一跳地开心地走上白骨阶梯，将她那娇小、充满弹性的身躯“吧唧”一声摔进巨大的骷髅王座时，整个维度都瞬间跪倒在地，展现出绝对的、充满恐惧的臣服。

"All hail Queen Rin! The Blind Terror of the Twin-Tails!" the demon horde chanted.

“恭迎小铃女王！双马尾的盲眼梦魇！”恶魔大军齐声高呼。

Fujiwara stood at the base of the throne, his tyrannical eyes sweeping over the endless, writhing sea of perfect demonic curves, wings, and tails. He licked his lips, completely ignoring the terrifying dark magic suffocating the air.

藤原站在王座下方，他那充满暴君气息的双眼扫过眼前这片无边无际、扭动着的完美恶魔曲线、肉翼与尾巴的海洋。他舔了舔嘴唇，完全无视了空气中那令人窒息的恐怖黑魔法。

"Sayuri, Chika, Miyetsuko... lock the dimensional gates," Fujiwara grinned wickedly, unbuckling his imperial belt with a loud, dominant snap. "Since my little demon has officially ascended as the Queen, it is my absolute sovereign duty to officially declare myself the 'Demon King Consort'! And as the new Consort, I am commandeering this entire realm! The Emperor needs to conduct a comprehensive, highly intensive physical inspection of the entire succubus workforce! Someone fetch me a silken whip, we are going to be here a very, very long time!"

“小百合、千夏、美悦子……把维度大门给老子焊死，”藤原恶劣地坏笑着，伴随着一声响亮霸道的脆响，解开了他的皇家腰带。“既然朕的小恶魔已经正式登基成了女王，那么朕就有绝对的主权责任，正式宣布自己成为‘魅魔王夫’！而作为新任王夫，朕现在宣布彻底征收这整片领土！皇帝陛下现在需要对全魔界的魅魔劳动力进行一次全面、高强度的体能大检查！来个人给朕拿条丝绸鞭子来，咱们要在这里待上很长、很长一段时间了！”
