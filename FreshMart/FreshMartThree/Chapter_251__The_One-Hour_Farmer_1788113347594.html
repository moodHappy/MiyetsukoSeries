Chapter 251: The One-Hour Farmer

发布时间：2026/8/31 02:09:06

第二百五十一章：一小时农夫体验卡

The next morning, the bright sun rose over the muddy fields, casting a cruel, unforgiving light on "Fresh Mart's" newly expanded agricultural workforce.

次日清晨，火辣辣的太阳高悬在泥泞的农田上空，无情地炙烤着刚刚扩编的“新鲜玛特”农耕小分队。

Miyetsuko stood at the edge of the paddy field, wiping sweat from her forehead with the back of a muddy hand. A malicious, deeply satisfied smirk curved across her exhausted face as she watched Fujiwara, Sayuri, and Rin struggling with their heavy hoes.

美悦子站在田埂边，用满是泥巴的手背擦了擦额头上的汗水。看着藤原、小百合和铃正笨手笨脚地挥舞着锄头叫苦连天，她那疲惫的脸上顿时露出了大仇得报、幸灾乐祸的阴险笑容。

"Well, well, well," Miyetsuko chuckled darkly, leaning heavily on her hoe. "Yesterday you were living like emperors in a love hotel. Today, welcome to the proletariat revolution. Suffer with me, capitalists!"

“哼哼，昨天在外面左拥右抱不是很潇洒吗？”美悦子冷笑着叉起腰，心情舒畅无比，“风水轮流转！今天就让你们也尝尝无产阶级社会主义大改造的铁拳，谁也别想跑！”

"Ugh! This mud is attacking my shoes!" Rin whined, trying to yank her sneaker out of the sticky soil.

“靠！这破泥潭有毒吧，靴子都快陷进去了！”铃一边狼狈地拔着脚底板，一边气急败坏地抱怨。

"Ahem! As the store manager, I demand executive exemption from manual labor!" Fujiwara wheezed, his back already aching after a mere ten minutes of digging.

“咳咳！本店长抗议！作为领导阶级，我要求享受体力劳动豁免权！”藤原才刚挥了两下锄头就累得直喘粗气。

However, their misery was cut remarkably short.

然而，老天爷似乎并不打算让他们受太多的苦。

Barely an hour into their back-breaking labor, a loud siren blared across the countryside, followed by an official announcement booming from the village loudspeaker:

就在他们热火朝天地干了一个小时左右，村头的大喇叭突然刺耳地响了起来，随后传出了镇干部慌乱的广播声：

"Attention all villagers! Attention all villagers! Due to a high-level whistleblower report filed with the municipal authorities regarding illegal forced labor and human resource conscription, the town government has been forced to cancel the agricultural mobilization immediately! All residents are instructed to drop their tools and return home voluntarily. Repeat, conscription is cancelled!"

“全体村民请注意！全体村民请注意！因接到上级紧急通报，有人实名举报我镇涉嫌违法强征劳动力、侵犯公民合法权益，现上级已下令立刻叫停！所有被征召人员立刻停止农活，就地解散回家！重复一遍，农田统筹征召立刻取消！”

The fields went dead silent.

田野里瞬间安静了下来。

Fujiwara, Sayuri, and Rin froze, exchanged one disbelieving glance, and then burst into wild, euphoric cheers.

藤原、小百合和铃动作一僵，面面相觑地对视了两秒钟，随后爆发出了一阵劫后余生般的狂喜欢呼。

"Freedom!" Fujiwara yelled, dropping his hoe into the mud and throwing his arms in the air. "The government saved us! Let's go home, girls!"

“解放啦！”藤原把锄头往泥地里一扔，兴奋得差点原地起飞，“万岁！正义的举报人万岁！我们回家喽！”

Sayuri and Rin giggled, happily tossing aside their tools and wiping their hands.

小百合和铃也笑得合不拢嘴，开开心心地拍拍手准备收工。

Only one person in the entire field was not celebrating.

然而，田埂上的另一个人却当场石化了。

Miyetsuko stood frozen like a statue, her jaw practically unhinged as she stared at the village loudspeaker in absolute, unadulterated disbelief. Her eye twitched violently.

美悦子呆呆地僵在原地，下巴差点砸到脚面上，死死盯着大喇叭，整个人陷入了彻底的凌乱与抓狂。她的眼角疯狂抽搐：

"Wait... WHAT?!" Miyetsuko shrieked, her voice cracking in pure outrage as she pointed an accusing finger at the cheering trio. "That's not fair! Are you kidding me?! I slaved away, covered in mud and sweat, acting as a miserable farm slave for three whole days... and the exact second these three freeloaders finally show up, the government cancels it after one single hour?!"

“等等！凭什么？！”美悦子气急败坏地尖叫出声，指着那三个准备开溜的家伙，心态彻底崩了，“这不公平！凭什么我顶着大太阳当了三天三夜的苦力牛马，他们三个倒好，合着就来田里体验了一小时的乡村农家乐，政策就直接解散了？！我不服！这绝对有黑幕！”

"Hahaha! That's just the difference in political karma, Director!" Fujiwara cackled mercilessly, wrapping an arm around a laughing Sayuri as they skipped away down the path. "Better luck next time!"

“哈哈哈哈……这就叫福报懂不懂，美悦子厅长！”藤原幸灾乐祸地大笑起来，一把揽过笑得直不起腰的小百合，大摇大摆地往家走去，“革命尚未成功，同志仍需努力啊！”

Left all alone in the middle of the muddy field with her hoe, Miyetsuko let out a long, blood-curdling scream of pure, academic defeat.

留下一脸绝望的美悦子独自站在泥地里，伴随着夕阳，发出了绝望而凄厉的呐喊。
