Chapter 101: The Dermatological Catastrophe

发布时间：2026/8/25 22:43:12

第一百一章：皮肤医学的“灾难”

The peaceful morning routine of "Fresh Mart" was abruptly shattered by a sound that could only be described as a shriek of pure, unadulterated scientific horror coming from the upstairs bathroom.

“新鲜玛特”平静的清晨日常，被一声从楼上浴室里传出来的、堪称“纯粹科学恐惧”的尖叫声骤然打破。

Rin nearly dropped her coffee mug, while Sayuri looked up from her chores with a startled gasp. Before anyone could move, Miyetsuko burst out of the hallway. Her usually immaculate, composed face was pale, her eyes wide behind her glasses, and her finger was trembling as she pointed accusingly at the mirror.

铃差一点把手里的咖啡杯砸了出去，小百合也吓了一跳，停下手里的活惊疑不定地抬头张望。还没等大家回过神来，美悦子便脸色煞白地从走廊里冲了出来。她那张平日里无可挑剔、冷静沉着的面孔此刻毫无血色，镜片后的双眸圆睁，手指颤抖着指着自己的脸颊。

"A systemic structural failure," Miyetsuko gasped, her voice trembling with unprecedented panic. "An anomalous, localized inflammation has erupted directly on my left zygomatic arch. A dermal blemish! A textbook breakout of acne vulgaris!"

“系统性结构发生崩塌，”美悦子倒吸了一口凉气，声音里带着前所未有的惊恐，“左侧颧弓处竟然突发了一起局部异常炎症！一个皮肤瑕疵！这简直是寻常痤疮的教科书级爆发！”

Rin walked over, peered closely at her cheek, and let out a dry, amused snort. "Relax, doc. It's just a tiny pimple. You look like the world's financial market just crashed."

铃走上前去仔细端详了一下她的脸颊，随后发出一声干巴巴的轻笑：“冷静点，博士。不就是一个小痘痘吗？不知道的还以为全球金融市场崩盘了呢。”

"You don't understand!" Miyetsuko protested, clutching her head in absolute despair. "My facial symmetry has been compromised! The aesthetic equilibrium of my biological data points is utterly ruined! How can I maintain a rational, authoritative posture when my epidermal integrity is under siege?"

“你根本不懂！”美悦子绝望地抱住脑袋抗议道，“我的面部对称性遭到破坏了！我生物特征数据的审美平衡被彻底毁了！当我的表皮完整性正遭受围攻时，我怎么还能保持理性而权威的姿态？”

Seeing her genuinely distressed, Fujiwara couldn't help but chuckle. He walked over, gently wrapping his arms around her tense shoulders.

看着她这副大难临头的样子，藤原忍不住轻笑出声，走上前去温柔地揽住她紧绷的肩膀。

"Alright, alright, don't panic, my brilliant doctor," Fujiwara soothed, kissing the top of her head. "If it's a medical emergency, we treat it like one. Come on, I'll take you to the clinic right now."

“好啦好啦，别慌，我的大博士，”藤原轻笑着在她发顶亲了一下，“既然是医疗紧急状况，那咱们就按标准程序处理。走吧，我现在就带你去诊所。”

Ten minutes later, Fujiwara had parked his scooter outside Donglai Island’s local community health clinic. Inside the sterile examination room, an elderly village doctor adjusted his reading glasses, peering intently through a magnifying glass at the tiny red bump on Miyetsuko’s cheek.

十分钟后，藤原的电动车已经停在了东莱岛社区卫生所门外。在消毒水气味的诊疗室里，老村医推了推老花镜，拿着放大镜对着美悦子脸颊上那个微红的小疙瘩仔细端详了半天。

Miyetsuko sat rigidly upright on the examination chair, holding her breath as if awaiting a terminal diagnosis, her hands gripping her knees tightly.

美悦子像个等待终审判决的犯人一样笔直地坐在诊疗椅上，双手死死攥着膝盖，连大气都不敢出一口。

The doctor finally lowered his magnifying glass, let out a casual chuckle, and shook his head. "Young lady, your pulse is fine, your blood pressure is normal, and your internal organs are working just fine. It's just a standard, garden-variety pimple. Probably ate a bit too much fried food or stayed up late reading research papers. Apply a little ointment, get eight hours of sleep, and it'll be gone in two days."

医生终于放下放大镜，随和地轻笑了一声，摇了摇头：“小姑娘，你这脉搏挺好，血压正常，内脏机能也没问题。这就是个普通得不能再普通的小青春痘。估计是最近油炸食品吃多了，或者熬夜搞研究累的。抹点消炎膏，睡足八个小时，过两天自己就消下去了。”

Miyetsuko froze. The high-tension panic in her posture slowly melted into a profound, deeply awkward silence.

美悦子瞬间石化。她身上那股草木皆兵的高压恐慌，在一秒钟内融化成了一片尴尬而寂静的沉默。

"A... a routine follicular blockage?" Miyetsuko blinked, her voice dropping an octave as her rational faculties awkwardly tried to reboot. "No hidden pathogens? No structural skin necrosis?"

“一……一个常规的毛囊堵塞？”美悦子眨了眨眼，随着理智系统艰难重启，她的声音也跟着降了一个八度，“没有隐藏病原体？也没有皮肤结构性坏死？”

"Nope, just a regular zit," the doctor smiled, handing her a small tube of anti-inflammatory cream. "Go on home, you'll be fine."

“没有，就是个普通痘痘，”医生笑着递给她一小支红霉素软膏，“回去吧，没事。”

Stepping out into the warm island sunshine, Miyetsuko clutched the tiny tube of ointment, her cheeks flushing with a rare, adorable shade of embarrassment.

走出诊所，沐浴着温暖的岛屿阳光，美悦子紧紧攥着那支小小的软膏，一向清冷的脸颊罕见地泛起了一抹羞窘的红晕。

Fujiwara burst into laughter, wrapping an arm around her waist as they walked back to the scooter.

藤原忍不住放声大笑起来，伸手揽住她的纤腰，带着她朝电动车走去。

"See? What did I tell you?" Fujiwara teased gently, kissing her blushing cheek. "Even the smartest scientist in the world is allowed to panic over a little acne. Come on, let's go home. Your facial symmetry is going to survive, I promise."

“看吧？我就说没什么事吧？”藤原温柔地调侃着，在她羞红的脸颊上亲了一下，“哪怕是全世界最聪明的科学家，偶尔允许自己为了一颗小痘痘大惊小怪一次。走吧，回家。我保证，你的面部对称性绝对完好无损。”
