Chapter 189: Reality Bites

发布时间：2026/8/29 12:18:51

第一百八十九章：残酷的现实

True to the unpredictable, chaotic nature of Donglai Island, the universe decided to answer Fujiwara’s scandalous joke much faster than anyone anticipated.

正如东莱岛那不可预测的魔幻作风一样，命运以所有人意料之外的速度，迅速回应了藤原先前那场荒唐的玩笑。

Just three days after their playful breakfast banter, the doorbell of "Fresh Mart" chimed crisply. Rin groaned, tossing her magazine onto the coffee table.

就在那场早餐闲聊的三天后，“新鲜玛特”的门铃清脆地响了起来。铃无奈地叹了口气，把杂志扔在茶几上。

"Ugh, that must be her," Rin muttered, rubbing her temples. "My mother actually took me seriously and decided to drop by for a surprise visit."

“唉，肯定是她来了，”铃揉着太阳穴嘟囔道，“我妈居然把那句玩笑话当真了，还真搞了个突然袭击来串门。”

Fujiwara’s eyes lit up instantly. A wicked, expectant grin spread across his face as he leaped toward the door, secretly bracing himself for a dramatic, sultry mother-in-law entrance straight out of a tabloid drama.

藤原的眼睛瞬间亮了起来，脸上挂满了坏笑和期待，一个箭步冲向门口，心里暗暗做好了迎接某种八卦杂志风格的妖娆岳母登场的准备。

He swung the heavy front door wide open—and his brain instantly flatlined.

他猛地拉开厚重的大门——然后大脑当场彻底宕机。

Standing on the welcome mat was not a glamorous, mature siren capable of launching a neighborhood scandal. Instead, it was a remarkably sweet, decidedly elderly grandmother with silver-streaked hair, a cozy floral cardigan, and a pair of spectacles resting gently on the bridge of her nose. She looked like she belonged behind a rocking chair knitting baby socks, not starring in a forbidden domestic thriller.

站在门垫上的并不是什么能引发邻里风波的风韵熟女，而是一位慈祥和蔼、满头银发、穿着碎花针织开衫、鼻梁上架着老花镜的老奶奶。她看起来分明应该坐在摇椅里织着婴儿袜，而不是去演什么禁忌的家庭伦理剧。

"Hello there, young man! You must be Fujiwara," the elderly woman beamed warmly, leaning heavily on a polished wooden walking cane and offering a cheerful wave.

“你好呀小伙子！你就是藤原吧？”老人家慈祥地笑着，手里拄着一根精致的木质拐杖，热情地朝他挥了挥手。

Every single degenerate, wicked, MILF-inspired fantasy Fujiwara had harbored over the last seventy-two hours evaporated into thin air in less than a second, instantly replaced by sheer, overwhelming panic and traditional filial piety.

在这一瞬间，藤原过去七十二小时里脑子里积攒的所有下流、邪恶、少妇风的幻想瞬间烟消云散，取而代之的是纯粹的惊慌失措和满腔肃然起敬的传统孝道。

"M-Mother-in-law...!" Fujiwara stammered, his face instantly draining of color as he scrambled to straighten his posture into a rigid 90-degree bow. "Welcome to Fresh Mart! Please, come in! Watch the step, grab my arm—let me treat you with the absolute highest level of respect!"

“岳、岳母大人……！”藤原结结巴巴地喊道，脸色瞬间变得惨白，手忙脚乱地挺直腰杆鞠了个九十度的躬，“欢迎光临新鲜玛特！请进请进！小心台阶，扶着我的胳膊——晚辈一定用最高规格的礼遇来孝敬您！”

Behind him, Rin, Miyetsuko, and Sayuri crowded into the hallway, taking one look at the sweet old lady and then at Fujiwara’s violently shattered, pale expression before bursting into uproarious, helpless laughter.

在他身后，铃、美悦子和小百合挤在玄关处，看着慈祥的老奶奶，又瞅瞅藤原那副三观尽碎、面色发青的惨样，当场爆发出了一阵毫无同情心的狂笑。

"Well, well, look at our fearless household pervert!" Rin cackled, clutching her stomach as she leaned against the wall. "What happened to your grand neighborhood scandal plans, Fujiwara? Why aren't you asking her to move in permanently?"

“哎呀呀，瞧瞧咱们家天不怕地不怕的变态大拿！”铃扶着墙笑得直不起腰，“藤原，你那宏伟的邻里八卦改造计划呢？怎么不邀请人家常住下来好好照顾你啦？”

Miyetsuko adjusted her glasses, offering a deadpan, highly amused analysis. "A textbook collision between delusional fantasy and biological reality. The visitor is clearly past the threshold of geriatric eligibility for your degenerate experiments, rendering any illicit domestic restructuring both physically impossible and ethically terrifying."

美悦子推了推眼镜，冷静而毒舌地分析道：“完美的白日梦与残酷生物学现实的激情碰撞。这位长辈显然已经超越了你那些变态实验的年龄阈值，任何非法的家庭结构重组不仅在物理上绝无可能，在伦理上也堪称恐怖片。”

Sayuri stepped forward gracefully, taking the old woman’s frail hand with a warm, welcoming smile. "Welcome, ma'am. Please pay no attention to our resident idiot—he was just having a brief, temporary psychiatric episode."

小百合优雅地走上前去，热情地握住老奶奶枯瘦温暖的手：“欢迎您，老人家。请别理会我们家的这位笨蛋——他刚才只是间歇性精神错乱犯了而已。”

As the sweet grandmother settled comfortably into the living room with a steaming cup of green tea, completely oblivious to the perverted conspiracies that had been swirling around her arrival just moments prior, Fujiwara slumped miserably into a corner chair. He wiped the cold sweat from his forehead, quietly erasing every single unholy thought from his mind forever, vowing never to joke about mothers-in-law ever again.

当慈祥的老奶奶端着热气腾腾的绿茶舒舒服服地坐在起居室的沙发上，对刚才围绕着她登场展开的那些下流阴谋一无所知时，藤原已经生无可恋地缩在墙角的椅子里。他擦着额头上的冷汗，默默在心底把所有不健康的杂念永远清空，并暗暗发誓以后再也不敢拿岳母开玩笑了。
