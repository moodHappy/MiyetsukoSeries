Chapter 658: The Tactical Flanking and Midnight Indulgence

第六百五十八章：侧翼突袭与深宫闺房荒淫

The heavy velvet curtains of the imperial bedchamber were drawn tight, sealing off the chilly night air and turning the vast gilded room into an intimate, candlelit sanctuary of silk and incense.

深宫寝殿的厚重丝绒帷幕严严实实地拉合拢来，将夜风与寒意尽数隔绝在外，将整座奢华的鎏金暖阁变成了一方弥漫着龙涎香与丝绸气息的香艳私密天地。

Emperor Fujiwara sat lounging on the edge of the wide brocade bed, his sovereign robes shed aside, wearing a relaxed, triumphant smirk as he watched his frontier titaness kneel upon the plush silk cushions before him.

藤原大帝大刺刺地坐在宽阔的织锦龙榻边缘，早已褪去了繁复华贵的帝袍，脸上挂着胜券在握、恶趣味十足的坏笑，看着眼前这位来自高重力星球的丰腴女贵族乖乖跪伏在厚实的丝绸软垫上。

True to his word, she lowered her upper body, resting her chest against the cushions while arching her lower back high—presenting those monumental, pillowy mounds of flesh while leaving the lower path open to his "tactical flanking" maneuver.

正如他先前所言，这位体态惊人的贵妇将上半身缓缓伏低，丰满的胸脯贴着软垫，腰肢则高高塌起，将那对堪称奇观的浑圆丰臀高耸在后，却又顺着体态的起伏，把下方的路径毫无保留地敞露在他的“侧翼突袭”路线之前。

"See how cleanly the geometry works out?" Fujiwara chuckled, leaning forward as his large hands gripped her waist, guiding the angle with practiced, shameless precision. "No wasted distance, no getting lost in the hills—straight through from underneath to the very heart of the matter."

“瞧瞧这几何角度多么顺理成章？”藤原低笑着向前倾身，一双大手稳稳扣住她的纤腰，以老辣而无耻的精准度调整着切入方位，“不浪费半寸行程，也不至于迷失在重峦叠嶂之中——直接从下方长驱直入，直捣核心。”

In the warm amber glow of the palace lamps, the scene unfolded with all the decadent extravagance expected of the Fujiwara court—satin sheets tangled around bare limbs, heavy breathing echoing softly against the silk canopies, and the Emperor moving with unhurried, tyrannical rhythm as he turned his "tactical exploit" into a prolonged display of imperial excess.

在宫灯暖融融的琥珀色光晕里，一切都按照藤原皇室一贯的奢靡荒淫节奏徐徐展开：缎面床单缠绕着裸露的肌肤，沉重急促的喘息在锦帐间低回，大帝以从容不迫、专横霸道的韵律挺动身躯，将自己所谓的“战术捷径”演变成一场极尽挥霍的宫廷欢愉。

Outside the inner curtains, the rest of the harem lingered in the adjacent lounge, listening to the muffled sounds from the bedchamber while sipping chilled fruit wine.

垂花帐外，后宫众美正坐在相邻的偏厅里，一边小口啜饮着冰镇果酒，一边听着内寝传来的隐约声响。

Chika leaned back against a silk bolster, rolling her eyes with a mixture of grudging admiration and sheer disbelief.

千夏靠在丝绸软枕上，翻了个白眼，眼神里交织着几分不服气的佩服与难以置信。

"I still say it's cheating," Chika muttered, though her tone lacked any real bite. "He spent all afternoon drawing angles like a math professor, and now he's acting like he just conquered a whole star system."

“我还是觉得这算作弊，”千夏嘟囔着，语气里却没多少真火，“他下午像个数学教授似的画了一堆角度图，现在倒好，搞得好像刚打下整个星系似的。”

Sayuri delicately tapped her velvet fan against her palm, her lips curving into a knowing, aristocratic smile.

小百合用丝绒折扇轻点着掌心，唇角勾起一抹心照不宣的贵妇笑意。

"For a ruler whose entire empire runs on theatrical excess, even a posture adjustment must be elevated to grand strategy," Sayuri purred smoothly. "Let him enjoy his 'geometry.' By morning, he'll be back to complaining about tax tariffs."

“对于一个把整座帝国都建在铺张荒唐之上的君主而言，连换个姿势都得拔高到宏大战略的高度，”小百合软糯地笑道，“由着他去享受他的‘几何学’吧。到了天亮，他照样得回来头疼税收关税的事。”

Miyetsuko adjusted her 183828号 Death God glasses, jotting a brief note on her ledger without looking up.

美悦子推了推鼻梁上的183828号死神眼镜，头也不抬地在账册上记下一行简短的批注。

"Tactical flanking confirmed successful. Nighttime noise index within normal imperial parameters," Miyetsuko noted dryly.

“侧翼包抄确认成功。夜间噪音指数处于皇室正常浮动区间内。”美悦子冷冰冰地补充道。

Rin giggled softly, twirling her succubus tail as she hopped onto a plush chaise lounge.

小铃咯咯轻笑一声，卷着魅魔尾巴跳上了一张柔软的贵妃榻。

"At least he's not running off to sign decrees mid-scene tonight! That's what I call progress!" Rin cheered.

“至少今晚他没在半中间突然跑去批圣旨！在我看来，这已经算得上是划时代的进步啦！”小铃欢快地起哄道。

Behind the silk curtains, the candles burned low, casting shifting shadows over a night of unabashed royal indulgence—where tyranny, extravagance, and carnal pleasure blended seamlessly into the everyday life of the Fujiwara court.

锦帐深处，烛火渐低，在无遮无拦的皇室纵欲之夜中投下摇曳的光影——专横、奢靡与欢愉，就这样自然而然地融进了藤原宫廷永不落幕的荒唐日常里。
