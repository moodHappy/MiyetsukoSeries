Miyetsuko had developed a bizarre, insatiable domestic habit: the very second her eyelashes fluttered open from sleep, no matter where she was in the sprawling penthouse, she had to hunt down Fujiwara immediately. It didn't matter whose bed the Emperor had graced the night before—whether he was tangled up in Sayuri’s silk sheets, dozing under Rin’s velvet canopy, or buried against Chika’s overflowing dairy pillow—the bedroom door would inevitably creak open.

美悦子不知怎的，突然染上了一个荒诞又极度磨人的闺房怪癖：每天清晨只要那双美眸一睁开，不管自己人在庄园的哪个犄角旮旯，第一件事就是光着脚丫满屋子翻找藤原。管他昨晚帝王是临幸了谁的寝殿——是在小百合那张满是暗香的绣榻上温存，还是在小铃的天鹅绒罗帐里补眠，亦或是被千夏那对软糯溢奶的雪峰严严实实埋着——那扇雕花厚重的大门，准保会在破晓时分被悄无声息地推开一条缝。

Padding across the hardwood floors with bare feet, her oversized silk nightshirt hanging loose off one shoulder, Miyetsuko would slip beneath the duvet like a boneless serpent. Pressing her warm, sleep-scented skin flush against Fujiwara, she would rub her flushed cheeks into his chest, whining in a sweet, milk-toned baby whisper that made everyone's toes curl:

她光着白玉般的脚丫踩过地板，身上松垮垮地套着一件滑落半边香肩的真丝睡裙，宛如一条无骨的美女蛇般轻车熟路地钻进锦被深处。将那身带着初醒甜香与微热体温的软肉严丝合缝地贴在藤原身上，两腮在他胸口蹭来蹭去，掐着一口又软又糯、奶声奶气的幼嫩嗓音，羞答答地附在他耳边低声哼唧：

"Master... Miyetsuko wants... I want to make love right now, the very second I wake up..."

“主人……美悦子想……人家想在刚睡醒的时候做爱嘛……”

This morning, the imperial sanctuary happened to be Chika’s lavish, jasmine-scented suite.

而今儿个清晨遭殃的，恰好是千夏那间弥漫着浓郁茉莉甜香与奶香的粉色闺房。

Chika had barely started her morning nursing routine, cradling her heavy, buoyant peaks in front of Fujiwara's lips, when the bedroom doors swung open. Miyetsuko drifted straight past the silk screens, lifted the edge of the duvet without the slightest hesitation, and crawled onto the mattress on all fours, wedging her pliant hips straight between Fujiwara’s thighs.

千夏才刚睡眼惺忪地支起身子，正准备将胸前那对经过顶级御医精炼调理、高耸饱满的雪白软肉凑到藤原嘴边喂早点，寝殿大门便应声而开。美悦子幽灵般穿过屏风，连眼皮都没眨一下，径直掀开锦被爬上了大床，手脚并用地横陈在被褥之间，直接把自己那副柔韧滑腻的腰臀霸道地卡进了藤原的大腿中间。

"Hey! Miyetsuko-nee! What are you doing?!" Chika shrieked in spoiled mortification, clutching her chest defensively as she was jostled across the pillows. "This is my room! Today is my morning shift! You didn't even knock! You just barged in, took off your panties under the blanket, and climbed right on top of him! Can't you wait ten minutes for the Emperor to finish breakfast?!"

“喂！美悦子姐姐！你干什么呀？！”千夏被冷不丁挤到了一旁，气急败坏地捂着沉甸甸乱晃的胸脯娇嗔大叫起来，“这是我的房间！今早轮到我当值伺候早点！你连门都不敲一下！大摇大摆闯进来，在被窝里随手把小内裤一扯就直接骑上去了！你就不能等老板把早饭吃完了再发浪吗？！”

Miyetsuko didn't even blush away; instead, she turned her dewy, heavy-lidded eyes toward Chika, letting out a soft, bashful whimper while her slender arms tightened around Fujiwara’s neck.

美悦子非但没有被抓包的慌乱，反而转过那张春潮泛滥、睡眼惺忪的精致俏脸，软绵绵地哼唧了一声，两条白嫩的藕臂死死缠紧了藤原的脖颈，整个人像块融化的麦芽糖一样挂在他身上。

"I can't help it, little sister..." Miyetsuko murmured in that timid, honey-sweet whisper, squirming against Fujiwara's rising heat with shameless desperation. "The second my eyes open... my body just aches all over. It feels so hollow inside... If Master doesn't fill me up immediately before my brain fully wakes up, I feel like I'm going to melt into a puddle. Master... please... hurry, push it inside me..."

Hearing this, Sayuri, who had glided into the doorway wearing an emerald silk dressing gown to check on the morning commotion, leaned against the doorframe and let out a long, helpless sigh of amused surrender.

恰在此时，披着墨绿真丝长袍、被动静引来看热闹的小百合也款款倚在了门框边，掩唇发出一声既好气又好笑的慵懒长叹。

From behind Sayuri's robes, blind Rin popped her head in, her milky eyes blinking angelically as she sniffed the air with a sweet succubus giggle.

小百合身后的长袍边，盲人小铃探出了一颗小脑袋，纯白空灵的眸子无辜地眨巴着，吸了吸空气中弥漫的暧昧潮气，发出银铃般的清脆娇笑。

"Um... hello, Miyetsuko-nee? Rin can hear your heartbeat going thump-thump-thump from all the way down the hall!" Rin chimed in brightly. Chika-nee, don't be mad. While Miyetsuko-nee is getting her medicine, Rin can come over and share your milk with Master!"
