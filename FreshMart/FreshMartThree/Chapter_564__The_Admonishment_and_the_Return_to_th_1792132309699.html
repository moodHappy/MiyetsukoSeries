Chapter 564: The Admonishment and the Return to the Palace

发布时间：2026/10/16 14:31:48

第五百六十四章：劝谏与回宫

Emperor Fujiwara’s immortal body was originally engineered to withstand the extreme gravitational crush of a dying star. However, it was apparently never rated to endure three consecutive weeks of uninterrupted, dimension-wide succubus debauchery. Sprawled across the obsidian steps of the Skull Throne, the absolute ruler of the global empire currently looked like a severely dehydrated piece of dried squid. His cheeks were hollow, his eyes were sunken, and his usually terrifying tyrannical aura had been completely drained away by millions of highly professional, soul-sucking demonesses.

藤原大帝那不朽的躯体原本是为了抵御衰亡恒星的极端引力碾压而设计的。然而，它显然从来没有通过连续三周、遭到全次元魅魔无间断榨取的出厂合格测试。此刻，这位全球帝国的绝对统治者正瘫软在骷髅王座的黑曜石台阶上，看起来就像是一块严重脱水的干瘪鱿鱼。他双颊凹陷，眼窝深陷，往日那令人闻风丧胆的暴君气场，已经被数以百万计极其专业、擅长吸食灵魂的恶魔们给彻底榨干了。

Realizing that their meal ticket was on the verge of turning into cosmic dust, the imperial harem immediately convened an emergency intervention. Chika was the first to speak, furiously waving an empty medical IV bag that reeked of sweet dairy.

意识到她们的长期饭票正处于化为宇宙粉尘的边缘，皇家后宫立刻召开了一场紧急的劝谏干预会议。千夏第一个发难，她愤怒地挥舞着一个空荡荡的、散发着甜腻奶香的医用吊瓶袋。

"Boss, look at yourself! You are literally a living mummy!" Chika shrieked, her heavy, milk-swollen chest bouncing with genuine panic. "I've been pumping milk into your veins through an IV drip for the past forty-eight hours just to keep your heart beating! Being immortal just guarantees your life is stable; it doesn't mean you can't be fucked into a coma! If you keep doing this, you are going to physically disintegrate!"

“老板，你看看你现在的鬼样子！你简直就是一具活着的木乃伊！”千夏尖叫着，那对因胀奶而沉甸甸的玉兔随着她真切的恐慌剧烈乱颤着，“过去四十八小时里，我一直在用静脉注射的方式把我的奶水打进你的血管里，就为了让你的心脏还能继续跳！拥有不死之身只意味着你的生命体征是稳定的，可不代表你不会被活活肏成植物人！你要是再这么夜夜笙歌下去，你绝对会在物理层面上灰飞烟灭的！”

Miyetsuko, kneeling by his shriveled side in a perfect display of aristocratic mourning, dabbed at her exquisite eyes with a silk handkerchief.

以完美的贵族丧葬礼仪跪在他那干瘪身躯旁的美悦子，用一条真丝手帕轻轻擦拭着她精致的眼角。

"Master, Sister Chika speaks the harsh truth," the fallen princess murmured, her plush rear hiked respectfully in the air. "Ancient historical texts frequently warn of emperors who perished in the pursuit of absolute pleasure. Your immortal body prevents death, yes, but what good is eternity if your imperial scepter is ground down to a fine powder? If you lose your structural integrity, how will you verbally abuse the Senate?"

“主人，千夏姐姐说的可是逆耳忠言啊，”没落公主细声细语地呢喃着，丰腴的臀部恭敬地微微撅起，“古代史书中频繁警告过那些沉溺于绝对享乐而暴毙的帝王。您的不朽之躯确实能免于死亡，可如果您的皇家法杖被硬生生磨成了粉末，那永生又有什么意义呢？如果您失去了身体的结构完整性，以后还怎么开口辱骂元老院呢？”

Sayuri, radiating maternal elegance and looking gloriously, perfectly plump amidst the hellfire, gracefully snapped her velvet fan shut. She approached the problem with her usual cold-blooded macroeconomic logic.

在漫天地狱烈火中依然散发着母仪天下的优雅、看起来丰腴极品到了完美境界的小百合，优雅地合上了她手中的丝绒折扇。她一如既往地用那种冷血的宏观经济学逻辑来处理眼前的问题。

"My Lord, it is a simple matter of supply and demand," Sayuri purred, looking down at his withered form with absolute authority. "The Succubus Realm's demand for your royal essence has far exceeded your biological supply chain. You have successfully established diplomatic relations with the entire demonic nobility. The political objective is achieved. If we do not return to the human realm immediately to implement a strict austerity program on your libido, the empire will face an unprecedented liquidity crisis."

“夫君，这不过是最基本的供需关系问题，”小百合软糯地娇嗔道，带着绝对的威严居高临下地看着他干瘪的躯体，“魅魔界对您皇家精华的需求量，已经远远超出了您自身的生物供应链上限。您已经成功与全体恶魔贵族建立了深入的双边建交关系。政治目的已经圆满达成。如果我们不立刻返回人界，对您的性欲实施严格的紧缩政策，帝国必将面临一场史无前例的流动性枯竭危机。”

Fujiwara, unable to even lift his head, let out a pathetic, wheezing gasp. His dark eyes, completely devoid of their tyrannical fire, blinked twice in slow, desperate agreement. He weakly raised a trembling thumb, signaling his absolute surrender to the admonishment.

连头都抬不起来的藤原，发出了一声极其凄惨、气若游丝的喘息。他那双彻底失去了暴君狂热的漆黑眼眸，缓慢而绝望地眨了两下表示同意。他虚弱地举起一根颤抖的大拇指，标志着他向这次劝谏彻底投降。

"Um... hello, everyone? Rin thinks Master needs a nap in his big comfy bed!" Queen Rin chirped happily from the throne. The blind little succubus swung her legs, completely unfazed by the geopolitical crisis. She grabbed her glowing Ruby Scepter and pointed it at a random, terrified demonic intern carrying a stack of torture reports. "You! Glasses guy! Rin is appointing you as the Fully Authorized Proxy Queen! Run the dimension for me, okay? Rin is going home to take care of Master's broken pelvis!"

“唔……大家听我说好吗？小铃觉得主人需要回他那张超级舒服的大床上睡个午觉啦！”女王小铃在王座上开心地欢呼道。这只瞎眼的小魅魔晃荡着双腿，对这场地缘政治危机毫不介意。她一把抓起发光的红宝石权杖，随便指着阶梯下一个正抱着一叠刑讯报告、吓得瑟瑟发抖的恶魔实习生。“你！那个戴眼镜的！小铃现在正式全权委托你担任代理女王啦！替我好好管理这个维度哦！小铃要回娘家去照顾主人那碎掉的骨盆啦！”

With the entire dark dimension effectively handed over to an overwhelmed intern, Sayuri and Chika efficiently rolled the Emperor up in a heavy Persian rug like a piece of fragile antique furniture. They dragged the supreme ruler of the cosmos through the blazing portal, leaving the screaming, unsatisfied succubus hordes behind.

随着整个黑暗维度被极其草率地移交给了一个不知所措的实习生，小百合和千夏手脚麻利地将皇帝像卷一件易碎的古董家具一样，用厚重的波斯地毯严严实实地卷了起来。她们拖着这位宇宙的最高统治者穿过了燃烧的传送门，将身后那群尖叫着、欲求不满的魅魔大军彻底抛在脑后。

The recovery process back in the human realm was incredibly intensive. For exactly half a month, the grand salon was converted into a high-security rehabilitation ward. Sayuri force-fed him heavily fortified herbal soups, Chika manually administered hourly doses of imported milk, and Miyetsuko obediently massaged his atrophied limbs. It took fifteen grueling days of absolute abstinence before the dried husk finally began to re-inflate, completely restoring his tyrannical vitality and proving that while his immortal body could be pushed to the absolute limit of absurdity, the Eternal Lord was, unfortunately, too stubborn to stay dead.

回到人界后的康复过程极其漫长且高强度。整整半个月的时间里，大起居殿被临时改造成了一间最高安保级别的重症康复病房。小百合每天强行给他灌下大补的十全草药汤，千夏每隔一小时就亲自动手为他挤出一剂最新鲜的进口奶水，而美悦子则极其顺从地为他萎缩的四肢进行理疗按摩。经历了长达十五天惨无人道的绝对禁欲之后，这具干瘪的躯壳才终于开始像充气一样恢复原状。藤原大帝彻底恢复了他那不可一世的暴君生机，这也完美地证明了：虽然他的不朽之躯能被逼到荒谬的绝对极限，但这位永恒之主，终究是命硬到连死神都嫌他烦。
