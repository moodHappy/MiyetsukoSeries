Chapter 89: The Rooftop Sanctuary

发布时间：2026/8/25 15:33:20

第八十九章：天台的秘密日光浴

As the tail end of summer began to soften the scorching edge of the Donglai Island sun, Miyetsuko decided that a scientific, dermatological intervention was urgently required.

随着夏末的余热开始褪去东莱岛那滚烫的锐气，美悦子认为，一场科学且契合皮肤医学的保养干预已经刻不容缓。

Gathering the girls in the living room earlier that afternoon, Miyetsuko had laid out her proposal with the precision of a seasoned researcher.

就在当天下午，美悦子将女孩们召集在起居室里，以资深学者的严谨态度提出了她的策划案。

"From a physiological standpoint, absorbing late-summer ultraviolet rays at a controlled dosage optimizes vitamin D synthesis and promotes cellular metabolism," Miyetsuko had stated, adjusting her glasses. "Therefore, to maximize skin rejuvenation before autumn arrives, I propose an exclusive, full-nude rooftop sunbathing session. Strictly ladies only."

“从生理学角度来看，在可控剂量下吸收夏末的紫外线能够优化维生素D的合成并促进细胞代谢，”美悦子推了推眼镜认真说道，“因此，为了在秋天来临前实现皮肤状态的最大化焕新，我提议在楼顶天台进行一次全裸日光浴。仅限女士参加。”

Rin had immediately grinned, giving a enthusiastic thumbs-up, while Sayuri had blushed and nodded with a sweet, agreeable smile.

铃当时便咧嘴一笑，兴奋地比了个大拇指，而小百合则羞红了脸，带着甜美顺从的微笑点头应允。

Now, the flat rooftop of "Fresh Mart" had been transformed into a secluded, private oasis. Surrounded by low parapet walls that shielded them from prying eyes, the rooftop offered an uninterrupted panoramic view of the sparkling blue ocean and the vast, cloudless sky.

此刻，“新鲜玛特”平坦宽敞的楼顶天台已被打造得宛如一处私密的世外桃源。低矮的女儿墙完美挡住了外界窥探的视线，让这里既拥有直面蔚蓝海景与万里无云晴空的开阔视野，又兼具绝对的隐蔽。

The three women lay stretched out on luxurious, oversized lounge chairs, completely shedding the burden of clothing. Stripped bare under the warm, caressing rays of the afternoon sun, their skin gleamed with a fine sheen of organic coconut oil, catching the golden light like polished marble.

三个女人舒展身体躺在奢华宽大的躺椅上，彻底卸下了衣物的束缚。在午后温暖轻柔的阳光下赤裸相见，她们白皙细腻的肌肤上泛着一层淡淡的天然椰油光泽，如温润的玉石般折射出动人的金色光晕。

Miyetsuko lay with her eyes closed behind dark designer sunglasses, a damp lavender-scented cloth draped over her forehead. Even in a state of complete undress, her posture remained impeccably elegant, her slender, pale limbs stretched out in a picture of cool, intellectual composure.

美悦子戴着一副黑色太阳镜闭目养神，额头上搭着一块带有淡淡薰衣草香气的湿毛巾。即便在不着寸缕的状态下，她那修长白皙的四肢和优雅的坐姿依然散发着冷静而知性的美感。

A few feet away, Rin stretched her athletic, beautifully toned body with a contented sigh. Her sun-kissed curves and toned waist reflected the vibrant, untamed energy of the island, her skin drinking in the golden warmth as she lazily flipped a page of a magazine she wasn't really reading.

不远处的躺椅上，铃舒展着她那线条优美、充满健康活力的健美身躯，发出一声满足的喟叹。她那被阳光吻过的紧致曲线在光影下流转着野性的美感，正懒洋洋地翻动着一本其实根本没在看的杂志。

In the center, curled up comfortably on her plush towel, lay Sayuri. The soft, gentle breeze rustled her dark hair against the white linen pillow. Without a stitch of clothing to hide her, her flawless, creamy skin seemed to glow from within, radiating the soft, effortless grace of a true queen basking in her natural element.

而在正中央，小百合安逸地蜷缩在柔软的浴巾上。温柔的海风吹拂着她黑色的发丝，在白色的亚麻枕头上轻轻晃动。没有任何衣物的遮掩，她那毫无瑕疵、白皙细腻的肌肤仿佛自带柔光，完美诠释了一位隐退女王在自然怀抱中无与伦比的优雅。

"Ah, the skin absorption rate at this hour is truly optimal," Miyetsuko murmured lazily, breaking the peaceful silence with a soft hum of satisfaction. "No interruptions, no customers, and zero UV interference."

“啊，这个时段的皮肤吸收率简直堪称完美，”美悦子懒洋洋地打破了宁静，发出一声满足的低喃，“没有打扰，没有顾客，也没有任何多余的紫外线干扰。”

"Told you it was a brilliant idea, doc," Rin smirked, closing her eyes and letting her head fall back against the cushion. "Who needs a spa when you've got a private island rooftop and the best company in the world?"

“早就说了这是个绝妙的主意吧，美悦子博士，”铃勾起嘴角坏笑着，将头舒服地靠在靠垫上，“有私人海岛的天台和全世界最好的姐妹陪伴，谁还去什么高级水疗馆呀？”

Sayuri turned her head slightly, offering a radiant, contented smile as the warm breeze washed over them. High above the world, bathed in golden sunlight and absolute freedom, the three women shared a moment of pure, unadulterated tranquility before the final chapter of summer drew to a close.

小百合微微偏过头，在阵阵温暖的海风中露出一抹明艳而满足的微笑。在这高悬于世俗之上的天台，沐浴着金色的日光与绝对的自由，三个女人在夏末的尾声里，共同享受着这份纯粹而静谧的惬意时光。
