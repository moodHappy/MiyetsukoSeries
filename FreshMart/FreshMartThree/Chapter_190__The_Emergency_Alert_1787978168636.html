Chapter 190: The Emergency Alert

发布时间：2026/8/29 12:36:07

第一百五十章：紧急通报

True to the unpredictable, chaotic rhythm of Donglai Island, the fragile peace of "Fresh Mart" was shattered in a single, heart-stopping second.

正如东莱岛那不可预测的魔幻作风一样，“新鲜玛特”里短暂的平静，在短短一秒钟内被彻底粉碎。

Just a few days after the mother-in-law fiasco, Miyetsuko was sitting quietly at the kitchen island reviewing some administrative data sheets when her smartphone suddenly shrieked with a high-priority emergency broadcast alert.

在那场有惊无险的岳母探访过去几天后，美悦子正安静地坐在厨房岛台旁审阅行政数据表，手机突然发出最高等级的紧急广播警报声。

She picked it up and glanced at the screen—and her blood ran instantly cold.

她拿起来扫了一眼屏幕——浑身的血液瞬间冻结。

“Breaking News: Active shooter reported on the central campus of Tokyo University. Multiple casualties confirmed. Lockdown in immediate effect.”

“突发新闻：东京大学主校区发生校园枪击案。已确认有多人伤亡。校园已进入紧急全面封锁。”

Miyetsuko’s breath hitched. That was Chika’s university.

美悦子呼吸一滞。那正是千夏所在的学校！

Before she could even process the horror, her phone buzzed again with a frantic, incoming text message directly from Chika:

还没等她消化这股恐惧，手机再次剧烈震动起来，弹出了一条千夏发来的绝望短讯：

[Master... someone with a gun... people are screaming... I'm hiding inside the campus library storage room... please...]

[主人……有人拿着枪……外面到处都是尖叫声……我躲在图书馆的储藏室里……求求你……]

The message cut off abruptly in the middle of a sentence.

讯息在一句话中间戛然而止。

The living room plunged into a suffocating, terrifying silence. For the first time, the usual playful banter and degenerate joking of "Fresh Mart" vanished completely, replaced by a raw, suffocating panic.

起居室里瞬间陷入了一片令人窒息的恐怖死寂。这一刻，“新鲜玛特”里平日里的玩笑与荒诞荡然无存，取而代之的是最原始、最揪心的恐慌。

"Miyetsuko? What is it? You look pale as a ghost," Fujiwara asked, stepping into the room with a cup of coffee. But as soon as he saw Miyetsuko’s trembling hands and tear-filled eyes, his own heart skipped a beat.

“美悦子？怎么了？你的脸白得像见鬼了一样，”藤原端着咖啡走进屋里。可当他看到美悦子颤抖的双手和泛红的眼眶时，自己的心跳也漏了半拍。

"Chika..." Miyetsuko choked out, her voice cracking with unprecedented terror. "There's a school shooting at her campus. Her last message just cut off."

“千夏……”美悦子声音颤抖着，带着前所未有的恐惧，“她的学校……发生校园枪击案了。她的讯息刚刚断了。”

Fujiwara’s coffee mug slipped from his fingers, shattering into a thousand pieces against the hardwood floor.

藤原手里的咖啡杯从指间滑落，“啪”的一声在地板上摔得粉碎。

"Move! Everyone in the car, right now!" Fujiwara roared, his voice cracking with fierce, desperate panic as he sprinted toward the front door to grab his car keys. Rin, Miyetsuko, and Sayuri didn't waste a single millisecond—they dropped everything and bolted right behind him.

“动起来！所有人立刻上车！”藤原双眼血红，声音因极度的恐慌而沙哑，疯狂地冲向玄关抓起车钥匙。铃、美悦子和小百合没有浪费哪怕一毫秒，丢下一切紧随其后狂奔而出。

They piled into the car like a storm, tearing out of Donglai Island toward the mainland highway leading straight to Tokyo. But as fate would have it, the morning rush hour traffic had transformed into an immovable, agonizing wall of steel and brake lights.

他们像狂风般冲进汽车，疯了似地驶离东莱岛，冲向通往东京的内陆高速。然而命运弄人，早高峰的车流此刻却汇聚成了一堵密不透风、令人绝望的钢铁长城。

"Damn it! Move, you piece of garbage truck!" Fujiwara slammed his fists against the steering wheel, screaming curse words at the endless sea of red tail lights blocking the highway.

“该死的！前面那辆大卡车给老子滚开！”藤原疯狂地砸着方向盘，对着堵得水泄不通的高速公路声嘶力竭地咒骂着。

In the back seat, Miyetsuko gripped her phone with white knuckles, while Rin and Sayuri prayed with trembling lips, all of them burning with a shared, frantic desperation to reach their beloved little maid before it was too late.

后座上，美悦子指节发白地攥着手机，铃和小百合双唇微颤地祈祷着，所有人的心中都只有一个疯狂的念头：快一点，再快一点，一定要赶在来不及之前去救回他们的小丫鬟！
