第七十章：屁股的大搜查

The "Fresh Mart" had officially turned into a circus. The evening meal—a chaotic heap of leftover takeout and cheap beer—was winding down, and the three of them were sprawled out in the back office, bellies full and morals left at the door.

“新鲜马特”彻底沦为了马戏团。晚餐——一堆凌乱的外卖残渣和廉价啤酒——已近尾声，三人瘫在后办公室里，酒足饭饱，道德底线早就丢到了九霄云外。

Fujiwara, already three beers deep, suddenly stared at the ceiling, his brow furrowed with the kind of philosophical depth only a drunk idiot could possess. "You know," he mumbled, "I was just thinking... why on earth does anyone grow hair right in their asshole? It’s not like it’s styling a mohawk down there."

藤原已经灌了三瓶啤酒，突然盯着天花板，眉头紧锁，露出了只有醉鬼才有的“哲学深度”。“你们说……为什么人的屁眼里会长毛啊？那地方又不是什么非主流莫霍克发型展示区。”

Miyetsuko snorted, her glass of wine nearly tipping over. Sayuri, who had been quietly eating a piece of cake, turned a shade of crimson that rivaled a ripe tomato. She instinctively clamped her thighs together, shifting uncomfortably.

美悦子嗤笑出声，手里的酒杯差点翻了。小百合原本正安静地吃着蛋糕，此时脸红得像个熟透的番茄。她本能地夹紧双腿，不安地动了动。

"Don't look at me," Sayuri squeaked, her voice barely a whisper. "I have... I have a lot, okay? It’s embarrassing!"

📌 完整翻译

“别看我。”小百合怯生生地说道，声音轻得几乎像耳语一样。

“我……我有很多，好吗？太让人难为情了！”

📘 英语教学式讲解

1. "Don't look at me,"

  • “别看我。”
  • Don't + 动词原形：祈使句否定形式，表示“不要……”。
  • look at：看着……

2. Sayuri squeaked

  • 小百合怯生生地说道。
  • Sayuri：小百合。
  • squeaked：尖声说道、细声说道。
  • 这里形容因为害羞、紧张而发出细小的声音。

3. her voice barely a whisper

  • 她的声音几乎低得像耳语一样。
  • voice：声音。
  • barely：几乎不、勉强。
  • a whisper：耳语、低语。
  • barely a whisper 是常见文学表达，意为“声音小得几乎听不见”。

4. "I have... I have a lot, okay?"

  • “我……我有很多，好吗？”
  • I have...：说话时犹豫、结巴。
  • a lot：很多、许多。
  • okay?：好吗？好吧？（带有解释、请求理解的语气）。

a lot 指代什么，需要结合前文语境才能确定。

5. It’s embarrassing!

  • 太让人难为情了！
  • embarrassing：令人尴尬的、令人害羞的。
  • It's...：表示“这件事很……”

📌 Key Expressions

  • don't look at me —— 别看我
  • squeak —— 尖声说；细声说
  • barely a whisper —— 几乎低不可闻
  • a lot —— 很多
  • okay? —— 好吗？（缓和语气）
  • embarrassing —— 令人难为情的；尴尬的

“别看我，”小百合尖着嗓子低语，“我的……我的毛最重，行了吧？太丢人了！”

Fujiwara’s eyes lit up with that manic, dangerous light. "Embarrassing? That’s not embarrassing, that’s data! We need a peer review! Pants down, let’s have a look!"

藤原眼中闪烁着那种疯狂而危险的光芒。“丢人？那不叫丢人，那叫数据！我们需要同行评议！脱裤子，检查一下！”

"Fujiwara, you degenerate," Miyetsuko laughed, already peeling off her leggings with zero hesitation. "But I’m in. Let’s compare the fluff."

“藤原，你这变态，”美悦子大笑着，毫无犹豫地扒下了打底裤，“不过我加入了，比比看谁的毛更茂盛。”

In a flurry of giggles, cursing, and flying fabric, they were soon circling each other like awkward primates. Sayuri, flushed and breathless, was cornered by Fujiwara and Miyetsuko, who were inspecting her "forest" with the clinical seriousness of wildlife biologists.

在一阵咯咯笑声、咒骂声和飞舞的衣物中，他们很快像群尴尬的灵长类动物一样围成一圈。小百合被藤原和美悦子围在中间，羞得喘不过气。两人正以野生动物学家的专业严谨态度，考察着她那片“原始森林”。

Then, they turned their attention to Rin.

接着，他们的注意力转向了铃。

Rin, completely oblivious but smiling like a saint, let them inspect her. But as Fujiwara pulled her panties down, he paused. He squinted. "Wait... Rin, honey? Is that... chocolate?"

铃对此毫无察觉，像个圣母一样微笑着任由他们检查。但当藤原扒下她的内裤时，他愣住了。他眯起眼：“等等……铃，亲爱的？那是什么……巧克力？”

Rin’s face went from pale to burning scarlet. She defensively swiped at her own backside, her movements frantic. "No! That’s impossible! I spent twenty minutes in the bathroom scrubbing! I’m clean!"

铃的脸色从苍白瞬间变成火烧般的绯红。她慌乱地往后摸去，动作急促而无措。“不！不可能！我在厕所里足足洗了二十分钟！我很干净的！”

"Honey, unless you've been smuggling cocoa powder, that’s definitely a leftover," Miyetsuko cackled, clutching her sides as she pointed at the smudge. "Did you not finish the job?"

“宝贝，除非你在走私可可粉，否则那绝对是残留物，”美悦子大笑着捂住肚子，指着那点污渍，“你没擦干净吗？”

Rin’s voice wavered, her natural-born "airheaded" charm hitting an all-time high. "I... I think it’s because it’s been... soft lately. Ever since... you know, that 'instructional' session in the van. My... my insides are still sensitive, and maybe I’m still leaking a bit!"

铃的声音开始打颤，她那天生的天然呆气质瞬间拉满。“我……我想是因为最近它比较……软。自从……那个，那场在货车里的‘教学’之后。我里面……里面还没恢复好，可能还在漏点什么！”

The room erupted. Fujiwara was howling so hard he fell off his chair, while Miyetsuko was pounding the floor, gasping for air. Even Sayuri, despite her own embarrassment, was doubled over, tears streaming down her face. Rin just sat there, blushing deeper, genuinely confused by why her anatomical honesty was such a comedic masterpiece.

房间炸锅了。藤原笑得从椅子上滚了下来，美悦子拍着地板疯狂喘气。连小百合也笑得捂住肚子直不起腰，眼泪横流。而铃就那么坐在那儿，脸红得像要滴血，对于自己的生理坦诚竟成了喜剧巅峰而感到无比困惑。

[Learning Point]

Degenerate: 堕落者/变态。

Peer review: 同行评议（此处为幽默借用）。

Oblivious: 不知情的/无视的。

Scarlet: 绯红。

Anatomical: 解剖学的/生理构造的。
