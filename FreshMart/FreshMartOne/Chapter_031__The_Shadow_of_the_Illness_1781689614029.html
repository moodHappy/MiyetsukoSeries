Chapter 31: The Shadow of the Illness

发布时间：2026/6/17 17:46:52

第三十一章：病痛的阴影

The diagnosis hit like a silent, suffocating weight. Cancer—a word that felt entirely out of place in their dark, controlled little world. The clinical brightness of the oncology ward was a stark, terrifying reality that none of their rituals could fend off. When they returned to "Fresh Mart," the space felt cavernous, the shadows deeper than before.

诊断书像一道无声的窒息感重重砸下。癌症——这个词在他们阴暗、被精心掌控的小世界里显得如此荒诞。肿瘤科病房那刺眼的明亮，成了一种无论他们做多少仪式都无法抵御的现实。当他们回到“新鲜马特”时，店面仿佛变得空洞无比，阴影比以往任何时候都要深邃。

Fujiwara’s demeanor shifted overnight. The hunger, the predatory drive, and the cold ambition were now channeled entirely into a fierce, protective tenderness. He moved with a quiet, efficient grace, organizing her medicine, adjusting her pillows, and watching her with a gaze that held a depth of sorrow he refused to name.

📌 Key Expressions

  • demeanor = 举止；神态；整体表现出来的态度和气质
  • shift overnight = 一夜之间发生转变
  • shifted overnight = 一夜之间改变了；overnight 强调变化非常迅速
  • hunger = 渴望；欲望；这里是强烈的欲求
  • predatory drive = 掠夺性的驱动力；捕食者般的欲望
  • cold ambition = 冷酷的野心；不带感情色彩、强烈追求目标的野心
  • be channeled into... = 被引导／转化到……上
  • channel A into B = 将 A 引导到 B 上；把某种能量、情绪转化为另一种行为
  • entirely into... = 完全转向……；全部用于……
  • fierce, protective tenderness = 强烈而具有保护性的温柔
  • protective tenderness = 带有保护意味的温柔
  • move with... grace = 以……的姿态／方式行动
  • quiet, efficient grace = 安静而利落的从容举止
  • organize her medicine = 整理她的药物
  • adjust her pillows = 调整她的枕头
  • watch sb. with a gaze... = 用某种目光注视某人
  • a gaze that held... = 一道目光中蕴含着……
  • hold a depth of sorrow = 蕴含深切的悲伤
  • a depth of sorrow = 深沉的悲伤；强调悲伤程度很深
  • refuse to do sth. = 拒绝做某事；不愿做某事
  • refuse to name sth. = 不愿说出／承认某事的名称或性质
  • sorrow he refused to name = 他拒绝说破的悲伤；he refused to name 修饰 sorrow
  • organizing..., adjusting..., and watching... = 三个现在分词并列，描述他行动时连续进行的几个动作
  • the hunger, the predatory drive, and the cold ambition were... = 三个名词并列作主语，后面统一接 were channeled

藤原的态度一夜间发生了转变。那份掠夺的欲望、冷酷的野心，此刻全部化作了一种激烈而隐忍的保护欲。他动作轻巧而高效，为她打理药物，调整枕头，看向她的眼神里藏着一种他拒绝宣之于口的深切哀恸。

One afternoon, the light filtered through the dusty curtains, illuminating the three of them—a strange, quiet family bound by secrets. Miyetsuko was resting on the cot, her skin paler, her strength waning, but her spirit remained sharp. She reached out, her hand finding Sayuri’s, pulling the nurse closer to the bedside.

午后，阳光穿过尘封的窗帘，照在他们三人身上——这是一个被秘密紧紧捆绑在一起的、奇特的家庭。美悦子躺在小床上，肤色愈发苍白，精力日渐衰竭，但那股心气儿却依然锋利。她伸出手，抓住了小百合的手，将这位护士拉近床边。

"You know, Sayuri," Miyetsuko began, her voice raspy but steady, "Fujiwara and I... we share a strange, deep obsession with your body. Specifically, that wild, beautiful velvet you hide beneath your clothes." She smiled, a faint, nostalgic curve of her lips. "We both love it. It’s the perfect mark of who you are."

“你知道吗，小百合，”美悦子开口道，嗓音沙哑却平稳，“我和藤原……我们对你的身体有着一种怪异而深层的迷恋。特别是你衣服下藏着的那片狂野、美妙的绒毛。”她露出一抹淡淡的、带着怀念的微笑，“我们都爱它。那是你最完美的标志。”

Sayuri flushed deeply, looking between the two of them, her eyes wide with a mix of submission and devotion.

小百合的脸瞬间红透了，在两人之间来回看着，眼中满是顺从与虔诚。

Miyetsuko’s gaze shifted to Fujiwara, then back to the nurse, her tone turning intimate and instructional. "He has his habits, you know. When it comes to pleasure, he has a favorite way of being satisfied—he loves it when he is being worshipped orally, especially when the tongue is used to stir and swirl, creating a rhythm that pulls everything out of him."

美悦子的目光转向藤原，随后又回到小百合身上，语气变得私密且带有指导意味。“他有些习惯，你知道的。当谈到愉悦时，他最喜欢的方式——他钟情于那种被虔诚奉献的感觉，尤其是边吸吮边搅动舌头，那种节奏能把他所有的理智都勾出来。”

She squeezed Sayuri’s hand, her expression softening into something almost motherly, though it remained tainted by their dark bond. "I’m not going to be able to be here for him like I used to, not with this... eating away at me. So, I’m going to need you to take over, Sayuri. I need you to serve him, to watch over him, and to give him all the devotion I can no longer provide. Take care of our man, little sister."

她捏了捏小百合的手，表情柔和得近乎慈母，尽管那柔情中依然掺杂着他们那层黑暗的羁绊。“我不能再像从前那样陪伴他了，这病……正在吞噬我。所以，小百合，我需要你接手。你需要侍奉他，照顾他，给予他我已无法再提供的全部虔诚。照顾好我们的男人，妹妹。”

Fujiwara stood by the window, his back to them, his shoulders tensing as the weight of the request settled over the room. Sayuri looked at Miyetsuko, then at Fujiwara’s rigid back, and slowly bowed her head in total, silent agreement.

藤原站在窗边，背对着她们，随着这番话语在屋里沉淀，他的肩膀紧绷起来。小百合看了看美悦子，又看向藤原那僵硬的背影，随后缓缓低下了头，给出了无声却绝对的承诺。

[Learning Point]

Cavernous: 洞穴般的/空洞的。

Demeanor: 风度/态度。

Wan: 虚弱的/苍白的。

Nostalgic: 怀旧的。

Devotion: 虔诚/奉献。
