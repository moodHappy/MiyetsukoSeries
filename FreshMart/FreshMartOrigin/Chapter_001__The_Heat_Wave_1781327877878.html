Chapter 1: The Heat Wave

发布时间：2026/6/13 13:17:45

第一章：热浪

The back office was suffocating. The air conditioner had died again. Lin Miyetsuko fanned herself with a thick stack of invoices, her patience wearing thin.

后台办公室闷热得令人窒息。空调又罢工了。林美悦子用一叠厚厚的发票给自己扇风，耐心即将耗尽。

"Chen Mo!" she snapped. "Is the repair guy coming or not?"

“陈默！”她厉声道。“维修工到底来不来？”

The intern peeked from behind a pile of flyers. "He said he’s stuck in traffic, Sister Miyetsuko."

实习生从传单堆后面探出头。“他说他堵在路上了，美悦子姐。”

The door swung open. Fujiwara walked in, looking far too cool for this sweltering room.

门被推开了。藤原走了进来，在这个酷热的房间里，他看起来冷静得有些过分。

"Running a sauna, are we?" he teased, glancing at the sweat on her forehead.

“我们这是在开桑拿房吗？”他调侃道，瞥了一眼她额头上的汗珠。

Miyetsuko glared at him. "Not now, Fujiwara. We have two hundred boxes of dumplings arriving in ten minutes, and the loading dock is blocked."

美悦子瞪了他一眼。“现在别闹，藤原。十分钟后有两百箱水饺送到，卸货区被堵住了。”

Fujiwara stopped smiling. He glanced at the broken forklift near the dock.

藤原收起了笑容。他看了一眼卸货区旁边那辆坏掉的叉车。

"That's a disaster," he muttered.

“那真是场灾难，”他低语道。

[Learning Point]

Wear thin: (耐心、借口等) 逐渐耗尽 / 变薄。例：My patience is wearing thin. (我的耐心快没了。)

Snap: (急躁地) 说 / 厉声说。比 say 更具情绪张力。
