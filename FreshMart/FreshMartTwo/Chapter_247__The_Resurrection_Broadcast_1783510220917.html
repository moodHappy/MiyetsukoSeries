Chapter 247: The Resurrection Broadcast

发布时间：2026/7/8 19:30:19

第二百四十七章：复活的直播

The silence of Donglai Island was beginning to grate on them. It was a sterile, curated silence. Every evening at 6 PM, the simulated sunset hit the library windows with the exact same orange hue as it had in the old manor. It was maddeningly perfect.

东莱岛的寂静开始让他们感到磨人。那是一种无菌的、精心策划的寂静。每天傍晚六点，模拟的夕阳都会以和老庄园一模一样的橙色光晕洒在图书馆窗台上。这种完美简直令人发疯。

Miyetsuko sat at the center of the room, looking at the mainland news feed on a massive, high-definition screen. The anchors were mourning her "tragedy" while simultaneously mocking her policies. She took a slow sip of wine. "They really are creative with their insults, aren't they?" she mused, gesturing to the screen where her own face—a blurred, unflattering photo—was displayed next to the word Traitor.

美悦子坐在房间中央，看着巨型高清屏幕上的大陆新闻流。主播们一边哀悼她的“惨剧”，一边嘲讽她的政策。她慢悠悠地抿了一口酒。“他们对侮辱我的辞藻还挺有创造力的，不是吗？”她指着屏幕，那里正显示着她自己的脸——一张模糊且丑陋的照片，旁边印着“叛徒”二字。

"They’re missing the irony, Miyetsuko," Fujiwara said, his hand resting carelessly on Sayuri’s thigh. He was drunk, eyes glassy, watching the political punditry with a twisted grin. "If you’re a traitor, then they’re just the scavengers picking at the carcass. You should tell them that. Let’s do a live broadcast."

“他们没发现这其中的讽刺意味，美悦子，”藤原的手漫不经心地搭在小百合的大腿上。他醉意朦胧，眼神涣散，带着扭曲的笑容看着那些政治评论员。“如果你是叛徒，那他们就只是在啃食尸体的秃鹫。你应该告诉他们这一点。咱们来搞个直播吧。”

Sayuri giggled, leaning back against the cushions. She had been darning the maid's skirt—the clone maid was currently kneeling at her feet, naked, serving as a footrest. "A live broadcast? While we’re 'dead'? That would be scandalous. I love it."

小百合咯咯笑着，向后靠在垫子上。她刚才一直在补那条女仆的裙子——那个克隆女仆此刻正赤身裸体地跪在她的脚下，当做脚垫。“直播？在我们‘死’了之后？那肯定很劲爆。我喜欢。”

Rin laughed, spinning a dagger on the table. "Do it. Hack the signal. It’s not like they have the tech to trace us through the encrypted satellite uplink on this island. Let’s see the look on their faces when the 'ghost' starts talking back."

铃笑了笑，在桌上转动着一把匕首。“干吧。黑进信号。反正他们也没那个技术能通过这座岛上的加密卫星上行链路追踪到我们。真想看看当‘幽灵’开口反击时，他们那副见鬼的表情。”

Miyetsuko’s eyes lit up—not with warmth, but with the cold spark of a predator finding a new game. "Fujiwara, get the comms array ready. Rin, find me the most provocative outfit I have. If I’m to be a ghost, I’m going to be the most beautiful one they’ve ever seen."

美悦子的双眼亮了起来——不是那种温情，而是掠食者发现新游戏时那种冰冷的火花。“藤原，准备好通讯阵列。铃，给我找件我这儿最挑逗的衣服。既然我要当个幽灵，那我就要成为他们见过最美的那个。”

The preparation was effortless, almost ritualistic. Fujiwara didn't stop drinking; he just set the controls, his hands unsteady, occasionally pausing to reach down and roughly knead Sayuri’s breasts while she fed him grapes. The maid, the genetic mirror of the woman who had died on the manor floor, watched them with vacant, obedient eyes, waiting for the next command.

准备工作毫不费力，几乎成了一种仪式。藤原没有停止喝酒；他只是调试着控制台，手虽然不太稳，但偶尔会停下来，粗暴地揉捏小百合的乳房，而她则喂他吃着葡萄。那个女仆，那个死在庄园地板上的女人的基因镜像，用空洞而顺从的眼神看着他们，等待着下一个指令。

"You know," Sayuri whispered to Fujiwara, her voice wet and suggestive as she leaned in to lick his neck, "we’re acting out a play for people who think we don't exist. It makes the adrenaline feel... different."

“你知道吗，”小百合在藤原耳边低语，声音湿润且充满暗示，她凑过去舔了舔他的脖子，“我们正在给那些以为我们不存在的人演一场戏。这让肾上腺素的感觉……变得不太一样了。”

Miyetsuko returned, draped in a gown that left little to the imagination, her hair perfectly styled. She sat in front of the camera, looking less like a fugitive and more like a goddess reclaiming her throne. "Signal is live in ten seconds," she announced. She leaned forward, staring directly into the lens, her gaze piercing through the screen to the millions of people who were just about to have their reality shattered.

美悦子回来了，身上裹着一件几乎什么都遮不住的长袍，头发梳理得完美无瑕。她坐在摄像机前，看起来不像个逃亡者，倒像是个即将重登王座的女神。“十秒后信号连通，”她宣布道。她身体前倾，直视着镜头，那目光仿佛穿透了屏幕，直视着即将被击碎现实的那数百万人。

"Don't look so glum, everyone," she said, her voice smooth as silk. "Did you really think the sea could swallow the truth?"

“大家别那么垂头丧气嘛，”她说，声音柔滑如丝。“你们真以为大海能吞没真相吗？”
