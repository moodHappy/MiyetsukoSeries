第一百七十八章：未删减的王座

The studio was bathed in neon pink and deep violet. "Midnight Unfiltered" was the nation’s most notorious late-night talk show, known for blurring the line between celebrity gossip and raw, X-rated truth. The host, a sleazy guy named Rex, leaned forward, his eyes glinting. "We have the Royal Family—or at least, the working parts of it."

演播室浸润在霓虹粉与深紫色的暧昧灯光中。“午夜直白”是国内最臭名昭著的深夜脱口秀，以模糊名人八卦与大尺度真相的边界而闻名。主持人雷克斯身体前倾，眼中闪烁着八卦的精光，“今晚我们请到了皇室成员——或者说，皇室最‘好用’的那几位。”

"Sayuri, let's start with the hard questions," Rex smirked, gesturing to the Queen. "Balancing the crown and the casting couch. How do you do it? Is the scepter a good substitute for... other things?"

“小百合，咱们先来点劲爆的，”雷克斯坏笑着，指了指女王，“王冠和片场，你是怎么平衡的？那个权杖，是不是比……其他东西好用？”

Sayuri giggled, crossing her legs. The audience let out a collective gasp as the slit of her dress revealed a dangerous amount of skin. "It’s all about time management, Rex. Honestly, the parliament is boring, but the film sets are efficient. The biggest difference? On the throne, you have to keep your legs together for diplomatic etiquette. In a scene, well... let’s just say I get a lot more exercise for my glutes."

小百合咯咯笑着，换了个二郎腿。裙摆的高开叉让现场观众集体倒吸一口凉气。“雷克斯，这完全取决于时间管理。说真的，议会无聊透顶，但片场效率高啊。最大的区别？在王座上，为了外交礼仪，你得把腿并拢；但在片场嘛……咱们就说，我的臀肌能得到更好的锻炼。”

The audience roared with laughter.

现场哄堂大笑。

"And Rin," Rex turned, tapping his notepad. "You’re the Acting Queen. You’re the one dealing with the bureaucrats while she’s getting drilled on camera. What’s the psyche? Do you hate it, or are you secretly addicted to the power?"

“还有铃，”雷克斯转过身，点了点笔记本，“你是代理女王。她在镜头前被干的时候，你在替她对付那些官僚。什么心态？你恨这差事，还是暗地里对权力上瘾？”

Rin took a slow sip of her whiskey, looking unimpressed. "It’s just high-stakes roleplay, Rex. Sitting there, looking all regal, while some idiot drone talks about fiscal policy? I just imagine I’m the one dominating them in a BDSM dungeon. Keeps me from slapping them. The power trip is... yeah, it’s addictive. But let’s be real, the best part of being Acting Queen isn't the policy; it’s the diplomatic immunity. I can basically tell anyone to go fuck themselves and they just call it 'eccentric charm'."

铃慢悠悠地喝了口威士忌，一脸淡漠。“这不过是高风险的角色扮演，雷克斯。坐在那儿装模作样，听着某个蠢货唠叨财政政策？我就想象自己在地下室里狠狠调教他们。这能防止我给他们两耳光。权力确实让人上瘾……但说真的，当代理女王最好的部分不是政策，而是外交豁免权。我基本可以随便叫谁去吃屎，他们还得管这叫‘古怪的魅力’。”

Rex turned to Fujiwara, who was sitting comfortably, looking like he’d just come from an orgy and a board meeting simultaneously. "Fujiwara, you’re the man in the middle. You’ve fucked the Queen and you’ve fucked the Acting Queen. Give us a comparison. What’s the difference in the sheets?"

雷克斯转向藤原，他正舒适地靠着，看起来像是刚开完董事会又刚参加完群交派对。“藤原，你是中间人。你睡过女王，也睡过代理女王。给我们对比一下。在床上有什么不同？”

The studio went silent.

演播室一片死寂。

Fujiwara laughed, adjusting his cufflinks. "They’re different genres, Rex. Sayuri? She’s like a big-budget blockbuster. High production value, plenty of theatrics, lots of noise, and she loves it when you use the 'props.' She wants the fantasy. She wants you to be the director, the cameraman, and the antagonist all at once."

藤原笑了，整了整袖扣。“这是两种类型的片子，雷克斯。小百合？她就像那种大制作的好莱坞大片。制作精良，戏剧性强，动静大，而且她喜欢你用各种‘道具’。她要的是那种幻想感。她希望你既是导演，又是摄影师，还得是片子里的反派。”

He glanced at Rin, who was rolling her eyes.

他瞥了一眼正在翻白眼的铃。

"Now, Rin... she’s like a hard-boiled noir thriller," Fujiwara continued, grinning. "It’s gritty. It’s dark. No props, no theater. It’s just raw, angry, and competitive. With Sayuri, I’m the star. With Rin, it’s a knife fight. She tries to dominate, I push back, and we usually end up breaking the furniture. She’s the only one who doesn't care about the 'Queen' persona; she just wants to know if I can keep up with her teeth."

“至于铃……她就像那种硬汉黑色惊悚片，”藤原咧嘴笑道，“粗砺，黑暗。没道具，没排场。只有原始的愤怒和竞争。跟小百合在一起，我是主角；跟铃在一起，那是场肉搏战。她想压制我，我反击，通常我们最后都会把家具拆了。她是唯一一个不在乎什么‘女王’人设的人，她只想知道我能不能受得了她的牙口。”

Rin smirked, leaning over to Fujiwara. "You forgot to mention the part where I win."

铃坏笑着凑到藤原耳边。“你忘了提我赢的那一部分了。”

The audience lost their minds. It was the most viral segment in the history of the show.

观众彻底疯了。这成了该节目历史上点击量最高的片段。

[Learning Point]

The "Meta" Reality: “元”现实。将她们的生活直接搬上脱口秀，这是一种极其高明的自我解构。通过公开谈论性癖和权力欲望，她们让公众觉得“这家人没什么好隐瞒的”，从而把“丑闻”变成了“坦诚”。

Comparison as Characterization: 通过性行为来刻画人物。Fujiwara 对两人的对比（大片 vs. 黑色电影）极其精准地总结了她们的性格——Sayuri 是为了表演而活的“空洞偶像”，而 Rin 是充满竞争意识的“暗黑政客”。

The Currency of Scandal: 丑闻的价值。在这个时代，隐私已经不再是私有财产，而是交换的货币。她们越是把这些私密的话题拿出来公开讨论，她们就越显得“真实”和“强大”，进而进一步巩固了她们的统治基础。
