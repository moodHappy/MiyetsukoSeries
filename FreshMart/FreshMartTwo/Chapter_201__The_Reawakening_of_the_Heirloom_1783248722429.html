Chapter 201: The Reawakening of the Heirloom

发布时间：2026/7/5 18:52:00

第二百零一章：传家宝的复苏

Sayuri and Rin retrieved Fujiwara from the nursing home. To their surprise, he didn't look like a broken man anymore; he was radiant, brimming with a vitality that seemed to have been bottled up for months. Rin ran a hand over his shoulder, a playful glint in her eyes. "It seems the mountains and rivers really nourished you," she said, her voice dripping with mock-seriousness. Once they got him home, he didn't just behave like a man; he dominated. Within the hour, he had utterly defeated them, leaving the two Queens sprawled on the bed like two piles of wet clay, completely spent.

小百合和铃把藤原从养老院接了回来。出乎意料的是，他看起来完全不像个废人，反而精神焕发，那种消失已久的精力又回到了他身上。铃顺手抚摸着他的肩膀，眼里闪烁着戏谑的光芒。“看来山水之间确实把你滋养得不错，”她阴阳怪气地调侃道。回到家，他一刻没耽误，展现出了惊人的雄风。不到一个小时，他就彻底征服了这两位女王，把她们折腾得瘫在床上，像两团烂泥一样动弹不得。

Miyetsuko returned from a grueling diplomatic visit. She felt lethargic after the long flight, her head spinning with trade agreements and the crushing weight of jet lag. The moment she stepped into the sanctuary of her bedroom, Fujiwara emerged from the shadows like a predator. He caught her in a surprise embrace, pulling her into a rhythm that she hadn't expected. Three hours later, the Prime Minister of the nation was just as incapacitated as her sisters, lying still with a smile plastered across her face.

美悦子结束了漫长的外交访问回国。长途飞行后她感到昏昏沉沉，脑子里全是贸易协定和时差带来的眩晕。就在她刚踏入卧室的避风港时，藤原像个捕食者一样从阴影里窜了出来。他打了一个措手不及的突袭，把她卷入了一场意料之外的缠绵。三小时后，这位一国首相也和那两人一样彻底瘫软，瘫成了一坨烂泥，脸上却挂着满足的微笑。

The massive king-sized bed had become a glistening river, staining the silk sheets with the mingled fluids of all three of them. They lay there in the wreckage, limbs tangled, breathing heavily in the quiet room. It was a scene of domestic absurdity, yet they were all grinning, looking perfectly content with the carnage.

这张巨大的床上宛如一条天河，流淌着三人交织的体液，把丝绸床单浸得湿透。他们躺在狼藉的“战场”中，四肢交缠，在安静的屋子里大口喘息。这是一幅荒诞的家庭图景，但他们都咧着嘴，在这片混乱中显得格外幸福。

"Well," Rin gasped, struggling to catch her breath as she propped herself up on an elbow. "I guess we don't need to replace the heirloom after all. He’s clearly got some life left in him."

“好吧，”铃喘着气，挣扎着撑起手肘，“看来我们确实不需要换掉这个传家宝了。他这家伙显然还有大把的精力。”

Sayuri laughed, brushing a strand of hair from her sweat-dampened forehead. "Some life? He’s been hiding this 'forestry' training from us! Who knew the nursing home was actually a training camp for gladiators?"

小百合笑得花枝乱颤，拨开额头上被汗水浸湿的头发。“何止是精力？他瞒着我们练了什么‘林业’技术！谁能想到那养老院竟然是个角斗士训练营？”

Miyetsuko lay in the center, eyes closed, a look of pure laziness on her face. "Whatever they fed him out there, keep doing it. I’ve got a war to run, and honestly, having a personal masseur who can work out all the tension in three hours flat? That’s the best domestic policy I’ve enacted all year."

美悦子躺在中间，闭着眼睛，一脸纯粹的懒散。“不管他们在外面喂了他什么，继续给他吃。我还有一场战争要指挥呢，说实话，有个能在三小时内把全身疲劳按散的私人按摩师，这绝对是我今年推行过最好的内政。”
