Chapter 221: The Malignant Mandate

发布时间：2026/7/6 07:57:22

第二百二十一章：恶性的命令

Miyetsuko sat at the head of the long, mahogany table, her eyes fixed on the maritime charts. "Whaling has been restricted for many years," she announced, her voice vibrating with a dangerous authority. "That moratorium ends today. I want the harpoons sharpened." The room went cold. The issue is highly contentious, a geopolitical suicide note that would earn them global condemnation, but Miyetsuko didn't care about the world. Her ministers, sweating through their expensive suits, tried to steer the ship away from the iceberg. "Prime Minister, please," one urged, trembling. "Perhaps we could pivot to something more... manageable? We could focus on the lucrative shrimp industry instead." Miyetsuko looked at him as if he had just suggested she trade her crown for a rubber duck. The suggestion was ludicrous.

美悦子坐在长长的红木桌首位，目光紧锁在海图上。“捕鲸业已经被限制多年了，”她宣布道，声音里透着危险的权威，“那种暂停令今天就结束。我要把鱼叉磨得锋利些。”房间里顿时凉了下来。这个问题引起了极大的争议，那是一封注定会招致全球谴责的政治自杀信，但美悦子根本不在乎世界怎么看。部长们汗透了昂贵的西装，试图让这艘船避开冰山。“首相，请三思，”一人颤抖着劝道，“或许我们可以转向一些……更易于管理的产业？比如，我们可以转而专注于利润丰厚的捕虾业。”美悦子看着他，眼神仿佛在看一个建议她用王冠去换只橡皮鸭的白痴。这个建议简直荒谬至极。

The tension in the room was cut short when her private physician entered, looking pale and grim. He handed her a folder, his hands shaking slightly. The biopsy results were back. There was no delicate way to put it: The tumor was found to be malignant. Miyetsuko didn't blink. She simply placed the folder on top of the whaling directive, signed the document with a flourish, and dismissed the doctor.

房间里的紧张气氛被她私人医生的闯入打断，他脸色苍白，表情凝重。他递给她一个文件夹，手微微颤抖。活检结果出来了。没有任何委婉的说法：肿瘤被发现是恶性的。美悦子连眼睛都没眨一下。她只是把文件夹压在捕鲸令上，挥笔签下了那份文件，然后挥手让医生退下。

Back at the manor, the family was enjoying an evening of casual gossip, completely oblivious to the terminal shadow hanging over the table.

庄园里，一家人正享受着随意的八卦之夜，完全没意识到餐桌上方那即将到来的死亡阴影。

"Shrimp!" Rin burst out laughing, spilling her drink. "Can you imagine? The Tsar of the seas, reduced to a trawler captain chasing tiny pink crustaceans? That minister is lucky he wasn't turned into chum for the whales."

“捕虾！”铃爆发出一阵大笑，洒了杯中的酒。“你能想象吗？大海的沙皇，沦落为一个追逐小粉虾的拖网渔船船长？那个部长没被丢进海里喂鲸鱼，真是走运。”

Sayuri wiped her eyes, giggling. "And Miyetsuko’s reaction was priceless. She looked at him as if he’d suggested we start a knitting circle for the navy. Though, I suppose if the tumor is as bad as the rumors suggest, she doesn't have time for a shrimp farm."

小百合擦掉眼角的笑泪，咯咯笑着。“美悦子的反应真是无价。她看他那眼神，就好像他建议海军去搞个编织俱乐部一样。不过，我想如果那个肿瘤真像传闻中那么糟糕，她确实没时间去搞什么虾场了。”

Fujiwara paused, his glass halfway to his lips. "Malignant, is it? Well, that explains the sudden hunger for whales. If you’re going to be eaten from the inside, you might as well spend your final months eating the biggest creatures on the planet."

藤原停下动作，酒杯举到唇边。“是恶性肿瘤吗？嗯，那倒是能解释她为什么突然对鲸鱼这么渴望了。反正都要被体内的东西吞噬了，不如在最后几个月里，把这个星球上最大的生物吃进肚子里。”

Miyetsuko walked into the room, her stride as steady as ever, though she moved with a new, icy focus. She heard the last of their chatter and offered a thin, sharp smile. "Don't look so somber. A malignant tumor is just a little extra ambition growing where it doesn't belong. It’s like the whaling ban—I’ve decided that if I’m going down, the entire ecosystem is coming with me. Now, who wants some whale sashimi?"

美悦子走进房间，步伐依然稳健，只是带上了一种新的、冰冷的专注。她听到了他们谈话的尾声，嘴角露出一抹削薄而锐利的笑。“别这么阴沉。恶性肿瘤不过是长错了地方的一点野心罢了。这就像那个捕鲸禁令一样——我决定了，如果我要完蛋，整个生态系统都得陪葬。现在，谁想来点鲸鱼刺身？”
