Chapter 225: The Tsar’s Highs and Lows

发布时间：2026/7/6 17:11:48

第二百二十五章：沙皇的烟雾与寒冬

The manor was practically vibrating with excess. Their extravagance knew no bounds, manifesting in a banquet so opulent it made the Roman emperors look like paupers. They had even hired a magician performing illusions on a central stage, his cards and doves serving as the perfect distraction for the guests. Amidst the spectacle, Miyetsuko sat at the head table, swirling her wine while deep in thought. She had been reading a report on the regional economic shifts. "Some countries have legalized marijuana for medical use," she muttered to Fujiwara. "If we push this forward, we could effectively monopolize the pharmaceutical supply chain and keep the restless masses sufficiently sedated. It’s an efficient sedative."

庄园里的奢侈几乎让空气都跟着震颤。他们的铺张浪费毫无节制，宴会的奢华程度让罗马皇帝看起来都像贫民。他们甚至请来了一位魔术师在中央舞台上表演戏法，他手里的扑克牌和鸽子成了宾客们绝佳的掩护。在这场奇观中，美悦子坐在主桌，晃动着红酒杯陷入了沉思。她一直在看关于区域经济变动的报告。“有些国家已经将大麻用于医疗用途合法化了，”她对藤原喃喃自语。“如果我们推进这项政策，就能有效地垄断医药供应链，让躁动不安的大众保持足够的镇定。这是一种高效的镇静剂。”

But the revelry was interrupted by a flash update on the wall-mounted monitors. A catastrophic natural disaster had struck the northern territory; an avalanche buried the village in a matter of seconds. Miyetsuko didn't even drop her glass. She simply signaled for her puppet Prime Minister, Yuto, to be deployed to the disaster zone immediately for the optics.

然而，狂欢被墙上监控的一则紧急快讯打断了。北部地区发生了一场毁灭性的自然灾害；一场雪崩瞬间掩埋了整个村庄。美悦子甚至连酒杯都没晃一下。她只是挥挥手，示意她的傀儡首相悠斗立刻前往灾区，好博取点民心。

Yuto, lacking any sense of direction or initiative, had been forced to rely on the most readily available transport: a rugged, unsavory driver he’d flagged down near the docks. The trucker took him there, grumbling all the way about the cost of fuel and the incompetence of politicians, while Yuto sat in the passenger seat, sweating through his expensive suit.

悠斗毫无方向感，也没有任何主见，只能依赖手头最方便的交通工具：他在码头附近随手拦下了一名粗犷、油腻的司机。货车司机载着他前往灾区，一路上都在抱怨油价和政客的无能，而悠斗则坐在副驾驶座上，昂贵的西装被汗水浸透。

Back in the living room, the family watched the grainy news footage of Yuto stumbling out of a dirty semi-truck, looking utterly lost in the snow.

客厅里，一家人看着电视里播放的模糊新闻画面：悠斗从一辆肮脏的半挂货车里踉踉跄跄地走出来，在雪地里显得茫然失措。

Rin cackled, clutching a piece of crystal-encrusted cake. "Look at him! He didn't even get a government vehicle. He looks like he’s hitchhiking to his own doom. The trucker took him there like he was delivering a load of scrap metal."

铃发出了尖利的笑声，手里还抓着一块镶满水晶的蛋糕。“看他！他连辆政府公车都没混上。他看起来就像是在搭顺风车去送死。那货车司机载他过去的样子，就像是在送一车废铁。”

Sayuri wiped her eyes, leaning against Fujiwara. "And the magician back at the banquet is still pulling rabbits out of hats, while the Prime Minister is being delivered by a trucker. The contrast is simply delicious. I hope the villagers appreciate the quality of their government aid."

小百合擦掉眼角的泪水，靠在藤原身上。“宴会那边的魔术师还在变兔子，而首相竟然是靠货车司机送过去的。这种反差简直太美妙了。希望那些村民能体会到他们政府援助的‘高质量’。”

Fujiwara smirked, pouring himself another drink. "I’m sure the villagers are thrilled. If the avalanche didn't finish them off, the sight of Yuto climbing out of a truck cab in that ridiculous tie certainly will. And Miyetsuko? She’s still debating medical marijuana. Truly, this country’s priorities are the funniest thing I’ve ever seen."

藤原冷笑着，给自己又倒了一杯酒。“我相信村民们一定会很兴奋的。如果雪崩没要了他们的命，看到悠斗系着那条滑稽的领带从卡车驾驶室里爬出来，绝对会把他们送走。至于美悦子？她还在讨论医疗大麻呢。真的，这个国家的优先事项是我见过最好笑的东西。”

Miyetsuko, finally turning her attention away from her phone, offered a cold, indifferent smile. "Let them laugh. While Yuto is playing delivery cargo, I’m busy deciding how to tax the medical marijuana supply. If the village is buried, at least they won't complain about the cost of the medicine, will they?"

美悦子终于把注意力从手机上移开，露出了一个冰冷、漠然的微笑。“让他们笑吧。当悠斗在玩送货服务的时候，我正忙着决定该怎么给医疗大麻供应征税呢。如果那个村子都被埋了，至少他们不会抱怨药价太贵了，对吧？”
