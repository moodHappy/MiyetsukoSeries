Chapter 193: The Dynamite Diplomacy

发布时间：2026/7/5 17:18:24

第一百九十三章：炸药外交

The nation was abundant in natural resources, a treasure trove buried under miles of dirt, just waiting for the right tyrant to extract it. Miyetsuko didn't waste time; she launched a massive "Great Development" campaign, planning to ship every ounce of raw material abroad to fill the national treasury and, naturally, her own pockets.

这个国家自然资源极其丰富，宝藏埋在地下几英里深处，只等着合适的暴君来开采。美悦子没有浪费时间，她发起了一场大规模的“大开发”运动，计划把每一盎司原材料都销往国外，以此来填补国库，当然，也顺便填补她自己的腰包。

The target was clear. The factory was located on the periphery of the city, sitting directly atop a massive mineral vein. To clear the land, she didn't bother with eviction notices or legal mediation. The building was destroyed with dynamite in a spectacular, controlled blast that shook the suburban windows. They literally blew it to the ground, leaving nothing but twisted steel and dust.

目标很明确。那家工厂位于城市边缘，正好坐落在一处巨大的矿脉之上。为了腾出土地，她根本没心思发拆迁通知或进行法律调解。那栋建筑被炸药夷为平地，那场壮观的定向爆破震碎了郊区所有的窗户。他们真的把它炸成了平地，只剩下扭曲的钢筋和尘土。

The factory owner, a relic from the previous administration, rushed forward to protest, shouting about rights and regulations. Miyetsuko simply turned and gave a scornful look, a silent command that sealed his fate. Before he could utter another word, the military police moved in and executed him on the spot.

那位工厂主，一个旧政权的遗老，冲上来抗议，高喊着权利和法规。美悦子只是转过身，投去了一个轻蔑的眼神，那是一个决定他命运的无声命令。他还没来得及说出下一个词，宪兵就冲上去当场枪毙了他。

The decision had many ramifications, but none of them were the kind the owner had hoped for. Instead of outrage, the public cheered. They had suffered under the factory’s pollution for years, and seeing a "dog of the old regime" disposed of without a trial was pure catharsis. Her approval ratings didn't just stabilize; they soared.

这个决定产生了许多影响，但没有一个是工厂主所期望的那种。民众不仅没有愤怒，反而欢呼雀跃。他们忍受这家工厂的污染已经多年，看着一个“旧政权的走狗”被不经审判就地正法，对他们来说简直是纯粹的宣泄。她的支持率不降反升，简直是火箭式蹿升。

Meanwhile, the international front was just as profitable. Miyetsuko met with the foreign head of state, a man who cared more about lithium than human rights. "We had a pleasant converse," she told the cameras later, swirling her drink. The mineral trade agreement was signed, sealed, and delivered—a perfect, blood-stained deal.

与此同时，国际战线上的收获同样丰厚。美悦子会见了外国元首，那是个比人权更在乎锂矿的男人。“我们进行了一次愉快的交谈，”她事后对着镜头一边摇晃着酒杯一边说道。矿产协议签署完毕，大功告成——一份完美、沾满鲜血的合同。

That night, the family gathered in the living room, relaxed and sipping fine wine. "You killed him so casually," Fujiwara remarked, scrolling through the positive news reports. "I love how the media called him an 'environmental hazard' rather than a businessman."

那天晚上，一家人聚在客厅里，放松地品着红酒。“你杀他的时候可真随意，”藤原翻看着积极的报道说道，“我太喜欢媒体把他称为‘环境危害’而不是商人的说法了。”

Rin laughed, kicking her heels up. "The plebs are easy to please. Give them a villain to hate, and they'll ignore the fact that we're selling the whole country out from under their feet."

铃笑着，踢了踢高跟鞋。“平民真是好打发。给他们一个可以恨的反派，他们就会忽略我们正把整个国家从他们脚下卖掉的事实。”

Sayuri nodded, looking at her reflection in the wine glass. "Just keep the mineral checks coming. As long as the champagne is cold and the world thinks we're 'resource-developing patriots,' I don't care if we have to blow up every factory in the city."

小百合点点头，看着红酒杯里的倒影。“只要矿产支票源源不断地送来就行。只要香槟够冰，世界觉得我们是‘开发资源的爱国者’，哪怕要把城里所有的工厂都炸了，我也无所谓。”
