Chapter 204: The Noble Imposter

发布时间：2026/7/5 19:17:38

第二百零四章：高贵的冒牌货

Miyetsuko stared at the war maps, facing a period of unprecedented adversity. Her generals were becoming increasingly pessimistic about the supply lines, but she refused to yield to their panic. She preferred to focus on the home front, where the real "conquest" was happening.

美悦子盯着作战地图，正面临着前所未有的逆境。她的将军们对补给线的情况变得越来越悲观，但她拒绝向恐慌屈服。她更愿意把精力集中在大后方，因为真正的“征服”正在那里上演。

She looked at the new Fujiwara—a mere imitation of her original husband. He had completely renounced his former identity as a brilliant astronomer to play this part. He was a counterfeit spouse, a puppet wearing a borrowed face, yet he was proving to be a perfect addition to their twisted domestic arrangement.

她看向那个新的藤原——不过是她原本丈夫的一个仿制品。他已经彻底放弃了作为杰出天文学家的前身，来扮演这个角色。他是个伪劣品，一个戴着借来的面孔的木偶，但他被证明是她们那扭曲的家庭结构中完美的补充。

As for the breeding program, a fledgling branch of the organization was looking for a new intermediary to manage the intake of "volunteers." One finalist for the position had recently emerged—a man who claimed to have noble ancestry, boasting of royal bloodlines that were clearly nothing more than a convenient fabrication.

至于繁育计划，该组织的一个初创分支机构正在寻找一位新的中间人来管理“志愿者”的接收工作。该职位的一位决赛选手最近脱颖而出——一个声称自己有高贵祖先的男人，吹嘘着那些显然不过是权宜之计的编造出来的皇室血统。

Rin laughed, tossing a file onto the coffee table. "He’s a joke. He thinks if he acts posh, we won't notice his shoes are falling apart. Do we really need an intermediary with such a pathetic ancestry?"

铃大笑着，把一份档案丢在咖啡桌上。“他简直是个笑话。他以为只要装得有派头，我们就看不出他的鞋子都要烂掉了吗？我们真的需要一个有着这种可怜祖先的中间人吗？”

Sayuri smirked, looking at Fujiwara, who was busy pouring their drinks with the precision of a trained servant. "Leave him be. It's funny. Besides, if we find a finalist who actually has common sense, he might start questioning why we're treating his 'noble' peers like cattle in the breeding rooms."

小百合冷笑着，看向正忙着为她们倒酒、动作精准得像个训练有素的仆人的藤原。“随他去吧。挺有意思的。再说，如果我们找个真正有常识的决赛选手，他没准儿会开始质疑，为什么我们要把他的那些‘高贵’同类像牲口一样关进配种室里。”

Miyetsuko took a sip of her wine, her expression unreadable. "Let the fledgling bureaucrat try his hand. If he fails, he's just another counterfeit we can discard. Life is an imitation of power, and he’s clearly just reading from a script he doesn't understand."

美悦子抿了一口红酒，表情让人捉摸不透。“让那个初出茅庐的官僚去试试吧。如果他搞砸了，他也不过是另一个我们可以随意丢弃的冒牌货。生活本身就是权力的模仿，而他显然只是在照本宣科，却根本读不懂剧本。”
