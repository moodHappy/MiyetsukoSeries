Chapter 246: The Resurrection of the Queen

发布时间：2026/7/7 01:09:15

第二百四十六章：女王的归来

A rhythmic, precise tapping sound echoed from the grand staircase—a sound so familiar it felt like a hallucination. Rin turned, her hand instinctively reaching for the empty space where the lookalike maid had been standing just a second ago. The girl had vanished into the shadows, leaving behind nothing but the scent of fading perfume.

楼梯上传来沉稳、有节奏的脚步声——那声音太熟悉了，熟悉到让人以为是幻觉。铃猛地转身，手下意识地伸向刚才那名女仆站立的地方，可那里空空如也。女孩不知何时已经消失在阴影里，只留下一缕即将散去的香水味。

The footsteps continued. Slowly, deliberately, a figure descended into the living room light.

脚步声还在继续。那个人影缓缓地、刻意地走进了客厅的灯光中。

Fujiwara, Rin, and Sayuri stood paralyzed, their jaws dropping in utter disbelief. Standing there, dry, immaculate, and wearing the same severe suit she had worn on the yacht, was Miyetsuko. She didn't look like a woman who had just survived a maritime disaster; she looked like a CEO walking out of a boardroom.

藤原、铃和小百合僵在原地，大跌眼镜，脸上满是不可置信的惊愕。站在那儿的，正是美悦子。她身上干爽、整洁，穿着她在游艇上时那套干练的西装。她看起来一点也不像个刚经历过海难的幸存者，倒像个刚刚结束董事会议、风度翩翩走出的女总裁。

"Miyetsuko?" Sayuri whispered, her voice trembling so violently it cracked. "How... you were pulled into the deep. We saw the water swallow you."

“美悦子？”小百合低声唤道，声音颤抖得几乎破碎，“怎么可能……你被卷进了深海。我们亲眼看着海水把你吞没。”

Miyetsuko didn't smile. She simply smoothed out a non-existent wrinkle on her sleeve and walked toward the wet bar to pour herself a drink. "The sea is a petty tyrant, much like the ones I used to overthrow," she said, her voice smooth and cold. "It thought it could bargain with my life using a few thousand tons of pressure and a bit of salt water."

美悦子没有笑。她只是漫不经心地抚平了袖口上并不存在的褶皱，径直走向吧台给自己倒了一杯酒。“大海是个卑劣的暴君，就像那些我曾经推翻过的独裁者一样，”她声音平缓而冰冷，“它以为能用几千吨的压力和一点咸水来跟我的命做交易。”

She took a sip, her gaze sweeping over her three stunned companions as if she were waiting for them to regain their composure. "When the wave hit, I didn't fight the current. That’s a amateur’s mistake. You don't fight the storm; you sink into it. I let the abyss take me. I held my breath, I closed my eyes, and I waited for the sea to realize that it couldn't digest me."

她抿了一口酒，目光扫过三个目瞪口呆的同伴，仿佛在等着他们恢复理智。“巨浪袭来时，我没有对抗洋流。那是业余选手的错误。你不能对抗风暴，你得沉入其中。我任由深渊吞没我。我屏住呼吸，闭上眼，等着大海意识到它根本消化不了我。”

"And then?" Fujiwara asked, his voice thick with a mix of awe and terror.

“然后呢？”藤原问道，声音里混杂着敬畏与恐惧。

"Then?" Miyetsuko laughed, a sharp, crystalline sound. "I simply swam. I found an underwater current, a vein of the earth that leads to the island's secret channels. It was cold, it was dark, and my lungs burned, but I have a kingdom to build. I refused to die in a place as vulgar as the middle of the ocean. My will was stronger than the pressure of the deep. That is the difference between a leader and the mob, isn't it?"

“然后？”美悦子笑了，那是阵尖锐而清脆的笑声，“我只是游了上来。我找到了一股海底暗流，那是通往这座岛屿秘密水道的地球脉络。水很冷，很黑，肺部火辣辣地疼，但我还得建立我的王国。我拒绝死在像大洋中心这种粗俗的地方。我的意志比深海的压力更强。这就是领袖与暴民的区别，不是吗？”

She walked over to them, the click of her heels on the floorboards ringing out like a gavel in a courtroom. She reached out and tilted Rin's chin up, staring into her eyes. "Did you miss me, Rin? Or were you already measuring the furniture for your own style?"

她走到他们面前，高跟鞋敲击地板的声音像法庭上的木槌一样清脆。她伸出手，挑起铃的下巴，直视着她的眼睛。“想我了吗，铃？还是说，你已经在按自己的风格重新布置家具了？”

Rin swallowed hard, unable to look away. "I... I think I'm still in shock."

铃艰难地咽了口唾沫，无法移开视线。“我……我想我还在震惊中。”

"Good," Miyetsuko said, turning back to the group. "Shock keeps you sharp. Now, stop trembling like cowards. We have a replica manor to maintain, a war to ignore, and a new godhead to establish. Pour me another drink, Fujiwara. The resurrection makes me thirsty."

“很好，”美悦子转身看向众人，“震惊能让你们保持清醒。现在，别像懦夫一样发抖了。我们要维护这座复刻的庄园，要无视那场战争，还要建立一个新的神格。给我倒酒，藤原。复活让我感到口渴。”
