Chapter 123: The Charity Performance

发布时间：2026/7/3 04:37:19

第一百二十三章：慈善表演

The black sedan pulled up to the local parish—a crumbling community center that smelled of damp concrete and cheap disinfectant. Miyetsuko adjusted her silk scarf, her expression turning from "business tyrant" to "concerned servant of the people" in a heartbeat. Beside her, Sayuri and Rin were vibrating with boredom, their designer handbags clutched like lifelines.

黑色轿车停在当地教区——一处散发着潮湿水泥和廉价消毒水味的破旧社区中心。美悦子整理着丝巾，表情瞬间从“商业暴君”切换到了“忧国忧民的公仆”。旁边，小百合和铃正无聊得浑身发痒，紧紧攥着名牌手袋，仿佛那是她们唯一的救命稻草。

"Listen to me," Miyetsuko hissed, her voice barely a whisper but sharp as a razor. "This isn't a party. People here have lost their homes. You don't talk about 'thrills,' you don't talk about 'old clients,' and for the love of god, keep your legs closed. We are here to look holy, not like we just walked out of a dive bar."

“都给我听好了，”美悦子压低声音，语气却锋利如刀，“这不是去派对。这里的人家破人亡。别给我提什么‘刺激’，别提什么‘老顾客’，还有，看在上帝的份上，把你们的腿给我管好。咱们是来装圣人的，不是来演那种烂酒吧出来的戏码的。”

Rin rolled her eyes, stepping out of the car and into the mud. "You're so dramatic, Miyetsuko. We just want to see how the other half lives. It’s been weeks since we saw anyone without a suit on."

铃翻了个白眼，踩着高跟鞋走进泥地里。“你太夸张了，美悦子。我们就是想看看这帮穷苦大众过得什么日子。咱们都几周没见过穿西装以外的人了。”

Inside, the parish was crowded with displaced families, children crying, and old men staring blankly at the walls. Miyetsuko immediately began her routine, shaking hands, nodding solemnly, and whispering platitudes about "reconstruction" and "resilience." It was a practiced, sickeningly perfect performance.

社区中心里挤满了流离失所的家庭，孩子在哭，老人们呆滞地盯着墙壁。美悦子立刻进入了状态，握手、点头、表情凝重，嘴里念叨着关于“重建”和“韧性”的陈词滥调。那是一场排练过无数次的、恶心得完美的表演。

Sayuri, however, stood near a group of women who were trying to wash clothes in a bucket. She saw a young girl staring at her. Instead of turning away, Sayuri knelt in the dirt, ignoring the mess, and smiled the same smile she used to give the camera. "It’s going to be okay," she said, her voice soft and practiced.

小百合却走到了一群试图用桶洗衣服的妇女身边。她看到一个小女孩正盯着她看。小百合没有避开，反而直接跪在泥地里，完全不在乎弄脏衣服，露出了她以前对着镜头时那种职业微笑。“都会好起来的，”她柔声说，声音轻柔而专业。

Rin watched her and smirked. "Look at her. She’s treating this like a film set. She can't help it—she literally doesn't know how to exist without an audience."

铃在一旁看着，冷笑一声。“瞧她。她把这儿当片场了。她改不了了——离开观众，她简直就不会活。”

Suddenly, a middle-aged man recognized Sayuri. He didn't cheer; he just looked at her with a mix of recognition and bitterness. "I know you," he muttered, his voice gravelly. "You're that girl from the tapes. The one they 'sanitized' into a hero."

突然，一个中年男人认出了小百合。他没欢呼，只是带着一种复杂又苦涩的眼神看着她。“我认识你，”他声音沙哑地嘟囔，“你是录像带里的那个姑娘。那个被他们‘清洗’成英雄的货色。”

The room went quiet. Miyetsuko froze mid-handshake, her smile faltering for the first time. Rin stepped forward, her hand sliding into her handbag as if reaching for something, her eyes turning cold—the look of a woman who used to know how to deal with "difficult" clients.

房间安静了下来。美悦子握手握到一半僵住了，笑容第一次出现了裂痕。铃向前迈了一步，手伸进包里，眼神变得冰冷——那是她以前处理“难搞顾客”时才会有的眼神。

"And what of it?" Rin asked, her voice dangerously steady. "She's wearing a medal, and you're wearing rags. Who won, exactly?"

“那又怎样？”铃问，声音稳得可怕。“她戴着勋章，你穿着破烂。到底是谁赢了？”

Miyetsuko quickly recovered, stepping between them with a practiced, politician’s grace. "We are all survivors here," she announced, her voice booming. But her eyes were fixed on her sisters, and the message was clear: Shut up and get back in the car.

美悦子迅速反应过来，以一种政客特有的从容站到两人中间。“我们都是幸存者，”她高声宣布。但她的眼神死死盯着两个妹妹，传达着清晰的讯息：闭嘴，滚回车里去。
