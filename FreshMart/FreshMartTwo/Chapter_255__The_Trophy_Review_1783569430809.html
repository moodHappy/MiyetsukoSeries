Chapter 255: The Trophy Review

第二百五十五章：战利品总结报告

The living room was warm, filled with the scent of fine whiskey and the relaxed laughter of a family that had spent the afternoon "networking." Fujiwara sprawled out on the oversized sofa, his tie undone, looking completely satisfied. Sayuri was sitting on the floor by his feet, gently massaging his calves, her eyes filled with nothing but devotion.

客厅里暖意融融，弥漫着名贵威士忌的酒香和一家人午后“拓展人脉”归来后的闲谈。藤原瘫在超大的沙发上，领带松散，一脸餍足。小百合坐在地板上，伏在他脚边轻柔地揉捏着他的小腿，眼神里盛满了温顺的依恋。

"So," Rin said, leaning against the bar, swirling her drink. "The rancher's wife. Give us the breakdown. Was she worth the trip to the countryside?"

“那么，”铃靠在吧台边，晃着杯子，“牧场主的老婆。给咱们交个底。这趟乡下没白跑吧？”

Fujiwara chuckled, taking a long sip of his drink before resting a hand on Sayuri’s head. "She was something else. You could tell she’s used to hard labor. Her legs were incredibly strong—when she wrapped them around my waist, I thought she was going to snap my spine. But the tightness... incredible. Like she’d been saving it for a city boy."

藤原笑了笑，喝了口酒，手顺势搭在小百合头上。“那女人可真带劲。看得出来她平时干了不少重活，那双腿结实得要命——当她缠住我腰的时候，我真怕她把我的脊椎骨给夹断了。不过那紧致度……绝了。就像是专门攒着等个城里人去破似的。”

Miyetsuko, looking perfectly composed as always, tapped her chin. "And the foreman's wife? The one from the landfill. She was a different breed, surely."

美悦子一如既往地从容，指尖轻点下巴。“那填埋场工头的妻子呢？那可是完全不同品种的货色，我猜。”

"Oh, she was a firecracker," Fujiwara grinned. "The contrast was the best part. Her life is literally surrounded by trash, but she’s desperate for something clean, something sharp. She cried the whole time, but it wasn't sadness—it was just pure, overwhelming release. Very loud, too. I think the bulldozer driver might have heard us if the engine wasn't running."

“噢，那是个小辣椒，”藤原咧嘴笑道，“反差感才是最棒的。她每天活在垃圾堆里，却渴望着那种干净利落的冲击。她全程都在哭，但不是因为难过——那是纯粹的、排山倒海的宣泄。叫得也很响。要不是那推土机的引擎轰鸣着，恐怕开车的都能听见我们动静。”

Sayuri looked up, smiling softly. "I'm glad you enjoyed yourself, darling. Did you get them to sign up for the 'Fresh Mart' membership?"

小百合抬起头，温柔地笑了笑。“很高兴你能玩得开心，亲爱的。那你让她俩办‘新鲜玛特’的会员卡了吗？”

"They're coming by tomorrow," Fujiwara said, leaning down to kiss Sayuri’s nose. "With their husbands' blessing, of course. I told them we needed 'consultants' for the... baby planning department."

“她们明天就过来，”藤原俯下身，亲了亲小百合的鼻尖，“当然，是在她们老公的首肯下。我跟她们说，我们需要一些……婴儿计划部门的‘顾问’。”

Rin burst out laughing, clapping her hands. "God, you have no shame. I love it. Tell me, did you use the techniques we practiced? Or did you go freestyle?"

铃大笑起来，拍手称赞。“天呐，你真是毫无廉耻。我爱死你了。快告诉我，你用了咱们练习的那几招吗？还是说你自由发挥了？”

"Little bit of both," Fujiwara winked. "But honestly? Nothing compares to home. You three have ruined me for anyone else. They’re great for a quick fix, but there’s no... soul. It’s just mechanics with them. With you three, it’s a full production."

“一半一半吧，”藤原眨了眨眼。“不过说实话？没人比得上家里这几个。你们三个简直把我胃口养刁了，别人我都有点看不上了。她们也就图个新鲜，但我感觉不到什么……灵魂。跟她们做就像是在走机械流程。跟你们三个在一起，那才叫大制作。”

Miyetsuko walked over and sat on the other side of Fujiwara, running a finger down his chest. "Save your breath, you pig. If you're as good as you say, you should be ready for the night shift. We’ve had a busy day, and I think we all deserve a 'consultation' of our own."

美悦子走过来，坐在藤原另一侧，手指顺着他的胸膛下滑。“省省力气吧，你这头种猪。要是你真像你吹的那么厉害，那你应该准备好上夜班了。今天忙了一天，我觉得咱们也该给自己来个‘内部咨询’了。”

Sayuri didn't even blink; she just started unbuttoning Fujiwara’s shirt with nimble, practiced fingers. "The house is quiet, and the recruits are coming tomorrow. Why don't we celebrate your 'networking' success, Fujiwara?"

小百合眼都没眨一下，手指灵活而娴熟地解开藤原的衬衫。“家里现在安安静静的，那些‘顾问’明天才来。藤原，咱们不如庆祝一下你这次成功的‘人脉拓展’吧？”
