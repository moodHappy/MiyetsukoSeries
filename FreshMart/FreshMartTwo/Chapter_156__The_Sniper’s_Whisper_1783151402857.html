Chapter 156: The Sniper’s Whisper

发布时间：2026/7/4 15:50:00

第一百五十六章：狙击手的低语

The motorcade moved through the city like a black serpent, its scales reflecting the neon lights of the capital. Miyetsuko sat in the backseat of the reinforced limousine, reviewing a stack of economic recovery reports. The cabin was a bubble of silence, guarded by the highest state-level security. Or so they thought.

车队像一条黑色巨蟒穿行于都市，鳞片反射着首都的霓虹。美悦子坐在防弹豪华轿车的后座，翻阅着一叠经济复苏报告。车厢内是一片宁静的泡沫，由最高级别的国家安保体系守护着。至少他们是这么认为的。

Crack.

砰。

It was a sound so sharp it didn't register as a gunshot at first—it sounded like the world itself had snapped. The rear window of the limousine turned into a spiderweb of cracks, the bullet hole perfectly centered near where Miyetsuko’s head had been a second prior.

那声音太尖锐，以至于起初没人觉得是枪声——听起来就像世界本身碎裂了。豪华轿车的后窗变成了一张蛛网般的裂纹，弹孔的位置精准地对准了美悦子一秒钟前头颅所在的位置。

Aya didn't shout. She didn't panic. Before the glass had even finished falling, she had tackled Miyetsuko to the floor of the vehicle, covering her with her own body. "Sector Four! Hard left!" Aya barked into her comms, her voice a cold, military-grade command.

绫没有尖叫，没有恐慌。甚至在玻璃碎片落地之前，她就已经将美悦子扑倒在车厢地板上，用自己的身体护住她。“四区！紧急左转！”绫对着通讯器低吼，声音是冷酷的、准军事化的指令。

The car swerved violently, tires screaming against the asphalt, as a second shot pinged off the armored chassis—a near miss that sent a shower of sparks flying.

汽车剧烈转向，轮胎在沥青路上发出刺耳的摩擦声，第二发子弹擦过装甲底盘，迸发出火花。

"It was a suppressed subsonic round," Aya whispered against Miyetsuko’s ear, her eyes scanning the dark skyline through the tinted, shattered glass. "Professional. Someone who knew the route. Someone who knew the protocol."

“是消音亚音速弹，”绫贴着美悦子的耳边低语，双眼透过暗色且破碎的玻璃，扫描着黑暗的天际线。“专业人员。有人知道路线。有人知道安保方案。”

When they reached the safety of the residence, the tension didn't dissipate; it curdled into something uglier. Miyetsuko stood in the study, watching the security footage. The sniper had vanished before the police even cordoned off the block.

当他们抵达宅邸的安全区时，紧张感并未消散，反而变质成了某种更丑陋的东西。美悦子站在书房里，看着监控录像。狙击手在警方封锁街区之前就已人间蒸发。

"Who?" Fujiwara asked, leaning against the doorframe, his face pale. "Sato’s loyalists? They're supposed to be in hiding."

“是谁？”藤原靠在门框上，脸色苍白。“佐藤的忠诚派？他们不是应该都在躲藏吗？”

Miyetsuko touched the glass of her drink, her expression unreadable. She walked to the map on the wall, marking the sniper’s vantage point with a red pin.

美悦子触碰着酒杯，表情令人捉摸不透。她走到墙上的地图前，用一枚红头针标出了狙击手的狙击点。

"Sato’s loyalists are bureaucrats; they love paperwork more than guns," Miyetsuko murmured, her eyes cold. "The military brass? They are too cowardly to act while the polls are this high. No, this was someone else."

“佐藤的忠诚派只是官僚，他们爱文书多过爱枪，”美悦子低语，双眼冰冷。“军方高层？在民调这么高的时候，他们太懦弱，不敢动手。不，这是另有其人。”

She turned to Aya. "That shot wasn't meant to kill me instantly. It was meant to warn me. It was a message. Whoever did this has the clearance of a ghost."

她转向绫。“那一枪不是为了瞬间杀死我。那是为了警告我。这是一个信号。能做到这一点的人，有着幽灵般的权限。”

"The opposition?" Rin suggested, entering the room.

“反对派？”铃推门进来，提议道。

"No," Miyetsuko shook her head. "This is from the 'New Guard'—the radical faction within the intelligence services. The ones who didn't serve Sato, but served the State. They don't want to restore the old regime; they want to dismantle us because they think I’m corrupting the nation’s soul. They aren't assassins, Rin. They’re 'patriots' who believe they are surgeons cutting out a cancer."

“不，”美悦子摇了摇头。“这是‘新卫队’——情报部门内部的激进派系。他们不效忠佐藤，他们效忠的是国家。他们不想恢复旧政权；他们想铲除我们，因为他们认为我正在腐蚀国家的灵魂。他们不是普通的刺客，铃。他们是那些自以为正在切除肿瘤的‘爱国者’。”

The room went silent. The threat wasn't a shadow of the past; it was a blade from within the very system Miyetsuko now led.

房间陷入寂静。威胁不再是过去的幽灵；它是来自美悦子如今所领导的体制内部的一把利刃。

[Learning Point]

The Complexity of the Enemy: 敌人的复杂性。美悦子遇到的不仅是佐藤的残余，而是由“国家主义者”构成的深层政府（Deep State）。这种敌人更难对付，因为他们披着“爱国”的外衣，且深谙体制内的运行规则。

The Role of the Protector: 守护者的角色。Aya 的表现确认了她作为“剧情推进器”的价值。她不仅提供了物理安全，还通过对弹道的分析，为美悦子提供了关于敌人构成的关键线索。

The Political Noir Atmosphere: 政治黑色氛围。刺杀事件揭示了即使美悦子获胜，真正的“斗争”才刚刚开始。从“生存”到“治理”的转换过程中，体制内部的自我清洗（Self-purification）是必然的阵痛。
