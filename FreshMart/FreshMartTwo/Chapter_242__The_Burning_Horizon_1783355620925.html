第二百四十二章：烽火中的逃亡

The rotor blades of the helicopter sliced through the heavy, smoke-choked air. Below them, the manor was being devoured by a sea of angry, desperate faces. The mob had breached the gates, and the scene was absolute anarchy. Gold statues were being smashed, tapestries were being torn, and the mob was fighting over the remaining bottles of vintage wine like starving dogs.

直升机的旋翼划破了沉重而浓烟弥漫的空气。在他们脚下，庄园正被一片愤怒、绝望的人潮所吞噬。暴民已经冲破了闸门，场面彻底陷入了无政府状态。金质雕像被砸碎，挂毯被撕扯，暴民们像饿狗一样争抢着剩下的陈年红酒。

Fujiwara pressed his face against the plexiglass, looking down. He caught a glimpse of the maid from earlier—the one he had "enlisted" so roughly only hours ago. She was currently being pinned down against a fountain by three men, her maid's uniform in tatters. Fujiwara let out a low, disappointed sigh. "What a shame. I hadn't even finished teaching her how to handle my balls. Such a waste of good training."

藤原把脸贴在有机玻璃上向下看。他瞥见了那个女仆——那个几小时前他才粗暴“参军”的女孩。她此刻正被三个男人按在喷泉边，女仆装已成了碎布条。藤原发出一声低沉、失望的叹息。“真是可惜。我还没来得及教她怎么伺候我的那玩意儿呢。浪费了这么好的调教。”

Miyetsuko, sitting calmly with her eyes closed, didn't even turn to look. "Assets are meant to be replaced, Fujiwara. Focus on the horizon. We aren't looking back."

美悦子闭着眼平静地坐着，甚至没转头看一眼。“资产就是要用来替换的，藤原。盯着地平线。我们不回头。”

They landed on the coastline, where the yacht waited, glistening white against the dark, churning sea. As they scrambled aboard, the engine roared to life, and the world of chaos fell away behind them. On the deck, champagne was already flowing.

直升机降落在海岸线上，游艇在黑暗、翻滚的海面上闪烁着白光。当他们匆忙登船，引擎轰鸣，混乱的世界被抛在了身后。甲板上，香槟已经开启。

"Donglai Island," Miyetsuko announced, pouring a glass for everyone. "It’s a sovereign private enclave. I bought it three years ago under a shell company. It’s got a private airstrip, its own power grid, and, more importantly, no extradition treaties."

“东莱岛，”美悦子宣布道，为大家倒上一杯酒，“那是一个主权私人飞地。我三年前用一家空壳公司买下的。那里有私人简易机场、独立的电网，更重要的是，没有引渡条约。”

Sayuri leaned against the railing, watching the burning coastline recede into a tiny glow. She turned back to the others, her eyes shimmering with a dangerous excitement. "And what about the 'entertainment' for the trip?" she asked, walking over to Fujiwara and unzipping his pants. "I’m tired of playing nice. We have the whole ocean to ourselves. I want to see how far we can push things."

小百合靠在栏杆上，看着燃烧的海岸线缩成一个小小的光点。她转过身，眼中闪烁着危险的兴奋。“那旅途中的‘娱乐’呢？”她问道，走到藤原身边，拉开了他的裤链。“我厌倦了循规蹈矩。我们拥有整片海洋。我想看看我们到底能玩得有多疯。”

Rin laughed, reaching out to pull Miyetsuko’s dress down, revealing her bare shoulders. "I agree. We have a new kingdom to build, but that doesn't mean we can't enjoy the spoils of the old one." She gestured to the sprawling cabin behind them. "Let’s christen this boat with a little 'sovereign' pleasure. Fujiwara, since you're so disappointed about losing your toy, maybe you should make it up to us?"

铃笑了起来，伸手拉下美悦子的裙子，露出她光洁的肩膀。“我同意。我们要建立一个新的王国，但这不代表我们不能享受旧世界的战利品。”她指了指身后宽敞的船舱。“让我们用一场‘主权’级的欢愉来为这艘船洗礼吧。藤原，既然你那么遗憾丢了你的玩具，或许你该好好补偿我们？”

Fujiwara grinned, his eyes dark and hungry. He grabbed the champagne bottle and poured a stream of it over Sayuri’s breasts, watching it run down her skin. "I think I can manage that. I’ll make sure none of us remember we ever had a country to begin with."

藤原咧嘴一笑，眼中满是阴沉的渴望。他拿起香槟瓶，将酒液倾倒在小百合的胸前，看着它们顺着皮肤流下。“我觉得这我能搞定。我会让咱们都忘了自己曾经有过一个国家。”

As the yacht cut through the waves, headed for the solitude of Donglai Island, the four of them fell into the familiar, frenzied rhythm of their shared depravity. The fire on the shore was just a memory now; the only thing that mattered was the heat of the bodies and the absolute, unchecked power they still held over one another.

游艇破浪前行，驶向东莱岛的孤寂。四个人重新陷入了他们那熟悉的、疯狂的共同堕落的律动中。岸上的大火已成往事；此刻唯一重要的，是交缠身体的热度，以及他们对彼此依然拥有的、绝对而毫无节制的支配权。
