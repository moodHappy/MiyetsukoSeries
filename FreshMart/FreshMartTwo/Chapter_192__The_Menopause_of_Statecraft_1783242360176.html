Chapter 192: The Menopause of Statecraft

发布时间：2026/7/5 17:05:59

第一百九十二章：更年期政治学

Another decree was enacted with the stroke of a pen. Public places must display: "Smoking is detrimental to health." It was a trivial bit of social engineering, just one of the endless regulations that now covered every inch of the public sphere.

公共场所必须张贴：“吸烟有害健康。”又一条法令生效了。这不过是微不足道的社会工程学手段之一，像这样的监管条例如今覆盖了公共领域的每一个角落。

A tornado destroyed several houses, flattening a small village on the outskirts of the capital. Miyetsuko went to inspect the rubble. Unfortunately, she is going through menopause, and her tolerance for incompetence was lower than ever. She snapped at a local official who tried to offer a pathetic excuse about building codes, yelling at him until his face turned purple.

一场龙卷风摧毁了几栋房子，把郊区的一个小村庄夷为平地。美悦子去视察灾区。不幸的是，她正处于更年期，对无能的容忍度降到了冰点。当一位地方官员试图为建筑规范找借口时，她脾气暴躁地骂了他一顿，直到那官员的脸涨成了猪肝色。

The result was an unexpected PR victory. The people adored her, viewing her rage as the righteous fury of a Prime Minister truly championing the people's cause. In the process, the chaotic, anarchic state of the past was completely purged from the social fabric, leaving a silence that passed for stability.

结果却大火。民众爱戴她，认为这种发火是为民做主的首相那种正义的愤怒。在这一过程中，过去那种混乱的无政府状态被彻底清除，留下了一种被视作稳定的沉寂。

Miyetsuko carefully cultivated a persona of a spartan life, often photographed eating a dry crust of bread or sipping plain tea to prove her solidarity with the suffering masses. However, those performances were strictly for the cameras. Every time there was a high-level gala, there is plentiful food at the party, with enough caviar and vintage wine to sink a ship.

美悦子精心打造着一种苦行僧式的生活形象，常被拍到啃着干硬的面包或抿着清茶，以证明她与受苦受难的民众站在一起。然而，这些表演纯粹是演给镜头看的。每次举行高层晚宴时，聚会上有很多食物，那里的鱼子酱和陈年佳酿多得足以把船压沉。

In the living room, the family debriefed on the day's events. Fujiwara laughed at the footage of the trembling official. "You scared the hell out of him, Miyetsuko. He looked like he was about to faint."

在客厅里，一家人复盘着当天的活动。藤原看着那个颤抖官员的录像笑得不行。“你把他吓坏了，美悦子。他看起来像是快要晕倒了一样。”

Sayuri leaned back, checking her manicure. "Well, menopausal rage is a powerful tool. It’s better than an army. If the public thinks you’re angry for them, they’ll let you get away with murder."

小百合靠在椅背上，端详着自己的指甲。“嗯，更年期的怒火可是件利器。这比军队还管用。如果公众觉得你是在‘替他们’发火，哪怕你杀人他们都会放过你。”

Rin chuckled, sipping her own wine. "Just keep the signs up about the smoking. It makes the streets look clean, even if the air smells like fear. As long as she plays the part of the suffering ascetic, we can keep the banquet tables full."

铃轻笑一声，抿了一口酒。“那个‘吸烟有害’的牌子就挂着吧。它让街道看起来很干净，哪怕空气里闻起来全是恐惧的味道。只要她继续扮演那个苦行的圣人，我们的宴席就能一直满当当的。”
