Chapter 252: Fresh Mart Recruitment Agency

发布时间：2026/7/9 11:25:38

第二百五十二章：新鲜玛特招募中心

The reality of the "baby plan" had hit them hard: three women, even at ten rounds a day each, were simply not enough to keep the manor lively—or, more importantly, to keep the quota moving as fast as Fujiwara desired. The solution, naturally, was to open a convenience store on the main road of Donglai Island. They named it "Fresh Mart," a bland, unassuming front for the most bizarre recruitment agency in the world.

“造人计划”的现实让几人很快意识到：哪怕每个人一天十次，三个女人也根本不够——更重要的是，没法达到藤原那种贪婪的进度。解决办法嘛，也很直接：他们在东莱岛主干道上开了一家便利店，叫“新鲜玛特”。这名字平淡无奇，正好掩盖了这是世界上最离谱的人口招募中心的事实。

Inside the back office, the air was thick with the smell of instant coffee and the tension of a high-stakes card game.

后厨办公室里，空气中弥漫着速溶咖啡的味道，还有一种高风险博弈的紧张感。

"Alright," Rin said, shuffling a deck of cards to decide the order. "We’re doing Rock-Paper-Scissors to see who has to go out and 'scout' for customers today. Loser hits the sales floor."

“行吧，”铃洗着牌决定顺序，“咱们石头剪刀布，输了的那个去店里‘物色’今天的客户。输的那个去前台干活。”

Fujiwara, currently nursing a sports drink and looking like he’d just run a marathon, added a caveat. "And let me be clear—my standards haven't dropped. If you bring back some troll, I’m not doing it. She has to be beautiful. Stunning. Face like a doll, body like a goddess. If she’s not a ten, my dick isn’t moving. I’m not a charity case."

藤原正喝着运动饮料，那模样活像刚跑完一场马拉松。他补充了一句：“把话说在前头——我的标准可没降。要是你们带回来歪瓜裂枣，我可不干。得美，得是那种国色天香的。脸蛋要像洋娃娃，身材要像女神。要是不到十分，我那玩意儿根本硬不起来。我可不是做慈善的。”

Sayuri nodded earnestly, her eyes wide. "Don't worry, Fujiwara. I have a good eye for beauty. I promise I’ll find someone who makes your heart beat faster."

小百合认真地点点头，眼睛亮晶晶的。“放心吧，藤原。我挑人的眼光很好的。我保证找到那种能让你心跳加速的美女。”

The shifts on the sales floor were a chaotic mix of comedy and absurdity.

店里的轮班生活简直是一场混乱、荒谬的喜剧。

One morning, Rin spotted a stunning young student browsing the snack aisle. She approached her with a grin, whispering, "Hey, want to make a lot of money and have a life-changing experience? We have a very... private job involving a very handsome man." The student looked at her, then at the store, then back at Rin. "You’re a pervert, aren't you? Leave me alone or I’m calling the police!" Rin just sighed and waved her off.

有一天早上，铃在零食区发现了一个漂亮得惊人的女学生。她坏笑着凑过去，低声说：“嘿，想赚大钱顺便体验下改变人生的经历吗？我们有个非常……私密的工作，对象是个大帅哥。”女学生看了看她，又看了看店，最后盯着铃。“你是变态吧？离我远点，不然我报警了！”铃只能叹了口气，挥手让她滚蛋。

Then there was the middle-aged couple. Miyetsuko had spotted the wife, who was arguably one of the most beautiful women on the island. As she began her pitch, the husband—a burly man with a strange glint in his eyes—actually interrupted. "Wait, you're asking my wife to sleep with him?" He looked at Fujiwara, who was hiding behind a shelf, and then whispered, "Can I watch? I’ve actually been looking for this kind of arrangement."

后来还有对中年夫妻。美悦子看中了那位妻子，那是岛上数一数二的美女。正当美悦子开口游说时，那丈夫——一个眼神闪烁着古怪光芒的壮汉——居然插嘴道：“等等，你是说让我老婆去陪他？”他看了看躲在货架后面的藤原，然后小声问：“我可以观战吗？其实我一直想找这种安排。”

And, of course, the awkward ones. Sayuri once approached a woman who had her parents with her. "Excuse me, miss, would you be interested in—" She stopped as she realized the parents were staring right at her. The father looked horrified, the mother looked confused, and the daughter just stood there, biting her lip. Sayuri quickly retreated, blushing to the roots of her hair. "Sorry, sorry! Wrong customer!"

当然，也有尴尬的时候。有次小百合盯上了一个带着父母逛街的女人。“打扰一下，小姐，你有兴趣……”她话没说完，才发现人家父母正死死盯着她。父亲一脸惊恐，母亲一头雾水，女儿则站在原地咬着嘴唇。小百合赶紧撤退，脸红到了耳根。“对不起对不起！找错人了！”

Fujiwara watched the revolving door of rejections and weirdos from the back, laughing until his sides ached. "This is the best reality show ever," he chuckled, grabbing Sayuri and pulling her into his lap. "Success or failure, it doesn't matter. Watching you three try to be recruiters is more fun than the actual sex."

藤原在后面看着这走马灯似的拒绝和怪咖，笑得肚子疼。“这简直是史上最棒的真人秀，”他轻笑着，把小百合拉进怀里，“成不成功无所谓。看你们三个当星探的样子，比真的做爱还有趣。”
