Chapter 260: The Price of Disbelief

发布时间：2026/7/10 13:45:02

第二百六十章：质疑的代价

The "Fresh Mart" was thriving. Business was so good that Fujiwara had to recruit an extra hand just to keep the coffee machine running. But not everyone who walked in looking for "lifestyle consulting" was grateful.

“新鲜玛特”生意兴隆。生意好到藤原不得不额外招了个人手，专门负责盯着咖啡机。但并不是每个走进店里寻求“生活方式咨询”的人都心怀感激。

A young man—a former activist who had been beaten at the square—was sitting in the lounge, his face bruised and his eyes full of fire. He looked around at the plush armchairs, the imported scotch, and the beautiful women serving coffee, and he sneered. "This whole place is bogus," he spat, throwing his cup onto the table. "You’re not 'consultants.' You’re just parasites. You’re preying on people who’ve lost everything, peddling some fake dream while you live like kings."

一个年轻人——一个在广场被殴打的前活动分子——正坐在休息室里，脸上带着伤，眼中满是怒火。他环顾着四周天鹅绒扶手椅、进口威士忌和送咖啡的美女，冷笑一声。“这鬼地方全是骗人的，”他啐了一口，把杯子摔在桌上，“你们根本不是什么‘顾问’。你们就是寄生虫。你们在掠夺那些一无所有的人，兜售着虚假的梦，自己却过得像国王一样。”

Rin, who was pouring herself a drink, didn't even look up. "Parasites, consultants, gods—call us what you want, darling. But look around. You’re dry, you’re warm, and you’ve got scotch in your glass. Does it feel bogus when you're finally safe?"

正给自己倒酒的铃连头都没抬。“寄生虫、顾问、神明——亲爱的，随你怎么称呼。但你瞧瞧周围。你现在身上是干的，暖和的，杯子里还有威士忌。当你真正感到安全时，这感觉还假吗？”

The man stood up, ready to flip the table, but Fujiwara was faster. He didn't use violence; he just stepped into the man's personal space, radiating a calm, terrifying kind of intimacy. He placed a gentle hand on the man's shoulder. "Easy, friend. I understand your anger. I really do. You’ve been trampled. But look at Sayuri." He gestured toward Sayuri, who was smiling as she organized the books on the shelf, completely at peace. "Doesn't she look happy to you? We aren't selling a fake dream. We’re selling a sanctuary. You can stay and find your peace, or you can walk back out into the cold and see how long it takes for the 'real world' to crush you again."

年轻人站起身，想要掀翻桌子，但藤原更快。他没有动手，只是迈步走进那人的私人空间，散发着一种冷静而可怕的亲近感。他轻轻把手搭在对方肩上。“放轻松，朋友。我理解你的愤怒。真的。你被践踏过。但看看小百合。”他指着正在整理书架的小百合，她正微笑着，一副完全祥和的模样。“她看起来不快乐吗？我们卖的不是假梦。我们卖的是避难所。你可以留下，找回你的平静；或者你可以走回寒冷里，看看那个‘真实世界’需要多久再次把你碾碎。”

The young man trembled, his anger deflating like a punctured balloon. He sank back into the armchair, exhausted by his own rage.

年轻人颤抖着，那股怒火像被扎破的气球一样泄了下去。他瘫回扶手椅里，被自己的狂怒耗尽了力气。

Sayuri walked over, her expression soft and maternal. She poured a fresh cup of coffee and placed it in his hands. "There now," she cooed, smoothing his hair. "Why be angry? Everything outside is falling apart. Why not just let go and trust us? We only want to help you."

小百合走过来，神情温柔，充满母性。她重新倒了一杯热咖啡，塞进他手里。“好啦，”她柔声细语，抚摸着他的头发，“何必生气呢？外面的一切都在崩塌。为什么不干脆放手，信任我们呢？我们只想帮你。”

Miyetsuko watched from the back office, observing the scene with a satisfied nod. She tapped her glass against the doorframe. "Exactly. Loyalty is just a matter of showing them the alternative. Fujiwara, keep him here. Rin, make sure he fills out the 'loyalty questionnaire' before the night is over."

美悦子在后办公室看着这一幕，满意地点点头。她用杯子敲了敲门框。“没错。忠诚不过是让他们看到还有别的选择。藤原，让他留在这儿。铃，确保他在天黑前填好那份‘忠诚度问卷’。”

Fujiwara pulled Sayuri back into his lap, nuzzling her neck as they watched the young man slowly sip the coffee. "You're amazing, Sayuri. You have this way of making people melt."

藤原把小百合拉回腿上，蹭着她的脖颈，看着那年轻人慢慢喝着咖啡。“你太棒了，小百合。你总是有办法让人融化。”

"I just want everyone to be as happy as we are, darling," Sayuri giggled, leaning into his chest. "If they accept our help, they're part of the family, right? And family stays together."

“我只是想让大家都像我们一样幸福，亲爱的，”小百合咯咯笑着，贴着他的胸口。“如果他们接受了我们的帮助，他们就是家里人，对吧？家里人就该待在一起。”

"Right," Fujiwara murmured, kissing her cheek. "Family. Let’s make sure he never wants to leave."

“没错，”藤原低语，亲了亲她的脸颊。“家里人。咱们得确保他这辈子都不想走。”
