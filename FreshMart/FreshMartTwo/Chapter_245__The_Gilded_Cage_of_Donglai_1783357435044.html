Chapter 245: The Gilded Cage of Donglai

发布时间：2026/7/7 01:03:53

第二百四十五章：东莱岛的镀金囚笼

The landing on Donglai Island was shrouded in a funereal silence. The sea air, usually invigorating, felt heavy with the weight of the missing goddess. Fujiwara, Rin, and Sayuri stepped onto the private dock, their clothes stained with salt and the grim reality of their loss. Miyetsuko was gone, and with her, the chaotic purpose that had defined their lives.

东莱岛的登陆仪式笼罩在葬礼般的寂静中。海风本应沁人心脾，此刻却压抑着女神失踪后的沉重。藤原、铃和小百合踏上私人码头，身上沾染着海盐与现实的苦涩。美悦子不见了，随她一同消失的，还有那曾经定义他们生活的疯狂目标。

Awaiting them at the end of the pier was the Island Administrator, a man of terrifying efficiency, bowing with a precision that bordered on robotic. "Welcome home," he said, his voice flat. "Your residence is ready."

码头尽头，岛屿行政长官早已等候多时。他有着令人恐惧的办事效率，行礼的标准精准得近乎机器人。“欢迎回家，”他平淡地说道，“您的居所已经准备好了。”

They were driven in a black sedan to a secluded cliffside, where a set of imposing gates stood. As they pushed open the mahogany doors, the breath left their lungs. It wasn't just a house—it was a memory. The layout, the scent of the floor wax, the precise slant of the afternoon sun through the library windows—it was an exact replica of their manor back home. Every scratch on the floorboards, every worn spot on the velvet rugs, was copied with haunting fidelity.

他们坐着黑色轿车来到一处僻静的悬崖边，那里矗立着一扇宏伟的大门。当他们推开红木门时，瞬间窒息了。这不仅仅是一座房子——这是一段记忆。布局、地板蜡的气味、下午阳光透过图书馆窗户的精确角度——这分明就是老家庄园的完美复刻。地板上的每一道划痕、天鹅绒地毯上的每一处磨损，都以一种令人心悸的还原度被复制了出来。

"How..." Sayuri’s voice trembled. She touched the banister, her eyes brimming with tears. "It’s impossible. How did you do this?"

“怎么会……”小百合声音颤抖。她抚摸着扶手，眼里泛起泪花。“这不可能。你们是怎么做到的？”

The Administrator smiled faintly. "Your late leader was a woman of foresight. This project began months ago. She knew that power is fragile, so she built this sanctuary as a contingency."

行政长官露出一丝浅笑。“你们已故的领袖是个有远见的人。这个项目数月前就开始了。她深知权力脆弱，因此建造了这个庇护所作为备选方案。”

Then, a soft sound drifted from the kitchen. Footsteps. The three of them froze as a maid emerged, carrying a tray of tea. Their hearts stopped. It was her—the maid Fujiwara had 'enlisted' in the final hours of the riot. The same face, the same trembling grace, the same hesitant gaze.

这时，厨房里传来轻微的响动。脚步声。三人僵住了，只见一名女仆端着茶盘走了出来。他们的心脏在那一刻停止了跳动。是她——那个藤原在暴乱最后时刻“征召”的女仆。同样的脸庞，同样的颤动姿态，同样的犹豫眼神。

"Madam, shall I pour the tea?" the girl asked, her voice an identical echo of the one left behind in the flames.

“夫人，需要倒茶吗？”女孩问道，声音与留在那片火海中的那个人一模一样。

Fujiwara stepped forward, his eyes narrowed, his hand gripping the girl’s chin to inspect her face. There was no deception here; this wasn't makeup or surgery. It was a genetic mirror. "The casting process for this... 'model'..." Fujiwara muttered, his voice cold. "How many did you go through?"

藤原走上前，双眼微眯，伸手捏住女孩的下巴细细端详。没有伪装，不是化妆或整容，这是基因层面的镜像。“为了挑选这个……‘原型’……”藤原冷冷地嘟囔道，“你们海选了多少人？”

"Ten thousand," the Administrator replied, unbothered by the cruelty. "We found her in a remote village across the sea. She has no past, no family, and no name other than the one we gave her. She is simply... your maid."

“一万人，”行政长官回答道，对其中的残酷毫无波澜。“我们在海的另一端一个偏远村庄找到了她。她没有过去，没有家人，除了我们给她的名字，她没有别的身份。她仅仅是……您的女仆。”

Rin walked over, circling the girl like a predator, a bitter laugh escaping her throat. "Miyetsuko really did love her toys, didn't she? Even in death, she makes sure we live in a simulation of our own ego."

铃走过去，像掠食者一样绕着女孩打转，喉咙里发出苦涩的笑声。“美悦子真的很爱她的玩具，不是吗？哪怕死了，她也要确保我们活在自己虚荣心的模拟游戏里。”

Sayuri collapsed onto the sofa, the replica of her favorite spot, and buried her face in her hands. The room was perfect. Everything was perfect. And yet, the silence of the house—the absence of Miyetsuko’s commanding presence—made it feel like a tomb. They had regained their home, but they had lost the queen who gave it meaning.

小百合瘫倒在沙发上，那是她最喜欢的位置，她将脸深深埋进掌心。房间太完美了。一切都太完美了。然而，屋内的死寂——美悦子那统治性的存在感的缺失——让这里更像是一座坟墓。他们找回了家，却失去了赋予这个家灵魂的那个女王。
