YEARS LATER on his deathbed Aureli-ano Segun-do would remember the rainy afternoon in June when he went into the bedroom to meet his first son.

多年后，奥雷连诺第二在临终前仍记得六月的那个下雨的下午，当时他走进卧室去见他的第一个儿子。

Even though the child was languid and weepy, with no mark of a Buendía, he did not have to think twice about naming him.

尽管这个孩子无精打采、哭泣，没有布恩迪亚的痕迹，但他在给他取名时毫不犹豫。

"We'll call him José Arcadio," he said.

“我们称他为何塞·阿卡迪奥，”他说。

Fernanda del Carpio, the beautiful woman he had married the year before, agreed.

他前一年娶的美丽女人费尔南达·德尔·卡皮奥（Fernanda del Carpio）同意了。

úrsula, on the other hand, could not conceal a vague feeling of doubt.

另一方面，乌苏拉却掩饰不住一种模糊的怀疑感。

Throughout the long history of the family the insistent repetition of names had made her draw some conclusions that seemed to be certain.

在这个家族漫长的历史中，名字的不断重复使她得出了一些似乎是确定的结论。

While the Aureli-anos were withdrawn, but with lucid minds, the José Arcadios were impulsive and enterprising, but they were marked with a tragic sign.

奥雷连诺家族性格孤僻，但头脑清醒；而何塞·阿卡迪奥斯家族则冲动、进取，但带有悲剧性的特征。

The only cases that were impossible to classify were those of José Arcadio Segun-do and Aureli-ano Segun-do.

唯一无法分类的案例是何塞·阿卡迪奥第二和奥雷利亚诺第二的案例。

They were so much alike and so mischievous during childhood that not even Santa Sofía de la Piedad could tell them apart.

他们在童年时期非常相似，而且都非常顽皮，甚至连圣索菲亚·德拉皮达都无法区分他们。

On the day of their christening Amaranta put bracelets on them with their respective names dressed them in different colored clothing marked with each one's initials, but when they began to go to school they decided to exchange clothing and bracelets and call each other by opposite names.

在他们洗礼的那天，阿玛兰塔给他们戴上了写有各自名字的手镯，给他们穿上了不同颜色的衣服，上面标有各自名字的首字母，但当他们开始上学时，他们决定交换衣服和手镯，并用相反的名字互相称呼。

The teacher, Melchor Escalona, used to knowing José Arcadio Segun-do by his green shirt, went out of his mind when he discovered that the latter was wearing Aureli-ano Segun-do's bracelet and that the otone said, nevertheless, that his name was Aureli-ano Segun-do in spite of the fact that he was wearing the white shirt and the bracelet with José Arcadio Segun-do's name.

老师梅尔乔·埃斯卡洛纳 (Melchor Escalona) 以前因何塞·阿卡迪奥第二的绿色衬衫而认识何塞·阿卡迪奥第二，当他发现后者戴着奥雷利阿诺第二的手镯时，他疯了，尽管他穿着白色衬衫，手镯上写着何塞·阿卡迪奥第二的名字，但音却说他的名字叫奥雷利阿诺第二。

-From then on he was never sure who was who.

——从那时起，他就分不清谁是谁了。

Even when they grew up and life made them different.

即使他们长大了，生活也让他们变得不同。

úrsula still wondered if they themselves might not have made a mistake in some moment of their intricate game of confusion had become changed forever.

乌苏拉仍然想知道他们自己是否可能在他们错综复杂的混乱游戏的某个时刻犯了一个错误，这个游戏已经永远改变了。

Until the beginning of adolescence they were two synchronized machines.

直到青春期开始之前，他们都是两台同步的机器。

They would wake up at the same time, have the urge to go to the bathroom at the same time, suffer the same upsets in health, and they even dreamed about the same things.

他们会同时醒来，同时有上厕所的冲动，在健康上遭受同样的困扰，甚至梦到同样的事情。

In the house, where it was thought that they coordinated their actions with a simple desire to confuse, no one realized what really was happening until one day when Santa Sofía de la Piedad gave one of them a glass of lemonade and as soon as he tasted it the other one said that it needed sugar.

在房子里，人们认为他们协调行动只是为了迷惑，没有人意识到到底发生了什么，直到有一天，圣索菲亚·德拉皮达给了其中一个人一杯柠檬水，他一尝，另一个人就说需要加糖。

Santa Sofía de la Piedad, who had indeed forgotten to put sugar in the lemonade, told úrsula about it.

圣索菲亚·德拉皮达确实忘记在柠檬水中加糖，她把这件事告诉了乌苏拉。

"That's what they're all like," she said without surprise.

“他们都是这样的，”她毫不惊讶地说道。

"crazy from birth."

“从出生起就疯狂。”

In time things became less disordered.

随着时间的推移，事情变得不那么混乱了。

The one who came out of the game of confusion with the name Aureli-ano Segun-do grew to monumental size like his grandfathers, the one who kept the name of José Arcadio Segun-do grew to be bony like the colonel, and the only thing they had in common was the family's solitary air.

从奥雷连诺第二这个名字的混乱游戏中脱颖而出的那个人，像他的祖父一样成长为巨大的人物，而保留何塞·阿卡迪奥第二这个名字的那个人则像上校一样瘦骨嶙峋，他们唯一的共同点是家庭的孤独气氛。

Perhaps it was that crossing of stature, names, and character that made úrsula suspect that they had been shuffled like a deck of cards since childhood.

也许正是身材、名字和性格的交叉让乌苏拉怀疑他们从小就像一副纸牌一样被洗牌。

The decisive difference was revealed in the midst of the war, when José Arcadio Segun-do asked Colonel Geri-neldo Márquez to let him see an execution.

决定性的差异在战争期间显现出来，当时何塞·阿卡迪奥第二请求赫里内尔多·马尔克斯上校让他观看处决。

Against úrsula's better judgment his wishes were satisfied.

尽管乌苏拉做出了更好的判断，但他的愿望还是得到了满足。

Aureli-ano Segun-do, on the other hand, shuddered at the mere idea of witnessing an execution.

另一方面，奥雷连诺第二一想到目睹处决就感到不寒而栗。

He preferred to stay home.

他更喜欢呆在家里。

At the age of twelve he asked úrsula what was in the locked room.

十二岁时，他问乌苏拉锁着的房间里有什么。

"Papers," she answered.

“文件，”她回答。

"Melquíades' books and the strange things that he wrote in his last years."

“梅尔基亚德斯的书和他晚年写的奇怪的东西。”

Instead of calming him, the answer increased his curiosity.

这个答案不但没有让他平静下来，反而增加了他的好奇心。

He demanded so much, promised with such insistence that he would not mistreat the things, that úrsula, gave him the keys.

他要求如此之高，如此坚持地承诺他不会虐待这些东西，乌苏拉把钥匙给了他。

No one had gone into the room again since they had taken Melquíades' body out and had put on the door a padlock whose parts had become fused together with rust.

自从他们把梅尔基亚德斯的尸体抬出来并在门上装上一把挂锁（其零件已经锈迹斑斑）之后，就没有人再进过这个房间了。

But when Aureli-ano Segun-do opened the windows a familiar light entered that seemed accustomed to lighting the room every day and there was not the slightest trace of dust or cobwebs, with everything swept and clean, better swept and cleaner than on the day of the burial, and the ink had not dried up in the inkwell nor had oxidation diminished the shine of the metals nor had the embers gone out under the water pipe where José Arcadio Buendía had vaporized mercury.

但是，当奥雷连诺第二打开窗户时，熟悉的光线照进来，似乎每天都会照亮房间，没有一丝灰尘或蜘蛛网的痕迹，所有东西都打扫得很干净，比埋葬当天打扫得更好，而且墨水池里的墨水没有干涸，金属的光泽也没有因氧化而减弱，何塞·阿卡迪奥·布恩迪亚蒸发汞的水管下的余烬也没有消失。

On the shelves were the books bound in a cardboard--like material, pale, like tanned human skin, and the manuscripts were intact.

书架上的书是用类似纸板的材料装订的，颜色苍白，就像晒黑的人皮一样，手稿完好无损。

In spite of the room's having been shut up for many years, the air seemed fresher than in the rest of the house.

尽管这个房间已经关闭多年，但空气似乎比房子其他地方更新鲜。

Everything was so recent that several weeks later, when úrsula went into the room a pail of water and a brush to wash the floor, there was nothing for her to do.

一切都那么新鲜，几周后，当乌苏拉拿着一桶水和一把刷子走进房间去洗地板时，她却无事可做。

Aureli-ano Segun-do was deep in the reading of a book.

奥雷连诺第二正在埋头读书。

Although it had no cover and the title did not appear anywhere, the boy enjoyed the story a woman who sat at a table and ate nothing but kernels of rice, which she picked up with a pin, and the story of the fisherman who borrowed a weight for his net from a neighbor and when he gave him a fish in payment later it had a diamond in its stomach, and the one about the lamp that fulfilled wishes and about flying carpets.

虽然它没有封面，标题也没有出现在任何地方，但男孩很喜欢这个故事：一个女人坐在桌子旁，只吃米粒，她用别针捡起米粒；还有一个渔夫向邻居借了一个网重，当他付钱给他一条鱼时，鱼肚子里有一颗钻石；还有一个关于实现愿望的灯和飞毯的故事。

Surprised, he asked úrsula if all that was true and she answered him that it was, that many years ago the gypsies had brought magic lamps and flying mats to Macon-do.

他惊讶地问乌苏拉这一切是否属实，她回答说是的，很多年前吉普赛人把神灯和飞垫带到了马孔多。

"What's happening," she sighed, "is that the world is slowly coming to an end and those things don't come here any more."

“正在发生的事情，”她叹了口气，“世界正在慢慢走向末日，那些东西再也不会来到这里了。”

When he finished the book, in which many of the stories had no endings because there were pages missing, Aureli-ano Segun-do set about deciphering the manuscripts.

当他完成这本书时，其中许多故事因为缺页而没有结局，奥雷连诺第二开始破译手稿。

It was impossible.

这是不可能的。

The letters looked like clothes hung out to dry on a line and they looked more like musical notation than writing.

这些字母看起来就像挂在绳子上晾干的衣服，它们看起来更像是乐谱而不是文字。

One hot noontime, while he was poring over the, manuscripts, he sensed that he was not alone in the room.

一个炎热的中午，当他仔细阅读手稿时，他感觉到房间里并不孤单。

Against the light from the window, sitting with his hands on his knees, was Melquíades.

梅尔加德斯双手放在膝盖上，背对着窗外的光线坐着。

He was under forty years of age.

他当时还不到四十岁。

He was wearing the same old-fashioned vest and the hat that looked like a raven's wings, and across his pale temples there flowed the grease from his hair that had been melted by the heat, just as Aureli-ano and José Arcadio had seen him when they were children.

他穿着同样的老式背心，戴着乌鸦翅膀般的帽子，苍白的太阳穴上流淌着被高温融化的头发上的油脂，就像奥雷连诺和何塞·阿卡迪奥小时候看到的那样。

Aureli-ano Segun-do recognized him at once, because that hereditary memory had been transmitted from generation to generation and had come to through the memory of his grandfather.

奥雷连诺第二一眼就认出了他，因为那份遗传记忆是代代相传的，是通过他祖父的记忆而来的。

"Hello," Aureli-ano Segun-do said.

“你好，”奥雷连诺第二说。

From then on, for several years, they saw each other almost every afternoon.

从此以后的几年里，他们几乎每天下午都会见面。

Melquíades talked to him about the world, tried to infuse him with his old wisdom, but he refused to translate the manuscripts.

梅尔基亚德斯与他谈论这个世界，试图向他灌输他古老的智慧，但他拒绝翻译手稿。

"No one must know their meaning until he has reached one hundred years of age," he explained.

“任何人在一百岁之前都必须知道它们的含义，”他解释道。

Aureli-ano kept those meetings secret forever.

奥雷里亚诺永远对这些会议保密。

On one occasion he felt that his private world had fallen apart because úrsula came in when Melquíades was in the room.

有一次，他觉得自己的私人世界已经分崩离析，因为梅尔基亚德斯在房间里时乌苏拉进来了。

But she did not see him.

但她没有看到他。

"Who were you talking to?" she asked him.

“你在跟谁说话？”她问他。

"Nobody," Aureli-ano Segun-do said.

“没有人，”奥雷连诺第二说道。

"That's what your great-grandfather did," úrsula, said. "He used to talk to himself too."

“这就是你的曾祖父所做的，”乌苏拉说。 “他也常常自言自语。”

José Arcadio Segun-do, in the meantime, had satisfied his wish to see a shooting.

与此同时，何塞·阿卡迪奥第二满足了他观看枪击事件的愿望。

For the rest of his life he would remember the livid flash of the six simultaneous shots-and the echo of the discharge as it broke against the hills and the sad smile and perplexed eyes of the man being shot, who stood erect while his shirt became soaked with blood, and who was still smiling even when they untied him from the post and put him in a box filled with quicklime.

在他的余生中，他会记得同时发生的六枪的铁青闪光，以及子弹在山上爆发时的回声，以及被枪杀的人悲伤的微笑和困惑的眼睛，他笔直地站着，而他的衬衫被鲜血浸透了，甚至当他们把他从柱子上解开并把他放入一个装满生石灰的盒子时，他仍然微笑着。

"He's alive," he thought.

“他还活着，”他想。

"They're going to bury him alive."

“他们要把他活埋。”

It made such an impression on him that from then on he detested military practices and war, not because of the executions but because of the horrifying custom of burying the victims alive.

这给他留下了深刻的印象，从那时起他就厌恶军事行动和战争，不是因为处决，而是因为活埋受害者的可怕习俗。

No one knew then exactly when he began to ring the bells in the church tower and assist Father Antonio Isabel, the successor to "The Pup," at mass, and take can of the fighting cocks in the courtyard of the parish house.

当时没有人确切知道他什么时候开始敲响教堂塔楼的钟声，协助“小狗”的继承人安东尼奥·伊莎贝尔神父做弥撒，并在教区庭院里取斗鸡罐头。

When Colonel Geri-neldo Márquez found out he scolded him strongly for learning occupations repudiated by the Liberals.

当赫里内尔多·马尔克斯上校发现后，他严厉斥责了他学习被自由党拒绝的职业。

"The fact is," he answered, "I think I've turned out to be a Conservative."

“事实是，”他回答道，“我想我已经成为一名保守党人了。”

He believed it as if it had been determined by fate.

他相信这一切就像是命中注定一样。

Colonel Geri-neldo Márquez, scandalized, told úrsula about it.

杰里内尔多·马尔克斯上校感到震惊，他把这件事告诉了乌苏拉。

"I go Tuesday nights," he confessed. "if you promise not to tell anyone I'll take you next Tuesday."

“我周二晚上去，”他承认道。 “如果你保证不告诉任何人，我下周二就带你去。”

Indeed, on the following Tuesday Petronio came down out of the tower with a wooden stool which until then no one had known the use of, and he took José Arcadio Segun-do to a nearby pasture.

事实上，在接下来的星期二，佩特罗尼奥带着一张木凳从塔楼下来，直到那时还没有人知道它的用途，他带着何塞·阿卡迪奥第二去了附近的牧场。

The boy became so taken with those nocturnal raids that it was a long time before he was seen at Catarino's.

男孩对这些夜间袭击非常着迷，以至于很长时间之后才在卡塔里诺家被人看见。

He became a cockfight man.

他成了一名斗鸡人。

"Take those creatures somewhere else," úrsula ordered him the first time she saw him come in with his fine fighting birds.

“把这些生物带到别的地方去，”乌苏拉第一次看到他带着他那只漂亮的斗鸟进来时就命令他。

"Roosters have already brought too much bitterness to this house for you to bring us any more."

“公鸡已经给这所房子带来了太多的痛苦，你不能再给我们带来太多的痛苦了。”

José Arcadio Segun-do took them away without any argument, but he continued breeding them at the house of Pilar Ternera, his grandmother, who gave him everything he needed in exchange for having him in her house.

何塞·阿卡迪奥第二没有任何争辩地把它们带走，但他继续在他的祖母皮拉尔·特内拉的家里饲养它们，她给了他所需的一切，以换取他在自己家里的权利。

He soon displayed in the cockpit the wisdom that Father Antonio Isabel had given him, and he made enough money not only to enrich his brood but also to look for a man's satisfactions.

很快，他就在驾驶舱中展现了安东尼奥·伊莎贝尔神父赋予他的智慧，他赚到了足够的钱，不仅可以养家糊口，还可以寻找一个人的满足感。

úrsula compared him with his brother at that time and could not understand how the twins, who looked like the same person in childhood, had ended up so differently.

乌苏拉当时将他与他的兄弟进行了比较，无法理解这对童年看起来像同一个人的双胞胎为何结局如此不同。

Her perplexity did not last very long, for quite soon Aureli-ano Segun-do began to show signs of laziness and dissipation.

她的困惑并没有持续太久，因为很快奥雷连诺第二就开始表现出懒惰和放荡的迹象。

While he was shut up in Melquíades' room he was drawn into himself the way Colonel Aureli-ano Buendía had been in his youth.

当他被关在梅尔基亚德斯的房间里时，他就像奥雷连诺·布恩迪亚上校年轻时那样陷入了自我封闭。

But a short time after the Treaty of Neerlandia, a piece of chance took him out of his withdrawn self and made him face the reality of the world.

但尼兰迪亚条约签订后不久，一次偶然的机会让他走出了孤僻的自我，面对了世界的现实。

A young woman who was selling numbers for the raffle of an accordion greeted him with a great deal of familiarity.

一位正在出售手风琴抽奖号码的年轻女子非常熟悉地向他打招呼。

Aureli-ano Segun-do was not surprised, for he was frequently confused with his brother.

奥雷连诺第二并不感到惊讶，因为他经常与他的兄弟混淆。

But he did not clear up the mistake, not even when the girl tried to soften his heart with sobs, and she ended taking him to her room.

但他并没有澄清这个错误，即使女孩试图用抽泣来软化他的心，她最终还是把他带回了自己的房间。

She liked him so much from that first meeting that she fixed things so that he would win the accordion in the raffle.

从第一次见面起，她就非常喜欢他，以至于她安排了一些事情，以便他能够在抽奖中赢得手风琴。

At the end of two weeks Aureli-ano Segun-do realized that the woman had been going to bed alternately with him and his brother, thinking that they were the same man, and instead of making things clear, he arranged to prolong the situation.

两周后，奥雷连诺第二意识到那个女人一直与他和他的兄弟轮流上床，以为他们是同一个男人，他没有把事情弄清楚，而是安排延长这种情况。

He did not return to Melquíades' room.

他没有回到梅尔加德斯的房间。

He would spend his afternoons in the courtyard, learning to play the accordion by ear over the protests úrsula, who at that time had forbidden music in the house because the mourning and who, in addition, despised the accordion as an instrument worthy only of the vagabond heirs Francisco the Man.

他会在院子里度过下午，在乌苏拉的抗议声中学习拉手风琴，当时乌苏拉禁止在家里播放音乐，因为哀悼，此外，他鄙视手风琴，认为只有流浪汉弗朗西斯科的继承人才配得上手风琴。

Nevertheless, Aureli-ano Segun-do became a virtuoso on the accordion and he still was after he had married and had children and was one of the most respected men in Macon-do.

尽管如此，Aureli-ano Segun-do 成为了一名手风琴演奏家，在他结婚生子之后仍然如此，并且是 Macon-do 最受尊敬的人之一。

For almost two months he shared the woman with his brother.

在将近两个月的时间里，他与他的兄弟分享了这个女人。

He would watch him, mix up his plans, and when he was sure that José Arcadio Segun-do was not going to visit their common mistress that night, he would go and sleep with her.

他会看着他，搅乱他的计划，当他确定何塞·阿卡迪奥第二当晚不会去拜访他们共同的情妇时，他就会去和她一起睡觉。

One morning he found that he was sick.

一天早上，他发现自己病了。

Two days later he found his brother clinging to a beam in the bathroom, soaked in sweat and with tears pouring down, and then he understood.

两天后，他发现弟弟在浴室里扶着横梁，浑身是汗，泪流满面，他才明白了。

His brother confessed to him that the woman had sent him away because he had given her what she called a low-life sickness.

当何塞·阿卡迪奥第二认为这条河可以通航时，他向他的兄弟详细介绍了他的计划，后者给了他企业所需的资金。

他消失了很长一段时间。

据说，他买船的计划只不过是当一艘奇怪的船只接近小镇的消息传开后，他用哥哥的钱来骗钱的诡计。

马孔多的居民不再记得何塞·阿卡迪奥·布恩迪亚的伟大事业，他们跑到河岸上，难以置信地看到第一艘也是最后一艘停靠在该镇的船抵达。

那只不过是一个木筏，由粗绳拉动，由二十个人沿着岸边行走。

何塞·阿卡迪奥第二在船头指挥着这一艰巨的演习，眼中闪烁着满意的光芒。

木筏是何塞·阿卡迪奥第二能够带到马孔多的唯一船只，而且只有一次，但他从未承认自己的事业失败，而是宣称自己的行为是意志力的胜利。

他向他的兄弟仔细地算了一笔帐，很快又回到了斗鸡的生活中。

美人雷梅黛丝被拥立为王后。

乌苏拉对曾孙女那令人不安的美丽感到不寒而栗，但她无法阻止这个选择。

在那之前，她成功地阻止了她上街，除非是和阿玛兰塔一起去参加弥撒，但她让她用黑色披肩遮住脸。

最不虔诚的人，那些会伪装成牧师，在卡塔里诺的店里做亵渎弥撒的人，会去教堂，目的是为了一睹美人雷梅迪奥斯的面容，哪怕只是一瞬间，她传奇般的美貌在整个沼泽地中引起了令人震惊的兴奋。

他们花了很长时间才能够这样做，如果他们从来没有这样做过，那对他们来说会更好，因为他们中的大多数人再也没有恢复平静的睡眠习惯。

使这一切成为可能的外国人，永远失去了平静，陷入了卑鄙和痛苦的深渊，多年后，他在铁轨上睡着了，被火车切成碎片。

从他出现在教堂的那一刻起，穿着绿色天鹅绒套装和绣花背心的他就没有人怀疑他来自远方，也许是来自国外某个遥远的城市，被美人雷梅黛丝的神奇魅力所吸引。

他是如此英俊，如此优雅端庄，如此风度，彼得罗·克雷斯皮在他身边简直就是个花花公子，许多女人都带着恶意的微笑低声说他才是真正应该戴披肩的人。

他没有与梅肯多的任何人交谈。

周日黎明时分，他像童话故事中的王子一样出现，骑着一匹银色马镫、铺着天鹅绒毯子的马，弥撒结束后离开了小镇。

The power of his presence was such that from the first time he was seen in the church everybody took it for granted that a silent and tense duel had been established between him and Remedios the Beauty, a secret pact, an irrevocable challenge that would end not only in love but also in death.

他的存在是如此强大，以至于从他第一次出现在教堂里开始，每个人都理所当然地认为他和美人雷梅黛丝之间已经建立了一场无声而紧张的决斗，这是一个秘密的契约，一个不可撤销的挑战，不仅会以爱情结束，还会以死亡告终。

第六个星期天，这位绅士出现了，手里拿着一朵黄玫瑰。

他听到群众起立，一如往常，最后他走到美人雷梅黛丝面前，递给她那朵孤零零的玫瑰。

她以一种自然的姿势接过它，仿佛她已经做好了迎接这种致敬的准备，然后她揭开脸，微笑着向她致谢。

That was all she did.

这就是她所做的一切。

Not only for the gentleman, but for all the men who had the unfortunate privilege of seeing her, that was an eternal instant.

不仅对这位绅士来说，而且对所有有幸见到她的男人来说，那都是永恒的瞬间。

From then on the gentleman had a band of musicians play beside the window of Remedios the Beauty, sometimes until dawn.

从那时起，这位绅士就派了一群音乐家在美人雷梅迪奥斯的窗边演奏，有时演奏到黎明。

Aureli-ano Segun-do was the only one who felt a cordial compassion for him and he tried to break his perseverance.

奥雷连诺第二是唯一对他怀有诚挚同情心的人，他试图打破他的坚持。

"Don't waste your time any more," he told him one night.

“别再浪费时间了，”一天晚上他告诉他。

"The women in this house are worse than mules."

“这家里的女人连骡子都不如。”

他向他提出了友谊，邀请他去香槟沐浴，试图让他明白他家里的女人的内心都是燧石做的，但他无法削弱他的固执。

Exasperated by the interminable nights of music, Colonel Aureli-ano Buendía threatened to cure his affliction with a few pistol shots.

奥雷利亚诺·布恩迪亚上校被无休止的音乐之夜激怒了，他威胁要开几枪来治愈他的痛苦。

Nothing made him desist except his own lamentable state of demoralization.

除了他自己可悲的士气低落之外，没有什么能让他停下来。

From a welldressed and neat individual he became filthy and ragged.

他从一个穿着考究、整洁的人变得肮脏、衣衫褴褛。

It was rumored that he had abandoned power and fortune in his distant nation, although his origins were actually never known.

有传言说他已经放弃了遥远国家的权力和财富，尽管他的出身实际上无人知晓。

He became argumentative, a barroom brawler, and he would wake up rolling in his own filth in Catarino's store.

他变得爱争论，在酒吧里打架，他会在卡塔里诺的店里醒来，在自己的污秽中打滚。

The saddest part of his drama was that Remedios the Beauty did not notice him not even when he appeared in church dressed like a prince.

他的戏剧中最悲伤的部分是，即使当他打扮得像王子一样出现在教堂时，美人雷梅迪奥斯也没有注意到他。

She accepted the yellow rose without the least bit of malice, amused, rather, by the extravagance of the act, and she lifted her shawl to see his face better, not to show hers.

她毫无恶意地接受了黄玫瑰，相反，她对这种奢侈的行为感到好笑，她掀起披肩，以便更好地看到他的脸，而不是露出她的脸。

Actually, Remedios the Beauty was not a creature of this world.

事实上，美人蕾梅黛丝并不是这个世界的生物。

Until she was well along in puberty Santa Sofía de la.

直到她正处于青春期，圣索菲亚德拉。

Piedad had to bathe and dress her, and even when she could take care of herself it was necessary to keep an eye on her so that she would not paint little animals on the walls with a stick daubed in her own excrement.

皮达得给她洗澡、穿衣，即使她能照顾自己，也得看管她，免得她用沾有自己排泄物的棍子在墙上画小动物。

她二十岁了，不识字，不识字，无法使用餐桌上的银器，赤身裸体在屋子里闲逛，因为她的本性拒绝一切习俗。

当年轻的卫队长向她表白时，她拒绝了他，只是因为他的轻浮让她吃惊。

"See how simple he is," she told Amaranta.

“看看他是多么简单，”她告诉阿玛兰塔。

"He says that he's dying because of me, as if I were a bad case of colic."

“他说他因为我而死，就好像我是一个绞痛病人一样。”

When, indeed, they found dead beside her window, Remedios the Beauty confirmed her first impression.

当他们确实在她的窗边发现死者时，美人雷梅黛丝证实了她的第一印象。

It seemed as if some penetrating lucidity permitted her to see the reality of things beyond any formalism.

似乎某种透彻的清醒让她能够超越任何形式主义看到事物的现实。

That at least was the point of view of Colonel Aureli-ano Buendía, for whom Remedios the Beauty was in no way mentally retarded, as was generally believed, but quite the opposite.

至少奥雷利亚诺·布恩迪亚上校是这么认为的，对他来说，美人雷梅迪奥斯绝不是像人们普遍认为的那样有智力障碍，而是恰恰相反。

“就像她从二十年的战争中回来一样，”他会说。

乌苏拉则感谢上帝赐予这个家庭一个异常纯洁的生物，但同时她也为自己的美貌感到不安，因为这对她来说似乎是一种矛盾的美德，是她纯真的中心的一个恶魔陷阱。

正是出于这个原因，她决定让她远离这个世界，保护她免受尘世的一切诱惑，却不知道美人雷梅黛丝从在母亲子宫里的时候起就没有受到任何传染。

她从来没有想到他们会选举她为狂欢节狂欢节的选美皇后。

尽管不情愿，她最终还是被说服了，同意了加冕礼。

雷梅迪奥斯·布恩迪亚将成为节日最高统治者的消息在几个小时内就超出了沼泽的范围，到达了她的美貌无人知晓的遥远地方，引起了那些仍然认为她的姓氏是颠覆象征的人的焦虑。

The anxiety was baseless.

这种焦虑毫无根据。

If anyone had become harmless at that time it was the aging and disillusioned Colonel Aureli-ano Buendía, who was slowly losing all contact with the reality of the nation.

如果说当时有谁变得无害的话，那就是年迈且幻灭的奥雷利阿诺·布恩迪亚上校，他正在慢慢失去与国家现实的所有联系。

在他的工作室里，他与世界其他地方的唯一关系就是他的小金鱼生意。

在和平的最初几天里，一名守卫他的房子的士兵会去沼泽的村庄里卖掉它们，然后带着硬币和消息回来。

他会说，在自由党的支持下，保守党政府正在改革日历，以便每位总统都能继续执政一百年。

与罗马教廷的协约终于签署了，一位红衣主教从罗马而来，戴着钻石王冠和纯金王座，自由党部长们跪下亲吻他的戒指拍照。

一家西班牙公司的女主角途经首都时被一群蒙面强盗绑架，并在接下来的周日在共和国总统的避暑别墅里裸体跳舞。

“别跟我谈论政治，”上校会告诉他。

"Our business is selling little fishes."

“我们的生意是卖小鱼。”

有传言说他不想听到任何有关该国局势的消息，因为他在自己的工作室里变得越来越富有，当这件事传到乌苏拉耳中时，她笑了起来。

With her terrible practical sense she could not understand the colonel's business as he exchanged little fishes for gold coins and then converted the coins into little fishes, and so on, with the result that he had to work all the harder with the more he sold in order to satisfy an exasperating vicious circle.

以她糟糕的现实感，她无法理解上校的做法，他用小鱼换金币，然后把金币换成小鱼，如此循环往复，结果他只能越卖越卖力，以满足令人恼火的恶性循环。

Actually, what interested was not the business but the work.

其实，感兴趣的不是生意，而是工作。

He needed so much concentration to link scales, fit minute rubies into the eyes, laminate gills, and put on fins that there was not the smallest empty moment left for him to fill with his disillusionment the war.

他需要高度集中精力来连接鳞片、在眼睛里镶嵌微小的红宝石、层压鳃、装上鳍，以至于没有留下哪怕一丁点的空白时间来填补他对战争的幻灭。

So absorbing was the attention required by the delicacy of his artistry that in a short time he had aged more than during all the years of the war, and his position had twisted his spine the close work had used up his eyesight, but the implacable concentration awarded him with a peace of the spirit.

他的精致艺术所需要的注意力如此引人入胜，以至于在很短的时间内，他就比战争期间的所有岁月都苍老了，他的位置扭曲了他的脊椎，近距离的工作耗尽了他的视力，但不屈不挠的专注给了他精神上的平静。

The last time he was seen to take an interest in some matter related to the war was when a group of veterans from both parties sought his support for the approval of lifetime pensions, which had always been promised and were always about to be put into effect.

上一次看到他对一些与战争有关的事情感兴趣是在一群两党退伍军人寻求他支持批准终身养老金时，这些养老金一直是承诺的，而且总是即将付诸实施。

"Forget about it," he told them.

“忘了这件事吧，”他告诉他们。

"You can see how I refuse my pension in order to get rid of the torture of waiting for it until the day I died."

“你可以看到我如何拒绝领取养老金，以摆脱等待直到死亡那天的折磨。”

起初，赫里内尔多·马尔克斯上校会在黄昏时拜访他，他们都会坐在临街的门口谈论过去。

But Amaranta could not bear the memories that that man, whose baldness had plunged him into the abyss of premature old age, aroused in her, and she would torment snide remarks until he did not come back except on special occasions and he finally disappeared, extinguished by paralysis.

但阿玛兰塔无法忍受那个因秃顶而陷入过早衰老的深渊的男人在她心中唤起的回忆，她会用讽刺的言论折磨他，直到他除了特殊场合外不再回来，最后消失，瘫痪殆尽。

Taciturn, silent, insensible to the new breath of vitality that was shaking the house, Colonel Aureli-ano Buendía could understand only that the secret of a good old age is simply an honorable pact with solitude.

奥雷连诺·布恩迪亚上校沉默寡言，对震撼整个房子的新的活力气息麻木不仁，他只能明白，安享晚年的秘诀就是与孤独达成一项光荣的契约。

He would get up at five in the morning after a light sleep, have his eternal mug of bitter coffee in the kitchen, shut himself up all day in the workshop, and at four in the afternoon he would go along the porch dragging a stool, not even noticing the fire of the rose bushes or the brightness of the hour or the persistence of Amaranta, whose melancholy made the noise of a boiling pot, which was perfectly perceptible at dusk, and he would sit in the street door as long as the mosquitoes would allow him to.

早上五点，他会在浅睡后起床，在厨房里永远喝一杯苦咖啡，把自己关在车间里，一整天都把自己关在车间里，下午四点，他会拖着一张凳子沿着门廊走，甚至没有注意到玫瑰丛的火焰，也没有注意到时刻的明亮，也没有注意到阿玛兰塔的坚持，他的忧郁发出沸腾的锅的声音，这在黄昏时是完全可以察觉的，他会坐在临街的门口，只要蚊子会让他这样做。

Someone dared to disturb his solitude once.

曾经有人敢打扰他的孤独。

"Right here," he answered. "Waiting for my funeral procession to pass."

“就在这里，”他回答道。 “等待我的送葬队伍过去。”

因此，因他的姓氏再次公开出现而引起的与雷梅黛丝美人加冕有关的焦虑是毫无根据的。

进入青春期后很久，她还要桑塔索菲亚·德拉·彼达为她洗澡穿衣；等到她生活能够自理的时候，仍得有人留神她，防止她用自己的一撅儿粪便在墙上画小动物。

她到了二十岁还没有学会读写，不会使用餐具，赤着身子在家里走来走去，因为她天生拒斥一切常规。

当年轻的警卫队队长向她表白爱意时，她被这冒失的举动吓了一跳，当下拒绝了。

“你看他好傻，”她对阿玛兰妲说，“他说他因为我难受得要死，好像我是绞肠痧似的。”

当那年轻人果真死在她的窗下，美人儿蕾梅黛丝便证实了自己最初的印象。

“你们看，”她评论道，“他就是太傻了。”

她仿佛拥有一种敏锐的洞察力，能够透过一切表象看到事物的本质。

至少奥雷里亚诺·布恩迪亚上校这样认为，在他看来美人儿蕾梅黛丝根本不像人们所想的那样弱智，恰恰相反。

“她就好像是打了二十年仗回来的人。”

他常这样说。

至于乌尔苏拉，她常常感谢上帝赐予这家里一个纯洁无瑕的灵魂，但同时也为其美貌而惶惶不安。

她觉得那是与美德相冲突的优点，是隐藏在纯真之中的邪恶圈套。

为此乌尔苏拉决定让她远离尘世，避开凡间一切引诱，殊不知她早在母亲腹中就注定永不受玷染。

她从未想过在一场群魔乱舞的狂欢节上成为选美女王。

然而一心想化装成老虎的奥雷里亚诺第二把神甫安东尼奥·伊莎贝尔请到家中，说服乌尔苏拉相信狂欢节并非像她认为的那样是异教节日，其实出自天主教传统。

最终她被说服，勉强同意了举行女王加冕礼。

蕾梅黛丝·布恩迪亚将成为狂欢节女王的消息在短短几个小时内飞越大泽区的边界，传至遥远到从未听说过她超凡美貌的地方，引发了一些人的担忧。

在他们眼中，这一姓氏仍与反叛作乱联系在一起。

这种担忧是毫无根据的。

如果那个时期还有谁能做到与世无争，那便是日渐衰老、归于平凡的奥雷里亚诺·布恩迪亚上校，他渐渐失去了与家国现世的一切联系。

他待在作坊里足不出户，小金鱼生意是联结他与外界的唯一纽带。

停战初期被派来监视他家的士兵中有一个负责将成品拿到大泽区的村镇去贩卖，又带着金币和最近的消息回来。

他说保守党政府获得了自由党人的支持，正在修改历书使每任总统可以在任一百年。

他说政府终于与教廷达成协定，还有一位红衣主教从罗马被派来，头戴钻石冠冕，端坐在实心的黄金宝座上，而自由党的部长们都跪下来亲吻他的戒指并摄影存照。

他说一家西班牙剧团经过首都时，女主唱在化妆室里被一群戴面具的人绑架，到了星期天却出现在共和国总统的消夏别墅里跳裸体舞。

“别跟我谈政治，”奥雷里亚诺·布恩迪亚上校对他说，“咱们只管卖小金鱼。”

坊间有流言说他对国家现状不闻不问是因为靠着作坊发了财，但传到乌尔苏拉耳中时只引发了她的讥笑。

从她纯粹的实用主义观念出发，她实在难以理解上校的生意意义何在：用小金鱼换来金币，随即把金币变成小金鱼，如此反复，卖得越多活计越辛苦，却只是为了维持一种不断加剧的恶性循环。

实际上上校在乎的不是生意，而是干活本身。

他必须全神贯注地投入，嵌上片片鱼鳞，用红宝石微粒镶鱼眼，锤出鱼鳃，添上尾鳍，再没有余暇为战后的失落而烦恼。

这门精密的手艺极其耗费心神，令他在短短时间内比在整个战争年代衰老得更甚。

不变的坐姿令他脊柱变形，精确到毫米的工艺使他视力受损，但不容丝毫分心的专注让他获得了心灵的平静。

一群来自两个党派的老兵寻求他的支持，希望解决养老抚恤金的问题，因为政府一直许诺却从未落实，那是他最后一次涉入与战争相关的事务。

“忘了这回事吧，”他劝道，“你们看我早就放弃了养老金，免得傻等到死受罪。”

开始的时候，赫里内勒多·马尔克斯上校每到傍晚都来拜访，两人坐在大门口追忆过往。

但阿玛兰妲无法忍受这个疲累的男人勾起自己的回忆，他的秃顶正将他引向未老先衰的深渊。

于是她无理地令他处处难堪，终于他只在特殊情况下才登门，最后因瘫痪而彻底消失。

沉默寡言的奥雷里亚诺·布恩迪亚上校对家中重新焕发的活力视若无睹，约略懂得幸福晚年的秘诀不过是与孤独签下不失尊严的协定罢了。

每天浅睡一觉后五点起床，照例到厨房喝上一杯不加糖的咖啡，然后一整天关在作坊里，到下午四点才拖着一张矮凳走过长廊，如火如荼的玫瑰、明媚耀眼的暮色、阿玛兰妲的漠然—每到黄昏时分她的忧郁就发出开锅般清晰可闻的声响—都无法吸引他的注意。

他在临街的门口坐下，直到蚊虫开始肆虐才回到家中。

一次，有人鼓起勇气打扰了他的独处。

“您近来可好，上校？”那人走过时问道。

“就在这儿待着，”他回答，“等着给我下葬。”

由此可见，因美人儿蕾梅黛丝被封为女王，他的姓氏重新出现在公众视野而引发的担忧实际上缺乏事实根据。

然而很多人并不这样想。

市镇上的人们对迫近的悲剧一无所知，广场上人声鼎沸，一派欢天喜地的热闹景象。

狂欢节达到疯狂的最高潮，奥雷里亚诺第二实现了扮成老虎的愿望，快乐地在狂热的人群中奔跑，因长久吼叫而嗓音嘶哑。

就在此时，通往大泽区的路上出现了一队庞大的化装人群，他们抬着一座金光耀眼的花台，上面是一位人们所能想象出的最迷人的女子。

一时间，马孔多本来心境平和的居民都摘下面具好看清这位神奇的美人，她头戴翡翠王冠，身披白鼬皮斗篷，俨然拥有货真价实的权柄，而不仅是闪光的缀片和皱纹纸打扮起来的女王。

不乏目光敏锐的人觉察到这是一种挑衅行为。

但奥雷里亚诺第二马上摆脱了困惑，宣布将新来者视作贵宾，并很聪明地让美人儿蕾梅黛丝和外来的女王在同一高台上就座。

直到午夜时分，那些化装成贝都因人的外来者仍在热闹狂欢，并用绚丽的烟火和精彩的杂耍助兴，让人想起当年吉卜赛人的本领。

突然间，就在节日的最高潮，有人打破了这微妙的平衡。

“自由党万岁！”他喊道，“奥雷里亚诺·布恩迪亚上校万岁！”

步枪倾泻的弹雨压倒了烟火的光彩，惊恐的喊叫盖过了乐声，欢乐被恐慌所取代。

多年以后，人们仍确信外来女王的亲卫队实际上是一个中队的政府军，每人的外袍下都暗藏着军用枪械。

政府在一份特别通告中否认了这一指控，并许诺将彻底清查这场血案。

然而真相从未澄清，广为流传的说法是女王卫队在未受到任何挑衅的情况下，根据指挥官的暗示进入战斗状态，毫无怜悯地向人群开火。

当一切恢复平静，那些假贝都因人都不见了。

广场上有死有伤，倒下了九个小丑、四个科隆比纳、十七个纸牌大王、一个魔鬼、三个音乐家、两个法国贵族和三个日本皇后。

在惊惶困惑中，何塞·阿尔卡蒂奥第二救出了美人儿蕾梅黛丝，而奥雷里亚诺第二把外来女王抱在怀里带回了家，她的衣服被撕扯成碎片，白鼬皮斗篷上满是血迹。

她名叫费尔南达·德尔·卡皮奥。

她在全国最美丽的五千名女性中力拔头筹，他们领她到马孔多来，答应封她为马达加斯加女王。

乌尔苏拉把她当作女儿对待。

人们不但没有质疑她的无辜，反而都同情她的天真。

屠杀发生六个月后，当伤者都已痊愈，公墓上最后的花朵全部凋落的时候，奥雷里亚诺第二远赴她和父亲生活的城市找她。

后来在马孔多与她成婚，喧闹的欢庆活动持续了二十天。
