第七章

On the fifth day-again, as always, it was thanks to the sheep-the secret of the little prince's life was revealed to me. Abruptly, without anything to lead up to it, and as if the question had been born of long and silent meditation on his problem, he demanded:

第五天——再一次，像往常一样，多亏了那只羊——小王子生活的秘密向我揭开了。突然，没有任何铺垫，仿佛这个问题是他对自己面临的难题进行了长时间默默深思后产生的，他问道：

¨A sheep—if it eats little bushes, does it eat flowers, too?¨ ¨A sheep,¨ I answered, ¨eats anything it finds in its reach.¨ ¨Even flowers that have thorns?¨

“一只羊——如果它吃小灌木，它也吃花吗？”“一只羊，”我回答说，“碰到什么就吃什么。”“连有刺的花也吃吗？”

¨Yes, even flowers that have thorns.¨ ¨Then the thorns—what use are they?¨

“是的，连有刺的花也吃。”“那么刺——它们有什么用呢？”

I did not know. At that moment I was very busy trying to unscrew a bolt that had got stuck in my

我不知道。那时我正忙着想拧下一颗卡在我的

engine. I was very much worried, for it was becoming clear to me that the breakdown of my plane was extremely serious. And I had so little drinking-water left that I had to fear for the worst.

发动机里的螺栓。我非常着急，因为我越来越清楚地意识到，飞机的故障极其严重。而且我剩下的饮用水已经很少了，我不得不做最坏的打算。

¨The thorns—what use are they?¨

“那些刺——它们有什么用呢？”

The little prince never let go of a question, once he had asked it. As for me, I was upset over that bolt. And I answered with the first thing that came into my head:

小王子一旦提出一个问题，就决不放弃。至于我，我正为那颗螺栓心烦意乱。于是我脑子里想到什么就顺口回答了：

¨The thorns are of no use at all. Flowers have thorns just for spite!¨ ¨Oh!¨

“那些刺一点用也没有。花长刺纯粹是出于恶意！”“哦！”

There was a moment of complete silence. Then the little prince flashed back at me, with a kind of

有那么一阵子，周围一片死寂。接着，小王子冲我回了一句，带着一种

resentfulness:

怨愤：

¨I don't believe you! Flowers are weak creatures. They are naive. They reassure themselves as best they can. They believe that their thorns are terrible weapons ...¨

“我不信！花是柔弱的生物。它们很天真。它们只是在尽力保护自己。它们以为自己的刺是可怕的武器……”

I did not answer. At that instant I was saying to myself: ¨If this bolt still won't turn, I am going to knock it out with the hammer.¨ Again the little prince disturbed my thoughts:

我没有回答。就在那一刻，我正在心里对自己说：“如果这颗螺栓还是拧不动，我就用锤子把它敲掉。”小王子再次打断了我的思绪：

¨And you actually believe that the flowers-¨

“你居然真的相信花——”

¨Oh, no!¨ I cried. ¨No, no, no! I don't believe anything. I answered you with the first thing that came into my head. Don't you see—I am very busy with matters of consequence!¨

“哦，不！”我叫道。“不，不，不！我什么也不信。我只是想到什么就顺口对你说了。你没看到吗——我正忙着正经事呢！”

He stared at me, thunderstruck. ¨Matters of consequence!¨

他目瞪口呆地盯着我。“正经事！”

He looked at me there, with my hammer in my hand, my fingers black with engine-grease, bending down over an object which seemed to him extremely ugly ...

他看着我站在那里，手里拿着锤子，手指上沾满了黑色的发动机油污，正弯着腰摆弄一个在他看来极其丑陋的东西……

¨You talk just like the grown-ups!¨

“你说话的样子就像那些大人们一样！”

That made me a little ashamed. But he went on, relentlessly: ¨You mix everything up together ...

这让我感到有些惭愧。但他毫不留情地继续说道：“你把所有的东西都混为一谈了……

You confuse everything ...¨ He was really very angry. He tossed his golden curls in the breeze.

你把什么都搞混了……”他是真的非常生气。他在微风中甩动着他金色的卷发。

¨1 know a planet where there is a certain red-faced gentleman. He has never smelled a flower. He has never looked at a star. He has never loved any one. He has never done anything in his life but add up figures. And all day he says over and over, just like you: 'I am busy with matters of consequence!' And that makes him swell up with pride. But he is not a man-he is a mushroom!¨

“我认识一个星球，上面有一个红脸先生。他从来没有闻过一朵花。他从来没有看过一颗星星。他从来没有爱过任何人。他一生中除了算账以外，什么事也没做过。他一整天都像你一样，反反复复地说：‘我正忙着正经事呢！’这让他骄傲得膨胀起来。但他不是一个人——他是一个蘑菇！”

¨A what?¨

“一个什么？”

¨A mushroom!¨

“一个蘑菇！”

The little prince was now white with rage.

小王子气得脸色惨白。

¨The flowers have been growing thorns for millions of years. For millions of years the sheep have been eating them just the same. And is it not a matter of consequence to try to understand why the flowers go to so much trouble to grow thorns which are never of any use to them? Is the warfare between the sheep and the flowers not important? Is this not of more consequence than a fat red-faced gentleman's sums? And if I know—I, myself—one flower which is unique in the world, which grows nowhere but on my planet, but which one little sheep can destroy in a single bite some morning, without even noticing what he is doing-Oh! You think that is not important!¨

“几百万年来，花儿一直长着刺。几百万年来，羊也照样吃它们。难道试图去弄明白，为什么花儿要费那么大劲去长出那些对它们毫无用处的刺，这不是一件正经事吗？羊和花之间的战争难道不重要吗？难道这不比一个胖乎乎的红脸先生的算账更重要吗？如果我——我自己——认识一朵世界上独一无二的花，它除了长在我的星球上，别的地方都不长，但是某天早晨一只小羊可能一口就把她给毁了，甚至都没意识到自己在做什么——哦！你觉得这不重要吗！”

His face turned from white to red as he continued:

他接着往下说，脸色由白转红：

¨If some one loves a flower, of which just one single blossom grows in all the millions and millions of stars, it is enough to make him happy just to look at the stars. He can say to himself, 'Somewhere, my flower is there ...' But if the sheep eats the flower, in one moment all his stars will be darkened ... And you think that is not important!¨

“如果一个人爱上了一朵花，一朵在千百万颗星星上只长了这么一朵的花，那么只要看着这些星星，就足以让他感到幸福。他可以对自己说，‘我的花就在那儿，在某个地方……’可是如果羊吃掉了那朵花，那么在这一瞬间，他所有的星星都会暗淡无光……你居然觉得这不重要！”

He could not say anything more. His words were choked by sobbing.

他再也说不下去了。他的话被抽泣声噎住了。

The night had fallen. I had let my tools drop from my hands. Of what moment now was my hammer, my bolt, or thirst, or death? On one star, one planet, my planet, the Earth, there was a little prince to be comforted. I took him in my arms, and rocked him. I said to him:

夜幕降临了。我已经放下了手中的工具。现在我的锤子、我的螺栓、口渴或者死亡，还有什么要紧的呢？在一颗星星上，在一颗行星上，在我的行星，在地球上，有一个小王子需要安慰。我把他抱在怀里，摇着他。我对他说：

¨The flower that you love is not in danger. I will draw you a muzzle for your sheep. I will draw you a railing to put around your flower. I will—¨

“你爱的那朵花没有危险。我会给你的羊画一个口罩。我会给你的花画一个栅栏。我会——”

I did not know what to say to him. I felt awkward and blundering. I did not know how I could reach him, where I could overtake him and go on hand in hand with him once more.

我不知道该对他说些什么。我感到很尴尬，手足无措。我不知道怎样才能触及他的心，怎样才能再次赶上他，和他携手并肩。

It is such a secret place, the land of tears.

泪水的国度，是如此神秘的一个地方。
