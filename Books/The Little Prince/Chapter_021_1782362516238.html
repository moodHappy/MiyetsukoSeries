第二十一章

It was then that the fox appeared. ¨Good morning,¨ said the fox.

就在这时，狐狸出现了。“早上好，”狐狸说。

¨Good morning,¨ the little prince responded politely, although when he turned around he saw nothing. ¨I am right here,¨ the voice said, ¨under the apple tree.¨

“早上好，”小王子礼貌地回答，尽管他转过身去什么也没看到。“我在这里，”那个声音说，“在苹果树下。”

¨Who are you?¨ asked the little prince, and added, ¨You are very pretty to look at.¨ ¨I am a fox,¨ the fox said.

“你是谁？”小王子问，并补充道，“你长得真好看。”“我是一只狐狸，”狐狸说。

¨Come and play with me,¨ proposed the little prince. ¨I am so unhappy.¨ ¨I cannot play with you,¨ the fox said. ¨I am not tamed.¨ ¨Ah! Please excuse me,¨ said the little prince. But, after some thought, he added: ¨What does that mean—'tame'?¨

“来跟我一起玩吧，”小王子提议道。“我太伤心了。”“我不能跟你玩，”狐狸说。“我还没有被驯化。”“啊！请原谅，”小王子说。但思索了一会儿后，他又补充道：“‘驯化’是什么意思？”

¨You do not live here,¨ said the fox. ¨What is it that you are looking for?¨

“你不是这里的人，”狐狸说。“你在找什么？”

¨I am looking for men,¨ said the little prince. ¨What does that mean—'tame'?¨

“我在找人，”小王子说。“‘驯化’是什么意思？”

¨Men,¨ said the fox. ¨They have guns, and they hunt. It is very disturbing. They also raise chickens.

“人，”狐狸说。“他们有枪，他们打猎。这真让人头疼。他们还养鸡。

These are their only interests. Are you looking for chickens?¨

这些就是他们仅有的兴趣。你在找鸡吗？”

¨No,¨ said the little prince. ¨I am looking for friends. What does that mean-'tame'?¨ ¨It is an act too often neglected,¨ said the fox. It means to establish ties.¨ ¨To establish ties'?¨

“不，”小王子说。“我在找朋友。‘驯化’到底是什么意思？”“这是人们经常忽视的一种行为，”狐狸说。“它的意思是‘建立联系’。”“‘建立联系’？”

¨Just that,¨ said the fox. ¨To me, you are still nothing more than a little boy who is just like a hundred thousand other little boys. And I have no need of you. And you, on your part, have no need of me. To you, I am nothing more than a fox like a hundred thousand other foxes. But if you tame me, then we shall need each other. To me, you will be unique in all the world. To you, I shall be unique in all the world ...¨

“正是如此，”狐狸说。“对你来说，我只是一只和成千上万只别的狐狸一模一样的狐狸。我不需要你。你对我来说也只是一个小男孩，和成千上万个别的小男孩毫无区别。但是，如果你驯化了我，我们就彼此需要了。对我来说，你就是世界上独一无二的；对你来说，我也是世界上独一无二的……”

¨I am beginning to understand,¨ said the little prince. ¨There is a flower ... I think that she has tamed me . .

“我有点明白了，”小王子说。“有一朵花……我想她已经驯化我了……”

¨It is possible,¨ said the fox. ¨On the Earth one sees all sorts of things.¨ ¨Oh, but this is not on the Earth!¨ said the little prince.

“这是可能的，”狐狸说。“在地球上，什么奇奇怪怪的事都能见到。”“哦，可这不在地球上！”小王子说。

The fox seemed perplexed, and very curious. ¨On another planet?¨

狐狸显得很困惑，也非常好奇。“在另一个星球上？”

¨Yes.¨

“是的。”

¨Are there hunters on that planet?¨ ¨No.¨

“那个星球上有猎人吗？”“没有。”

¨Ah, that is interesting! Are there chickens?¨ ¨No.¨

“啊，那挺有意思！有鸡吗？”“没有。”

¨Nothing is perfect,¨ sighed the fox. But he came back to his idea.

“没有什么是完美的，”狐狸叹了口气。但他又回到了刚才的话题。

¨My life is very monotonous,¨ the fox said. ¨I hunt chickens; men hunt me. All the chickens are just alike, and all the men are just alike. And, in consequence, I am a little bored. But if you tame me, it will be as if the sun came to shine on my life. I shall know the sound of a step that will be different from all the others. Other steps send me hurrying back underneath the ground. Yours will call me, like music, out of my burrow. And then look: you see the grain-fields down yonder? I do not eat bread. Wheat is of no use to me. The wheat fields have nothing to say to me. And that is sad. But you have hair that is the color of gold. Think how wonderful that will be when you have tamed me! The grain, which is also golden, will bring me back the thought of you. And I shall love to listen to the wind in the wheat...¨

“我的生活非常单调，”狐狸说。“我抓鸡，人抓我。所有的鸡都一模一样，所有的人也都一模一样。因此，我有点厌烦。但是，如果你驯化了我，我的生活就像洒满了阳光一样。我会认出一种与众不同的脚步声。别的脚步声会让我慌忙钻回地底下。而你的脚步声会像音乐一样，把我从洞穴里召唤出来。再看吧：你看到那边的麦田了吗？我不吃面包。小麦对我也没什么用。麦田对我也没什么意义。这未免太悲哀了。但是你有一头金色的头发。想想看，等你驯化了我，那该有多美妙啊！金黄的麦子会让我想起你。我也会喜欢听风吹过麦浪的声音……”

The fox gazed at the little prince, for a long time. ¨Please—tame me!¨ he said.

狐狸久久地注视着小王子。“求求你——驯化我吧！”他说。

¨I want to, very much,¨ the little prince replied. ¨But I have not much time. I have friends to discover, and a great many things to understand.¨

“我很想这么做，”小王子回答说。“可是我没有多少时间了。我还有朋友要去找，还有许多事情要弄懂。”

¨One only understands the things that one tames,¨ said the fox. ¨Men have no more time to understand anything. They buy things all ready made at the shops. But there is no shop anywhere where one can buy friendship, and so men have no friends any more. If you want a friend, tame me ...¨

“只有被驯化的东西，你才能去了解，”狐狸说。“现在的人们再也没有时间去了解任何东西了。他们在商店里买现成的东西。但是没有哪个商店里有朋友可买，所以人类再也没有朋友了。如果你想要一个朋友，那就驯化我吧……”

¨What must I do, to tame you?¨ asked the little prince.

“我该怎么做呢？”小王子问。

¨You must be very patient,¨ replied the fox. ¨First you will sit down at a little distance from me— like that— in the grass. I shall look at you out of the corner of my eye, and you will say nothing. Words are the source of misunderstandings. But you will sit a little closer to me, every day ...¨

“你必须非常有耐心，”狐狸回答说。“首先你要离我稍微远一点儿——就像那样——坐在草地上。我用眼角的余光偷看你，你什么也不说。话语是误会的根源。但是，每天你可以坐得离我更近一点儿……”

The next day the little prince came back.

第二天，小王子又来了。

¨It would have been better to come back at the same hour,¨ said the fox. ¨If, for example, you come at four o'clock in the afternoon, then at three o'clock I shall begin to be happy. I shall feel happier and happier as the hour advances. At four o'clock, I shall already be worrying and jumping about. I shall show you how happy I am! But if you come at just any time, I shall never know at what hour my heart is to be ready to greet you ... One must observe the proper rites ...¨

“最好在同一个时间来，”狐狸说。“比方说，如果你下午四点钟来，那么从三点钟起，我就会开始感到幸福。时间越接近，我就越感到幸福。到了四点钟的时候，我已经坐立不安、心慌意乱了；我就会发现幸福是多么昂贵！可是，如果你随便什么时候来，我就不知道该在什么时候准备好我的心情……我们需要仪式……”

¨What is a rite?¨ asked the little prince.

“什么是仪式？”小王子问。

¨Those also are actions too often neglected,¨ said the fox. ¨They are what make one day different from other days, one hour from other hours. There is a rite, for example, among my hunters. Every Thursday they dance with the village girls. So Thursday is a wonderful day for me! I can take a walk as far as the vineyards. But if the hunters danced at just any time, every day would be like every other day, and I should never have any vacation at all.¨

“这也是经常被人们忽视的行为，”狐狸说。“它就是使某一天与其他日子不同，使某一时刻与其他时刻不同。比如说，我的猎人们就有一种仪式。他们每周四都和村里的姑娘们跳舞。所以星期四对我来说是个美妙的日子！我可以一直散步到葡萄园去。如果猎人们随便什么时候跳舞，日子就会千篇一律，我也就沒有假期了。”

So the little prince tamed the fox. And when the hour of his departure drew near-¨Ah,¨ said the fox, ¨I shall cry.¨

于是，小王子驯化了狐狸。当他离开的时刻临近时——“啊，”狐狸说，“我会哭的。”

¨It is your own fault,¨ said the little prince. ¨I never wished you any sort of harm; but you wanted me to tame you ...¨

“这怪你自己，”小王子说。“我从来没想过要伤害你，可你却非要我驯化你……”

¨Yes, that is so,¨ said the fox.

“是的，确实如此，”狐狸说。

¨But now you are going to cry!¨ said the little prince. ¨Yes, that is so,¨ said the fox.

“可你现在却要哭了！”小王子说。“是的，确实如此，”狐狸说。

¨Then it has done you no good at all!¨

“那对你一点好处也没有哇！”

¨It has done me good,¨ said the fox, ¨because of the color of the wheat fields.¨ And then he added: ¨Go and look again at the roses. You will understand now that yours is unique in all the world. Then

“对我还是有好处的，”狐狸说，“因为麦田的颜色。”接着他又补充道：“去重新看看那些玫瑰花吧。现在你就会明白，你的那朵玫瑰花是世界上独一无二的。然后

come back to say goodbye to me, and I will make you a present of a secret.¨

回来跟我道别，我要送你一个秘密作为礼物。”

The little prince went away, to look again at the roses.

小王子离开去重新看那些玫瑰花。

¨You are not at all like my rose,¨ he said. ¨As yet you are nothing. No one has tamed you, and you have tamed no one. You are like my fox when I first knew him. He was only a fox like a hundred thousand other foxes. But I have made him my friend, and now he is unique in all the world.¨

“你们一点也不像我的玫瑰，”他说。“此时此刻，你们什么都不是。还没有人驯化过你们，你们也没有驯化过任何人。你们就像我刚认识那只狐狸时的样子。他当时也只是和成千上万只别的狐狸一模一样。但是我已经把他当成了我的朋友，现在他是世界上独一无二的了。”

And the roses were very much embarrassed.

玫瑰花们听了感到十分尴尬。

¨You are beautiful, but you are empty,¨ he went on. ¨One could not die for you. To be sure, an ordinary passerby would think that my rose looked just like you—the rose that belongs to me. But in herself alone she is more important than all the hundreds of you other roses: because it is she that I have watered; because it is she that I have put under the glass globe; because it is she that I have sheltered behind the screen; because it is for her that I have killed the caterpillars (except the two or three that we saved to become butterflies); because it is she that I have listened to, when she grumbled, or boasted, or ever sometimes when she said nothing. Because she is my rose.

“你们很美，但你们是空虚的，”他又继续说道。“没有人会为你们去死。当然啦，一个普通的过路人会觉得我的玫瑰和你们长得差不多——那朵属于我的玫瑰。但她单独一朵就比你们成百上千朵加起来还要重要，因为她是我浇灌出来的；因为她是我放在玻璃罩下的；因为她是我用屏风保护起来的；因为为了她，我才消除了毛毛虫（除了留下两三只用来变成蝴蝶的）；因为倾听过她的抱怨和吹嘘，甚至有时哪怕她默默无语，我也在一旁倾听。因为她是我的玫瑰。

And he went back to meet the fox. ¨Goodbye,¨ said the fox.

说罢，他回去找那只狐狸。“再见，”狐狸说。

¨Goodbye,¨ said the fox. ¨And now here is my secret, a very simple secret: It is only with the heart that one can see rightly; what is essential is invisible to the eye.¨

“再见，”狐狸说。“现在这是我的秘密，一个非常简单的秘密：只有用心才能看得清；实质性的东西，用眼睛是看不见的。”

¨What is essential is invisible to the eye,¨ the little prince repeated, so that he would be sure to remember.

“实质性的东西，用眼睛是看不见的，”小王子重复道，以便自己能牢记在心。

¨It is the time you have wasted for your rose that makes your rose so important.¨

“你在你的玫瑰花身上耗费的时间，使得你的玫瑰花变得如此重要。”

¨It is the time I have wasted for my rose—¨ said the little prince, so that he would be sure to remember.

“我在我的玫瑰花身上耗费的时间——”小王子重复道，以便自己能牢记在心。

¨Men have forgotten this truth,¨ said the fox. ¨But you must not forget it. You become responsible, forever, for what you have tamed. You are responsible for your rose ...¨

“人类已经忘记了这个真理，”狐狸说。“但你千万不要忘记。对于你驯化的东西，你永远要负起责任。你要对你的玫瑰负责……”

¨I am responsible for my rose,¨ the little prince repeated, so that he would be sure to remember.

“我要对我的玫瑰负责，”小王子重复道，以便自己能牢记在心。
