第十章

He found himself in the neighborhood of the asteroids 325, 326, 327, 328, 329, and 330. He began, therefore, by visiting them, in order to add to his knowledge.

他发现自己刚好在325、326、327、328、329和330号小行星附近。因此，他决定先去拜访它们，以便增长些见识。

The first of them was inhabited by a king. Clad in royal purple and ermine, he was seated upon a throne which was at the same time both simple and majestic.

第一颗小行星上住着一位国王。他身穿紫红色的白鼬皮长袍，坐在一个既简单又威严的宝座上。

¨Ah! Here is a subject,¨ exclaimed the king, when he saw the little prince coming. And the little prince asked himself:

“啊！来了一个臣民，”国王看到小王子走过来，大声喊道。小王子心里暗自纳闷：

¨How could he recognize me when he had never seen me before?¨

“他以前从来没见过我，怎么能认出我呢？”

He did not know how the world is simplified for kings. To them, all men are subjects.

他不知道在国王们眼里，世界被大大简化了。对他们来说，所有人都是臣民。

¨Approach, so that I may see you better,¨ said the king, who felt consumingly proud of being at last a king over somebody.

“走近点，好让我看得更清楚些，”国王说道，他终于能成为某个人的国王了，心里感到无比骄傲。

The little prince looked everywhere to find a place to sit down; but the entire planet was crammed and obstructed by the king's magnificent ermine robe. So he remained standing upright, and, since he was tired, he yawned.

小王子四下张望，想找个地方坐下来；但是整个星球都被国王那件华丽的白鼬皮长袍塞满了。所以他只好一直站着，又因为太累了，他打了个哈欠。

¨It is contrary to etiquette to yawn in the presence of a king,¨ the monarch said to him. ¨I forbid you to do so.¨

“在国王面前打哈欠是违背礼仪的，”这位君主对他说，“我禁止你这么做。”

¨I can't help it. I can't stop myself,¨ replied the little prince, thoroughly embarrassed. ¨I have come on a long journey, and I have had no sleep ...¨

“我实在忍不住了。我控制不住自己，”小王子窘迫极了，回答道，“我赶了很长的路，一直没有睡觉……”

¨Ah, then,¨ the king said. ¨I order you to yawn. It is years since I have seen anyone yawning. Yawns, to me, are objects of curiosity. Come, now! Yawn again! It is an order.¨

“啊，既然这样，”国王说，“我命令你打哈欠。我已经好多年没见过别人打哈欠了。对我来说，打哈欠是件稀罕事。来吧！再打个哈欠！这是命令。”

¨That frightens me ... I cannot, any more ...¨ murmured the little prince, now completely abashed.

“这让我很害怕……我打不出来了……”小王子嘀咕着，现在他彻底不知所措了。

¨Hum! Hum!¨ replied the king. ¨Then I—I order you sometimes to yawn and sometimes to—¨ He sputtered a little, and seemed vexed.

“哼！哼！”国王回答说。“那么我——我命令你有时打哈欠，有时——”他支吾了一下，似乎有些恼火。

For what the king fundamentally insisted upon was that his authority should be respected. He tolerated no disobedience. He was an absolute monarch. But, because he was a very good man, he made his orders reasonable.

因为这位国王最坚持的根本原则就是他的权威必须得到尊重。他绝不容忍任何违抗。他是一位专制君主。不过，因为他是个非常善良的人，所以他下达的命令都是合情合理的。

¨If I ordered a general,¨ he would say, by way of example, ¨if I ordered a general to change himself into a sea bird, and if the general did not obey me, that would not be the fault of the general. It would be my fault.¨

“如果我命令一位将军，”他举例说道，“如果我命令一位将军变成一只海鸟，而那位将军没有服从我，那不是将军的错。那将是我的错。”

¨May I sit down?¨ came now a timid inquiry from the little prince.

“我可以坐下吗？”小王子怯生生地问了一句。

¨I order you to do so,¨ the king answered him, and majestically gathered in a fold of his ermine mantle.

“我命令你坐下，”国王回答他，并威严地将他那件白鼬皮斗篷的下摆收拢了一下。

But the little prince was wondering ... The planet was tiny. Over what could this king really rule?

但小王子心里很纳闷……这颗星球这么小。这位国王到底能统治什么呢？

¨Sire,¨ he said to him, ¨I beg that you will excuse my asking you a question-¨

“陛下，”他对国王说，“请原谅我想问您一个问题——”

¨I order you to ask me a question,¨ the king hastened to assure him.

“我命令你问我问题，”国王急忙向他保证。

¨Sire—over what do you rule?¨

“陛下——您统治着什么呢？”

¨Over everything,¨ said the king, with magnificent simplicity.

“统治一切，”国王极其简单明了地说。

¨Over everything?¨

“统治一切？”

The king made a gesture, which took in his planet, the other planets, and all the stars.

国王做了一个手势，把他的星球、其他的星球以及所有的星星都囊括在内。

¨Over all that?¨ asked the little prince.

“统治所有这一切？”小王子问。

¨Over all that,¨ the king answered.

“统治所有这一切，”国王回答。

For his rule was not only absolute: it was also universal.

因为他的统治不仅是绝对的，而且是全宇宙的。

¨And the stars obey you?¨

“那星星也都服从您吗？”

¨Certainly they do,¨ the king said. ¨They obey instantly. I do not permit insubordination.¨

“那是当然，”国王说，“它们立刻就会服从。我不允许有任何违抗。”

Such power was a thing for the little prince to marvel at. If he had been master of such complete authority, he would have been able to watch the sunset, not forty-four times in one day, but seventy-two, or even a hundred, or even two hundred times, without ever having to move his chair. And because he felt a bit sad as he remembered his little planet which he had forsaken, he plucked up his courage to ask the king a favor:

这样的权力让小王子惊叹不已。如果他掌握了如此绝对的权威，他就能在一天之内不仅看到四十四次日落，而是七十二次，甚至一百次，或者两百次，而且根本不需要挪动他的椅子。因为想起了自己抛弃的那颗小星球，他心里感到有些难过，于是他鼓起勇气请求国王帮个忙：

¨I should like to see a sunset... Do me that kindness ... Order the sun to set...¨

“我想看日落……请您行行好……命令太阳落山吧……”

¨I have nothing more to do here,¨ he said to the king. ¨So I shall set out on my way again.¨

¨If Your Majesty wishes to be promptly obeyed,¨ he said, ¨he should be able to give me a reasonable order. He should be able, for example, to order me to be gone by the end of one minute. It seems to me that conditions are favorable ...¨

As the king made no answer, the little prince hesitated a moment. Then, with a sigh, he took his leave.

见国王没有回答，小王子犹豫了片刻。然后，他叹了口气，动身离开了。

¨I make you my Ambassador,¨ the king called out, hastily. He had a magnificent air of authority.

“我任命你为我的大使，”国王急忙大声喊道。他摆出了一副威严的派头。

¨The grown-ups are very strange,¨ the little prince said to himself, as he continued on his journey.

“大人们可真奇怪，”小王子一边继续他的旅程，一边心里暗自嘀咕。
