第五章

As each day passed I would learn, in our talk, something about the little prince's planet, his departure from it, his journey. The information would come very slowly, as it might chance to fall from his thoughts. It was in this way that I heard, on the third day, about the catastrophe of the baobabs.

随着时间一天天过去，在我们的交谈中，我逐渐了解到了一些关于小王子星球、他的离开以及他的旅程的事情。这些信息来得非常缓慢，往往是他在沉思中偶然流露出来的。就在第三天，我以这种方式听说了关于猴面包树的灾难。

This time, once more, I had the sheep to thank for it. For the little prince asked me abruptly—as if seized by a grave doubt—¨It is true, isn't it, that sheep eat little bushes?¨

这一次，我又得感谢那只羊了。因为小王子突然问我——好像陷入了深深的疑惑——“羊吃小灌木，这是真的，对吧？”

¨Yes, that is true.¨ ¨Ah! Iam glad!¨

“是的，那是真的。”“啊！我很高兴！”

I did not understand why it was so important that sheep should eat little bushes. But the little prince added: ¨Then it follows that they also eat baobabs?¨

我不明白为什么羊吃小灌木显得这么重要。但是小王子接着问道：“那么，它们也吃猴面包树吗？”

I pointed out to the little prince that baobabs were not little bushes, but, on the contrary, trees as big as castles; and that even if he took a whole herd of elephants away with him, the herd would not eat up one single baobab.

我向小王子指出，猴面包树不是小灌木，恰恰相反，它们是像城堡一样大的树；而且就算他带走一整群大象，这群大象也吃不完哪怕一棵猴面包树。

The idea of the herd of elephants made the little prince laugh. ¨We would have to put them one on top of the other,¨ he said.

一群大象的想法让小王子笑了起来。“那我们不得不把它们一只只叠起来，”他说。

But he made a wise comment:

但他又说了一句很聪明的话：

¨Before they grow so big, the baobabs start out by being little.¨

“在它们长得那么大之前，猴面包树一开始也是很小的。”

¨That is strictly correct,¨ I said. ¨But why do you want the sheep to eat the little baobabs?¨

“完全正确，”我说。“可是你为什么想让羊去吃小猴面包树呢？”

He answered me at once, ¨Oh, come, come!¨, as if he were speaking of something that was self- evident. And I was obliged to make a great mental effort to solve this problem, without any assistance.

他立刻回答我：“哦，拜托！”好像在说一件理所当然的事情。而我不得不费尽心思，在没有任何帮助的情况下去解开这个问题。

Indeed, as I learned, there were on the planet where the little prince lived—as on all planets—good plants and bad plants. In consequence, there were good seeds from good plants, and bad seeds from bad plants. But seeds are invisible. They sleep deep in the heart of the earth's darkness, until some one among them is seized with the desire to awaken. Then this little seed will stretch itself and begin— timidly at first—to push a charming little sprig inoffensively upward toward the sun. If it is only a sprout of radish or the sprig of a rose-bush, one would let it grow wherever it might wish. But when it is a bad plant, one must destroy it as soon as possible, the very first instant that one recognizes it.

的确，正如我所了解到的，在小王子居住的星球上——就像在所有星球上一样——有好的植物和坏的植物。因此，好植物结好种子，坏植物结坏种子。但是种子是看不见的。它们沉睡在黑暗的大地深处，直到其中有一颗突然产生了苏醒的渴望。于是，这颗小种子就会伸伸懒腰，开始——起初还很怯生生地——朝着太阳的方向，无害地探出一根迷人的小嫩枝。如果它只是一棵萝卜苗或者玫瑰树的嫩枝，人们大可以任由它想长在哪儿就长在哪儿。可如果它是一棵坏植物，就必须在认出它的那一刻，尽早将它拔除。

Now there were some terrible seeds on the planet that was the home of the little prince; and these were the seeds of the baobab. The soil of that planet was infested with them. A baobab is something you will never, never be able to get rid of if you attend to it too late. It spreads over the entire planet. It bores clear through it with its roots. And if the planet is too small, and the baobabs are too many, they split it in pieces ...

在小王子的家乡，那个星球上就有一些可怕的种子；这些就是猴面包树的种子。那个星球的土壤里到处都是这种种子。对于猴面包树，如果你处理得太晚，就永远、永远也无法摆脱它了。它会蔓延到整个星球。它的根会把星球完全穿透。如果星球太小，而猴面包树又太多，它们就会把星球撑得四分五裂……

¨It is a question of discipline,¨ the little prince said to me later on. ¨When you've finished your own toilet in the morning, then it is time to attend to the toilet of your planet, just so, with the greatest care. You must see to it that you pull up regularly all the baobabs, at the very first moment when they can be distinguished from the rosebushes which they resemble so closely in their earliest youth. It is very tedious work,¨ the little prince added, ¨but very easy.¨

“这是一个纪律问题，”小王子后来对我说。“当你早晨梳洗完毕之后，就该仔细地去给你的星球梳洗打扮了。你必须坚持按时去拔掉所有的猴面包树，就在它们刚能和玫瑰树区分开来的那一刻，因为它们在很小的时候长得非常像。这是一项非常乏味的工作，”小王子补充道，“但却非常容易。”

And one day he said to me: ¨You ought to make a beautiful drawing, so that the children where you live can see exactly how all this is. That would be very useful to them if they were to travel some day. Sometimes,¨ he added, ¨there is no harm in putting off a piece of work until another day. But when it is a matter of baobabs, that always means a catastrophe. I knew a planet that was inhabited by a lazy man. He neglected three little bushes ...¨

有一天他对我说：“你应该画一幅漂亮的画，好让你住的那个地方的孩子们能清楚地看到这一切是怎么回事。如果有一天他们去旅行，这对他们会非常有用的。有时候，”他补充说，“把工作推迟到改天做也没有什么坏处。但如果遇到的是猴面包树，那总是意味着一场灾难。我认识一个星球，上面住着一个懒汉。他忽略了三棵小灌木……”

So, as the little prince described it to me, I have made a drawing of that planet. I do not much like to take the tone of a moralist. But the danger of the baobabs is so little understood, and such considerable risks would be run by anyone who might get lost on an asteroid, that for once I am breaking through my reserve. ¨Children,¨ I say plainly, ¨watch out for the baobabs!¨

于是，根据小王子的描述，我把那个星球画了下来。我不太喜欢用说教者的口吻说话。但是猴面包树的危险鲜为人知，如果有谁在小行星上迷了路，将面临巨大的风险，因此这一次我打破了我的矜持。“孩子们，”我坦白地说道，“当心猴面包树！”

My friends, like myself, have been skirting this danger for a long time, without ever knowing it; and so it is for them that I have worked so hard over this drawing. The lesson which I pass on by this means is worth all the trouble it has cost me.

我的朋友们和我一样，很久以来一直在这个危险的边缘徘徊而不自知；所以正是为了他们，我才如此努力地画出了这幅画。我通过这种方式传递的教训，值得我为之付出的所有辛劳。

Perhaps you will ask me, ¨Why are there no other drawing in this book as magnificent and impressive as this drawing of the baobabs?¨

也许你会问我：“为什么这本书里没有其他像这幅猴面包树一样宏伟壮观、令人印象深刻的画呢？”

The reply is simple. I have tried. But with the others I have not been successful. When I made the drawing of the baobabs I was carried beyond myself by the inspiring force of urgent necessity.

答案很简单。我尝试过了。但在画其他的画时，我并没有成功。当我画猴面包树的时候，我被一种迫在眉睫的使命感所鼓舞，超常发挥了。
