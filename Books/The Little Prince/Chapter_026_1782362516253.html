CHAPTER XXVI

第二十六章

Beside the well there was the ruin of an old stone wall. When I came back from my work, the next evening, I saw from some distance away my little prince sitting on top of a wall, with his feet dangling. And I heard him say:

在那口井旁边，有一道旧石墙的废墟。第二天傍晚，我干完活儿回来，远远地就看见我的小王子坐在墙顶上，两腿悬空晃荡着。我听见他说：

"Then you don't remember. This is not the exact spot." Another voice must have answered him, for he replied to it: "Yes, yes! It is the right day, but this is not the place."

“那么你不记得了。不是在这个确切的地方。”一定是有另一个声音回答了他，因为他接着对那声音说：“是的，是的！日子是对的，但地方不对。”

I continued my walk toward the wall. At no time did I see or hear anyone. The little prince, however, replied once again:

我继续朝石墙走去。我始终没有看见也没有听见任何人。然而小王子又一次回答道：

"—Exactly. You will see where my track begins, in the sand. You have nothing to do but wait for me there. I shall be there tonight."

“——一点不错。你会看到我的足迹在沙地上是从哪里开始的。你只要在那里等着我就行了。我今晚会到那里去。”

I was only twenty meters from the wall, and I still saw nothing. After a silence the little prince spoke again:

我离石墙只有二十米了，还是什么也没看见。一阵沉默之后，小王子又说道：

"You have good poison? You are sure that it will not make me suffer too long?" I stopped in my tracks, my heart torn asunder; but still I did not understand. "Now go away," said the little prince. "I want to get down from the wall."

“你的毒液管用吗？你确定不会让我疼太久吗？”我停住脚步，心都要碎了；可我还是不明白怎么回事。“现在你走吧，”小王子说，“我想从墙上下来了。”

I dropped my eyes, then, to the foot of the wall—and I leaped into the air. There before me, facing the little prince, was one of those yellow snakes that take just thirty seconds to bring your life to an end. Even as I was digging into my pocket to get out my revolver I made a running step back. But, at the noise I made, the snake let himself flow easily across the sand like the dying spray of a fountain, and, in no apparent hurry, disappeared, with a light metallic sound, among the stones.

于是，我把目光垂向墙脚——我一下子跳了起来。在我面前，正对着小王子的，是一条那种只需三十秒就能夺人性命的黄色毒蛇。我一边伸手往口袋里掏左轮手枪，一边奔跑着往后退了一步。可是，那蛇听到我弄出的声响，便像喷泉散落的水花一样，轻松地滑过沙地，不慌不忙地，带着一丝轻微的金属声，消失在石头缝里了。

I reached the wall just in time to catch my little man in my arms; his face was white as snow. "What does this mean?" I demanded. "Why are you talking with snakes?"

我正好赶到墙边，一把将我的小家伙搂在怀里；他的脸像雪一样白。“这是什么意思？”我质问道。“你怎么跟蛇说起话来了？”

I had loosened the golden muffler that he always wore. I had moistened his temples, and had given him some water to drink. And now I did not dare ask him any more questions. He looked at me very gravely, and put his arms around my neck. I felt his heart beating like the heart of a dying bird, shot with someone's rifle ...

我解开了他一直围着的金色围巾。我沾湿了他的太阳穴，喂他喝了一点水。此时，我不敢再多问他任何问题。他非常郑重地望着我，伸出双臂搂住我的脖子。我感觉到他的心跳，像一只被步枪击中、快要死去的小鸟的心在跳……

"I am glad that you have found what was the matter with your engine," he said. "Now you can go back home—"

“我很高兴，你找到了你发动机的毛病，”他说。“现在你可以回家去了——”

"How do you know about that?"

“这事你是怎么知道的？”

I was just coming to tell him that my work had been successful, beyond anything that I had dared to hope. He made no answer to my question, but he added: "I, too, am going back home today ..." Then, sadly—

我正好要来告诉他，我的修理工作出乎意料地成功了。他没有回答我的问题，只是加了一句：“我，今天也要回家去了……”然后，忧伤地——

"It is much farther ... It is much more difficult..."

“那要远得多……也要难得多……”

I realized clearly that something extraordinary was happening. I was holding him close in my arms as if he were a little child; and yet it seemed to me that he was rushing headlong toward an abyss from which I could do nothing to restrain him ...

我清楚地意识到，某种非同寻常的事情正在发生。我把他紧紧搂在怀里，就像抱着一个小孩子；可我却觉得，他正一头朝着一个深渊直坠下去，而我根本无法拉住他……

His look was very serious, like someone lost far away.

他的神情非常严肃，像一个迷失在远方的人。

"I have your sheep. And I have the sheep's box. And I have the muzzle ..." And he gave me a sad smile.

“我有你的绵羊。我还有装羊的盒子。我还有嘴套……”他冲我忧伤地微微一笑。

I waited a long time. I could see that he was reviving little by little. "Dear little man," I said to him, "you are afraid ..." He was afraid, there was no doubt about that. But he laughed lightly. "I shall be much more afraid this evening ..."

我等了很久。我看得出他正一点一点地缓过来。“亲爱的小家伙，”我对他说，“你在害怕……”他确实在害怕，这毫无疑问。但他轻轻笑了笑。“今天晚上，我会更害怕……”

Once again I felt myself frozen by the sense of something irreparable. And I knew that I could not bear the thought of never hearing that laughter any more. For me, it was like a spring of fresh water in the desert.

我再次感到自己被某种无法挽回的感觉冻住了。而且我知道，一想到再也听不见这笑声，我就无法忍受。对我来说，它就像沙漠里的一汪清泉。

"Little man," I said, "I want to hear you laugh again." But he said to me:

“小家伙，”我说，“我想再听听你的笑声。”可他对我说：

"Tonight, it will be a year ... My star, then, can be found right above the place where I came to the Earth, a year ago ..."

“今天晚上，就满一年了……我的那颗星，会正好停在我一年前落到地球的那个地方的上空……”

"Little man," I said, "tell me that it is only a bad dream—this affair of the snake, and the meeting-place, and the star..."

“小家伙，”我说，“告诉我，这一切只是一场噩梦——这蛇的事、这约会的地点、这颗星……”

But he did not answer my plea. He said to me, instead: "The thing that is important is the thing that is not seen ..." "Yes, I know ..."

但他没有回应我的恳求，而是对我说：“重要的东西是看不见的……”“是的，我知道……”

"It is just as it is with the flower. If you love a flower that lives on a star, it is sweet to look at the sky at night. All the stars are a-bloom with flowers ..."

“这就像那朵花一样。如果你爱上了一朵长在一颗星星上的花，那么晚上仰望天空，就会觉得很甜蜜。所有的星星都像是开满了鲜花……”

"Yes, I know ..."

“是的，我知道……”

"It is just as it is with the water. Because of the pulley, and the rope, what you gave me to drink was like music. You remember—how good it was."

“这也像水一样。因为有了辘轳，有了绳子，你给我喝的水就像音乐一样。你记得——那水有多好喝。”

"Yes, I know..."

“是的，我知道……”

"And at night you will look up at the stars. Where I live everything is so small that I cannot show you where my star is to be found. It is better, like that. My star will just be one of the stars, for you. And so you will love to watch all the stars in the heavens ... they will all be your friends. And, besides, I am going to make you a present..."

“到了晚上，你会仰望星空。我住的地方，一切都那么小，我没法指给你看我的星星在哪里。这样反而更好。对你来说，我的星星只不过是满天繁星中的一颗。这样你就会喜欢看天上所有的星星了……它们都会变成你的朋友。而且，我还要送你一件礼物……”

He laughed again.

他又笑了。

"Ah, little prince, dear little prince! I love to hear that laughter!"

“啊，小王子，亲爱的小王子！我就爱听这笑声！”

"That is my present. Just that. It will be as it was when we drank the water ..." "What are you trying to say?"

“这就是我的礼物。就是这样。就像我们喝水时那样……” “你想说什么？”

"All men have the stars," he answered, "but they are not the same things for different people. For some, who are travelers, the stars are guides. For others they are no more than little lights in the sky. For others, who are scholars, they are problems. For my businessman they were wealth. But all these stars are silent. You—you alone—will have the stars as no one else has them—"

“每个人都有星星，”他回答说，“但对不同的人来说，它们并不一样。对旅行者来说，星星是向导。对另一些人来说，它们不过是天空中的小光点。对学者来说，它们是研究的课题。对我遇到的那个商人来说，它们是财富。但所有这些星星都是沉默的。而你——只有你——拥有的星星是别人没有的——”

"What are you trying to say?"

“你想说什么？”

"In one of the stars I shall be living. In one of them I shall be laughing. And so it will be as if all the stars were laughing, when you look at the sky at night... You—only you—will have stars that can laugh!"

“我将会在某颗星星上生活。我将会在某颗星星上笑。所以，当你在夜晚仰望天空时，就好像所有星星都在笑……你——只有你——拥有会笑的星星！”

And he laughed again.

他又笑了。

"And when your sorrow is comforted (time soothes all sorrows) you will be content that you have known me. You will always be my friend. You will want to laugh with me. And you will sometimes open your window, so, for that pleasure ... And your friends will be properly astonished to see you laughing as you look up at the sky! Then you will say to them, 'Yes, the stars always make me laugh!' And they will think you are crazy. It will be a very shabby trick that I shall have played on you ..."

“当你的悲伤得到抚慰时（时间会抚平所有悲伤），你会因为认识了我而感到心满意足。你永远都会是我的朋友。你会想和我一起笑。有时候，你会为了这份快乐，像这样打开窗户……你的朋友们看到你仰望着天空笑，准会大吃一惊！那时你就对他们说：‘是啊，星星总是让我发笑！’他们会以为你疯了。这就算是我跟你开的一个挺恶作剧的玩笑吧……”

And he laughed again.

他又笑了。

"It will be as if, in place of the stars, I had given you a great number of little bells that knew how to laugh ..."

“那就好像，我送给你的不是星星，而是一大堆会笑的小铃铛……”

And he laughed again. Then he quickly became serious: "Tonight—you know ... Do not come." "I shall not leave you," I said.

他又笑了。然后他很快变得严肃起来：“今天晚上——你知道……别来。”“我不会离开你的，”我说。

"I shall look as if I were suffering. I shall look a little as if I were dying. It is like that. Do not come to see that. It is not worth the trouble ..."

“我看上去会像是在受罪。我看上去会有点像是要死了。就是那样的。别来看这个。不值得费这个心……”

"I shall not leave you." But he was worried.

“我不会离开你的。”可他很担心。

"I tell you—it is also because of the snake. He must not bite you. Snakes—they are malicious creatures. This one might bite you just for fun ..."

“我告诉你——这也是因为那条蛇。不能让它咬到你。蛇——它们是恶毒的东西。这条蛇可能会为了好玩咬你一口……”

"I shall not leave you."

“我不会离开你的。”

But a thought came to reassure him:

不过，有个念头让他又放下心来：

"It is true that they have no more poison for a second bite."

“没错，它们第二次咬的时候就没有毒液了。”

That night I did not see him set out on his way. He got away from me without making a sound. When I succeeded in catching up with him he was walking along with a quick and resolute step. He said to me merely:

那天夜里，我没有看到他动身上路。他悄无声息地从我身边走开了。等我终于追上他时，他正迈着又快又坚决的步子走着。他只是对我说：

"Ah! You are there ..."

“啊！你在这里……”

And he took me by the hand. But he was still worrying.

然后他拉住了我的手。可他还在担心。

"It was wrong of you to come. You will suffer. I shall look as if I were dead; and that will not be true ..." I said nothing.

“你不该来的。你会难过的。我看上去会像是死了；但那不是真的……”我没说话。

"You understand ... it is too far. I cannot carry this body with me. It is too heavy." I said nothing. "But it will be like an old abandoned shell. There is nothing sad about old shells ..." I said nothing. He was a little discouraged. But he made one more effort:

“你明白的……路途太远了。我没法带着这具身体走。它太沉了。”我没说话。“但它只会像个被遗弃的旧壳一样。旧壳没什么可悲伤的……”我没说话。他有点泄气了。但他又努力了一下：

"You know, it will be very nice. I, too, shall look at the stars. All the stars will be wells with a rusty pulley. All the stars will pour out fresh water for me to drink ..."

“你知道，会很美好的。我也会看星星。所有的星星都会变成有生锈辘轳的水井。所有的星星都会给我倒出清凉的水来喝……”

I said nothing.

我没说话。

"That will be so amusing! You will have five hundred million little bells, and I shall have five hundred million springs of fresh water ..."

“那该多有趣啊！你会有五亿个小铃铛，我会有五亿汪清泉……”

And he too said nothing more, because he was crying ... "Here it is. Let me go on by myself."

然后他也什么都没再说了，因为他哭了……“就是这里了。让我自己走吧。”

And he sat down, because he was afraid. Then he said, again:

他坐了下来，因为他害怕。然后他又说道：

"You know—my flower ... I am responsible for her. And she is so weak! She is so naïve! She has four thorns, of no use at all, to protect herself against all the world ..."

“你知道——我的花……我对她是有责任的。而她又是那么柔弱！她又是那么天真！她只有四根刺，一点用处也没有，用它们来对抗整个世界……”

I too sat down, because I was not able to stand up any longer. "There now—that is all..."

我也坐了下来，因为我再也站不住了。“好了——就这样吧……”

He still hesitated a little; then he got up. He took one step. I could not move.

他又犹豫了一会儿；然后他站了起来。他迈出一步。我却动弹不得。

There was nothing but a flash of yellow close to his ankle. He remained motionless for an instant. He did not cry out. He fell as gently as a tree falls. There was not even any sound, because of the sand.

只见他的脚踝边闪过一道黄光。他一动不动地站了片刻。他没有叫喊。他像一棵树那样轻轻地倒下了。因为是在沙地上，甚至没有发出任何声响。
