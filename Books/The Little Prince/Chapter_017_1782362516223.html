第十七章

When one wishes to play the wit, he sometimes wanders a little from the truth. I have not been altogether honest in what I have told you about the lamplighters. And I realize that I run the risk of giving a false idea of our planet to those who do not know it. Men occupy a very small place upon the Earth. If the two billion inhabitants who people its surface were all to stand upright and somewhat crowded together, as they do for some big public assembly, they could easily be put into one public square twenty miles long and twenty miles wide. All humanity could be piled up on a small Pacific islet.

当一个人想卖弄聪明的时候，他有时就会稍微偏离一点事实。我刚才在向你们讲述点灯人的时候，并不完全诚实。我意识到，我可能会让那些不了解我们这颗星球的人对它产生一种错误的印象。人类在地球上占据的空间其实非常小。如果居住在地球表面的二十亿居民全部直立站着，并且像参加某种大型公众集会那样稍微挤一挤，他们可以轻而易举地被塞进一个长二十英里、宽二十英里的公共广场里。全人类完全可以堆积在太平洋的一个小岛上。

The grown-ups, to be sure, will not believe you when you tell them that. They imagine that they fill a great deal of space. They fancy themselves as important as the baobabs. You should advise them, then, to make their own calculations. They adore figures, and that will please them. But do not waste your time on this extra task. It is unnecessary. You have, I know, confidence in me.

当然了，当你把这些告诉大人们的时候，他们是不会相信你的。他们自以为占据了很大的空间。他们把自己想象得像猴面包树一样重要。那么，你应该建议他们自己去算一算。他们迷恋数字，这会让他们很高兴的。但你不要把时间浪费在这个额外的任务上。那没有必要。我知道，你是信任我的。

When the little prince arrived on the Earth, he was very much surprised not to see any people. He was beginning to be afraid he had come to the wrong planet, when a coil of gold, the color of the moonlight, flashed across the sand.

当小王子降落到地球上时，他惊讶地发现竟然一个人也没有。他开始害怕自己是不是来到了错误的星球，就在这时，一圈月光般颜色的金色东西在沙地上闪过。

¨Good evening,¨ said the little prince courteously. ¨Good evening,¨ said the snake.

“晚上好，”小王子礼貌地说道。“晚上好，”蛇说。

¨What planet is this on which I have come down?¨ asked the little prince. ¨This is the Earth; this is Africa,¨ the snake answered. ¨Ah! Then there are no people on the Earth?¨

“我降落下来的这颗是什么星球？”小王子问。“这是地球；这是非洲，”蛇回答道。“啊！这么说，地球上没有人类吗？”

¨This is the desert. There are no people in the desert. The Earth is large,¨ said the snake. The little prince sat down on a stone, and raised his eyes toward the sky.

“这里是沙漠。沙漠里是没有人的。地球很大，”蛇说。小王子在一块石头上坐了下来，抬眼望着天空。

¨I wonder,¨ he said, ¨whether the stars are set alight in heaven so that one day each one of us may find his own again ... Look at my planet. It is right there above us. But how far away it is!¨

“我在想，”他说，“天上的星星之所以被点亮，是不是为了让我们每个人有一天都能重新找到属于自己的那一颗……看看我的星球。它就在我们正上方。可是它离得有多远啊！”

¨It is beautiful,¨ the snake said. ¨What has brought you here?¨

“它很美，”蛇说。“是什么把你带到这里来的？”

¨I have been having some trouble with a flower,¨ said the little prince. ¨Ah!¨ said the snake.

“我和一朵花闹了点别扭，”小王子说。“啊！”蛇说。

And they were both silent.

然后他们俩都沉默了。

¨Where are the men?¨ the little prince at last took up the conversation again. ¨It is a little lonely in the desert...¨

“人类都在哪里呢？”小王子终于又挑起了话头。“在沙漠里有点孤独……”

¨It is also lonely among men,¨ the snake said. The little prince gazed at him for a long time.

“在人群中也一样孤独，”蛇说。小王子盯着它看了很长时间。

“无论我触碰到谁，我都能把他送回他所出生的那片土地，”蛇再次开口说道。“但你是纯洁诚实的，而且你来自一颗星星……”

The little prince made no reply.

小王子没有回答。

¨You move me to pity—you are so weak on this Earth made of granite,¨ the snake said. ¨I can help you, some day, if you grow too homesick for your own planet. I can-¨

“你让我心生怜悯——在这个由花岗岩构成的坚硬地球上，你是如此柔弱，”蛇说。“如果有一天你太想念你的星球了，我能帮你。我能——”

¨Oh! I understand you very well,¨ said the little prince. ¨But why do you always speak in riddles?¨ ¨I solve them all,¨ said the snake. And they were both silent.

“哦！我非常明白你的意思，”小王子说。“可是你为什么总是像打哑谜一样说话呢？”“我能解开所有的谜，”蛇说。然后他们俩都沉默了。
