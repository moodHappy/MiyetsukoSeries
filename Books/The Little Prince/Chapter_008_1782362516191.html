CHAPTER VIII

发布时间：2026/8/8 19:10:49

第八章

I soon learned to know this flower better. On the little prince's planet the flowers had always been very simple. They had only one ring of petals; they took up no room at all; they were a trouble to nobody. One morning they would appear in the grass, and by night they would have faded peacefully away. But one day, from a seed blown from no one knew where, a new flower had come up; and the little prince had watched very closely over this small sprout which was not like any other small sprouts on his planet. It might, you see, have been a new kind of baobab.

我很快就对这朵花有了更多的了解。在小王子的星球上，花儿一直都非常简单。它们只有单层花瓣，完全不占地方，也不给任何人添麻烦。某天早晨它们会在草丛中出现，到了晚上又会悄然凋谢。但是有一天，从一颗不知从哪儿吹来的种子里，长出了一朵新的花；小王子非常仔细地照看着这棵小苗，它和他星球上的任何其他小苗都不一样。你要知道，这有可能是一种新的猴面包树。

The shrub soon stopped growing, and began to get ready to produce a flower. The little prince, who was present at the first appearance of a huge bud, felt at once that some sort of miraculous apparition must emerge from it. But the flower was not satisfied to complete the preparations for her beauty in the shelter of her green chamber. She chose her colors with the greatest care. She dressed herself slowly. She adjusted her petals one by one. She did not wish to go out into the world all rumpled, like the field poppies. It was only in the full radiance of her beauty that she wished to appear. Oh, yes! She was a coquettish creature! And her mysterious adornment lasted for days and days.

这棵小灌木很快就不再长高，开始准备开出一朵花来。小王子看到那个巨大的花蕾初露端倪时，立刻就感觉到从里面肯定会绽放出某种奇迹般的幻景。可是这朵花并不满足于在她那绿色的闺房里完成为了展现美丽的准备工作。她极其精心地挑选着自己的颜色。她慢条斯理地梳妆打扮。她把花瓣一片一片地调整好。她不愿意像田野里的虞美人那样，带着满身褶皱来到这个世界。她只愿在自己最容光焕发的时候出现。哦，是的！她是个十分爱美的家伙！她那神秘的梳妆打扮持续了好几天。

Then one morning, exactly at sunrise, she suddenly showed herself.

终于，在一天早晨，就在日出的时候，她突然展露了真容。

And, after working with all this painstaking precision, she yawned and said:

经过了这番费尽心机、一丝不苟的劳作之后，她打了个哈欠说道：

¨Ah! I am scarcely awake. I beg that you will excuse me. My petals are still all disarranged ...¨ But the little prince could not restrain his admiration:

“啊！我才刚刚睡醒。请你原谅。我的花瓣还是乱蓬蓬的……”但是小王子抑制不住自己的爱慕之情：

¨Oh! How beautiful you are!¨

“哦！你真美！”

¨Am I not?¨ the flower responded, sweetly. ¨And I was born at the same moment as the sun ...¨

“是吧？”花儿甜甜地回答，“而且我是和太阳同时出生的呢……”

The little prince could guess easily enough that she was not any too modest—but how moving—and exciting—she was!

小王子很容易就能猜到她不太谦虚——但是她多动人——多令人激动啊！

¨I think it is time for breakfast,¨ she added an instant later. ¨If you would have the kindness to think of my needs—¨

没过多久她又接着说：“我想现在是吃早餐的时候了。如果你能行行好，替我考虑一下我的需要——”

And the little prince, completely abashed, went to look for a sprinkling-can of fresh water. So, he tended the flower.

于是小王子满脸羞愧，赶紧去找了一把浇水壶，装满清水。就这样，他伺候起了这朵花。

So, too, she began very quickly to torment him with her vanity—which was, if the truth be known, a little difficult to deal with. One day, for instance, when she was speaking of her four thorns, she said to the little prince:

同样地，她也很快就开始用她的虚荣心来折磨他了——说实话，这虚荣心还真有点难对付。例如有一天，当她谈到自己的四根刺时，她对小王子说：

¨Let the tigers come with their claws!¨

“让老虎带着它们的利爪来吧！”

¨There are no tigers on my planet,¨ the little prince objected. ¨And, anyway, tigers do not eat weeds.¨ ¨I am not a weed,¨ the flower replied, sweetly. ¨Please excuse me ...¨

“我的星球上没有老虎，”小王子反驳道，“而且，不管怎样，老虎是不吃草的。”“我不是草，”花儿甜甜地回答。“请原谅……”

¨I am not at all afraid of tigers,¨ she went on, ¨but I have a horror of drafts. I suppose you wouldn't have a screen for me?¨

“我一点也不怕老虎，”她继续说道，“但我很怕穿堂风。我想你大概没有屏风吧？”

¨A horror of drafts—that is bad luck, for a plant,¨ remarked the little prince, and added to himself, ¨This flower is a very complex creature ...¨

“怕穿堂风——这对一株植物来说可真是不幸，”小王子评论道，并在心里暗想，“这朵花的心思可真复杂……”

¨At night I want you to put me under a glass globe. It is very cold where you live. In the place I came from-

“到了晚上，我要你把我放在一个玻璃罩里。你住的这个地方太冷了。在我来的那个地方——”

But she interrupted herself at that point. She had come in the form of a seed. She could not have known anything of any other worlds. Embarrassed over having let herself be caught on the verge of such a naive untruth, she coughed two or three times, in order to put the little prince in the wrong.

但说到这里她突然打住了。她来的时候只是一颗种子。她不可能对任何其他世界有什么了解。她因为差点脱口说出这样一句天真的谎言而被当场抓包，感到十分尴尬，于是她咳了两三下，想把过错推给小王子。

¨The screen?¨

“屏风呢？”

¨I was just going to look for it when you spoke to me ...¨

“你跟我说话的时候，我正准备去找呢……”

Then she forced her cough a little more so that he should suffer from remorse just the same.

📌 Key Expressions

  • force a cough = 故意咳嗽；刻意装作咳嗽
  • a little more = 再稍微……一点
  • so that... = 以便；为了让……
  • should suffer from... = 会因……而感到痛苦
  • remorse = 懊悔；悔恨；内疚
  • suffer from remorse = 受到悔恨的折磨；感到深深的内疚
  • just the same = 同样地；也一样

于是她又故意加重了咳嗽声，好让他依然感到内疚。

So the little prince, in spite of all the good will that was inseparable from his love, had soon come to doubt her. He had taken seriously words which were without importance, and it made him very unhappy.

尽管小王子的爱总是伴随着满满的善意，但他还是很快就开始怀疑她了。他把一些无关紧要的话当了真，这让他感到非常不快乐。

¨I ought not to have listened to her,¨ he confided to me one day. ¨One never ought to listen to the flowers. One should simply look at them and breathe their fragrance. Mine perfumed all my planet. But I did not know how to take pleasure in all her grace. This tale of claws, which disturbed me so much, should only have filled my heart with tenderness and pity.¨

“我不该听她的，”有一天他向我倾诉道，“人们永远都不该听花儿的话。人们只需要静静地看着她们，闻闻她们的芳香就好了。我的花让我的整个星球都充满了香气。可是我却不懂得如何去享受她的魅力。那个让我如此不安的关于利爪的谎话，本应该只会让我的心中充满柔情和怜惜的。”

And he continued his confidences:

他继续向我吐露心声：

¨The fact is that I did not know how to understand anything! I ought to have judged by deeds and not by words. She cast her fragrance and her radiance over me. I ought never to have run away from her ... I ought to have guessed all the affection that lay behind her poor little stratagems. Flowers are so inconsistent! But I was too young to know how to love her ...¨

“事实是我那时什么都不懂！我本应该通过她的行动而不是她的话语来评判她。她把她的芬芳和光彩洒向了我。我绝对不应该离开她逃走……我本应该猜到，在她那拙劣的小把戏背后，隐藏着所有的深情。花儿就是这样口是心非！可是我太年轻了，还不懂得该如何去爱她……”
